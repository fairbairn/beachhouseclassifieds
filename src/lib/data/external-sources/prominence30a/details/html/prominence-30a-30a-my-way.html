About

Prominence pet friendly 30A rental, 30A My Way, located in Watersound, FL by Panhandle Getaways is a 3 bedroom, 2.5 bathroom vacation home with golf cart included along with all of the conveniences of home. 6 Seater Golf Cart, Pet Friendly, 4 Bikes

Traveling with a pet? Upon arrival, your pup will receive a Posh Pet Gift Pack filled with fun, beach-ready goodies. Each pack also includes a Posh Pet Photo Contest card to share a photo of your furry friend enjoying their new gear and bandana. Sunshine, sand, and tail-wagging adventures await!

PET FRIENDLY WITH STIPULATIONS***
We welcome well-trained non-shedding dogs to stay in this home. A daily pet fee will be charged after your reservation is made, should you decide to bring a furry friend (this includes emotional support animals). We require that your dog be completely potty trained and prefer dogs older than 2 years old. Crate-trained pups are preferred to deter mischievous behavior while you and your party are enjoying the beach. It is understood that while we do allow dogs with the mentioned stipulations, any damages that occur during your stay will be charged to the credit card on file with the minimum fee being $350.00. Please no cats for allergenic reasons.

About Prominence on 30A - Watersound, Florida
Prominence is one of the most popular communities on 30A and features incredible amenities, open green spaces and easy access to over 17 miles of bike paths. Prominence is perfectly located to new and long time 30A haunts as well as the beautiful white beaches of Scenic 30A. Guests will enjoy a beautiful town center and 2 resort style pools.

Located at the town center, the Big Chill is a hot-spot for entertainment and features an outdoor 25 ft LED theater screen for sporting events and movie nights on the green. Concerts, family activities, restaurants, and boutique shops are just some of the things to do within a less than 5 minute walk. 

Prominence is centrally located on 30A between Seagrove and Seaside to the west and Alys and Rosemary Beach to the east.

Please note: Pool heated seasonally is subject to change at any time by the HOA for the community. Panhandle Getaways is not responsible for any change in seasonal heating of the one pool in Prominence. No refunds for any change to pool heated seasonally.

Beach Accesses for ALL Prominence homes are via South Walton public beach access points. Most Prominence guests utilize Deer Lake State Park beach access or other nearby public beach accesses. Prominence homes do not have direct beach access via Watersound Club nor do they have access to Watersound private club facilities.

INITIAL SUPPLIES - UPON ARRIVAL
Panhandle Getaways furnishes a few essential items for guests to utilize until they can get to the grocery store. Initial Supplies include: Dishwasher soap, small washing machine powder, each bathroom has amenities (like hotel but NOT restocked) shampoo, conditioner, soap bar. One roll of toilet paper in each bathroom & one paper towel roll in the kitchen. All bed linens and towels are provided. We encourage guests to bring beach towels for use at the pool and beach.

ABOUT MARE E SOLE:

Welcome to Mare E Sole, a beautifully maintained family-friendly vacation rental south of Scenic Highway 30A, located in the gated Sunset Beach community in Seacrest, Florida. Ideally situated between Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach, this coastal retreat offers one of the most sought-after features along 30A: private, deeded beach access just a short walk from the home, no busy roads to cross and no crowded public beach entrances.

This 2-bedroom, 3-bath beach home is thoughtfully designed for families, two couples traveling together, or a small group of friends, offering both comfort and flexibility in one of the most desirable locations on 30A. Guests enjoy a quiet, residential setting while being within walking distance to restaurants, coffee shops, ice cream spots, boutique shopping, bike paths, and all the charm that defines the 30A lifestyle.

Outdoor living is an integral part of the experience at Mare E Sole. Spend your mornings at the beach, afternoons at the community pool, and evenings gathered in the private courtyard, ideal for grilling and relaxed dinners together. The courtyard and upstairs balcony provide multiple spaces for both group time and quiet moments.

Inside, the home welcomes you through double French doors into an open-concept living area designed for easy gathering. The fully stocked kitchen features stainless steel appliances and granite countertops, making meal preparation simple for families and groups. The dining area comfortably seats six, and a full bathroom on the main level adds everyday convenience.

Upstairs, the sleeping accommodations are arranged to provide privacy and versatility. The primary bedroom features a queen bed and an ensuite bathroom, while the second bedroom includes a queen bed, a twin daybed with trundle—ideal for children or additional guests. A full hallway bathroom with a travertine bathtub serves the second bedroom. Both bedrooms open onto a shared balcony, creating a peaceful retreat to start or end the day.

Guests consistently appreciate Mare E Sole for its prime location south of 30A, private beach access, walkability to Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach, and thoughtful layout that works especially well for families and couples traveling together. The combination of a gated community, outdoor living spaces, and easy access to both the beach and pool makes this home a favorite for repeat visits.

Located along Florida's famed Emerald Coast, Seacrest Beach offers a relaxed yet vibrant coastal setting. Enjoy scenic bike rides, family-friendly dining, seasonal events, and miles of sugar-white sand beaches, all within easy reach from this ideally positioned home.

Mare E Sole is the perfect choice for guests seeking a vacation rental south of 30A with private beach access, walkability, and comfortable coastal living for families and friends alike. Book your stay today and experience the best of 30A.

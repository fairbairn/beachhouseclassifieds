ABOUT EASTERN SHORES 209:
Through a set of sliding glass doors lies a picturesque view of the breathtaking blue-green Gulf, inviting you to the balcony where a comfortable rocking chair awaits. The accommodation includes a spacious king-size bed, queen sofa bed, or built-in twin bunks to accommodate 1-6 guests, complete with ceiling fans and a washer/dryer. The Jack-and-Jill style bathroom layout offers convenience for all occupants. Modern amenities include flat-panel TVs in the living room and master bedroom, as well as DVD player, i-Home/i-Pod dock, and wireless internet access. Guests of Eastern Shores benefit from private beach access along with an outdoor shower for added convenience.

With 17 miles of meticulously paved bike trails at your disposal, you have the choice to either bike or drive to embark on exciting adventures. This condominium is an ideal accommodation for a memorable family vacation, an extended weekend getaway, or a cozy winter retreat.

ABOUT THE AREA:
Seagrove Beach is a charming coastal community located along Scenic Highway 30A in the Florida Panhandle. Situated between Seaside and Rosemary Beach, Seagrove Beach epitomizes the laid-back and picturesque beach lifestyle that the area is known for. Known for its stunning white sandy beaches, emerald green waters, and towering sand dunes, Seagrove Beach offers a serene and relaxed ambiance that attracts visitors looking for a peaceful getaway. The beach in Seagrove is perfect for swimming, sunbathing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and simply enjoying the natural beauty of the Gulf Coast.

In addition to its beautiful beach, Seagrove Beach is surrounded by natural beauty, including rare coastal dune lakes, pine forests, and state parks. Visitors can explore hiking and biking trails, go birdwatching, or take a leisurely walk along the scenic coastline to appreciate the area's unique ecosystem. Seagrove Beach is also home to a variety of charming beach cottages, luxury vacation rentals, boutique shops, and delicious restaurants serving fresh seafood and local cuisine.

The community has a laid-back and friendly vibe, making it a favorite destination for families, couples, and outdoor enthusiasts looking to unwind and reconnect with nature. Along Scenic Highway 30A, visitors can explore neighboring beach communities, each with its own distinct character and amenities. From art galleries and coastal dune lakes to live music events and farmers markets, there is always something to see and do along this scenic stretch of coastline. Overall, Seagrove Beach is a hidden gem along Scenic Highway 30A, offering a perfect blend of natural beauty, relaxation, and Southern hospitality that captures the essence of the Florida Gulf Coast.

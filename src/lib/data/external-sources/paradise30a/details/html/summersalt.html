ABOUT SUMMER SALT:

Welcome to Summer Salt, a charming studio condo perfectly located south of 30A in Seagrove Beach, offering stunning balcony views of the Gulf. Situated on the second floor of Beachfront II Condos, this spacious studio comfortably sleeps four guests with a queen-sized bed and a queen sleeper sofa. Enjoy the convenience of a full-sized kitchen, in-unit washer and dryer, and an expansive balcony, all accessible via elevator.

Summer Salt is your gateway to the best of Seagrove Beach and the 30A area. This southern-facing condo allows guests to indulge in a variety of experiences, from lounging by the community pool and hot tub to exploring the beautiful Emerald Coast. Whether biking along 30A, dining at top-notch local restaurants, shopping at nearby boutiques, or sightseeing from Panama City Beach to Destin, there's something for everyone.

Relax and unwind at Summer Salt, where your perfect 30A vacation begins.

ABOUT THE AREA:
Seagrove Beach is a charming coastal community situated along Scenic Highway 30A in the Florida Panhandle. Known for its natural beauty, serene atmosphere, and pristine beaches, Seagrove Beach offers a perfect blend of relaxation and outdoor activities.

One of the defining features of Seagrove Beach is its abundance of lush greenery, including scrub oaks, magnolias, and pines, creating a picturesque backdrop for beachfront living. The area is dotted with coastal dune lakes, rare geographical formations where fresh and saltwater meet, providing unique ecosystems for exploration.

Visitors to Seagrove Beach can enjoy a wide range of outdoor activities, including swimming, paddleboarding, kayaking, and fishing in the crystal-clear waters of the Gulf of Mexico. The nearby Point Washington State Forest offers hiking and biking trails for nature enthusiasts, while golfers can tee off at world-class courses just a short drive away.

Seagrove Beach is also home to an array of charming boutiques, art galleries, and restaurants serving up fresh seafood and local cuisine. The community is known for its laid-back vibe and friendly locals, making it a welcoming destination for families, couples, and solo travelers alike.

With its natural beauty, tranquil ambiance, and proximity to other popular beach communities along 30A, Seagrove Beach is a hidden gem waiting to be explored and enjoyed by all who appreciate the beauty of coastal living.

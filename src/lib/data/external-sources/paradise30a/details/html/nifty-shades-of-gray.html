ABOUT NIFTY SHADES OF GRAY:
Welcome to Nifty Shades of Gray - Your Luxurious Beachfront Retreat where luxury meets the beauty of the Gulf.

Discover the epitome of luxury at Nifty Shades of Gray, an exquisite beachfront home offering breathtaking panoramic ocean views and stunning, professionally curated décor. With 5 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, and parking for up to 4 vehicles, this beautiful property is ideally situated on the east end of 30A, just a short stroll from the vibrant downtown of Rosemary Beach, where you'll find charming open markets, boutique shops, and delightful restaurants.

After a sun-soaked day at the beach or exploring the scenic 30A area, unwind on the expansive deck and balcony, the perfect spots to soak in a world-class coastal sunset. Nifty Shades of Gray provides ample space for families to spread out and relax, making it an ideal destination for multiple families vacationing together or for hosting friends and loved ones.

As you enter the first floor, you'll be captivated by the ocean view that greets you straight through the house. This floor features a cozy bunk room that accommodates 6, with 2 twin-over-queen bunks, a full bathroom, and a comfortable second living room. The first-floor deck is designed for outdoor enjoyment, offering incredible views, generous seating, a built-in cooking area, and a fire pit for those cool coastal evenings.

On the second floor, the open-concept kitchen and living area are highlighted by spectacular panoramic views through striking hexagonal windows. This floor also includes three luxurious king bedrooms, two of which are ensuites, and a second bunk room equipped with a twin-over-full bunk bed with a trundle.

To enhance your stay, Nifty Shades of Gray comes with 4 complimentary Paradise Properties bikes, perfect for exploring the East side of 30A, Inlet Beach, and Rosemary Beach.

ABOUT THE AREA:
Inlet Beach is a charming beach neighborhood located along Florida's scenic Highway 30A. Known for its stunning white sandy beaches, crystal-clear waters, and relaxed atmosphere, Inlet Beach offers a peaceful coastal getaway for visitors seeking a more laid-back vacation experience. This quaint community is characterized by its quiet residential streets lined with beautiful beach cottages and upscale vacation homes, perfect for those looking to unwind and enjoy the natural beauty of the Gulf Coast.

Inlet Beach provides easy access to a variety of outdoor activities, including swimming, sunbathing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and fishing. The nearby Camp Helen State Park offers nature trails for hiking and birdwatching, while Lake Powell - one of Florida's largest coastal dune lakes - provides opportunities for boating and wildlife viewing. Visitors can also explore the neighboring beach communities of Rosemary Beach, Seacrest Beach, and Aly's Beach, each offering unique shopping, dining, and entertainment options.

With its pristine beaches, serene ambiance, and proximity to a wealth of recreational opportunities, Inlet Beach is a hidden gem along Highway 30A, perfect for those seeking a peaceful retreat on Florida's Gulf Coast.

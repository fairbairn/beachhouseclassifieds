ABOUT THE FLIPSIDE 30A:
Welcome to The Flipside 30A, a luxury condo with great ocean views, updated decor, and remodeled throughout to help you relax and enjoy your time on the beach. The Flipside 30A sleeps 6 and has 3 bedrooms 2 bathrooms in a spacious 1,300 square foot condo. Relax with your family as you take in the excitement of the Village Town Center and enjoy the views of the Gulf of Mexico from the private balcony. The Flipside 30A is the perfect condo for a family vacation to 30A, a romantic getaway, a couples trip to Paradise 30A, or a snowbird's retreat to the beach.....

This three bedroom floor plan boasts one king guest bedroom with a flat screen TV and adjoining bathroom; a queen bedroom with flat screen TV, and closet for extra storage; the third bedroom is a spacious King master with Jacuzzi tub and standalone shower ensuite, flat screen TV, access to the balcony and ocean views. The balcony is the place to be as you enjoy the excitement of The Village Town Center and the views of the Gulf of Mexico.

Guests of The Flipside 30A enjoy being steps from Seacrest Beach, sitting by the pool, the shops and restaurants below, the concerts in the loop, a bike ride through Alys Beach, Rosemary Beach, or Inlet Beach, the seasonal tram service to the beach, and so much more. With all of the amenities, the location to the great restaurants, ocean views, and shopping; you truly are in Paradise. Come enjoy the beach at The Flipside 30A!

ABOUT THE AREA:
Seacrest Beach, Florida, is a charming coastal community nestled along the renowned Emerald Coast. Situated between the iconic beach destinations of Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach offers a tranquil escape with easy access to the area's vibrant attractions and natural splendor.

Beyond its breathtaking beaches, Seacrest Beach boasts a laid-back atmosphere and a sense of community that is both welcoming and inclusive. Stroll along the cobblestone streets lined with quaint cottages and lush landscaping, and you ll discover a thriving village center brimming with boutiques, cafes, and restaurants. From eclectic eateries serving up fresh seafood to charming shops offering unique souvenirs and artisanal goods, there is something to delight every palate and preference.

For those seeking outdoor recreation, Seacrest Beach offers a wealth of options to explore its natural beauty. Take a leisurely bike ride along scenic trails, meander through the unique shops and boutiques, or embark on a sunset hike to catch breathtaking views of the coastline. Nearby state parks and nature preserves provide opportunities for birdwatching, wildlife spotting, and immersive encounters with the area's diverse ecosystems. In addition to its natural attractions, Seacrest Beach hosts a variety of events and activities throughout the year, from live music performances and art festivals to farmers markets and community gatherings. Whether you are a year-round resident or a visitor seeking a memorable vacation, there's always something exciting happening in Seacrest Beach.

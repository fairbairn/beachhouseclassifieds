ABOUT GOLDEN ON 30A:
Perched in a prime location overlooking the Towne Center of Santa Rosa Beach and situated directly across from the renowned World's Most Beautiful Beaches, the fully furnished coastal condo at Golden on 30A presents an unparalleled opportunity for a beachside retreat. A mere 5-minute stroll from the elevator leads guests to a boardwalk beckoning towards the pristine sandy shores and shimmering emerald waters. In addition, residents of Golden on 30A enjoy access to three inviting swimming pools.

Immersed within a 25-acre community designed to infuse resort-style amenities with the relaxed essence of beachfront living, this enclave offers a myriad of offerings to ensure a memorable vacation experience. Featuring picturesque walking trails, expansive green spaces with numerous park benches, outdoor patios adorned with large umbrellas, and palm-lined streets, the setting is ideal for relaxation and rejuvenation. Whether permanent residents or short-term visitors, individuals can congregate to appreciate original art, upscale shopping, delectable local cuisine, and live musical performances in a laid-back ambiance.

This condo showcases a panoramic view of the beach from its fully furnished private balcony, with the added advantage of prime people-watching opportunities. For those inclined towards a quieter setting, the terrace affords delightful sights of charming canines on their daily strolls. With a choice between dining at any of the six local restaurants within the Towne Center or opting for a cozy in-room meal, guests are presented with a wealth of culinary options. The private condo at Golden on 30A further provides the flexibility of indoor or outdoor dining arrangements, complemented by a fully-equipped kitchenette ideal for preparing meals or assembling beachside picnics.

Featuring a plush king-sized bed and a queen sleeper sofa, guests are ensured a restful night's sleep, setting the stage for a day of indulgence at the exclusive onsite day spa. Offering a range of treatments from massage therapy to pampering pedicures, guests can luxuriate in a serene environment conducive to relaxation and tranquility. From romantic getaways for two to fun-filled group trips with friends, family vacations, or solo adventures, the experience at Golden on 30A caters to a diverse range of preferences and occasions.

ABOUT THE AREA:
Dune Allen Beach, situated along the scenic Highway 30A in the Florida Panhandle, is a hidden gem renowned for its pristine white sand beaches, turquoise waters, and tranquil ambiance. This charming coastal community offers a perfect blend of natural beauty and luxury amenities, making it a sought-after destination for travelers seeking a peaceful beach retreat. Visitors to Dune Allen Beach can enjoy a variety of activities, from sunbathing and swimming in the Gulf of Mexico's crystal-clear waters to exploring the nearby coastal dunes and nature trails. The area is also known for its world-class golf courses, upscale shopping boutiques, and fine dining establishments, providing opportunities for relaxation and indulgence.

With its laid-back vibe and stunning natural surroundings, Dune Allen Beach offers a serene escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life. Whether you're looking for a romantic getaway, a family vacation, or a solo adventure, this coastal paradise has something to offer for everyone.

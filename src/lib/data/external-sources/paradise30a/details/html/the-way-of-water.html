ABOUT THE WAY OF WATER:
Welcome to "The Way of Water: This stunning 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom vacation rental is your perfect retreat on the Northside of Scenic 30A in Inlet Beach, Florida. Newly constructed and designed with modern elegance, this home comfortably accommodates up to 10 guests, making it an ideal spot for family getaways or group vacations. With brand new furnishings, sleeper sofa in the living room, a fully equipped kitchen, and versatile indoor/outdoor dining spaces, your stay here promises comfort and style.

The third-floor family suite offers ample space for relaxation and entertainment, while the entire home exudes a welcoming ambiance that will make you feel right at home. Pet owners rejoice—this private residence is dog-friendly, ensuring that your furry friend can join in on the fun. Guests can enjoy the amenities of a gated community pool and explore the vibrant 30A area with 2 complimentary bicycle cruisers.

With local shops, restaurants, and the charm of 30A Avenue just a short ride away, "The Way of Water" is your gateway to an unforgettable beachside experience.

ABOUT THE AREA:
Inlet Beach is a charming beach neighborhood located along Florida's scenic Highway 30A. Known for its stunning white sandy beaches, crystal-clear waters, and relaxed atmosphere, Inlet Beach offers a peaceful coastal getaway for visitors seeking a more laid-back vacation experience. This quaint community is characterized by its quiet residential streets lined with beautiful beach cottages and upscale vacation homes, perfect for those looking to unwind and enjoy the natural beauty of the Gulf Coast.

When you stay in Inlet Beach you are never far from the beach, or a magnificent view. To explore 30A, take a bike ride to Rosemary Beach to enjoy the farmers market, discover the new 30A Avenue shops, taste the fine local cuisine, and visit local businesses along 30A. Visitors can also explore the neighboring beach communities of Rosemary Beach, Seacrest Beach, and Alys Beach, each offering unique shopping, dining, and entertainment options. For additional shopping and adventure, take a family drive to Pier Park in Panama City Beach or travel to the Sandestin Outlet shops.

Inlet Beach provides easy access to a variety of outdoor activities, including swimming, sunbathing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and fishing. The nearby Camp Helen State Park offers nature trails for hiking and birdwatching, while Lake Powell - one of Florida's largest coastal dune lakes - provides opportunities for boating and wildlife viewing.

With its pristine beaches, serene ambiance, and proximity to a wealth of recreational opportunities, Inlet Beach is a hidden gem along Highway 30A, perfect for those seeking a peaceful retreat on Florida's Gulf Coast.

Overview ↑↓

Welcome to Inlet Dunes 205- Salt & Light - your 30A escape in the peaceful and picturesque neighborhood of Inlet Beach!

Remodeled in 2025 with modern, elegant finishes, this one-bedroom retreat sleeps like a 2BR - the twin-sized hallway bunks have curtains for privacy, with a door on the hallway for when it's time to put the kiddos to bed and unwind! A queen sleeper sofa is also available for extra guests. The spacious, open-concept living area is ready for you to make memories!

The private balcony overlooks the community pool, and is shaded by live oaks. Salt & Light is nestled in a small gated community that is directly across the street from the beach boardwalk! Literally less than 500 feet from the water!

With its unbeatable location and relaxing atmosphere, Salt & Light is your gateway to unforgettable beach days, breathtaking sunsets, and laid-back coastal living.

Book your stay at Salt & Light and experience the magic of Inlet Beach and 30A - where every day feels like paradise!


ABOUT THE AREA
Located on the eastern edge of the renowned 30A corridor, Inlet Beach offers a perfect mix of tranquility and adventure. Known for its laid-back atmosphere and pristine beauty, this coastal gem is home to sugar-white sand beaches, crystal-clear waters, and stunning natural landscapes. When you stay at Salt & Light, you'll be steps away from everything that makes Inlet Beach a favorite destination for vacationers.

Start your day with a morning stroll along the scenic boardwalks leading to the beach. Spend your afternoon soaking up the sun, paddleboarding, or exploring nearby attractions. Just minutes away, you'll find Camp Helen State Park, a haven for nature lovers with walking trails and views of Lake Powell, one of Florida's rare coastal dune lakes.

Feeling adventurous? Discover the vibrant communities along 30A, including the upscale shopping and dining of Rosemary Beach, located just a short bike ride away. Enjoy farm-to-table dining, quaint boutiques, and charming cafes that embody the local spirit of the area. Explore the nearby Alys Beach and its iconic architecture, or head to Seaside, famous for its pastel-colored cottages and lively outdoor markets.

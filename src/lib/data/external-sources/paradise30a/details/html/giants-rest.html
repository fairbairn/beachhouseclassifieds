ABOUT GIANT'S REST:
Welcome to Giant's Rest, a serene getaway nestled in the tranquil community of Inlet Beach. This newly furnished coastal retreat spans three stories and boasts six bedrooms and four bathrooms, offering an idyllic setting for a beach vacation. Admire the stunning gulf views and captivating sunsets from the dual outdoor decks, while the private pool below invites relaxation.

The third floor showcases a luxurious master bedroom and bath, featuring access to a shared balcony with panoramic ocean views and a refreshing sea breeze. Start your day with a sunrise on the balcony or unwind in the evening to the soothing sounds of the ocean waves. This floor also includes a second master bedroom with a private bath, both sharing the balcony and breathtaking vistas.

On the second floor, the living, dining, and kitchen area awaits, along with two bedrooms accommodating up to eight guests. The living area is equipped with a 60" HDTV and a Blu-ray Sony Play Station, perfect for entertainment. The spacious family-style dining area comfortably seats 10 guests with additional seating for up to 18. A well-appointed kitchen with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and ample cookware completes the space. Additionally, a colorful bunk room and a queen bedroom with access to an outdoor deck offer cozy retreats on this floor.

The first floor features two bedrooms and a game room accommodating six guests. The kids' bedroom boasts three twin beds, while the comfortable queen bedroom provides direct access to the pool area. The game room, opening to the pool, features a 42" HDTV and a futon, ideal for post-dinner entertainment.

Outdoor living at Giant's Rest includes a spacious patio with a private pool, lounging deck, gas grill, and ample seating to soak up the Florida Sunshine. A two-car garage offers convenient parking, with an additional refrigerator for larger groups.

Just steps from the secluded beaches of Inlet Beach, Giant's Rest is a hidden gem at the eastern end of Highway 30-A, near Rosemary Beach. Explore the dining, shopping, and recreational offerings of 30-A while enjoying a peaceful retreat in Inlet Beach. Make Giant's Rest your next beach destination and enjoy the complementary heatable pool for a cozy swim experience.

ABOUT THE AREA:
Inlet Beach is a charming beach neighborhood located along Florida's scenic Highway 30A. Known for its stunning white sandy beaches, crystal-clear waters, and relaxed atmosphere, Inlet Beach offers a peaceful coastal getaway for visitors seeking a more laid-back vacation experience. This quaint community is characterized by its quiet residential streets lined with beautiful beach cottages and upscale vacation homes, perfect for those looking to unwind and enjoy the natural beauty of the Gulf Coast.

Inlet Beach provides easy access to a variety of outdoor activities, including swimming, sunbathing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and fishing. The nearby Camp Helen State Park offers nature trails for hiking and birdwatching, while Lake Powell - one of Florida's largest coastal dune lakes - provides opportunities for boating and wildlife viewing. Visitors can also explore the neighboring beach communities of Rosemary Beach, Seacrest Beach, and Aly's Beach, each offering unique shopping, dining, and entertainment options.

With its pristine beaches, serene ambiance, and proximity to a wealth of recreational opportunities, Inlet Beach is a hidden gem along Highway 30A, perfect for those seeking a peaceful retreat on Florida's Gulf Coast.

ABOUT INLET DUNES 201:
Inlet Dunes #201 presents a refined family-style condominium on the first floor, conveniently situated across from the beach. Located within the exclusive gated community of Inlet Dunes, this tranquil retreat near the Inlet Beach Regional Access beckons families for a serene vacation experience year after year. Boasting a generously sized pool, an outdoor grilling area, and a prime location adjacent to Scenic 30A, guests can expect a seamless and enjoyable stay.

Recently renovated, Inlet Dunes #201 has a great updated interior, providing accommodation for up to six guests. The layout features a king bedroom, a charming twin bunk nook, and a queen sleeper sofa, ensuring comfort and convenience for all. Guests of Inlet Dunes #201 can relish direct access to a private walkout patio offering picturesque views of the gated pool and community grilling area. Additionally, a private beach gate leads guests to the Inlet Beach Regional Access, equipped with amenities such as restrooms, outdoor showers, ample parking, and picnic areas.

The charming coastal town of Rosemary Beach is a mere five-minute bicycle ride away, while the upscale shops, restaurants, and outdoor concerts of 30Avenue can be reached in under five minutes to the North. Whether it's a modest family seeking a memorable vacation on the stunning Emerald Coast or an intimate group of friends in search of a celebratory getaway, Inlet Dunes #201 ensures a delightful experience. Check-in time commences at 4 PM at this great retreat.

ABOUT THE AREA:
Inlet Beach is a charming beach neighborhood located along Florida's scenic Highway 30A. Known for its stunning white sandy beaches, crystal-clear waters, and relaxed atmosphere, Inlet Beach offers a peaceful coastal getaway for visitors seeking a more laid-back vacation experience. This quaint community is characterized by its quiet residential streets lined with beautiful beach cottages and upscale vacation homes, perfect for those looking to unwind and enjoy the natural beauty of the Gulf Coast.

Inlet Beach provides easy access to a variety of outdoor activities, including swimming, sunbathing, paddleboarding, kayaking, and fishing. The nearby Camp Helen State Park offers nature trails for hiking and birdwatching, while Lake Powell - one of Florida's largest coastal dune lakes - provides opportunities for boating and wildlife viewing. Visitors can also explore the neighboring beach communities of Rosemary Beach, Seacrest Beach, and Alys Beach, each offering unique shopping, dining, and entertainment options.

With its pristine beaches, serene ambiance, and proximity to a wealth of recreational opportunities, Inlet Beach is a hidden gem along Highway 30A, perfect for those seeking a peaceful retreat on Florida's Gulf Coast.

Relax and unwind in this cozy Seagrove condo. Boasting Gulf views and direct beach access, this one-bedroom getaway comes fully equipped with all the comforts of home. The full kitchen comes ready for home cooking with a full suite of major appliances, including a dishwasher for easy cleanup. The private balcony is perfect for sipping a morning cup of coffee or a sunset cocktail. The spacious primary bedroom includes a 40 inch smart TV for added entertainment options. A built-in hallway bunk nook is great for kids to have their own space.

WHAT'S NEARBY
This property is part of the Beachside Condos, a Gulf-front complex in Seagrove with a private boardwalk that leads to the beach. You'll be across the street from several shops, eateries, and recreational outfitters that rent bikes and kayaks.

THINGS TO KNOW

Parking notes: 1 spot per unit. No assigned spot, can park in any spot.

Golf cart rentals are allowed. You can rent a gas powered golf cart and utilize your 1 parking space OR rent a battery powered golf cart rental across the street at Butterfly Bike and Kayak and park/charge there.

This rental is located on floor 2. No elevator onsite.

Sorry, HOA does not allow pets.

🌅 Top-floor corner unit with vaulted ceilings — no upstairs neighbors in a low-density building
🏝️ Private beach access shared by only 24 units
⛱️ Seasonal beach chair service included (2 chairs + umbrella | March 1–Oct 31)
🪑 Extra Tommy Bahama backpack chairs, umbrellas, cooler & beach towels provided (save ~$25/day)
🚲 2 adult beach cruiser bikes included (save ~$50/day)

🚶‍♀️ Prime location- 1.3 miles to Seaside, Walk to restaurants & shopping

Relax and unwind in this cozy Seagrove condo. Boasting Gulf views and direct beach access, this two-bedroom getaway comes fully equipped with all the comforts of home. The full kitchen comes ready for home cooking with a full suite of major appliances, including a dishwasher for easy cleanup. The gulf front private balcony is perfect for sipping a morning cup of coffee or a sunset cocktail. The spacious primary bedroom includes an ensuite bathroom and beautiful gulf front views and a second door to the balcony. A second bedroom includes a queen bed and bunk beds along with a bathroom with bathtub and shower.

WHAT'S NEARBY
This property is part of the Beachside Condos, a Gulf-front complex in Seagrove Beach with a private boardwalk that leads to the beach. You'll be across the street from several shops, eateries, and recreational outfitters that rent bikes and kayaks.

THINGS TO KNOW
Parking notes: 1 spot per unit. No assigned spot, can park in any spot.

Golf cart rentals are allowed. You can rent a gas powered golf cart and utilize your parking space OR rent a battery powered golf cart rental across the street at Butterfly Bike and Kayak and park/charge there.

This rental is located on floor 3. No elevator onsite.

Sorry, HOA does not allow pets.

Welcome to Turf to Surf

Your perfect coastal escape awaits at Turf to Surf, a beautifully designed and tastefully decorated retreat tucked away in the peaceful community of Shadow Pines in Santa Rosa Beach.

This stunning 3-story home features 4 bedrooms (each with a private en suite bath), a 6-seater golf cart, and modern amenities that make it the ideal base for family vacations or group getaways. Just a short stroll from the beach, you’ll be close to all the charm and excitement of 30A while enjoying a quiet neighborhood setting.

ABOUT THE AREA

Nestled just steps away from the iconic sugar-white sands and crystal-clear waters of Florida’s Emerald Coast, Gulf Place is one of 30A’s most vibrant communities.

Located at the intersection of 30A and 393, Gulf Place offers charming palm-lined streets, nature trails, outdoor dining, and some of 30A’s favorite spots like Shunk Gulley and The Perfect Pig — ideal for beachside bites or sunset dinners.

Whether you’re planning lazy beach days, scenic bike rides, or dining adventures, Turf to Surf places you right in the heart of it all.

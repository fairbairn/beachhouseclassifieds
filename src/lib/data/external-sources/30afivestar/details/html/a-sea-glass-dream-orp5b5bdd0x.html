Welcome to A Sea Glass Dream – a designer 3-bedroom, 3.5-bath retreat in the heart of WaterColor, one of 30A’s most sought-after communities.

This home sleeps 8 and blends coastal charm with modern luxury. Guests enjoy exclusive access to the WaterColor Beach Club, Camp WaterColor, and multiple resort-style pools.

From the moment you arrive, the Southern porch—with its swing and Adirondack chairs—sets the tone for relaxation. Inside, vaulted ceilings, a striking two-story gas fireplace, and sunlit shiplap interiors create an inviting space for gathering.

The open kitchen is a chef’s delight, featuring quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and seating for 12 between the dining table and breakfast bar.

Each of the three bedrooms is beautifully appointed, offering a restful escape after sun-filled days.

Spend mornings biking scenic trails, afternoons at the Beach Club’s gulf-front pools and dining venues, and evenings exploring Seaside’s iconic shops and restaurants—just minutes away.

Whether you’re planning a multi-generational family trip or a coastal getaway with friends, A Sea Glass Dream offers the perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and access to the very best of 30A living.

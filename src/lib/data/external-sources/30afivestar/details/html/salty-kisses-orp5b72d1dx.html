Welcome to Salty Kisses 🌊

Your private coastal retreat in the gated Villages of Crystal Beach, one of Destin’s most sought-after beach communities.

🌴 SALTY KISSES — RESORT AMENITIES & GUEST PERKS

⭐ Professionally managed with 24/7 guest support by 30A Five Star Properties
🏝️ 30,000 sq. ft. tropical lagoon-style pool — one of the area’s most stunning resort pools
🔥 Pool heated seasonally (March 1–April 15, Thanksgiving week, Christmas week & New Year’s week) for comfortable swimming year-round
🏖️ Beach wagon, chairs & toys included so beach days are easy and stress-free
💦 Three waterfalls, relaxing hot tub & surround-sound music create a true resort-style experience
🍹 Shaded tiki bar perfect for poolside lounging and vacation cocktails
🚎 Seasonal beach trolley service (March 1–November 1) offering convenient pick-up & drop-off to the beach
🌴 Beautifully landscaped walkways throughout a private gated community for peaceful strolls
✨ Whether you're relaxing poolside or hopping on the trolley to the beach, Salty Kisses puts coastal luxury and convenience right at your fingertips.

This custom-built coastal retreat is designed for comfort, entertaining, and unforgettable vacations.

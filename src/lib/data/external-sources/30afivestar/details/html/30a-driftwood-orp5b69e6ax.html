Unwind at this stunning beach bungalow blending coastal charm with modern comforts. Enjoy open, sunlit spaces, sleek finishes, and a breezy private deck overlooking the pool/spa. It's the ultimate retreat for a relaxed yet sophisticated beachside lifestyle.

Nestled in a private community with direct access to the Beachwood Villas access point. Thoughtfully designed for ultimate comfort and convenience, it's perfect for a romantic couples' getaway or a fun-filled family retreat for up to six.

This charming 2-bedroom beach bungalow offers the perfect coastal escape. The master bedroom features a plush king-sized bed, while the second bedroom is ideal for families, with a comfortable queen-sized bed and cozy bunk beds.

Step from your master bedroom onto the patio, where you'll be greeted by the sea breeze and poolside views. Enjoy your morning coffee at a shaded breakfast table by the pool/spa surrounded by mature palm trees.

Step into a warm and inviting first-floor retreat where an open-concept living room and kitchen welcome you. The modern kitchen, complete with island seating, is perfect for casual breakfasts or sipping coffee while chatting. A spacious dining area is ideal for family meals and gatherings. Fully stocked with all the essentials—fridge, microwave, toaster, coffee maker (including a Keurig!), and dishwasher—the kitchen makes cooking and cleanup a breeze, so you can spend more time relaxing and enjoying your stay.

Beautifully furnished master bedroom comprises a king bed with a 55" TV, down duvet, and soft linens. The bathroom, newly renovated, boasts a walk-in shower with fluffy white towels and soaps.

Conveniently located across the hall is your second bedroom comprising a queen bed with a set of bunks, all with duvet covers and soft linens, and a 43" TV. The bathroom, also newly renovated, features both a tub and shower with fluffy white towels and soaps.

The bungalow is located within a couple blocks of some of the best food in the area served by Cafe Thirty-A, Reel 'Em In Seafood, and Brozinni's Pizzeria. Not to mention only 1.5 miles from Seaside.

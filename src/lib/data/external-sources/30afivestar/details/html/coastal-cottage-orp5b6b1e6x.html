This is a beautiful 3-bedroom beach cottage with all the updates and amenities you could ask for plus an incredible backyard oasis. From the screened-in porch to the fenced yard draped with greenery, the yard feels like a true secret garden. This home greets you with open concept living decorated in soothing neutrals with pops of coastal blues. Soaring ceilings and comfortable furnishings make this cottage a relaxing spot for your 30A vacation.

No flights of stairs to worry about at this beautiful cottage, everyone is conveniently on the same level. Spend your days relaxing indoors or out. There's plenty of spots to grab a seat and relax. You're minutes from the Gulf waters and also have your choice of 2 pools within the neighborhood. Walk to all of Seagrove's best restaurants or hop on your bike and explore more of 30A. A stay at Coastal Cottage puts you central to all things 30A!

Cassine Village:
Cassine Village is a charming neighborhood in a natural setting. This property located in the heart of beautiful Seagrove Beach features on-site tennis courts, your choice of 2 pools, a nature trail through a native cypress forest, and a pond. Take a 5-minute walk along the bricked sidewalks to the pristine sands of the Emerald Coast. The kids can explore the nature trail or fish from the bridge over the community pond. And, they can enjoy their bikes along the quiet streets or the family can pedal to Seaside. There are 26 miles of trails along 30A. You’re only a short walk for a pizza at Brozinni’s Pizzeria or your favorite pasta dish at Angelina’s. Make reservations at Café Thirty-A for an upscale dining experience, or bike to Goatfeather’s Seafood for the catch of the day.

Seagrove Beach:
Named for the thick grove of oak trees lining its coastline, Seagrove Beach is located in the heart of 30A. The coastal villages of Seaside, Grayton, and WaterColor lie within a mile or two to the West, while Rosemary Beach, Alys Beach, and WaterSound lie a few miles East. There's no better spot to experience everything 30A has to offer!

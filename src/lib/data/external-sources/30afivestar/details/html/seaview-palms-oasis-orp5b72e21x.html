Welcome to Seaview Palms Oasis, your modern coastal getaway just moments from the turquoise waves and sugar-white sands of 30A. This stylish condo blends comfort and convenience, offering a relaxing king bedroom, cozy bunk beds, and bright living spaces perfect for families or groups.

Guests love the easy access to Seagrove Beach, walkability to Seaside, and the peaceful, gated community amenities. Spend your days biking along scenic trails, exploring local shops and restaurants, or unwinding by the pool after a beach-filled morning.

Seagrove is a longtime favorite in South Walton, offering a laid-back coastal atmosphere with plenty to explore. Don’t miss a local classic like Café 30A or a sunset bike ride to Seaside and WaterColor.

We’re so excited to welcome you to Seaview Palms Oasis. Your beach escape awaits!

Condo is on the 3rd floor. No elevator on site.

Sorry, HOA does not allow pets.

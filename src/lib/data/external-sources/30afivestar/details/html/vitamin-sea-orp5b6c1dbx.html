VITAMIN SEA FEATURES:

- Private Beach for the Palms owners and guests
- Chair Service for 2 included with your stay
- 2 Adult Bikes included with your stay
- Updated interior and professionally decorated
- Ground Floor (no stairs!)
- King Bed with Hall Bunks
- 2 full size bathrooms
- Washer/Dryer inside condo
- Walk to Seaside
- Community pool and grill
- Fully Stocked Kitchen including drip and Keurig coffee maker
- Community bike racks for personal or rented bikes
- Walking distance to many shopping and dining options

Here you’re only steps away from the sugar white sands and turquoise waters of 30A! This gorgeous, updated condo is the perfect vacation getaway for any party of two. The comfortable king bed and private first floor patio will make Vitamin Sea feel like your home away from home. The spacious living area opens up to a fully equipped kitchen.

The Palms at Seagrove offers gated beach and pool access, generous green space, charcoal grills and bike racks. Take advantage of the 22 mile bike path and enjoy some of the natural surroundings of South Walton.
Vitamin Sea is located on the first floor of building D with patio access.

Seagrove Beach is a favorite for longtime South Walton visitors. When you’re not soaking up the Florida sunshine, there are plenty of activities to enjoy and places to explore! Ride a bike and take a quick ride to Seaside and Watercolor or bike along the Timpoochee Trail. Eating at Café 30A is a Seagrove tradition! Fine dining, shopping and entertainment is just minutes away.

We can’t wait to host you at Vitamin Sea!

DETAILS:
30A Beach Bungalow is a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom retreat offering 1,428 sq ft of coastal charm on the south side of 30A. Tucked down a private, tree-lined driveway in Seagrove, the home is ideally located near beach access, Eastern Lake, and the area's best dining. With its inviting front porch, large backyard, and thoughtfully designed interior, this home is perfect for a laid-back beach vacation with family or friends.

Inside, guests will find a comfortable living room with a Smart TV and no-fuss, streaming entertainment. The kitchen includes ample counter space, bar seating, and a walk-in pantry. The adjoining laundry room adds extra convenience. A queen bedroom and full bath are located on the main level, while upstairs hosts additional bedrooms that share a full bath.
The outdoor space is a standout feature, offering a tranquil, shaded yard, cornhole, a dining area, a lounge zone, and a gas grill for easy al fresco meals. The home also comes with two complimentary bikes for exploring the 30A area.

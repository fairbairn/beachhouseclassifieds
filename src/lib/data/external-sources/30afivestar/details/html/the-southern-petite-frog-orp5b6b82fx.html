Welcome to The Southern Frog & The Petite Frog – Your Perfect Coastal Retreat on 30A Nestled in the heart of the beautiful WaterColor community, just steps from the stunning beaches of Florida's renowned 30A, The Southern Frog and The Petite Frog offer a spacious, stylish escape with a blend of coastal chic elegance and modern comfort. Designed with crisp white interiors, accented by cool blues and metallic touches, this home perfectly captures the laid-back yet sophisticated spirit of the Florida Panhandle.

Location, Location, Location! Ideally situated within WaterColor, you'll enjoy easy access to the area’s charming boardwalks, with a quick 2-minute bike ride to the newly updated Camp WaterColor, and just a 1-minute stroll to the Frog Pool. Whether you’re exploring nearby shops, dining at local restaurants, or simply lounging by the pool, you’re never far from the best that 30A has to offer. The Home: With room for up to 10 guests, The Southern Frog and The Petite Frog are ideal for families or groups looking for both togetherness and privacy. The main house offers a king suite on the first floor, while the second floor features two additional king suites and a charming bunk room with four twin beds—perfect for the kids or younger guests. For even more space, the detached carriage house loft is a cozy hideaway complete with a queen bed, a queen sleeper sofa, a dining area, and a full kitchen. This private space is perfect for grandparents, the nanny, or anyone who might appreciate a bit of extra seclusion during their stay.

- Enjoy exclusive access to both the Southern Frog (main house) and the Petite Frog (carriage house) when you book this listing.
- Booking this listing gives you the entire property to yourselves, so you can relax and enjoy complete privacy.

Welcome to your perfect coastal getaway at Beachumall!

This charming home is ideally located just a short walk from the stunning Eastern Lake, offering guests easy access to one of the area’s most picturesque natural settings. Whether you enjoy kayaking, paddleboarding, or simply taking in the serene beauty of the lake, it’s right at your doorstep. And for those seeking the perfect beach day, the beach access to the Gulf of Mexico is just a few minutes away, making it simple to enjoy the soft white sands and crystal-clear waters.

With both Eastern Lake and the beach so close, you’ll have the best of both worlds: peaceful lakefront views and easy access to the beach!

Experience the Ultimate Coastal Escape at Beachumall in Seagrove Beach, FL

Located just SOUTH of 30A, this stunning 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home is the perfect place for you and your loved ones to unwind. Beachumall offers a spacious retreat with plenty of room to spread out and enjoy everything Seagrove Beach has to offer.

Listen to the soothing sound of the Gulf’s waves from the comfort of your home, and know that the pristine shoreline is just a short stroll away. Seagrove Beach’s exclusive, small-town charm combines with the convenience of being right in the heart of Scenic Highway 30A, where you’ll find renowned beaches, dining, and shopping just moments from your front door.

Whether you’re here for beach days, bike rides along the 30A corridor, or exploring the natural beauty of the area, Beachumall is the perfect home base for your next family getaway or group retreat.

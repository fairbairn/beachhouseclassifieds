Amenities – Allows pets Internet Near Ocean (more...)

Nestled in the charming beachside community of Old Florida Cottages, just south of 30A, this beautifully furnished four-bedroom, three-bath retreat offers the perfect blend of coastal comfort and relaxation. Unwind on the spacious porches while taking in breathtaking Gulf sunsets, or take a short stroll to the sugar-white sands via the nearby Gulfview Heights beach access. Prefer to stay close? Enjoy the community pool with family and friends.

Inside, the first-floor king owner's suite features a private en suite with an oversized shower and direct access to the wraparound porch. This level also includes a second king bedroom and a cozy bunk room, both sharing a well-appointed bath. Upstairs, the open-concept second floor boasts a bright and airy living area, a fully equipped kitchen, and a dining space perfect for gathering. An additional bedroom on this level provides extra comfort.

For the ultimate beach getaway, the third-floor tower room offers a fun and spacious retreat, sleeping six with two sets of bunks. Whether you're here for sun-soaked beach days or laid-back evenings on the porch, this coastal escape is ready to welcome you!

🌊 EMERALD TIDE HIGHLIGHTS

⭐ Professionally managed with 24/7 guest support by 30A Five Star Properties for a smooth, worry-free vacation
🏡 Spacious 3,264 sq ft home featuring 5 bedrooms & 4.5 bathrooms — perfect for families & groups
🍳 Gourmet kitchen with professional-grade appliances for effortless vacation dining
🏊‍♂️ Private pool & hot tub for ultimate relaxation (pool heat available for $65/day for entire stay)
🏎️ 6-seat electric golf cart / LSV included for easy trips to the beach, pools & nearby attractions
🚲 4 YOLO beach cruiser bikes included for exploring scenic neighborhood paths
🏖️ 12 wristbands included for access to all WaterColor community pools, including Camp WaterColor and the WaterColor Beach Club
⛱️ 6 beach chairs & 3 umbrellas provided so your beach days are easy and comfortable

Welcome to Emerald Tide, your dream escape in WaterColor’s exclusive Crossings District. This home blends high-end luxury with effortless coastal comfort—the perfect setting for family vacations, group getaways, or long-overdue beach retreats.
From the moment you step inside, you’ll be welcomed by exceptional furnishings and an airy, open-concept layout. The main living area boasts soaring ceilings, a large flat-screen TV, and a cozy fireplace, perfect for winding down after a day on the beach. The gourmet kitchen is a chef’s dream, complete with a spacious marble island, premium appliances, and an adjoining dining area bathed in natural light, featuring stylish seating for six.

The first-floor master suite offers a serene escape with a king-sized bed, elegant coffered ceilings, and tasteful, rustic-chic décor. Its emerald-toned en-suite bath is a sanctuary in itself, showcasing dual vanities, a walk-in shower, and a luxurious soaking tub. Step directly onto the patio each morning and sip your coffee in the calm of your private outdoor haven.

Upstairs, you’ll find two additional king suites with private baths, a cozy guest room with a full bed and access to a shared hallway bath, and a bonus bunk room with two twin-over-twin bunk beds—perfect for kids or extra guests. A second living area provides even more space to relax, complete with plush seating, a TV, and balcony access.

The outdoor space is where 'Emerald Tide' truly shines. Spend your days lounging by the pool, unwinding in the spa, or enjoying meals on the screened-in lanai, which features a built-in natural gas grill, fireplace, and mounted TV. Watch the kids splash and cannonball while you soak in the Florida sunshine from comfortable wicker seating.

With every detail designed for comfort and luxury, 'Emerald Tide' is more than a vacation rental—it’s your home away from home in beautiful WaterColor. Come create memories that will last a lifetime.

Welcome to Watercolor Waves, a beautifully appointed luxury condo in the heart of WaterColor’s popular Park District. Designed with relaxed coastal elegance, this retreat offers the perfect balance of comfort, style, and convenience. Enjoy easy access to WaterColor’s award-winning community pools, the Boathouse on Western Lake, and the pristine WaterColor Beach, all within walking distance. For a taste of local charm, Seaside is just a short bike ride away, offering boutique shopping, dining, and vibrant activity.

Step through the French doors onto the well-appointed second-floor balcony, where you can sip your morning coffee while taking in peaceful neighborhood views. Inside, the living room welcomes you with hardwood floors, coastal-inspired décor, and freshly painted walls—the perfect space to unwind after a day on 30A. A large flat-screen TV makes movie nights with family or friends extra special.

The kitchen is a chef’s dream, featuring Dorian countertops, crisp white cabinets, and all-new stainless steel appliances. It opens to the dining area with seating for six, creating a seamless space for meals or entertaining. A conveniently located half bath completes the main living area, making everyday living effortless.

The thoughtful floor plan places both bedrooms on the first floor for privacy and convenience. The primary bedroom features a king bed and a private bath with a shower and tub. Just down the hall, the guest bedroom offers a queen-over-queen bunk bed and a private bathroom with a shower/tub combo, perfect for families or groups.

Whether you’re planning a family getaway, a relaxing beach escape, or an adventure along 30A, Watercolor Waves offers the perfect home base for unforgettable memories.

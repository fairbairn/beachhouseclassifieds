Welcome to Casa Muditā, a newly built 4-bedroom, 3.5-bath retreat in the heart of Seagrove Beach. Nestled just south of Scenic Highway 30A, this home offers the perfect mix of coastal fun and peaceful lakeside living.

Directly across the street, a family-friendly park features charcoal grills, a fishing dock, and access to Eastern Lake—one of 30A’s most beautiful coastal dune lakes. Guests can kayak, fish, or even paddle board directly from the lake out to the Gulf.

The community pool is just outside the front door along a short walking path, making it easy to cool off after the beach.

Inside, the home blends modern comfort with coastal style. The open-concept main floor boasts a bright kitchen with stainless steel appliances, a large dining area, and a cozy living space perfect for gathering after a day on the water. Upstairs, 4 spacious bedrooms (2 king and 2 queen) offer private and shared baths, plus a balcony with relaxing views.

When you’re ready for sand and surf, the nearest public beach access is just a quick ride away.

Guests also enjoy peaceful walking trails and easy access to 30A’s shops, restaurants, and state parks—all just minutes away.

Whether you’re seeking adventure on the water, family beach days, or evenings exploring 30A, Casa Muditā delivers it all.

About

Milly Mae’s Cottage ~ 214 Dogwood Street ~ Santa Rosa Beach FL 32459

Need a last-minute stay? Please call our office at 844-941-1213 to make last-minute bookings via telephone, as availability within the next 7-nights will not show on calendar. We do not allow online bookings within 7-nights/one week of an arrival date.

30% Discount off Spring Rates! 4-night minimum with flexible arrival/departure days. Valid on new bookings only for stays completed prior to May 22nd. Discount is reflected in online pricing.

20% Discount off Summer 2026! 4-nt minimum with flexible arrival/departure days. Valid on new bookings only for stays completed prior to  August 8th! Discount is reflected in online pricing.

Milly Mae’s Cottage is a refurbished, family-friendly home offering private pool (not heated) and an approximate 7-minute walk from both Seaside and the Dogwood/Thyme Beach Access. With 1,842 interior square feet, Milly Mae’s Cottage sleeps up to 10 and offers 3 bedrooms + bunk nook, 3 full baths, cozy screened porch and parking for 4 cars.  On-street parking is not allowed, so please plan accordingly.

 

Main Level:  3 Bedrooms. 3 Full Baths. Bunk Nook. Fully Equipped Kitchen. Dining Table seats up to 8. Bar Seating for 4. Laundry Closet. Living Room. Screened Porch. Private Pool (not heated). Please note: fireplace is decorative only (not for guest use).

#1 En-Suite Bedroom has 2 bunks (4T), walk-in-shower.

#2 "Bedroom" is the open Bunk Nook in hallway (2T).

#3 Queen Bedroom, private access to hall bath, door to back screened porch, pack-n-play in closet. 

Hall Bath shared by #2 Bunk nook and #3 Bedroom, walk-in-shower.

#4 En-Suite King Bedroom, walk-in shower, tub, door to back screened porch.

 

Please note – no local channels on any Smart TV’s, streaming services available with guest account login. Living room fireplace decorative only (not for guest use).


*Please note there is a new home under construction across the street from Milly Mae's Cottage as of October 2024.

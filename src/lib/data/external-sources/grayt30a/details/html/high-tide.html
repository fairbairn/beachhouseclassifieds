About

 High Tide ~ 46 Gulf Ridge Drive ~ Grayton Beach FL 32459 

Need a last-minute stay? Please call our office at 844-941-1213 to make last-minute bookings via telephone, as availability within the next 7-nights will not show on calendar. We do not allow online bookings within 7-nights/one week of an arrival date.

20% Discount off Summer 2026! 4-nt minimum with flexible arrival/departure days. Valid on new bookings only for stays completed prior to  August 8th! Discount is reflected in online pricing.

High Tide is an outstanding home with Gulf views and private heated pool (heating fee applies), located just 200 feet from the private neighborhood beach access. With 4,778sf of indoor and outdoor porch living (2,932 interior sf), High Tide has 5 bedrooms, 5 baths, and will sleep up to 10 guests.

Enjoy being tucked away from 30A in this private Gulf Trace neighborhood, surrounded by the Grayton Beach State Park (1 mile of pristine beach!) and the Gulf of Mexico; the beaches along this stretch are among the most beautiful and private along 30A. PLEASE NOTE - Golf carts and on-street parking are STRICTLY PROHIBITED in the Gulf Trace neighborhood, per neighborhood covenants.

 

High Tide borders the Grayton Beach State Park, named America’s #1 Beach in 2020 by Dr. Stephen Leatherman, aka Dr. Beach! “This beach boasts of its sugar-white sand and emerald green water where development has been restrained so big sand dunes still dominate the landscape. At the same time, all the amenities of great restaurants and accommodations are close by in the old town of Grayton Beach or Seaside," wrote Leatherman. Originally named the country’s top beach in 1994, Grayton Beach State Park has been listed FOUR times since 1991, finishing 6thin 2016 and 3rd in 2019.

 

Main Floor: 2 Bedrooms. 2 Bathrooms. Living room. Dining table seats 8. Bar seating for 5. Fully equipped kitchen. Laundry room. Screened front porch. Covered dining/grill deck. Open deck. Porch and deck furniture.

#1 En-Suite (Master) Bedroom has a King bed, with walk-in shower, separate tub, 2 vanities (fireplace decorative only).

#2 En-Suite Bedroom has a twin-over-double bunk, with tub/shower.

 

Upper Floor: 3 bedrooms. 3 baths. Secondary living room Covered porch with Gulf views. Porch furniture.

#3 Bedroom has a Queen bed; closet can fit pack-n-play)

Full Bath, with tub/shower

#4 En-Suite Bedroom has a Queen bed, tub/shower, porch entrance; closet can fit pack-n-play.

#5 En-Suite (Master) Bedroom has a King bed, separate tub, walk-in shower, porch entrance; closet can fit pack-n-play.

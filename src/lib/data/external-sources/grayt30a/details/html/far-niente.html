Far Niente ~ 385 Western Lake Drive ~ WaterColor FL 32459

Far Niente is a luxurious 3,242sf home located in the Park District of WaterColor sleeping up to 12 guests, with 5 bedrooms/5.5 baths and 2 living rooms. Bordering the nature preserve along Western Lake, Far Niente offers privacy and views of the lake and nature preserve from expansive porches. Access to WaterColor's community pools and Beach Club; $100 credit toward beach chair/umbrella rentals through WaterColor's Beach Club! Wristbands for access to resort amenities (required for all guests ages 5 years and older) await inside the home for your arrival.

 

Main level:  Open, vaulted entry hallway. Secondary living room, laundry room, 3 en-suite bedrooms, powder room, covered porch.

#1 En-suite Bedroom has a King bed, walk-in shower

#2 En-Suite Bedroom has a Queen bed, walk-in shower

#3 En-Suite Bedroom has 2 sets of bunks (4Twins), walk-in shower

 

Second Level: Main living room, dining table for 8-10, screened porch, fully equipped kitchen, 2 bedrooms, 2 baths, balcony. Please note: fireplace is decorative only.

#4 Bedroom has a Queen bed

Full bath, with walk-in shower

#5 En-Suite Bedroom (Master) has a King bed, separate tub, walk-in shower, walk-in closet

Grayton Pines is a newly renovated, family friendly, post-and-pier home in a prime location next to the Grayton Beach State Park with Gulf and Park views. With ­­­2,928 square feet (1,178 interior), Grayton Pines sleeps up to 8 and offers 3 bedrooms and 2 full baths, parking for 4 cars, and 2/10th’s mile distance to the beach, via the State Park sandy trail. A short beach walk will take you to downtown Grayton icons The Red Bar and The Zoo Gallery. Total interior and exterior professional renovation and design just completed – everything is brand new and absolutely beautiful!!

Historic Grayton Beach is 30A’s original beach community, founded in the mid-1880’s, and has stayed true to its funky, eclectic vibe. With its designated Blue Wave beach, Grayton Beach has been listed in Dr. Steven Leatherman's (“Dr. Beach”) infamous Top 10 Beaches of America FOUR times since 1991 (#1 in 1994, #6 in 2016, #3 in 2019, #1 in 2020), thanks to its pristine coastline and untouched dunes. Grayton Pines – and Grayton Beach itself - has so much to offer, you may never want to venture farther out along 30A!

Please note – beach vendors are not allowed to set up chairs/umbrellas in the State Park. You may want to order the totable chairs/umbrellas through VayK Gear utilizing your Beach Gear credit (you take these back and forth with you).

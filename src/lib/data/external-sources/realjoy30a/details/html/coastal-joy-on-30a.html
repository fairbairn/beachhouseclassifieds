Coastal Joy on 30A is a gorgeous 5BR/4.5BA home in the Highland Parks community of Blue Mountain Beach, just off Hwy 30A. Under 1 mile to the beach, this stunning home features a bright, airy feel with gorgeous hardwood flooring, spacious patio and balcony, 12ft ceilings and custom millwork and cabinetry. When not enjoying the fabulous local restaurants nearby, fire up a home-cooked meal on the Viking gas stove! The main house offers 4 spacious bedrooms and 3.5 baths. Each bedroom features a flat screen TV, and the 2nd floor has new wood floors throughout. Additionally, there is a carriage house studio above the garage with a private entrance, a queen bed, full bathroom, and kitchenette. The spacious driveway and turnaround space allows ample room for everyone. Relax on the shaded porch and balcony in Adirondack chairs or take a short walk over to the pool pavilion for the world-class pool, viewing tower, grilling and entertainment. The new, burgundy 6-Seater Golf Cart is street legal and a huge convenience for this neighborhood. You can head to the beach or grab a treat at the Blue Mountain Beach Creamery! 

Don’t let a wheelchair keep a family member or friend from joining you at the beach. Luxury Coastal Joy on 30A has been updated to be wheelchair accessible. Guests will enjoy easy access to the home and front porch with the newly installed wheelchair accessibility ramp. The first floor bath has been converted to a walk in shower that a wheelchair can easily access. Bring the whole family and make vacation memories of a lifetime at Coastal Joy on 30A!

Highland Parks is the newest neighborhood in Blue Mountain Beach featuring a fantastic neighborhood pool with a waterfall, hot tub, gazebo, fire pit, snack bar, and observation tower. The area is the highest point in South Walton County and surrounded by great eateries and local eclectic shops. Highland Park is perfectly situated between Redfish Village and Gulf Place Town Center on Hwy 30A! The neighborhood is adjacent to Forest Preserve biking and hiking. Enjoy dining at local favorites The Perfect Pig, Red Door, Local Catch, or Basmati's. Check out the local art scene at Justin Gaffery’s art gallery.

Ed Walline Regional Beach Access is ADA accessible, and has two picnic shelters, parking, restrooms, beach wheelchairs and lifeguards during peak season. Located at the intersection of Scenic 393 and Scenic 30A in Santa Rosa Beach, just across the street from Gulf Place rentals, shopping, and dining. It's also a great place to spot surfers and dolphins, with a two-story viewing platform overlooking the beach and gulf.

Area Attractions: 

This is a fantastic location with Gulf Place Town Center only 1.5 miles to the West and Watercolor, Grayton Beach, Seaside, Seacrest and Rosemary Beach just minutes away to the East. Biking is a great activity to enjoy as the scenic bike path meanders along 30A. Bike over to Blue Mountain Beach Creamery for some homemade scoops that will have your taste buds coming back! You'll have an array of dining options to choose from! 

30A is a fantastic destination, the beaches are some of the world’s best with sugar white sand and emerald green waters. You will find plenty of beach accesses along a 17 mile stretch of Santa Rosa Beach. It boasts unique shopping, fantastic cuisine and the incredible architecture that makes 30A famous, there are activities for everyone; kayak rentals, bike rentals, paragliding, fishing, golfing, hiking, swimming, tennis -- you'll find it all right here.

The snorkelers in your group will love the new artificial reef called Sea Turtle Reef, deployed in 2017. Located just 783 feet out from the Grayton Beach State Park access, it is becoming a haven for sea life. Each of the 4 snorkeling reefs comprises 40 acres of permitted sea bottom. The Sea Turtle Reef is at a depth of 12-19 feet. We strongly recommend that snorkelers use a kayak, paddleboard, or other flotation device when visiting the snorkel reefs. Sea conditions can and do change quickly and often. Enjoy!

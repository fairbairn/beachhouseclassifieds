Description
Top Reasons to Book this Beautiful NatureWalk home with access to FREE, PUBLIC BEACHES:

*COMPLIMENTARY: 6-SEATER ELECTRIC GOLF CART ($500 refundable deposit required), 4 BIKES, BEACH UMBRELLA, 6 BEACH CHAIRS, PICKLEBALL RACKETS, 10 SANDLESS BEACH TOWELS, AND PERSONAL CONCIERGE SERVICE.

*Seagrove Beach has the largest stretch of free, public beaches on 30A, accessible less than a mile away by bike via the Lilly Lane Beach Trail or by golf cart/car via 30A

*King bed master en-suite AND living area - on both 1st and 2nd floor

* Next to Watercolor and Seaside communities

* 2 Large Porches for Relaxing and Dining

* Outdoor Shower, Stainless Steel Gas Grill, Second Refrigerator, and Cornhole Boards

* Access to "The Gathering Place" with TWO Pools, Hammocks, Grills, and Fire Pit

* Quiet Neighborhood, with bike and walking trails, and surrounded by Point Washington State Forest

* Walking or Biking Distance to Fabulous Shopping and Dining Options

* Professionally Managed; 24/7 Service

You will fall in love with the beautiful coastal décor that is highlighted by sea life images on canvas, taken by the owners. Octopus's Garden is a large 4-BEDROOM, 3.5-BATH HOME with everything you need for rest and relaxation. With TWO KING MASTER EN-SUITE BEDROOMS (ONE ON EACH FLOOR), QUEEN EN-SUITE BEDROOM, BUNKROOM FULL-OVER-FULL BEDS, LOFT SLEEPER SOFA, and a designated workstation; this home comfortably SLEEPS 12 GUESTS (INCLUDING INFANTS) with plenty of privacy.

The GOURMET KITCHEN is a chef's dream with everything you need, including built-in appliances, and a 5-BURNER GAS STOVE. The OPEN LIVING AREA has gorgeous shiplap walls and boasts ample seating. The living room has a large, HD SMART TV, with WI-FI SOUND-BAR. In addition, there are 4 other HD SMART TVs and WI-FI THROUGHOUT the home. The LOFT WITH TV, GAMES, AND PUZZLES, is ideal FOR KIDS.

Enjoy the sunset from the TWO LARGE PORCHES that feature ceiling fans, swinging benches, and OUTDOOR DINING. The UPPER PORCH IS SCREENED for comfort and safety. There is a large driveway with PARKING FOR 2 VEHICLES. In the garage (no car parking) you will find 4 NEW BIKES so you can explore 30A and the trails. The garage houses the 6-SEATER, ELECTRIC, GOLF CART, a SECOND REFRIGERATOR (perfect for beverages), cornhole boards, 6 BACKPACK BEACH CHAIRS, BEACH UMBRELLA, and a small cooler. Just outside the garage, there is also a stainless-steel GAS GRILL and an OUTDOOR SHOWER for your convenience. For PICKLEBALL LOVERS, our beautiful vacation home PROVIDES 4 RACKETS with 2 BALLS for your enjoyment. The pickleball courts are located at the end of our street.

AMENITIES:
NATUREWALK AMENITIES at the "Gathering Place" include a large ZERO-ENTRY POOL, an additional HEATED LAP POOL, a HOT TUB, lounge chairs, tables and chairs, a SHADED SPLASH POOL for toddlers, HAMMOCKS, outdoor TV LOUNGE, OUTDOOR KITCHEN WITH GAS GRILLS, and GAS FIRE PIT for family enjoyment. NatureWalk is the only community along 30A that offers food trucks at the "Gathering Place" for your family's enjoyment. Additional amenities include 2 PLAYGROUNDS, PICKLEBALL COURTS, WALK / BIKE PATHS, outdoor “Energi” FITNESS STATIONS, and a BASKETBALL COURT.

BEACH ACCESS:
Area ATTRACTIONS: 30A is a fantastic destination, the BEACHES are some of the world’s finest with sugar WHITE SAND and EMERALD-GREEN WATERS. You will find plenty of public beach access along the 19-mile stretch of 30A.

Our SEAGROVE BEACH HAS THE LARGEST STRETCH OF FREE, PUBLIC BEACHES ON 30A, ACCESSIBLE BY BIKE VIA NATUREWALK’S “LILLY LANE TRAIL” (1.5 miles), through the beautiful Point Washington State Forest. NOTE: Golf carts are not allowed on Lilly Lane or any other State Forest trails. Or TAKE THE GOLF CART / CAR (2.8 miles) via 30A to “SAN JUAN” and “SANTA CLARA” BEACH ACCESSES with FREE ENTRY and PARKING.

Another FAVORITE is GRAYTON BEACH STATE PARK, which offers a long stretch of beautiful WHITE SAND BEACH (4.4-mile drive via 30A). Besides the gorgeous beach, this PARK has a LARGE BATHHOUSE, CANOE RENTALS, and NATURE TRAIL. The cost is $5/car but FREE ENTRY in the SUMMER (Memorial Day through Labor Day). Please see the interactive maps here for all the beaches along 30A https://visitsouthwalton.com/beach-bay-access-locations/.

GOLF CART:
Take advantage of the NEW 6-SEATER, ELECTRIC, GOLF CART included with your stay! That is greater than a $100/day savings for you.

LOCATION:
NatureWalk, a quiet family neighborhood surrounded by numerous bike and walking trails, and is convenient to everything 30A has to offer.

NatureWalk is ideally LOCATED off 30A, on Hwy 395 adjacent to WATERCOLOR and SEASIDE. NatureWalk is surrounded by Point Washington State Forest. Explore NatureWalk and the surrounding Watercolor and Seaside by bike or on foot. Rent a KAYAK or PADDLEBOARD and explore Western Lake or the Gulf at GRAYTON BEACH STATE PARK nearby.

PUBLIX in “WATERCOLOR CROSSINGS” is conveniently located NEXT TO NATUREWALK. There you will also find Pizza by the Sea, Boca Boutique, Marble Slab Creamery, Great American Cookie, UPS Store, Emerald Coast Wine and Spirits, Perfect Pig Restaurant, Pedego Electric Bikes, and Yellowfin Sports - to meet all of your needs. The SHOPS and RESTAURANTS of Seaside, Watercolor, and Seagrove Beach are a short bike or golf cart ride away. Don’t miss the funky SHOPS and RESTAURANTS in Old Grayton Beach also on 30A.

COMMUNITY:
This area BOASTS UNIQUE SHOPPING, FANTASTIC CUISINE, AND INCREDIBLE ARCHITECTURE THAT MAKES 30A FAMOUS. There are activities for everyone; KAYAKING, PADDLEBOARDING, BIKING, FISHING, GOLFING, HIKING, SWIMMING, PICKLEBALL, TENNIS, AND AMAZING SUNRISES AND SUNSETS -- YOU FIND IT ALL RIGHT HERE.


Come experience the VACATION OF A LIFETIME AT OCTOPUS'S GARDEN in NatureWalk 30A Resort Community!

Catch the wind and find Blue Mountain Breeze, a charming 4 bedroom, 3 bathroom home that is the perfect place for your next 30A beach getaway! This rental has a king master bedroom with ensuite bathroom and balcony, king guest room, queen guest room, a bunk room with two twin-over-twin bunk beds, a loft area with a twin-over-twin bunk bed, and a sleeper sofa in the family room, sleeping 14 comfortably. Step outside to the patio and enjoy the private, saltwater pool or cook up something delicious on the propane grill. This beautiful home is nestled in the SandCastles By the Sea community in a quiet cul-de-sac; only a short walk to several local dining spots, sugar white sands, and emerald waters. Blue Mountain Breeze will enthrall you with its charm and beauty, and give you a 30A vacation that will make you want to return here year after year.

The Old Blue Mountain Beach Community is located in the heart of Blue Mountain Beach. Tucked in between the tranquil Big Redfish Lake and the emerald waters of the Gulf of Mexico the Old Blue Mountain Beach community is conveniently located. You’ll be within a couple of blocks of Redfish Village with shops and restaurants, bike and surf rentals and other conveniences. Don’t forget to walk over to Blue Mountain Beach Creamery for a homemade scoop! Enjoy the beautiful sugar white sand beaches that made 30A world famous. Explore Redfish Lake, one of the few coastal dune lakes in the world. Come and see for yourself what makes Blue Mountain Beach and the 30A area so special!

Area Attractions:
This is a fantastic location with Gulf Place Town Center only 2 miles to the West and Watercolor, Grayton Beach, Seaside, Seacrest and Rosemary Beach just minutes away to the East. Biking is a great activity to enjoy as the scenic bike path meanders along 30A.  Bike over to Blue Mountain Beach Creamery for some homemade scoops that will have your taste buds coming back!

30A is a fantastic destination, the beaches are some of the world’s best with sugar white sand and emerald green waters. You will find plenty of beach accesses along a 17 mile stretch of Santa Rosa Beach. It boasts unique shopping, fantastic cuisine and the incredible architecture that makes 30A famous, there are activities for everyone; kayak rentals, bike rentals, paragliding, fishing, golfing, hiking, swimming, tennis -- you'll find it all right here.

The snorkelers in your group will love the new artificial reef called Sea Turtle Reef, deployed in 2017. Located just 783 feet out from the Grayton Beach State Park access, it is becoming a haven for sea life. Each of the 4 snorkeling reefs comprises 40 acres of permitted sea bottom. The Sea Turtle Reef is at a depth of 12-19 feet. We strongly recommend that snorkelers use a kayak, paddleboard, or other flotation device when visiting the snorkel reefs. Sea conditions can and do change quickly and often. Enjoy!

Awesome Week at the Beach
We were looking for a large beach house on 30A and decided to try Blue Mountain Beach. The beach lived up to our expectations; gorgeous emerald water and smooth white sand was just a block from the house (a very easy walk). The house itself was just what we were looking for. Comfortable, spacious, and with a 60" television and wireless internet. The kitchen is huge and we cooked almost nightly. A gas grill is also provided. Publix is a five minute drive, and there's a great ice cream place around the corner. There are also a few bars and restaurants across the street. The pool is compact but provided for a change of pace from the beach. All in all, this was a fantastic place to stay. I would recommend it to anyone.

Blue Mountain review
We stayed here with my family, and it was just terrific! I have four kids, 2 boys and two girls, they are very rambunctious young adults and this location was perfect for them! The high ceilings made for great acoustics for guitar playing/singing (house does not come with guitar). A fresh wall paint job made the house very bright inside, even when just using natural daylight. All of the bedrooms and bathrooms were clean and nice. And the porch/patios were a great place to hang out. Two of us actually utilized the structural pillars to string up our hammocks underneath the first floor for a relaxing afternoon nap. The beach is a stones throw away, and the coul-de-sac makes for great games of pass with a football or soccer ball. Frisbee is always best at the beach, assuming you don't throw it into the ocean! I would definitely stay here again down the road. The pool is almost finished and it looks very relaxing. All in all great visit, and very comparable price to other vacation homes in the area for the quality of the stay.

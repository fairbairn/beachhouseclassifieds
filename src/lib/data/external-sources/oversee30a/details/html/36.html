Sunseeker by Oversee 30A | Perfect in Walton County Awards – Three-Time Diamond Winner – Best Rental Mgmt. 2026, 2025, 2024

Tucked among magnolias and pine trees, Sunseeker is a modern plantation-style retreat offering plenty of seclusion. Enjoy the feeling of privacy while still being only steps away from all the 30A action. Stroll along the winding paths to enjoy two pools, a tennis court, and a kids' play area, just steps away from your door.

The first-floor guest bedroom, queen, comes with its own private bath, perfect for guests. Down the hall, the spacious living area invites you in with a chef’s kitchen, dining space for six (plus room for four more at the island), and a cozy living room with plenty of seating for movie nights or catching the big game on a large TV with surround sound.

French doors lead out to a large screened-in porch where you can unwind and enjoy the peaceful, natural surroundings.

Upstairs, the primary suite, king, offers a private bath, TV, and a relaxing balcony.

Another guest bedroom, queen, features a private bath and TV. A cozy second-floor lounge is great for a quiet retreat with a TV and a comfy couch. Need to catch up on work? There’s even a desk for your convenience.

Sunseeker has all the essentials for a perfect 30A getaway. You and your crew will have a blast!

Our dedicated team at Oversee 30A, a local company, is committed to ensuring your stay is nothing short of exceptional. We strive for perfection and are just a stone's throw away should you require any assistance. Reach out to us anytime, seven days a week, with the added peace of a 24/7 emergency line.

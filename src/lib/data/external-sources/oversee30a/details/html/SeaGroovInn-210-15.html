SeaGroovInn by Oversee 30A | Perfect in Walton County Awards – Three-Time Diamond Winner – Best Rental Mgmt. 2026, 2025, 2024

Welcome to SeaGroovInn! When you're not at the beach, you're still on the water! The lakefront sanctuary, south of 30A, rests on the edge of a rare coastal dune lake. Embraced by the live oaks along Eastern Lake, the lush expansive property is in the shade of over 50 trees!

Inside, the slice of paradise is equally enchanting. The coastal-inspired décor, spacious living areas, and fully equipped kitchen is fabulous, no doubt, but don’t be surprised to be lured back outside to the 2000+ square feet of waterfront decking.

The house is handicap accessible (the main floor is on the ground floor), making it easy to get to the water.

A ramp connects the backyard deck to the floating dock, for fishing and kayak access. The views from here are just a reminder of Mother Nature’s artistry.

Have a seat at the water’s edge. Dangle your toes in the water. And start thinking about your paddleboard excursions to the beach. This is where you’ll start to unwind. And slow down a bit. The outdoor oasis entices all to simply relax, as Old Seagrove demands.

Welcome! Our dedicated team at Oversee 30A, a local company, is committed to ensuring your stay is nothing short of exceptional. We strive for perfection and are just a stone's throw away should you require any assistance. Reach out to us anytime, seven days a week, with the added peace of a 24/7 emergency line.

Overview:
- .4 miles to the beach (Eastern Lake Access) In the event of a high outflow at the Eastern Lake access point, please use the Ramsgate access to the west.
- 3 bedrooms, 3.5 baths
- 2260 sq ft
- Propane grill
- Wi-Fi
- 4 complimentary bikes
- One SUP, one large canoe, one 2 man ocean kayak & 6 single kayaks
- Parking for 3 vehicles
- Dogs considered, an additional $210 nonrefundable fee for each dog

Overnight:
- Queen, and bunks with two twins, in 1st-floor guest room; private bath; TV
- King in 2nd floor primary bedroom; private bath with shower; TV
- Queen in 2nd-floor guest room; shared bath; TV
- Twin trundle (connected to queen guest room/sliding door); shared bath

Check-in is 4:00 PM CST. Check-out is 10:00 AM CST.

The outdoor space for this rental is incredible. Gorgeous views, beautiful live oaks, expansive decks with lots of seating arrangements, a bed swing, relaxing wind chime and a dock for fishing, SUPing and kayaks. It’s a close walk to the beach – note that this beach access requires you to wade a bit to get to it! The bikes that come with the house are a little rusty. We rented golf carts but honestly I wouldn’t recommend it, bike rentals would be a better investment since golf carts aren’t welcome in a lot of the communities (and the batteries run down quickly). Back to the house, our only complaint was the first floor bedroom was small and not as nice as the upstairs rooms, and there were some temperature control issues where some rooms were hot and others were freezing. Otherwise the kitchen is well stocked, there are plenty of bathrooms, and everything is super comfy. The location is great, we could hear the music from the Old Florida Fish House from our deck!

Three generations celebrated a vacation during this pandemic, and there were activities and relaxation for all. At this lake house off a beach bordering the gulf, we were able to kayak, canoe,pandleboard,fish,crab,bike,walk and enjoy the outdoors as well as shop in the villages along 30A and enjoy the various eating spots. We enjoyed a different stay in a quieter area on a lake. The landscaping of this property provided a woodsy feel. There is a huge deck with native trees poking out of the decking providing shade. There were several seating areas, a large round teak table and chairs for at least 8-10 people, another seating area with sofa and chairs overlooking the lake, and a covered porch with a swinging bed to relax in while still able to watch the goings on at the water’s edge. My grandchildren loved the canoe, kayaks and paddleboard, being able to paddle to the sandy beach and cross over it to another beach lining the gulf. They could canoe beach chairs,towels, umbrellas and rafts. Other times my son in law used his truck to bring the beach items to the beach. We could walk about a quarter mile to the beach too. Next time hopefully the house has a motorized golf cart to use. The house was clean! Newly renovated. The great room,kitchen, three bedrooms and 3 1/2 baths easily handled a crowd of 12. I would say that the kitchen needs more cooking utensils for cooking. It has a great ice maker.We enjoyed biking to the villages to shop and explore. A great place with added dimensions by having the lake so near the beach and gulf.

Beach Heaven by Oversee 30A | Perfect in Walton County Awards – Three-Time Diamond Winner – Best Rental Mgmt. 2026, 2025, 2024

Your family will love this newly built, four-bedroom beach house. Spacious and beautiful, Beach Heaven is in the incredible Highland Parks neighborhood of Blue Mountain Beach, on the quieter west side of 30A.

Highland Park’s large lagoon pool is wonderful and includes a community grilling area, fire pit, and is only a few steps from Beach Heaven.

Highlands Parks is also next to numerous hiking and biking trails in the 15,000-acre Point Washington State Forest, which can be accessed via a nearby entrance to the trails, only .2 miles from the house. The beach is 5 minutes by car, 10 minutes by bike (four premium bikes are included) or about 15 minutes on foot.

Inside, a massive family room opens to a fully equipped kitchen, and a guest room, king, with a full bath. A second living area and three bedrooms are on the second floor. A primary bedroom, king, has a seating area and en-suite bathroom, with a spacious shower.

A guest room, queen, is down the hall, as is a queen bed and the kids’ favorite, a bunk room with two sets of twin bunks, sleep four. The house has ample parking for 3-4 cars, or 3 cars and a golf cart.

Welcome! Our dedicated team at Oversee 30A, a local company, is committed to ensuring your stay is nothing short of exceptional. We strive for perfection and are just a stone's throw away should you require any assistance. Reach out to us anytime, seven days a week, with the added peace of a 24/7 emergency line.

Overview:
- 1.1 miles to the Blue Mountain Regional Public Beach Access
- Across the street from a large resort-style pool (seasonally heated) and cabana
- 4 bedroom, 3 bathroom
- 2,292 square feet
- Wi-Fi
- New Royal EV six-seater golf cart
- 4 premium Heron Bicycle Company adult bikes included
- Parking for 3-4 vehicles
- Entrance to hiking and biking trails in state forest .2 miles away
- Community fire pit, hot tub, and grills

Overnight:
- King in 1st floor guest room; shared hall bathroom
- King in 2nd floor primary bedroom; en suite bathroom
- Queen in 2nd floor guest bedroom; shared Jack and Jill bathroom
- Two twin over twin bunks (4 beds) in 2nd-floor guest bedroom; shared Jack and Jill bathroom

Check-in is 4:00 PM CST. Checkout is 10:00 AM CST

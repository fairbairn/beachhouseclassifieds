Two Rocking Chairs by Oversee 30A | Perfect in Walton County Awards – Three-Time Diamond Winner – Best Rental Mgmt. 2026, 2025, 2024

Welcome to Two Rocking Chairs, in the 256-acre, gated community of Watersound West Beach. Resting between Seaside and Rosemary Beach, the gorgeous retreat comfortably accommodates 10.

Families will love the amazing amenities at WaterSound West! A seasonally heated, zero-entry pool features cabanas, outdoor grills, and a fireplace.

Go exploring on boardwalks and nature trails that wind through the community, over the large pond and rolling dunes, and directly to the beach. Absolutely gorgeous, a must do!

With Horseshoe Park and Deer Lake State Park nearby, a 9-hole putt-putt course, and bike and kayak rentals, WaterSound West promises fun for everyone during your peaceful beach vacation. Oh yeah: Complimentary golf cart!

Inside, the large open living, dining, and kitchen area is a charming space, and leads out to the adorable, screened-in front porch.

Nearby, a primary bedroom, king, has a private bath with a double vanity and walk-in shower. The second floor has another primary bedroom, king, with private bath, a guest room, king, with private bath, and a two-twin bunk-nook. For additional sleeping, the living room has a full sleeper sofa.

Out back, there’s a gas grill and an outdoor shower. Beach access is just a short walk or bike ride away.

Welcome! Our dedicated team at Oversee 30A, a local company, is committed to ensuring your stay is nothing short of exceptional. We strive for perfection and are just a stone's throw away should you require any assistance. Reach out to us anytime, seven days a week, with the added peace of a 24/7 emergency line.

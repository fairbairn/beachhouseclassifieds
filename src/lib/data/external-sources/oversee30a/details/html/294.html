Sea Stars by Oversee 30A | Perfect in Walton County Awards – Three-Time Diamond Winner – Best Rental Mgmt. 2026, 2025, 2024

Resting on a prime spot between Camp Creek Lake and Alys Beach, Sea Stars is all about easygoing luxury. Your crew will love the private deeded beach access.

The living and kitchen area is bright, inviting, and ideal for unwinding after a day in the sun. With seating for seven at the breakfast bar and dining table, it’s great for morning coffee, post-beach snacks, and everything in between.

But the real highlight of Sea Stars? Each bedroom has its own private balcony or porch, so there’s always a spot to soak up the fresh air!

The first-floor guest room, with king bed, private bath, and custom-tiled walk-in shower, opens onto a unique and cozy screened-in porch.

With comfy seating, this is a delightful space, ideal for kicking back with a good book or an afternoon nap.

Upstairs, two more bedrooms offer room to recharge. The primary suite? King bed, private balcony, and an en-suite bath with a soaking tub that’s made for relaxation. The guest room? A king bed, private bath, a shower/tub combo, and of course, a private balcony.

Three adult bikes are included, making it easy to explore 30A at your own pace. With a fantastic location and thoughtful extras, Sea Stars is the kind of place that keeps guests coming back.

At Oversee 30A, we take care of the details so you can focus on the fun. Our local team is available seven days a week, with a 24/7 emergency line—just in case.

Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Cancellations or changes that result in a shortened stay made within 30 days of the arrival date forfeit the full reservation amount.

Beach Walk Inn Features
- 175 yards to the Spooky Lane Beach Access. Straight path all the way to the sand
- New Pool Deck added for 2025
- 2 Min bike ride to Gulf Place with many shopping and dining options, including award winning Shunk Gulley
- 1 Min drive to Ed Walline Beach Access with parking, wheelchair accessible parking and beach access, and public restrooms
- Near Topsail State Park, a birdwatching and hiking paradise
- Gulf view from 3rd floor furnished balcony and the King Suite
- Private pool (10' x 20'), which can be heated for an additional fee for reservations October – April
- Access to a 6-seater LSV (Golf Cart). Must sign a waiver and be at least 21 years old licensed/insured driver
- Access to 4 Taxi bikes
- Fenced-in sundeck with lounge seating and outdoor dining for 4
- WIFI throughout home with streaming TV available. Sign into your favorite services for movie night! Live TV available through YouTube TV
- Full kitchen with standalone ice maker, pantry, and high-end appliances
- 2nd Floor Dining area with table for 6 plus breakfast bar seating for 3
- 2nd Floor Main Living area with Smart TV and sound bar
- 2nd Floor covered balcony with cushioned seating
- Outdoor shower
- Propane Outdoor Grill
- 3rd Floor Laundry room
- Driveway parking for 4-5 vehicles
- Concierge services including grocery delivery, baby equipment rentals, additional bike rentals, and beach chair setup available
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee

Welcome to your perfect coastal retreat in the heart of Dune Allen! This spacious 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home offers the ideal blend of comfort, style, and convenience, making it the ultimate getaway for families or groups looking to experience the best of the Florida Gulf Coast. Beach Walk Inn blends designer coastal inspired interiors with upscale amenities, including a private heated pool and golf cart, for a luxury vacation experience

The beach access is only steps away at the Spooky Lane Beach Access. Beach Walk Inn is also a short bike ride to the Ed Walline Regional Beach access, Gulf Place and all the shops, art galleries, world class restaurants and art and music festivals that 30A has to offer. Shunk Gulley, voted one of the Top 100 restaurants in the US by Yelp, is a 2-minute bike ride away. And don't forget about Growler Garage, with over 40 beers on tap. Only a 5-minute stroll from the home, and perfect for listening to live music at Happy Hour.

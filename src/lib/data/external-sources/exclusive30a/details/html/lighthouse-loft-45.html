Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. WaterColor - Park District

2 Baths

Lighthouse Loft Features
- Pet Friendly for up to 2 dogs, 60 pounds or less, and non-aggressive breeds only. Approval from Exclusive 30A and payment of a non-refundable pet fee is required prior to your arrival. Unauthorized pets will result in fines and possible eviction.
- Parking for 1 vehicle in parking lot behind building. Parking is first come, first serve in the building lot.
- Access to 2 Taxi Bikes, serviced regularly to keep them in top shape for your adventures
- Condo is all one level - No Stairs! Elevator access to 3rd floor unit available
- Condo overlooks Cerulean Park, with lots of greenspace for your kids or pup to run around.
- Laundry closet with washer and dryer
- WIFI and Cable
- Smart TV in main living room
- Full kitchen well-stocked for meal prep. Set up grocery delivery for your arrival!
- Elevator and stair access to 3rd floor keyless entrance
- Dining for 6 plus outdoor dining area on covered patio
- Screened-in porch with cushioned lounger plus balcony overlooking Cerulean Park
- Private balcony off king suite
- Next to dining and shopping including Wine World, Scratch Biscuit Kitchen, and Beach Happy Cafe
- Building is 100 yards to WaterColor Beach Club and adjacent to Cerulean Park
- Beach Towels available at the WaterColor Beach Club and Camp WaterColor (subject to change by the neighborhood)
- Access to the WaterColor Beach Club, Camp WaterColor and all community pools included for 4 guest age 5 and up. Additional wristbands are not available per the HOA.
- Community pools heated during cooler months (subject to change by the neighborhood)
- Beach service available at the WaterColor Beach Club, and advanced reservations recommended
- LSV (golf cart) rentals at this condo are restricted as there are no charging stations at the building
- Concierge services available, including grocery delivery, baby equipment rentals, and more
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee

Relax in this professionally decorated, open condo in the heart of Watercolor. This ultra chic condo is the best of the best, with luxurious touches, plush bedding, and stunning views. With an open floor plan and ample outdoor living spaces, this home is spacious and comfortable for up to 4 people. Enjoy the view of Cerulean Park from either the screened in or covered porch. The King suite includes its own balcony for added privacy.

The luxe king suite has lots of natural light plus curtains for more restful moments. The private ensuite bath includes a dual vanity and combination shower with garden tub. Additional sleeping is available on a queen bed in a private room off the main living area and features a custom platform bed with built in storage, private balcony access, and plush cotton linens. An additional Full bath with a walk-in shower and hallway access is available nearby.

***Pets are welcome with prior approval by Exclusive 30A. Please request your pet’s stay at time of reservation. Payment of our non-refundable pet fee must be paid at time of approval.***

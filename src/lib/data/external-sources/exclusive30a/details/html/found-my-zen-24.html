Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Cancellations or changes that result in a shortened stay made within 30 days of the arrival date forfeit the full reservation amount.

Found My Zen
Chic Rosemary Beach Retreat Steps from Pools, Dining, and Beach Access

Home Introduction
Unwind in style at Found My Zen, a beautifully appointed 3-suite retreat nestled along a quiet Rosemary Beach boardwalk. With designer interiors, multiple private outdoor spaces, and access to premier community amenities, this home offers the perfect blend of relaxation and convenience just minutes from the beach.

Home Highlights

  • Beach Access — 2-minute bike ride to Walkover C Beach Entrance
  • Access to all Rosemary Beach pools, including the Sky Pool
  • Enjoy 4 Taxi Bikes
  • Pet Friendly — One dog under 30 pounds, non-shedding, approval, and fee required
  • Gourmet Kitchen — High-end stainless-steel appliances
  • Outdoor Living — Covered balcony with cushioned swing and seating
  • Private Courtyard — Cushioned seating and outdoor grill
  • Semi-Private Outdoor Shower
  • Sleeping for 8 Guests — 2 king suites plus bunk suite
  • All Bedrooms Ensuite — Renovated full baths for each bedroom
  • 1st Floor Living Room — Floor to ceiling curtains for privacy
  • Dining Seating — Table for 6 plus breakfast bar for 4
  • Electric Vehicle Charger — Tesla adapter included
  • Full Size Washer and Dryer
  • Parking for 2 Vehicles — Driveway and parallel parking
  • Cable and WiFi Throughout Home
  • Covered Front Porch — Rocking chairs overlooking the boardwalk
  • Concierge Services Available — Bikes, baby gear, private chef, grocery delivery
  • Beach Chair Service Available at all Rosemary walkovers

Location Perks

  • 1 minute walk to Sky Pool
  • 1 minute walk to Rosemary Beach Racquet Club
  • 2-minute bike ride to Walkover C Beach Access
  • 1 minute bike ride to Rosemary Beach dining and shopping

Know Before You Go

  • Golf carts are not permitted in Rosemary Beach per HOA regulations
  • A mid-stay clean is required for reservations of 10 days or longer and is included in your stay
  • Additional cleans may be scheduled for an extra fee
  • Pet approval is required prior to arrival; unauthorized pets will result in fines or possible eviction
  • Construction may be present in the surrounding area for arrivals January through March 2026; no refunds or discounts will be given for related disturbances

***Pets are welcome with prior approval by Exclusive 30A. Please request your pet’s stay at time of reservation. Payment of our non-refundable pet fee must be paid at time of approval.***

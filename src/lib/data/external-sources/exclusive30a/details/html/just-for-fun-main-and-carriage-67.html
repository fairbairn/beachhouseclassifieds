Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Cancellations or changes that result in a shortened stay made within 30 days of the arrival date forfeit the full reservation amount.

Just for Fun Main and Carriage

Seaside Proper - Walk to Beach

4 Bedrooms
5.5 Baths
Sleeps 13
2434 SQ FEET

Video Tour

This beautiful corner unit home is the best of location and design. When you step inside, you are reminded of the sleek styling of a teak-trimmed sailboat. The second floor is open and airy, with brilliant natural light bathing the main living area and tall ceilings. All new plush furniture decorates the living room along with a large Smart TV and Sonos sound system.

The home is recently updated, including all new bathrooms, an updated kitchen with white cabinetry and quartz countertops, and a new laundry room and powder room on the 2nd floor of the main house.

The incredible private outdoor spaces include a rooftop sundeck and covered watchtower that offer 360-degree views of Seaside and the Gulf! You can feel the Gulf breeze as you rest on the bench or lather on the sunscreen to relax on the sundeck.

This listing includes the well-appointed Carriage House, which offers all the same amenities of a larger Seaside home - two full baths, a full kitchen, in unit washer and dryer, living room, dining area, and outdoor dining on a private porch. You won't find anything else like this in Seaside!

Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. Jewel Box at Watersound

WaterSound - Community Pool

2 Bedrooms
2 Baths
Sleeps 5
1434 SQ FEET

Video Tour

Welcome to our Jewel Box, in the beachfront Compass Point building within the gated community of Watersound! Compass Point is the closest beachfront building in the main Watersound neighborhood. Incredible Gulf and Dune views plus beautiful interiors combine for a relaxing and inspiring vacation experience. 

Watersound is a walkers’ paradise with miles of trails within the neighborhood and adjoining undeveloped Deer Lake State Park. The gated Watersound community limits access to guests staying in the neighborhood, making it perfect for quiet, peaceful vacations along 30A. There are many parks and nooks throughout the neighborhood to enjoy the amazing 30A weather year-round. 

To further elevate the luxurious Watersound experience, the building’s exterior will undergo updates beginning in November 2025. During certain phases of the project, temporary scaffolding may be present around sections of the building. Exterior construction outside this unit is anticipated to be completed by Summer 2026. All community amenities are expected to remain open and fully accessible throughout the renovation period. 

Jewel Box at Watersound Features 
- Top Floor Unit with vaulted ceiling overlooking pristine, secluded beaches and dunes 
- Condo is all one level! Elevators and stairs available to the unit 4th floor location 
- Footsteps to the beach boardwalks. Walk from the building to the sand in 3 minutes 
- Flexible Summer Arrivals available! 
- Covered balcony with outdoor dining and sunset views over the Gulf 
- Adjacent to Dunesider community pool, which is heated during cooler months (subject to availability by the HOA) 
- Enjoy 2 Taxi bikes, serviced regularly to keep them in top shape 
- Two King Suites with Gulf Views 
- Full kitchen with stainless steel appliances including a Cuisinart Brew Station with drip and k cup coffee maker 
- Dining table plus breakfast bar seating for 6 
- Smart TVs throughout condo 
- Reclining chairs in the living room - they can be swiveled to face the Smart TV or face the Gulf 
- Built-in desk with seating in case you need to get some work done before heading to the beach 
- Cable and WIFI available 
- Garage parking for 1 vehicle. Additional parking for 1 vehicle available on a first come, first serve basis in surrounding parking lot. RVs, trailers, and street parking restricted per the HOA 
- Access to private Compass Point Pool and the community Dunesider Pool (located in front of the condo) 
- Community outdoor kitchen with built in gas grills and outdoor dining area 
- In-unit laundry room with Washer and Dryer 
- Beach Chair Rentals available through La Dolce Vita with reservations recommended. All you need is your book and your sunscreen. 
- Luggage carts available near the elevator in the parking garage 
- On-site EV charger from Chargepoint 
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee 
- Concierge services available, including grocery delivery, boat charters, and catered beach bonfires 
- Golf cart rentals in Watersound are restricted. Medical exceptions may be provided by the HOA on a case-by-case basis

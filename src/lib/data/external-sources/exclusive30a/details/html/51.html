Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. Home Introduction

Escape to Grove Getaway, a beautifully updated 3-bedroom, 2-bath condo in Seagrove Beach offering the perfect blend of coastal living and modern convenience. Just a 2-minute bike ride from the pristine Beachwood Villas and Santa Clara Beach Access, this one-level retreat features serene lake views, a screened balcony, and access to a peaceful community pool.

Home Highlights

  • Beach Proximity — 2-minute bike ride to Beachwood Villas Public Beach Access

  • Flexible Stays — Available year-round

  • One-Level Living — Elevator and stair access to 2nd-floor condo

  • 4 Taxi Bikes — the preferred vehicle for exploring 30A

  • Outdoor Living — Screened-in balcony with cushioned seating, ceiling fans, and dining for 4

  • Lake View — Enjoy peaceful water views from the balcony

  • Beach Gear — Complimentary use of a beach wagon, small cooler, and towels

  • Community Pool — Overlooking large pond with grill, dining, and lounge seating

  • Secure Access — Keyless entry for added safety

  • Prime Location — 2-minute bike ride to Café 30A and Angelina’s Pizzeria

  • Full Kitchen — Equipped with drip and Keurig coffee makers

  • Dining Options — Table seating for 4 plus breakfast bar for 2

  • Entertainment — Flat-screen TVs, cable, and Wi-Fi

  • Laundry — Stacked washer/dryer in-unit

  • Parking — Available for 2 vehicles, first-come, first-served

Location Perks

  • 2-min bike ride to Beachwood Villas Public Beach Access

  • 2-min ride to Santa Clara Regional Beach Access (with beach service)

  • 2-min bike ride to Café 30A and Angelina’s Pizzeria

  • Close to 30A dining, shopping, and entertainment

Know Before You Go

  • A mid-stay clean is required for reservations 10+ days (included in rate)

  • Additional cleans available for an added fee

  • Concierge services available, including grocery delivery, baby equipment rentals, and additional bike rentals

  • Parking is first come, first served (2 spaces)

  • Community management does not allow pets

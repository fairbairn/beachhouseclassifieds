Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Cancellations or changes that result in a shortened stay made within 30 days of the arrival date forfeit the full reservation amount.

Rosie Disposition

Seacrest - Centrally Located

4 Bedrooms
4.5 Baths
Sleeps 16
2163 SQ FEET

Rosie Disposition features
- Driveway parking for 2 cars (street parking, RV, and trailers not allowed)
- Pet Friendly for up to 2 dogs, 60 pounds or less, and non-aggressive breeds only. Approval from Exclusive 30A and payment of a non-refundable pet fee is required prior to your arrival.
- Access to 5 Taxi Bikes, serviced regularly to keep them in top shape 
- Tram Service to Seacrest Beach from 10am - 5pm, March 1 - October 31, 2026
- 5-minute drive to Inlet Beach public beach access with parking and restrooms. This is our recommended beach entrance and beach service is available at this location.
- 4-minute drive to the largest Publix in America. Curbside pick-up available
- 3-minute bike ride to Alys beach, with upscale shopping and dining plus deluxe playground for kids
- 4-minute bike ride to Rosemary Beach with even more shopping and dining options. We recommend you tour the neighborhood to see the unique architecture and beautiful vistas
- Spacious main living area with wood beam ceiling and plush sectional sofa
- Full gourmet kitchen with stainless steel KitchenAid appliances, pantry, and polished countertops
- Dining area with seating for 6 plus breakfast bar seating for 4
- Covered front porch with comfortable seating
- 2nd floor laundry center with HE washer and dryer
- WIFI and cable plus large Smart TV in the living room
- Incredible bunk room with sleeping for up to 8 in custom bunks
- Outdoor seating on front porch and back deck
- Outdoor shower
- Outdoor gas grill
- Designer Interiors throughout the home
- Community pool across the street - only shared with a few homes (pool is not heated)
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee
- Concierge services available including grocery delivery, additional bike rentals, a catered beach bonfire, baby equipment, and more
- Golf cart rentals are not recommended as they are not allowed in Seacrest Beach, Alys Beach, Rosemary Beach, or along Hwy 98.

Beautiful designer home in the gated neighborhood of Treetops of Seacrest. Located between Hwy 98 and Hwy 30, this community features lush, landscaped spaces, a community pool with lounge seating and bath house, plus gated entrances. Be close to Rosemary Beach, Alys Beach, and Seacrest with added privacy and solitude at an incredible price.

Don't forget to include your pup in your vacation plans. Rosie Disposition is pet friendly! Guests must pay a non-refundable pet fee and receive written approval from Exclusive 30A to bring your dog, but once that is completed the entire family will love enjoying 30A together.

***Pets are welcome with prior approval by Exclusive 30A. Please request your pet’s stay at time of reservation. Payment of our non-refundable pet fee must be paid at time of approval.***

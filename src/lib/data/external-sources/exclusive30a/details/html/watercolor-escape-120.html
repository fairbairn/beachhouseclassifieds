Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Cancellations or changes that result in a shortened stay made within 30 days of the arrival date forfeit the full reservation amount.

WaterColor Escape Features
- Private Pool and Spa. Can be heated for an additional fee for reservations October - April
- Complimentary access to a 6-Seater LSV (Golf Cart)- some restrictions apply and must sign waiver to use cart during your stay 
- Pet Friendly for up to 2 dogs, 60 pounds or less, and non-aggressive breeds only. Pets not allowed in the private pool. Approval from Exclusive 30A and payment of a non-refundable pet fee is required prior to your arrival.
- Access to 5 Taxi Bikes, serviced regularly to keep them in top shape
- 2 Min bike ride or LSV (golf cart) drive to Camp WaterColor. Access included with your reservation
- 2 Min bike ride to WaterColor Publix, Pizza by the Sea, The Perfect Pig, and Driftwood Wine and Spirits
- Fenced-in backyard oasis with sundeck, outdoor dining, built-in grill, fireplace, and cushioned loungers
- Built-in gas grill poolside
- Lots of covered porch and balcony space with seating, with three overlooking the pool area
- Gourmet kitchen with stainless steel top-of-the-line appliances including double ovens, double dishwashers, and built-in cappuccino machine
- 1st Floor Main living area with large Smart TV and sound-bar
- Dining area with seating for 10 plus breakfast bar seating for 5
- Queen Suite on the 1st floor is perfect if you are traveling with the grandparents
- Cable and WIFI throughout the home
- Office area on 2nd floor with built in desk and chair. Perfect for remote working!
- Driveway parking for 4 sedans
- Washer and Dryer in 1st floor laundry room
- Access to the WaterColor Beach Club, Camp WaterColor, and neighborhood pools included with your reservation
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 14 wristbands.
- Concierge services available including grocery delivery, private chef, baby equipment rentals, beach service, and more
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee

Incredible WaterColor vacation home! Located a quick 2-minute LSV (golf cart) or bike ride to Camp WaterColor and bordered by the famous WaterColor walking trails, your family will love this serene location. The extras available at the home are unparalleled! A private pool, outdoor lounge, bikes, designer interiors, an LSV (golf cart), and more create a vacation package that you will never want to leave.

The 3,953 square foot floor plan is spacious and well appointed. With all suite bedrooms and lots of private outdoor space, everyone will enjoy relaxing together in style. A luxurious guest suite is available on the first floor and is perfect for the grandparents. The top floor features a unique guest suite with sleeping for up to 5 thanks to a king bedroom plus adjoining alcove with twin daybed and queen bed. This suite is perfect if you are traveling with little ones you want to keep close while still enjoying a little privacy.

***Pets are welcome with prior approval by Exclusive 30A. Please request your pet’s stay at time of reservation. Payment of our non-refundable pet fee must be paid at time of approval.***

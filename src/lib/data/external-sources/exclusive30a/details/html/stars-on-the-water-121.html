Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Stars on the Water

Seagrove - Walk to Beach

5 Bedrooms
6.5 Baths
Sleeps 14
4693 SQ FEET

Stars on the Water features:
- Panoramic Gulf and Lake Views from the top floor balcony. The balcony also features a sitting area and outdoor dining table
- 400 feet to the Eastern Lake Beach Access and 1 Min bike ride to the Ramsgate Beach Access
- Large private pool with sun shelf (approx. 17’x15’). The pool can be heated for an additional fee for reservations October – April
- Fenced in pool deck with summer kitchen with natural gas grill, ice maker, cushioned seating area, poolside lounge chairs, and bench swing.  It’s like staying in a private resort!
- Access to a 6-seater LSV (golf cart) to explore the neighborhood and 30A. Some restrictions apply and must sign a waiver for to use the cart during your stay.
- Access to 5 Taxi Bikes, serviced regularly to keep them in top shape
- Living room with Smart TVs on ALL levels. Spread out or gather – you have so many options.
- 3rd Floor Main living room, Kitchen, and Dining Area with Gulf Views
- Gourmet kitchen with high-end stainless-steel appliances including 3 ovens, microwave, Keurig Duo coffee maker (drip and k-cup) and an ice maker. The kitchen is well stocked for your meal prep needs
- Nearby wet bar with deluxe wine and beverage fridge
- Dining area with seating for 8 plus breakfast bar seating for 4
- All bedrooms ensuite
- Private elevator which travels to all floors. Adult use only. Please ask for details regarding mobility considerations for anyone in your group.
- Cable and WIFI available with Smart TVs throughout the home
- Sonos Sound System available
- Driveway parking for 4 vehicles
- 1st and 2nd floor laundry room
- Port-a-crib and highchair available
- Outdoor shower
- Concierge services available including additional baby equipment rentals, boat charts, grocery delivery and more
- 6 Beach chairs and 2 umbrellas available 
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee

Welcome to your luxurious retreat nestled amidst the serene beauty of the Gulf and a tranquil lake. This stunning new, custom-built property boasts five exquisitely designed suites and separate indoor and outdoor living areas on each of 3 floors, offering unparalleled comfort and privacy for you and your guests. From the top floor, you're greeted by the panoramic vistas of the Gulf and the shimmering waters of Eastern Lake, creating a picturesque backdrop for your stay. Whether you're lounging by the private pool, strolling to the nearby beach, or exploring the surrounding area on the bicycles provided, every moment promises to be filled with relaxation and adventure.

Inside, the ambiance is equally captivating. The spacious great room, dining room and gourmet kitchen create an alluring space to gather your group or family/friends for meals and relaxation before taking drinks to the patio to enjoy the amazing view and gorgeous colors at sunset. And on those special moonlit nights, you will experience the "Stars on the Water". With multiple living rooms thoughtfully designed for comfort and entertainment, there's ample space for everyone to unwind and reconnect.

Indulge in the ultimate luxury experience at this exclusive retreat, where every detail has been carefully curated to ensure your stay exceeds expectations and leaves you longing to return.

The 30A area is continually growing and evolving. As a result, nearby construction, renovations, or public works projects may occur during your stay. While we strive to provide an exceptional experience, these activities are beyond our control. Refunds or discounts cannot be offered for construction-related noise, disruptions, or changes in views.

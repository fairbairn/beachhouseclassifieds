Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. Just for Fun Main House

Seaside Proper - Gulf View

3 Bedrooms
3.5 Baths
Sleeps 8
1984 SQ FEET

Video Tour

You visit Seaside "Just For Fun" but when you stay at our newly renovated home, you will fall in love with all 30A has to offer. This beautiful corner unit home is the best of location and design. Very quick 2 min walk to Odessa Beach Access and across the street from the Westside Pool. 

Renovated in 2024 to include an updated kitchen, new laundry room, new powder room, and updated bathrooms.  This home is a stunning example of mixing retro touches with glamorous style. 

When you step into the home, you're reminded of the sleek styling of a teak trimmed sailboat. The 2nd floor is open and airy, with brilliant natural light bathing the main living area and tall ceilings adding to the expansive feel. Plush furniture decorates the living room including large Smart TV with Sonos. The covered balcony is a calming retreat when you want to enjoy an afternoon drink or relax with a book. A full kitchen with stainless steel appliances + dining for 6 completes the main living area.

The incredible private outdoor spaces continue to a rooftop sundeck covered watchtower featuring 360 degree views of Seaside and the Gulf! Feel the Gulf breeze as you rest on the bench or lather up your sunscreen to relax on the sundeck.

A family vacation isn't complete w/out your beloved pup. Just For Fun is pet friendly for non-shedding dogs 45 pounds or lighter and payment of a non-refundable pet fee. (Pet must be approved prior to arrival by Exclusive 30A. Bringing an unauthorized pet will result in fines and possible eviction without refund.)

Want some extra space? Ask about adding the separate carriage house apartment with sleeping for 6 to your reservation. The main house and carriage house are rented separately, and there is a high likelihood another guest with a pet will be in the carriage house during your stay. The carriage house has a private entrance across the courtyard from the main house.

** Please note the video tour of the home does feature both the main house and carriage house. Not all areas featured in the video will be available with your reservation. Please refer to the property description to verify what is included with your stay.**

Step into a timeless coastal retreat where vintage charm meets relaxed sophistication. Perfectly perched in the heart of Seaside, Beluga offers an unparalleled location—just 20 yards from the iconic Ruskin Street Beach Pavilion—and sweeping Gulf views from its 2nd and 3rd floor balconies. Here, every detail is designed for effortless living by the shore.

Inside, warm wood tones, curated coastal décor, and soft, sun-washed textures create a sense of nostalgic elegance. The showpiece Primary King Suite spans the 2nd floor with two private ensuite baths (no sharing required), a spacious Gulf-facing balcony, and a charming daybed swing to relax away the afternoons.

Gather on the 3rd floor where the gourmet Viking kitchen, dining area, and airy main living room open onto a grand Gulf-view balcony—perfect for sunset dinners or lingering with morning coffee. A private elevator (adult use only) connects all levels, making access easy for everyone.

Five beautifully appointed suites—three with king beds and two bunk suites—offer space for up to 18 guests, blending comfort with coastal character. After a sun-kissed day, enjoy the Seaside lifestyle at your doorstep: stroll to Bud & Alley’s in a minute, browse boutique shops, or take in a concert on the green.

Beluga includes exclusive access to Seaside’s private beach gates, community pools (heated seasonally), and fitness center. Five vintage-style taxi bikes are ready for a breezy ride through town, and concierge services are available to add even more ease to your stay.

This is Seaside living at its most iconic—where Gulf breezes mingle with timeless style, and every day feels like it belongs to another, slower era.

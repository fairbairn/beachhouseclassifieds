Home Introduction
Unwind in style at Lake-Front Serenity, a beautifully appointed 5BR/4.5BA retreat nestled in the heart of WaterColor. With serene water views, expansive wraparound porches, a 6-seater LSV, and 4 Taxi bikes, this elegant coastal home offers the perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and convenience—just minutes from Camp WaterColor and the beach.

Home Highlights

  • 6-Seater LSV (Golf Cart) — Complimentary Access included with stay (waiver required; restrictions apply)
  • 4 Taxi Bikes — Serviced regularly by La Dolce Vita
  • Water Views from Multiple Rooms and Outdoor Spaces
  • Wraparound Front Porch + Wraparound Balcony with Lounge Areas and Ceiling Fans
  • Gas Grill + Outdoor Dining for 10
  • 3rd Floor Loft — Foosball table, seating areas, and TV (accessed via spiral staircase)
  • Gourmet Kitchen — Stainless appliances, Keurig + drip coffee makers, wet bar and standalone ice maker
  • 2nd Floor Main Living Area — Fireplace, sound bar, Smart TV with streaming and pond views
  • Dining for 14 — Table seating for 8 + breakfast bar for 6
  • Office Nook — 1st floor workspace ideal for remote work or school
  • Sleeping for 12 Guests — 2 King Suites + 2 Queen Suites + Bunk Room
  • HE Washer and Dryer + Mini Fridge (1st Floor)
  • Outdoor Shower — Hot and cold water
  • Driveway Parking for 2 Vehicles + Garage Parking for LSV (no street parking)
  • Access to WaterColor Beach Club, Camp WaterColor and Community Pools (for 12 guests ages 5+)

Location Perks

  • 1-Minute Bike Ride to Camp WaterColor
  • Easy Access to WaterColor Beach Club and Community Pools (heated seasonally) 
  • Scenic Pond Setting in the Heart of WaterColor
  • Quick Bike or LSV Ride to Beach Access, Dining and Shopping

Know Before You Go

  • Summer rentals are Saturday–Saturday only. Nightly (with seasonal minimums) and weekly rates available during other times of year.
  • Access to WaterColor amenities is included for up to 12 guests ages 5 and older.
  • Strict No Pet Policy due to allergies — unauthorized animals will result in fines and possible eviction plus deep cleaning fees.
  • A mid-stay clean is required for reservations of 10+ days (included in rate). Additional cleans available for a fee.
  • Street parking is prohibited. Neighborhood parking is first come, first serve for up to 2 additional vehicles.

* One promotion or discount per reservation. Promotions or discounts cannot be combined

Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Escape the commotion of everyday life and join us for the most relaxing vacation get-a-away that you've ever experienced. Located beachside on Highway 30A in the gated community of Sunset Beach, Beach Music offers a vacation of a lifetime including a Gulf front community pool! Our two bedroom, three bath home is nestled a few blocks away from the prestigious and highly sought after destination of Rosemary Beach as well as famous Alys Beach. Decorated by our team of professional designers combines chic interiors and relaxed design for the ultimate getaway.

Beach Music is an exquisitely decorated two-bedroom, three bath home approximately 150 steps from the Emerald Coast. Catch a glimpse of the Gulf from the balcony! The main level of Beach Music features a comfortable, tastefully decorated living room where guests can unwind in style! The furniture and accessories have been carefully and thoughtfully purchased to provide a tranquil coastal atmosphere.

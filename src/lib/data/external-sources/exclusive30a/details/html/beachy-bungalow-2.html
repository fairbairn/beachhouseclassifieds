Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Cancellations or changes that result in a shortened stay made within 30 days of the arrival date forfeit the full reservation amount.

Beachy Bungalow Features
- Gulf Views! Catch a glimpse of the Gulf and the WaterColor Beach Club from the balcony and living room
- 2 Taxi Bikes included with your stay, serviced regularly to ensure they are ready for your arrival
- Parking for 1 Vehicle in building parking lot on a first come, first serve basis. ADA parking available in lot. Your parking tag will be waiting for you in the condo.
- Elevator access to condo - code entry required after hours
- Next to dining and shopping, including Wine World, Scratch Biscuit kitchen, and Beach Happy Cafe
- Building is adjacent to Cerulean Park and 100 yards to the WaterColor Beach Club, with multiple Gulf facing pools, cantina, and towel service 
- 5 Min bike ride to Camp WaterColor, with multiple pools, waterslides, lazy river, playground, basketball court, cantina, and towel service. This is a must visit when you stay in WaterColor.
- Furnished Balcony with view of WaterColor Beach Club and Gulf View
- Outdoor cushioned seating and outdoor dining for 4 on the balcony
- Dining area with seating for 4
- Full kitchen, well stocked for meal prep
- Plush sofa and reading chairs in the living room
- Large TV in the living room and TVs in both bedrooms
- Cable and WIFI available
- Laundry closet with washer and dryer
- Access to WaterColor Beach Club, Camp WaterColor, and community pools included with your reservation for 4 guests, age 5 and up
- Community pools heated during cooler months. Towel service available at the Beach Club and Camp WaterColor (subject to change by the HOA)
- Low Speed Vehicle (Golf cart) rentals are restricted at this location as access to charging outlets cannot be guaranteed
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee

Charming WaterColor Condo with a Prime Location and view of the WaterColor Beach Club

Located just 100 yards from the WaterColor Beach Club, this elegant two-suite condo offers the ideal beach retreat in the heart of WaterColor. Perfect for couples or small families, the home features a peek of the Gulf, a spacious furnished balcony for outdoor dining or lounging, and complimentary use of two bikes to explore 30A in style.

Inside, enjoy an open-concept layout with comfortable coastal furnishings, a well-equipped kitchen, and two private bedroom suites for maximum comfort. Whether you're relaxing on the balcony, biking to local shops and restaurants, or spending your day at the Beach Club, this condo offers the perfect blend of location, views, and charm.

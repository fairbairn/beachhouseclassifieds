Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Rosemary Renaissance

Rosemary Beach - Gulf View

5 Bedrooms
5.5 Baths
Sleeps 16
3526 SQ FEET

Video Tour

Rosemary Renaissance will surpass your idea of a vacation home. This stunning home, designed by Bobby McAlpine, is an achievement in marrying style, comfort, luxury, and location. It is truly a rebirth of the luxury vacation experience.

Located on Eastern Green, Rosemary Renaissance is one of only a handful of homes with this distinct location. Walk to the upscale shopping and dining of Rosemary Square in minutes. The home is only 75 yards to the Coquina pool. If you are planning a wedding in Rosemary, this is the ideal home for the bridal family.

Rosemary Renaissance Features
- Breathtaking Gulf Views from the front balcony
- Pet Friendly for up to 2 dogs, 45 pounds or less, and non-shedding breeds only. Approval from Exclusive 30A and payment of a non-refundable pet fee is required prior to your arrival.
- Overlooks Eastern Green
- 3 Houses from the Eastern Green Beach Access Walkover C. Advanced beach chair reservations recommended.
- 75 yards to Coquina Pool - access included with your reservation. Pool heated seasonally (subject to change by the HOA)
- Complimentary Use of 5 Taxi Bikes, serviced regularly to ensure they are in top shape for your arrival
- Designer Interiors Throughout
- Private courtyard with outdoor dining and propane grill
- 1st Floor Gourmet kitchen and dining area plus sitting room with TV. Kitchen features two stoves, two dishwashers and two refrigerators
- 2nd floor main living room with wet bar featuring a coffee maker and ice machine
- Wrap around balconies overlooking courtyard
- Outdoor lounge on covered balcony
- WIFI throughout home. TVs have streaming capability.
- 1st floor laundry room with washer and dryer
- Garage parking for 2 vehicles (not recommended for large vehicles). Additional neighborhood parking is available on a first come, first serve basis and is limited. Parking passes provided. RVs, trailers, or oversized vehicles prohibited within Rosemary community per the HOA
- Golf carts not allowed within the Rosemary community per the HOA
- Concierge services including beach chair rentals, baby equipment rentals, grocery delivery, and private chefs available. Ask for details!
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee

Area Construction - Due to the outstanding beaches and amazing communities, our area is getting more popular each year. There is a high possibility of nearby construction, home remodels, community changes and county projects on the roads and bridges along 30A year-round and throughout every single community.  Please be aware that refunds will not be issued due to noise disturbance or unsightly views due to nearby projects.

***Pets are welcome with prior approval by Exclusive 30A. Please request your pet’s stay at time of reservation. Payment of our non-refundable pet fee must be paid at time of approval.***

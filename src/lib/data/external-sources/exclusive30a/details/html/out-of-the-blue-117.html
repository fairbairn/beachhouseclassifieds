Out of the Blue
Iconic Seaside Retreat with Watchtower Views and Steps to Town Square

Home Introduction

Unwind in style at Out of the Blue, a beautifully renovated five-bedroom retreat in the heart of Seaside. With designer interiors, multiple outdoor living spaces, and a top-floor watchtower with Gulf glimpses, this home offers the perfect blend of charm and location just steps from the beach and Central Square.

Home Highlights

  • Watchtower Lounge — Top floor with 360-degree views and glimpses of the Gulf
  • Beach Access — 3 minute stroll to East Ruskin Beach Pavilion
  • Prime Location — 1 minute walk to Seaside Square shopping and dining
  • Resort Amenities — Access to the Adult Pool, Family Pool, and Fitness Center is a 2 minute stroll from the home (Family Pool heated seasonally)
  • 4 Taxi Bikes — Professionally serviced and ready to ride
  • Gourmet Kitchen — Built-in refrigerator, four dishwasher drawers, double gas oven, and 6-burner range
  • Dining for 12 — Table seating for 10 plus breakfast bar for 2
  • Main Living Room — Second floor with 60 inch Smart TV
  • Outdoor Living — Covered porch with cushioned seating and ping pong table
  • Covered Balcony — Outdoor dining, rocking chairs, ceiling fan, and cushioned seating
  • Reading Nook — Cozy space with twin daybed between third and fourth floors
  • Designer Interiors — Fully renovated in 2024 with elevated finishes throughout
  • Laundry — First floor washer and dryer
  • Parking — Two neighborhood parking passes provided
  • Complete Clean Linen Program — High-end cotton towels and linens provided

Location Perks

  • 1 minute walk to Seaside Town Square dining, shopping, and entertainment
  • 3 minute walk to East Ruskin Beach Pavilion with private access
  • 2 minute walk to Seaside pools and fitness center
  • Steps to local favorites including cafés, markets, and live music

Know Before You Go

  • No golf carts allowed in Seaside per HOA regulations (limited medical exceptions may apply)
  • Access to private beach entrances and all community pools included; some pools heated seasonally
  • Mid-stay clean required for reservations of 10 days or longer (included in rate)
  • Additional cleaning services available for an extra fee
  • Concierge services available for beach rentals, grocery delivery, bikes, and baby equipment
  • Area summer camps and kids classes available seasonally
  • No pets allowed; unauthorized pets may result in fines or eviction

Additional Details

Out of the Blue is located on one of Seaside’s most desirable streets, offering direct access to the energy of town while maintaining a private residential feel. Enjoy live music from your porch, walk to dining and shopping in seconds, and relax in a home that captures the timeless architecture and charm that made Seaside famous.

Traveling with a larger group? Ask about adding the nearby two-bedroom carriage house for additional accommodations.

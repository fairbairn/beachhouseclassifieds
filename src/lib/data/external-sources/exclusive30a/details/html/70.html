Dune Allen - Private Beach

3 Bedrooms
2 Baths
Sleeps 6
1045 SQ FEET

Designer Gulf Place Condo Features
- Two King Suites plus a Bunk Room
- Access to three community pools. Gulf Place Cabanas and Caribbean pools are heated seasonally (subject to change by the HOA).
- Access to two deeded beach entrances as well as 100 yards to the Ed Walline Beach Access featuring parking, restrooms, and ramp access to the beach
- Access to 3 Taxi Bikes, serviced regularly to keep them in top shape
- Dedicated parking spot in garage for 1 vehicle. Additional parking for 1 vehicle in parking lot on first come, first serve basis
- All one level! Elevator access to floor is available
- Enjoy the community Pickleball and Tennis Courts
- Smart TVs in the bedrooms and living room - stream all your favorite shows
- Sonos Sound System
- Designer interiors throughout the modern condo
- Full kitchen with stainless steel appliances from GE and Samsung, drip and k-cup coffee maker
- Breakfast bar seating for 4 along the waterfall kitchen island
- Covered balcony with cushioned seating set and a partial Gulf view
- Cable and WIFI
- Laundry closet with HE washer and dryer
- Located in the beloved Gulf Place community with lots of shopping and dining, an Artist Colony, and an amphitheater for community events
- Condo overlooks the Gulf Place Amphitheater. You can catch a glimpse of the Gulf from the balcony.
- Gulf Place features resort amenities including multiple community pools (some heated per HOA discretion), two deeded beach accesses, pickleball, and tennis. 
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee
- Concierge services available, including grocery delivery, baby equipment rentals, eBike rentals, beach service, and more

Gulf Place is the perfect spot for a relaxing vacation with access to adventure! You'll be in love the moment you arrive.

Located in the Gulf Place Residences, every aspect of this home was carefully crafted. The condo is all one level, and an elevator is available directly from the dedicated ground floor garage parking spot. When you walk into the first-floor condo, you are created with crisp white interiors accented with tones of stone, silver, and bronze. The well-appointed king suites feature Samsung Smart TVs, ceiling fans, ample storage, and modern baths. A bunk room with sleeping for 2 is tucked away in a private bedroom.

When it is time to venture out, you will be impressed with all the amenities available. Access to three community pools is available, including a pool overlooking the Gulf Place lagoons. The Gulf Place Cabana and Caribbean pools are heated during cooler months. TWO deeded beach accesses are within 200 yds of the condo. Easily walk to the regional Ed Walline Beach Access in under 2min. Bring your racquets to enjoy Pickleball or tennis! Gulf Place offers 3 pickleball courts and 1 tennis court.

You'll be so impressed with Gulf Place you may never get into your car until it is time to depart. Dining including The Perfect Pig, award winning Shunk Gulley, Pizza by the Sea, Shelbys Beach Bar and Grill, and Sunrise Coffee Co are just outside your door. Don't forget to shop at the unique Artists at Gulf Place market to purchase one-of-a-kind arts and crafts from 30A artists. Your private balcony overlooks Gulley Amphitheater, an expansive green space for playing or enjoying community events.

Don't forget the Wine Walk every 3rd Thursday of the month! Enjoy samplings of hand-selected wines, light appetizers, live music, and giveaways while venturing to all the unique Gulf Place shops. Featuring jewelry, artwork, clothing and many other unique gifts, event goers can enjoy the evening sipping, dancing, and shopping while you stroll around beautiful Gulf Place.

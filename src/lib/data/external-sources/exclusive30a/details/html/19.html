Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Coral Cottage Features
- Flexible Summer Stays - No more Saturday-to-Saturday requirements!
- Keyless entry and no contact check-in
- Across from the Dragonfly Pool. Access included with your reservation
- 5 Minute bike ride to Seaside. Enjoy shopping, dining, and live entertainment
- 4 Minute bike or LSV ride to Camp WaterColor. Enjoy multiple pools, snack bar, water slides, and a lazy river
- Enjoy 4 Taxi bikes, perfect for exploring WaterColor and 30A. Bikes are serviced regularly to keep them in top shape.
- Complimentary Use of a 6-seater LSV (golf cart). Some restrictions apply and must sign a waiver to use the cart during your stay
- Laundry on 1st floor
- Driveway parking for 2-3 cars. We recommend you bring no more than 1 oversized vehicle. Nearby neighborhood public spaces available on first come, first serve basis. RVs, trailers, and street parking are prohibited by the HOA.
- Cable and WIFI throughout home
- 1st Floor wrap-around screened-in porch with outdoor dining, cushioned seating, bench swing, and ceiling fans
- 2nd Floor wrap-around balcony with sundeck plus covered section
- Full kitchen with high-end appliances plus coffee bar with Keurig and drip coffee maker
- Dining for 8 plus breakfast bar seating for 4
- 1st floor main living room with plush sectional sofa, swivel chair, and Samsung Smart TV
- Buffet with wine chiller and icemaker plus barware
- Main living area and a King Suite on 1st Floor - ideal setup if you are traveling with the grandparents
- 2nd Floor TV room with queen sleeper sofa and entrance to wrap-around open-air balcony
- Corner unit overlooking landscaped wetlands
- Outdoor Propane Grill
- Outdoor shower
- 1.5 miles to Eastern Lake Trail Head of Point Washington State Forest. Enjoy miles of hiking and biking trails plus wildlife viewing.  The trail features a 3.5-, 5-, and 10- mile loop.
- Access to the WaterColor Beach Club, Camp WaterColor, and all community pools included for 12 guests age 5 and older
- Community pools heated during cooler months and towel service available at Camp WaterColor and the WaterColor Beach Club (subject to change by the neighborhood)
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee
- Strict No Pet Home due to owner allergies
- Concierge services available including grocery delivery, baby equipment rentals, catered beach bonfires, beach service, and more!

Welcome to Coral Cottage! This inviting home was decorated by our design team and is in the quiet Crossings section of WaterColor. Coral Cottage offers warm, cozy, and chic interiors as well as a spectacular location. Across from the Dragonfly pool and only 0.6 miles to the Gulf, you will love being close to it all. With room for up to 12 in 5 bedrooms, your family will enjoy the spacious layout. Enjoy 4 Taxi bikes and a complimentary use of the 6-seater LSV (golf cart) during your stay.

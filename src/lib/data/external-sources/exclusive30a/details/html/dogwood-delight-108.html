Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Cancellations or changes that result in a shortened stay made within 30 days of the arrival date forfeit the full reservation amount.

 Dogwood Delight Features
- Private Pool on a huge double lot - pool heating available October through April for an additional fee (hot tub in photos is not available)
- Landscaped and fenced-in yard. The double lot offers more privacy without another home immediately next door.
- Pet Friendly for up to 2 dogs, 45 pounds or less, and non-shedding. Approval from Exclusive 30A and payment of a non-refundable pet fee is required prior to your arrival.
-1 Block to Dogwood/Thyme Beach Access. Beach service available at this entrance.
-1 Block to Seaside and 2 Min Bike Ride to the Seaside Shopping and Dining District. You will be close to all the popular 30A spots while still enjoying a quiet part of the neighborhood. Best of both worlds!
- Access to 4 Taxi Bikes, serviced regularly to ensure they are in top shape for your arrival
- Full kitchen with stainless steel appliances
- Outdoor dining on 2nd floor balcony. Catch glimpses of the Gulf while you dine al fresco
- 1st floor sitting room with conversation seating set
- Dining table with seating for 5 plus breakfast bar seating for 5
- Smart TVs with streaming services and WIFI throughout home (no cable)
- Washer and Dryer
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee

We are in love with Dogwood Delight! This pet friendly home on a huge double lot is perfect for large families plus their pup to reunite for a relaxing vacation experience. Only 1 block to the Dogwood/Thyme beach entrance and a 2-minute bike ride to shopping and dining in Seaside. You are so close to the beach, you can see the crystal blue waters from the balcony! This spacious home, both inside and out, welcomes you for a chic and comfortable vacation in historic Seagrove.

Area Construction - Our area is getting more popular each year! There is a possibility of nearby construction, community changes and projects on the roads and bridges along 30A year-round and throughout every single community. Please be aware that refunds will not be issued due to noise disturbance or unsightly views due to nearby projects.

***Pets are welcome with prior approval by Exclusive 30A. Please request your pet’s stay at time of reservation. Payment of our non-refundable pet fee must be paid at time of approval.***

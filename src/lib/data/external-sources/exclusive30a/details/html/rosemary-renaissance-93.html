Rosemary Renaissance
Iconic Designer Home Overlooking Eastern Green with Gulf Views

Home Introduction

Experience elevated coastal luxury at Rosemary Renaissance, a stunning designer home by Bobby McAlpine located in one of the most coveted spots in Rosemary Beach. Overlooking Eastern Green and just steps from the beach and Coquina Pool, this exceptional home blends timeless design with unmatched location.

Home Highlights

  • Gulf Views — Enjoy breathtaking views from the front balcony
  • Prime Location — Overlooking Eastern Green in the heart of Rosemary Beach
  • Beach Access — Just 3 homes from Eastern Green Beach Access Walkover C
  • Community Pool — Only 75 yards to Coquina Pool (seasonally heated, subject to HOA)
  • Taxi Bikes — 5 Taxi bikes included and professionally maintained
  • Designer Interiors — Thoughtfully curated by renowned architect Bobby McAlpine
  • Gourmet Kitchen — Two stoves, two refrigerators, and two dishwashers for effortless entertaining
  • Multiple Living Spaces — First-floor sitting room plus second-floor main living room with wet bar
  • Coffee and Bar Area — Wet bar with coffee maker and ice machine
  • Outdoor Living — Private courtyard with dining area and propane grill
  • Wraparound Balconies — Overlooking the courtyard with covered lounge seating
  • Smart Living — WiFi throughout with streaming-enabled TVs
  • Laundry — First-floor washer and dryer
  • Parking — Garage parking for 2 vehicles plus limited neighborhood parking (no oversized vehicles, RVs, or trailers)

Location Perks

  • Steps to Rosemary Beach Town Center for upscale shopping and dining
  • 1 minute walk to the beach access with chair service available
  • Easy access to community pools and green spaces
  • Ideal location for weddings and special events in Rosemary Beach

Know Before You Go

  • A mid-stay clean is required for reservations of 10 days or longer (included in your stay)
  • Additional cleanings can be scheduled for an extra fee
  • Golf carts are not permitted within the Rosemary Beach community
  • Parking is limited and strictly enforced; oversized vehicles, RVs, and trailers are not allowed
  • Concierge services available including beach chair setups, grocery delivery, private chefs, and baby equipment rentals
  • Nearby construction and community projects may occur; refunds are not provided for related disturbances

Home Overview

Rosemary Renaissance is a rare and remarkable property that redefines the luxury vacation experience. Perfectly positioned on Eastern Green, this home offers a front-row seat to one of the most picturesque settings along 30A.

Designed with both elegance and functionality in mind, the home features multiple living spaces ideal for gathering and entertaining. The gourmet kitchen is equipped to handle large groups with ease, while the adjacent dining and sitting areas provide inviting spaces to relax.

The second-floor living room serves as a central hub, complete with a wet bar and access to expansive wraparound balconies. These outdoor spaces offer peaceful views of the courtyard and glimpses of the Gulf, creating the perfect setting for morning coffee or evening cocktails.

The private courtyard adds another layer of charm, offering a secluded outdoor dining area perfect for al fresco meals.

Whether you are planning a family getaway, a special celebration, or a wedding stay, Rosemary Renaissance delivers an unforgettable blend of luxury, location, and timeless coastal design.

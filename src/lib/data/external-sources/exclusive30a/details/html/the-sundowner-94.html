Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Cancellations or changes that result in a shortened stay made within 30 days of the arrival date forfeit the full reservation amount.

The Sundowner Features
- Panoramic Gulf Views from all floors
- Designer interiors, including cotton bedding and towels for every room
- Flexible summer stays! No more Saturday-to-Saturday requirements.
- Private elevator which travels to all floors. Must sign a waiver to gain access to the elevator during your stay. Adult use only. Please ask for details regarding mobility considerations for anyone in your group.
- Private Pool and deck with lounge seating, propane grill, and umbrella. Pool heating included for arrivals October 1 - April 30
- 10 yards to beach entrance
- Enjoy the 4 Taxi bikes provided at the home. Perfect for exploring the neighborhood.
- Pet Friendly for up to 2 dogs, 60 pounds or less, non-aggressive breeds only.  Approval from Exclusive 30A and payment of a non-refundable pet fee is required prior to your arrival.
- Dogs are restricted from the nearby beach access, but the home is less than 10 minutes away from Dog Beach at Pier Park. Enjoy a day at the beach with your pup!
-  Next to Thomas Donuts. The morning aroma is intoxicating. Additional shopping and dining options nearby
- Outdoor Gas Grill
- Driveway parking for 4 vehicles
- 2nd Floor Family Room - Gulf View, sectional sofa facing Smart TV, card table with seating for 4, mini fridge and Keurig coffee maker. Perfect for online school or working from the beach 
- Whole home WIFI plus printer
- 3rd Floor Gulf Facing Living Room with TV and two seating areas
- 3rd Floor Dining Area with seating for 10 plus breakfast bar seating for 4
- 3rd Floor Gulf facing balcony with cushioned outdoor seating
- Laundry Rooms on 2nd and 3rd Floors
- 3rd Floor Gourmet Kitchen with stainless steel Italian appliances including an 8 burner range, wine chiller, standalone ice maker, Keurig and drip coffee makers,  and pasta pot faucet.
- 12-minute drive to Rosemary Beach and Inlet Beach
- 9-minute drive to Pier Park, with incredible shopping, Gulf front dining, movie theater, and Ferris wheel
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee

The Sundowner is everything you are looking for in a beach vacation home - high-end designer interiors, spacious floor plan, private pool, panoramic Gulf views, and more! Located next to the famous Thomas Donuts on Front Beach Road, enjoy the aroma of fresh baked goods while sipping your morning coffee on the Gulf facing balconies. Truly a vacation experience for all your senses! More shopping and dining options are nearby while also enjoying the quieter atmosphere of this section of gorgeous Laguna Beach.

The beach entrance is 10 yards away and you will enjoy panoramic views of the crystal blue waters of the Gulf from every level of the three-story home. Enjoy the backyard pool for a private resort experience. You may never want to leave!

***Pets are welcome with prior approval by Exclusive 30A. Please request your pet’s stay at time of reservation. Payment of our non-refundable pet fee must be paid at time of approval.***

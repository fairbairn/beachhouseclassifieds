Cancellation Policy
Our cancellation policy allows you to cancel and receive a refund, minus a $175 cancellation fee, if you notify us via email prior to 60 days before your check-in date. If you notify us via email you wish to cancel your reservation between 31-59 days of your arrival date, you forfeit 50% of the full reservation amount and a $175 cancellation fee. Dandelion – Americana-Coastal Luxury in the Heart of WaterColor

About This Home
Welcome to Dandelion, a welcoming 3,400-sq-ft designer home in the original WaterColor community — where Americana charm meets coastal sophistication. Located directly across from the WaterColor Tennis Center and minutes from Seaside, this home offers one of the most desirable locations in all of WaterColor.

This spacious retreat features a brand-new kitchen, updated bathrooms, and décor inspired by an Americana-meets-beachy aesthetic — airy, welcoming, and stylishly comfortable.

Homes this close to the front of the neighborhood are rare — reserve early before your dates are gone!

Highlights
- Prime Location: Across from WaterColor Tennis Center
- 5 Complimentary Taxi Bikes – explore WaterColor, Seaside, and the Beach Club in minutes
- Pet Friendly for up to 2 dogs, 60 pounds or less. Approval from Exclusive 30A and payment of a non-refundable pet fee is required prior to your arrival.
- Driveway Parking for 4 Vehicles
- Porch and Balcony Overlooking Nature Preserve and Walking Trail
- Gas Grill and Outdoor Adirondack Seating
- Private Outdoor Shower
- Access to WaterColor Beach Club, Camp WaterColor and Heated Community Pools (for up to 14 guests ages 5+)
- Laundry Room with Washer and Dryer

Living Spaces
Main Living Area (2nd Floor)
Open-concept great room with 20-ft wood-beamed ceilings, a fully equipped chef’s kitchen, dining for 13, and double French doors leading to a screened balcony with dining for 10 and a cushioned sitting area.

Second Living Room (1st Floor)
A family-friendly space perfect for kids or movie nights — featuring a sectional sofa, flat-screen TV, DVD player, and a library of children’s books.

Outdoor Spaces
Enjoy the front porch swing with views of the Tennis Center or unwind on the balcony overlooking the nature preserve and walking trail.

Location

You’ll be right in the heart of WaterColor — an unbeatable location to enjoy everything 30A offers:
- 2-min bike ride to Camp WaterColor
- 3-min bike ride to Seaside (via Viridian Drive cut-through)
- 4-min bike ride to WaterColor Beach Club and Gulf of Mexico

Kick off your shoes and sink your feet into the powder-white sand and emerald-green waters that make this area world-famous.

Booking Notes
- Nightly and weekly rates available year-round (seasonal minimums apply)
- Reservation requests are confirmed within 24 hours of booking
- Guests must be 25+ years old to reserve and must occupy the home during the stay
- At least one mid-stay clean is required for any reservation 10 days or longer and this cost is included in your reservation. Extra cleans can be scheduled for an additional fee

***Pets are welcome with prior approval by Exclusive 30A. Please request your pet’s stay at time of reservation. Payment of our non-refundable pet fee must be paid at time of approval.***

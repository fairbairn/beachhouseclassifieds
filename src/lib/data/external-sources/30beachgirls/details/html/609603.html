ABOUT THE HOME: Welcome to 30A Good Vibes Only, a stunning coastal retreat in the heart of Blue Mountain/Gulf Place. With a complimentary golf cart included, getting to the beach, local shops, and dining is effortless.
Inside, soaring ceilings and an open-concept layout create a bright, inviting space, while a wall of glass doors seamlessly connects you to the private pool and outdoor entertainment area. The chef-ready kitchen is fully equipped, making group meals easy and enjoyable.
With four bedrooms and four and a half bathrooms, the home comfortably sleeps up to ten guests---perfect for families and group getaways. Ideally located near both the Gulf and Choctawhatchee Bay, you'll have easy access to beach days, boating, and unforgettable coastal adventures.
ABOUT THE AREA: Blue Mountain is one of the 30A coastal communities that rests between Gulf Place and Grayton Beach. The Blue Mountain community is known for having a quieter family atmosphere compared to some of the other communities but still provides ample restaurants, shops and activities.
BEACH ACCESS: Gulfview Heights beach access is the closest to 30a Good Vibes (approximately 3 blocks from the house). It has parking, restrooms, showers, picnic tables with shelter, and lifeguards during peak season. From the house, turn right on 30A and left at Goatfeathers Seafood restaurant at Gulfview Heights street, then 1/4 mile to the gulf.

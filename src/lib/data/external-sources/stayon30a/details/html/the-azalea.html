About this space​

Welcome to ‘The Azalea’, professionally managed by Stay on 30A! A stunning multi-level home designed for families and groups of up to 16. With four spacious king bedrooms, a bunk room that sleeps 8 kids, and 4.5 bathrooms, everyone has space to relax in comfort, while the first-floor primary suite adds convenience for parents. Living areas on the first and third floors create the perfect flow for gathering and spending time together, and the third-floor balcony with an outdoor kitchen and the fourth-floor observation deck offer panoramic Gulf views ideal for sunsets and coastal evenings. Pool days, firepit nights, arcade battles, and bike rides along scenic 30A this home delivers fun at every turn with a heated communal pool, fully equipped kitchen, and six complimentary adult bikes for all ages to enjoy. Just a short walk to the beach, this home sits in the charming boutique community of The Grove at Inlet, with a communal pool, green space, and easy access to Highway 98’s scenic path and all that 30A has to offer. Designed for unforgettable 30A escapes, this home blends coastal elegance with resort-style comfort, giving your group the perfect place to relax, reconnect, and soak in every moment of beachside living.  Luxury Village Feel: Tucked within a single, thoughtfully designed block, The Grove at Inlet features just nine upscale beach homes, a private community pool, and a spacious green area for gathering and play. With direct pedestrian access to Highway 98’s scenic walking path, this intimate enclave offers a true village-style experience perfect for families seeking a refined and relaxed 30A beach escape. Unbeatable 30A Location: Perfectly positioned near the heart of it all, this newer neighborhood delivers easy access without the crowds. Enjoy a short walk to Rosemary Beach, 30Avenue, and nearby dining and shopping, while retreating back to a quieter, more peaceful setting. This is coastal living done right and 30A vacationing at its finest. Inlet Beach Regional Access is the largest county access and features three dune walkovers and 117 parking spaces. 2. Wall Street Beach Public Access. This access is at the end of South Wall Street. About 200 yards east of the intersection of Scenic 30A and Hwy 98, turn south on to South Wall Street then proceed two blocks to the dead end at the gulf.

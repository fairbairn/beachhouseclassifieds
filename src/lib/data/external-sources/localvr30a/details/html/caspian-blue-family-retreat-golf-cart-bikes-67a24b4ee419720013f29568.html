Summary

Escape to 30A Caspian Blue Paradise, a luxury retreat featuring a 5-bedroom main cottage and a charming 1-bedroom carriage house. This combined rental sleeps up to 22 guests, offering high-end amenities, a 6-person golf cart, complimentary bikes, and access to a resort-style heated pool. Located in Magnolia Cottages by the Sea, Just a short walk to deeded beach access, it's ideal for multi-family vacations or large groups.

Space

Location perks: Seacrest Beach Access, (0.7 miles), Alys Beach (1.8 miles), The Big Chill (1.8 miles), Rosemary Beach (2.5 miles), Deer Lake State Park (2.4 miles) Main Cottage Interior: — Gourmet kitchen (Calcutta Gold quartz countertops, wine fridge, stainless steel appliances) — Two spacious living areas with Smart TVs (75” in main living room) — High-speed WiFi (100 Mbps)​ — Two laundry rooms (each with a full-size washer and dryer) — Central heat and A/C​ Carriage House Interior: — Kitchen (drip coffee maker, cooking utensils, tableware)​ — Living room (Smart TV, queen sleeper sofa)​ — Free WiFi (100 Mbps)​ — Central heat and A/C Exterior: — Large screened-in porch with seating — Outdoor dining area (seats 10)​ — On-site parking for 4 cars (3 at the cottage, 1 at the carriage house)​​ — 6-seater golf cart​ — 6 bikes (4 at Cottage, 2 at Carriage House) — Shared community pool (heated seasonally)​ Floor Configuration: Cottage: Main floor: Living room, kitchen, dining area, 1 bedroom (king bed, en-suite bathroom), half bathroom Upper floor: 3 bedrooms (king beds, en-suite bathrooms), 1 bunk room (2 queen-over-queen bunks, 1 twin-over-twin bunk, en-suite bathroom) Carriage House: Main floor: Open-concept living room with sleeper sofa (queen), kitchen, dining area, 1 bedroom (queen bed), 1 full bathroom Beach Access: — Deeded beach access directly across from the neighborhood​ — Beach service available (2 chairs & umbrella rental required for seating near the access)​ — Public beach areas are within a short walk (2 minutes to the right, 4 minutes to the left)

Located between Alys Beach and Watersound, Caspian Blue is minutes from world-class dining, boutique shopping, and the pristine white-sand beaches of 30A. Explore nearby Rosemary Beach and The Big Chill for live music and family-friendly entertainment.

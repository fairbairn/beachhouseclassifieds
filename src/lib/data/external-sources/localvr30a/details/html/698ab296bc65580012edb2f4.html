Summary

Escape to our Santa Rosa Beach vacation rental where Gulf breezes and palm trees set the scene. This sprawling 3,509-square-foot home sleeps 18 and features two levels of stylish living space, including five full bathrooms. Highlights include a heated pool, a grill, and a prime location near 30A bike paths + only a 3 minute walk from the Gulfview Heights Beach Access. Whether hosting an event or a family getaway, our property offers the space and amenities needed for a premier Florida retreat.

Neighborhood

Nestled in a quiet pocket of Scenic 30A, this neighborhood offers a breezy, laid-back atmosphere. You are within walking distance of the Gulfview Heights beach access and the vibrant Gulf Place Town Center, known for its eclectic boutiques, art galleries, and popular restaurants like Shunk Gulley. The area is a haven for outdoor enthusiasts, featuring extensive bike paths that connect the unique coastal communities of 30A. Nearby Grayton Beach State Park and Blue Mountain Beach offer pristine white sands and rare coastal dune lakes for exploring.

Transit

While a car is helpful for exploring further destinations like Destin or Miramar Beach, many local attractions are easily accessible by foot or bike.

Access

Guests will have access to the entire home, private pool, and wraparound decks, and beach amenities. Parking is available on-site.

Notes

Always check the flag color on the beach before entering the ocean. Golf cart and bikes are a bonus amenity that may not be functional during a stay, which is why they are not marketed.

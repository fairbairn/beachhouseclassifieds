Summary

Located in the exclusive Magnolia Cottages by the Sea community, this 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom coastal retreat offers modern elegance and convenience. This home features an open-concept living space, a gourmet kitchen, and a screened-in porch. Enjoy the provided golf cart and bikes for easy trips to nearby beaches, dining, and shopping. With access to a community pool and scenic trails, this home is perfect for families and groups looking for a relaxing getaway.

Space

The Crab House is a beautiful, well designed home in the quaint neighborhood of Magnolia Cottages by the Sea.  The neighborhood is just a short bike ride to Rosemary Beach, Alys Beach and The Big Chill.  This charming beach home is conveniently located next to the resort style heated pool and offers 4 bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms (3 Kings, 2 twin over queen bunks, and bunks in hallway).  There is a large screened porch, perfect for morning coffee, an afternoon book or evening cocktail.   The fully equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances and professional ice maker provides everything needed for meals at home. A gas grill is also provided. Once you are ready to explore, you can walk, bike or run along the 18 mile Timpoochee Trail, which provides easy access to all that 30A has to offer. You are certain to enjoy your stay at The Crab House along Florida's 30A corridor! TRANSPORTATION - A golf cart is provided - 4 bikes are also included with this property. Should you decide to rent some, a discount code to a local bike shop will be provided with your arrival information. HELPFUL NEIGHBORHOOD INFORMATION - Golf Cart Parking: There's designated parking at the front of the neighborhood between Morgan Street and the Waterhouse condos. -  Large resort style pool (heated depending on ambient nightly temperatures) BEACH ACCESS/SERVICE: - There is deeded beach access directly across from the neighborhood. - Beach Set Up - A limited number of "sets" (2 chairs and an umbrella) are available for rent for Magnolia Cottages guests. If a set is rented, other guests at the house can sit around the chairs and not need to move to the nearby public sections. Information available upon request. Please note: The beach set must be rented directly through the chair vendor. A set is not included with this home. SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (SLEEPS 14) - Primary Suite - 1st floor: King bed, private bathroom with walk in shower - King Bedroom 2nd floor:  King bed,  private bathroom with walk in shower - King Bedroom 2nd floor: King bed, private bathroom with walk in shower - Bunk Room 2nd floor: TwinXL/Queen bunks (2 sets) private bath with tub/shower - Bunk Alcove 1st floor: Twin bunks FEATURES: -  4 Bedroom / 4.5 Bathroom House -  Sleeps 14 (King, King, King, 2 TXL/Queen bunks, 2 twin bunks) -  Street Legal 6-seater Golf Cart - waiver required prior to use; must be 25 to operate -  4 adult bikes - Beach Chairs, Towels - Gas grill - Huge Screened Porch - Fully Appointed Kitchen - Full Size Refrigerator - Ice Maker - Full Size Stove with Oven - Full Size Washer & Dryer - TV in Living Room & Bedrooms - Complimentary WIFI - Parking for 2 cars in driveway and 1 along street - Large Resort Style Pool only steps away - Close Proximity to The Beach - neighborhood is directly across 30A from beach access - 17 Mile Bike Path Along 30A - Close to The Big Chill, Seaside, Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach

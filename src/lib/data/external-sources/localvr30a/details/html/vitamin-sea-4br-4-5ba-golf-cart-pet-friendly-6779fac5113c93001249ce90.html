Summary

Welcome to Vitamin Sea, perfect for families and groups located in the highly sought-after Magnolia Cottages by the Sea. Featuring 4 bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms, and plenty of space to unwind, this home includes modern conveniences like cable Smart TVs in every room, a fully equipped kitchen, and a private golf cart for exploring the area. Check out the shops and stores of Rosemary Beach for a night out or enjoy a cool dip in the shared pool.

Space

Nothing is better for the body and soul than Vitamin Sea. This pretty home is nestled in the coveted neighborhood of Magnolia Cottages by the Sea, located in Old Seacrest Beach, FL. This spacious and charming home is steps away from your private deeded beach access ( 2-minute golf cart ride) and a mere 150 feet from the resort-like community  pool. VITAMIN SEA is conveniently located near a variety of sought-after 30A beach destinations like The Big Chill, Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach, surrounded by charming shops, top-rated restaurants and entertainment for families and adults alike. Vitamin Sea is pet-friendly, comes with a 6-seat golf cart and boasts smart TVs in every bedroom, and offers 6 beach chairs and beach towels.  The moment you pull up to Vitamin Sea vacation begins. A generous and welcoming screened-in front porch invites family and friends to enjoy coffee, happy hours, laughs and memories You and your companions will love your stay at Vitamin Sea! TRANSPORTATION - 6 person golf cart (with signed waiver - must be 25 to operate). - Bikes are popular on 30A! Notes

We love dogs and are happy to provide a dog friendly space for your vacation! However, it's important to note that dogs are not allowed on any of the beaches along 30A. There is a dog beach at Pier Park located at 16303 Front Beach Rd. Panama City Beach where Fido can meet some new friends and enjoy our gorgeous Emerald Coast! A Note on Shared Amenities: Please keep in mind that these spaces are operated by the HOA. Because of this, they are subject to seasonal availability—common seasonal closures may include April-May and Sept-Nov—and we can't be held responsible for unexpected closures or repairs. If you are booking specifically for a shared amenity, please feel free to reach out with any questions in advance!

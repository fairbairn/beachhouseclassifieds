Summary

This 30A vacation rental blends style and convenience across 4 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms. Highlights include a well-equipped kitchen, patio dining, a small backyard, and a shared community pool. Relax in the open living area with a 50” Smart TV, cook with LG appliances, or unwind on the deck after a day at the beach. With 500 Mbps WiFi, central A/C, and parking for 4 cars, our home makes vacation easy.

Space

Location perks: Camp Helen State Park (0.8 miles), Inlet Beach Regional Access (1.1 miles), Rosemary Beach (1.4 miles), Pier Park (8.6 miles), Frank Brown Park (9.2 miles). We also provide our favorite local spots in our house manual. Interior — Kitchen (drip coffee maker, toaster, microwave, air fryer, blender, cooking utensils, tableware) — Smart TV (50”) with streaming in living room (Vizio) — In-unit washer and dryer (LG) — Free WiFi (500 Mbps down / 50 Mbps up) — Central heat and A/C — Square footage (approx. 2,600 sq. ft.) Exterior/Nearby — Deck or balcony — Patio — Backyard — Charcoal Grill — Outdoor dining area (4-person dining set) — On-site parking for 4 cars in driveway (no street parking) — Shared Pool Access Floor plan: Main floor: Kitchen, dining area, living room, 1 bedroom (king bed, en-suite bathroom), 1 half bathroom Upper floor: 3 bedrooms (1 queen, 1 queen, 1 twin-over-queen bunk), 2 full bathrooms (1 Jack-and-Jill shared bath)

Neighborhood

Set within Grande Pointe Neighborhood in the Inlet Beach area, the home sits on a quiet residential street with easy access to everyday essentials. It’s a comfortable base for families and groups looking for a spacious stay with reliable amenities, fast WiFi, and hassle-free parking.

Transit

A car is recommended. Use the driveway for on-site parking (up to 4 vehicles). Street parking is not allowed.

Access

Guests have access to the entire home, deck, patio, backyard, and the in-unit washer/dryer. Parking is in the driveway for up to 4 vehicles; street parking is not permitted. Entry is via the Level app invitation.

Notes

Pets are not allowed at this property There are 7 steps to reach the entry Please dispose of trash in the cans located on the right side of the home when facing the house

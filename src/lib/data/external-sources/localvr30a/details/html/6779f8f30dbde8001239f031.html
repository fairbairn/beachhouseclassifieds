Summary

Luxurious 4BR Seagrove 30A home just ½ mile to beach! Enjoy a private heated pool, spa, and direct Eastern Lake access with kayak & paddleboard. Dog-friendly with fenced yard. Includes golf cart, bikes, beach chairs & umbrella—no extra fees. Walk to Old Florida Fish House & Greenway Station. Perfect for families or groups seeking the ultimate 30A getaway with top amenities included.

Space

Welcome to Sea Change at Eastern Lake!  This beautiful pet friendly home with private heated pool (80 degrees) , jetted hot tub (100 degrees) and fully fenced-in yard is located in a quiet, charming neighborhood in the Seagrove beach community.  Sea Change at Eastern Lake sits 1 block from a boat ramp and within ½ mile of two public beach accesses. This well-equipped home is also located less than a mile from the new Greenway 30A station offering multiple retail and dining options! Walk to the Old Florida Fish House (a staple on 30A for years), Beach Bean Coffee, Buddy's Seafood Market and Coast Creamery. Beach access is a breeze with the bikes. Seagrove is a quaint town with old Florida charm.   This home offers everything you need for a memorable vacation including beach chairs, toys, beach towels, umbrellas, kayak, BOTE paddle board, and 4 bikes for your convenience. TRANSPORTATION - 4 bikes are included. Should you decide to rent more a discount code to a local bike shop will be provided with your arrival information - 1 electric golf cart available for use. NOTE: drivers must be over the age of 25 to operate HELPFUL NEIGHBORHOOD INFORMATION - Lake access one block from home (use of kayak included) - South of 30A: Neighborhood street takes you to the beach access 1/2 mile from home - Walkable to Old Florida Fish House and the new Greenway Station shops and eateries - The Big Chill approx. 1 mile east, Seaside is approx. 3 miles to the west SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (SLEEPS 10) - PRIMARY SUITE: 2nd floor, King bed, ensuite bathroom with walk in-shower and jacuzzi tub - QUEEN BEDROOM: 2nd floor, Queen bed, ensuite bathroom with walk-in shower - QUEEN BEDROOM: 2nd floor, 1 Queen bed, plus twin trundle under bed, shared bathroom with tub/shower combo - BUNK ROOM: 2nd floor, 2 twin over twin bunks, shared bathroom with tub/shower combo FEATURES - 4 Bedrooms / 3.5 Baths (King, Queen, 2 twin-over-twin bunks, 2 twins) – sleeps 10 - Private heated pool (80 degrees) and spa - TVs in living room and every bedroom - Well-equipped kitchen - Pet friendly - $250/stay non-refundable pet fee applies - Large, fenced in yard - Use of kayak - Use of paddleboard - 4 bikes - 1 golf cart - Beach supplies (chairs, towels, toys) - Large fenced back yard (includes a corn hole game!) - Fire pit - Lake access 1 block - Beach accesses 1/2 mile - Walking distance to local establishments SECURITY: - There is a smart doorbell Walton County Certification # STR23-001502

Notes

We love dogs and are happy to provide a dog friendly space for your vacation! However, it's important to note that dogs are not allowed on any of the beaches along 30A. There is a dog beach at Pier Park located at 16303 Front Beach Rd. Panama City Beach where Fido can meet some new friends and enjoy our gorgeous Emerald Coast!

Space

Welcome to "B Coastal" located in the new neighborhood of Hawks Landing in Santa Rosa Beach. Your pet-friendly coastal retreat awaits! Experience the brand-new Townhome at Hawk's Landing, an exclusive townhome community nestled just under four miles from the sparkling Gulf of Mexico. Located in the desirable Santa Rosa Beach area, this inviting community offers a perfect mix of luxury, convenience, and coastal charm for those seeking a vacation getaway. The spacious 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom townhome is designed with comfort and style in mind. This newly completed unit features a thoughtful floor plan with all bedrooms on the second level, providing an open and airy main floor that is perfect for family gatherings, entertaining, or simply relaxing. The townhome comfortably accommodates up to 8 guests with a queen bed in the primary, a second queen in the guest room, and two twin beds in the third bedroom, plus a sleeper sofa offers additional flexibility for larger groups. Step inside and be greeted by beautiful EVP flooring throughout. The main living space boasts 9-foot ceilings and a spacious layout that flows seamlessly into a gourmet kitchen with a center island, white cabinetry, and stunning granite countertops. Equipped with stainless steel appliances—including a smooth top stove, microwave, and dishwasher—this kitchen is a chef’s dream, whether you’re preparing a quick breakfast, a picnic for the beach, or a delicious family dinner. Step out and explore everything this charming area has to offer! "B Coastal" is surrounded by a delightful array of eateries, cafes, cozy coffee spots, and weekly farmer’s markets. For boating enthusiasts, Cessna Landing, which is a county park on Highway 393, just 1.5 miles away, offers easy bay access for launching your boat, as well as docks, picnic shelters, basketball and squash court as well as a playground.  Spend your days kayaking, paddle boarding, or just soaking up the sun along Scenic Hwy 30-A—famous for its stunning coastline and laid-back atmosphere. Come and make "B Coastal" your vacation haven!  We'd love to host you (and your dog - pet fee applies). Notes

We love dogs and are happy to provide a dog friendly space for your vacation! However, it's important to note that dogs are not allowed on any of the beaches along 30A. There is a dog beach at Pier Park located at 16303 Front Beach Rd. Panama City Beach where Fido can meet some new friends and enjoy our gorgeous Emerald Coast! Please note that county quiet hours begin at 10pm.

Summary

Escape to this delightful cottage located in the highly sought-after Magnolia Cottages by the Sea just a 2-minute walk from deeded beach access! This cozy 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom cottage features a fully equipped kitchen, a private screened porch, and comfortable accommodations for up to 8 guests. Enjoy access to a community pool, complimentary bikes, and the best of 30A, including Alys Beach and Rosemary Beach. Whether you're lounging by the shore, exploring charming local shops, or biking along scenic trails, this home promises the ultimate coastal getaway.

Space

Welcome to The Story House. Walk inside and enjoy the ambience of a place dedicated to a love for stories, and create your own family vacation tale in this beautiful beach house! This house finds its home in Magnolia Cottages by the Sea, a cozy community on 30a, close to unique shopping, restaurants, several state parks, the 18 mile Timpoochee bike/walk trail,  and a very short bike ride to Aly’s, Rosemary, and Seacrest Beaches. The open living/dining area provides space for relaxing in comfort and style. Step out and you'll find a private screened porch surrounded by Florida palms and brush. There are two porch swings for your children's joy or an afternoon with a friend or a book. A table and chair set is ready for a lunch outside in with hints of sea air. The triple bunkbed is just where your kids will want to to rest after a day of fun in the ocean waves, with an adjoining carpeted space for play or a movie. The master bedroom with ensuite bath is a peaceful retreat for parents with a canopy bed framed by a wall of coconut leaves. A third bedroom offers another queen bed with ensuite bath, with an additional bed by way of a pull-out sofa in the living room. Throughout the house you will find watercolors of favorite childhood stories by artist Elizabeth Wade (calling friends of Narnia!). You can easily prepare family meals in the fully equipped kitchen, then enjoy them at the breakfast bar or gather around the dining table for memorable family dinners. With deeded beach access a 2 minute walk from your door, you can have every day bathed in sun and surf on the lovely white shores of the Gulf Coast. You are invited to plan your family seaside story at The Story House, Magnolia Cottages by the Sea! Neighborhood

Magnolia Cottages by the Sea is a small, discreet neighborhood tucked away between Seagrove and Alys Beaches. It is close to everything, but still offers the comforts of a beach vacation coupled with a quiet getaway.

Access

Guests can access all of the home and share access to the pool and deeded beach access.

Notes

We love dogs and are happy to provide a dog friendly space for your vacation! However, it's important to note that dogs are not allowed on any of the beaches along 30A. There is a dog beach at Pier Park located at 16303 Front Beach Rd. Panama City Beach where Fido can meet some new friends and enjoy our gorgeous Emerald Coast! We would like everyone to have a wonderful experience while visiting. Please note that county quiet hours begin at 10pm. A Note on Shared Amenities: Please keep in mind that these spaces are operated by the HOA. Because of this, they are subject to seasonal availability—common seasonal closures may include April-May and Sept-Nov—and we can't be held responsible for unexpected closures or repairs. If you are booking specifically for a shared amenity, please feel free to reach out with any questions in advance!

Summary

Escape to the Caspian Blue Carriage House, a stylish retreat in the sought-after Magnolia Cottages by the Sea neighborhood. This cozy 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom getaway offers modern decor, a well-equipped kitchen, and a spacious living area with a queen sleeper sofa. Just steps from the community pool and a short walk to the deeded beach access, this home is perfect for couples or small families. Rent separately or with the main cottage for larger groups.

Space

Escape to the charming 30A Caspian Blue Carriage House, a beautifully designed retreat nestled in the picturesque Magnolia by the Sea neighborhood. Perfect for a relaxing getaway this one-bedroom one-bathroom homes combines modern luxury with coastal charm. Featuring a full kitchen with a designer tiled bar and backsplash, matte black fixtures and custom made glass cabinet knobs and pulls, every detail has been thoughtfully curated for your comfort. The spacious living area includes a comfortable sleeper sofa for additional guests, making it ideal for a small family.  This home also boasts a 10' ceiling, giving the space an open, airy feel. Step outside and take advantage of the community pool, just steps away, or enjoy a day at the beach - both only a short walk or bike ride from your front door. This property is ideal for snowbirds or couples looking for a longer term stay.  Your dog is welcome (under 50# on flea and tick preventative, no puppies). Have a large group? Neighborhood

Magnolia Cottages by the Sea is a small, discreet neighborhood tucked away between Seagrove and Alys Beaches. It is close to everything, but still offers the comforts of a beach vacation coupled with a quiet getaway.

Access

Guests can access all of the carriage home and have access to the community pool as well as deeded beach access. Guests are allowed to utilize 2 of the bikes.

Notes

We love dogs and are happy to provide a dog friendly space for your vacation (fee applies)! However, it's important to note that dogs are not allowed on any of the beaches along 30A. There is a dog beach at Pier Park located at 16303 Front Beach Rd. Panama City Beach where Fido can meet some new friends and enjoy our gorgeous Emerald Coast! We would like everyone to have a wonderful experience while visiting. Please note that county quiet hours begin at 10pm. A Note on Shared Amenities: Please keep in mind that these spaces are operated by the HOA. Because of this, they are subject to seasonal availability—common seasonal closures may include April-May and Sept-Nov—and we can't be held responsible for unexpected closures or repairs. If you are booking specifically for a shared amenity, please feel free to reach out with any questions in advance!

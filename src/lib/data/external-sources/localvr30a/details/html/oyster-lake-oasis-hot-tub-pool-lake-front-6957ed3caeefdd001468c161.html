Summary

Nestled along the shores of coastal dune Oyster Lake, our Santa Rosa Beach vacation rental provides a majestic retreat for your next getaway. Our property boasts 8 spacious bedrooms and a private pool, ensuring comfort for up to 24 guests, including a private carriage house with a separate kitchen for added privacy. Enjoy mornings on the dock, golf cart rides to town or a short walk or to the sand, just 9 minutes away. Experience the best of the pool, lake, and beach at this luxe estate.

Space

Step inside this expansive 4,840-square-foot lakefront estate, where modern luxury meets coastal comfort. The main house features an open-concept living area anchored by a massive 85" Smart TV and a gourmet chef’s kitchen. This well-stocked kitchen is equipped with high-end finishes, a Keurig, a drip coffee maker, and a toaster oven, perfect for preparing large family feasts. With multiple king suites and a massive third-floor bunk room that can sleep a crowd, our property is designed for privacy and ease. For added flexibility, the carriage house serves as a self-contained retreat, featuring two private bedrooms and its own full kitchen, making it ideal for separate families or groups traveling together. The outdoor living space is a private oasis designed for ultimate relaxation and recreation. Transition seamlessly from the house to the sun-drenched patio, where you’ll find a private pool and a hot tub overlooking the serene waters of Oyster Lake. Whether you are taking a refreshing dip or soaking in the spa at sunset, the views of the coastal dune lake provide a breathtaking backdrop. The estate also includes a private wooden dock, offering a peaceful spot for morning coffee or direct water access for a day of lakefront fun. Adventure is at your fingertips with a premium collection of amenities included with your stay. Explore the calm waters of Oyster Lake using the provided kayaks and paddleboards, or take the 6-person golf cart for a quick spin or walk (9 minutes) to the Gulf’s sugar-white sands. If you prefer to travel on two wheels, hop on the provided bikes to cruise the scenic 30A corridor. After a day of exploring Dune Allen and the surrounding beach communities, return to our property and enjoy the convenience of on-site parking for up to 8 vehicles in the large private driveway. Neighborhood

Located in the heart of Santa Rosa Beach, this neighborhood offers a tranquil coastal vibe centered around the natural beauty of Oyster Lake and the Gulf of Mexico. The area is renowned for its sugar-white sand beaches and rare coastal dune lakes. Guests can enjoy a variety of activities including paddleboarding on the lake, cycling along the scenic 30A corridor, or exploring local boutique shops and upscale dining in nearby seaside communities.

Transit

A car is recommended to fully enjoy the attractions and beaches along the 30A corridor. Guests will have access to 6 bikes and a 6-seat golf cart. Our property offers significant on-site parking with space for up to 8 vehicles in the private driveway.

Access

Guests have exclusive access to the entire 8-bedroom estate, including the main house and the carriage house suite with the exception of the garage. This access includes the private pool, the in-pool hot tub, and the private wooden dock. On-site parking for up to 8 vehicles is available in the private driveway.

Notes

Please note that the pool and hot tub are serviced between guests. Please note that the garage is not accessible.

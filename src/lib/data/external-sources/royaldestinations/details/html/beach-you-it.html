Description: 

Beach You To It by Royal Destinations

Location: Seagrove Beach
Address: 119 Hickory Street

Details:
Beach You To It is a beautifully appointed Old Seagrove retreat offering 3,110 square feet of indoor and outdoor living space just steps from the beach. Constructed in 2023, this thoughtfully designed home comfortably accommodates up to 12 guests with five bedrooms and 4.5 baths. Hardwood flooring, high ceilings, carefully selected lighting and artwork, and stunning coastal interiors create an elevated yet comfortable setting for your 30A getaway. Outside, enjoy the private heated saltwater pool, screened porch, screened balcony, and multiple spaces designed for relaxing along the coast. Hickory Street Beach Access is approximately a two-minute walk from the home, while the shops, dining, and attractions of Seaside are less than a mile away. With its beautiful design, private pool, and exceptional Old Seagrove location, Beach You To It provides an ideal setting for families and groups looking to experience the best of 30A.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
-Distance to Beach Access: Approximately 2-Minute Walk to Hickory Street Beach Access
-Distance to Seaside: Approximately 0.7 Mile
-Parking Spaces: (4) in Driveway
-Dining Accommodations: Table Seating for 6, Island Seating for 3
-Coffee Maker Available: Keurig + Drip Coffee Maker
-Private Heated Saltwater Pool: 8’ x 20’*
-Screened Porch
-Screened Balcony
-Outdoor Shower

-Outdoor Gas Grill
*Pool heat is available for an additional $60 (pre-tax) per night and must be arranged prior to arrival.
*Pool heating is not recommended when ambient temperatures fall below 50 degrees, as the heater may be unable to reach the desired temperature.

Please Note:
*On-street parking is not permitted. The property accommodates a maximum of (4) vehicles in the driveway.
*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

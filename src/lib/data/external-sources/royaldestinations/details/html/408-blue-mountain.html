408 Blue Mountain by Royal Destinations

Location: Blue Mountain Beach
Address: 408 Blue Mountain Road Santa Rosa Beach, FL 32459

Details:
Welcome to 408 Blue Mountain, a stunning Gulf front vacation rental located in the heart of Blue Mountain Beach along scenic 30A. This luxury beachfront home offers the ultimate Emerald Coast getaway, featuring direct private beach access, breathtaking panoramic views of the Gulf, and upscale amenities designed for unforgettable stays. Perfect for travelers searching for a high-end 30A vacation rental, this modern coastal retreat boasts a spacious open-concept layout, a large private pool overlooking the sugar-white sand beaches, and a fully equipped outdoor kitchen ideal for entertaining. Expansive balconies provide front-row seats to spectacular sunsets, while the home’s light-filled interiors and elevated coastal design create a relaxing yet refined atmosphere. Whether you're planning a family beach vacation, a luxury group getaway, or a serene escape on Florida’s Emerald Coast, 408 Blue Mountain delivers the best of beachfront living in one of the most sought-after locations on 30A.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
- Gulf front location in Blue Mountain Beach along scenic 30A.
- Private beach access to the sugar-white sands of the Emerald Coast.
- Expansive Gulf views from multiple rooms and two large balconies.
- *Private heated pool (10’ x 30’) overlooking the beach.
- Outdoor kitchen and spacious entertaining area.
- Private in-home elevator for easy access to all levels.
- Ideal for family vacations, group getaways, or luxury beach retreats.
- Prime location near 30A dining, shopping, and local attractions.
- Unforgettable sunsets over the Gulf.
- Parking Spaces: 4 in Driveway, 1 in Garage
- Dining Accommodations: Table Seating for 14, Bar Seating for 8.
- Coffee Maker Available
*Pool heat is available for an additional $330 (pre-tax). The pool heater does require 24 hours to reach the optimal temperature of 83-85 degrees. Please contact Reservations to add this feature prior to your stay.

6 Bedrooms | 6 Full & 2 Half Baths

Second Floor:

- Bedroom 5: King Bed with Shared Bath (Shower/Tub Combo)
- Bunk Room: (2) Full/Full Bunk Beds and (1) Twin/Twin Bunk Beds with Shared Bath (Shower/Tub Combo)
- Kitchenette / Dining Area (Table seating for 4, Bar Seating for 4) / Living Room

*Seasonal: March - October

Please Note:
*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

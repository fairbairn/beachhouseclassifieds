Grayton Pointe by Royal Destinations

Location: Grayton Beach
Address: 159 Defuniak Street

Details:
Grayton Pointe is a luxurious retreat located along Defuniak Street in the heart of historic Grayton Beach. Built in 2018, this thoughtfully designed property includes both a spacious main house and private carriage house, comfortably accommodating up to 8 guests with four bedrooms, four full baths, and two half baths. Beautiful hardwood floors, 10-foot ceilings, premium finishes, and a gourmet kitchen with Wolf, Sub-Zero, and Asko appliances create an elevated setting for your 30A getaway. At the center of the outdoor living space is a private heated pool surrounded by comfortable seating and built-in pool loungers. The covered poolside porch provides additional space for dining and relaxing, while an outdoor shower and built-in natural gas grill make it easy to spend the day outside. The beach and downtown Grayton Beach are approximately a seven-minute stroll from the home, placing the community’s iconic restaurants, galleries, and laid-back coastal atmosphere within easy reach. With its private carriage house, exceptional outdoor living spaces, and prime Grayton Beach location, Grayton Pointe provides an ideal setting for families and groups traveling together.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

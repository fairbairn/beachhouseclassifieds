Description: 

Sunny Day by Royal Destinations

Location: Seagrove Beach
Address: 50 Sugar Sand Lane

Details:
Sunny Day delivers the best of Seagrove Beach living, with the brand new Seagrove Regional Beach Access just a three-minute walk away and beach gear provided, making days on the sand easy and stress-free. Step right out the back door to the community pool (Community pool heated November through New Year’s Day, as well as in March) for the perfect balance of beach time and poolside relaxation. This spacious 2,400+ square-foot home is larger than many nearby homes and is ideal for multiple families traveling together. The bright, open floor plan features hardwood floors and a gourmet kitchen with granite countertops and stainless-steel Bosch appliances, designed for both entertaining and everyday comfort. With five well-appointed bedrooms (including two king suites, two queen suites, and a custom kids’ bunk room), Sunny Day offers room for everyone to unwind. Private covered porches, close proximity to popular dining spots like Café Thirty-A and Angelina’s Pizzeria & Pasta, and an unbeatable location make this home an ideal choice for a relaxed, memorable beach getaway.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Sweet Home: A Note From The Homeowner- The name Sunny Day comes from our grandson, who loved his raisin bran cereal with the smiling sun on the box, often calling it 'Sunny Day'. Seagrove captured the essence of a true beach community—quaint, friendly, and with wide-open beaches. Our home is perfect for family and friends, with 4 bedrooms and a bunk room for 4. A queen bedroom on the first floor is ideal for older family members. We have multiple outdoor spaces to relax, plus a community pool (heated seasonally) just beyond the patio. Two beach accesses are nearby, with a third coming soon.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

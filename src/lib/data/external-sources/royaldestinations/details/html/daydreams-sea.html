Description: 

Daydreams By The Sea by Royal Destinations

Location: Seacrest Beach
Address: 233 Geoff Wilder Lane

Details:
Daydreams by the Sea is a stunning 3-bedroom, 3-bath coastal retreat located just 100 yards from the breathtaking Gulf of Mexico. This 2,142-square-foot home features newly remodeled bathrooms and updated flooring, combining modern comfort with timeless beachside charm. Nestled within the exclusive gated community of Waters Edge, adjacent to the luxury enclave of Rosemary Beach, this property offers both privacy and convenience. Enjoy easy access to the best of Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Alys Beach, all just a short walk or bike ride away. Spend your days lounging by the beautiful private pool, strolling to your deeded beach access, or exploring the nearby shops, restaurants, and local markets. Whether you’re seeking relaxation or adventure, Daydreams by the Sea is the perfect coastal getaway.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
-Distance to Beach Access: 50 Yards
-Parking Spaces: 2 in Driveway
-Dining Accommodations: Table Seating for 5, Bar Seating for 4
-Coffee Maker Available
-Gas Grill Available (Propane Provided)
-*Private Heated Pool: 15’ x 10’
-Available For Winter Monthly Rentals- Contact our team with any questions regarding pricing for monthly winter rentals.
*Pool heat is available for an additional $310 (pre-tax).
*The pool heater does require 24 hours to reach the optimal temperature of 83-85 degrees.
*Please contact Reservations to add this feature prior to your stay.

Home Configuration + Sleeping Arrangements:
Sleeps: 6
3 Bedrooms / 3 Bathrooms

First Floor:
-Bedroom 1: King Bed with Private Bath (Shower/Tub Combo)
-Bedroom 2 (Master): King Bed with Private Bath (Shower)

Second Floor:
-Bedroom 3: King Bed with Shared Bath (Shower)
-Kitchen / Living Area / Dining Area
-Laundry Room

Amenities Include:
-4 Adult Bikes
-1 Beach Service Set*
*Seasonal: March – November

Please Note:
*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

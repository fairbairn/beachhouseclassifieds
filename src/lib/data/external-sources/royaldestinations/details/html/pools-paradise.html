Pool’s Paradise by Royal Destinations

Location: Seacrest Beach
Address: 116 Seacrest Beach Boulevard East

Details:
If you’re searching for an exclusive oasis on 30A, look no further than Pool’s Paradise. Located in the private, gated community of Seacrest Beach, enjoy the proximity of great shopping and dining at Rosemary Beach and Alys while still enjoying the exclusivity of Seacrest Beach. Take full advantage of the incredible Florida weather from the comfort of your outdoor oasis. Featuring a large 25’ x 13’ pool and a spacious patio for entertaining, Pool’s Paradise is sure to be a destination in itself. With an easy access to the neighborhood green space just beyond the pool, children can play catch or run around all while they are still visible to those enjoying the pool area. Step inside and be greeted by a spacious living room, open kitchen, and dining room. Located just off the kitchen and dining room is your escape to the private backyard oasis through the first-floor covered balcony. The first floor offers a king-sized bedroom with adjoining bath while the second-floor master king suite offers a private balcony overlooking the pool and neighborhood green space. Also, enjoy the walk-in shower and jacuzzi tub with his and her vanities. Also on the second floor is the kid’s bunk bedroom and a king guest suite with shared bath and laundry room. For those looking for a quiet place to escape, tucked away on the third floor is a built-in bunk area.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

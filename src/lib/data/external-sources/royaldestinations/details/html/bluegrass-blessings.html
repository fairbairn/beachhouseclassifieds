Bluegrass Blessings by Royal Destinations

Location: Rosemary Beach
Address: 277 Water Street, Rosemary Beach, FL 32461

Details:
Experience your dream 30A getaway at Bluegrass Blessings! Located in the heart of Rosemary Beach, this beautifully remodeled 30A vacation rental combines luxury and charm. Just a 3-minute walk to the Emerald Coast's pristine beaches and 3 minutes to the vibrant Rosemary town square, guests can enjoy easy access to boutique shops, top-rated restaurants, and cozy coffee spots. This Rosemary Beach vacation rental is your perfect blend of relaxation and adventure. Having completed a full renovation in 2024, Bluegrass Blessings now offers an incredible outdoor sanctuary, perfect for fully embracing the 30A lifestyle. This 30A luxury rental now boasts an incredible outdoor sanctuary. Those searching for 30A vacation rentals with private pools will fall in love with the brand-new splash pool which is sure to delight kids and adults alike. The brand-new outdoor kitchen and bar offer the perfect setup for entertainment. Inside, this 30A rental is an oasis of comfort, featuring an en-suite bathroom in each of the four bedrooms, a state-of-the-art kitchen, and a serene outdoor dining area. Every detail has been thoughtfully designed to create a relaxing and unforgettable 30A experience.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
- Distance To Beach Access: 0.1 Miles (3-Minute Walk)
- 3-Minute Walk to Rosemary Town Center (Restaurants, Shopping, Activities)
- *Private Heated Splash Pool
- Outdoor Kitchen and Bar
- Access To Private Community Beach Access, 4 Neighborhood Pools (Barbados Pool, Cabana Pool, Coquina Pool, and Sky Pool), Fitness Center, and Racquet Club (Fees Apply for Fitness Center and Racquet Club)
- State-Of-The-Art Kitchen
- Deck off the kitchen and dining area for outdoor dining.
- Parking Spaces: 2
- Adjacent to Timpoochee Trail
*Pool Heat is available for an additional $330 (pre-tax).
*The pool heater does require 24 hours to reach the optimal temperature of 83-85 degrees.
*Please contact Reservations to add this feature prior to your stay.

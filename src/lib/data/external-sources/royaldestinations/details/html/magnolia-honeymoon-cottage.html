Magnolia Honeymoon Cottage by Royal Destinations

Location: Seaside
Address: 2334 East County Highway 30A

Details:
Magnolia Honeymoon Cottage is a beautifully remodeled Gulf-front retreat designed exclusively for two adults seeking a romantic escape in the heart of Seaside. Inspired by Thomas Jefferson’s Monticello and tucked privately behind the dunes, this iconic Honeymoon Cottage offers approximately 1,032 square feet of indoor and outdoor living space. Soft neutral tones, hardwood floors, elegant finishes, and thoughtfully designed spaces create a timeless and tranquil setting for a honeymoon, anniversary, or special getaway. The first floor features a spacious King suite with a marble walk-in shower and direct access to a private dune-level porch with a soaking tub. Upstairs, the beautifully appointed living area showcases panoramic Gulf views alongside a charming kitchen and bistro dining for two. Step onto the covered Gulf-front porch to enjoy al fresco dining, morning coffee, evening cocktails, or the swinging bed overlooking the Gulf of Mexico. Guests also enjoy access to Seaside community amenities and are within walking distance of the restaurants, boutiques, galleries, and attractions surrounding Seaside’s Town Center.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Please Note:
*Magnolia Honeymoon Cottage accommodates a maximum of (2) adults ages 25 and older. Infants and children are not permitted.
*Golf carts and on-street parking are prohibited within Seaside.
*(2) Seaside amenity wristbands and (1) parking pass are provided for guest use. Missing wristbands or parking passes are subject to a replacement fee of $150 plus tax per item.
*Swim and Fitness passes are automatically included with the reservation. If you do not wish to use the community pools or fitness center, please contact our team immediately after booking regarding removal of this charge. Once removed, the amenity access cannot be added back to the reservation.
*Daily beach service is available seasonally from March through November.
*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

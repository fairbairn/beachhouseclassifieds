Description: 

Crossings at WaterSound B105 by Royal Destinations

Location: WaterSound Bridges
Address: 337 Bridge Lane, Unit B105

**Please Note: Nearby construction is underway. Exterior work will begin on this building in December 2025, with an expected completion in March 2026. For additional details please contact our reservations team at.

Details:
Escape to unmatched tranquility at WaterSound Crossings B105, where luxury meets one of the most secluded beachfronts on 30A. Tucked away on the eastern edge of WaterSound, this stunning Gulf front condo offers exclusive access to a stretch of sand so private, the nearest public beach access points are over a mile away in both directions. Step inside to panoramic Gulf views that will take your breath away. The beautifully renovated interiors feature an open-concept layout designed for effortless entertaining, with spacious living and dining areas that flow onto expansive porches overlooking the pool and shoreline. Every detail of this condo exudes comfort, elegance, and high-end coastal style. If you're looking for a luxurious 30A getaway with unparalleled privacy and views, WaterSound Crossings B105 is your dream escape.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
-Distance to Private Community Beach Access: 100 yards (The nearest public beach access is over a mile in either direction, so you can enjoy an exceptionally quiet and uncrowded stretch of sand.)
-Parking Spaces: 1 Space in Driveway, 1 in Parking lot
-Dining Accommodations: Dining Table Seating for 8, Kitchen Seating for 4
-Regular Drip Coffee Maker Available (Standard Filter)
-Community Grill Available by Pool

WaterSound Community Highlights:
-Over 250 Acre Gated Beach Community on the Gulf of Mexico between Seaside and Rosemary Beach
-(2) Community Pools (Dunesider Pool in Main Gate and Bridges Pool within the Bridges Gate)
-Kayak Rentals
-Parks, Walking Trails and Meandering Boardwalks
-Bike to Alys, Seacrest and Rosemary Beach
-A short walk to The Big Chill
*Please Note: Access to the WaterSound Beach Club is not included.
*WaterSound does not allow golf cart rentals from outside vendors.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

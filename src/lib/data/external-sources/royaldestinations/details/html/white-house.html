Description: 

The White House by Royal Destinations

Location: Seagrove Beach
Address: 2635 East County Highway 30A

Details:
Welcome to The White House, where beach days begin right across the street! Location doesn’t get better than this. The White House sits directly across 30A from the beach access, making it effortless to dip your toes in the sugar-white sand or catch a sunset over the Gulf whenever you please. Light, fresh, and beautifully designed, this charming home features an open floor plan perfect for gathering with family and friends. Upstairs, the master suite offers a romantic retreat complete with a private balcony overlooking the emerald waters — the perfect spot for morning coffee or evening stargazing. Relax on the screened front porch, take a refreshing swim in the private backyard pool, or enjoy the rare grassy yard, perfect for a casual game of croquet or frisbee. From biking along Scenic 30A to walking to nearby restaurants and shops, you’ll experience everything this coveted stretch of coast has to offer all from a home that truly has it all. Your perfect 30A getaway starts here!

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

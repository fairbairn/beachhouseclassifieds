Billiken Bungalow by Royal Destinations

Location: Seagrove Beach
Address: 320 Forest Street

Details:
Tucked away in Old Seagrove and just steps from Seaside, Billiken Bungalow is a beautifully appointed retreat designed with families and multi-generational groups in mind. Offering approximately 2,820 square feet of interior living space, this spacious home accommodates up to 10 guests with four bedrooms and 4.5 baths. Generously sized bedrooms, separate living areas for adults and children, a well-equipped kitchen, and inviting indoor and outdoor gathering spaces make it easy for everyone to relax and enjoy time together. Outside, a large screened porch overlooks the private heated pool, creating the perfect setting for unwinding after a day at the beach. The Dogwood/Thyme Beach Access is just a leisurely walk away, while Seaside is approximately 100 yards from the home, putting its shopping, dining, and town center within easy reach. With its private pool, spacious layout, and exceptional Old Seagrove location, Billiken Bungalow offers a wonderful setting for experiencing the best of 30A.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Please Note:
*On-street parking is not permitted. The property accommodates a maximum of (4) vehicles in the driveway.
*The living room fireplace is decorative only and is not available for guest use.
*Guests do not have access to the poolside storage cottage.
*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

Description: 

Modern Magnolia of Grayton by Royal Destinations

Location: Grayton Beach
Address: 138 Magnolia St, Santa Rosa Beach, FL 32459

Details:
Just steps from the sugar-white sands of Grayton Beach, Modern Magnolia of Grayton delivers the best of coastal living with a rare large private pool and unbeatable location. One of the most sought-after 30A vacation rentals with private pools, this stunning home is a standout among luxury 30A rentals, offering effortless access to the beach, boutique shops, and local dining while maintaining a peaceful, private setting. Surrounded by towering magnolias and Gulf breezes, the home blends modern elegance with the laid-back charm that defines the best Grayton Beach rentals. Inside, soaring beamed ceilings, shiplap walls, and designer furnishings create a refined yet inviting atmosphere. Every bedroom features en-suite accommodations, providing comfort and privacy for families and groups alike. The gourmet kitchen boasts marble countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a gas range—perfect for preparing fresh local seafood or relaxed family meals. Outdoors, enjoy one of South Walton’s largest private pools with generous lounge seating, an outdoor TV, surround sound, and a built-in gas grill, ideal for unforgettable evenings under the stars. As one of the premier 30A pet friendly rentals, Modern Magnolia of Grayton offers luxury, convenience, and authentic 30A charm in one exceptional coastal retreat.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Sweet Home: A Note From The Homeowner- I love how my home blends modern amenities with old Florida charm. The open floor plan is perfect for families and groups, and you’ll find original shiplap and wooden floors throughout. The backyard is spacious and includes a saltwater pool and fire pit for late night chats and roasting s'mores! I feel instantly relaxed when I walk in, and I hope my guests do too.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

After Dune Delight by Royal Destinations

Location: Grayton Beach
Address: 215 Garfield Street

Details:
Welcome to After Dune Delight, a stunning four-story coastal retreat designed for unforgettable gatherings along 30A. With six spacious bedrooms, all-new bedding and décor, and room to comfortably accommodate up to 22 guests, this home offers the perfect blend of style, comfort, and space. Enjoy stellar views from expansive wrap-around balconies, where four sets of glass doors open to reveal breathtaking vistas of Western Lake and the Gulf of Mexico. The private pool, generous living area, and multiple outdoor spaces create an ideal setting for relaxation and entertaining. Perfectly situated within view of the beach access and just across the street from Grayton Beach’s beloved landmark, The Red Bar, this home places you in the heart of one of 30A’s most iconic destinations. Whether you’re planning a family reunion, group getaway, wedding, or special event, After Dune Delight delivers the ultimate Grayton Beach experience—where coastal luxury meets laid-back charm.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Sweet Home: A Note From The Homeowner- We toured nearly 30 homes along 30A, Sandestin, and Destin, but After Dune Delight stood out instantly—it had everything we wanted: wraparound porches, stunning views of the Gulf and Western Lake, and that breezy, coastal charm. Just a block from the beach and across from the iconic Red Bar, it's like a front-row seat to all things Grayton—including Tribe Kelley concerts. With plenty of space, parking, and the perfect layout for hosting, it felt made for memory-making. We love the wraparound porches most—they're where mornings, sunsets, and music-filled evenings happen. Grayton Beach itself adds to the magic with its quirky, small-town vibe, barefoot bike rides, and unbeatable walkability. Adding an elevator made the home even better, giving easy access to all four floors and those top-level views. It’s the heart of 30A with its own soul—laid-back, welcoming, and unforgettable.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

The home was very nice with room to spread out and sprawling porches with a view of the ocean and the lake or simply enjoy the fun atmosphere of Grayton. The house sits right in the heart of Grayton and is an easy walk to the beach even with 4 small children. The boardwalk to the beach is flat with no stairs and was close enough to run back to the house for lunch or a bathroom break easily. The kids enjoyed playing in the shallows/estuary. It was fascinating watching the crystal clear water turn into sweet tea throughout the week. We enjoyed having the option of the pool although it was a little cool because we were there in early May. The couples enjoyed walking to Chiringo's for a game of corn hole or grabbing a Hotz Coffee during nap times for the kids. The area is golf cart friendly which the grandkids loved and is a nice area for walking, running, or biking as well. Note to families with young children, the staircases are wide, so if your babies require a gate, a standard baby gate will not work.

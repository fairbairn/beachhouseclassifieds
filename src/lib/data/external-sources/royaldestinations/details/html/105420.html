143 Forest Street by Royal Destinations

Location: Seagrove Beach
Address: 143 Forest Street

Details:
Welcome to 143 Forest Street—your perfect 30A getaway! This beautifully designed five-bedroom home blends luxury and coastal charm with plenty of space for the whole family. Located in historic Old Seagrove, you’re just 530 yards (a one-minute bike ride) from the beach and a short stroll to Seaside’s shops and dining. Enjoy dining space for six, bar seating for three, and outdoor seating for ten, plus a courtyard with propane grill and spacious decks and porches for relaxing in the coastal breeze. Cool off in the private pool (8’ x 20’) or unwind on the sundeck—perfect for lazy afternoons in the sun. With parking for two cars and all the comforts of home, every detail has been thoughtfully designed for your stay.

Let the Royal Destinations team help you plan less and experience more with hand-tailored service and unforgettable 30A experiences. Relaxation awaits at 143 Forest Street!

Home Highlights:
-Distance to Beach Access: 530 Yards (1 Minute Bike Ride)
-Parking Spaces: 2 in Driveway
-Dining Accommodations: Table for 6, Bar Seating for 3 and Outdoor Seating for 10
-Coffee Maker Available
-Entertaining Courtyard with Propane Grill
-Plentiful Deck and Porches
-Private Heated Pool: 8’ x 20’
*Pool Heat is available for an additional $330 (pre-tax).
*The pool heater does require 24 hours to reach the optimal temperature of 83-85 degrees.
*Please contact Reservations to add this feature prior to your stay.

Please Note:
*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

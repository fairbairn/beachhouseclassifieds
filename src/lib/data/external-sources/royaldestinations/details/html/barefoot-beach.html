Description: 

Barefoot at the Beach by Royal Destinations (Previously known as Moore Relaxing)

Location: Watersound Beach
Address: 41 N Watch Tower Lane

Details:
Welcome to Barefoot at the Beach, a beautifully designed coastal retreat located in the exclusive community of WaterSound. Newly furnished and thoughtfully decorated, this stunning home combines luxury, comfort, and coastal charm, creating the perfect backdrop for making memories that last a lifetime. Accommodating up to 14 guests comfortably, the home offers an open and inviting layout with plenty of room for everyone to gather and unwind. The main floor opens into a bright, welcoming foyer, leading to a king bedroom with private bath and sophisticated coastal décor. The great room features a beautiful stone fireplace, while the adjoining gathering area, gourmet kitchen, and dining spaces flow seamlessly together—ideal for entertaining and family meals. Step outside to enjoy the screened porch, spacious deck, and outdoor shower, or follow the private boardwalk just beyond the back door to reach the beach or community pool. Upstairs, the family media room provides the perfect space to watch movies, play games, or simply relax after a day in the sun. Whether you’re looking for peaceful mornings, afternoons by the shore, or cozy evenings with loved ones, Barefoot at the Beach is your ideal 30A getaway.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
-Distance to Private Beach Access: 50 Yards (1 Minute Walk)
-Parking Spaces: 2 Spaces in Driveway
-Dining Accommodations: Table Seating for 8, Bar Seating for 5
-Coffee Maker Available
-Gas Grill Available (Propane Provided)

WaterSound Community Highlights:
-Over 250 Acre Gated Beach Community on the Gulf of Mexico between Seaside and Rosemary Beach
-(2) Community Pools (Dunesider Pool in Main Gate and Bridges Pool within the Bridges Gate)
-Kayak Rentals
-Parks, Walking Trails and Meandering Boardwalks
-Bike to Alys, Seacrest and Rosemary Beach
-A short walk to The Big Chill (formerly The Hub)
*Please Note: Access to the WaterSound Beach Club is not included. WaterSound does not allow golf cart rentals from outside vendors.

Home Configuration + Sleeping Arrangements:

Second Floor:
-Bedroom 2 (Master): King with Newly Remodeled Private Bath (Soaking Tub with Shower), Smart TV, Private Porch
-Bedroom 3: Queen Bed with Private Bath (Shower/Tub Combo)

-Bedroom 5: Two Twin/Twin Bunks with Shared Bath (Shower)

Please Note:
Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Home Sweet Home: A Note From The Homeowner- Location, location, location! We’d vacationed on 30A for years but had never explored WaterSound. After discovering the incredible location at a price we couldn’t believe, we bought the house without even seeing it in person. When we finally did, we were thrilled to find a spacious, well-maintained home just six houses from the beach—perfect for family and friends. Barefoot at the Beach blends timeless elegance with comfort. We wanted a home we could truly live in, not one that felt like a museum, and this house strikes that perfect balance. WaterSound is a hidden gem—gated, serene, and not crowded like other communities. The private beach, with plenty of space even in peak season, ensures no stress over finding a spot. The home’s layout is ideal for large families, with multiple living spaces, plus five bedrooms. We recently renovated the master bath with a steam shower and soaking tub, perfect after a day in the sun. We can’t wait to call this place home full-time.

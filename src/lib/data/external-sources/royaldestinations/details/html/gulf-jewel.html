Gulf Jewel by Royal Destinations

Location: Blue Mountain Beach
Address: 2226 S County Highway 83

Details:
With Gulf views and a great location just steps from the beach access, the 30A vacation rental ‘Gulf Jewel’ is sure to please! This Blue Mountain vacation rental has been thoughtfully designed with comfort and relaxation in mind. Guests looking for 30A vacation rentals with private pools are sure to enjoy the one Gulf Jewel offers, perfect for a relaxing dip after a day at the nearby beach. The location of this Blue Mountain vacation rental is walkable to delicious restaurants and sweet treats such as Blue Mabel and Blue Mountain Beach Creamery, markets, and shopping. With 12-foot ceilings, travertine and hardwood floors, high-end furnishings, outdoor dining/patio area, and a private pool, guests of this 30A vacation rental are sure to make this their new home-away-from-home!

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
-Distance to Beach Access: 50 Steps to the Beach (1 Minute Walk)
-Parking Spaces: 2 Spaces in Driveway
-Dining Accommodations: Table Seating for 8, Bar Seating for 3
-Coffee Maker
-Propane Grill
- Gulf Views
-Walkability to restaurants such as Red Fish Taco, sweet treats at Blue Mountain Creamery, shopping, and markets!
-Private Heated Pool:
*Pool heat is available for an additional $180 (pre-tax)
*The pool heater does require 24 hours to reach the optimal temperature of 83-85 degrees.
*Please contact Reservations to add this feature prior to your stay.

Please Note:

*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Home Sweet Home: A Note From The Homeowner- Welcome to Gulf Jewel, our cherished beach house. From the moment we stepped inside, we knew it was the one. The cozy, elegant feel and abundant natural light made it instantly feel like home. We love morning walks on the beach, just a minute away, followed by a stop at The Shake Shop for coffee and donuts. The nearby Blue Mountain Beach Creamery serves delicious homemade ice creams and sorbets, a favorite for all ages. On warm days, we relax by our private pool with a good book or a cocktail. We often bring our parents, who especially appreciate the downstairs master bedroom with no stairs. Gulf Jewel has a unique charm, with one-of-a-kind furniture, light fixtures, and handpicked art that add to its beauty.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

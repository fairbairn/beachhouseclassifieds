Description: 

Caribbean Breeze by Royal Destinations

Location: Seacrest Beach
Address: 34 Clareon Drive

Details:
Caribbean Breeze is a bright, updated home just 50 yards from the beach access with 8 bedrooms, 7 baths, and comfortably sleeps 23. Guests can enjoy the private heated saltwater pool and gulf views. Walk straight out the front door to a quiet, private beach access, allowing you to avoid the traffic and crowds with plenty of room to set up your umbrella right next to the water - even in the summer! Two separate living areas (one on the ground floor) make the home great for large families. The ground floor also includes a king bed master suite with a new private bathroom (shower) and kitchenette, perfect for guests who prefer to avoid stairs. Enjoy the wonderful Gulf views from the expansive covered porches and living areas. The house comes with internet-connected TV’s, an Apple TV, games and puzzles, and DVD players. When you are not at the beach, you can relax and enjoy the beautifully landscaped yard that contains a large 24 foot by 12 foot newly paved and refinished, heated, saltwater swimming pool. The large, spacious lawn now also includes a real putting green making it the perfect place to play bocce ball or other lawn games or to work on your golf game. There’s even a basketball goal in the driveway for the kids and cornhole next to the swimming pool.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

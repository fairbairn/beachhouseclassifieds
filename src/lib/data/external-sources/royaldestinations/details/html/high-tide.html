High Tide by Royal Destinations

Location: Grayton Beach
Address: 46 Gulf Ridge Drive

Details:
High Tide is a spacious coastal retreat offering beautiful Gulf views and an exceptional location within the private Gulf Trace neighborhood of Grayton Beach. Surrounded by Grayton Beach State Park and the Gulf of Mexico, this inviting home offers approximately 2,932 square feet of interior living space and comfortably accommodates up to 10 guests with five bedrooms and four baths. Two separate living areas, a thoughtfully equipped kitchen, and generous indoor and outdoor gathering spaces provide plenty of room for families and groups to enjoy time together. Outside, relax beside the private heated pool or enjoy the Gulf breeze from the home’s screened and covered porches. Private neighborhood beach access is approximately 200 feet away, providing convenient access to one of the most peaceful stretches of shoreline along 30A. With Gulf views, two private neighborhood beach accesses, and the natural beauty of Grayton Beach State Park surrounding the community, High Tide offers an ideal setting for a relaxing coastal getaway.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Please Note:
*LSVs and on-street parking are strictly prohibited within the Gulf Trace neighborhood.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

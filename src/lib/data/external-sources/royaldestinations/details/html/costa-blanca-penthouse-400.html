Costa Blanca Penthouse 400 by Royal Destinations
*Available exclusively for 30-night stays!

Location: Dune Allen Beach
Address: 4463 W County Highway 30A, Santa Rosa Beach, FL

Details:
This is your chance to live 30A differently! Costa Blanca Penthouse 400 is available exclusively for 30-night stays - a rare opportunity to trade the typical weeklong trip for a full month of Gulf front living. Take full advantage of extended time to truly unwind, settle in, and experience the coast the way it was meant to be enjoyed.

Gulf front uninterrupted views take center stage in this fourth-floor penthouse. Welcome to Costa Blanca, an exclusive community of just 14 luxury residences along Scenic Highway 30A. Penthouse 400 captures the beauty of emerald waters and stunning sunsets from every angle. Expansive glass walls and lofty ceilings invite the horizon inside, while a generous private balcony offers a front-row view of sugar-white sands and the gentle surf.

Spanning over 2,800 square feet, this four-bedroom, four-bath residence combines spaciousness with sophistication, featuring finishes that exude timeless coastal charm. Driftwood-inspired tile flooring flows throughout the light-filled interiors, which include a chef's kitchen equipped with Wolf and Sub-Zero appliances, quartz countertops, and custom cabinetry. Designed for effortless gatherings, the open floor plan seamlessly integrates kitchen, dining, and living areas, providing the comforts of a private home.

Penthouse 400 comes with resort-style amenities, including a gulf-front pool and spa, a fitness studio, and over 200 feet of pristine beach. Just across 30A, Gulf Place offers convenient access to dining, shopping, and entertainment, enhancing the vibrant yet tranquil atmosphere of the location. With its exclusivity, elegance, and breathtaking gulf views, this residence epitomizes the ultimate in 30A living.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

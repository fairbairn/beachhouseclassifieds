Description: 

Serendipity by Royal Destinations

Location: Dune Allen Beach
Address: 22 Daybreak Court

Details:
Serendipity is a welcoming coastal home located within the gated Sunrise Beach community near Gulf Place. Offering 2,607 square feet of indoor and outdoor living space, this retreat comfortably accommodates up to 8 guests with three bedrooms and three full baths. Multiple porches offer Gulf or pool views, while the home's open living, dining, and kitchen areas provide a comfortable setting for families to gather and enjoy their time along 30A. Outside, guests can relax beside the private fenced pool or take the neighborhood boardwalk to the beach for a day along the Gulf. Daily beach service is included for added convenience. The third-floor tower provides another special place to unwind, with daybed seating, a bistro table, and an open balcony offering Gulf views. Serendipity is also a short walk from Gulf Place, putting restaurants, shopping, galleries, and local entertainment within easy reach.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
-Distance to Beach Access: Private Neighborhood Boardwalk to Beach
-Distance to Gulf Place: Approximately 3/10 Mile
-Parking Spaces: (3) in Driveway
-Dining Accommodations: Table Seating for 6, Island Seating for 4, Tower Bistro Seating for 2
-Coffee Maker Available: Keurig and Drip Coffee Maker
-Private Fenced Heated Pool*
-Daily Beach Service Included: (4) Chairs and (2) Umbrellas
-Gated Community
-Gulf and Pool Views
-Multiple Outdoor Porches
-Third-Floor Tower with Gulf-View Balcony
-Outdoor Charcoal Grill
-Outdoor Shower
*Pool heat is available for an additional $60 (pre-tax) per day from March 1 through May 15 only.
*Pool heating must be arranged prior to arrival.

Please Note:
*Parking is limited to a maximum of (3) vehicles in the driveway. On-street parking is prohibited within the Sunrise Beach community.
*LSVs are permitted but must be parked in the driveway and count as one vehicle toward the three-vehicle parking maximum.
*Pool heat is available only from March 1 through May 15 and must be arranged in advance.
*The balcony overlooking the pool is not accessible to guests.
*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

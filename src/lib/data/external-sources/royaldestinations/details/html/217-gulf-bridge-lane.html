Description: 

217 Gulf Bridge Lane by Royal Destinations

Location: WaterSound Beach-The Peninsula
Address: 217 Gulf Bridge Lane, WaterSound, FL 32461

Details:
Gulf and lake views surround this timeless retreat in the exclusive, gated community of WaterSound Beach. Spanning nearly 2,800 square feet, 217 Gulf Bridge Lane is a coastal masterpiece showcasing high-end finishes and thoughtful design throughout. Perfectly positioned lakefront beside a community dock, the home offers both beauty and convenience, with ample parking and an outdoor shower—ideal after a day in the emerald waters of the Gulf. Designed for relaxation and recreation, guests enjoy full access to WaterSound Beach’s premier amenities, including a fitness center, multiple community pools, lush green spaces, children’s parks, seven miles of scenic boardwalks and walking paths, and one mile of pristine sugar-white beaches. Make 217 Gulf Bridge Lane the destination for your next unforgettable 30A escape—where every view inspires and every detail delights.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
-Lake Front and Gulf Views
-Parking Spaces: 3 in Driveway
-Dining Accommodations: Table for 8 / Bar Seating for 4 / Kitchen Seating 2
-Coffee Maker and Keurig Available
-(4) Adult Bikes

WaterSound Community Highlights:
-Over 250 Acre Gated Beach Community on the Gulf of Mexico between Seaside and Rosemary Beach
-(2) Community Pools (Dunesider Pool in Main Gate and Bridges Pool within the Bridges Gate)
-Kayak Rentals
-Parks, Walking Trails and Meandering Boardwalks
-Bike to Alys, Seacrest and Rosemary Beach
-A short walk to The Big Chill (formerly The Hub)
*Please Note: Access to the WaterSound Beach Club is not included. WaterSound does not allow golf cart rentals.

Home Configuration + Sleeping Arrangements:

-Half Bath

Second Floor:
-Bedroom 2 (En Suite): King Bed with Private Bath
-Bedroom 3 (En Suite): Queen Bed, Two Twin Beds, Private Bath
-Large Balcony with Gulf and Lakefront Views
-Laundry Room

Please Note:
*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

Description: 

Blissful Beach by Royal Destinations

Location: Grayton Beach
Address: 215 Barton’s Way West

Details:
This newly redecorated home features a relaxed, casual coastal design and a spacious family friendly layout that will make your cares slip away. Sit a spell on the oversized front porch, or join others in the large family room equipped with three couches and game table space fit for entertaining a crowd. A farm style table and kitchen with all the amenities round out a living space for intimate gatherings or multi-family meals. The Preserve at Grayton Beach is a quiet, gated community complete with two pools (one heated) and tennis courts. This home features ample parking, an outdoor grill and outdoor shower with mudroom entry. Come and enjoy a relaxing piece of coastal living!

A LSV (Low Speed Vehicle) is available upon request for an additional $600 (plus tax)!

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
-Distance to Public Beach Access: 0.8 Miles (5 Minute Bike Ride)
-Parking Spaces: 4 Spaces in Driveway
-Dining Accommodations: Table Seating for 8, Bar Seating for 2
-Coffee Maker Available
-Propane Grill Available (Propane Provided)
-Access to 2 Community Pools
*Larger pool located at the West end of the community is only heated during Thanksgiving and Christmas.
-Community Tennis Court Access
-*LSV (Low Speed Vehicle) Available (6-Seater)
*Available for an Additional Fee of $600 (plus tax)
*Please Contact Reservations to add this feature prior to your stay.

Please Note:
-Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Neighborhood Guidelines-
The Preserve at Grayton is a HOA governed, gated community featuring deeded beach access, community pools, pickleball courts. Please note the following important guidelines:
- Space for parking 4 vehicles is provided; 2 on the drive and 2 on the shoulder of the street. No boats, trailers, or RVs are permitted overnight. Failure to comply may result in fines. Rental golf carts are not permitted. The homeowner offers a 6-seater golf cart for an additional rate.
- Community quiet hours are from 10 pm - 8 am daily.
- All garbage must be placed in a fully closed and fully secured trash receptacle due to wildlife in the area. Trash pick up is on Monday and Thursday and trash should be put out after 5pm the day prior and brought in by noon the following day. Failure to comply may result in fines.
- Do not hang towels on the railings of the porch. Failure to comply may result in fines. An outdoor shower and space for storage of beach gear is available at the back entry of the home.
- This home features exterior cameras for security.
- In addition to the parking for 4 vehicles, the homeowner stores a vehicle at the property that will not interfere with your stay.

Description: 

Sea-Rest by Royal Destinations

Location: Seacrest Beach 30A
Address: 131 Clareon Drive, Inlet Beach, FL 32461

Details:
Searching for a 30A vacation rental for your next getaway? Look no further than Sea-Rest! Guests of this 30A vacation rental have an abundance of activities to choose from all within close proximity of the home. Whether you're looking to spend time at the beach access (just 350 yards from the home), enjoy the large private pool, or take a tour of all of the delicious dining options Seacrest Beach and the neighboring communities offer, all are possible within a short walking distance or even shorter ride on one of the complimentary bikes. Sea-Rest comfortably sleeps 13 with 4 bedrooms and 4 bathrooms spread out over 2 floors. Guests searching for 30a vacation rentals with private pools are sure to fall in love with Sea-Rest! Don't forget your four-legged family members as this home is pet-friendly!

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
-Distance to Beach Access: 350 Yards
-Parking Spaces: 4
-Dining Accommodations: Table for 8 and Bar Seating for 5
-Coffee Maker Available
-Propane Grill
-Private Heated Pool
-Pet-Friendly With Approval
*Pool Heat is available for an additional $330 (pre-tax).
*The pool heater does require 24 hours to reach the optimal temperature of 83-85 degrees.
*Please contact Reservations to add this feature prior to your stay.
*Pet friendly for an additional cost of $350 per pet (pre-tax). Please contact reservations to add this feature prior to your stay.

Home Configuration + Sleeping Arrangements:

4 Beds / 4 Baths

First Floor:

-Kitchen / Dining Area / Living Room

Second Floor:
-Bedroom 2 (Master): King Bed with Private Bath (Soaking Tub and Shower)
-Bedroom 3: King Bed with Private Bath (Shower/Tub Combo); Twin Bed
-Bedroom 4: (2) Twin XL/Queen Bunk Beds with Private Bath (Shower/Tub Combo)
- Laundry

-4 Adult Bikes

Please Note:
*Nearby construction is underway.
*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

Kevin's Heaven by Royal Destinations

Location: Grayton Beach
Address: 268 Garfield Street

Details:
If you're searching for a 30A vacation rental with a hard-to-beat location in the heart of Grayton Beach, look no further than Kevin's Heaven. Located just 20 yards from the Grayton Dunes Beach Access, a stay at this Grayton beach rental puts you mere steps from the sugar white sand beaches. Start and end your day by watching the sun rise or set over the beautiful clear blue Gulf waters from one of the two large porches. In the mornings, take in the warm breezes and explore Grayton the best way we know how... on 1 of your 4 complimentary adult bikes! Spend long days at the spacious beach access enjoying the refreshing Gulf waters. Don't let your getaway pass you by without exploring the Coastal Dune Lake (Western Lake). If you're lucky, you may get to experience the lake breach the berm of the sand and flow into the Gulf's blue waters. This creates a rare brackish ecosystem home to both fresh and saltwater species that are sure to keep you exploring for hours. After the sun sets, guests can walk and grab drinks at Chiringo, shop at Brian and Brittney Kelley's surf shop Tribe Kelley, and enjoy great live music and fresh seafood delicacies at Grayton Beach's famous Red Bar. An endless list of activities are possible all within steps from this 30A vacation rental!

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

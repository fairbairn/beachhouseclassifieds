A Wave From It All by Royal Destinations

Location: Blue Mountain Beach
Address: 491 Old Beach Road

Details:
Welcome to A Wave From it All! Look no further for your ultimate Gulf front vacation dream home. This stunning six-bedroom home is nestled in the gulf-front gated community of Old Florida Beach. Once you enter the home, you're greeted by the expansive main staircase leading up to the main living area. The living room opens up to the second-floor wrap-around balcony with breathtaking panoramic views of white sands and turquoise water. This spacious home has some of the best views on 30A with a newly renovated community pool, private beach access, and space for intimate events! This property is the perfect place to hold wedding receptions (up to 30 guests), family reunions and other small gatherings! Event fee applicable, please call reservations to add. A Wave From It All is also a short walk to the Long Leaf Hiking Trail, a a 19-mile bike path that stretches all along the 30A strip and provides a perfect opportunity to spend an afternoon cruising on the bikes provided to visit the neighboring communities' shops and restaurants.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

-Dining Accommodations: Table Seating for 12, Bar Seating for 6

Home Sweet Home: A Note From The Homeowner- A Wave from It All has been a true gift to our family, welcoming us with its warmth every time we arrive. The scent of cypress and the soaring vaulted ceiling create an inviting space, while the ocean views from the main living area still take our breath away. We love gathering on the upper deck to relax, read, or watch the sunset. Though spacious, the home has the cozy charm of a classic Florida beach house, nestled in the peaceful, gated Old Florida Beach community—complete with a pool, beach access, and scenic paths along Draper Lake.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

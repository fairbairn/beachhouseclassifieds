B402 Sunset Beach Villa by Royal Destinations

Location: Seacrest Beach
Address: 10140 East County Highway 30A, Suite B402

*Please Note: The Sunset Beach boardwalk will be closed in January and February 2026 for reconstruction. During this time, the closest beach access for B402 Sunset Beach Villa will be the Inlet Beach Regional Access (1.5 miles).*

Details:
Welcome to B402 Sunset Beach Villa, a beautifully renovated Gulf-front condo offering four bedrooms and breathtaking views of the white-sand beaches and emerald waters of the Gulf of Mexico. This stunning retreat has been thoughtfully updated with new tile flooring throughout, stainless-steel appliances, and an elegantly refreshed master bathroom. Inside, you’ll find fresh paint, white plantation shutters, flat-screen TVs, and new furniture and bedding, creating a bright and relaxing coastal ambiance. The open-concept floor plan seamlessly connects the kitchen, living, and dining areas, all leading to a spacious balcony—perfect for enjoying morning coffee or sunset cocktails with a Gulf breeze. From the moment you enter the foyer, your gaze is drawn straight to the shimmering horizon beyond the great room. Come unwind, take in the view, and make unforgettable memories at B402 Sunset Beach Villa—your perfect beachfront escape.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Sweet Home: A Note From The Homeowner- We've been part of the 30A community since 2002, and from the beginning, we always dreamed of owning a place in Seacrest Beach. B402 Sunset Beach Villa immediately captured our hearts, especially with its breathtaking Gulf views from the top floor. There’s something truly special about waking up each day to the sparkling waters and sugar-white sand just steps away. We love being able to walk to great restaurants and shops. The convenience adds so much to the laid-back lifestyle that makes 30A so unique. The condo itself has been recently remodeled, so everything still feels fresh, modern, and thoughtfully designed for comfort and relaxation. We hope you love our second home as much as we do!

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

Description: 

Summer Breeze by Royal Destinations

Location: Dune Allen Beach
Address: 6312 W County Highway 30A

Details:
Stunning coastal construction is discovered in this beautiful beach house. Boasting 7 bedrooms and 7 bathrooms, including a private resort-style pool surrounded by a large yard with a privacy fence, Summer Breeze is a well-equipped and carefully crafted home that seamlessly combines luxury design with seaside charm. Guests can enjoy the ultimate outdoor space featuring a resort-style pool, patio area with a grill, and open-air shower. Enjoy the large front porch overlooking 30A. Situated in Dune Allen Beach on the west end of 30A, only a short walk to the beach, this property offers quick access to the sugar-white sand and emerald waters of the Gulf of Mexico. Our home offers serenity while still being within close proximity to all of the action!

**The two bedroom, full kitchen carriage house is available for rental separate from the main house on select weeks! If you're looking for a getaway but aren't needing the entire space, contact our reservations team for special dates to rent the beautiful carriage house. Online quotes include the main house and carriage house.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

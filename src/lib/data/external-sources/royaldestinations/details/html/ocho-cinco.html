Ocho Cinco by Royal Destinations

Location: Seagrove Beach
Address: 85 San Juan Avenue

Details:
Ocho Cinco is a beautifully appointed coastal retreat located in Seagrove Beach, just 528 feet from the San Juan Beach Access. Built in 2018, this spacious home offers approximately 2,876 square feet of interior living space and comfortably accommodates up to 12 guests with five bedrooms and four-and-a-half baths. Designed with families and groups in mind, Ocho Cinco features two living areas, modern interiors, generous bedroom accommodations, and multiple covered porches that provide plenty of space to gather or unwind. Outside, enjoy a private heated saltwater pool surrounded by a fenced yard, along with an outdoor shower and comfortable porch spaces for relaxing after a day at the beach. The nearby San Juan Beach Access offers convenient parking, a shower, and foot wash, while Seaside is approximately 1.5 miles away and easily accessible via the Timpoochee Trail. With its private pool, proximity to the Gulf, and spacious layout, Ocho Cinco offers an ideal setting for experiencing the best of Seagrove Beach and 30A.

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

13 Madaket Way by Royal Destinations

Location: WaterSound
Address: 13 Madaket Way

Details:
The newly renovated 13 Madaket Way is ready to welcome you! Located in the private, gated Gulf front community of WaterSound, this 30A luxury rental features an outdoor oasis complete with a private pool, firepit, and grill. Spanning 4,782 square feet, 13 Madaket Way offers timeless beauty with all the amenities one could need. With a location just a quick stroll away from the WaterSound pool and entry to the beach, relaxation is right at your fingertips. This spacious home offers 6 bedrooms, 6.5 baths, and a bright open-concept living area that transitions to the outdoors in true coastal fashion. A screened porch overlooks the pool with an outdoor dining area, perfect to enjoy a cool summer night. The interior is professionally designed to display high-end upgrades and finishes and the kitchen is designed with top-of-the-line Thermador appliances. Adjacent to this home and just a short bike ride away is The Big Chill (formerly The Hub), home to a delightful array of fun eateries and shopping. Relaxation awaits at this WaterSound vacation rental!

If you’re looking to plan less and experience more, contact the Royal Destinations team to discover your perfect 30A vacation rental. Known for unrivaled guest satisfaction, our team is committed to providing hand-tailored experiences that go beyond the four walls of your 30A luxury vacation rental.

Home Highlights:
-*Private heated pool
-Community pool
-Two Dishwashers
-State-of-the-art kitchen
-6 adult bikes
-Access to outdoor storage which houses beach tents, beach chairs and skimmer boards
*Pool heat is available for an additional $310 (pre-tax)
*The pool heater does require 24 hours to reach the optimal temperature of 83-85 degrees.
*Please contact Reservations to add this feature prior to your stay.

WaterSound Community Highlights:
-Over 250 Acre Gated Beach Community on the Gulf of Mexico between Seaside and Rosemary Beach
-(2) Community Pools (Dunesider Pool in Main Gate and Bridges Pool within the Bridges Gate)
-Kayak Rentals
-Parks, Walking Trails and Meandering Boardwalks
-Bike to Alys, Seacrest and Rosemary Beach
-A short walk to The Big Chill (formerly The Hub)
*Please Note: Access to the WaterSound Beach Club is not included. WaterSound does not allow golf cart rentals.

Home Configuration + Sleeping Arrangements:
Sleeps: 12
6 Bedrooms and 6 1/2 Baths

First Floor:

-Half Bath
-Laundry Room

Second Floor:
-Bedroom 2: King bedroom and sitting area with private bath to include a shower and a separate tub
-Bedroom 3: King bedroom with private bath to include a tub/shower combination
-Bedroom 4: King bedroom with private bath to include a shower (no tub)

Third Floor:
-Bedroom 5: 4 Twin over queen bunk beds with large private bath to include a tub/shower combination

Carriage House:
-Bedroom 6: King bedroom with private bath to include a tub/shower combination
-Coffee counter with a microwave and refrigerator

Please Note:
*For the security of guests and the safety of the home, external security cameras are present.
*Beach access locations and beach vending companies are subject to change, per local county ordinances. For the most up-to-date information, please contact our office prior to your arrival.

Lined with artsy villages, coastal lakes, upscale boutiques, eclectic beach bars, and world-class restaurants, Florida's Scenic Highway 30A has something for everyone. This unique destination has 25,000 acres of protected habitats. Among many things, this area is known for its family-friendly environment and is home to the world's most beautiful white sand beaches. The Timpoochee Trail bike path connects all the villages of 30A. Be sure to take your bike out and explore! The villages of 30A include: Dune Allen Beach, Blue Mountain Beach, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove Beach, WaterSound, Alys Beach, Seacrest Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Inlet Beach.

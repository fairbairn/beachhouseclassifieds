Description

2424 EAST COUNTY ROAD HIGHWAY 30-A

1 KING, 1 QUEEN

An Iconic Gulf-Front Retreat in Seaside, Florida

Dune House is a rare and remarkable beachfront home, perfectly positioned on a double Gulf-front lot with sweeping, unobstructed views of the emerald waters of the Gulf of Mexico. Designed by renowned architect Scott Merrill, this exceptional residence honors the original cottage charm that defined early Seaside, Florida, while thoughtfully incorporating modern comfort and understated elegance. Adding to its legacy, Dune House played a unique role in Seaside’s cinematic history, serving as the home of The Truman Show director during the filming of the iconic movie.

First-Floor Suites Designed for Comfort and Privacy

The first floor of Dune House features two beautifully appointed ensuite bedrooms, making it ideal for two couples or a small family seeking a private beachfront escape. The primary king suite is a serene retreat, complete with a renovated spa-style bathroom featuring a glass-enclosed rain shower, multiple shower heads, and an oversized ceiling-mounted rain feature. Just off the primary bedroom, a screened-in private porch offers stunning Gulf views through the dunes, furnished with a hammock and double rocker—perfect for morning coffee accompanied by the sound of the waves.

The second bedroom is a queen suite with its own ensuite bath, featuring a single vanity and a soaker tub with shower. This room also opens to a screened front porch, surrounded by lush landscaping that enhances privacy and tranquility.

Second-Floor Living with Panoramic Gulf Views

Upstairs, the home opens into a bright and inviting open-concept living space that seamlessly connects the kitchen, dining area, and living room. Designed to maximize natural light and Gulf views, this floor offers an effortless blend of comfort and functionality. The dining area seats five and provides peaceful glimpses of the water, making every meal feel special.

The living room is wrapped in windows, offering panoramic views of the Gulf and filling the space with coastal light. The adjacent kitchen is fully equipped for home-cooked meals, featuring a large double-door refrigerator with ice maker and water dispenser, a six-burner Viking gas range and oven, microwave, coffee maker, and ample cookware. A convenient half bath on this level completes the space.

Expansive Beachfront Porch & Sunset Views

The crown jewel of Dune House is its expansive open-air beachfront porch, shaded by a canopy and offering breathtaking, uninterrupted views of the Gulf. This private outdoor living space includes two chaise loungers for sunbathing and a dedicated dining area, creating the perfect setting for al fresco meals and unforgettable sunsets over the water. Whether relaxing with a book, dining outdoors, or simply taking in the view, this porch delivers a truly immersive Gulf-front experience.

(Please, no children under the age of 10) 

* Sat to Sat 7-night minimum Summer, 5-night minimum Spring, 3-night minimum Fall, 2-night minimum Winter.  Must be 25 years of age to rent and sign registration.

FEATURES

  • 1,938 square feet of Gulf-front living space
  • 49 feet | 1-minute walk to beach access
  • Private Seaside residential beach access at Tupelo Pavilion, the largest stretch of private beach on 30A
  • Beachfront location on the east side of Seaside with stunning, unobstructed Gulf views
  • Outdoor dining for four, perfect for al fresco meals
  • Renovated primary bathroom featuring a luxurious rain shower
  • Peacock Alley linens with fresh, luxurious towels and sheets throughout the home
  • Access to Seaside amenities, including 3 swimming pools, 5 clay tennis courts, 3 pickleball courts (court fees apply), and a fitness center
  • Welcome tote with 2 beach towels, a complimentary bottle of wine, and 2 rental bikes for pickup at Seaside Transit Authority
  • Personalized service throughout your stay, with starter amenities provided
  • Parking & golf cart policy: A golf cart is considered a car. Golf carts for non-homeowners visiting Seaside are prohibited. Special circumstances and medical needs require a golf cart permit
  • No pets allowed

Description

62 PENSACOLA STREET

1 KING, 1 QUEEN, 2 TWIN, 1 BUNKBED - sleeps 8 in all

Prime Seaside Location Near the Beach & Downtown

Moonshine is a standout Seaside, Florida vacation rental with an unbeatable location just 377 feet from beach access and only a 2-minute walk to the Seaside Amphitheater and downtown Seaside. With more than 35 shops, restaurants, and local favorites steps from your front door, this home places you in the heart of Seaside while still offering privacy, space, and unforgettable views.

Iconic Four-Story Design with Porches & Tower Views

This four-story Seaside home welcomes you with classic coastal architecture, featuring two spacious porches and a fourth-floor tower with an observation deck. The screened-in front porch spans the full width of the home and includes rockers, a picnic table for alfresco dining, and privacy curtains—making it an ideal gathering spot for morning coffee, family meals, or evening wine. A second-floor porch adds another outdoor retreat, while the true showstopper is the fourth-floor enclosed tower and open-air observation deck, offering some of the best gulf views and sunsets in all of Seaside.

Open-Concept Living & Updated Kitchen

Inside, Moonshine features an inviting open floor plan designed for connection and comfort. The living room offers two sofas and a large flat-screen TV, perfect for movie nights or relaxing after a beach day. The adjacent dining area comfortably seats eight beneath a fun coastal chandelier, while the updated kitchen includes stainless steel appliances, a four-burner electric cooktop, a large refrigerator, and breakfast bar seating for three. A full bathroom with a walk-in shower is located just off the living area and connects directly to the enclosed outdoor shower—perfect after sandy beach days.

Three En-Suite Bedrooms for Comfort & Privacy

Moonshine is a rare find in Seaside, offering three bedrooms each with a private en-suite bathroom, all conveniently located on the second floor—ideal for families. The king primary suite features ample storage, a mounted flat-screen TV, a closet, and a beautifully updated en-suite bath with a glass-enclosed walk-in shower. A full-size washer and dryer are located just off the primary suite for added convenience. The primary suite also shares access to an open-air balcony with Adirondack seating, perfect for a quiet afternoon break.

The second bedroom includes a queen bed, mounted flat-screen TV, generous storage, and a private en-suite bathroom with a tub/shower combination, while sharing access to the same balcony. The third bedroom features two twin beds, a flat-screen TV, a closet, and its own en-suite bath with a tub/shower combo.

A third-floor loft adds even more flexibility, featuring a bunk bed and dresser—an ideal kids’ nook or extra sleeping space.

Fourth-Floor Tower & Observation Deck with Gulf Views

A ship’s ladder leads to the crown jewel of the home: the fourth-floor enclosed tower and open-air observation deck. Surrounded by windows, the enclosed tower offers seating for four with sweeping gulf and town views. Step through double doors onto the observation deck, where bar-top seating for four delivers panoramic views of the Gulf of Mexico, Seaside rooftops, and spectacular sunsets—an unforgettable setting you’ll return to night after night.

Thoughtful Amenities for a Memorable Seaside Stay

Moonshine includes everything you need for a relaxed Seaside vacation, including a full-size washer and dryer, enclosed outdoor shower, and a rear charcoal grill. With its multiple porches, rare en-suite bedroom layout, and iconic tower views, Moonshine is a repeat family favorite and the perfect home base for experiencing the charm, beauty, and walkability of Seaside, Florida.

*   5-night minimum Spring, Spring Break and Summer; 3-night minimum Fall; 2-night minimum Winter. Must be 25 years of age to rent and sign registration.

FEATURES

  • 2,056 square feet: Spacious four-story Seaside home with multiple living levels
  • Seaside Private Residential Beach: Exclusive access at Pensacola Pavilion
  • Unbeatable walkability: Just 377 feet to beach access and a 2-minute walk to the Seaside Amphitheater, shops, and restaurants
  • Private driveway & parking: Parking available at the home
  • In-home media: Flat-screen TVs in every bedroom
  • Signature tower & views: Fourth-floor enclosed tower and gulf-view observation deck
  • Outdoor amenities: Enclosed outdoor shower and private charcoal grill
  • Luxury linens: Peacock Alley Linens — fresh, luxurious sheets and towels throughout the home
  • Resort-style amenities access: 3 swimming pools, 5 clay tennis courts & 3 pickleball courts (court fees apply), plus a fitness center
  • Welcome tote: Includes 2 beach towels, a complimentary bottle of wine, and 2 complimentary rental bikes for pickup at the Seaside Transit Authority
  • Elevated guest experience: Personalized service throughout your stay with starter amenities provided
  • Parking & golf cart policy: 2 parking passes are issued for two-bedroom and larger homes. A golf cart is considered a car. Golf carts for non-homeowners visiting Seaside are prohibited. Special circumstances and medical needs require a golf cart permit
  • No pets allowed

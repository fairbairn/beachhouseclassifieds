Description

48 TUPELO STREET

1 QUEEN, 1 BUNKBED, 1 TWIN - Sleeps 5

As Featured in Southern Living! 

Dreamsicle is a charming two-bedroom Seaside beach bungalow located on iconic Tupelo Street, just 270 feet from the private Tupelo Pavilion beach access—a quick one-minute walk to the sugar-white sands and only five minutes to downtown Seaside. Tucked along a quiet sandy footpath in the beloved Rosewalk neighborhood, this cottage is surrounded by shaded trees, classic architecture, and timeless coastal character. As the original beach cottage of Rosewalk, Dreamsicle offers a storybook setting that feels both nostalgic and wonderfully welcoming—an ideal retreat for young families seeking an authentic Seaside, Florida vacation.

A Storybook Setting in the Heart of Seaside

Upon arrival, enjoy designated parking directly in front of the home before stepping onto the inviting open-air front porch. This spacious porch features outdoor dining for six, additional rocker seating, and a charming bench swing—perfect for slow mornings with coffee or relaxed evenings with cocktails. Overlooking the shaded paths of Robert’s Walk, the porch offers a peaceful escape just moments from the beach and town center.

Bright, Playful Interiors with Retro Charm

Inside, Dreamsicle welcomes you with an open-concept living space that blends thoughtful renovation with playful personality. The fully renovated kitchen is both stylish and functional, featuring a retro SMEG refrigerator, an Italian Bertazzoni four-burner gas range and oven, a compact dishwasher, and all the essentials needed for easy meals at home. The adjacent living area offers comfortable seating, creating a cozy space to unwind after beach days. (Please note: the living room does not include a TV.)

One of the cottage’s standout features is the custom built-in dining nook, complete with cushioned booth-style seating for six or more—anchored by a whimsical disco ball overhead that adds a fun, unexpected touch to family meals and late-night conversations.

Comfortable Sleeping Spaces for the Whole Family

Just off the living area, the primary bedroom features a queen bed, a large mounted flat-screen TV, and a closet for storage. The second bedroom includes twin bunk beds, a built-in closet, and a laundry closet with a stackable washer and dryer. These bedrooms share a beautifully renovated full bathroom with a single vanity and a tub/shower combination.

For additional sleeping space, a ship’s ladder leads to a bonus loft with a twin bed—bringing total sleeping accommodations to five guests.

Outdoor Living Made Easy

After long days at the beach, rinse off in the private enclosed outdoor shower, conveniently accessed from inside the home. Just off the front porch, you’ll also find a charcoal grill, perfect for casual family dinners under the trees.

A Magical Rosewalk Retreat Steps from It All

With its unbeatable location just one minute from the beach and five minutes from Central Square, Dreamsicle places you within easy walking distance of the Seaside Amphitheater, live entertainment, and more than 35 shops and restaurants. Whether you’re building sandcastles, strolling shaded footpaths, or soaking up Seaside’s timeless charm, Dreamsicle offers a joyful, relaxed escape—perfect for creating unforgettable family memories in Seaside, Florida.

Must be 25 years of age to rent and sign the registration. 3-night minimum Spring, Spring Break | 5-night minimum Summer | 3-night minimum Fall | 2-night minimum Winter

FEATURES

  • Features 671 square feet
  • Private Seaside residential beach access at Tupelo Pavilion, the largest stretch of private beaches on 30A
  • 240 feet or a 1-minute walk to beach access
  • Gas grill
  • Stackable washer & dryer
  • Access to 3 swimming pools, 5 clay tennis courts, 3 pickleball courts (court fees apply), and a fitness center
  • Welcome tote with 2 beach towels, a complimentary bottle of wine, and 2 complimentary rental bikes for pickup at the Seaside Transit Authority
  • Personalized service throughout your stay with starter amenities provided
  • 2 parking passes issued for two-bedroom and larger homes. A golf cart is considered a car. Golf carts for non-homeowners visiting Seaside are prohibited. Special circumstances and medical needs requests require a golf cart permit.

Luxury Designer Home in the Heart of Seaside

Introducing Tradewinds, a stunning luxury designer home new to the Seaside rental market and beautifully renovated from top to bottom with exceptional custom detail throughout. Perfectly located next to the iconic Seaside Chapel, this one-of-a-kind home blends refined coastal elegance with thoughtful family-friendly design—making it an ideal retreat for discerning guests seeking comfort, style, and an unbeatable location.

Inviting Outdoor Spaces & Charming Curb Appeal

Tradewinds sleeps up to eight guests and offers multiple exceptional outdoor living areas. Upon arrival, guests are welcomed by an open front porch with three rocking chairs, ideal for slow mornings with coffee. The porch overlooks a generous fenced side yard featuring a custom-built double swing, perfect for children and relaxed outdoor moments. Completing the outdoor experience is a truly spa-like enclosed outdoor shower, designed to feel indulgent and serene after a day at the beach.

Luxurious First-Floor Bedrooms & Spa-Inspired Baths

The first floor features two beautifully appointed bedrooms along with a custom-built sleeping nook. The queen primary bedroom offers a pencil post bed, sofa lounger, luxurious linens, flat-screen TV, and a walk-in closet. The private marble primary bathroom is a standout, featuring a single vanity, mounted flat-screen TV, deep soaking tub, and a glass-enclosed marble walk-in shower with rain shower head and wand. A private door leads directly to the grand enclosed outdoor shower, complete with rain shower head, shower wand, and built-in bench—creating a true spa retreat.

The adjacent queen guest bedroom is equally refined, with designer furnishings, stunning linens, a corner chair, flat-screen TV, and a private ensuite bath featuring marble finishes, a single vanity, and a glass-enclosed walk-in shower with built-in bench, rain shower, and wand.

Custom Sleeping Nook & Family-Friendly Details

Tucked beneath the staircase is a thoughtfully designed built-in twin bunk nook with a twin trundle, a favorite space for younger guests. This cozy area includes privacy shades for the upper bunk, a mounted flat-screen TV, recessed lighting, and custom millwork—perfectly positioned between both bedrooms for added peace of mind.

Chef’s Kitchen, Bar Area & Open-Concept Living

At the top of the stairs, a custom built-in bar area welcomes you with a dedicated coffee station featuring a Moccamaster, beverage refrigerator, and an ice maker producing coveted Sonic-style ice. Adjacent is the home’s stunning open-concept living, dining, and kitchen space.
The chef’s kitchen features a large marble center island with seating for four, top-of-the-line appliances including a Wolf six-burner gas range, Sub-Zero refrigerator, deep galley sink, and a wall of windows overlooking the side yard and Seaside Chapel. The dining area includes a round expandable table seating six to eight guests, while the living room offers designer furnishings, ample seating, a large mounted flat-screen TV, and a queen Murphy bed. A convenient half bath completes this level.

Screened Porch Living with Views of the Seaside Chapel

One of the most loved spaces in the home is the expansive screened-in outdoor living porch located just off the bar area. Designed for both relaxation and entertaining, this space features two outdoor sofas, two oversized loungers, a large mounted outdoor TV, and an outdoor dining table seating eight guests. Overlooking the fenced yard and the Seaside Chapel, this porch is perfect for movie nights, game days, and memorable gatherings.

An Exceptional Seaside Home for Families, Couples & Special Occasions

With its prime chapel-side location, luxurious finishes, and thoughtful layout, Tradewinds is ideal for young families, two couples traveling together, or guests attending a Seaside wedding or special event. Rarely does a home of this caliber, location, and design become available—making Tradewinds a truly unforgettable Seaside vacation experience.

* For rent in summer only. 5NT minimum for Summers. Must be 25 years of age to rent and sign registration.

  • Outdoor living and dining area with mounted television
  • Peacock Alley linens, featuring fresh, luxurious sheets and plush towels throughout the home

FAQs

What are your check in and check out times?

Check-in time begins at 4PM.   You will receive a folder that includes parking passes, a Seaside map, beach access cards, swim & fitness passes (if applicable) and general information.  Please contact us at 855-411-1557 if you anticipate a late arrival.  If you arrive before 4 pm and your cottage is not ready, please plan to pay to park around Smolian Circle.  

Check-out time is 10 AM.  Please turn off all lights and electronics, make sure the windows and doors are closed, trash is removed, and the house is secure.

How do I reserve beach chairs?

You can reserve beach chairs and umbrellas from Cabana Man onsite in Seaside, Florida. Once you reserve your home a Cabana Man booking code will be sent with your reservation confirmation email and you can book your beach set up by going to http://www.cabanaman.com.

Seagrove Beach "8 Hiker" 8 Hiker Street
Santa Rosa Beach, Florida
Bedrooms 4
Beds 8
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- 0.7 Miles from the closest beach access
- Community heated pool (seasonal) and pool house
- Pool is located directly down Hiker St
- Next to the shopping district of Greenway Park
- Greenway Station includes shops, restaurants, and a gourmet market
- 4 adult bikes
- Close to a forest in a peaceful setting with 3 porches
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Nestled in the serene beauty of Seagrove Beach, 8 Hiker is a stunning retreat that seamlessly blends comfort and style. Boasting 4 elegant bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms, this haven is thoughtfully designed to accommodate up to 12 guests. Located minutes away from delightful Greenway Park, visitors can enjoy boutique shopping, delectable local fare, and even a gourmet market. With over 2,200 square feet of charming living space, 3 breezy porches with scenic views, and easy access to the sparkling community pool, relaxation is simple. Best of all, the dazzling Emerald Coast beaches are only 0.7 miles away!

Step inside this 2-story property from the cozy front porch, where plush outdoor furniture offers peaceful neighborhood views. The entryway welcomes guests with a picturesque coastal vibe, featuring a convenient bench and thoughtful touches that set a relaxed tone. The open concept home unfolds into a vibrant great room, filled with natural light that illuminates the white shiplap walls and warm wood flooring. Settle into the living area, where a plush couch and matching armchairs provide cushy seating for the whole family to enjoy a movie night together. This area flows seamlessly into the dining area, which features a rustic table with chair and bench seating. The adjacent chef’s kitchen shines with sleek white cabinetry, showcasing high-end appliances and a convenient kitchen island that seats 3 for coffee or quick bites. Off the foyer is a spacious primary bedroom boasting a luxurious king bed and private bathroom—perfect for the ultimate escape. There’s a laundry room for convenience. A charming back porch with cute café seating leads to an outdoor shower and quaint walkway.

Upstairs, the second floor is thoughtfully designed for rest and connection. A cozy sitting area with a queen sleeper sofa and TV makes for a great hangout spot, while the bunk room with 2 twin-over-twin bunks and a twin trundle bed provides a great retreat for kids. This floor also hosts 2 lovely guest bedrooms with king beds—one boasting a private bathroom and gorgeous covered balcony!

Discover coastal comfort at 8 Hiker, featuring on-site parking and 4 complimentary bikes to explore Point Washington State Forest and Eastern Lake. For beach access, head right on 30A, left onto Belmar Drive, and follow Eastern Lake Road left as it curves right. Ramsgate Beach Access is on the left! Book now!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 12):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed with Twin Trundle and a Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
- Additional Living Area: Queen Sleeper Sofa

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger.

**Street parking is prohibited in this community.

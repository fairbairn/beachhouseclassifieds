Seagrove Beach "The Long Exhale" 83 South Lake Drive
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-080654
Bedrooms 4
Beds 5
Sleeps 9
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Lake views with private dock
- 4 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Welcome to The Long Exhale! Tucked in Seagrove Beach, this stunning lakefront home offers the perfect blend of luxury and tranquility. Featuring breathtaking lake views and a private dock, this 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home spans 3,000 square feet and comfortably sleeps up to 9 guests. With ample outdoor space and nearby beach access, it’s the ultimate destination for an unforgettable vacation!

Serenity greets every guest from the moment of arrival. A 2-story white brick façade and cheerful teal door, framed by towering trees, set the tone for a peaceful escape. Inside, natural light dances off tall ceilings and an elegant chandelier in the spacious great room. Relaxation awaits in the living area, which features tufted furnishings and 2 cozy armchairs facing the large TV mounted above the fireplace—2 sets of French doors open wide, inviting a fresh coastal breeze inside. The open, airy layout transitions smoothly into the dining area, where a rustic live-edge table seats 8 for memorable meals. Nearby, the gourmet kitchen delights with glossy countertops, ample cabinet space, high-end appliances, and an island that doubles as a 4-person breakfast bar—perfect for entertaining while preparing meals. Sliding glass doors lead to an enclosed patio with plush furnishings and a TV overlooking the lake. Stairs guide guests down to the open deck for a closer view of the water, or an afternoon spent unwinding in the hammock. The private dock on the lake also offers comfortable seating, creating an idyllic setting for late-night cocktails and conversation. The sunlit primary bedroom features a king bed, TV, walk-in closet, and a luxurious private bathroom with a soaking tub and separate shower. A laundry room completes this floor.

A sleek staircase with a modern glass railing leads to the inviting second-floor guest bedrooms with a shared bathroom, boasting 1 queen bed and 2 twin beds, respectively. Another spacious guest bedroom offers versatility, with 1 king and 1 twin bed, a TV, private bathroom, and balcony access with sweeping lake views.

The Long Exhale provides everything needed for a seamless beach escape, including on-site driveway parking for 6 vehicles and 4 bikes for easy adventure. For beach access, head down South Lake Drive to Center Avenue and turn right. Next, take a left onto South Gulf Drive and follow it until reaching Eastern Lake Road. Beach access is on the left. Book now!

Seagrove Beach "Tide Together" 85 N Ryan Street
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 4
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- 2 Community pools
- Private boardwalk to the beach
- Central location to shopping and restaurants
- SONOS sound system
- 3 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Experience a carefree beach getaway at Tide Together! This beautiful 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom Seagrove Beach home offers delightful accommodations for up to 8 guests—minutes from the beach! Located South of Scenic 30A and part of the Summer’s Edge community, this charming destination offers access to 2 neighborhood pools and an easy, quiet walk to the private boardwalk and Greenway Station’s dining, shopping, and coffee shop. Imagine luxurious mornings sunbathing on the white-sand beach or paddleboarding across Eastern Lake, followed by lunch at the Seagrove Village Market. End the day unwinding on the home’s rooftop deck while watching the sunset.

Nestled within a peaceful tree-lined, family-friendly neighborhood, this 3-story beach abode welcomes guests with a charming front porch with Adirondack seating. Inside, an open layout invites group gatherings in the great room. After a day in the sun, kick back on the living room’s 2 plush sofas and comfy armchair for a relaxing night around the TV. The fully equipped kitchen includes high-end appliances and a breakfast bar for 3—perfect for guests who relish entertaining while they cook. The traditional dining table has space for all 8 guests to dine only a few feet away. Finish the night with a glass of chilled wine on the furnished back porch as the stereo system plays throughout the whole house, setting the mood. Retire in the tranquil guest bedroom with a king bed at the end of the evening. Each of the home’s bedrooms includes direct access to a private covered porch!

A spacious primary bedroom awaits on the second floor, boasting a king bed and a private bathroom. A family-friendly guest bedroom with 1 queen bed and 1 twin-over-twin bunk bed completes the floor.

Continue to the third level for a breathtaking panorama of Scenic 30A and peek-a-boo views of the water from the crow’s nest lookout, furnished with 4 chairs and 2 garden seats.

Enjoy a Seagrove Beach vacation full of family, friends, and fun at Tide Together. On-site parking, plus 3 complimentary adult bikes, makes it easy to explore. The community beach access is to the left of the pool on South Ryan Street—a quick 3-minute walk. Book this home today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 8):
FIRST FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Not attached Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
-Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed & Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

THIRD FLOOR:
-Crow's Nest Lookout

*Low-Speed Vehicle rentals are not permitted in the Summer's Edge Neighborhood.

*The summer's edge beach access will not be available 1/5/26-2/5/26 due to repairs. During this time, the closest beach access available would be the Ramgate beach access.

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger

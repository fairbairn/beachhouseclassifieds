WaterSound 99 Compass Point Way S #305
Watersound, FL
Bedrooms 2
Beds 3
Sleeps 6
Full Baths 2

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Beachfront in WaterSound
- Private Deeded Beach Access
- Located in a secure, gated community with on-site security
- Short walk to the Dunesider Pool and WaterSound Beach Access
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach -service set (2 beach chairs and 1 umbrella)
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.
- This property comes with 1 designated parking space and 1 first-come first-serve parking space. Please note first come first serve parking is limited and not guaranteed. This condo permits a maximum of 2 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: Stunning coastal and dune views and recently remodeled interiors make this WaterSound condo the perfect setting for your next beach vacation! This beautiful home offers 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and accommodations for 6 guests.

A warm welcome awaits just inside, where guests will enter into the home's inviting main living area. This space features an immaculate gourmet kitchen with stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and lovely custom cabinetry. The breakfast bar boasts 4 dining seats, and an additional 6 seats are available the adjacent dining table. The living room offers a wonderful place to relax and unwind, with plush and comfortable seating, a flat-screen television, wonderful natural light from the large windows, and breathtaking coastal views. French doors lead out to the home's balcony, where guests can enjoy an invigorating sunrise with their morning cup of coffee or a peaceful sunset with a refreshing evening cocktail.

This home features two beautifully furnished bedrooms, both with exquisite emerald coast views. The primary bedroom boasts a luxurious upholstered king bed, a large flat-screen television, large windows and lots of natural light, and access to the home's balcony. The en suite primary bathroom offers dual sinks, a glass-front shower, and a gorgeous soaking tub. The guest bedroom is appointed with 2 upholstered queen beds, a mounted flat-screen television, and a private bathroom with a single vanity and shower/tub combo.

Guests in this home are just steps from the sugar white sand beach, which is accessible via a dune walkover. A private community pool and outdoor grill area offer additional space to enjoy the sunshine and ocean breeze.

This home is a picturesque setting for your next 30A getaway.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (Sleeps 6):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Two Queens, Shared Bathroom With Shower/Tub Combo
- Primary Bedroom: King, Private Bathroom Tub/Shower

IMPORTANT INFORMATION BELOW.
*From October 2025 through 2026, WaterSound Compass Point will undergo major exterior refurbishment. Residents and guests should expect construction, boarded windows, limited balcony and garage access, and possible parking disruptions. These safety measures are essential for project completion.

*HOA regulations do not allow for guest Low-Speed Vehicle rentals. Additionally, bringing your own Low-Speed Vehicle onto the property is prohibited.
*This is a peaceful community. To ensure everyone's enjoyment, weddings, parties, gatherings exceeding a reasonable number of guests, and guests of guests are strictly prohibited. Violation of this policy may result in immediate removal from the property, forfeiture of any remaining rental fees, and potential fines from the HOA, community, and 360 Blue.
* To ensure a comfortable stay for all guests, pets are not permitted on the property. Please note that any damage caused by pets will be the guest's responsibility.
*No Smoking

DETAILS: One look at this luxurious Santa Rosa beach home will leave you wanting "OMG! By the Sea!" to be the destination for your next coastal getaway! Featuring 6 bedrooms, 7 bathrooms, stunning Emerald Coast views, a private heated pool, and a location just steps from the beach, this home has everything you need to accommodate 13 guests wonderfully!

This home offers three beautifully appointed levels with plenty of space for everyone. The main living level is on the second floor, which boasts an open-concept floor plan joining the living, kitchen, and dining areas. Large windows and French doors allow natural light to fill this bright and airy space. Guests will have their choice of comfy spots in the living room, which is furnished with two plush sofas and two cozy armchairs. The large, flat-screen television sits ready for a family movie night, or guests can enjoy a favorite board game around the oversized coffee table. The nearby sitting area offers additional seating with a sectional sofa next to the three-sided gas fireplace.

The family cooks will love preparing meals in the gourmet kitchen. This beautiful space boasts spacious granite countertops, elite stainless steel appliances, and a 6-burner gas range.
Guests can enjoy family meals around the large dining table - which seats 8 - and additional seats are available at the adjacent breakfast bar.

Down on the first floor are two beautiful bedrooms. The first offers a queen bed and access to a shared bathroom with a shower. The second bedroom downstairs is a stunning primary suite, which features a luxurious king bed and a private bathroom with a walk-in shower, a soaking tub, and dual vanities. The main level offers a convenient king bed guest room, featuring a private bathroom, a sitting area, mounted flat-screen television, and a kitchenette. The final three bedrooms are located on the third floor. Guests can choose from a bunk room with a twin-over-full bunk and a private bathroom with a shower; a king bed guest room and private bathroom with a shower/tub combo and dual-sink vanity; or a second primary suite offering a king bed, stunning gulf views, and a private bathroom with a large shower, a spacious soaking tub, and dual granite vanities.

When it's time for fun in the sun, guests can enjoy splashing in the private pool, which can be heated seasonally for an additional fee, or take a quick walk down to the sugar-white sand beach! This home is equipped with 6 custom adult beach cruiser bikes, perfect for exploring Santa Rosa Beach and the best local dining, shopping, and entertainment in our 30A coastal towns! Book your getaway to "OMG! By the Sea" and experience the luxurious vacation you've been waiting for!

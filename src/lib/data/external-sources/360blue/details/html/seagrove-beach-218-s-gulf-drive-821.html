Seagrove Beach 218 S Gulf Drive
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 7
Beds 11
Sleeps 18
Full Baths 7
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Parking lot is available at Eastern Lake Beach Access
- 7 adult bikes
- 4 vehicle parking
- Private pool *Pool heating is available from October 1st through May 1st for an additional fee of $40 per day.
- New build
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Welcome to the epitome of luxury coastal living at 218 S. Gulf Drive. This 3-story, 7-bedroom, 7.5-bathroom coastal home sleeps 18 in the heart of Seagrove Beach near Eastern Lake. With 4,600 square feet of living space, guests will love the water views from the top-floor balcony, the additional living space with a ping-pong table, and the private backyard pool with an attached sitting spa. Plus, the quick 3-minute walk to the nearest beach access!

Step inside the expansive first-floor great room, where modern elegance meets relaxed coastal charm for an inviting atmosphere. Spend time with loved ones in the living area, featuring an oversized sectional couch and 2 armchairs, arranged around a large TV with Sonos speakers. The gourmet kitchen boasts pristine white cabinets, 2 French door refrigerators, and a sleek marble island with built-in appliances and seating for 5. The dining area offers ample room with 2 spacious tables accommodating 16 guests. Enjoy access to the ultimate backyard oasis with a covered porch, alfresco dining table, a private pool with an attached sitting spa, and multiple loungers. Finally, retreat to the 1 guest bedroom on this floor with a king-sized bed and private bathroom.

Experience ultimate relaxation on the second floor, boasting 3 comfortable bedrooms—1 primary bedroom and 2 guest bedrooms, each equipped with a luxurious king bed, private bathroom, and access to an inviting second-floor deck with neighborhood views.

The third floor is a vibrant hub for socializing and having a good time, featuring an additional living area with a ping pong table and a wet bar. There are also 2 guest bedrooms with a king bed and a private bathroom, and a dedicated bunk room that can comfortably sleep 6 people. Guests can also enjoy access to the covered deck on the top floor, which provides stunning water views.

218 S. Gulf Drive is the perfect place to entertain friends and family! With on-site parking for 7 vehicles—plus 7 adult bikes and a 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle—it’s easy to explore the local area. For Eastern Lake Public Beach Access, head south (right) on Eastern Lake Road until the beach access is visible between 471 and 493 Eastern Lake Road. Book this exceptional coastal property today!

DETAILS: Spooky Blues is a majestic 5-bedroom, 5.5-bathroom, Gulf-view home that sleeps 16, nestled in the serene Blue Mountain Beach community. Located along the glittering Emerald Coast, this spacious 3,200-square-foot sanctuary boasts 2 spacious balconies, a private saltwater pool, a propane grill, and dedicated beach access only 250 yards away!

The ground floor of this 3-story residence greets guests with warm hardwood floors and a sun-filled living area, complete with an adjoining wet bar and 3-stool bar top. A bunk room with 2 queen-over-queen beds and a private bathroom provides a cozy sanctuary for kids or those young at heart. French doors lead to a covered patio dressed in comfortable outdoor furniture and an entertainment-ready TV set against a private saltwater pool backdrop. Dip in the shimmering water before relishing the sun on loungers or gently swaying in the breeze on 2 hammocks.

Use the in-home elevator or decorative staircase to ascend the second floor—the heart of this home. Culinary enthusiasts will delight in the kitchen, a testament to form and function with its granite countertops, custom cabinets, and high-end appliances. Morning banter and evening cocktails can be enjoyed around the spacious kitchen island that doubles as a 6-stool breakfast bar. The living area, an inviting ensemble of plush furniture and gorgeous picture windows, showcases exquisite views. The primary bedroom offers a serene escape, featuring a king bed and a private bathroom with a spa tub. Venture outside from the great room to find a second-floor deck where a picnic table and sitting area await, shaded for comfort and perfect for alfresco dining.

The top floor boasts a thoughtfully designed primary bedroom with a king bed, a private bathroom, a sleeper sofa, and a wet bar. Step outside to the private balcony, lounge on the day bed, or sink into additional seating as the skyline stretches beyond. Two more bedrooms, each with a king bed and private bath, ensure unparalleled privacy. A laundry room can also be found on this floor for convenience.

Spooky Blues is an ideal getaway for families and friends eager to create lasting memories. With on-site parking for 4-5 vehicles and 6 complimentary adult bikes, it’s easy to explore the area. The nearest beach access is a 3-minute walk to Spooky Lane Public Beach, which can be found across the boardwalk on Spooky Lane. Book now!

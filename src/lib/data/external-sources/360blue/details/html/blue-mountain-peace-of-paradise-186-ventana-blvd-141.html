Blue Mountain "Peace of Paradise" 186 Ventana Blvd
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 4
Sleeps 7
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Professionally decorated
- Community pool
- 6-seater Gas Low-Speed Vehicle
- 4 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant—All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: A wonderful getaway awaits you at Peace of Paradise. This impeccably decorated 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom Blue Mountain Beach home can accommodate up to 7 guests. It also features access to the community pool, a 6-seater golf cart, and four adult bikes, making it ideal for exploring the distinctive communities of 30A. After a perfect morning at the beach, relax with friends and family poolside, and head to the Blue Mountain Creamery for a frozen dessert!

The home is single-level. The living area has a fully equipped kitchen with plenty of counter space for meal preparation, stainless steel appliances, and a breakfast bar. Enjoy your culinary creations at the dining table that seats Six or the breakfast bar, which has room for 3. Access to an intimate deck can be found just off the kitchen. There’s also a lovely sitting area on the front porch.

The living room is adjacent to the dining table. It boasts a comfortable sofa, three plush armchairs, and a 65” TV—the perfect space to come together in the evening as a group for vacation movie night! Enjoy the charming screened-in porch at the back of the home with plenty of outdoor seating and an open deck just a few steps down to the backyard.

After a busy day frolicking on the beach, guests can get some rest. The first bedroom comes replete with a plush king bed, a 50” TV, and a separate bathroom with a shower. The second bedroom is ideal for the kids in your group, as it features a twin-over-full bunk bed, a 42” flatscreen TV, and a shared bathroom with a stand-alone shower and a separate bathtub. The third bedroom has a king bed and a 50” TV and shares the bathroom with the second bedroom.

Peace of Paradise is the perfect 30A getaway for a family or two couples. The sugar-soft sands of the Emerald Coast are a short distance away, as are the shops and restaurants of Blue Mountain Beach. Seaside and WaterColor are just down the road. Don’t miss your chance to unwind at this one-of-a-kind Emerald Coast retreat. Book Peace of Paradise today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 7):
FIRST FLOOR:
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
-Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Full Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property, forfeit any rental payments, and be subject to HOA, community, and Callista Vacation Rentals fines.

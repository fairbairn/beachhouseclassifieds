Blue Mtn. Adagio Unit A-103 - 2421 W. Hwy 30A
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 5
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
UNIT HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf views
- Community gas grill
- 2 dedicated parking spots
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Enjoy the beautiful white sand beaches and turquoise water on the highest point on the gulf in Blue Mountain Beach. Relax on your patio or enjoy your spacious living room with breathtaking views of the Gulf of Mexico. This 2,500 square foot beautiful condominium at Adagio features a main living area is that is spacious and gorgeously decorated and overlooks the Gulf of Mexico. Relax in this cozy, yet elegant room. The kitchen has all the amenities, including granite countertops, and Viking appliances. The view from the kitchen is incredible as well looking out over the turquoise waters. The primary bedroom boasts breathtaking gulf views! Spend countless hours on the beach or splashing in the two Adagio community pools. This adagio unit comes with two parking spaces. Check availability and book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 10):
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed with private bathroom
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed with jack and jill bathroom with shower/tub combo
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed with jack and jill bathroom with shower/tub combo
-Guest Bedroom: 2 Queen Beds

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

*Starting 1/1/2017, guests must rent any bike rentals through Adagio's exclusive bike vendor, La Dolce Vita.

*Per HOA policy, a MAX of 10 guests can stay at this property.

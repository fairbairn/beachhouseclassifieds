DETAILS: Indulge in carefree luxury at Coast is Clear, a breathtaking 7-bedroom, 8-bathroom home that accommodates 26 guests in unrivaled comfort. Nestled in enchanting Seagrove—with the tempting beach only a 2-minute walk away—this property offers captivating Gulf views and proximity to Seaside’s shops, restaurants, and local entertainment. Imagine sun-splashed days relaxing in the private pool’s crystalline waters, cheerful competitions at the pool table, and lively cookouts at the outdoor kitchen!

Leisure reigns on the first level! Play a friendly game of pool or cards while sipping fruit-infused cocktails made at the wet bar. Outside, swaying palm trees create a tropical atmosphere for afternoons of swimming or sunbathing. The outdoor kitchen, complete with a grill, beverage refrigerator, 3-person bar, and 6-person table, is an entertainer’s dream. At bedtime, retire to the guest bedroom featuring a king bed and private bathroom, while a sleeping nook offers 2 built-in, twin-over-twin bunks. An outdoor shower, laundry room, Peloton bike, and in-home elevator add convenience throughout guests’ stay.

The second-level great room embodies laid-back elegance, with cascades of light from the numerous windows illuminating the blonde wood floors and dramatic wood ceiling. In the living space, relax with loved ones or unwind while watching TV. The square 16-person dining table encourages vibrant conversation while indulging in home-cooked meals from the gourmet kitchen—featuring premier appliances and an expansive center island that seats 3. The first balcony, located off the living room features a 6-seat table, while the second balcony is conveniently situated next to the kitchen. Unwind for the night in the lavish primary bedroom, boasting a king bed, private bathroom, and Smart TV. The bunk room is ideal for social guests with 2 twin-over-twin bunks and 1 queen-over-queen bunk.

On the top floor, discover a balcony featuring a 4-person table and unique spiral staircase leading to the rooftop deck. Here, a 6-person table awaits, offering breathtaking Gulf views.

There are 2 guest bedrooms and 1 primary bedroom, each boasting a king bed, alongside 1 guest bedroom with a king bed and built-in twin-over-twin bunk—all 4 feature a private bathroom.

Experience an extravagant Seagrove Beach escape at Coast is Clear. Parking for 5 vehicles, a 6-passenger low speed vehicle, and 7 complimentary adult bikes makes it easy to explore. The nearest beach access is the Azalea-Camellia street access—only a 2-minute walk away. Book today!

Seagrove Beach, The Legacy, 3880 East County Highway 30-A #401
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 7
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf-front
- Corner unit on the top floor
- Gulf-front community pool (not heated)
- Newly renovated
- 4 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: This newly renovated Gulf-front condo is perfect for groups looking for a seamless Seagrove vacation! With direct beach access, a gulf front community pool, and convenient beach service, you won't have to lift a finger! This open floor plan boasts stunning unobstructed views and connects all corners of this living area. With a wet bar, dining room, kitchen, and living room, this spot is perfect for gathering the whole crew after a long beach day.

The primary bedroom is equipped with a king bed, porch access, and an enormous private bath with spacious shower and jetted tub. Just down the hall, you will find a guest bedroom with a king bed and a private bathroom behind the sliding barn door. There are 2 more guest bedrooms found down the hall - 1 with a queen bed and 1 with 2 twin-over-twin bunks, separated by a Jack and Jill bathroom.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 10):
FOURTH FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Bunk Room: Two Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo (Photos Coming Soon)

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

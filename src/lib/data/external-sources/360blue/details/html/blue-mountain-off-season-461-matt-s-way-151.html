Blue Mountain "Off SEAson" 461 Matt's Way
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 7
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Located in the community of Lakeside at Blue Mountain Beach
- Connected walkway to The Shops at Redfish Village
- Short bike ride to the beach; walking distance of 20 minutes
- 6-seater Low-Speed Vehicle
- 1 large front porch, 2 large screened-in rear porches
- Kid's game room with board games
- 15 min to shopping outlet malls in Destin
- 4 miles to Seaside/Watercolor. Beautiful paved bike path the whole way
- Right next to Grayton Beach State Park, Bars and Restaurants
- 100 yds from ice cream shop: Blue Mountain Creamery
- Community pool (Heated during Spring Break, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's)
- 4 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant—All linens, including comforter covers, and laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: The amenities you want in a community you’ll love! “Off Season” is a 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom 30A getaway in the heart of Blue Mountain Beach—and it’s just a short walk to the sugar-soft sand of the Emerald Coast. This vacation retreat is located in the gated community of Lakeside and features a gourmet-style kitchen, plenty of spacious decks—and access to the beautiful Lakeside community pool. All of the fun and excitement of Seaside and WaterColor are just four miles away.

Beginning on the first floor, gorgeous walnut floors are complemented by beautiful finish carpentry throughout. The gourmet-style kitchen features select stainless appliances, including a gas range stove and a wine fridge. Granite countertops include a spacious island that incorporates seating for 4. The kitchen opens onto the living room, where you’ll find plenty of comfy seating and a vast flat-screen TV. Adjacent is the dining area with table seating for 6. The first floor also has the first of the two master suites, with a king bed and a private bathroom with a shower only. Down the hall is a spacious laundry room with a beautiful custom barn door to close the space off and a half bath.

Ascending to the second floor, you’ll find a beautiful and spacious primary wing. It has a king bed with a private bathroom offering a lovely soaking tub and a spacious separate shower. A few steps away is a bedroom-sized walk-in closet with plenty of storage space, which could also fit a pack-and-play or trundle bed. There is also a private large screened-in porch which is an excellent place for morning coffee or watching the sunset. This wing can be closed off to create plenty of privacy from the rest of the house if desired. Down the hall is a second guest bedroom with a queen bed and walk-in closet. The third guest bedroom has 2 twin-over-full bunk beds. This is a large room with plenty of space for multiple people with a walk-in closet. Next to this bedroom is a “hang out” space with bean bag chairs, board games, and a smart TV with gaming capabilities creating a space for everyone. Both guest bedrooms share a hall bathroom with a tub and shower, along with two separate sinks.

The outdoor spaces truly set “Off Season” apart. The welcoming front porch has a comfy twin-sized bed swing and two teak rockers, making it the perfect place to relax with friends and loved ones. Around the corner is a grilling area. The spacious screened-in porch has beautiful french doors off the kitchen with views of Redfish Lake. This home is just steps from the bike trail, offering easy access to plenty of shopping and restaurants!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 12)
FIRST FLOOR:
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub Separate
-Guest Bedroom: Two Twin over Full Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
-Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

**Please note that in order to utilize the Low-Speed Vehicle that comes with the home, you will be required to pay a $30 non-refundable deposit to 30a Gulf Carts.

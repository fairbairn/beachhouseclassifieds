Seagrove "Belle Plage" 517 Eastern Lake Rd
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 8
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf-front
- Private beach access
- Private pool and sitting spa **Pool heating is available for this property from Oct 1-May 1 for $40 per day** The pool and spa are heated together.
- (2) Complimentary beach setups each morning which include 4 beach chairs with 2 umbrella each (Service provided Mar 1-Oct 31)
- Featured on HGTV
- Professionally decorated
- 6 Passenger golf cart
- Outdoor shower
- Low-Speed Vehicle
- 5 Adult bikes
- 4 dedicated parking spots
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Discover unmatched relaxation at Bella Plage, a Gulf-front, 2-story home boasting 5 bedrooms and 5.5 bathrooms. Sleeping 12 in the quintessential beach town of Seagrove, guests will find a plethora of warm-weather activities to fill the day. Paddleboard across the quiet waters, bike the Timpoochee Trail, or simply stretch out on the sugar-soft sand and bask in the golden sunshine. Guests will adore this HGTV-featured home’s private pool overlooking the beach—with a boardwalk off the back deck!

Coastal blues and French country accents abound inside this newly updated home. Natural light illuminates the chef’s kitchen, featuring double ovens, stone countertops, and a center island that seats 2. Double doors connect to the quaint courtyard with a 4-person table steps away for alfresco dining—a second courtyard with a 6-person table and grill is across the hall. Continue into the living and dining area, where a table for 12 waits. Winsome mix-and-match chairs paired with floral toile de Jouy curtains curate an atmosphere of laid-back elegance. Gather together for a serene evening watching TV on the matching couches and 2 armchairs in the living area. Outside, enjoy the balmy weather with a pitcher of lemonade at the 8-person and 4-person tables on the covered porch. Swim in the crystalline pool, relax in the Adirondack chairs, or walk to the beach! This floor has 1 charming guest bedroom with a queen bed and window seat, as well as an extra sleeping area with a twin-over-twin bunk bed, TV, and corner sofa.

Upstairs, the primary bedroom includes a king bed and breathtaking views of the Gulf from a private porch with a bed swing. Down the hall are 3 comfortable guest bedrooms—1 bedroom with a twin-over-twin bunk bed and 2 bedrooms with queen beds. A queen sleeper sofa in the hallway is perfect for last-minute guests.

With a stunning Gulf-front location, private pool, and beach access off the back deck, Bella Plage is the ultimate setting for a Seagrove Beach vacation. Exploring with the whole family is easy, with 5 complimentary bikes, a 6-seater golf cart, and on-site parking for 4 vehicles. Book this beachside home today!

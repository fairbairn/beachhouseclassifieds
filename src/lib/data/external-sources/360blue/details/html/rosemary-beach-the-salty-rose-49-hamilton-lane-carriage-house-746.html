Bedrooms 2
Beds 3
Sleeps 6
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- This home has been designed by and is owned by Kate from Kate Marker Interiors
- Located Near Town Center
- Private Beach
- 1 vehicle parking
- 4 Neighborhood Pools
- Fitness Center and Racquet Club Access
- 2 Adult Bikes
- Adjacent to Timpoochee Trail
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: The Salty Rose Carriage House is a 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom Rosemary Beach Carriage House that sleeps 6. This posh Carriage House feels like a full-sized home thanks to its spacious living room, full kitchen, and sophisticated interiors. Guests access the Carriage House from an exterior spiral staircase.

Stepping inside, guests will find a lovely living area and kitchen with hardwood floors, a high vaulted ceiling, and a pale color palette. A chef’s kitchen has a Subzero refrigerator, Wolf 4-burner gas range, a farmhouse sink, marble countertops, and a dine-in island that seats 3. Guests can curl up on a sofa and enjoy the gas fireplace, or watch a movie on a 50” Smart TV. A sophisticated primary bedroom has a queen bed, a 50” Smart TV, and a shared bathroom.

A white spiral staircase leads to a loft with 2 full beds, lots of natural light, and a private bathroom.

Guests can explore all that Rosemary Beach has to offer on 2 included adult bikes. With these
finishes and amenities, The Salty Rose Carriage House will book quickly.


SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (Sleeps 6)
CARRIAGE HOUSE:
Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
Guest Bedroom: 2 Full Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

*Your rental property may share a courtyard or lot with a carriage house or main house that is owner-occupied or rented to separate guests from your party. Unfortunately, the private pool is for the main house only.

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Walking distance to the beach
- 3 King en suites
- Access to the 12,000-square-foot community pool
- Complimentary Shuttle Service to the beach March 2-October 31
- 4 Adult Bikes
- Propane grill
- Jumbo-Sized Scrabble Board
- Handicap Accessible - ramp on the side of the home
- 2 dedicated parking spots with additional parking available throughout the community.
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

Please note this community requires a Guest Registration Form to be completed within two weeks of arrival to access the community amenities.

DETAILS: Nestled in the charming community of Seacrest Beach, Our Blue Beso boasts 2,600 square feet of living space with newly updated furniture. With 4 bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms, this lovely abode sleeps 14 a few steps from white Emerald Coast beaches and the Seacrest community pool—the largest pool on 30A! Guests will love the relaxing top-floor deck, fun-filled jumbo-sized Scrabble board, and a handicap-accessible ramp, ensuring everyone enjoys this paradise.

Natural light illuminates the home's beautifully updated furniture and wood floors. Enjoy the oversized queen sleeper sofa and matching armchair in the living area, or sink into the chair beside the 55" TV with a soundbar. The sleek kitchen has high-end appliances, a gorgeous tile backsplash, and spacious island. Enjoy meals at the 8-person dining table or breakfast bar. A beautifully appointed guest bedroom on this floor has a king bed and private bathroom featuring an accessible shower. The propane grill and outdoor shower provide added convenience.

The second floor boasts 3 gorgeous bedrooms. The primary bedroom has a king bed, private bathroom, and balcony. The other bedrooms are equally welcoming—one with a lavish king bed and private bathroom and the other with a twin-over-double bunk and twin trundle. There's a laundry room for added convenience.

With plush furnishings and a versatile queen sleeper sofa, the additional living area on the third floor is perfect for hosting. Guests can entertain themselves with the jumbo Scrabble board and wall-mount TV. An impressive top-floor deck provides a wet bar, a 32" TV, plush outdoor sectional, rocking chairs, and an alfresco dining table—plenty of room to hang out.

Our Blue Beso is the perfect retreat for luxury and comfort. With 2 dedicated on-site parking spots and additional parking throughout the community—plus 4 adult bikes—it's easy to explore Rosemary or Alys Beach. Book this incredible home today!

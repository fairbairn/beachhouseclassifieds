Rosemary Beach "Living the Dream" 32 Rosemary Avenue- Main House
Rosemary Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 7
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf Views
- Spacious Porches and balconies
- 4 Adult Bikes
- Steps from Shopping and Dining
- Short Walk to the Beach
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Start Living the Dream! Tucked a short 2-minute stroll from the white sand beach and Gulf, this beautiful Rosemary Beach home offers an enchanting Emerald Coast escape. With 4 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms, and multiple indoor/outdoor spaces, it comfortably accommodates groups and families up to 10. Enjoy starting the morning with Gulf views in the lookout tower or relaxing on the balconies before heading to the beach, 4 community pools, or the bustling town center for Iconic 30A fun!

A charming porch and light-filled living space welcome guests to this 3-story home’s first level. The 2 guest bedrooms each feature a plush queen bed, while the colorful bunk room has 2 twin-over-twin beds—each includes outdoor access and a private bathroom for ultimate convenience. Guests will also find a laundry room on this floor, so beach gear is always ready to go!

Head up to the second level to discover a stunning, open great room with a flowing floor plan, gorgeous molding, and a mix of contemporary glam and classic coastal styles. The living area’s soft couch, 2 plush armchairs, and TV invite nightly movie marathons. Right behind the space is a wet bar and a concrete breakfast table featuring banquette seating and 3 chairs beneath a beach-inspired chandelier. A few feet away, a round dining table for 8 awaits lively dinner parties over delicious, homemade food from the kitchen, which boasts high-end appliances, striking blue cabinetry, and plenty of storage and prep space. A window seat in the dining area provides extra seating, and down the hall is a cozy den with a loveseat and armchair where kids can play—or adults can retreat. Outside, 2 covered balconies with comfy seating and peaceful views create a luxurious spot to unwind or sip coffee.

The third floor hosts a wet bar and a spacious primary bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom, and peek-a-boo Gulf views from the balcony.

Take in the sweeping views of the water from the lookout tower’s cushioned window seat or bar-top table for 4.

Revel in Living the Dream and a Rosemary Beach getaway! On-site parking and 6 complimentary adult bikes make exploring a breeze. For the nearest beach access, follow Rosemary Avenue, turn right onto South Green Turtle Lane, take another right at Water Street, and find the boardwalk in the middle of the block. Book today!

Guests also have access to 4 or Rosemary's incredible pools, including the Coquina Pool, the Sky Pool, the Barbados Pool and the Cabana Pool. Five adult bikes are on hand to whisk you go and from the beach, amenities, and all of the wonderful shopping and dining opportunities.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (SLEEPS 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: 2 Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

THIRD FLOOR:
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community, and management fines.

*It is possible that your rental property may share a courtyard or lot with a carriage house that is owner-occupied or rented to separate guests from your party.

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Located just north of 30A
- Gulf views from second and third-floor balconies
- Access to Seacrest's 12,000-square-foot community pool
- Complimentary beach shuttle service March 2nd-October 31st
- Surround sound on the third-floor outdoor porch and third-floor bonus living area

- 3 dedicated parking spots with additional parking available throughout community
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

Please note this community requires a Guest Registration Form to be completed within two weeks of arrival to access the community amenities.

DETAILS: Sunny beach days await you at "Pura Vida," a fabulous, three-story vacation haven located in Seacrest Beach. Situated just north of Scenic Highway 30A, "Pura Vida" offers breathtaking views of the Gulf of Mexico from the second and third-floor balconies and is just a short walk to the famed beaches of the Emerald Coast. The spacious living, kitchen, and dining area provides a wonderful place to entertain friends and family while cooking your favorite meal. The third floor includes a second living room complete with a sleeper sofa as well as an outdoor kitchen and gas grill on the balcony. Seacrest Beach's 12,000 square foot pool is also just a short walk away, and guests will greatly enjoy the proximity to the various shopping and dining venues offered by neighboring Rosemary Beach.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 13):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom With Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower Tub Combo

THIRD FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Full-over-Full Bunk Bed With Twin Trundle, Shared Bathroom With Shower Tub Combo
- Bonus Living Area: Queen Sleeper Sofa

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and Callista Vacation Rentals fines.

Grayton Beach "Bella Waves" 60 Hotz Avenue
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-094784
Bedrooms 6
Beds 9
Sleeps 20
Full Baths 8
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Incredible Gulf views
- Private heated pool on 2nd floor, overlooking the Gulf **Pool heating is available for this property from October 1 through May 1st for $30 per day**
- Close proximity to local dining establishments - fantastic food and lively atmosphere
- 1st-floor living area with 3 TVs
- Shuffleboard
- In-home elevator
- Free WiFi
- 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 6 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Experience a luxurious and lively getaway when you book your stay in this incredible home! "Bella Waves" features 6 beds, 8.5 baths, a private pool, a shuffleboard table, and wonderful accommodations for 20 guests. Located just steps from Chiringo and AJ's - two of the best dining spots in Grayton Beach - and with easy access to the local beach tailgate access point, this home is a perfect vacation destination for a large group of your closest adult friends!

The second floor showcases the home's interior common areas, with an open concept floor plan connecting the kitchen, dining, and living room. Exposed wood beams accentuate the expansive footprint, and shiplap walls amplify the abundant natural light from transom windows. The gourmet kitchen will be a favorite spot for the foodies in your group. This area features bright white cabinetry and countertops, a premium gas range, top-of-the-line stainless steel appliances, and a large center island - perfect for sharing hors d'oeuvres and watching the cooks at work. The gorgeous living room is furnished with a large sofa, two plush armchairs, and a mounted flat-screen television.

Guests can choose from a variety of beautiful bedrooms in this home. The first floor offers a spacious bunk room with four sets of twin-over-twin bunk beds and two private bathrooms, as well as a king bed guest room and private bathroom with a shower. The main level features a convenient king bed guest room and a spacious private bathroom. The stunning primary suite is on the third level and boasts a luxurious king bed and a private bathroom with a shower. Guests can also choose from two additional king bedrooms - each featuring a private bathroom with a shower - or a bunk area with a twin-over-twin bunk bed and access to a shared bathroom with a shower.

Entertainment options abound, between the shuffleboard table and game room on the first floor, a stunning covered pool (which can be heated seasonally) and pool deck on the second floor, the close proximity to the best spots in Grayton Beach, including Chiringo, AJ's, and beloved local favorite The Red Bar.

Grab your closest friends and enjoy the getaway you've all been dreaming of. Book your stay at "Bella Waves"!

- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

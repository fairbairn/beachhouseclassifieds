Seagrove Beach 51 Bramble Lane
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 6
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- 6 Passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 2 Community pools
- 4 Bikes
- Elevator
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Enjoy a restful beach getaway at 51 Bramble Lane, a 4-bedroom, 4-bathroom Seagrove Beach home that sleeps 10 guests. Tucked within a gated community, this custom home offers access to 2 community pools and proximity to WaterColor and Old Seagrove. Imagine sun-splashed days spent casually exploring Scenic 30A via the Timpoochee Trail or spending afternoons kayaking across Eastern Lake’s glittering waters. Guests will love relaxing on the home’s shady porches or strolling to the white-sand beach—only a 10-minute walk away.

A spacious front porch furnished with Adirondack chairs sets the tone for a tranquil getaway. Inside the home, swathes of natural light highlight the gorgeous hardwood floors throughout the first floor. To the right is a living space, where a slip-covered corner sofa and 2 plush armchairs offer seating around the 65” Roku TV. On the left, an 8-person wood dining table awaits lively meals, while a spacious kitchen features quartz countertops, a Viking 5-burner gas stove, and a breakfast bar with 3 stools. The back door leads to an airy screened porch with soft patio seating and peaceful nature views—the perfect spot to indulge in a morning brew. Off the porch, guests will find additional seating and a propane grill for delicious barbecues. There is a sleeping nook with a built-in twin-over-twin bed, or guests can retire to the private guest bedroom, accessible only from the back porch.

Take the center staircase or the in-home elevator up to the second level for more sleeping accommodations. The well-appointed primary bedroom boasts a plush king bed, a private bathroom, and a screened-in balcony with cushioned armchairs and a porch swing. Additionally, there are 2 lovely guest bedrooms, each with a queen bed, TV, and a private bathroom. For convenience, the laundry room is located on this level.

Experience the charm and beauty of Seagrove Beach at 51 Bramble Lane. Exploring is easy thanks to parking for 2 vehicles—plus a 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle and 4 complimentary adult bikes. The nearest beach access is at the end of South County Highway 395, with multiple access points of East County Highway 30A. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only (bedroom access is through back porch)
- Bunk Room: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

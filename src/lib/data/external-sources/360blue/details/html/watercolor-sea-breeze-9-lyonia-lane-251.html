WaterColor "Sea Breeze" 9 Lyonia Lane
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 8
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 10 wristbands.
- 4 Adult Bikes
- Gas Grill
- Keurig
- New Large Flat Screen with Apple TV
- WIFI
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout
- This property comes with 2 dedicated parking spot. Additional parking can be found throughout the community.

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: The primary bedroom with a king bed enjoys porch access and a private bathroom with a shower. Another guest bedroom is on the first floor with a queen bed and connecting bath. There are two additional guest bedrooms upstairs, one furnished with a king and the other with two sets of bunks. A new walk-in shower with rainfall shower head is also on the second floor. There are flat screen TV's in every bedroom except the bunk room and a large wall-mounted flat screen with Apple TV in the living room.

9 Lyonia Lane is located in the Phase I WaterColor Tennis District within walking distance to the WaterColor tennis courts and Seaside's Artist District and restaurants. This home is 6 blocks from the beach- perfect for active families who want to experience all that WaterColor resort has to offer. With its prime location leave your car behind and use the 4 adult bikes provided to explore the neighborhood and local restaurants.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 10):
First Floor
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom

Second Floor
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom With Large New Walk-In Rainfall Shower
- Guest Bedroom: 2 Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom With Large New Walk-In Rainfall Shower

WATERCOLOR NEIGHBORHOOD HIGHLIGHTS:
- Named the #1 family vacation destination in the U.S.
- A 499 acre coastal resort and residential community
- Ideal bike community perfect for family getaways
- Pristine sugar sand beaches
- 4 swimming pools
- Award-winning tennis facilities
- Championship golf nearby
- Unparalleled beachside, poolside and lakeside dining
- Acres of parks, gardens and foot paths
- Nearby state parks and preserves

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community and 360 Blue fines.

*Please note: Effective March 15, 2014, Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be made through The Electric Cart Company and will be limited to 80 vehicles throughout the community at any given time. Only one cart may be rented per home and rentals will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis.

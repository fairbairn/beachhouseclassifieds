Beds 7
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Minutes from Rosemary shopping & dining
- One block from Seacrest's 12,000 sq. foot community pool
- 2 dedicated parking spots
- 3 adult bikes
- Screened first-floor porch
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

Please note this community requires a Guest Registration Form to be completed within two weeks of arrival to access the community amenities.

DETAILS: Sunny Shores is a beautiful 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom beach cottage, sleeping 10 in the coveted community of Seacrest Beach. Composed of sun-dappled beaches, tempting eateries, and beguiling boutiques, Seacrest Beach offers a laid-back, family-friendly atmosphere for an ideal Scenic 30A vacation. Enjoy a leisurely day on the sugar-soft beaches or bask in the sunshine by the sparkling community pool—only 1 block away—before returning for a quiet night sipping sangria on the spacious screened front porch.

Surrounded by lush greenery, this charming home which was recently updated with stylish new furnishings welcomes guests with a picnic table on the front lawn, beckoning for cookouts on the Weber gas grill. Continue along the stepping stones to the screened porch with a 4-seat table and 2 cushioned chairs—a restful spot to enjoy your morning coffee, happy hour cocktails, or a cozy al fresco dinner. Inside, coastal-inspired décor, hardwood floors, and a soothing palette set the scene for a relaxing getaway. The living space features 4 armchairs, 2 ottomans, and a comfortable sofa arranged around the 70” brand new Samsung smart TV. On the opposite side of the great room is a dining table for 8, with 4 additional seats at the breakfast bar in the open kitchen. High-end appliances, well-stocked cabinets and plenty of counter space make it easy for the chef of the group to whip up a delicious dinner of local seafood. Keep swimsuits and towels ready for the next jaunt to the water with the stacked washer and dryer. For sleeping accommodations, the living space has a queen sleeper sofa. A study nook with a spacious desk provides a comfortable, stylish and semi-private spot for remote work or study right off the living area.

Upstairs, the primary and guest bedrooms both have king beds. They share access to a covered porch that overlooks the mature trees and greenery and is furnished with 2 chairs and a hanging swing bed. Curl up with the latest beach read and breathe in the salty breeze. Additionally on the second floor, there is a bunk room that is perfect for kids with 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds.

With quick community pool access and a 9-minute walk from the beach, Sunny Shores is a captivating setting for a Seacrest Beach vacation. Delight in exploring the area with 3 adult bikes and 2 parking spots. Access to the beach is a via the Villas at Sunset Beach directly south of Seacrest Beach. Book this lovely beach house today!

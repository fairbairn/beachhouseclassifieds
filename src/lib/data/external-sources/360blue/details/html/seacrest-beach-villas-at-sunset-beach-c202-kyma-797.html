Seacrest Beach Villas At Sunset Beach C202 "KYMA"
Seacrest Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 4
Sleeps 11
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf-front balcony with spectacular views
- Heated community pool (Heated Nov-Apr)
- 5-minute walk to Rosemary Beach
- 10-minute walk to Alys Beach
- Easy access to some of the area's best restaurants, including La Crema, Cowgirl Kitchen and Paradis in Rosemary Beach, George's and Caliza in Alys Beach.
- 5-minute walk east to Crabby Steve's beach bar and restaurant, accessible only from the beach
- Open concept kitchen, dining, and living
- Smart TV in the living room
- 4 adult bikes
- This property only allows 2 cars per unit
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: This impeccable Seacrest Beach unit, "KYMA," has been completely refurbished and offers all the style and comforts one anticipates and expects in a luxury beach-front 30A property. A sophisticated palette of soft beach hues is complemented by the exquisite gulf waters and sugar-white sand. The coastal-inspired, beach-chic furnishings, amazing fixtures, and unique finishes reflect the coveted, relaxed 30A style. With a sprawling and open living, dining, and kitchen space overlooking the stunning Gulf of Mexico, it sets the perfect stage for entertaining friends and family.

Comfortably sleeps 11 in this 4 bedroom, 3 full bath condo, including two master suites, one additional guest bedroom, and a bunk room. After a day in the sun at your backyard beach, take a dip in the community pool! Book your perfect beach getaway at "KYMA" today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 11):
- Master Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Dual Head Shower Only
- Master Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower and Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom With Shower and Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Two Twin Bunk Beds with One Twin Trundle, Shared Bathroom With Shower/Tub Combo

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

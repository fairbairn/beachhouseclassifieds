Rosemary Beach "Red Stick" 190 E Water St
Inlet Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 6
Sleeps 11
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 2
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Short walk to the beach
- Carriage House
- Walking distance to Barrett Square
- Convenient location
- 4 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforters, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Escape to Red Stick in the beautiful Rosemary Beach community located along the highly desired Scenic 30A corridor. This luxurious home and attached carriage house offer 4 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, 2 half-baths, and sleeps 11 people. Guests will have access to Rosemary Beach’s luxurious amenities, including 4 community pools, a fitness facility, and a tennis center. This home is just a short walk from the beach and Barrett Square.

Red Stick’s main house welcomes guests with an open-concept living, dining, and kitchen area. The architectural ceiling beams, natural tones, and wood flooring are warm and inviting. Family and friends can gather and socialize in this combined area. Guests relaxing on the comfortable sofa, or 4 chairs can easily share conversation with others in the kitchen or at the 6-seat dining table. The gourmet kitchen is a delight with the large kitchen island that seats 3, easy-to-use stainless-steel appliances, and a French-door refrigerator. For added convenience, this home has a full-size washer and dryer.

Red Stick offers families or groups of friends the chance to enjoy a beach vacation yet, still have a degree of privacy with 3 bedrooms in the main house—and 1 bedroom in the carriage house. On the second floor of the main house, guests can choose from 1 primary bedroom, 1 guest bedroom, and a bunk bedroom with a full-over-queen bunk.

For even more privacy, guests can sleep in the carriage house using the sleeper sofa, twin Murphy bed, or private guest bedroom. This carriage house is a quaint little getaway with an additional living, dining, and kitchen area.

For guests looking for adventure, the local area offers many different shops and eateries with the chance to sample local cuisine, tapas, or wine. Guests can explore the Rosemary Beach area on 4 bikes included in this vacation home. Red Stick offers spacious accommodations and is a popular destination, so it will book fast!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 11):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Half Bathroom

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bedroom, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: Full-over-Queen Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

CARRIAGE HOUSE:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bedroom, Private Half Bath, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
- Additional Living Space: Twin Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only


In addition, you also have access to these fantastic Rosemary Beach amenities:
- Coquina Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.
- Sky Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 6 am to 10 pm (Adult Only Hours, 6 am-9 am & 8 pm-10 pm)
- Cabana Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.
- Barbados Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.

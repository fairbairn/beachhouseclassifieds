DETAILS: Experience a charming coastal getaway at 83 West Salt Box! Tucked within the picturesque gated community of WaterSound, this 5-bedroom, 6-bathroom Main Home and Carriage House offer exceptional accommodations for 16 guests. Discover a thriving neighborhood of winding boardwalks, rolling dunes, and luxe shared amenities, including 2 pools and a puttering park. Take a morning stroll to the beach—only 7 minutes away—before heading over to The Big Chill for a night of food and fun! Guests will love spending quiet afternoons relaxing on the beautiful porches or cooling off in the private saltwater pool.

Modern aesthetics and seaside style create an airy and elegant escape inside the 2-story Main Home’s great room. After a day on the beach, the inviting living area’s soft sofas and 2 armchairs encourage guests to pile in for a viewing of the latest Summer blockbuster on the 64” Smart TV. A nearby dining table for 10 awaits seafood feasts and lively nights of board games. The expansive gourmet kitchen is a food lover’s playground, featuring a Thermador stove and a 3-stool breakfast bar for entertaining. Sip lemonade in the blissful breeze on the screened-in porch or barbecue on the outdoor kitchen’s natural gas grill. In the courtyard, bask in the sunshine on the 4 poolside loungers and let the serene sound of the pool’s waterfalls chase worries away. Enjoy a refreshing night’s sleep in the stunning primary bedroom with a plush king bed and a private bathroom.

Upstairs are 2 guest bedrooms with king beds and private bathrooms, alongside a nautical-inspired bunk room with 4 twin-over-twin beds. All the rooms feature access to the spacious upper porch with a 4-person patio table and cushioned porch swing.

Inside the secluded Carriage House is a full kitchen, a sunlit living area with a TV and 2 armchairs—plus 1 guest bedroom boasting a king bed and private bathroom. A twin-over-twin bunk bed in the hallway offers extra sleeping accommodations.

Let 83 West Salt Box host an unforgettable WaterSound getaway. There’s parking for 2 vehicles, and guests can leisurely explore with 5 complimentary adult bikes and a 4-passenger golf cart. For beach access, use any of WaterSound’s boardwalks. Book today!

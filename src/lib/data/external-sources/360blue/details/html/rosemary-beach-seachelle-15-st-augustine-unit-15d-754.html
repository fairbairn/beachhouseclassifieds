Rosemary Beach "SeaChelle" 15 St Augustine Unit 15D
Rosemary Beach, FL
Bedrooms 2
Beds 2
Sleeps 5
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Blocks from Rosemary Center
- St. Augustine Park views
- Private balcony
- Building elevator
- Located next to St. Augustine Park and the Rosemary Beach Community Complex and Pool
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: “SeaChelle” is a 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom condo that sleeps 5 in Rosemary Beach and is only a 7-minute walk to famous sugar-white sands and emerald waters. Guests will enjoy the private balcony with views of St. Augustine Park, walking along quaint cobbled streets and tree-lined boardwalks, and being close to gorgeous pools, shopping, and award-winning cuisine.

Beautiful condo, lots of natural light, and carefully selected artwork create a comfortable coastal feel in SeaChelle. Guests will enjoy the privacy of having the 2 bedrooms located behind the kitchen and living spaces. Both bedrooms feature award-winning, environmentally responsible Saatva Classic mattresses to help guests get the best sleep of their lives. A primary bedroom has a king bed and a private bathroom with a soaking tub and separate shower. A guest bedroom has a queen bed and shares a bathroom with a shower/tub combo.

A fully stocked galley kitchen with granite countertops overlooks a round dining table that seats 4. A cozy sofa provides a comfy spot to gather and watch TV or enjoy views from the floor-to-ceiling windows that fill SeaChelle with soft, natural light. French doors open onto a wide, shaded porch overlooking Rosemary Beach’s popular St. Augustine Park.

Exit the front entrance to explore St. Augustine Park or take the back entrance and discover the Butterfly Garden, fitness trail, and other popular pocket parks along a wooden boardwalk system that leads to the beach. SeaChelle is in a quieter area of Rosemary Beach that offers a bit of respite from the busy goings-on along 30A and the beach but is close to amenities like the Sky Pool, the Racquet Club, beach access, and restaurants. It’s the ultimate beach escape! It won’t be available for long!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Rollaway bed stored in closet

In addition, you also have access to the these fantastic Rosemary Beach amenities:
- Coquina Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.
- Sky Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 6 am to 10 pm (Adult Only Hours, 6 am-9 am & 8 pm-10 pm)
- Cabana Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.
- Barbados Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

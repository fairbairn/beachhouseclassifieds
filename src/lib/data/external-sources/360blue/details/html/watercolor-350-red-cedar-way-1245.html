WaterColor 350 Red Cedar Way
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-049062
Bedrooms 3
Beds 5
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 8 wristbands.
- *Newly remodeled bathrooms
- 3 Adult Bikes and Helmets
- Private backyard
- Additional Second-Floor Living Space
- Natural Gas Grill
- Screened-in Porch with Daybed
- 2 Blocks from Camp WaterColor and Tennis Center
- Premium Cable Includes HBO, SHOWTIME and Sports Package
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: This thoroughly charming 3-bedroom, 2.5-bath cottage in WaterColor's Camp District is the perfect blend of comfort, style and convenience. Tucked away on a wooded lot just 2 blocks from Camp WaterColor and the Tennis Center, 350 Red Cedar Way features stunning design with a screen-in porch, wood ceilings, a chef's kitchen and a second living space on the second floor.

Entering through the front door, cherry hardwood floors and overstuffed sofas accent the living space which features a vast flat-screen television - the perfect place for vacation movie night. The kitchen features granite countertops, designer cabinets and select stainless appliances. The dining area offers table seating for 8 before a fireplace, complete with a stone hearth and chimney. The counter has additional seating for 3, while a private breakfast nook offers seating for 4.

Located on the first floor the primary bedroom has a king bed, flat-screen television and a private bath with shower only. Ascending the staircase, a second living space features a sectional sofa, flat-screen television and a twin-over-twin bunkbed - the perfect space for the young and the young at heart alike. Adjacent are two guest bedrooms -- both with king beds, flat-screen televisions, and a shared bath with shower only.

It's the outdoors spaces that truly set this home apart. The screened-in porches and private backyard allow guests to enjoy the natural setting of WaterColor itself. A daybed on the porch is a great place for a nap. The thoughtful design that incorporates the character of Old Florida with modern amenities makes 350 Red Cedar Way the perfect coastal retreat for up to 8 guests.

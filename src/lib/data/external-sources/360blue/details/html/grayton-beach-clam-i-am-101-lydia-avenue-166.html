Grayton Beach "Clam I Am" 101 Lydia Avenue
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 2
Beds 4
Sleeps 6
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- South Side of 30A Grayton Beach
- On Lake with Private Dock
- Walk or Bike to The Red Bar and Chiringo!
- Large Back Deck with Outdoor Seating and Firepit
- Walking distance to the beach
- 2 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Nestled among the towering oak trees of 30A's Grayton Beach, Clam I Am is a beautifully remodeled 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom cottage that sleeps 6. Boasting almost 100 feet of direct Western Lake access and a private dock, this charming home offers endless opportunities for lake adventures, from paddling to the beach to kayaking over to WaterColor for a quick lunch. Guests will love the large back deck with outdoor seating and a firepit, which is perfect for relaxing. Best of all, the world-renowned beaches of Grayton are only a short 5-minute walk away!

Step onto the welcoming front porch, where a spacious picnic table, seating 8, invites guests to unwind in the gentle breeze and enjoy views of the shimmering lake—a rare coastal treasure! The beautifully designed great room, adorned with modern coastal décor, captures the laid-back essence of beach life, where guests can relax and reminisce with friends on the comfy couch and 3 armchairs or connect to the integrated Sonos for a private dance party. A quaint dining table seats 4 for sit-down meals, while the chic dining nook seats 4 more —ideal for game nights or quiet conversation. With light creamy cabinetry, sleek countertops, and high-end appliances, the chef's kitchen is perfect for preparing gourmet meals while savoring the art of cooking. The primary bedroom offers restful nights, featuring a luxurious king bed, TV, and private bathroom, all connected to an inviting seating area with a TV and oversized chair—perfect for quiet moments or relaxed entertainment. An equally delightful guest bedroom showcases a cozy king bed, TV, and private bathroom alongside a charming sleeping nook with 2 twin trundle beds and a TV—perfect for families with small children. A washer and dryer add convenience. Spend quality time together on the spectacular screened porch, featuring cozy seating and tranquil lake views, or gather on the spacious deck for memorable alfresco meals and golden sunsets. Evenings are best spent around the firepit, making s'mores, surrounded by the natural beauty of Walton County's pristine coastal dune lakes.

Clam I Am artfully combines comfort and style with a connection to nature. On-site parking and 2 complimentary adult bikes make it easy to explore the surrounding area, including The Red Bar and Chiringo! Don't miss an incredible experience in one of the most pristine beach destinations in the nation—book today!

Blue Mountain Beach "Beachin on Porpoise" 53 Porpoise Street
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-120710
Bedrooms 5
Beds 5
Sleeps 11
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Short walk to the beach
- Private pool *Pool heating is available from October 1st through May 1st for an additional fee of $25 per day.

- 6 passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 5 adult bikes
- 2 Washer and dryer sets
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: "Beachin on Porpoise" is a 5-bedroom, 5-bathroom Blue Mountain Beach home that sleeps 11. This spacious home features a private pool with plenty of deck space and areas to lounge, a fenced yard, 2 balconies, and a 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle—all only minutes from beach access, shops, and restaurants.

A cozy front porch opens to a lovely living area and a small hall with hooks and a bench for bags and beach gear. A door off this area opens to a cheerful guest bedroom with a queen bed and private bathroom. Guests can relax on a sofa or armchairs and enjoy a favorite show on a 65” Roku Smart TV, with a dry bar beneath it. Sliding glass doors lead to a covered porch with hanging wicker chairs. Chaise lounge chairs and cushioned benches surround a beautiful pool with views of the yard.

On the second floor are 2 guest bedrooms with queen beds and private bathrooms. An open living, kitchen, and dining area enjoy beautiful neighborhood views. A dining table seats 6 and is surrounded by large windows. The adjacent kitchen area has premium appliances, a dine-in island that seats 5, and a 6-burner gas range. A comfortable living area has 2 sofas and armchairs that surround a 75” Smart TV, and enjoy views from sliding glass doors that open to a balcony with 2 hanging wicker chairs.

On the third floor, kids will enjoy a bunk room with a twin-over-twin bed with twin trundle, while guests staying in the primary bedroom will appreciate the clean lines of this spacious bedroom. A king bed enjoys views of the private balcony. Guest can recline on a hanging daybed swing on the balcony and enjoy the breeze!

Guests staying at 53 Porpoise Street will love the 5 complimentary adult bikes and included 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle. To reach the Blue Mountain Regional Beach Access, turn left onto Flounder Street and right onto Blue Fish Street. Follow to Scenic 30A, then turn left. At the intersection of County Highway 83, turn right to reach the beach access. This is about a 3-minute drive. Book this incredible Blue Mountain Beach home today.

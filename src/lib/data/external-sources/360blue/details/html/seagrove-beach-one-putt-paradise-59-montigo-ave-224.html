Seagrove Beach "One Putt Paradise" 59 Montigo Ave
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 8
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 2
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Located in the heart of Seagrove Beach
- Private pool *Pool heating is available October 1 through May 1st for an additional fee of $30 per day.
- Putting green
- Peloton bike
- 4 adult bikes
- In-home elevator
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Located in the heart of Seagrove Beach, One Putt Paradise is a beautiful Seagrove Beach home comfortably accommodating 12 guests with 4 bedrooms, 3 full bathrooms, and 2 half baths. Generations of families have flocked to this quintessential 30A beach town for its sugar-white sand and natural beauty. Enjoy sun-kissed days relaxing poolside, playing on the putting green, and hitting up the beach—only a 3-minute stroll away!

Step inside this 2-story coastal retreat to discover dark hardwood floors and shiplap detailing throughout the combined living, dining, and kitchen spaces. Meals are a breeze thanks to the fully equipped kitchen’s high-end appliances and plentiful counter space. In the living area, a soft white couch and 2 matching armchairs provide comfortable seating around the TV. A few steps away is a round table for 4, where guests can enjoy savory meals or lively card games. Meander outside to swim in the private pool or refine skills on the putting green. A 6-seat table and propane grill call for barbecue dinners while enjoying the sunset. When bedtime arrives, choose between the 2 guest bedrooms with private bathrooms on this floor—the first bedroom has a king bed, and the second has a queen bed.

Take the outdoor stairs or the in-home elevator to the second level. An additional living area with a couch, armchairs, and TV offers space for guests to spread out—or let the kids have their own place to play while the adults hang out downstairs. The bright sunroom features a Peloton bike for quick workouts and a 4-seat table. On the upper balcony, sip cocktails at the high-top table for 4 while surrounded by greenery. This level hosts 2 guest bedrooms with queen beds and a bunk room with 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds.

Experience a carefree Seagrove Beach getaway at One Putt Paradise. Exploring is easy with parking for 3 vehicles—plus 4 complimentary adult bikes and proximity to the Timpoochee Trail. Santa Clara Regional Beach Access is a quick walk. Head down Montigo Avenue, turn right on East County Highway 30A, and turn left onto Bramble Grove Place. Book today for a beach vacation to remember!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (SLEEPS 12):
FIRST FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Jack and Jill Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Jack and Jill Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
-Bunk Room: Two Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds Opening out to an Extra Living Area

*Please note that there are no interior stairs to get to the second floor from inside the house. There are exterior stairs, or the use of the elevator

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger.

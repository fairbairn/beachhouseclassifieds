WaterColor "Beachside #8" 1848 E. Co. Hwy 30A
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 6
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
*Per Watercolor HOA, please include all guests aged 5 and older in your total count of adult guests to receive amenity and pool access wristbands. Sleep arrangements are provided as a courtesy and are not considered in the occupancy count.
**This home is allowed 10 amenity access bands.**

- Gulf-front !!
- Exclusive condo pool
- Steps away from the WaterColor Beach Club
- Amazing balcony views of the emerald waters
- 3 Adult Bikes (4 Max Per HOA)
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforters, laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Serenity on 30A is a 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom, Gulf-front condo that sleeps 10 in the world-renowned resort community of WaterColor. With access to a shared private condo pool directly off the back porch and the beach just steps beyond that, this property provides the ultimate fun-in-the-sun experience for the whole family.

The main floor welcomes guests in with natural wood and light tones that create a calming ambiance in this two-story condo. The kitchen is a cook's delight, complete with pristine white cabinetry and granite countertops, surrounded by two breakfast bars—seating up to 7 guests. The traditional dining area, with a modern light fixture overhead, accommodates 6 guests. The living area is sure to impress with its comfortable white linen sectional and two armchairs, all situated around the Smart TV. Step onto the furnished outdoor balcony with an alfresco dining table, while taking in the gorgeous emerald water views from the second floor. There are two bedrooms inside where guests can retire—One primary and one guest bedroom, both with private bathrooms.

Downstairs, guests will enjoy an additional living area complete with queen sleeper sofa, Smart TV, and a cozy twin-over-twin bunk bed with trundle—perfect for kids or last-minute visitors. An adjacent guest bedroom with 2 comfortable queen beds is accessible through the French doors, while the laundry room is down the hall. Access the condo’s private pool and dune walkover from the back door!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS:
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Two Queen Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower
- Additional Living Area: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed with Twin Trundle Bed and Queen Sleeper Sofa, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower

Disclaimer: All Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be arranged through The Electric Cart Company. Only one cart is allowed per home, and rentals are on a first-come, first-served basis, with a limit of 60 vehicles allowed in the community at any time. Additionally, WaterColor condominium buildings do not have exterior outlets for charging Low-Speed Vehicles/Low-Speed Vehicles (LSVs). 360 Blue does not provide extension cords for charging. Please discuss charging needs directly with your rental company.

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments, as well as be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.


**No Pets or Smoking Are Permitted At This Property**

Seagrove "Sunset Grove 30A" 69 E Grove Ave
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 6
Beds 12
Sleeps 17
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 2
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- PUBLIC BEACH ACCESS STEPS AWAY!
- Private Heated Pool **Pool heating is available October 1-May 1 for $40 per day**
- 6 adult bikes
- 6-passenger LSV golf cart
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Escape for coastal fun at Sunset Grove 30A! Nestled in Seagrove Beach, this gorgeous home accommodates 17 guests and boasts 6 bedrooms, 5 bathrooms, and 2 half baths, making it perfect for large groups and families. Guests will love the private heated pool, outdoor kitchen, firepit, and quick beach access—only a short 3-minute stroll!

The embodiment of effortless elegance, this 3-level home’s light-filled foyer leads into a spacious great room, where warm laminate wood floors, a beamed ceiling, and crisp white décor create a cheerful atmosphere for entertaining. Prepare homemade meals in the open kitchen, equipped with Thermador appliances and ample counter space, including a roomy island that doubles as a 4-stool bar. For dining, pile into the charming breakfast booth for 6 or gather at the 8-person dining table. Steps away, a plush, slip-covered sectional and matching armchairs invite guests to unwind in the air conditioning or watch TV. The enclosed backyard offers a private oasis with elegant sun loungers and a sheltered patio featuring cushioned seating, a 6-seat alfresco table, and a built-in outdoor kitchen. Spend sunlit hours reclining poolside or cooling off in the crystal-clear water. When night falls, the firepit beckons for s’mores and stargazing. The beautiful primary bedroom, boasting a king bed and private bathroom, offers a secluded haven for rest, while the queen sleeper sofa in the living room provides additional sleeping accommodations.

A flex space with a 60” Smart TV and comfy seating await on the second level, alongside 2 well-appointed guest bedrooms, each featuring 1 king bed and a private bathroom, and 2 bunk rooms with 1 twin-over-queen bed and 2 twin-over-twin bunks, respectively. The latter bunk room and bedroom share access to a shaded porch with hanging wicker chairs.

On the third floor, enjoy a peaceful night in the luxurious primary bedroom with a king bed and a private bathroom.

Guests will love spending time in the fourth-floor lookout tower, complete with a wet bar and furnished deck.

Sunset Grove 30A has everything guests need for a blissful Seagrove Beach escape, including on-site parking—plus 6 complimentary adult bikes and a 6-passenger low-speed vehicle for easy exploration. To reach the nearest beach access, head west on East Grove Avenue, continue south on County Highway 395, cross East County Highway 30A, then follow the boardwalk to the beach. Book today!

- Guest Bedroom: Two Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds

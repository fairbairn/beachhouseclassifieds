Seacrest Beach "Barefoot Bungalow" 87 The Greenway Loop
Seacrest Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-082518
Bedrooms 3
Beds 4
Sleeps 6
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Walking distance to the beach
- Access to the 12,000-square-foot community pool
- Complimentary Shuttle Service to the beach March 2-October 31
- Propane grill
- Wireless Internet
- 3 Adult Bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Please note this community requires a Guest Registration Form to be completed within two weeks of arrival to access the community amenities.

DETAILS: Barefoot Bungalow is an adorable 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom Seacrest Beach home with refined
interiors that sleeps 6. Guests will enjoy the home’s two spacious porches and access to a 12,000
-square-foot community pool and seasonal shuttle service to the beach.

A shaded front porch with cushioned chairs and overhead fans welcomes guests to Barefoot
Bungalow. Stepping inside, guests discover elegant coastal interiors with white shiplap
wainscoting, hickory hardwood floors, and luxurious finishes. The spacious kitchen has views of
the front porch, beautiful quartz countertops, a 4-burner gas range, and lots of space to prepare
meals and store beach snacks. An elegant dining table divides the kitchen from the living area,
which is decorated in soothing tones of gray and taupe. Guests can unwind from a fun day in
the sun on one of the comfortable sofas and enjoy a favorite show on a 55” Smart TV equipped
with a SONOS surround sound system. French doors from the living area open to an expansive
back porch with a lovely outdoor dining table and a gas grill, making it simple to dine al fresco.
Off the living area are two guest bedrooms that share a bathroom and the primary bedroom.
Younger guests will love the bright surfing theme of a guest bedroom with two twin beds. A
second guest bedroom has a full/queen bed with an upholstered headboard and beaded chandelier.
Guests staying in the primary bedroom will find a calming, all-white space with a beautiful king
bed, beaded chandelier, and French doors that open to the back porch. The bedroom’s private
bathroom has marble tile, a glass-enclosed shower, and custom cabinets.

Guests can walk to many shops and restaurants in Seacrest Beach or explore nearby Alys
Beach and Rosemary Beach on 3 included adult bikes. With its gorgeous interiors and
fabulous location, Barefoot Bungalow will book quickly. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (SLEEPS 6):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: 2 Twin Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments, as well as be subject to HOA, community, and Callista Vacation Rentals fines.

Watercolor "Blessed Beaches" 116 East Royal Fern Way
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 10
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
-Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 12 wristbands.
- Steps Away from the Dragonfly Community Pool
- 5 adult bikes
- Bonus room
- Gas grill
- Surrounded by natural green space and parks
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the WaterColor Beach Club. It features a main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well as an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: A heavenly beach vacation awaits at Blessed Beaches in WaterColor, boasting 5 bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms. This lovely 2-level home accommodates up to 12 guests, only steps away from Dragonfly Pool and a short stroll to beach access. With the community’s range of resort-style amenities—including Camp WaterColor’s lazy river and the coveted beach club’s sunset bar and zero-entry pool—plus all of 30A’s warm-weather activities, every day promises new excitement!

Worries have no place in this tranquil beach abode. As guests arrive, a spacious screened-in porch sets the scene for unhurried mornings. Imagine sipping coffee on the Adirondack chairs or watching the quiet street from the comfortable porch swing, while the overhead fan coaxes a gentle breeze from the balmy air. Inside, coastal comfort and charm abound throughout the bright and airy great room. Solid wood flooring brings warmth to the neutral, cool tones and white wainscoting. Settle into the 2 soft sofas and 2 barrel armchairs around the TV for a comfy evening of togetherness. Past the living area, an elegant dining table seats up to 8 guests for syrupy breakfasts and lively dinners prepared in the chef’s kitchen, where guests will find high-end appliances, granite countertops, and a breakfast bar with 3 wicker stools. The laundry closet, with a side-by-side washer and dryer, adds convenience for light packers. After a day of excitement, the lovely primary bedroom invites rest with a plush king bed and a private bathroom.

A classic wooden staircase leads to the second level, where an additional living area with a queen sleeper sofa and TV offers a hangout for teens or a cozy spot to curl up with a book.

This level’s primary bedroom boasts a king bed, a luxurious private bathroom, and a furnished balcony, and the guest bedroom also features a king bed and private bathroom. Perfect for kids, the 2 bunk rooms respectively feature 1 twin-over-twin bunk bed and 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds.

Idyllic days of WaterColor fun await at Blessed Beaches! On-site parking, plus 5 complimentary bikes, makes it easy to explore Iconic 30A. For private beach access, visit the WaterColor Beach Club. The nearest public beach access is Scenic 395, only a short 13-minute stroll down East Royal Fern Way and South County Highway 395. Book today!

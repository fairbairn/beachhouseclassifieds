Watercolor 306 Red Cedar Way
Santa Rosa Beach, FL

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 10 wristbands.
- Located in the Camp District of WaterColor
- Access to community pools, nature trails, and dune lakes
- Conveniently located near Publix & restaurants
- Private Pool **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for $25 per day**
- Screened porch
- Outdoor fireplace
- Outdoor dining
- Gas grill
- 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 4 adult bikes
- 2 dedicated parking spots with additional parking throughout community.
- Complete Clean Linen Participant-ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Dreaming of a WaterColor vacation where you have the convenience of the beach, lake and still be in the center of all the action on 30A? You can have it all at 306 Red Cedar Way! This 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home has many notable highlights, including a private pool, screened porch, outdoor entertainment area, Low-Speed Vehicle, and complimentary bikes. To top it all off, it's located in the Camp District of WaterColor, which is near the Town Center of WaterColor, nature trails, dune lakes, Seaside, and it is a short bike ride to the beaches.

The first floor is the home's main floor, which encompasses an open living and kitchen area. The kitchen has premium finishes such as stainless-steel appliances and sparkling white backsplash and countertops. There is seating for 14 at both the breakfast bar and dining table. The living area has plenty of seating surrounding the flat-screen TV and opens up to the screened porch. The porch acts as an additional living area when guests want to enjoy the outdoors, featuring an outdoor fireplace, flat-screen TV, and separate dining area with seating for 12 at the custom-built farm table. There is also a grill for guests to enjoy preparing a fresh meal while enjoying the home's private pool. The first floor also has a half-bathroom and full laundry room down the hall off of the kitchen.

Upstairs, guests can find all of the bedrooms, including 3 matching king suites with private bathrooms. The final room features 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds with a bathroom that is shared with the hallway twin bunk beds. There is also a screened porch on this floor that overlooks the quiet streets of Red Cedar Way.

Guests will have access to the newly renovated WaterColor Beach Club, which has multiple beachfront pools and casual dining options for all ages to enjoy. It's easy to commute around 30A and the neighborhood with a 6-seater Low-Speed Vehicle and 4 adult bikes, making Publix grocery runs or bike rides to brunch in Seaside a breeze. This home is perfect for groups of friends or extended family looking for a peaceful, sun-filled getaway!

Please note: Effective Mar 15, 2014, Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be made through The Electric Cart Company and will be limited to 80 vehicles throughout the community at any given time. Only 1 cart may be rented per home, and rentals will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis.

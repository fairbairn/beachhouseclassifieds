Seaside "A Ritz Sea Inn" 27 Quincy Circle
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-040864
Bedrooms 2
Beds 3
Sleeps 6
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Short walk to the beach
- Gulf views from front porch balcony
- Steps from the famous Seaside Town Square featuring plenty of shopping and dining
- Parking for 1 vehicle
- 2 adult bikes
- Pet friendly
- Fitness Center
- Community pool
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Located in the scenic Seaside community, A Ritz Sea Inn is a luxurious 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom townhouse accommodating up to 6 guests. This pet-friendly property offers access to exclusive amenities like a fitness center and community pool. Situated steps from the Emerald Coast's pristine beaches and close to Seaside Town Square's shops and restaurants, it features captivating Gulf views from its front balcony, making it an ideal retreat for relaxation and convenience.

This second-floor luxury townhome offers both stair and elevator access, ensuring convenience right from the start. Inside, the space opens up to reveal 12-foot-tall ceilings and dark hardwood floors that elegantly blend with rich furnishings and modern interior touches throughout. The comfortable living area is outfitted with a queen sleeper sofa and cozy armchair facing a TV, making it an ideal spot for relaxation or entertainment. Natural light floods in through tall windows, highlighting the fully equipped kitchen that boasts high-end appliances, granite countertops, and a breakfast bar seating for 4. This makes both cooking and socializing a delight. The front porch balcony presents stunning Gulf views and features an expansive area with a full dining table, perfect for alfresco meals. Ample seating also makes it a serene spot for relaxing and soaking in the natural beauty of the area. Accommodation includes 2 beautifully-appointed guest bedrooms, each with a queen bed—1 bedroom boasts the added luxury of a private bathroom. There’s also a laundry room for added convenience.

A Ritz Sea Inn blends luxury with comfort, offering a perfect retreat for those seeking a Gulf-front location. With 1 designated parking spot and 2 adult bikes, it’s easy to explore the surrounding area and other beach communities. Enjoy access to the Town Center beach access, directly in front of Bud and Alleys Restaurant. It's only a short stroll away. Book this incredible townhome today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 6):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
- Living Room: Queen Sleeper Sofa

*Unfortunately, the HOA in this community prohibits Low-Speed Vehicle rentals or guests from bringing their own.

*We are happy to welcome up to 2 dogs, not to exceed the weight limit of 50lbs for an additional non-refundable pet fee of $400.

*Please note this unit is all one level on the 2nd floor.

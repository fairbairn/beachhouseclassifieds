Blue Mountain Beach 178 Buddy Street
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-024408
Bedrooms 5
Beds 9
Sleeps 15
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- 4 adult bikes
- Outdoor pergola with swing
- Grill
- Private pool *Pool heating is only available from October 1st through December 31st and March 1st through April 30th for an additional fee of $50 per day.
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Sun-warmed days of pool and seaside fun await at 178 Buddy Street, a 5-bedroom, 3-bathroom Blue Mountain Beach home that comfortably accommodates 15 guests. Named after the area’s indigenous blue lupine flowers, this Gulf community is famed for its laid-back atmosphere, artist studios, boutiques, and coastal dune lakes. Guests will love sunbathing by the private pool or taking a quick stroll to the beach—only 7 minutes away!

Crisp white shiplap walls, mixed with pops of blue, woven rugs, and natural wood floors, create an inviting and cheerful environment throughout this 2-story home. The living area’s plush couch and loveseat beckon guests to sit back and enjoy evenings filled with chardonnay and endless conversation. Adjacent barn doors lead to a media room with a TV, 2 armchairs, and a soft couch—a cozy spot for movie nights or entertaining little ones. Around the corner, past the dining table for 8, is the chef’s kitchen featuring high-end appliances and an island that doubles as a 4-stool breakfast bar. A sunny sitting room with 4 armchairs and a TV is to the side and leads out to the peaceful backyard. Stroll out to find a propane grill and a charming outdoor living area. Strung lights, a soft daybed swing, and cushioned wicker chairs create an idyllic atmosphere for unwinding beneath the pergola. On the deck, enjoy lazy days lounging beside the crystal-clear pool or sipping lemonade at the umbrella-shaded patio tables. At the day’s end, there are 2 bedrooms on this level—a primary bedroom with a king bed, Roku TV, and a private bathroom, plus a lovely guest bedroom with a queen bed.

On the second level, discover 2 guest bedrooms, each with a king bed and Roku TV, alongside a bunk area with 2 twin-over-twin beds and 1 twin bed.

Experience relaxed coastal living, and all Blue Mountain Beach offers at 178 Buddy Street. Parking for 3 vehicles makes it easy to explore the nearby Scenic 30A communities. For beach access, head west on Buddy Street and turn left onto Gulfview Heights Street. The beach access is at the end of the road. Book today!

- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

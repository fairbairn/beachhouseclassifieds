WaterSound Compass Point II #422
Watersound, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 4
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gated Community with on-site Security
- Gulf-front
- Short walk to the Dunesider Pool and Watersound Beach Access
- Panoramic Gulf views
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach -service set (2 beach chairs and 1umbrella)
- Private deeded beach access
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout
- NO PETS ALLOWED

*The property does not come with dedicated parking spaces, first come first serve parking only. Please note first come first serve parking is limited and not guaranteed. This condo permits a maximum of 2 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: Stunning 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home in WaterSound's Compass Point! This Gulf-front, professionally decorated condo is inspired by the signature style of luxury 30A design. It can sleep 8 guests. The modern interiors of the open kitchen and living room are set against a light gray wall, giving the space an opulent look and feel. The unit's towering floor-to-ceiling windows provide incredible panoramic views of the Gulf! The chef's kitchen is stocked with all the tools needed to prep a gourmet meal for your group. The formal dining table seats 8 guests, and the island seats an additional 3 guests. Custom cabinetry and hardware, stainless steel appliances, and quartz countertops are just a few of the fabulous highlights seen here. There is also a sprawling balcony with additional outdoor seating to provide the perfect backdrop for a happy hour mingle or al-fresco style brunch.

The primary bedroom includes a king bed, private bathroom, balcony access, and office nook. The second guest room also features a king bed with an attached bathroom and balcony access. 2 queen beds with a private bathroom are in the final bedroom, making this unit perfect for multi-generational families. There is also a half-bathroom off of the living area.

Guests will love the amenities of staying in a private, gated resort community with plenty of local activities nearby-including golf, playgrounds, nature trails, community pools, and more! Don't miss your chance to stay at Compass Point.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 8):
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower
- Guest Bedroom: Two Queen Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*From October 2025 through 2026, WaterSound Compass Point will undergo major exterior refurbishment. Residents and guests should expect construction, boarded windows, limited balcony and garage access, and possible parking disruptions. These safety measures are essential for project completion.

*Weddings or parties/groups of people or guest of guest are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

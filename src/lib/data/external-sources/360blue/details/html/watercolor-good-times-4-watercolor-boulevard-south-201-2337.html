WaterColor "Good Times" 4 Watercolor Boulevard South #201
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 2
Beds 2
Sleeps 4
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor Community guidelines the maximum number of wristbands this home is allotted is 4 bands to access WaterColor amenities and community pools
- Located in the beautiful Beachside district of WaterColor
- Surrounded by natural green space and parks
- In the heart of WaterColor with retail and restaurants
- 2 adult bikes provided
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

*Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the WaterColor Beach Club. It features a main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: A minute walk from beach access, in the heart of WaterColor, is where Good Times awaits. This lovely 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom condo sleeps 4 guests in luxe comfort. Enjoy exceptional proximity to shops, restaurants, and the numerous amenities of illustrious WaterColor. With a beach club, boathouse, lazy river, walking paths, and parks, this community features a never-ending roster of activities. Guests will love enjoying brunch on the shaded balcony before visiting the pristine beaches or community pools.

Discover an elegant interior of warm wood, detailed trim, and soaring ceilings inside this 2-level condo. Guests are greeted by a beautiful sunroom with a soft couch and armchairs—a tempting spot to unwind with a book or an afternoon catnap. Continue into the great room, adorned with modern art, stylish light fixtures, and contemporary furniture. The chef of the group will appreciate the spacious, open kitchen featuring KitchenAid appliances, quartz countertops, and a breakfast bar for 4. After a day at the beach, gather for chilled cocktails as dinner simmers before adjourning to the 4-person table. The living space is comfortably outfitted with a plush sofa, 2 matching armchairs, and 2 ottomans facing the 75” Smart TV and soundbar. French doors off the living space beckon guests onto a vast covered balcony with cushioned patio furniture and a 4-seat round table to enjoy the coastal atmosphere.

Venture upstairs to find a luxurious primary bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom, Smart TV, and a furnished balcony. The guest bedroom is equally inviting, with a comfy queen bed and Smart TV. A washer and dryer are located in the utility closet for convenience.

Experience a charmed WaterColor getaway at Good Times. On-site parking for 1 vehicle makes it easy to visit nearby Grayton and Seagrove Beaches. There are 2 complimentary adult bikes, or guests can hop on the seasonal trolley to explore the neighborhood. Designated beach access is available via the WaterColor Beach Club, only a short walk down WaterColor Boulevard North. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (Sleeps 4):
SECOND FLOOR:
-Master Bedroom: King Bed, Bathroom with Shower/Jacuzzi Tub Separate
-Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community and 360 Blue fines.

Blue Mountain "Beach Breezes" 189 Old Beach Road
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 8
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf views
- Private Beach Access
- Walking distance to the beach
- Private pool **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for $25 per day**
- Private, gated community with community pool - the pool is heated Spring and Fall months only
- Draper Lake views
- Convenient location
- Wet bar and outdoor commercial fridge
- Open sun deck
- 6 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: A white picket fence and a cute front porch greet guests to "Beach Breezes." This 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom Blue Mountain Beach home sleeps 12 and has a private pool, Gulf views, and a relaxed, coastal vibe.

Enjoy breakfast on the wide front porch from a table crafted from a surfboard, or greet neighbors from a comfy porch chair. Inside, floor-to-ceiling windows and exposed ceiling beams add formality to a casual living room with slipcovered furniture. Across from the living room is a primary bedroom with a king bed and a bathroom with a shower. A dining table has seating for 6 and leads to a colorful kitchen with premium appliances and a black granite-topped island with a farmhouse sink and seating for 5. Double-glass doors open to an outdoor kitchen with a commercial refrigerator and gas grill. A private courtyard has a swimming pool, chaise lounges, and a dining area. In the evening, dainty string lights above the dining table and lights from the pool create a lovely backdrop for meals.

On the second floor, guests may choose between 3 bedrooms that offer king, queen, or twin-over-full bunk beds. An open balcony has views of the neighborhood and connects to a side, screened porch. A ceiling fan, rocking chairs, and wooded views make the screened porch a relaxing spot to unwind. Tucked next to the staircase leading to the 3rd floor is a bonus room with a wet bar, coffee maker, flat-screen TV, and a queen sleeper sofa.

The third floor is a loft with access to the lookout tower. 2 twin daybeds enjoy sweeping views of the Gulf. A deck on the lookout tower has treetop views of Blue Mountain Beach and the Gulf. It's the ultimate spot to toast sunset each evening.

Guests can explore Blue Mountain Beach and the 30A bike path with 6 included adult bikes. Restaurants, shopping, and Draper Lake are all within a quick walk or bike ride. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS
FIRST FLOOR:
Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Semi-Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Hallway Bathroom with Shower Only
Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Full Bunk Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
Additional Living Area: Queen Sleeper Sofa

THIRD FLOOR:
Two Twin Daybeds

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

Unfortunately, the HOA in this community does not allow Low-Speed Vehicle rentals or guests to bring their own.

Seagrove Beach "Blue Heron Hideaway" 205 South Lake Drive
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 4
Sleeps 9
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Lakefront property on Eastern Lake
- Private dock
- EV charger
- 4 adult bikes
- Grill
- Fireplace
- SONOS
- 2 children's canoes
- Adult and children's life jackets
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Beach and lake life combine at Blue Heron Hideaway, a historic 4-bedroom, 4-bathroom Seagrove Beach home, comfortably accommodating 9 guests. Surrounded by lemon, peach, and magnolia trees, with stunning views of Eastern Lake, guests will love the picturesque setting. Guests can take full advantage of the provided paddleboards, kayak, or canoe, launching directly from the dock into Eastern Lake—while enjoying the convenient beach access only a 5-minute stroll away!

Step inside this lakeside home to find a bright interior of hardwood floors, classic furnishings, and coastal elements. Hurricane windows and doors create a quiet, peaceful environment for unwinding. In the living room, a leather couch, 2 large armchairs, and a 65” Smart TV invite guests to gather. Around the corner, a wooden dining table with 6 elegant woven chairs awaits lively dinners. The beautiful chef’s kitchen boasts gorgeous countertops, a 2-stool breakfast bar, and high-end appliances—including a juicer and multiple coffeemakers! For convenience, the laundry room is adjacent. In the back sitting area, sit back on the couch and 2 armchairs to take in views of Eastern Lake through the picture windows. For alfresco meals, the screened-in back porch provides a scenic backdrop, with a 6-seat table overlooking the water. To the side is a wicker patio set and a back door to the dock. At bedtime, retire to either the primary bedroom or guest bedroom on this level—both have a king bed, Smart TV, and private bathroom, while the primary bedroom enjoys access to the back porch.

Upstairs is an additional sitting area with a full sleeper sofa, armchair, Smart TV, and wet bar, alongside 2 guest bedrooms. The first guest bedroom has a king bed, private bathroom, Smart TV, and balcony facing the lake, while the second bedroom has 1 queen bed and 1 twin bed.

Experience days of on-the-water and beach fun at Blue Heron Hideaway in Seagrove Beach. Exploring is easy thanks to parking for 4 vehicles and 4 complimentary adult bikes. For beach access, Eastern Lake Public Beach Access is a short 5-minute walk. Head southwest on South Lake Drive, take a slight left onto South Gulf Drive, and continue onto Eastern Lake Road, where beach access is on the left. Book today!

- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed and Twin Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

DETAILS: Blissful beachside living awaits at 66 Spooky Lane, a Gulf-front paradise set in beautiful Blue Mountain Beach. Tucked within a private community, this 5-bedroom, 7-bathroom luxury property accommodates 12 guests in unmatched comfort. Indulge in days of basking in the sunshine beside the glittering private pool, taking in the shimmering Gulf view from the balconies, or strolling to the white sand beach using the dedicated walkover—directly behind the property.

Rolling sand dunes turn the sprawling first-level pool deck into a secluded oasis, sheltered from the beach. Stretch out on the cushioned loungers, cool off with a swim, or unwind in the shade of the oversized umbrella. An outdoor shower and an in-home elevator add the final touches to this floor.

On the second level, brilliant Gulf views draw guests into the elegant great room, as shades of taupe and brown, gleaming Travertine tiles, and seagrass elements set the scene for an elevated beach escape. Polished granite countertops and high-end appliances feature in the spacious kitchen, complete with an island and a breakfast bar for 6—a perfect spot for quick bites or hosting a private chef. Savor group dinners at the 10-person dining table before adjourning to the living space, where a 65” TV and soundbar provide top-of-the-line entertainment. On the north side of the home, a balcony overlooking the quiet neighborhood hosts a barbecue grill, while to the south is a furnished, covered deck made for enjoying the endless Gulf view. At bedtime, retire to the primary bedroom, boasting a king bed, private bathroom, and balcony access. There is also a laundry room on this level.

The third-level lounge includes a loveseat, TV, kitchenette, and dining table for 4, making it easy for guests to start the morning with a rich cup of coffee before meandering out to balcony seating. There are 2 lavish primary bedrooms featuring king beds, private bathrooms, and access to the balcony, alongside a family-friendly bunk room with a queen bed and twin-over-twin bunk.

The fourth-floor guest bedroom offers a private retreat, boasting a king bed and a private Gulf-facing balcony.

Experience an indulgent Gulf getaway at 66 Spooky Lane in Blue Mountain Beach. Effortlessly explore with parking for 3 vehicles, as well as 5 complimentary adult bikes. For beach access, follow the walkover off the back deck. Book today!

WaterColor 128 Mystic Cobalt
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-012550
Bedrooms 3
Beds 5
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 10 wristbands.
- Minutes from the Seaside Central Square and WaterColor Beach Club
- Short bike ride to WaterColor merchants and Boathouse
- Propane grill
- 1 dedicated parking spot with additional parking throughout the community.
- 3 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

Guests staying in WaterColor have access to the WaterColor Beach Club. It features a main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, the WaterColor Grill, and sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Nestled on a tree-lined street in WaterColor, 128 Mystic Cobalt boasts 3 bedrooms and 3-and-a-half bathrooms, sleeping up to 10 guests. This home includes access to WaterColor’s community pools, trails, and private beach club. Conveniently located along the Emerald Coast, enjoy breezy afternoons on the 2 screened-in porches with great views!

Step inside this charming beach home to find the combined living, dining, and kitchen spaces. A beach-inspired mosaic fireplace and winsome art mix with hardwood floors and tongue-and-groove wainscoting to create an inviting and cheerful atmosphere. Spend a relaxed morning making pancakes with the family in the kitchen, equipped with GE appliances and a breakfast bar for 4. The dining table has space for 6 to eat or play a friendly board game! After an exciting day, sink into the plush sofas and relax in front of the 40” TV. Both sides of the home have a screened-in porch, which offers alfresco dining at a table for 6 and comfortable seating to savor an evening drink or morning coffee. Sleeping accommodations on this floor include a primary bedroom with 1 king bed, a guest bedroom with 1 twin-over-full bunk bed.

Upstairs, discover a desk and a cozy twin daybed in the loft space—perfect for extra guests or as a quiet reading nook. There is 1 guest bedroom on this level with a queen bed and twin-over-twin bunk bed. All the bedrooms in this home have TVs.

Guests are sure to fall in love with the quiet tranquility and numerous amenities of 128 Mystic Cobalt in WaterColor. Easily explore with parking for 1 vehicle and 3 complimentary adult bikes to cruise the neighborhood. Guests can access the private beach club in main WaterColor, and there are 2 nearby public beach accesses—Van Ness Butler and Santa Clara. Book this family-friendly beach home today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed (2), Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Full Bunk Bed (3), Private Bathroom with Shower

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed (2) Bed and Twin-over-Twin Built in Bunk Bed (2), Private Bathroom with Shower
- Second Floor Loft: Twin Day Bed

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community and 360 Blue fines.

Please note: Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be made through The Electric Cart Company and is limited to 80 vehicles throughout the community at any given time. Only one cart may be rented per home and rentals will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis.

Seagrove Beach "Sand Dollar Retreat" 73 Sand Dollar Court
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-091056
Bedrooms 5
Beds 8
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Community pool
- Covered Front & Back Porches with Outdoor Seating
- 5 adult bikes
- Beach access across the street
- 1.6 miles away from The Big Chill-shopping and dinning
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Escape to tranquility at Sand Dollar Retreat, a 5-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom Seagrove Beach abode that sleeps 12. Nestled in the Village of Sugar Beach, enjoy the community pool and multiple dining options within a short bike ride. Delight in lounging on the sun-soaked back porch or the sugar sand beach—2 blocks away!

A pastel façade and an inviting porch swing welcome guests upon arrival at this 2-story cottage. Inside, coastal influences and streaks of sunlight create a warm and friendly atmosphere. Hang beach bags on the hooks by the door and continue into the combined living, dining, and kitchen. In the open kitchen, guests will find high-end appliances and an expansive center island with 4 woven wicker stools. A rich, dark wood dining table seats 10 under a Capiz shell chandelier. The spacious living area’s 2 plush couches and armchair provide a comfortable space to chat or unwind in front of the TV. The nearby desk is a convenient addition. Meander through the large glass doors at the back of the living space to discover an airy and sunlit screened veranda with a table for 10 and patio furniture—the perfect spot to relish the subtropical weather. Outside, Adirondack chairs and a propane grill beckon for an evening of barbecue and conversation under the stars. Around the corner is an outdoor shower. This floor has a guest bedroom with a king bed.

A cozy sitting area with a TV wait at the top of the bright stairwell. This level hosts 2 guest bedrooms with a king and queen bed, respectively. A bunk room with 2 twin-over-twin beds offers additional accommodations. Along with a private furnished porch that seats 6, the primary bedroom includes a king bed and a private bathroom.

Experience a balmy and blissful getaway at Sand Dollar Retreat in Seagrove Beach. Parking for up to 3 vehicles and 5 complimentary adult bikes makes exploring the neighborhood and nearby towns easy. Beachwood Villas Public Beach Access is a 2-minute bike ride across from Tom Thumb on Highway 30A. Book this enchanting abode today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 12):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: 4 Twin Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*Please note that the community pool cannot be heated.

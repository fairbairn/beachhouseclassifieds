Seaside "Ocean Eyes" 824 Forest Street
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-080062
Bedrooms 4
Beds 9
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 2
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Newly remodeled in 2025
- Short walk to the beach, community pools, and amenities
- Steps from the famous Seaside Town Square featuring plenty of shopping and dining
- Lookout tower
- Game room
- Close to Family Pool
- 2 Large decks
- Cut through area to Watercolor
- Parking for 2 vehicles
- 5 adult bikes
- Propane grill
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Experience convenience and serenity at Ocean Eyes, a remodeled beach residence boasting 4 bedrooms, 4 bathrooms, and 2 half baths that comfortably accommodate up to 12 guests. As the world’s first New Urbanist town, Seaside was designed for walkability—within minutes, enjoy a community pool, fitness center, tennis courts, playground shopping, dining, various beach accesses, and a special cut-through to WaterColor. After a day of sun and beachside fun, guests will love relaxing on the 2 spacious decks or lookout tower while enjoying the peaceful views and people-watching.

Nestled on the tranquil Forest Street, this 2-level home welcomes guests with a shaded front porch adorned with a bench swing, chairs, and dining space for 4. Step inside to find hardwood floors, pastel finishes, and slip-covered furniture, creating a cozy atmosphere. The living room features a 65” Smart TV, while the oval dining table for 6 leads to a cheerful kitchen with pale cabinets and whimsical, colorful tile work. When evening falls, retire to the 2 guest bedrooms on this level—all the home’s bedrooms feature a private bathroom. The first guest bedroom has 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds, and the second guest bedroom has a queen bed, a 50” Smart TV, and backyard access.

Ascend to the second floor to relax in the game room with a twin-over-twin bunk bed—perfect for children, teens, or guests who don’t mind sharing space. The beautiful primary bedroom offers a tranquil retreat with a king bed and a charming balcony. A guest bedroom with a king bed finishes the floor. Both bedrooms and the game room are equipped with 32" TVs.

Take the stairs to the lookout tower to enjoy 360-degree views of the neighborhood.

Proximity to community pools, beach accesses, and award-winning shopping and dining experiences make Ocean Eyes an ideal base for a delightful Seaside vacation. While the community is walkable, guests can take advantage of 2 on-site parking spaces—plus the 5 included adult bikes for effortless exploration. The nearest beach access is at the end of Odessa Street. Book this beach escape today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 12):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Two Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Common Area: Half Bath

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Game Room: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed
- Half bathroom

Unfortunately, the HOA in this community prohibits Low-Speed Vehicle rentals or for guests to bring their own.

***Unfortunately, we can not allow animals in this home unless the animal is a certified service animal. Failure to adhere to this rule will result in immediate eviction and loss of deposit. Guest is fully responsible for all damages.

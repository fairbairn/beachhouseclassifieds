Grayton Beach "The Sand Box" 277 Pine St
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 6
Beds 10
Sleeps 22
Full Baths 7
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Short walk to the beach
- Gulf views
- Private saltwater pool
- Outdoor covered entertainment areas with wet bar
- Elevator
- 6 adult bikes
- Walkable to nearby restaurants
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: The Sand Box is a 6-bedroom, 7-bathroom beachfront home in Grayton Beach—a boho beach town—that sleeps 22 comfortably. Enjoy beach access less than 100 yards away at Ray’s Multi-Mountain Beach and explore the nearby rolling dunes at Grayton Beach State Park. With 4 covered beachfront decks, a private pool, and spacious accommodations, this home provides everything needed for a wonderful Gulf vacation.

Enjoy the sounds of waves crashing against the shore from this spacious 2-story beachfront home. The ground floor is complete with all the amenities for endless summer entertainment, including a covered patio area with a wet bar and grill directly facing the private in-ground pool with rocking chairs, loungers, and a picnic table. There’s also an outdoor shower for washing the sand off your feet before heading upstairs via the in-home elevator. The first floor of this home is the epitome of elegance and comfort, with a welcoming great room filled with natural lighting from the wall of windows. A spacious living area offers Gulf-front views from the white linen sectional situated in front of the fireplace and TV. Enjoy access to the fully furnished balcony with an alfresco dining table and plush seating. The kitchen is every chef's dream, with marble countertops and a matching island doubling as a 5-stool breakfast bar. It has all the essentials for a home-cooked meal and more! Host friends and family in the adjacent dining area with a wood table that seats 10. There are 2 bedrooms—1 guest bedroom with a queen bed and 1 bunk room with 2 full-over-full beds and 2 twin trundle beds—located on this floor, ensuring ample space for everyone.

The top floor of this home contains the remaining 4 bedrooms—1 primary bedroom and 1 guest bedroom with king beds, 1 guest bedroom with 2 queen beds, and 1 other guest bedroom with a queen Murphy bed. All of these bedrooms have direct access to the outside deck with furniture and a swing bed. Guests will also find a washer and dryer on this floor.

The Sand Box is a wonderful place to spend a family vacation or time with friends. With on-site parking and 6 adult bikes, complimentary with the property, it’s easy to explore local shops and restaurants. Enjoy relaxation and convenience at The Sand Box. Book now!

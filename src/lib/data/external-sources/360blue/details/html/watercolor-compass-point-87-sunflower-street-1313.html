WaterColor "Compass Point" 87 Sunflower Street
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-092454
Bedrooms 4
Beds 6
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor Community guidelines the maximum number of wristbands this home is allotted is 12 bands to access WaterColor amenities and community pools
- Located in the beautiful Crossings II community of WaterColor
- Uniquely Located Next Door to the Dragonfly Pool
- Surrounded by natural green space and parks
- Private pool and spa **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for $25 per day**
- 6-seater Low-Speed Vehicle
- 4 adult bikes provided
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, a second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and a third pool with zero-entry, as well as an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and a new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Compass Point is a bright and spacious 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom WaterColor home that comfortably sleeps 12. Located near the Gulf Coast in the beautiful Crossings II community of WaterColor, this home has numerous amenities, including beach access, tennis courts, and multiple pools. Guests will love this home's private outdoor paradise with a saltwater pool, sitting spa, and TV.

This 2-story home features fresh, coastal architecture and tons of natural light. The group's chef will be thrilled in this kitchen with the spacious quartz countertops and top-of-the-line appliances. Open a bottle of wine and gather around the expansive island while dinner cooks! In the living room, bask in the sunlight from the 3 sets of French doors overlooking the serene backyard and woodlands. Step outside to swim in the saltwater pool surrounded by private fencing. There's seating for up to 10 guests, a 65" TV, and a basketball net for you to enjoy! After swimming and shooting hoops, retire to the primary bedroom with a king bed and private bathroom with a separate tub and shower on the first floor. A sink in the laundry room is perfect for washing delicate items.

Head up the stairs to find 3 guest bedrooms and an additional sleeping area with 2 twin beds. One guest bedroom has a king bed with access to a private porch. The second guest bedroom has a king bed, and the third bedroom has 1 twin-over-full bunk bed and a full sleeper sofa. All the guest bedrooms have a private bathroom.

Compass Point is the perfect destination for a peaceful vacation on the Gulf Coast, offering access to WaterColor community amenities and a spacious private outdoor retreat. There's parking for 3 vehicles in the driveway, and the complimentary 4 adult bikes and 6-seater Low-Speed Vehicle—with a full enclosure for chilly mornings or rainy days—make exploring easy! Choose from multiple beach accesses, including the private beach club in main WaterColor, the public Van Ness Butler access between WaterColor and Seaside, and the public Santa Clara beach access in Seagrove. Call today to book this home!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (Sleeps 12)
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Full Bunk Bed and Full Sleeper Sofa, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Additional Sleeping Area: Two Twin Beds

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments, as well as be subject to fines.

*Unfortunately, the ice maker, wine cooler, and outdoor fireplace is not for guest use.

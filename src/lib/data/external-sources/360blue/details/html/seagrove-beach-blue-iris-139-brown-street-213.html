Seagrove Beach "Blue Iris" 139 Brown Street
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 10
Sleeps 15
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Short walk to beach access
- 6-Passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- Private pool *Pool heating is available from October 1st through May 1st for an additional fee of $30 per day.
- 4 adult bikes
- Courtyard
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

DETAILS: Blue Iris is a newly updated 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home that sleeps 15 in the Seagrove Beach community of Scenic 30A. Less than 0.5 miles from the nearest beach access, guests will love the quick 6-minute walk to Eastern Lake Public Beach. This property offers luxury and comfort with 2 living spaces, a gorgeous courtyard with alfresco dining, and a private pool.

This 3-story home boasts a beautiful blue façade adorned with white trim, a welcoming aesthetic! The first floor of this home offers 2 well-appointed bedrooms—1 bunk room with 2 twin-over-twin beds and 1 guest bedroom. These accommodations also share access to an additional living space with a TV, and arcade, as well as a small wet bar with a coffee maker and mini fridge. There's also a laundry room for convenience. Step outside to the lovely courtyard with a privacy fence, where guests can comfortably sunbathe on 4 matching lounge chairs or enjoy alfresco dining for 6.

The spacious second floor is the primary gathering space of this home, where an open great room combines the kitchen, dining, and main living areas with wood floors and natural lighting. The living area features a plush, white linen sofa and a matching armchair for added comfort. The adjacent dining area offers seating for 8 with a seashell chandelier overhead and direct access to the second-floor furnished deck with gorgeous Gulf views. The well-equipped kitchen features a pantry, quartz countertops, and a kitchen island doubling as a 6-stool breakfast bar. Perfect for entertaining!

Breathtaking views and comfortable accommodations make up the top floor of Blue Iris. The remaining 2 bedrooms are on this floor—1 sprawling primary bedroom with a private, furnished balcony and 1 other cozy guest bedroom.

Blue Iris offers all the comforts of home with luxurious amenities and 4 complimentary adult bikes to explore all that 30A offers. Enjoy the sugary white beaches and emerald waters by heading right onto Brown Street and left onto Eastern Lake Road until the road begins to curve—the beach is between 471 and 493 Eastern Lake Road. With incredible proximity to the beach, it's time to relax and unwind at Blue Iris! Book now!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 15):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Two Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Additional Living Area: Queen Sleeper Sofa

SECOND FLOOR:
- Additional Sleeping: Full Sleeper Sofa

THIRD FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed and Twin Sleeper Chair, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

**Please note that in order to utilize the Low-Speed Vehicle that comes with the home, you will be required to pay a $30 non-refundable deposit to 30a Gulf Carts.

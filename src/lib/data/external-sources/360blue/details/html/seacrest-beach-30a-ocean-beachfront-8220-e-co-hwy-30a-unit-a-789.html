Seacrest Beach "30A Ocean Beachfront" 8220 E CO HWY 30A Unit A
Seacrest Beach, FL
Bedrooms 7
Beds 10
Sleeps 18
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf front
- Private Pool *Pool heating is available October 1st through May 1st for an additional fee of $50 per day* Pool chiller runs complimentary in July and August
- Complimentary beach setups each morning include 4 beach chairs and 2 umbrellas (Service provided March 1 - October 31)
- 180 Degree Gulf Views from All 3 Floors
- Private Beach Walkover
- Elevator Between All Three Floors
- Sonos Sound System & HDTVs
- Outdoor Natural Gas Grill
- 7 Adult bikes
- 6-Seater Low-Speed Vehicle
- Peloton bike
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Experience the serene opulence of 30A Ocean Beachfront, a Gulf-front oasis with 7 bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms that accommodates 18 guests in picturesque Seacrest. Discover a thriving community composed of tempting eateries, charming boutiques, and blissful beaches. Revel in the private pool with 180-degree views of the shimmering Gulf and a private walkover to the sugar-sand beach feet from the property. The pool has a cooling function that is turned on complimentary for July & August.

Sunlight illuminates the contemporary décor and sparkling travertine floor of this deluxe beach home’s open-concept kitchen, living, and dining area. Gleaming Thermador appliances and a grand, oversized peninsula that comfortably seats 6 guests feature in the elegant kitchen. Savor a decadent meal at the beautiful 8-person dining table before retiring to the living area, where a state-of-the-art Sonos soundbar and 65” Smart TV pair with 2 sumptuous sofas for an evening of indulgent relaxation. The sliding glass doors showcase the resplendent Gulf waters and open to an outdoor haven with a high-end natural gas grill, plush seating, and a private pool. Stroll down the private walkover to watch the luscious sunset. Retreat to the tasteful guest bedroom with 2 twin beds on this floor or the refined accommodations upstairs.

Take the stylish glass and metal staircase or in-home elevator to the second floor, where a guest bedroom with a king bed and a bunk bedroom with a twin-over-twin bunk bed wait. Enchanting Gulf views star in the primary bedroom alongside a king bed, queen sleeper sofa, private bathroom, Peloton, and balcony.

On the top floor, another primary bedroom offers dazzling views of the Gulf, a king bed, queen sleeper sofa, balcony, and private bathroom. A bunk room with a twin-over-twin bunk bed and a guest bedroom with a king bed finish the level.

Nestled along the glorious Gulf, enjoy a leisurely vacation at 30A Ocean Beachfront. The driveway has private parking for 3 vehicles, and guests can casually explore 30A on the complimentary 7 adult bikes and 6-passenger low speed vehicle. A private beach walkover leads to a public beach off the back of the home. Book this exceptional beach home today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 18):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Two Twin Beds

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed and Queen Sleeper Sofa, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

THIRD FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed and Queen Sleeper Sofa, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed

*Please note that Gulf-front bonfires are not permitted at this location per Walton County regulations.

*Parking for 3 vehicles is feasible, but better suited for 3 compact vehicles

*The beach before 30A Ocean Beachfront is not privately owned.

*Pool heat is unavailable when temps drop below 50°F. This is to protect the pool heater in cold temperatures.

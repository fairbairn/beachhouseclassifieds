WaterColor 102 Vermillion Way
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 8
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 12 wristbands
- Outdoor fireplace and summer kitchen
- 4 adult bikes
- 6-Passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- Steps away from Western Lake, biking distance from the beach
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the WaterColor Beach Club. It features a main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Welcome to 102 Vermillion Way, a beautiful 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom WaterColor home sleeping 12 guests. Paddle across sparkling Western Lake or take a leisurely bike ride to the sugar-white sand beach. Guests enjoy all the amenities of this resort-style community, including tennis courts, community pools, and the famous WaterColor Beach Club! Designed for coastal living, spend quiet evenings around the outdoor fireplace and grilling in the Summer kitchen.

Tucked within the Lake District, this 3-level home offers an elegant atmosphere. Enjoy a peaceful morning sipping coffee on the spacious front porch’s Adirondack chairs. A charming mudroom with cubbies for beach bags welcomes guests to the first floor. Continue inside to find an open-concept great room filled with natural light from the floor-to-ceiling windows and French doors in the living space, where a comfortable couch and 2 sets of armchairs flank the Smart TV. The kitchen’s quartz countertops and top-of-the-line appliances will delight culinary lovers. Chill a bottle of wine in the cooler for a shared dinner at the table for 6, with 4 additional seats at the breakfast bar. Or guests can eat on the screened-in porch, which includes a 6-seat table, sitting area, Smart TV, outdoor fireplace, and Summer kitchen. At the end of the night, retire to the guest bedroom with a king bed—all the bedrooms have a Smart TVs. A double set of washers and dryers in the hall makes it easy to refresh beach towels, and there’s also an outdoor shower.

On the second level, discover the lovely primary bedroom with a private balcony. The 2 guest bedrooms each have a king bed and share a second balcony with a daybed swing. Additionally, there is a bunk area with 2 full-over-full beds.

At sunset, head to the observation tower for stunning views of the neighborhood.

Experience the delights of WaterColor at 102 Vermillion Way. Easily explore with parking for 4 vehicles, 4 complimentary adult bikes, and a 6-passenger low-speed vehicle. There are 2 beach access points, one through the Beach Club and the other located on Goldenrod Circle, past the WaterColor Inn. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 12):
FIRST FLOOR
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate

SECOND FLOOR
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Bunk Room: Two Full-over-Full Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*The carriage house is owner-occupied.

*Please note: Effective March 15, 2014, Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be made through The Electric Cart Company and will be limited to 80 vehicles throughout the community at any given time. Only one cart may be rented per home and rentals will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis.

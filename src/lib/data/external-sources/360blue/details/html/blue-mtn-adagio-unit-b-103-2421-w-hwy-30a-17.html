Blue Mtn. Adagio Unit B-103 - 2421 W. Hwy 30A
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 8
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf views
- Private balcony
- Beach access
- Community pools
- Community gas grill
- 2 dedicated parking spots
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

DETAILS: Enjoy the beautiful white sand beaches and turquoise water on the Gulf's highest point in Blue Mountain Beach. This 2500 sq. ft. condo just steps away from the infinity edge Gulf front pool, as well as the 3-tiered pool close to the unit. Relax on your patio or enjoy your spacious living room with breathtaking views of the Gulf of Mexico. The view from the kitchen is striking as well, looking out over turquoise waters. The primary bedroom boasts breathtaking gulf views! Spend countless hours on the beach or splashing in the two Adagio community pools. This adagio unit comes with 2 parking spaces. Check availability and book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed with private bathroom with shower/jacuzzi
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed with jack and jill bathroom with shower/tub combo
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed with jack and jill bathroom with shower/tub combo
- Guest Bedroom: Two Queen Beds with attached bathroom and shower/tub combo
- Additional Living: One Twin-over-Twin Bunkbed
- Additional Living: King Sleeper

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

* Starting 1/1/2017, guests must rent any bike rentals through Adagio's exclusive bike vendor, La Dolce Vita.
* Per HOA policy, a MAX of 10 guests can stay at this property.

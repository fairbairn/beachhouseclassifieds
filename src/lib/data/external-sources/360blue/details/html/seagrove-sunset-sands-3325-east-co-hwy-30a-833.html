Seagrove "Sunset Sands" 3325 East Co Hwy 30A
Santa Rosa Beach, FL

Beds 16
Sleeps 36
Full Baths 7
Half Baths 2

- Steps from the beach
- Gulf views
- Private heated pool and sitting spa **Pool heating available Oct 1–May 1 for $100 per day** The pool and spa are heated together.
- 6 Passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- Professionally designed
- Elevator
- Spacious balconies
- Outdoor kitchen
- Outdoor shower
- 7 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Spectacular design meets incredible location! Imagine a 7-bedroom, 7-bathroom and 2-half bathroom 30A getaway with amazing outdoor spaces—including a private pool, sitting spa, and 3 balconies with sweeping Gulf views—all steps from the brilliant white sands and turquoise waters of the Gulf. Located in the heart of Seagrove Beach, “Sunset Sands” can accommodate up to 36 guests, making it the perfect retreat for large groups.

Entering through the courtyard, guests will discover a living room with a TV and access to an expansive wet bar that includes a wine chiller, a beverage fridge, sink, dishwasher, and seating for 7. Steps away is a partially covered pool with a waterfall feature and sitting spa. Next to the pool are an outdoor shower, TV, and grilling area. Guests can change in a half-bath that’s steps away and drop wet towels off in the adjacent laundry room, which includes 2 sets of front-loading washers and dryers. The home has both a staircase and an elevator to access all 4 stories.

The second floor is the heart and soul of the home. A gourmet kitchen seats 5 at the countertop and features premium stainless appliances, including a 6-burner stove and a massive 2-door fridge. A dining table seats 10. An adjacent half-bath makes it easy to wash up for meals. The kitchen opens onto the living area, which offers abundant seating and a TV—the perfect place to gather for vacation movie night. Twin doors open onto the balcony with table seating for 6 guests to dine al fresco. The first floor also has a primary bedroom with a private bath.

Ascending to the third floor, you’ll find another living room with access to a vast deck with spectacular Gulf views. 3 bedrooms are on this floor. Two of the bedrooms have queen beds with 2 twin built-in bunks and private bathrooms with shower/tub combos. The third bedroom has a queen bed with a private bath with a shower/tub combo.

The fourth floor offers another balcony—again with staggering Gulf views and chaise lounge chairs for sun worshipers. The 2 primary bedrooms have king beds and private baths, both with a shower only. A bunkroom is a perfect space for the young or young at heart. 4 sets of bunks—8 in all—and each includes its own TV! This bunkroom has a private bath.

Sunset Sands has parking for 2 vehicles and comes with a 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle and 7 adult bikes, allowing guests to easily explore the communities of 30A. Book Sunset Sands today!

- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate

THIRD FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed and Full-Over-Full Bunkbed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed and Full-Over-Full Bunkbed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

FOURTH FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Bunk Bedroom: 4 Full-Over-Full Bunk beds, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

*Active construction in the vicinity of the property as of 12/2023.

*We are happy to welcome up to 1 dog, not to exceed the weight limit of 50 lbs for an additional non-refundable pet fee of $400 plus tax.

*Both the living room and primary bedroom fireplaces are not for guest use.

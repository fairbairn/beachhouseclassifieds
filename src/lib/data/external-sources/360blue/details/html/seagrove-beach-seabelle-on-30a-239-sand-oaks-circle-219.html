DETAILS: Sea Belle on 30A is a luxury 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home that sleeps 7 in the gated community of Sea Nest Village in Seagrove Beach. This stunning property provides access to the neighborhood pool with a grill area and outdoor fireplace. With more than 2,200 square feet of living space, guests will love the outdoor patio, second-floor balcony views, and spacious accommodations. Best of all, it’s a quick 5-minute walk to the nearest deeded beach access!

The first floor of this 3-story home offers plenty of room to relax and unwind with 2 inviting bedrooms—1 guest bedroom with a queen bed and 1 bunk room with a twin-over-full bed. Enjoy access to a private patio featuring a fire pit surrounded by Adirondack chairs, a cozy sitting area, and a convenient outdoor shower to wash off sandy feet.

A stunning second-floor great room welcomes guests to the heart of this home, with an open floor plan that is aglow with natural light from large picture windows. The living area is filled with 4 plush armchairs and an inviting couch in front of a TV, creating a warm environment to relax. Step outside to the second-floor balcony with a 4-person alfresco dining table and a gas grill for fresh air. The kitchen is a sleek and modern haven, featuring beautiful white marble countertops and an expansive island, complete with a built-in sink and various small appliances. A sit-down meal in the traditional dining area offers comfortable seating for 6, while the adjacent breakfast bar can accommodate 3 more. There’s even a stacked washer and dryer for added convenience.

Upstairs on the third floor, guests will find a magnificent primary bedroom with a king bed and private bathroom featuring a luxurious jacuzzi tub and a unique porthole window overhead. Guests can access a private covered balcony with coastal views through French doors.

Get ready to indulge in ultimate relaxation at Sea Belle on 30A! With on-site parking for 2 vehicles—plus a Tesla charger—and 3 complimentary adult bikes, it’s easy to explore the nearby Seaside and WaterColor neighborhoods or local favorites like The Perfect Pig and Bronzinni’s Pizza. Book now!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS:
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom, Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Full Bunk Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

THIRD FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom with Gulf views: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Jacuzzi Tub Separate

Seagrove Beach "Dunes of Seagrove" 51 Chivas Lane #305C
Santa Rosa Beach, FL

Beds 3
Sleeps 6

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Private beach access
- Renovated in 2024
- Located in Dunes of Seagrove
- Access to two community pools, tennis courts, pickle ball, sauna, fitness center, BBQ area, steam room
- Covered parking
- Elevator
- 360 degree beachfront view from the 4th floor
- Complementary beach service set (2 beach chairs and 1 umbrella) that is first-come first-served
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Welcome to Dunes of Seagrove in Seagrove Beach! Comfortably accommodating up to 6 guests, this 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom condo is great for families or couple groups. Guests enjoy numerous community amenities, including access to 2 pools, tennis courts, pickleball courts, a sauna, fitness center, BBQ area, and steam room. A long-loved destination for generations of beach-going families, this quintessential beach town promises laid-back days of sandals, sun, and glistening blue waters.

Pale floors, white walls, and pops of blue accents set the scene for an enchanting coastal getaway inside this single-story condo. As guests enter the combined kitchen, living, and dining space, eyes are immediately drawn to the expanse of windows showcasing views of the brilliant Gulf and filling the space with natural light. The kitchen is fully equipped with high-end appliances, plenty of counter space, and a breakfast bar with 3-wicker stools—perfect for quick morning meals before heading off to the beach. The living space is comfortably outfitted with a soft loveseat and 2 upholstered armchairs flanking a TV for winding down after a day in the sun. A charming 4-seat round table to the side offers space to dine while enjoying the view. On the spacious covered balcony is a second 4-seat table and an arrangement of cushioned patio seating, where guests can savor the Gulf weather and view. For convenience, there’s a stacked washer and dryer in the hall closet. At bedtime, choose between the primary bedroom with a king bed or the guest bedroom with a queen bed—both feature private bathrooms. For additional guests, there is a queen sleeper sofa in the living area.

Dunes of Seagrove has everything guests need for a fun-filled beach getaway. Assigned parking for 1 vehicle makes it easy to explore all of Scenic 30A. For designated beach access, simply follow the boardwalk beside the pool! Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 6):
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Additional Sleeping Area: Queen Sleeper Sofa

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments, and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

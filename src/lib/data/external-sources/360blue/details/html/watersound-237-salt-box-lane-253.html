Watersound 237 Salt Box Lane
Watersound, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-035196
Bedrooms 3
Beds 4
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gated Community with on-site Security
- Private deeded beach access
- 3 adult bikes
- Community pool
- Golf Cart
- From March 1st to October 31st, 2024, the rental of this property includes one beach -service set (2 beach chairs and 1 umbrella)
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

This property comes with 1 designated parking space and 1 first-come first-serve parking space. Please note first come first serve parking is limited and not guaranteed. This home permits a maximum of 2 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: 237 Salt Box Ln is a gorgeous 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom beach cottage that sleeps 8 in the private gated community of WaterSound on Scenic 30A. This property provides luxurious amenities, including 2 community pools, easy access to nearby parks and walking trails, and several meandering boardwalks that lead directly to the Emerald Coast. Guests will love being only 500 yards away from the nearest white sand beach!

A screened-in front porch, perfect for enjoying a nice coastal breeze, welcomes guests to this 2-story home. The first-floor great room mixes the living, dining, and kitchen areas together seamlessly with professional coastal décor that creates a calming space. A breathtaking wall of windows illuminates the living area adorned with plush white slipcover furniture situated in front of the fireplace with a TV mounted overhead. The elegant kitchen has sleek white cabinetry, dark marble countertops, and high-end appliances. A traditional dining area comfortably seats 5 guests around the table, while the quaint breakfast bar can seat 2 more. There are 2 guest bedrooms located on this floor, both with king beds and stunning views.

There is 1 guest bedroom on the second level that is great for sharing with 2 queen-sized beds and 2 trundle beds. The top floor was designed with the goal of relaxation and comfort.

237 Salt Box Ln offers guests an incredible coastal retreat away from everyday life! With 3 complimentary adult bikes—plus a 4-seater golf cart—it's easy to explore the local area. Book now!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS:

- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Two Queen Beds with Two Twin Trundles, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate

WATERSOUND COMMUNITY HIGHLIGHTS:
-Over 250 Acre Gated Beach Community on the Gulf of Mexico between Seaside and Rosemary Beach
-(2) Community Pools (Dunesider Pool in Main Gate and Bridges Pool within the Bridges Gate)
-Kayak Rentals
-Parks, Walking Trails and Meandering Boardwalks
-Bike to Alys, Seacrest and Rosemary Beach

*Please Note:
*Access to the WaterSound Beach Club is not included
*WaterSound does not allow golf cart rentals from outside vendors
*Fireplace is not for guest use

*Weddings or parties/groups of people or guest of guest are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

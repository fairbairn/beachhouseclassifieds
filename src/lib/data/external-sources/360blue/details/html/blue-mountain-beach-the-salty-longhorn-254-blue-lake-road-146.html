Blue Mountain Beach "The Salty Longhorn" 254 Blue Lake Road
Santa Rosa Beach, FL

Beds 7

- Private Pool *Pool heating is available from October 1st through May 1st for an additional fee of $40 per day
- Gulf views

- Fire pit
- SONOS Sound System

- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Discover luxurious coastal living at The Salty Longhorn! This pet-friendly, 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom home accommodates up to 8 guests minutes from beach access. Imagine spending sun-filled days playing in the private pool’s crystalline waters, relaxing on the deck, or strolling along the sandy shore—only 1 block away!

Relaxation starts the moment guests arrive at this 3-story home. The front yard features a cozy firepit, perfect for evening gatherings under the stars, while a spacious front porch with a bench swing and chairs invites sleepy mornings sipping coffee in the fresh air. Inside, shiplap walls, wicker accents, and blonde wood floors create a beachy and cheerful vibe throughout the first-floor great room. Curl up on the comfy couch and 2 armchairs with glasses of white wine and a bowl of popcorn to watch TV in the living area, which seamlessly connects to the dining space with a round table for 6. The open-concept kitchen is a chef’s dream, with high-end appliances and a roomy island that doubles as a breakfast bar for 2. On the back porch, a 6-seat table and grill inspires alfresco dining. Head down to the pool deck and soak up sunshine from the 2 chairs or 2 loungers. An outdoor shower makes it easy to rinse off after beach trips. When it’s time to retire for the night, there is 1 guest bedroom with a king bed and a private bathroom on this level. Additionally, there’s a convenient laundry room with space for storing beach bags.

On the second level, find 1 primary bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom, and porch access as well as a guest bedroom with a queen bed, and a bunk room with 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds.

The third floor hosts a second living area with a couch, TV, desk, and a balcony with peek-a-boo views of the Gulf’s turquoise waters.

Beautiful outdoor spaces and quick beach access make for a perfect Blue Mountain Beach getaway at The Salty Longhorn. To explore, take advantage of the on-site parking—plus a 6-person Low-Speed Vehicle and 4 complimentary adult bikes. For beach access, head northeast on Blue Lake Road and turn left on Sea Grade Road. The public beach access will be across Blue Mountain Road. Book this beautiful beach property today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 8):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: 2 Twin-Over-Twin Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo.

*This home is pet-friendly with an additional non-refundable pet fee of $400, plus tax.

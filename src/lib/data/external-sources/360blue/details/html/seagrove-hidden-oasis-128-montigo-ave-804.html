Seagrove "Hidden Oasis" 128 Montigo Ave
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-012548
Bedrooms 3
Beds 5
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Located in the heart of Seagrove Beach
- 4-minute walk to the beach
- Private heated pool **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for $50 per day**
- Grill
- 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 3 adult bikes
- 3 Parking spots
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

*Bathroom renovation photos coming soon!

DETAILS: Vacation in the heart of Seagrove Beach at 128 Montigo Ave. This adorable 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom home has a private pool, is within walking distance to the beach, and sleeps 10.

Stepping inside the home, guests will discover beautiful wide-plank hardwood floors and a relaxing interior with touches of pale blue and nods to the beach. An open living room with 2 sofas leads to a dining area and kitchen overlooking an outdoor deck and views of the private pool. A hallway from the living room leads to a guest bedroom with 2 queen beds and a private bathroom with a shower. An additional bedroom features a king bed and a shared bathroom with a shower only. A primary bedroom has a king bed and a private bathroom with a shower/tub combo. Double-glass doors open from the bedroom onto the back deck and pool. On the deck, a stainless-steel gas grill, outdoor table, and cushioned chairs are the perfect spot to relax in the shade while the grill master cooks dinner. On the far end of the pool is a cornhole court. Talk about a great way to end the day! Splash in the pool, feast on grilled seafood, and then toast the sunset with a sandbag in hand.

Guests may also explore the surrounding shops and restaurants in Seagrove Beach on 3 included adult bikes. Call today to book 128 Montigo Ave. It won't be available for long with a central location on 30A, private pool, and comfortable furnishings.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (SLEEPS 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower/Tub Combo
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom With Shower Only
-Bedroom: Two Queen Beds, Private Bathroom With Shower Only
-Additional Sleeping Area: Queen Sleeper Sofa

*There is current construction behind this home.

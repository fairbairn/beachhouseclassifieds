NatureWalk "Unwind by the Gulf" 897 Sandgrass way
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-093162
Bedrooms 4
Beds 6
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Located in the beautiful NatureWalk community
- 6-seater Low-Speed Vehicle
- 4 adult bikes provided
- Professionally decorated
- Fully equipped kitchen
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Discover the beauty of “Unwind by the Gulf” 897 Sandgrass Way is a well-appointed house with lovely views! Located in the popular Nature Walk community, this 2-story property has 4 bedrooms, 3.5 baths, and sleeps 10 guests. Guests will love the spacious layout, proximity to San Juan Beach, and access to the Gathering Place with a community pool, spa, and playground—only a 5-minute walk!

As you enter the first floor, the open layout blends the primary living room and dining area together for a welcoming entrance. The plush hardwood floors, tall ceilings, and fireplace add a touch of elegance to this home as guests relax and watch TV on the 2 linen couches and armchair. Host guests at the 8-seat dining table to eat or play board games before heading to the beach. The adjacent kitchen features stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and an extensive kitchen island with 5 bar stools. Additionally, you'll find an extra sitting area adjoining the kitchen, replete with 4 linen armchairs and TV. There's also a full sleeper sofa in the main living room for more sleeping options.

On the second floor is where you will find the comfy bedrooms—1 primary bedroom with a spa-like tub, 2 guest bedrooms, and 1 bunk room. This home is perfect for family and friends staying all together on vacation!

Along the outside of Unwind by the Gulf, guests will fall in love with both screened-in porches on the front and backside of the home. With outdoor furniture, an alfresco dining table and chairs, and plenty of shade, there's no better place to be in this Florida weather. Finally, gather around the backyard firepit to enjoy cocktails under the stars at night.

If you're looking for extracurricular activities, try exploring the area on the complimentary 6-seat Low-Speed Vehicle or 4 adult bikes. Book Unwind by the Gulf for an incredible Florida retreat! Call today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Half Bathroom: Toilet Only
- Living Area: Full Sleeper Sofa

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: Full Bed, Shared Bathroom With Shower/Tub combo
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower/Tub combo
- Bunk Room: Twin over Full Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom With Shower/Tub Combo

*RVs, boats, and/or trailers of any kind are not permitted in the neighborhood and will be towed at your expense.

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger.

*Please note that The Gathering Place will be closed for a major pool resurfacing project from Monday, January 27th, to the end of February. Owner badge access will be turned off, and closure notices will be posted on both gate accesses.

WaterSound "Summer Wind" 40 Watch Tower Lane
Watersound, FL
Bedrooms 6
Beds 9
Sleeps 14
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Private Deeded Beach Access
- Located in a secure, gated community with on-site security
- Private infinity edge plunge pool with heating available from October 1st through May 1st for $25 per day
- Elevator
- Golf Cart
- Carriage House with Washer and Dryer and private balcony
- Third Floor deck for sunning and for cocktails with gulf views
- Private infinity edge plunge pool with heating available from October 1st through May 1st for $25 per day
- Complimentary beach setups each morning include 2 beach chairs and 1 umbrella (Service provided Mar 1-Oct 31)

*New amenities coming May 2025!
- TV lounge
- Game/work area
- Reading area
- Refreshment center
- Full bathroom

* This property comes with 3 designated parking spaces dependent on vehicle size. This home permits a maximum of 3 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: 40 Watch Tower Lane, a spacious and light-filled home, was designed with relaxation and comfort in mind. This luxurious home is situated in Dune Ridge within the gated, peaceful WaterSound Community and is just a short stroll down the boardwalk from the neighborhood's expansive private beach.

The first floor includes a well-appointed chef's kitchen and pantry, including a pebble icemaker. A spacious living and dining space opens to an outdoor courtyard with seating, a gas grill and an infinity edge plunge pool. Also found on the first floor are a covered porch, a spacious laundry room with a second refrigerator and beverage cooler, and an elegant primary bedroom outfitted with seating and a work/study area.

The second floor includes three more guest bedrooms and the ultimate primary retreat which features an immense bathroom with steam shower, soaking tub, spacious walk-in closet with washer and dryer, a work/study space, and a private porch.

A detached carriage house offers an excellent space for guests wanting their own privacy and includes comfortable seating, a washer and dryer and a private balcony.

Plan cocktails or dinner on the third-floor covered tower while taking in gulf views and stunning sunsets or soak up the sun in the lounge area.

This is a wonderful home where no expenses were spared. Multi-generational families will appreciate the elevator, providing access to all three floors, and the golf cart which make a trip to the beach a breeze. Enjoy the many amenities of the WaterSound Community (including access to golf nearby) and explore the surrounding 30A area and various shopping and dining venues in neighboring Grayton Beach, Seaside, Seagrove, Alys Beach and Rosemary Beach!

- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Ceiling Fan, Seating Area, Private Bathroom With Shower and soaking Tub.

- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Seating Area, Ceiling Fan, Private Bathroom With Shower, Washer and Dryer and Private balcony

Rosemary Beach "Mr. Sanders" 122 West Kingston Road -Main House
Rosemary Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 6
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Located North of 30A
- Private pool and courtyard
- Close proximity to neighborhood parks and community pools
- Fully equipped kitchen
- 4 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforters, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Revel in a dream Rosemary Beach vacation at Mr. Sanders, a luxury 2-level Main House that accommodates up to 12 guests between 4 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms. Tucked within a quiet section of Rosemary Beach—and only a 6-minute stroll to the beach walkover—this elegant property offers a secluded escape while still providing exceptional walkability. When not at the beach, exploring the local shops, or taking advantage of the community pools, guests will love enjoying sun-dappled days in the sheltered courtyard beside the private pool and bubbling fountain.

A picturesque setting for warm-weather getaways, this grand Main House was crafted in the colonial style of the Dutch West Indies. Step inside to find a spacious entry, perfect for slipping off sandals and depositing beach bags. In the great room, heavy wood beams and dark hardwood floors contrast merrily against the crisp white walls and cheerful artwork. The culinary-inspired will appreciate the kitchen’s Thermador appliances and plentiful counter space, including a 4-stool breakfast bar and an island that seats 2. The adjacent living area offers comfortable seating and a 75” Roku TV for entertaining. Attached is an ornate dining room with a formal table for 8, an iron chandelier, and abstract art to set the mood for intimate dinner parties.

Need to mix in a bit of work on the getaway? A desk is tucked into the hall across from the coffee and dry bar. Meander outside to discover the home’s heart, a beautiful courtyard with a pool, fountain, and patio furnishings, and adorned with strung lights and flourishing potted plants to create a luscious tropical haven. When it’s time to retire, retreat to the primary bedroom with a king bed and private bathroom. For convenience, all the home’s bedrooms feature a Roku TV. This level also includes a charming laundry room for refreshing beach towels. On the second floor, another primary bedroom with a king bed, couch, private bathroom, and private balcony invites restful nights. The 2 guest bedrooms include balcony access and respectively feature 1 king bed and 2 full beds.

Experience an enchanting Rosemary Beach holiday at Mr. Sanders Main House. On-site parking for 2 vehicles and 4 complimentary bikes invites guests to explore. The nearest beach access is Walkover I off West Water Street—a 6-minute walk away. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 12):
FIRST FLOOR:
Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
Other sleeping arrangement: Queen Sleeper Sofa in Living Room

SECOND FLOOR:
Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
Guest Bedroom: Two Full Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

In addition, you also have access to these fantastic Rosemary Beach amenities:
- Coquina Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.
- Sky Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 6 am to 10 pm (Adult Only Hours, 6 am-9 am & 8 pm-10 pm)
- Cabana Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.
- Barbados Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

*Your rental property may share a courtyard or lot with a carriage house that is owner-occupied or rented to separate guests from your party.

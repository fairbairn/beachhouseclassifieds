Seacrest Beach "Footprints in the Sand" 36 Kareny Lane
Seacrest Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-050790
Bedrooms 3
Beds 6
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Short walk to deeded beach access
- 6-Passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 3 adult bikes
- 300 FEET from the Community Pool (Seasonally Heated)
- Optimally located between Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach
- Screened-in Patio on the First Floor
- Screened-in Balcony on the Second Floor
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

DETAILS: Advantageously located between Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach, Footprints in the Sand is a 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom home that sleeps up to 8 guests in Seacrest Beach. With a family-friendly town center and laid-back atmosphere, Seacrest Beach is one of the best spots to enjoy Scenic 30A. Guests will especially love this property’s quick access to the community pool and short walk to the beach—only 5 minutes!

Sunshine and beach vibes mingle in perfect harmony inside this beautiful beach home, featuring hardwood floors and minimalistic coastal decor. The open kitchen includes KitchenAid appliances and a breakfast bar that seats 2, making it easy to prepare a delectable dinner. Dine at the rustic farmhouse dining table—a long bench and 2 chairs provide plenty of seating. Afterward, settle in for a movie night in the living area, complete with a 55” Samsung Smart TV, 2 armchairs, and a cozy couch. This floor has a guest bedroom with a king bed and a bunk room with 2 twin-over-twin beds for sleeping accommodations. Adjacent to the living area, an enclosed front porch beckons guests to bask in the fresh air while lounging on the hanging bed swing, relishing a sun-dappled breakfast at the table for 4, or curling up with a book on the wicker egg chair in the corner. On the side of the home is a grill.

Venture upstairs for the primary bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom, and delightful screened balcony—furnished with cushioned wicker chairs and an overhead fan.

Relaxation and sun-soaked days await at Footprints on the Sand in Seacrest Beach. There is parking for 2 vehicles—plus 3 complimentary adult bikes and a 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle. For private deeded beach access, follow Kareny Lane and turn left on Patina Boulevard. Continue until East County Highway 30A and find the beach access directly across the street. Book this lovely beach home today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (SLEEPS 8):
FIRST FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
-Bunk Room: Two Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

SECOND FLOOR:
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

*Please note that there is active construction next to this home.

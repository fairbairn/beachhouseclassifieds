HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
*Per Watercolor HOA, please include all guests aged 5 and older in your total count of adult guests to receive amenity and pool access wristbands. Sleep arrangements are provided as a courtesy and are not considered in the occupancy count.
**This home is allowed 4 amenity access bands.**

- 2 adult bikes provided
- Centrally located in WaterColor
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Glorious Gulf, Beautiful BeachClub, Divine Dining and Sensational Shopping....all just STEPS AWAY from this unique bi-level multi-family residence. Two furnished balconies, offering shade, sun and fresh Gulf air, complement its location on the beachside of wonderful 30-A. The convenient elevator quietly delivers guests right to their front door, which opens to a spacious first level floor plan including a laundry, bathroom, kitchen and open living/dining room. Ethereal lighting effected by transom French doors and innumerable casement windows draws attention to the gorgeous hardwood floors throughout and the eclectic furnishings including a queen sleeper sofa, Flat Screen TV and DVD player. Stainless steel appliances, black granite and maple cabinets stand ready for the gourmand or carefree chef. The ample dining/game table enjoys its own bright and airy nook adjacent to the first level balcony and seats six while the breakfast bar offers martini stools for an additional four. A full bath with shower and jetted tub, closets and separate laundry with stackable w/d complete the main level. The primary suite is privately located on the 2nd level and offers: a king size bed, window seat with treetop views, a Flat Screen TV with DVD, closet and stellar penthouse balcony for morning coffee, private sunbathing and sundowners. The en suite primary bath privately bathed in natural light, has an oversized walk-in shower, double sink vanity, separate water closet. Perfect for small family gatherings and/or romantic getaways, this condo offers privacy and yet is in the center of all that is WaterColor.

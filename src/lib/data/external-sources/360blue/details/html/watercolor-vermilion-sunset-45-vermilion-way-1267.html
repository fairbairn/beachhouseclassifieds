Watercolor "Vermilion Sunset" 45 Vermilion Way
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-059318
Bedrooms 7
Beds 13
Sleeps 14
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 2
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
-Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 14 wristbands.
- Short bike ride to the beach
- Close to community pools and Western Lake
- Private saltwater pool **Pool heating is available from Oct 1-May 1 for $60 per day**
- Onsite Peloton Bike, please remember to bring your bike shoes
- Game area with Golden Tee, Multicade and Playstation 4
- Gas Fireplace
- Carriage house
- 6 adult bikes
- Parking for up to 3 vehicles
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Details: Vermilion Sunset is on Vermilion Way, in WaterColor, sleeps 14 and is close to beach access, community pools, and Western Lake. This 6-bedroom, 5.5-bathroom home has a Carriage House, game room, and porches.

The first floor has 3 guest bedrooms with private bathrooms. A peaceful, light-filled guest bedroom has a King bed and private bathroom with double sinks, marble tiles, and a shower. A second guest bedroom has a King bed and a private bathroom with shower. Finally, another king bed and a private bathroom with a shower/tub combo

On the second floor are the kitchen and primary bedroom. The primary bedroom has a modern 4-poster King bed and access to a private screened porch. Its private bathroom has a separate shower and a soaking tub uniquely positioned across from a vanity. Guests will enjoy the space and thoughtful design of the kitchen, the open-concept living and dining room. Premium appliances, a farmhouse sink, pale blue and white cabinets, and a marble-topped island with seating for 4 forms a luxurious cooking space that guests will gravitate to. A round dining table has seating for 6. Doors open from the living and dining room onto a screened balcony with a wrap-around sofa and porch bed swing.

The third floor is all about having fun with some throwback video game nostalgia. Guests will love playing Donkey Kong and Centipede on a brand new1980s-era arcade cabinet. Comfy sofas, a Playstationand 100s of DVDs will keep everyone entertained in the media room.

A fourth-floor tower has a foosball table, a Peloton bike, and access to a balcony with lake views.

Inside the detached Carriage House you will find ample seating in the open living and kitchen area. A guest bedroom has a king bed and shares a bathroom with a shower with a bunk tower with 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds as well as a full sleeper sofa.

Guests can bike to the beach on 6 included adult bikes.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 14)
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower and Tub

THIRD FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Twin XL Daybed & Twin XL-Over-Full Bunkbed

CARRIAGE HOUSE:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom With Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: 2 Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
- Additional Sleeping Area: Full Sleeper Sofa

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property, forfeit any rental payments, and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

*Please note: Effective March 15, 2014, Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be made through The Electric Cart Company and will be limited to 80 vehicles throughout the community at any given time. Only one cart may be rented per home, and rentals will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis.

*The garage is not for guest use.

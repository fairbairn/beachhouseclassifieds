WaterColor "Outta the Blue" 32 Bluestem Lane
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-046752
Bedrooms 4
Beds 7
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 12 wristbands.
- Located in the Lake District of WaterColor
- Professionally decorated
- This home is equipped with Mediacom’s Xtream WiFi 360pro, featuring eero’s TrueMesh and the latest Wi-Fi 6 technology
- Roku smart TV with streaming capabilities and Mediacom DVR
- All windows have professional decorator blackout shades, and all bedrooms have additional light-blocking curtains
- Open concept kitchen, living, and dining area
- Keurig coffee maker
- Flat-screen TVs in all bedrooms
- Blu-ray players in both living areas, primary bedroom, and bunk room.
- Weber gas grill
- Outdoor fireplace
- 2 dedicated parking spots with additional parking found throughout community
- 4 adult bikes
- 6 seater Low-Speed Vehicle
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAIL: This cozy 4-bedroom, 4.5-bath beach home is located in the highly coveted WaterColor community and everything you imagine a beach house should be!

This professionally decorated home features an open concept kitchen, living, and dining area and is a haven for those who prefer privacy and quiet mornings on the porch.

Upon entering the home, the crisp white walls and calming coastal decor draw you into a comfortable layout made for entertaining. The first floor features two king suites, one with a deep soaking tub and tiled shower and the other with a beautifully tiled walk-in shower.

The second floor features an additional living space, kids' play area, primary suite, and bunk room. All rooms feature a flat-screen TV. The home also comes with DVR in the main living room.

Spill out to the relaxing patio, where a fireplace and dining area await your summer nights!

When you're ready to explore, this home is only 1 minute from Seaside and 2 minutes from the beach. Hop on a bike and take in all that Watercolor has to offer!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 12):
FIRST FLOOR
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Double Vanities, Rain Shower, and Separate Full-Size Kohler Soaking Tub
- Guest Bedroom: 2 Full Beds plus 2 Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments, as well as be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

Please note that there is currently construction going on behind the home.

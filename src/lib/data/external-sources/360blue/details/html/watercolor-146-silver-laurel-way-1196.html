Watercolor 146 Silver Laurel Way
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-017488
Bedrooms 5
Beds 5
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
*Per WaterColor HOA, please include all guests aged 5 and older in your total count of adult guests to receive amenity and pool access wristbands. Sleep arrangements are provided as a courtesy and are not considered in the occupancy count.
**This home is allowed 12 amenity access bands.**

- One of the highest rooftops in WaterColor with amazing Gulf views
- Access to community pools, nature trails, and dune lakes
- Conveniently located near Publix & restaurants
- Professionally decorated
- Multiple porches
- Outdoor shower
- 5 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant – ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, a second pool deck with additional lounge seating, a third pool with zero-entry, an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and a new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: This 5–bedroom, 5.5–bathroom WaterColor home at 146 Silver Laurel Way sleeps 12. Guests will love watching the sunset sky ablaze with color from a tower with Gulf views. Cozy reading nooks, huge porches, luxurious appliances, and close to community pools, parks, and beach access from the Park District!

On the first floor, a guest bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom with shower, and a private entrance sits across from a dining room with seating for 6. An open kitchen with a smartly designed L-shaped island features premium appliances, 2 sinks, a wine chiller, and gas range. The island extends into a table with seating for 5 and creates a nice sense of division in the living area. The living room surrounds a flat-screen TV with tons of natural light from floor-to-ceiling windows and doors that open onto a front porch. Doors from the living area also open to stairs that lead down to an outdoor patio with Adirondack chairs and a gas grill.

The second-floor guest bedrooms offer either a queen or king bed with a private bathroom or a semi-private bathroom, and the bunk area has a twin-over-twin bunk bed and a twin bed. The second-floor primary bedroom has a king bed, a private bathroom with a soaking tub, a shower with marble tiles, an enormous porch, and a large wet bar that leads to the porch. A porch bed swing, dining table, sofas, and chairs all enjoy wooded views and fresh breezes.

A landing on the third floor provides space to unwind and relax with a sofa and cozy armchair and leads to a bedroom with a king bed, twin bed, and private bathroom with shower.

A fourth-floor tower provides incredible views of the Gulf and the surrounding community with a comfortable spot to sit and eat or enjoy the sunset.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS:
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King, Semi-Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Bunk Area: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed with Twin Bed

THIRD FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed and Twin Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property, forfeit rental payments, and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

Please note: Per HOA Policy, all Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be made through The Electric Cart Company and will be limited to 80 vehicles throughout the community at any given time. Homes are permitted one Low-Speed Vehicle per home. Homes with owner-provided Low-Speed Vehicles are not permitted to rent an additional Low-Speed Vehicle.

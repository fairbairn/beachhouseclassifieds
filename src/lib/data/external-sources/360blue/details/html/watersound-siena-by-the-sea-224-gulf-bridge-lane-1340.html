WaterSound "Siena By The Sea" 224 Gulf Bridge Lane
Watersound, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 10
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gated Community with on-site Security
- Located in the Peninsula of WaterSound
- Short walk or bike ride to the beach
- Private pool **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for $30 per day**
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach service set (2 beach chairs and 1umbrella)
- Private Deeded Beach Access
- Professionally decorated
- Gourmet kitchen and high-end appliances
- Sonos
- 4-passenger golf cart
- 5 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

*This property comes with 2 designated parking spaces. This home permits a maximum of 2 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: Located in the Peninsula in WaterSound, "Siena By The Sea" is a stunning 5-bedroom, 5.5-bathroom home that can sleep 12 and features a private pool, 4-passenger golf cart, bikes, complimentary beach chair setups, and professionally decorated interiors. In addition, the home has been designed with top-of-the-line finishes and custom works of art throughout each room. The gourmet kitchen is on the first floor and has beautiful marble countertops and wooden accents through trey ceilings. There is seating for 11 at the island and a formal dining table to enjoy a meal together. The dining and living area have floor-to-ceiling windows and French doors that lead to the back patio. There is a beautiful king bedroom suite on the first floor with a private bathroom and oversized windows that spill natural light into the room.

The second floor, which has a balcony overlooking the neighborhood, has additional guest rooms. The first 2 rooms have king beds and private bathrooms, while the third has a queen bed and private bathroom. The loft on the third floor has an awesome lookout tower and indoor seating area with a flat-screen TV. The bunk room has 3 sets of twin-over-twin bunk beds and its bathroom--perfect for kids!

The exterior of the home is just as special as the interior. The large, fenced backyard has a boutique-sized pool with lounge chairs and covered patio seating with a grill area. So don't miss your chance to stay just steps from private beach access and all the fun that the award-winning community of WaterSound has to offer.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS

FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

THIRD FLOOR:
- Bunk Bedroom: Three Twin-over-Twin Bunk beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

*Weddings or parties/groups of people or guest of guest are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

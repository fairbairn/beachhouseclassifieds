DETAILS: Enjoy a fabulous retreat a block from the shimmering Gulf at The Sea Shak, a charming 8-bedroom, 8.5-bathroom Seacrest Beach home. Designed for hosting large groups and multiple families, this enchanting escape accommodates a total of 24 guests and features a private pool, indoor-outdoor spaces, and beach access a few steps away!

Modern décor and crisp shiplap walls curate a peaceful atmosphere throughout the first level, where guests will find a sprawling lounge area and wet bar. Spend leisurely afternoons playing shuffleboard, cards, or watching TV. The pool deck is steps outside for swimming and alfresco meals—made even easier thanks to the outdoor kitchen! Be sure to end the night relaxing around the firepit. When it’s time for bed, the bunk room features 2 built-in twin-over-twin bunks, or guests can take the in-home elevator upstairs.

The second-level great room is the focal point of the home, inviting guests to relax together. Matching couches and armchairs frame the TV in the living space, while an 10-person dining table encourages lively group meals. The chef’s kitchen features marble counters, an island that seats 4, and high-end appliances, including a Wolf 6-burner gas cooktop. When the fresh air calls, step onto the balcony, furnished with an outdoor table and a cozy seating area. The level’s 2 guest bedrooms each have 1 king bed and a private bathroom, while the 1 bunk room has 2 sets of twin-over-twin bunks.

On the top floor, discover the primary bedroom with a king bed, alongside another elegant living space with a TV, workplace, and kitchenette. Both connect to the spacious balcony, which boasts a porch swing and a spiral staircase to the observation deck. Savor Gulf views while lounging on the patio couch or sunbathing on the chaise loungers. Additionally, there are 2 guest bedrooms featuring king beds and private bathrooms, as well as 1 bunk room with 2 sets of twin-over-twin bunks.

Indulge in Florida fun and Gulf views at Seacrest Beach’s The Sea Shak. Parking for 5 vehicles makes exploring easy, or cruise along 30A’s bike path using the 8 complimentary adult bikes. The neighborhood beach access is across the street, east of 51 Green Street. Book today!

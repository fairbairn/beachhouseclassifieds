Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 7
Sleeps 10

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- 6 seater golf cart
- 5 adult bikes provided
- Stunning home in Watersound West Beach
- Large gourmet kitchen
- Large screened-in porch with sitting area that backs up to wooded area
- Enjoy the use of 2 complimentary backpack beach chairs provided with your home
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: 22 Sextant Lane is a beautiful Florida home with lovely views! Located in the popular Watersound West Beach community, this 2-story property sleeps up to 10 guests with 4 bedrooms and 5 bathrooms. Guests will love the 2 living spaces, 5-minute walk to the beach, and access to the community pool. Relax and enjoy the sun at this Gulf Coast home!

Upon entering the home, you will immediately be in awe of the natural textures, creamy colors, and subtle beach décor. The first floor has an open layout that marries the living, dining, and kitchen areas. Host friends and family in the living room on the 2 linen-white couches and 2 cozy armchairs before ushering them into the dining room with 8 seats. Listen to conversations of loved ones as you prep dinner in the chef’s kitchen with stainless steel appliances, large kitchen island with 3 stools, and plenty of cabinet space. Down the hallway, you will find a laundry room, guest bedroom with queen bed and private shower, and additional sleeping options with 1 twin bed and 1 twin trundle.

On the second-floor landing, you will find the additional living area with a TV and 2 armchairs. Throughout this level of the home are most of the bedrooms—2 primary suites with king beds and private baths, as well as 1 bunk room with twin-over-full bed—spaced out perfectly for additional privacy.

This Florida home wouldn’t be complete without the large yard and spacious screened-in porch. With enough room for the whole family, this area has an alfresco dining table, TV, 2 armchairs, and a linen couch. Backing up directly to a wooded area gives you peace and quiet.

If you're looking for a little more adventure, explore the area on the 6-seater electric golf cart, or the 5 adult bikes provided with the home. Don’t wait! Book 22 Sextant Lane today for an incredible Florida Coast retreat today.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Additional Living Area: Twin Bed and Twin Trundle

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Full Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

** Bicycles are allowed in the WaterSound West Beach community on the streets. Due to safety and legal concerns, the WaterSound West HOA will no longer allow bicycles on the West Beach boardwalks. However, they are still permitted on the beach walkover, with the following provisions. (1) Bicycles must be walked from where the pavement ends at the clubhouse to the entrance of the beach walkover, where the railing begins; and (2) Bicycles must be ridden at the lowest possible speed while on the beach walkover. **

* Unfortunately due to HOA rules the guest will not be able to rent golf carts.

*Please note- availability of owner-provided amenities is not guaranteed.

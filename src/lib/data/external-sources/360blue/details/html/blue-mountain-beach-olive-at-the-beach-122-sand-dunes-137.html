Blue Mountain Beach "Olive at the Beach" 122 Sand Dunes
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 10
Sleeps 14
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf views
- Private deeded beach access
- Convenient location
- Just steps from the beach
- Private pool **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for $25 per day**
- Outdoor grill
- Large porches
- 4 adult bikes provided
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Experience total relaxation at Olive at the Beach in Blue Mountain Beach. Nestled a short 3-minute stroll from deeded beach access, this 5-bedroom, 4-bathroom home sleeps up to 14 guests. Imagine sun-kissed days lounging by the private pool, unwinding on the large porches, or playing in the surf with family and friends.

Sprawling oak floors, beadboard accents, and coastal décor create an elegant yet laid-back interior inside this 3-story home. Drop off beach bags and flip-flops by the front door before heading into the open-concept great room. A blue-and-white kitchen with high-end appliances invites guests to whip up a local seafood feast. Spend cheerful mornings sipping coffee at the 3-stool breakfast bar before planning the day over pancakes and eggs at the rustic dining table for 9. A sitting space in the corner with a plush L-shaped sofa and an oversized coffee table is perfect for kid’s activities or family board games. Head out the glass doors to sip lemonade on the spacious back porch, furnished with Adirondack seating and a picnic table. Down the steps is the sparkling private pool, 5 lounge chairs, and a propane grill. There is a single guest bedroom on this level with a king bed.

The second level hosts a peaceful primary bedroom with a king bed. Attached is a room with 2 twin couch beds. Both rooms have access to the balcony—slip outside to relax on the deck or enjoy a sunrise yoga flow. The spa inspired primary bathroom boasts a soaking tub, twin vanities and separate shower. A second oversized guest bedroom with 1 queen bed, twin over twin bunk and twin over twin trundle bed, a sitting area with a TV and PlayStation completes the level. The third-level loft features a queen bed, a private bathroom, and a balcony with shimmering Gulf views.

A restorative Emerald Coast vacation awaits at Olive at the Beach in Blue Mountain Beach. Exploring is easy with parking for 4 vehicles and 4 complimentary adult bikes. Private beach access is a 3-minute walk away at the Gulf Point Road Access. The nearest public beach access is Blue Mountain Regional. Book this coastal home today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 14):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shares Common Bath with Tub/Shower Combo

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Attached Bath with Separate Shower and Tub
- Bedroom: Two Twin couch beds with Connecting Door to Primary Bedroom
- Guest Bedroom: One Queen Bed, Twin over Twin Bunkbed and Twin over Twin trundle bed, Sitting Area w/ Couch, TV and Play Station, Attached Bath with Shower Only

THIRD FLOOR:
-Loft: One Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo, Private Balcony with Gulf Views and beverage refrigerator

Weddings or other events are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

*Unfortunately the fireplace is not for guest use.

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger.

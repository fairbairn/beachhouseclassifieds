Dune Allen "Palms of Dune Allen 301" 4961 W County Rd 30A Unit 301
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 6
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Elevator
- Private Community Beach Access
- Community Pool
- 2 parking passes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

*This condo is going through a full renovation. New photos and descriptions to come!

DETAILS: Escape for a memorable Emerald Coast vacation at Palms of Dune Allen 301. This beautiful 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom condo sleeps 10 guests, making it a perfect family or group retreat. Enjoy lounging around the community pool and taking advantage of the dedicated beach access—right beside the property! Home to rare coastal dune lakes, quiet beaches, meandering hiking trails, and local boutiques, this well-loved section of 30A has something for every traveler!

Warm woods, woven elements, and neutral tones create an elegant, luxurious beach setting, beautifully contrasting against the vivid blue of the Gulf view. Meanwhile, the great room’s open layout encourages flowing conversation between the living, dining, and kitchen for maximum togetherness. Savor a relaxing evening making fresh seafood tacos in the kitchen, which features high-end appliances and a 4-stool breakfast bar, perfect for splitting a pitcher of sangria after a day in the sun. Dine beside water views at the wooden table for 4 before adjourning to the living space, where a pillow-soft sectional and 2 plush accent chairs offer comfortable seating for movie nights or games. Through the sliding glass doors, discover a spacious balcony furnished with a patio couch and a 4-person bar table—the perfect spot to sit back and watch the Gulf waves lap the shoreline.

At bedtime, the lovely primary bedroom welcomes restorative rest with a king bed, private bathroom, and serene sea-inspired décor. For additional sleeping accommodations, choose between the charming guest bedroom with a queen bed and private bathroom or the light-filled bunk room with 2 twin-over-queen bunk beds. A hall closet with a stacked washer and dryer adds convenience to each stay.

Palms of Dune Allen 301 has everything guests need for a fantastic Emerald Coast getaway, including stunning views, splendid amenities, and on-site parking for 2 vehicles. Book today for your next beach vacation!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Bunkroom: (2) Twin-over-Queen Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger.

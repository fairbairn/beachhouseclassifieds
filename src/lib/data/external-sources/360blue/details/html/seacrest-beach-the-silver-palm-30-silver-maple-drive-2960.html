DETAILS: An unforgettable beach escape awaits at The Silver Palm in Seacrest Beach! Nestled in a gated community with tranquil forest views, this stunning coastal home is perfect for large groups, accommodating up to 12 guests across 5 bedrooms and 5.5 bathrooms. Enjoy a relaxing swim in the private pool, visit the expansive community pool for the kids to splash around, or take advantage of the designated beach access for easy trips to the soft, sandy shores. Located only minutes from vibrant 30A, guests can explore unique shops, local restaurants, and endless entertainment!

Step inside this breathtaking 3-story property, showcasing a delightful blue-and-white exterior, with each level offering its own balcony and spectacular views. The first floor is the heart of the home, offering an inviting great room complete with a cozy fireplace and plush furniture. Sink into the couch and unwind with a good movie or spend quality time together around the warm fire. Beyond the living area is the dining table, a cozy spot for family meals and game nights. The modern kitchen is a chef’s dream, featuring sleek cabinetry, high-end appliances, and a spacious island with seating for 3—ideal for coffee or quick bites. French doors open to a stunning wraparound porch with an outdoor kitchen and fenced-in pool area. Enjoy hot-off-the-grill burgers at the 6-person alfresco table, catch the game on the wall-mounted TV, or play games in the private pool—it’s perfect for entertainment! After a busy day, unwind in the first-floor guest bedroom with a spacious king bed and private bathroom.

The second floor offers comfortable accommodations, including a lovely primary bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom, and balcony with a porch swing, alongside 2 guest bedrooms with private bathrooms. One guest bedroom features a king bed, while the other includes a king bed with 2 lofted XL twin beds for versatility. A laundry completes this floor.

A gorgeous third-floor primary bedroom offers complete privacy, featuring a king-sized bed, TV, workspace, and private bathroom with a soaking tub and shower. Enjoy the peaceful porch swing while taking in the views.

The Silver Palm is the perfect destination for creating unforgettable memories! With on-site parking for 3 vehicles—plus 5 complimentary bikes and a neighborhood shuttle—exploring Scenic 30A and using the dedicated beach access is effortless. Book now!

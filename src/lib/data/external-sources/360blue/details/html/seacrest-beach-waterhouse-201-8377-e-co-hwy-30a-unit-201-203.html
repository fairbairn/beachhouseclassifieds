- Steps from the beach
- Pet-friendly

- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: A serene escape awaits at Waterhouse 201, a beautiful 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom condo that sleeps 9 in Seacrest Beach. Nestled in Scenic 30A, this idyllic, laid-back community is known for its white-sand beaches and family-friendly spirit. Enjoy blissful days playing in the surf, browsing the local boutiques, or indulging in enticing eateries. Guests of this pet-friendly retreat can take advantage of the community rooftop pool with Gulf views,a hot tub, fitness center, sauna, grills, and private beach access—right across the street!

An airy and timeless atmosphere welcomes guests inside this single-story condo. Shiplap wainscotting and honey hardwood floors tie together the combined living, dining, and kitchen areas, decorated in a mix of soft blue and breezy white textiles. The living area’s 2 slip-covered couches, armchairs, and 65” Smart TV beckons guests to kick back and relax after a day of exploring. The thoughtfully designed kitchen features high-end Bosch and Viking appliances, ample counter space for meal prep, and a vast 6-person breakfast bar for entertaining. Savor sunlit morning brunches and relaxed evening meals at the charming dining table with 5 chairs and a cozy 3-person bench. Along the back wall, 2 sliding glass doors fill the space with natural light and lead out to a spacious balcony. Meander outside to dine alfresco at the table for 6 or sip a morning cup of coffee on the cushioned chairs. At evening’s end, retire for a restful evening in the primary bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom, and balcony access. There are 2 guest bedrooms—the first has a queen bed and a private bathroom, while the second has a full-over-full bed and a twin trundle. A convenient laundry room helps keep swimwear and beach towels fresh for tomorrow’s fun!

Escape to Waterhouse for a peaceful and memorable Seacrest Beach vacation—steps from the sugar-soft sand beach. On-site parking for 2 vehicles and 3 complimentary adult bikes makes it easy to explore or ride over to The Big Chill for entertainment and dining. To hit the beach, simply cross the street to the private beach access past the Timpoochee Trail. Book this convenient beach condo today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 9):
FIRST FLOOR:
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
-Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
-Guest Bedroom: Full-over-Full Bunk Bed and Twin Trundle, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*We are happy to welcome up to 2 dogs, not to exceed the weight limit of 50lbs for an additional non-refundable pet fee of $400.

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and Callista Vacation Rentals fines.

*This property has a strict non-smoking policy. Violation of this policy puts the guest at risk of being fined and/or evicted.

*Beach chair service is available at cost to the guests from March 1st to November 15th.

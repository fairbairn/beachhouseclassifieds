Blue Mountain Beach "Wales Tale" 369 Old Beach Road
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-050644
Bedrooms 6
Beds 12
Sleeps 18
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf-front
- Convenient location
- Private, gated community with community pool - the pool is heated Spring and Fall months only
- Highchair
- 6 adult bikes
- Parking for 3 vehicles
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Experience unparalleled vistas from "Wales Tale!" This premier beachfront retreat is in Blue Mountain Beach, complete with 180-degree views of the Gulf and Draper Lake! This 6-bedroom, 5.5-bathroom home has been professionally designed with a spacious open floor plan for optimal comfort. The first-floor entry level is complete with a secondary living room, queen suite, hallway bunk beds, and mudroom.

The second-floor living and kitchen areas were thoughtfully constructed to take advantage of the stunning Gulf views and oversized balcony to enjoy outdoor living at its best. The kitchen features stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, gas range, and custom cabinetry. A king guest bedroom is located down the hall with a king bed, complete with a sitting area, balcony access, and en-suite bathroom. There are also 2 guest rooms, both with a queen bed and vaulted ceilings.

The primary suite is on the third floor, accessible by the formal staircase. The idyllic floor-to-ceiling glass windows opening up to a private balcony frame the Gulf of Mexico perfectly, making mornings a dream to wake up and sip coffee with a view. The en-suite bathroom also features a spacious vanity area and a soaking tub. A bunk room is also on this floor, featuring 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds.

Highlights of this Old Blue Mountain home include an oversized wrap-around porch and ample parking. It is in a private, gated community on the West side of 30A, with a community pool and adjoining clubhouse. Enjoy 6 adult bikes with the home to cruise a short distance to world-class restaurants, outstanding shopping, local entertainment, and renowned golf courses.

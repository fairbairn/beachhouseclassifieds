Grayton Beach "Grayton Bliss" 120 Banfill Street
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 7
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf views
- Community pool
- 1 minute walk to expansive guaranteed public beach access. Only 4 houses back!!!
- Close proximity to local dining establishments - fantastic food and lively atmosphere
- 4 adult bikes
- Third-floor lookout tower with amazing Gulf views
- Professionally decorated
- Stand-alone ice maker
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: The aptly named "Grayton Bliss" is a 4-bedroom, 5-bathroom Gulf-view home that sleeps 12 in the heart of Grayton Beach. Guests will enjoy incredible Gulf views from a lookout tower and stunning views of a coastal dune lake and the surrounding Grayton Beach neighborhood from large windows throughout the home. 1 minute walk to the expansive public beach, pool, restaurants, Grayton Beach State Park, and shops.

All 4 bedrooms and a wet bar with a freestanding ice maker are conveniently located on the first floor. A spacious primary bedroom has a king bed, a 65” Smart TV, and a private bathroom with a glass-enclosed shower. Across the hall is a guest bedroom with a king bed, a 32” Smart TV, a private bathroom with a shower, and sliding glass doors that open to a front porch with Adirondack chairs. A second guest bedroom has a king bed, a 32” Smart TV, and a private bathroom with a shower/tub combo. A bunk room has 2 full-over-queen built-in bunk beds and a private bathroom with a shower.

A cozy mini-living room with views of Western Lake welcomes guests to the second floor. It's a beautiful spot for kids to gather and watch TV while adults gather in an adjacent kitchen and main living area. A huge open-concept kitchen is a chef’s dream! Gleaming marble countertops, premium Wolf appliances, and an eat-in island that seats 6 enjoy light from 3 large windows. A separate coffee/drinks station with a wine fridge and ice maker stands out with glittering chevron tile. The calming white and gray interior and ship-lap ceiling continue into the adjacent dining and living area. Here, guests can enjoy an 85” Smart TV with a SONOS sound system tucked into an alcove but viewed comfortably from 2 comfy slip-covered sofas. Or dine at a dining table that seats 8.

The third floor is all about the views! At the top of the stairs is a wet bar with a wine cooler and mini-fridge. Take in views of Western Lake while prepping drinks, or step outside and enjoy stunning Gulf views from the Lookout Tower. Watch the stars shine from the comfort of a wrap-around couch and enjoy moon-lit views of historic Grayton Beach late into the night.

Don't wait to book Grayton Bliss! Explore Grayton Beach on 4 included adult bikes and enjoy this incredible location! Book Grayton Bliss today!

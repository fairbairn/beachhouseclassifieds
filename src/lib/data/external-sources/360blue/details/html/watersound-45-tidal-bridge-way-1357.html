WaterSound 45 Tidal Bridge Way
Watersound, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 7
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gated Community with on-site Security
- Steps from Community Pool and Outdoor Lounge Area
- Steps from Beach Walk Over
- Wet Bar
- Five Adult Bikes
- Gourmet Kitchen and High End Appliances
- Large Outdoor Living Spaces
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach -service set (2 beach chairs and 1umbrella)
- Private Deeded Beach Access
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

This property comes with 2 designated parking spaces. This home permits a maximum of 2 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: Escape to this beautiful WaterSound Bridges home and let your cares float away. This spacious and inviting home features 5 bedrooms, 5.5 bathrooms, and lovely accommodations for 12 guests.

Gorgeous hardwood floors, vaulted ceilings, and tasteful coastal decor lend a refreshing and relaxing ambiance to this home's main living level. A spacious floor plan joins the kitchen, living, and dining areas and creates a wonderful space for families to enjoy time together. Guests can sink down into the lavish sectional sofa and enjoy a family movie on the living room's large flat-screen television. Granite countertops, custom cabinetry, and premium stainless steel appliances make the gourmet kitchen a delightful place to prepare family meals. The dining area offers seating for 6 guests, with additional seating nearby at the breakfast bar. French doors lead to the home's main balcony, which offers lots of seating, a gas grill, and breathtaking scenic views.

Three beautiful guest rooms on the first floor offer a variety of sleeping configurations. The home's first primary suite features a king bed, a private en suite bathroom with a shower and a separate soaking tub, and access to the private screened porch. The second guest room offers two full beds and a private bathroom with a shower/tub combo. Guests in the final first-floor guest room can enjoy two plush twin beds and a private bathroom with a shower/tub combo.

Gorgeous furnishings and high, vaulted ceilings create a luxurious ambiance in the second-floor primary suite. Guests in this room will love the lavish king bed, plush chairs, flat-screen television, and private en suite bathroom featuring an oversized vanity, dual sinks, and a glass-front shower.

The carriage house offers wonderful accommodations for guests seeking a little more privacy. This space features a queen bed guestroom and a private bathroom with a shower/tub combo. A mounted flat-screen television welcome guests to the living area, and the kitchenette boasts everything guests would need - including a dishwasher, a mini refrigerator, an oven/stove, ample cabinet and countertop space, and a bistro table and stools.

This incredible Watersound Bridges home offers everything you need for the ultimate 30A getaway. Don't miss out on this amazing property!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: Two Full Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Two Twin Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

CARRIAGE HOUSE:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

*Weddings or parties/groups of people or guest of guest are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

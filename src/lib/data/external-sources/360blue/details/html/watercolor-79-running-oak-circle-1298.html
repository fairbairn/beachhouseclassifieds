HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
-Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 12 wristbands.
- Spacious Porch
- 4 Adult Bikes
- Electric Low-Speed Vehicle
- Outdoor Grill
- 2 Designated Parking Spots
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Location meets coastal luxury! This gorgeous, recently renovated 2-story, 5-bedroom and 4.5-bathroom home in the heart of Watercolor's Forest District features spectacular outdoor spaces, gorgeous heart pine floor floors and a chef's kitchen. The vast front porch features a full-size porch swing and comfy rocking chairs, making it the perfect spot to have your morning coffee or tea. Stepping inside, you'll find beautiful vaulted ceilings. The chef's kitchen offers granite countertops, top-of-the-line Viking appliances and a 4-burner gas range. Adjacent, the dining area offers seating for six, while the living room features a large flat-screen television and Bluray. Just down the hallway is a conveniently situated laundry room with a full-size washer and dryer. The primary suite features a king bed, flat-screen TV, and a connecting ensuite bathroom with a walk-in shower. The first floor also offers access to a cozy screened-in patio where you'll find a vast flat-screen television and outdoor table seating for six - the perfect place for the whole family to gather! Just off the porch is a gas grill.

The second floor features two guest bedrooms and a cozy bunk room. The guest bedrooms both have queen beds, flat-screen televisions, and private bathrooms - each with a shower and tub combo. The bunk room has two twin-over-twin bunk beds and a flat-screen television, making it a favorite of the young and the young-at-heart! Adjacent to the main house is the gorgeous carriage house, offering additional privacy for guests. This cozy retreat-within-a-retreat features a guest bedroom with a queen bed and flat-screen television, and private bath with a shower and tub combo.

Guests are welcome to enjoy the four adult bikes and golf cart which are stored in a garage. The private beach, Camp Creek pool and Seaside are a short ride away.

WaterSound 99 Compass Point Way S #207
Watersound, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 4
Sleeps 7
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gated Community with on-site Security
- Private deeded beach access
- Gulf-front
- Furnished porch
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach -service set (2 beach chairs and 1 umbrella)
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

This property comes with 1 designated parking space and 1 first-come first-serve parking space. Please note first come first serve parking is limited and not guaranteed. This home permits a maximum of 2 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: Peaceful beach days and coastal fun await at Sunset Dunes, a beautiful 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom WaterSound condo comfortably accommodating up to 7 guests. Spend quiet mornings enjoying the beauty of the rolling dunes from the privacy of the 2 porches before heading to the community pool or beach—both within walking distance. For entertainment and dining, The Big Chill is a quick trip, or visit nearby Alys Beach, Rosemary Beach, Seagrove, and Seaside to discover why this stretch of Florida is a favorite family-friendly destination!

Natural light fills the open layout of this single-story condo’s great room, highlighting the serene décor of warm neutrals and polished hardwood floors. The living space, tucked within a curved alcove with floor-to-ceiling windows, invites slow, sunny days curled up with friends and family on the L-shaped couch and stylish armchairs. End the night with a movie on the TV. Home-cooked meals are a breeze thanks to the modern kitchen, featuring GE Monogram appliances and a 3-stool breakfast bar perfect for entertaining. When it’s time to eat, the 6-person table can fit the whole group. Head onto the porch off the kitchen to enjoy sunset drinks or morning coffee at the 4-seat dining set. A second porch with soft seating is located beside the dining table. At bedtime, guests can retire to the primary bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom, and TV. There are also 2 charming bedrooms—the first guest bedroom has 1 twin-over King bed, and the second guest bedroom has a queen bed.

With nearby entertainment, walkable beach access, and inviting outdoor spaces, Sunset Dunes in WaterSound has everything guests need for a spectacular Florida vacation. Easily explore with assigned parking for 1 vehicle and extra first-come, first-serve parking for 1 vehicle. For beach access, follow any of the WaterSound boardwalks to the beach—the nearest is by the Dunesider Pool. Book today and start planning the ultimate 30A getaway!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (Sleeps 7)
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Tub And Shower
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-King Bunk Bed, Private Bathroom with Walk In Shower
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*From October 2025 through 2026, WaterSound Compass Point will undergo major exterior refurbishment. Residents and guests should expect construction, boarded windows, limited balcony and garage access, and possible parking disruptions. These safety measures are essential for project completion.

*Unfortunately due to HOA rules the guest will not be able to rent Low-Speed Vehicles and will not be able to bring their own Low-Speed Vehicle.

*Weddings or parties/groups of people or guest of guest are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and Callista by 360 Blue fines.

*This home is not pet-friendly.

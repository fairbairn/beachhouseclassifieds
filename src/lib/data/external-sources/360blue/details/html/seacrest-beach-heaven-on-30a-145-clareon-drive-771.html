Seacrest Beach "Heaven on 30A" 145 Clareon Drive
Seacrest Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-098554
Bedrooms 4
Beds 14
Sleeps 18
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 2
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Private gated beach access 
- Short stroll to the beach
- Low-Speed Vehicle and bike parking at beach access. 
- Complimentary lithium battery golf cart
- Private pool with screened-in patio, grilling area, and outdoor dining
- Professionally decorated interior
- Entertainment options include table games, cornhole, and a foosball table
- Fitness enthusiasts can enjoy a Peloton bike
- Fiber internet with lightning-fast speeds up to 2 Gbps
- Convenient amenities: 4 adult bikes, 7 beach chairs, gas fire pit
- Rigorous cleanliness with Complete Clean Linen Participant certification
- Enjoy plush, complimentary beach towels for your use throughout your stay

Details: Discover the epitome of 30A coastal living at "Heaven on 30A." This 3-story, 4-bedroom retreat, boasting 4 baths plus 2 half-baths, accommodates up to 18 guests and is a mere stroll or bike ride from Alys Beach, Rosemary Beach, and The Big Chill.

The chef’s kitchen, adorned with marble countertops and designer cabinets, features premium stainless Jenn-Air appliances, including a 6-burner gas stove and a Keurig coffeemaker. The dining area offers bar seating for 5 and a farm table for 10, complemented by a half-bath for convenience.

Relax in either of the two living rooms, one with a generous 65” TV and a cozy sectional, the other featuring a love seat and comfortable chairs with a 55” TV. The first floor also hosts a laundry room for keeping your vacation wardrobe fresh.

The primary bedroom, located on the first floor, boasts a private bath with a shower and tub. Doors open to a screened-in porch with a stunning grilling area and outdoor wicker furniture, overlooking the inviting pool area.

Ascending to the second floor, discover two guest bedrooms with king beds, sharing a bath with a shower. Adjacent is another primary bedroom with a king bed and a private bathroom with a shower. The hallway features a bunk area with a twin-over-twin bunk bed and a private bath with a shower/tub combo. An additional sleeping space offers a queen-over-queen bunk.
The third floor is an entertainment haven with a Peloton bike, Foosball, and two queen beds. Unwind and savor every moment at this distinctive vacation home, featuring a total of two queen beds on this floor.

Book your stay at "Heaven on 30A" today! (Note: Weddings are not allowed at this home. Violation may result in eviction, forfeiture of rental payments, and fines.)

- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

- Additional Sleeping: Queen over Queen Bunk

THIRD FLOOR:
* Additional Sleeping: Two Queen Beds

WaterSound "Dunesview" 99 Compass Point Way S #202
Watersound, FL
Bedrooms 2
Beds 4
Sleeps 6
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gated Community with on-site Security
- Closest building to beach boardwalks and the Dunesider pool, which is heated year-round
- Gulf, dune, and sunset views
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach -service set (2 beach chairs and 1umbrella)
- Private deeded beach access
- Complete clean linen program - all linens, including comforter covers, are laundered between guests

This property comes with 1 designated parking space and 1 first-come first-serve parking space. Please note first come first serve parking is limited and not guaranteed. This home permits a maximum of 2 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: Dunesview's fantastic location and thoughtful upgrades ensure a convenient and comfortable stay in Watersound. With a redesigned master bath, pantry, and beverage center, updated electronics, and hardwood and tile flooring throughout, this condo offers options galore for a memorable beach vacation. The private patio provides additional outdoor living space with lounge seating perfect for enjoying morning coffee and evening sunsets. This luxurious residence provides walkable access to all Watersound Beach has to offer.

The primary bedroom has a king bed, balcony access, and a private bathroom with double sinks, separate seated vanity area, and shower. The second bedroom offers 2 queen beds and access to a connected bathroom with a combination bathtub/shower and 2 separate sink vanities.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower
- Guest Bedroom: Two Queen Beds, Shower/Tub Combo
- Living Room: Queen Sleeper

WATERSOUND AMENITIES:
-A 265 acre private, gated, beachfront community located South of Highway 30A and directly on the Gulf of Mexico between Deer Lake State Park and The Hub.
-A mile of beach and seven miles of boardwalks. Perfect for walking and biking.
-The puttering park, a 12-hole putting area suitable for all ages. Equipment provided.
-Use of 3 community pools (Dunesider, Bridges, and Compass Point. Dunesider & Bridges are heated year-round).

*From October 2025 through 2026, WaterSound Compass Point will undergo major exterior refurbishment. Residents and guests should expect construction, boarded windows, limited balcony and garage access, and possible parking disruptions. These safety measures are essential for project completion.

*Weddings or parties/groups of people or guest of guest are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

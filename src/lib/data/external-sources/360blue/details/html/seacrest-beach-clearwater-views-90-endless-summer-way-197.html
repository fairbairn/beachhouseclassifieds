Seacrest Beach "Clearwater Views" 90 Endless Summer Way
Seacrest Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-083846
Bedrooms 3
Beds 4
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Short bike ride to the beach and Rosemary
- Access to Seacrest’s 12,000 square foot community pool
- Complimentary tram service to the beach
- Professionally decorated
- Stainless appliances
- 4 adult bikes
- Firepit
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Experience the charm of a Seacrest Beach getaway at Clearwater Views, a dreamy 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom cottage that sleeps 8. Nestled along Scenic 30A, Seacrest Beach is known for its old-world appeal and enchanting beaches. Enjoy the 12,000-square-foot community pool, tram service to the beach, and multiple local restaurants and shops. Guests will love spending peaceful days relaxing on the porch and patio—with beach access minutes away!

A lovely front porch framed by lush palm trees welcomes guests to this adorable beach abode. Greet the morning sipping coffee on the rocking chairs or reading on the cushioned porch swing. Inside, guests will find an airy living, dining, and kitchen area with a dramatic beamed ceiling, wide-plank hardwood floors, and paneled walls. Wicker accents and luxurious finishes elevate the professionally decorated interior into a home that guests won’t want to leave. There’s room for the whole group to relax in the living space, furnished with a comfortable chaise sectional, plush armchair, and 70” TV.
The wood dining table seats 6 for meals, with space for 4 more at the nearby breakfast bar. The open kitchen was designed for effortless cooking and hosting, with marble countertops, high-end appliances, and a wine fridge. At the end of the hall, a door opens to a shaded outdoor patio with Adirondack chairs, a gas grill, string lights, and plenty of room for kids to play! When evening falls, retire to the well-appointed primary bedroom featuring an upholstered king bed, a 50” TV, and a private bathroom. Additionally, there is a guest bedroom with 1 queen bed and a guest bedroom with 2 full beds—both with TVs for late-night entertainment. All bedrooms have comfortable and updated bedding.

Clearwater Views offers a sophisticated and convenient Seacrest Beach escape. Exploring Scenic 30A’s beautiful communities and attractions is easy, with parking for 2 vehicles and 4 complimentary adult bikes. Beach access is located at The Villas at Sunset Beach—a short 11-minute walk away. Book this home for a beach escape today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 8):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: 2 Full Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*This home in NOT pet friendly.

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and Callista by 360 Blue fines.

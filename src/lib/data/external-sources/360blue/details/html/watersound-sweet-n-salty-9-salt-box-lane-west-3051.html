Watersound "Sweet n' Salty" 9 Salt Box Lane West
Watersound, Florida
Bedrooms 4
Beds 4
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gated Community with on-site Security
- LSV-Low Speed Vehicle
- 4 adult bikes
- Located next to Puttering Park
- Access to neighborhood pool and fitness center
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach service set (2 beach chairs and 1 umbrella)
- Private Deeded Beach Access
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Nestled in the charming WaterSound community, Sweet n' Salty offers 2,410 square feet of beautifully designed coastal living space with 4 bedrooms and 3.5 bathrooms, sleeping 8. Situated within a gated, low-speed-vehicle-friendly neighborhood, this 2-story retreat boasts incredible amenities, including easy access to Puttering Park next door, a sparkling swimming pool, a fitness center, and designated beach access—imagine serene beach days with fewer crowds! Set amidst winding boardwalks and picturesque dunes, this home perfectly blends relaxation and refinement for a memorable getaway!

Step inside the charming first-floor great room, where a bright, airy space with tall ceilings and abundant natural light welcomes friends and family. Bamboo reed blinds, white slip-covered sofas, and natural wood accents bring a laid-back coastal charm to the space, while the fireplace with a TV overhead adds a warm and inviting touch—perfect for sitting back, relaxing, and lingering awhile. Beyond the living area, a 3-stool breakfast bar, great for quick bites and post-beach snacks, seamlessly connects to the inviting dining space that comfortably seats 8. The chef’s kitchen boasts high-end appliances, sleek granite countertops, and a meal prep-friendly island. For added convenience, use the nearby laundry room and dedicated workspace. Spend sunny days outside on the fully-covered wraparound porch, recharging on the plush outdoor furniture with a cool breeze from the overhead ceiling fans. Gather around the 4-chair bar-top table, perfect for sipping drinks and sharing laughter, while the BBQ grill cooks up the day's fresh catch. After a picturesque beach day, unwind in the first-floor primary bedroom, boasting a king bed, walk-in closet, and private bathroom with a soaking tub and a glass shower.

Upstairs, an open second-floor loft provides an additional living space, perfect for family movie nights or the ultimate kids hangout, complete with a TV and couch. Guests can choose from 3 beautiful guest bedrooms—2 bedrooms with queen beds with a shared bathroom, and 1 bedroom with a king-sized bed and private bathroom. Take in stunning views from the second-floor balcony, where wooded landscapes and vibrant coastal skies provide an unforgettable backdrop.

With dedicated beach access and proximity to 30A communities like Rosemary Beach, Seaside, and WaterColor, Sweet n' Salty promises an unforgettable retreat! Featuring 3 parking spaces, a low-speed vehicle, and 4 adult bikes, exploring nearby spots and enjoying live music at The Big Chill is a breeze. Book now!

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*This property comes with 3 designated parking spaces. This home permits a maximum of 3 vehicles through the community security gate.

*Please note that the Low Speed Vehicle provided is NOT street legal and can only stay in the Watersound community.

*Weddings or parties/groups of people or guest of guest are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and management fines.

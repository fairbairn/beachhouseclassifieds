WaterColor "Fins Up" 1640 E Co Hwy 30A #101
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 2
Beds 3
Sleeps 6
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
*Per Watercolor HOA, please include all guests aged 5 and older in your total count of adult guests to receive amenity and pool access wristbands. Sleep arrangements are provided as a courtesy and are not considered in the occupancy count.
**This home is allowed 6 amenity access bands.**

- Located on the south side of 30A, steps from the beach.
- Fully equipped gourmet kitchen
- Two porches with outdoor living space
- Washer/Dryer
- Sonos ARC Sound System
- Smart TVs in Every Room
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Fins Up is a 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom condo in the Gulf district of WaterColor with brand new furnishings, artwork, and a new Smart TV with a SONOS bluetooth sound system. This corner unit has a screened porch and a balcony with beautiful wooded views and is on the south side of 30A—mere steps from the Beach Club, shops, and beach access.

Guests will love this adorable beach condo's colorful artwork and honey-colored pine floors. A primary bedroom on the first floor has a king bed with commanding views of scrub oak and pine trees from floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking a private screened porch. This peaceful outdoor porch has Adirondack chairs and a wicker sofa and opens to a gravel pathway. The bedroom’s spacious private bathroom has separate vanities, a soaking tub, and a glass-enclosed shower.

A guest bedroom and an open kitchen, living, and dining area are on the second floor. The guest bedroom has a queen bed and a shared bathroom with a shower. The kitchen and living area are surrounded by windows with beautiful views of the area. This light and bright space has plenty of room for everyone to gather and relax after a fun day. Decorated in blues and off-white, the living area has an amazing sofa and armchairs surrounding a 65” Smart TV and a beautiful gallery of vintage black and white "Old Florida" photographs from the 40's. A dining table seats 6 and is next to a door that opens to a spacious balcony. Guests can unwind, listen to birds chatter, or dine at an outdoor wicker table. In the kitchen, granite countertops and GE Monogram appliances provide lots of workspace for hungry guests. And a raised island bar offers additional seating for 3.

Guests can easily explore WaterColor on 2 included adult bikes, but with so much within a short walk from Fins Up, they might not want to! Fins Up is sure to book quickly with this quiet location in WaterColor’s most in-demand area. Book your next beach vacation today!

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
-Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 12 wristbands.
- Located in WaterColor's Lake District
- 8-minute bike ride to the beach
- 150 yards to the famous Frog Pool
- 5-minute walk to Camp Watercolor
- Fully-equipped gourmet kitchen
- Living room fireplace
- Expansive porches
- Propane grill
- 2 dedicated parking spots with additional parking throughout community
- 4 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Serenity and sun-drenched fun await at 147 Scrub Oak Circle, a 4-bedroom, 4-bathroom home that sleeps up to 12 guests in WaterColor’s Lake District. Enjoy prime access to the community’s resort-style amenities—the famous Frog Pool is 150 yards away, Camp WaterColor is a short 5-minute stroll, and the WaterColor Beach Club and gorgeous Gulf are only an 8-minute bike ride. When not swimming in the community pools or relaxing on the sugar-white beach, guests will love enjoying the comfortable back porch’s nature views.

Well-landscaped grounds and an inviting front porch hint at the peaceful getaway to come as guests arrive at this 3-level home. Enter through the side door into a convenient mudroom/laundry room, designed for easily dropping off beach bags or refreshing swimwear. Continue into the great room, where dark hardwood floors accentuate the high ceilings and sprawling layout. Savor leisurely mornings preparing brunch in the fully equipped gourmet kitchen, featuring a Wolf gas stove with a built-in griddle perfect for making pancakes. The formal wooden dining table seats 10, with extra seating available at the 4-stool breakfast bar. A spacious, slip-covered sectional, TV, and Blu-ray player in the living space invite cozy nights unwinding as a group. Along the back wall, French doors lead onto the comfortably furnished screened-in rear porch overlooking the walking trails that border Western Lake. Start the morning sipping coffee in the coastal air while savoring the view—and keep an eye out for local wildlife, including roaming deer! For a restful haven, the primary bedroom offers a king bed and a private bathroom.

Venture to the second level for additional sleeping accommodations, including a spacious guest bedroom with a king bed and a furnished balcony, as well as a guest bedroom with a queen bed and a bunk room with a twin-over-queen bunk—all feature a private bathroom.

Up the spiral staircase is the third-floor kids’ loft, boasting 3 twin beds and a TV.

Experience a charming, carefree WaterColor vacation at 147 Scrub Oak Circle. There’s parking for 2 vehicles in the driveway for easy exploration, or take advantage of the 4 complimentary adult bikes. For beach access, choose between the WaterColor Beach Club or the access located on Goldenrod Circle past the WaterColor Inn. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 12):

- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Queen Bunk Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

THIRD FLOOR
- Additional Kids Large Loft: 3 Twin Beds

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

Please note: Effective March 15, 2014, Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be made through The Electric Cart Company and will be limited to 80 vehicles throughout the community at any given time. Only one cart may be rented per home, and rentals will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis.

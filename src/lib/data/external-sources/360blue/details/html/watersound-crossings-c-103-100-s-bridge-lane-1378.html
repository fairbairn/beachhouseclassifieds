WaterSound Crossings C-103 - 100 S Bridge Lane
Watersound, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 5
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Private Deeded Beach Access
- Located in a secure, gated community with on-site security
- Great views of the Gulf and natural sand dunes
- Flat-screen TVs in every bedroom
- Multiple balconies with Gulf views
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach -service set (2 beach chairs and 1umbrella)
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout
- This property does not come with dedicated parking spaces, however 1 vehicle can enter the community for the opportunity to park in the first come first serve parking. Please note first come first serve parking is limited and not guaranteed. This condo permits a maximum of 1 vehicle through the community security gate.

*Beginning in March 2025 and continuing through 2026, the WaterSound Crossings condominiums will undergo an extensive exterior refurbishment project. During this period, residents and guests should expect significant construction activity, including the boarding of windows and restricted access to balconies in affected buildings. These measures are necessary to ensure safety and facilitate the successful completion of the work.

DETAILS: Discover a slice of paradise at WaterSound Crossings 103C, a beachfront 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom condo that sleeps 8. Explore the nearby communities of Scenic 30A, relish in the slow pace of life, and enjoy WaterSound neighborhood amenities, including stunning boardwalks and community pools. Guests will love the enchanting Gulf views and multiple balconies of this condo—all a quick stroll from the sparkling waters of the Emerald Coast.

Coastal patterns and colors weave throughout this 2-story condo to form a relaxing beach retreat. Guests enter on the bottom floor, where the primary bedroom with a king bed, private bath, TV, and Gulf-front balcony is located. A twin-over-twin bunk bed in the hall offers additional sleeping accommodations. The laundry room on this level means guests won’t need to worry about damp beach towels!

Upstairs, natural light shines through the back wall of windows in the living and kitchen areas, showcasing the gorgeous Gulf views. After a day of exploring, curl up on the 2 couches and watch TV with the family. Cook up some sea-inspired cuisine in the kitchen, featuring high-end appliances and seating for 3 at the breakfast bar. As the group eats at the 8-person dining table, open the glass doors to let in a refreshing breeze! A nightcap on the furnished balcony adds the final touch to a worry-free day. As evening falls, retire to either of the 2 guest bedrooms with a king bed, private bathroom, and a TV.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (Sleeps 8)
FIRST FLOOR:
Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower and Tub
Hallway Bunks: Twin-over-Twin Bunk

SECOND FLOOR:
Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower/Tub Combo
Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower Only

IMPORTANT INFORMATION
*HOA regulations do not allow for guest Low-Speed Vehicle rentals. Additionally, bringing your own Low-Speed Vehicle onto the property is prohibited.

*This is a peaceful community. To ensure everyone's enjoyment, weddings, parties, gatherings exceeding a reasonable number of guests, and guests of guests are strictly prohibited. Violation of this policy may result in immediate removal from the property, forfeiture of any remaining rental fees, and potential fines from the HOA, community, and 360 Blue.

* To ensure a comfortable stay for all guests, pets are not permitted on the property. Please note that any damage caused by pets will be the guest's responsibility.

*The garage is not for guest use.

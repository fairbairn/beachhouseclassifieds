WaterColor "Beachfront #21" 1848 E. Co. Hwy 30A
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 6
Sleeps 10

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 10 wristbands.
- Gulf-front
- Access to separate private condo pool
- Dune walkover is at your front door
- Steps away from the beach club
- Balcony amazing views of the emerald waters
- Seating for 6 at the table and 4 at the bar
- High-speed wireless internet
- Stereo and TV's with DVD players
- Open parking directly in front of the building under the trellis
- 6 Adult Bikes Provided
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Located in the coveted beachside Gulf District of WaterColor, this 2-story, 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom condo is in one of the best locations in town. Convenience is all around when you stay at Beachside #21 as it is ideally located between the beach access and the private Beachside Pool.

Natural light welcomes you into this beautifully finished open floor plan, highlighting the hardwood floors and stunning views. The primary bedroom can be found on the first floor, featuring a plush king bed and bunk bed, direct porch access, and an en suite with jetted tub and stand up shower. This suite also includes a quaint sitting area. The second bedroom on this floor also includes a king bed and bunk bed with a private bathroom.

On the upper level, you will find the spacious kitchen, which is equipped with stainless steel appliances and is overlooking the open dining room and living room. Two sets of french doors open to ocean views. There is a half-bath off the shared living space, along with a twin over twin bunk nook. The second-floor bedroom includes a plush king bed and private bath.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS:
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Bunk Bed, Sitting area, Private Bathroom with Shower and Separate Soaking Tub
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Bunk Bed with Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower Only
- Twin-over-Twin Bunk Nook
- Half Bathroom

Disclaimer: All Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be arranged through The Electric Cart Company. Only one cart is allowed per home, and rentals are on a first-come, first-served basis, with a limit of 60 vehicles allowed in the community at any time. Additionally, WaterColor condominium buildings do not have exterior outlets for charging Low-Speed Vehicles/Low-Speed Vehicles (LSVs). 360 Blue does not provide extension cords for charging. Please discuss charging needs directly with your rental company.

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments, and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

Grayton Beach "Grayt Dreams" and "Graytness" 158 Grayton Village Road
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 22
Beds 24
Sleeps 64
Full Baths 18
Half Baths 2
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- 2 Private pools *Pools can be heated for an additional fee of $35/day from Oct.1 -Mar. 1
- 2- 6 Passenger Low-Speed Vehicles
- 2 Elevators
- 2 Game rooms
- Wedding & Large Group Event-friendly
- 22 adult bikes
- Short walk to the Grayton Central Lot complimentary beach shuttle
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Life’s truly sublime at Grayt Dreams and Graytness—2 exquisite Grayton Beach homes offering a luxurious base to relax in 22 total bedrooms and 18.5 bathrooms, sleeping 64. Relish fresh seafood from the local eateries, hike the winding paths of Grayton Beach State Park, discover the beauty of the Underwater Museum of Art, or relax on the beach—only a 6-minute drive away. Boasting 2 private pools, a sitting spa, and dual outdoor kitchens, guests have everything they need for an unforgettable coastal experience.

Designed with leisure in mind, the first-level game room in each home features a pool table and 75” Smart TV. In both backyards, a pool and surrounding sun loungers encourage idle days in coastal bliss. Savor barbecue dinners on the screened-in patio, boasting a natural gas grill and beverage cooler, as well as options for alfresco dining at the outdoor tables. Stacked washers and dryers, along with in-home elevators, make handling luggage and laundry a breeze.

Natural light illuminates the second-level living spaces of each retreat, where guests will find a plush sectional and 75” TV, as well as similarly outfitted dens—plenty of space for games, movie nights, and breakout activities. Group meals are simple thanks 2 sets of refrigerators, ovens, and dishwashers in the kitchens of both homes. Vast dining tables are available in both, each with seating for 26 to 28 people, perfect for laughter-filled group meals.

Hosting up to 64 guests—perfect for corporate events, family reunions, bachelorette parties, weddings, and more—these 2 properties offer 22 total bedrooms and 18.5 bathrooms, with a mix of comfortable king beds and full-over-full bunks.

Luxuriate in relaxation and recreation at Graytness and Grayt Dreams for a charming 30A getaway. Exploring is easy with parking for 12 vehicles—plus 22 complimentary adult bikes and multiple 6-passenger low-speed vehicles. Take advantage of the free "Grayton Central"

parking and shuttle service to the beach—only 2 blocks away. For the nearest beach access, Grayton Dunes Beach is a quick 6-minute drive. Book today!

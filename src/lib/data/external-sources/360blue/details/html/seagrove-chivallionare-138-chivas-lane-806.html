DETAILS: Experience the beauty of Chivallionare, a stunning Gulf-view Main House and Guest House in the heart of Seagrove Beach! Accommodating 36 guests across 9 bedrooms and 8.5 bathrooms, this luxurious 4-story retreat spans 5,926 square feet with breathtaking Emerald Coast views. Large groups and families will love the expansive outdoor space and unbeatable location—steps from the beach!

Designed for making memories, guests can unwind on the pool’s splash deck or sitting spa, surrounded by loungers. An outdoor kitchen with a beverage cooler and grill sets the stage for sunset patio dinners, while plush furniture invites relaxation. Keep the excitement alive in the first-floor game room featuring air hockey, a pool table, and a large sectional for movie nights. Late-night fun abounds in the 2 bunk rooms—each with 1 twin-over-twin bed, 2 queen-over-queen beds, and a private bathroom.

In-home elevator doors open to the second-floor great room, showcasing elegant wood floors and glass French doors. Plan the day’s activities from 2 stylish sofas or matching armchairs in the living area. A wet bar with a wine chiller and ice maker enhances dinner at the 12-person dining table, while the 9-stool breakfast bar suits quick bites. Host a personal chef or cook together in the gourmet kitchen with high-end appliances, then enjoy alfresco dining or relaxation on the off-kitchen balcony boasting a sectional. Retire to 2 guest bedrooms, one with a private bathroom, each with king beds and balconies.

Relax in the third-floor primary bedroom featuring a king bed, private soaking tub, and a spacious private deck with ample seating and Gulf views. A bunk room with 2 full-over-full beds and 2 guest bedrooms with king beds—all with balcony access!

A spiral staircase leads to the fourth-floor rooftop deck, offering unparalleled views. The theater room includes a 75” TV, sectional couch, and nearby wet bar—plus a Peloton!

The Guest House features a kitchen, sitting area, queen sleeper sofa, balcony, private bedroom, bathroom, and a washer-dryer.

With 6 parking spots, 9 bikes, and a low speed vehicle, Chivallionare makes exploring easy. Enjoy beach access via Chivas Lane’s private boardwalk or the Walton Dunes Public Beach on Beachfront Trail. Book now!

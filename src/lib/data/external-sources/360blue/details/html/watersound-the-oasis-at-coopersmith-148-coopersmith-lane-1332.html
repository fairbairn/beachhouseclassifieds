Watersound, FL

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gated Community with on-site Security
- Amazing views of the coastal dunes and Gulf
- 3 minutes to nearest beach boardwalk
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach service set (2 beach chairs and 1umbrella)
- Private Deeded Beach Access
- Private heated pool **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for an additional $30/day fee
- Fully equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances
- 4th-floor lookout tower
- Covered porches and screened balcony
- Multiple living areas
- Gas grill
- 5 adult bikes
- Golf cart
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

This property comes with 3 designated parking spaces. This home permits a maximum of 4 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: Discover the ultimate beach escape at The Oasis at Coopersmith, a breathtaking WaterSound home bordering the protected Deer Lake State Park, offering unmatched nature views and privacy. With 4 spacious floors—including a kid’s loft—this 5-bedroom, 5-bathroom retreat sleeps 12 and is perfect for multi-generational getaways, large group beach trips, and even a girl’s weekend. Enjoy the largest private saltwater pool in the WaterSound rental collection, Gulf views from each floor, a 4th-floor lookout tower—all only 3 minutes from dedicated beach access.

A signature front porch lined with 2 swinging daybeds welcomes guests. Inside, a spacious sitting area offers a cool retreat after a sun-soaked day at the beach. The first-floor primary bedroom features a king bed, private bathroom with a clawfoot tub, and a porch overlooking the pool and nature preserve. Two guest bedrooms offer a king and queen bed, respectively, plus private bathrooms. There’s also a laundry room. At the heart of the property sits a heated saltwater pool, surrounded by lush landscaping, with a nearby gas grill for effortless poolside dinners—it’s the soul of The Oasis.

The second floor of this home is organized around a large open kitchen that flows seamlessly into the great room. Perfect for entertaining, it features a dual commercial-sized refrigerator and freezer, premium appliances, and a 4-stool breakfast bar. Dine at the 10-person table, savor an alfresco meal for 8 on the back porch, and unwind with a cocktail on the kitchen balcony. The great room was designed for gathering with plush furnishings and an airy layout—a place where the whole family comes together at the end of a beach day. Families will love the second-floor primary bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom, and porch, because the adjoining guest bedroom with 2 twin beds keeps kids close by.

The third-floor loft belongs to the kids, with 2 queen beds, a spacious sectional, 65" TV, and foosball table. Here, they can entertain themselves for hours while adults relax downstairs.

At the very top, the fourth-floor viewing tower captures sweeping Gulf and rare coastal dune views—ideal for sunrises and sunsets!

The Oasis at Coopersmith is where families gather, friends reunite, and 30A memories are made. With 3 parking spaces, a low-speed vehicle, and 5 bikes, exploring is effortless. Enjoy nearby amenities like The Beach Club, community pools, and The Big Chill, plus private deeded beach access leading to iconic dune boardwalks and one of 30A's most pristine stretches of sugar-white sand! Book now!

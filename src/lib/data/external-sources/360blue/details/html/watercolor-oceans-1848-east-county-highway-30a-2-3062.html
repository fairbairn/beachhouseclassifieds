WaterColor "Oceans" 1848 East County Highway 30A #2
Santa Rosa Beach, Florida
Bedrooms 3
Beds 5
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
*Per WaterColor HOA, please include all guests aged 5 and older in your total count of adult guests to receive amenity and pool access wristbands. Sleep arrangements are provided as a courtesy and are not considered in the occupancy count.
**This home is allowed 8 amenity access bands.**

- Gulf Views
- Beach Access
- Pool Views & Exclusive Access
- Located in the Gulf district of WaterColor
- 3 Adult Bikes (4 Max Per HOA)
- 2 Parking spaces
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, a second pool deck with additional lounge seating, a third pool with zero-entry, and an enhanced WaterColor Grill and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf.

DETAILS: Welcome to Oceans, a remarkable 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom coastal retreat located in the picturesque Gulf district of WaterColor. With 2,180 square feet of living space, this luxurious home comfortably sleeps 8 guests. Only steps away from breathtaking Gulf views and serene poolside lounging, Oceans offers easy access to WaterColor Inn, the Beach Club, and exclusive community amenities. Spend hot days relaxing at the neighborhood pools or soaking in the beauty of the Gulf from nearby beach access!

Step inside the bright and welcoming entryway, where a cozy bench beckons guests to unload beach bags, slip off shoes, and stay awhile. Unwind in the first-floor guest bedroom with a king bed, TV, and private bathroom, or retreat to the luxurious primary bedroom featuring a king bed, TV, private bathroom with a soaking tub, and a private covered deck with loungers for enjoying the warm Florida sun. The laundry room adds convenience.

The second floor of this 2-story getaway serves as the ultimate gathering space for friends and family with impressive Gulf views framed by glass French doors and incredible natural light. Sink into the 2 plush sofas and 2 matching armchairs in the sun-soaked living area—ideal for a laughter-filled movie night or a post-beach nap. The adjoining dining area features a round table that seats 7 for sit-down meals, while the nearby breakfast bar seats 4 more for quick bites. Whip up ornate family-style dinners in the gourmet kitchen, complete with high-end appliances, ample cabinetry, and elegant white countertops. When it’s time to retire for the night, the lovely second-floor guest bedroom offers a cozy retreat with a comfortable king bed and a private bathroom with a shower. For the little adventurers, an open nook near the living area provides additional sleeping options with a built-in twin-over-twin bunk bed. Experience the serene beauty of the Emerald Coast from the spacious deck, thoughtfully furnished with bar-top tables and chairs. Bask in the gentle glow of the morning sun while sipping coffee or take in the fiery hues of a coastal sunset while savoring a refreshing cocktail!

Oceans blends coastal luxury with astounding Gulf views for the ultimate getaway! With on-site parking for 2 vehicles—plus 3 bikes—it’s easy to explore nearby Seaside and other

Scenic 30A communities. For beach access, head to WaterColor Inn, located on Goldenrod Circle, or visit the WaterColor Beach Club. Book now!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 8):
FIRST FLOOR:
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate and Private Deck
Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower only

SECOND FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only-
Additional Sleeping Area: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed in Living Room Nook

*Disclaimer: All Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be arranged through The Electric Cart Company. Only one cart is allowed per home. 360 Blue does not provide extension cords for charging. Please discuss charging needs directly with your rental company.

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property, forfeit any rental payments, and be subject to HOA, community, and management fines.

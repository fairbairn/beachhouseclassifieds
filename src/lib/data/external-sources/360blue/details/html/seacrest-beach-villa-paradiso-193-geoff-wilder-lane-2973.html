Seacrest Beach "Villa Paradiso" 193 Geoff Wilder Lane
Rosemary Beach, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 9
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Located in a gated community on the south side of 30A, just 100 yards from the community private beach and directly next door to Rosemary Beach
- Private deeded beach
- Private heated pool *Pool heating available October 1-May 1 for $35 per day*
- 3 adult bikes
- Extended stay
- Complimentary beach setups each morning include 2 beach chairs with 1 umbrella (Mar 1-Nov 30)
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

*Spring Break 2026- Water's Edge community will have a 7pm curfew for unaccompanied minors under age 18 without parental supervision from March 1st through April 19, 2026.

DETAILS: Experience all the charm of Seacrest Beach with a peaceful retreat at Villa Paradiso, a 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home that sleeps 10. This laid-back community offers beautiful beaches and a family-friendly town center packed with tempting restaurants and local boutiques. Guests will love savoring the sunshine beside the home’s private pool, biking to next-door Rosemary Beach for a scoop of ice cream at The Sugar Shak, or strolling to the beach—only 100 yards away!

The first level of this 3-story coastal home welcomes guests with an open kitchen, living space, and dining area—all smoothly connected for effortless togetherness. The chef of the group will appreciate the kitchen’s high-end appliances, granite countertops, and spacious island, while the shiplap detailing creates a pleasant, coastal setting for preparing meals. A few steps away, the large dining table has plenty of bench seating, perfect for seafood feasts after a day spent on the beach. Recline on the living area’s 2 sofas and 2 armchairs to unwind and watch TV in the cool AC. Outside, a furnished porch overlooks the enclosed pool and surrounding palm trees. Sip sangria while enjoying the overhead fan’s sweet breeze or amble down to the poolside to stretch out on the comfortable loungers. Rest in luxury in the primary bedroom, which boasts a plush king bed and a private bathroom.

Take the stairs to the second level, where an additional primary bedroom with an inviting king bed, private bathroom, and balcony offers a tranquil haven. Kids and extra guests can take advantage of the delightful bunk room, featuring 2 full-over-full bunk beds and a private nook with 1 twin-over-twin bunk bed. A shared balcony is accessed off the hallway—start each morning with a moment relishing the fresh, seaside air.

The top-floor living space encourages guests to spread out and relax, with soft seating, a TV, and a sunlit porch for admiring the neighborhood and tantalizing Gulf views.

With a private pool, Gulf views, and walkable beach access, Villa Paradiso has everything guests need for a charming, unforgettable Seacrest Beach getaway. On-site parking and 3 complimentary adult bikes make exploring easy, though there’s no need to drive with community beach access right off Geoff Wilder Lane—simply follow the road or the walking path behind the home. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Two Full-over-Full Bunk Beds and Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed in Private Nook, Private Bathroom With Shower/Tub Combo

THIRD FLOOR:
- Additional Living Area: Loft and Porch with Gulf Views

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

*The Water's Edge Board of Directors has instituted a curfew for everyone 18 years old and under of 8:00 PM effective immediately!
This curfew will follow the same guidelines put forth by Rosemary Beach and they consist of:

1. Curfew for anyone under the age of 18 not accompanied by their parents. They will be asked to return to their rental property or leave Water's Edge if they are not guests.
Refusal may result in criminal charges.
Security will be on premises and will be verifying they are guests of Water's Edge.

2. No backpacks, coolers, bags, etc., capable of concealing alcohol or other illegal contraband will be allowed.

3. Dry beach (no alcohol) 8:00 PM to 6:00 AM.
This curfew will extend for the duration of Fall Breaks and may extend past that time at the direction of the Board of Directors, depending on compliance and the safety of the community.

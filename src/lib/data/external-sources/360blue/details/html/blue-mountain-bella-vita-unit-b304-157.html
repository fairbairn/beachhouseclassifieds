Blue Mountain Bella Vita Unit B304
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 4
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf views
- Walking distance from the beach
- Overlooks 350 feet of private deeded beach
- 2 dedicated parking spots
- 2 Gulf front pools
- Gated community
- Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Bella Vita Unit B304 sleeps 8 in an elegant 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom condo with breathtaking views of
the Gulf from Blue Mountain Beach’s highest elevation point. Renowned for its views, Bella Vita’s gated
community boasts 2 Gulf-front pools and private beach access.

Arched doorways, a muted color palette, hardwood floors, and floor-to-ceiling views greet guests. The open kitchen has double ovens, premium appliances, and an island with seating for 4. A wet bar is
brilliantly positioned between the kitchen and the living area, enabling guests to fix a cocktail or use the wine chiller before stepping through the sliding glass doors onto the balcony for sunset. Tucked behind
the living area is a dining table with seating for 6 and entry to the 3 bedrooms. A primary bedroom has a king bed, built-in bookshelves, and floor-to-ceiling views from the sliding glass doors that open onto a
private balcony. The spacious private bathroom has a soaking tub, separate shower, and beautiful artwork. A guest bedroom has a king bed and a private bathroom with a shower/tub combo. A second
guest bedroom offers guests 2 queen beds and a private bathroom with a shower/tub combo.

Guests will appreciate the convenience of 2 Gulf-front pools, dedicated beach access, and the ability to walk to restaurants and shops in Blue Mountain Beach. Book a breathtaking Blue Mountain Beach vacation in Unit B304 today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 8):
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Soaking Tub
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Two queen Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*Please note: This is a year around 7-day rental requirement.

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community, and Callista Vacation Rentals fines.

Seacrest Beach "Boho Rose" 401 Clareon Drive
Watersound, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-119752
Bedrooms 5
Beds 8
Sleeps 14
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Deeded beach access
- Private saltwater pool and sitting spa*Pool heating is available from October 1st through May 1st for an additional fee of $50 per day.
- 5 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Sun-kissed days of free-spirited fun await at Boho Rose, a beautiful 5-bedroom, 5.5-bathroom Seacrest Beach home that sleeps 14 guests. Play in the Gulf’s emerald-green waters, explore the local shops and restaurants, hit up a golf course, or go for a hike—the options are endless! With a heated saltwater pool and beach access only 10 minutes away, guests are sure to fall in love.

The charming front patio’s carved sofa, piled with a mix of comfy pillows and cushions, is the first nod to this 2-story home’s artistic and colorful interior. Head inside to the airy great room, where brightly hued furniture and artwork create a stunning contrast against the distressed oak wood floors and shiplap walls. The chef’s kitchen is well-equipped with Thermador appliances and an island that doubles as a 4-person breakfast bar. Mix up mimosas at the wet bar to the side and enjoy a lively brunch at the 8-seat dining table. In the living area, 2 barrel chairs and a fashionable velvet sofa invite guests to sit back and stream a movie on the 85” Smart TV. Glass sliding doors on the back wall lead to the pool deck with a chic seating area, 4-seat alfresco table, and grill set beneath overhead fans for ultimate comfort. After a day at the beach, easily rinse off sand and salt in the outdoor shower. Guests can retire for the night to the delightfully decorated primary bedroom featuring a queen bed and private bathroom.

Upstairs, discover a casual living space with a sleeper sofa, 65” Smart TV, and balcony. A convenient laundry room means guests can feel free to pack light. This level boasts 1 primary bedroom and 2 guest bedrooms, each with a king bed, a private bathroom, and unique décor. Additionally, there’s a kid-friendly bunk room with 2 twin-over-twin beds.

Experience a vibrant WaterSound vacation at Boho Rose. To explore, take advantage of on-site parking for 4 vehicles—plus 5 complimentary adult bikes. For the nearest beach access, walk down Clareon Drive, turn left on East County Highway 30A, and the access will be across the street from Seacrest Drive. Book this cheerful Emerald Coast home today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 14):

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Bunk Room: 2 Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Loft Space: Queen Sleeper Sofa

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger.

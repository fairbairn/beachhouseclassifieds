This property comes with 2 designated parking spaces. This home permits a maximum of 2 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: Nestled within the private gated community of WaterSound Beach, 290 Salt Box is a haven of relaxation and coastal elegance for up to 10 guests. With 4 cozy bedrooms, 4 refreshing bathrooms, and beautifully designed coastal chic decor, this home is perfect for indulging in the 30A lifestyle. With beach access only 500 yards away, a short stroll down one of the picturesque boardwalks leads to the breathtaking white sandy beaches of the Emerald Coast.

The first floor is the heart of this charming 2-story beach cottage, boasting an impressive open living space that blends the kitchen, living, and dining areas together on hardwood floors. Cozy up in the living area beside the gas fireplace during unseasonal chilly nights and enjoy the 65" TV for movie time. With high-end appliances, designer cabinetry, and pristine white countertops, this well-appointed kitchen is a foodie's dream. The dining area provides ample space for entertaining with 8 seats, while the breakfast bar offers 3 more. Retreat to luxurious comfort in the guest bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom, and a laundry room down the hall. Host the ultimate outdoor gathering with a state-of-the-art electric Traeger Pellet grill and smoker before sitting at the 6-person alfresco dining table with another 4-person table around the corner.

With various sleeping arrangements—including 2 guest bedrooms with 1 king bed and 1 queen bed as well as 1 bunk room with a full-over-full bed—the second floor makes everyone feel at home.

Experience beach living at its finest in 290 Salt Box! With fabulous amenities, including 4 complimentary adult bikes and a 4-passenger golf cart, this home is the ultimate spot for creating lasting memories. Book this incredible home today!

*Please Note:
*Access to the WaterSound Beach Club is not included
*WaterSound does not allow golf cart rentals from outside vendors

*Weddings or parties/groups of people or guest of guest are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

*The golf cart provided is not street-legal and can only be used inside of Watersound gates.

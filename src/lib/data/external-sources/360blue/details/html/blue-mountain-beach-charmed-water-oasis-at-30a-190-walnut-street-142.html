Blue Mountain Beach "Charmed Water Oasis at 30a" 190 Walnut Street
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 4
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Private pool *Pool heating is available from October 1st through May 1st for an additional fee of $40 per day.
- 4 adult bikes
- 6 Seater Low-Speed Vehicle *(Available only from March1, 2026 to October 31, 2026)
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Escape to Charmed Water Oasis at 30A! This beautiful 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom Blue Mountain Beach home sleeps 8 guests and boasts a spacious outdoor area—complete with a private pool! Enjoy days paddling across the magnificent coastal dune lakes, browsing the local artists’ galleries, relaxing on the beach, and indulging in delicious cold treats at Blue Mountain Beach Creamery.

A shady front porch with 2 cushioned chairs welcomes guests to this lovely 2-level home. Step inside to find a bright interior of pale wood and natural fiber accents. In the great room, stylish armchairs and a coastal-inspired, slip-covered couch offer a peaceful environment for casual gatherings over cocktails and plenty of comfy seating for movie nights. The chef of the group will appreciate the kitchen’s high-end appliances and open layout with a 3-seat breakfast bar—easily entertain while cooking meals or making snacks. To the side, a contemporary dining table offers seating for 6. Or, guests can head to the screened-in porch furnished with a 6-seat dining set for alfresco dining. The porch sitting area with a TV and overhead fan is perfect for a carefree afternoon. Continue into the fenced-in backyard, where a wicker patio set beneath a wide umbrella invites guests to unwind in the open air. Nearby, the pool beckons for a refreshing swim, and there is a grill and 4-person table for juicy burgers and hotdogs. When the day ends, retire to the primary bedroom with a king bed and private bathroom on this level. There is a washer and dryer for convenience.

The second-level landing hosts a delightful living space with soft seating and a TV, alongside 3 well-appointed guest bedrooms—each featuring a king bed and soothing neutral décor.

With a private pool and inviting outdoor spaces, Charmed Water Oasis at 30A has everything guests need for a memorable Blue Mountain Beach getaway! Exploring is easy with on-site parking for 3-4 vehicles and 4 complimentary adult bikes. For beach access, Gulfview Heights Beach Access is only a 3-minute drive or 4-minute bike ride away. Book today!

- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger.

**Please note that in order to utilize the golf cart that comes with the home, you will be required to pay a $30 non-refundable deposit to 30a Gulf Carts.

Seagrove Beach "Seaclusion Dunes" 425 Beachfront Trail
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-093792
Bedrooms 9
Beds 20
Sleeps 34
Full Baths 9
Half Baths 3
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Steps from private beach access
- Private pool and spa. Pool and sitting spa are heated together. **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for $50 per day**
- Roof bar/party room with big screen TV, ping pong table, Bluetooth stereo speaker, seating for 22, Gulf and lake views, an additional bathroom, and mini fridge
- Private Gated Community
- Shuffleboard
- 8 adult bikes provided
- 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 7 dedicated parking spots
- Elevator
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Topping 9,500 square feet with 9 bedrooms, 9 full bathrooms, and 3 half baths, Seaclusion Dunes has enough space to accommodate up to 34 people! Relax on the stunning sugary beaches or explore the local shops and restaurants that Seagrove Beach offers. Guests will love staying steps from the boardwalk and sunning by the private pool!

With much of the first-floor space composed of garage space and an entry floor, the true gem of the level is a luxurious bunk room with 3 twin-over-queen beds, an arcade game, private bathroom, and sitting area with a sleeper sofa.

The second floor lends itself to a large foyer area and gallery opening to a game room with a shuffleboard table and 75” Roku TV. There are 4 bedrooms—3 with queen beds and 1 with 3 twin-over-queen bunk beds. A desirable amenity is the French doors leading out to the elevated pool. Complete with vast deck space, it's easy to lose track of time lounging the day away!

While there is plenty of room for guests to spread out, it's no surprise that the third-floor open living room and gourmet kitchen are the heart of the home, bringing everyone back together. The living and dining areas are perfect for entertaining as they open to a beautiful covered porch with additional dining spaces and a grill. The third floor also includes 2 guest bedrooms with a king bed and 1 guest bedroom with 2 full beds.

The fourth floor has a primary bedroom with a king bed, private balcony, and views of both the Gulf and lake.

Seaclusion Dunes is full of amenities like Gulf and lake views, easy beach access, expansive porches, a private pool, and parking for 5 vehicles, making this one of the most desirable homes for a Seagrove Beach stay. Easily explore the area with a complimentary 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle and 8 adult bikes. There are 2 nearby beach accesses. The private beach access is the closest and is across the street between homes 410 and 426 Beachfront Trail, and the second is Walton Dunes Beach Access, about a 3-minute walk from the property. Book this fantastic home today!

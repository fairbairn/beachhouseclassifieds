WaterColor 135 Buttercup Street
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 6
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
*Per Watercolor HOA, please include all guests aged 5 and older in your total count of adult guests to receive amenity and pool access wristbands. Sleep arrangements are provided as a courtesy and are not considered in the occupancy count.
**This home is allowed 12 amenity access bands.**

- Located in the WaterColor Park District
- Outdoor Fireplace
- 4 adult bikes provided
- 6 passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 2 dedicated parking spots
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: 135 Buttercup is a wonderful home in a fantastic neighborhood. This inviting 4 bedroom, 4.5 bath home in WaterColor is sure to help you unwind. With its comfortable furnishings and fabulous location, this will be your perfect vacation retreat. You will be welcomed by the warm tones, high ceilings, hardwood floors, and plenty of cottage charm in the great room. You will be able to enjoy meals from the wonderful kitchen, with its high-end appliances, granite countertops, and stunning cabinetry. The peaceful living room, dining room, and splendid kitchen are all included in an open floor plan for everyone to enjoy after a fun-filled day at the beach. On the first floor, the primary bedroom has a king bed, flatscreen TV, and access to a screened porch. There is a connecting bathroom with double vanity, a tub, and a shower with an amazing rain shower head. You will also find a guest bedroom with a king bed with a connecting bathroom with a single vanity and a shower. The second floor has a second primary bedroom with a king bed and a connecting bathroom with double vanity, a shower/tub combination. Lastly, there is a bunk room that has 1 bunk and 2 full beds, which makes it great for the kids! This bedroom has a connecting bathroom with a single vanity and a shower/tub combo. This house has ample indoor and outdoor space, making for a truly great gathering spot for everyone to spend time together.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS:
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom One: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom Two: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Bunk Room: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed and Two Full Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Additional Living Space: Twin with Twin Trundle

Parties, weddings, or events held at the property must be pre-approved in writing by 360 Blue. Fees apply. Contact us for additional details.

Please note: Effective March 15, 2014, golf cart rentals in WaterColor must be made through The Electric Cart Company and will be limited to 80 vehicles throughout the community at any given time. Only one cart may be rented per home, and rentals will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis.

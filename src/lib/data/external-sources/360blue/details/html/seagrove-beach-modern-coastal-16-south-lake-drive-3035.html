DETAILS: Modern Coastal blends lake and coastal luxury in the heart of Seagrove Beach—only a 7-minute stroll from the beach! This exquisite Emerald Coast property includes a Main House and Carriage House, offering 6 spacious bedrooms with king beds and 6.5 bathrooms, accommodating 12 guests. Unwind and refresh in the private backyard’s sparkling private pool, paddleboard the rare dune lake, and bike along the Timpoochee Trail for an incredible vacation!

Enter the bright and airy Main House, where an inviting entryway wall lined with bench seating and handy hooks, accompanied by a wet bar—toast to vacation mode! Warm sunlight beams onto the shiplap walls and plush furnishings punctuated with pops of blue, creating a cozy space for evenings of laughter, card games, or family movie nights. A spacious galley-style kitchen, with white marble countertops and gold accents, features an eat-in island seating 5 and high-end appliances. Between the living and kitchen areas lies an open dining space with a wood-beamed ceiling and paper crane light fixture, opening to a cozy deck aglow with twinkling lights. Around the corner, a private pool deck awaits with sun loungers, a BBQ grill, and an alfresco dining table for outdoor fun. Tiled with a deeply romantic French phrase meaning 'yours forever,' the shower room is perfect for rinsing off after the pool or beach and flows into the laundry room for added convenience.

A sunlit landing on the second floor offers a designated workspace and small gym with a Peloton bike, flanked by 2 welcoming guest bedrooms with private bathrooms and balconies.

Peaceful accommodations on the third floor include a spacious primary bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom with a soaking tub, and scenic balcony, along with a lovely guest bedroom with a king bed and private bathroom. The 2-story Carriage House features a pool-facing entrance on the first floor, which includes a guest bedroom with a king bed and a private bathroom. The unconnected second floor, accessible via a side entrance, features a fully equipped kitchen with a sit-in

island, a cozy living area with a queen sleeper sofa, and a delightful guest bedroom with a king bed and shared bathroom.

Modern Coastal is the perfect lake-beach retreat! With on-site parking for 6 vehicles and 6 complimentary bikes—plus a 7-minute walk to Eastern Lake or Ramsgate Beach Access—adventure and relaxation await. Book now!

DETAILS: Casa Blanco redefines lakefront luxury! Hosting up to 18 guests, this 6-bedroom, 6.5-bathroom Seagrove Beach retreat is breathtaking, featuring stunning Gulf views and a sophisticated blend of modern and Mediterranean charm. Featuring a heated infinity pool, sitting spa, and wine bar, it’s the perfect lavish escape for large groups. Picture poolside elegance, peacefully paddleboarding Eastern Lake, or swimming in the beautiful Emerald Coast—only 3 minutes away!

Step inside this exquisite 3-story abode, where a stately great room with vaulted ceilings and seamless glass walls provides panoramic views of the shimmering lake. The living area, adorned with plush seating and a Smart TV, flows effortlessly into the nearby wine bar and dining room, seating 12 for formal meals. Cook and create in the gourmet kitchen, boasting high-end appliances and a spacious island with 5 stools—plus an elegant prep kitchen for additional space. Down the hall is a media room with comfy seating for a family movie night, as well as the first-floor primary bedroom, which boasts a king bed, a private bathroom with a spa-like tub, and a fireplace. Outside, the private waterfront terrace offers plush seating, an alfresco dining table, and a BBQ grill—only steps away from the private pool with a sun deck and loungers. A private dock provides easy access to the lake.

Upstairs, friends can hang out in the bunk room with a king bed, twin-over-twin bunk, and private bathroom, or recharge in the luxurious primary bedroom, featuring a king bed, private bathroom, and balcony access with great views! A Peloton treadmill-equipped gym and a private office add convenience to the second floor, alongside the laundry room.

The cozy third-floor living space includes a kitchenette, comfy seating, and a TV, as well as access to the top-floor balcony boasting 2 porch swings and incredible waterfront views. Guests can choose from 3 inviting accommodations—2 spacious guest bedrooms with king beds and private bathrooms, or 1 bunk room with 2 twin-over-full beds.

Casa Blanco delivers the ultimate Seagrove Beach experience, perfect for beach and lake adventures! With parking for 6 vehicles, a 6-passenger low-speed vehicle, and 6 adult bikes, exploring is a breeze. Enjoy the 2 provided Yolo paddleboards for water activities or visit local eateries like The Big Chill and The Red Bar. Beach access is only a 3-minute stroll to Sugar Dunes Beach and Port Property Beach. Book this elegant home today!

SECOND FLOOR:
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed and Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate

THIRD FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
-Bunk Room: Two Twin-over-Full Bunkbeds, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

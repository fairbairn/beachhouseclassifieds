Santa Rosa Beach, FL

- Event Friendly

- Private balconies with scenic views
- Nearby dining and shopping
- 13 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: For Shore, a luxurious home in the exclusive Blue Mountain Beach community, is 6 minutes from the beach with Emerald Coast views. Spanning over 5,000 square feet, this spacious retreat sleeps up to 30 guests with 13 beautifully-appointed bedrooms and 10.5 bathrooms. Ideal for retreats, weddings, and other special events, For Shore offers a luxurious setting with a lovely pool and outdoor area for unforgettable gatherings.

This 4-story coastal haven features a spacious outdoor entertainment area with a pristine green turf backyard—a versatile space that can be easily rearranged to suit any event. Barbeque on the natural gas grill, dine alfresco at the 6-person table, watch the kids splash in the pool, or sunbathe on the 4 loungers. Retire to the first-floor guest bedroom with a king bed and private bathroom—or hang out in the climate-controlled garage that doubles as a game room with a billiards table, foosball table, and TV. Beyond the laundry room with 3 washer and dryers, the in-home elevator takes guests to any of the four floors.

The main floor is the heart of the home—soft sunlight illuminates the spacious great room. A grandiose table seats 30 guests and turns any dinner party into an unforgettable celebration. The kitchen was designed for large parties, offering abundant cabinetry, counter space, and high-end appliances, including 2 refrigerators and 2 microwaves. The living area has 2 spacious sectionals and an impressive 86" TV, and the theater room's 75" TV and 7-person couch are great for casual lounging and movie nights.

The third floor, designated for sleeping, features a primary bedroom with a king bed, private bathroom, and balcony access, alongside a bunk room with a full-over-full bed and 4 guest bedrooms—2 of these bedrooms have private bathrooms, 3 have king beds, and the last has a queen bed.

The fourth-floor bedrooms mirror the third-floor layout, boasting a primary bedroom, 4 guest bedrooms, and a bunk room. All bedrooms throughout the home have TVs.

For Shore is the ultimate paradise for large gatherings along the Emerald Coast and offers on-site parking for 6 vehicles, 13 adult bikes, and a 6-passenger golf cart. Book this unforgettable home today!

-Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 16 wristbands.
- Private pool and sitting spa. Pool & spa are heated together. **Pool heating is available for this property from October 1st through May 1st for $70 per day**
- Complimentary Peloton Bike- an Alaya exclusive amenity
- 6 adult bikes
- Fabulous outdoor space
- Spacious third-floor living area
- Elevator
- Two primary suites
- Impeccably furnished
- 3 dedicated parking spots.
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Savor an elevated 30A getaway at A Coastal Indulgence. Tucked within coveted WaterColor, this stunning 6-bedroom, 6-bathroom home comfortably accommodates 16 guests. Revel in WaterColor’s resort-style amenities, including the famed beach club and family-friendly Camp WaterColor. Boasting an idyllic outdoor space and with the sugar-soft beach only a 5-minute bike ride away, this exceptional property is a perfect escape for a peaceful, pampered vacation.

Coastal elegance and rustic elements combine throughout this sprawling 3-story home, creating an enchanting setting for letting worries slip away—whether relaxing with a movie in the impeccably furnished living space, or gathering for an intimate dinner at the 8-person dining table. Culinary-inclined guests will love the gourmet kitchen’s Thermador appliances and dual dishwashers for breezy cleanup. French doors lead outside, where a picturesque oasis awaits. Enjoy succulent barbecue dinners at the outdoor kitchen and 6-person patio table. The private pool beckons for a mid-day refresh, or enjoy a tasty cocktail at the bar, complete with 3 stools and a mounted TV. A high-top table to the side provides extra seating for 3. When the sun disappears beneath the horizon, delight in the ambiance of the outdoor fireplace while enjoying a late-night soak in the sitting spa. Retreat for a restful night in the luxurious primary bedroom or the lovely guest bedroom; each features a king bed, and the primary bedroom includes a private bathroom.

The convenient in-home elevator makes it easy for guests to ascend to the second level, where a lavish primary bedroom and 2 beautiful guest bedrooms invite relaxation—each with a soft king bed, private bathroom, and access to a furnished porch.

The third-level living space encourages indoor fun with a Foosball table, TV, Xbox, wet bar, and a queen bed. The neighboring bunk room has plenty of sleeping accommodations for additional guests with 1 queen bed, 2 twin beds, and 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds.

A Coastal Indulgence has everything guests need for a deluxe 30A vacation, including parking for 3 vehicles and 6 complimentary adult bikes. For beach access, visit WaterColor Beach Club or use the access point on Goldenrod Circle next to WaterColor Inn. Book today!

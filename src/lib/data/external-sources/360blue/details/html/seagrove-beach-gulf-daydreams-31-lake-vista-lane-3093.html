Seagrove Beach "Gulf Daydreams" 31 Lake Vista Lane
Santa Rosa Beach, Florida
Bedrooms 4
Beds 5
Sleeps 9
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf views
- Elevator
- 4th Floor terrace
- Deeded beach access directly across the street
- Private pool
- Walkable distance to The Greenway Station- Shopping and Food
- 4 adult bikes
- Outdoor shower
- Complete Clean Linen Participant- ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Escape to Gulf Daydreams in Seagrove Beach, a 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom home sleeping 9, located directly across the street from beach access! Indulge in idyllic afternoons sunbathing by the private pool, lounging on the fourth-floor terrace, or taking in 30A’s turquoise waves and white sands from the complimentary 4-person beach setup—including chairs, an umbrella, and cooler. Savor mouthwatering seafood at local restaurants or find souvenirs at beach boutiques, both only a short stroll or bike ride down the Timpoochee Trail.

This 3-story property welcomes guests on the first level with a bright interior, a lovely guest bedroom with a queen bed and private bathroom, and an elevator to easily transport luggage and groceries. Outside, the crystal-clear pool and soft loungers beckon, surrounded by a privacy fence. A shaded patio with comfy seating, a fan, and TV creates the perfect place to gather, while the BBQ grill elevates outdoor cooking. The outdoor shower adds convenience.

Pale wood floors and crisp white shiplap walls stretch throughout the second-floor great room, beautifully outfitted with elegant décor and refined furnishings. A deep wraparound sofa and 2 pillow-soft accent chairs provide plenty of seating for popcorn-fueled movie nights and cozy gatherings. After a day of beach fun, catch up around the hefty wooden dining table with 2 long benches over a delicious meal. The expansive kitchen is well equipped for culinary creations, featuring KitchenAid appliances and roomy 2-stool island. Brew a cup of coffee to enjoy on the balcony, where a comfy porch swing awaits. At bedtime, rest easy in the guest bedroom with a king bed, TV, and private bathroom.

On the third level, the primary bedroom boasts a king bed, lavish private bathroom, and a covered, furnished balcony with peek-a-boo Gulf views. Down the hall is a laundry closet and a charming bunk room with a twin-over-full bed and a private bathroom.

On the fourth floor, discover the home’s gem—a Gulf view balcony with a 2-person high-top table that’s perfect for toasting the sunset!

With a private pool, easy beach access, and 4 complimentary adult bikes, Gulf Daydreams offers the ultimate leisurely Seagrove Beach experience! Though driving isn’t necessary from this walkabout location, on-site parking is available. To reach the beach, use the designated access at the end of Sea Turtle Drive—or check out nearby public access at Santa Clara Regional and One Seagrove Place. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (SLEEPS 9):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

THIRD FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Soaking Tub separate with access to Private Balcony
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Full Bunk Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

FOURTH FLOOR:
-Balcony with Gulf Views

*The garage is not for guest use.

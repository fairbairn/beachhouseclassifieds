Seacrest Beach - Tranquillity Sea La Vie - 8600 E. County Highway 30A 240
Seacrest Beach, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 3
Sleeps 6
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Beach front
- Amazing gulf views
- Professionally decorated
- Community Pool heated from October to April
- Balcony
- 3 Adult bikes
- Guests have access to the owners' beach chair closet
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Tranquillity Sea La Vie is a fantastic 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom Seacrest Beach condo that sleeps 6. White shiplap walls, floor-to-ceiling Gulf views, pale floors, and luxurious finishes create a 30A coastal retreat like no other. Guests will love the
beach views, large community pool, dedicated dune walkover, and gated condo access.

Stepping inside Tranquillity Sea La Vie, guests will find two comfortable guest bedrooms with king
beds off the entryway. A short hallway leads to incredible Gulf views that may be enjoyed from the open living, dining, and kitchen area. Guests will find everything they need to prepare meals
in a beautiful chef’s kitchen with white quartz countertops and a waterfall counter that seats 4.
A sofa and comfy armchair face a flat-screen TV and a cute electric fireplace in the living area.
Opposite this is a dining table with Gulf views through exquisitely large windows and sliding
glass doors that stretch across the living area’s wall and open to an expansive Gulf-front balcony. A
small table and chairs on the patio is a lovely place to begin the day, watching dolphins play or enjoying the sunrise. A primary bedroom off the living area enjoys Gulf views from large windows and a sliding glass door that opens to the balcony. Guests can relax in a spacious room with a king bed and an attached private bathroom with a glass-enclosed shower.

Guests can easily explore Seacrest Beach and nearby Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach on 3 included adult bikes. Tranquillity Sea La Vie is a gorgeous Gulf-front condo that will book quickly.
Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 6):
FIRST FLOOR:
Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Hallway Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the Property, forfeit any rental payments, and be subject to HOA, community, and Callista Vacation Rentals fines.

*No Pets are Allowed on the Property per the HOA rules.

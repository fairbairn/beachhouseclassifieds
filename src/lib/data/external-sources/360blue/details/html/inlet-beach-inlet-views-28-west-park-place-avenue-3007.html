Inlet Beach "Inlet Views" 28 West Park Place Avenue
Inlet Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-115952
Bedrooms 6
Beds 9
Sleeps 14
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gorgeous gulf views
- Rooftop hot tub
- Short walk to the beach
- Elevator
- 5 adult bikes
- 2 Living areas
- 2 Mini bars and wine fridge
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Luxuriate in a relaxing coastal escape at Inlet Views, a 6-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom home that sleeps up to 14 guests. Enjoy snorkel adventures around Grouper Reef, and afternoons lounging on the beach—only a short 4-minute stroll away. There are plenty of dining options and activities nearby, thanks to neighboring Rosemary Beach and Camp Helen State Park. With gorgeous Gulf views from the rooftop deck, an indulgent sitting spa, and multiple living spaces, this 4-level home is perfect for groups and families looking for a peaceful Inlet Beach vacation.

A lavish setting blending contemporary flair and coastal adornments, welcomes guests upon arrival. The first level features a collection of charming sleeping accommodations, including 1 bunk room with a twin-over-twin bunk bed and 2 lovely guest bedrooms, each with a king bed—1 boasts a furnished deck.

Ascend via stairs or the stylish glass elevator to the second level, where 2 well-appointed primary bedrooms encourage unwinding with inviting king beds and private bathrooms made for pampering.

On the third level, discover the elegant great room, infused with warm natural light from the vast picture windows and accordion glass doors, which open to Juliette balconies for indoor-outdoor immersion. Sink into the soft sofa and comfy accent chairs for laid-back mingling or watching TV. The adjacent chef’s kitchen puts snacks within easy reach and offers a beautiful setting with marble countertops and high-end appliances. Cook homemade meals to share at the 8-person dining table, with additional seating available at the 3-stool breakfast bar. Past the kitchen is a wet bar and a guest bedroom with a twin-over-twin bunk bed.

The group fun continues on the top level, where a spacious secondary living area hosts a plush sectional sleeper sofa, upholstered armchairs, an entertainment center, and a wet
bar. The spacious deck is the home’s indulgent outdoor space, complete with views of the turquoise Gulf, a sitting spa, grill, and ample seating.

Inlet Views has everything guests need for a delightful 30A vacation, including 5 complimentary adult bikes and on-site parking. For the quickest beach access, use Phillips Inlet Beach Access at the end of South Walton Lakeshore Drive. Book today!

- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate

THIRD FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Bunk Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

FOURTH FLOOR:
- Additional Living Area: Deck, Sleeper Sofa, and Half Bathroom

WaterColor "The Sea Section" 81 Needlerush Drive
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 6
Beds 12
Sleeps 12

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 12 wristbands.
- 1 Block from The Frog Swimming Pool
- 6 adult bikes provided
- 6 passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- Wet Bar
- Game Room
- Cornhole
- Park Views
- Outdoor Fireplace
- Outdoor Living Area
- Xbox
- EV charger
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: 81 Needlerush is located in the sought after Lake District in WaterColor, with its lush greenery and old Florida charm, this location will give you a relaxing vacation! This spectacular 6 bedroom, 5.5 bath house is sure to delight! With its comfortable furnishings and ample outdoor space, this is a fantastic vacation retreat! When entering the home, you will be welcomed by the great room, with its hardwood floors, high ceilings and tones of turquoise throughout. The kitchen has high end appliances, quartzite counter tops, stunning cabinetry and large island. This kitchen has all of the items needed to prepare that delicious meal, including a wet bar to help you unwind! The living room, dining room and kitchen, make up this open floor plan for everyone to enjoy!

Also on this floor, you will find one of the primary king bedrooms with a connecting spa-like bathroom with a double vanity, soaking tub and a glass shower. The first floor also includes the laundry room and half bath. On the second floor, you will see the second primary king bedroom including a connecting bathroom with a double vanity, soaking tub and glass shower. There is a swing bed on the balcony for you to enjoy the wonderful weather. There are also two more king bedrooms, both having connecting bathrooms with single vanities and showers. Lastly on this floor, there is a Bunk Room with 2 sets of bunk beds.

Last, but certainly not least, there is a third floor Bunk Room and living space! In addition to the single vanity and shower, you will also find a large sectional couch pointed toward the large flat screen TV equipped with DVD player and gaming systems. You will know exactly where to look if you cannot find the kids! This home also includes large porches, outdoor shower and grill. You and your family can also enjoy an evening out on the back porch around the fireplace, grilling, watching TV, and playing Cornhole. This incredible WaterColor home is simply stunning and gives you that luxurious feel, the serenity you have been craving and it is all here at one of the most beautiful beaches in the world!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS:
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom One: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Soaking Tub And Shower

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom Two: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
- Bunk Room: Two Twin over Twin Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

THIRD FLOOR:
- Bunk Room: Two Twin over Twin Bunk Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to fines.

Blue Mountain "SeaTime" 160 Seaward Drive
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 5
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf Views
- Steps away from the beach access
- Gated community
- Private heated pool **Pool heating available Oct 1–May 1 for $25 per day**
- Nearby dining and shopping
- Stainless steel kitchen appliances
- 3 adult bikes provided
- Complimentary beach service includes: 4 beach chairs and 2 umbrellas - March 1 - October 31st
- Complete Clean Linen Participant—ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Guests will love SeaTime’s location in Blue Mountain Beach, right at the edge of Dune Allen Beach. This 3–bedroom, 3–bathroom home sleeps 10 and has Gulf views, dedicated beach access, and a private pool. Guests can walk across 30A to enjoy a craft beer, get an ice cream cone, eat local seafood, or shop at Gulf Place.

This comfortable, no–fuss home has an open living, dining, and kitchen area. Slipcovered furnishings, wood floors, and a jute rug are beach–sand friendly and provide a comfy spot to relax after a day of jumping waves. KitchenAid appliances and the kitchen’s marble countertops offer ample space to prepare meals. A guest bedroom with a twin–over–full bunk bed and twin trundle share a bathroom with shower with the living area. A back door opens to a wood deck with an outdoor shower and a large pool and pool deck. Enjoy warm Gulf breezes and the sounds of crashing waves while relaxing by the pool.

On the second floor are 2 bedrooms. A primary bedroom has a king bed and a private bathroom with a shower. French doors open to a private porch with Gulf views. Listen to the wind rustle the palm trees and watch the sunset over the Gulf without ever leaving your room! A guest bedroom has a king bed and private bathroom with shower.

Get ready to enjoy the views from the third floor! A day bed and wet bar are surrounded by windows revealing the deep blue of the Gulf. Step out onto a balcony, toast the sunset, or enjoy a meal under the stars at a high-top table with 4 chairs. The views of the Gulf are fantastic and overlook the dunes at Ed Walline Park.

Guests will enjoy exploring the 30A bike path on 3 included adult bikes. With SeaTime’s location and pool, this home will book quickly. Book today!


SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Full Bunk Bed with Twin Trundle, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

THIRD FLOOR:
- Loft: Twin Daybed with Twin Trundle

NEARBY ATTRACTIONS:
- Yoga Studio & Gym (Steps Away)
- Gulf Place Restaurants and Shopping (Steps Away)
- Santa Rosa Country Club and Golf Course and The Vue Restaurant (One Mile)
- Longleaf Greenway Western Bike Trail (Steps Away)

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property, forfeit any rental payments, and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

*Unfortunately no pets allowed in this home at any time.

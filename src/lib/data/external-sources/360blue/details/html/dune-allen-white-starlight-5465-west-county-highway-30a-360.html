Dune Allen "White Starlight" 5465 West County Highway 30A
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-066190
Bedrooms 5
Beds 8
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 2
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf front
- 2 Beach Chairs and Umbrella
- Balconies and Gulf front patios
- Hot tub
- Elevator
- Smart TVs
- Fireplace
- Firepit
- EV charger
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Boundless views and unforgettable beach days await at White Starlight, a Gulf-front Dune Allen home that sleeps up to 12 guests with 5 bedrooms, 5 full bathrooms, and 2 half baths. Guests will love soaking in the beachside sitting spa, sipping chilled cocktails on the deck, and enjoying the 50 feet of beachfront!

The ground-level covered patio of this 3-level home is a carefree coastal haven. Indulge in a movie night in the fresh breeze, gathered on the patio sectional while the firepit merrily burns, or savor the peace of an ocean-side soak in the sitting spa. For refreshments, the outdoor kitchen is well-equipped.

Ascend to the beautifully designed second-level great room, where 2 walls of floor-to-ceiling windows showcase breathtaking Gulf panoramas. In the living space, 3 stylish armchairs and a wraparound sofa provide comfortable seating around the TV and coffee table. The gourmet kitchen promises culinary ease, with a Monogram 6-burner stove, double oven, and a spacious 7-stool breakfast bar. There’s a 6-person table for dining inside, or guests can eat alfresco on the balcony’s table for 6 while taking in views of the water. This floor hosts 1 guest bathroom with a king bed and private balcony—all the bedrooms feature a private bathroom. For convenience, there is a laundry room with dual washers and dryers.

The amazing primary bedroom is on the top floor, set beneath a cathedral ceiling and boasting an enchanting Gulf view with a soft king bed, a kitchenette with a pod coffee machine, and a private furnished balcony—perfect for savoring a morning beverage or a yoga flow in the ocean air. Additionally, there are 2 guest bedrooms with king beds and 1 bunk room with 2 twin-over-twin beds. The first guest bedroom has a private balcony overlooking the coast, and the second bedroom shares a balcony facing Oyster Dune Lake with the bunk room.

Enjoy a lavish and laid-back escape at White Starlight in Dune Allen. With parking for 4 vehicles and beach access off the lower-level porch, it’s easy to enjoy all the charm of Scenic 30A. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 12):
SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate (Wheelchair accessible)

THIRD FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate, Large gulf front balcony
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: 2 Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*The garage is available for guest use and can accommodate one vehicle. Please note that it can only be accessed from inside the house.

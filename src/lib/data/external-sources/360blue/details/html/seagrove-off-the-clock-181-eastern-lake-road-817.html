Seagrove "Off The Clock" 181 Eastern Lake Road
Santa Rosa Beach, FL

DETAILS: Set in gorgeous Seagrove Beach and sleeping up to 38 guests, Off The Clock boasts 9 bedrooms and 8 bathrooms. Natural beauty, family-friendly fun, and quintessential beach town charm await in this eastern 30A town. With a private pool and sitting spa—plus proximity to the beach—guests are sure to fall in love.

Inside and outside entertainment mix in this delightful beach haven. On the first floor, a relaxed living space with a pool table and 55” TV connects to the private backyard. The covered outdoor kitchen with a dining table for 8 begs for an afternoon of barbecue, while the zero-entry swimming pool and sitting spa tempt guests to bask in the sun. Sleeping accommodations include 2 bunk rooms with 2 queen-over-queen beds and 1 full-over-full.

White shiplap walls and a wood ceiling create a coastal cottage feel in the open great room on the second floor. Elegant, naval blue couches and 2 plush accent chairs continue the theme, providing guests a comfortable gathering spot. The kitchen hosts premium appliances and a marble-top island that seats 7. A contemporary dining table made of pale wood offers room for 8. The adjacent porch has a mini living area, TV, and dining table. The 2 guest bedrooms with king beds have access to a second porch with pool views.

On the third floor, the elevator opens onto a charming sitting area with a TV. Down the hall, discover the primary bedroom with a king bed, Gulf views, and access to a shaded porch with a spiral staircase to the fourth-floor lookout tower. There are 2 guest bedrooms with king beds and a bunk room with 2 twin-over-twin beds.

The attached Carriage House has a kitchenette, cozy living area with a sleeper sofa, and bedroom with a king bed.

Off the Clock’s luxurious finishes and private outdoor space make for the perfect Seagrove Beach getaway. Parking for 6 vehicles, 9 complimentary bikes, and a 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle make exploration easy. The closest beach access is a 7-minute walk between 471 and 493 Eastern Lake Road. Book this beach home for an unforgettable vacation today!

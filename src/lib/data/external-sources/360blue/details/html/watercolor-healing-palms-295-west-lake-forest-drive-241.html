WaterColor "Healing Palms" 295 West Lake Forest Drive
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-042754
Bedrooms 4
Beds 7
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 12 wristbands.
- Steps from Camp Watercolor
- Short bike ride to the beach
- Spacious porches with dining space
- Carriage house
- 4 adult bikes
- 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 2-car garage
- 4 dedicated parking spots with additional parking found throughout the community
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Additional details coming soon!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 12):
FIRST FLOOR:
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub

SECOND FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom
-Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
-Bunk Area: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed

CARRIAGE HOUSE:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
-Living Area: Queen Sleeper

*Weddings are not allowed at this home.

*This home is pet-friendly with an additional non-refundable pet fee of $400, plus tax.

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger.

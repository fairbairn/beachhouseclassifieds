- Neighbors Deer Lake State Park
- 6 Seater Low-Speed Vehicle
- Community pool
- Newly renovated
- Grill

- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

DETAILS: Welcome to La Maison Bleue, a recently renovated 5-bedroom, 5.5-bathroom Seagrove Beach house that sleeps 14. Located in the laidback Seagrove Beach neighborhood, this 3-story home is less than a 10-minute walk from Walton Dunes Public Beach access. Guests will love all the amenities of the area and the home— a shared community pool, a 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle, and nearby Eastern Lake boat ramp.

Guests are welcomed to this spacious home by a classic front porch with gorgeous pillars. Inside, the open layout includes elegant vaulted ceilings, wood-like tile, and large picture windows. The gourmet kitchen boasts quartz countertops and top-of-the-line Sub-Zero and Thermador appliances—plus a 5-stool breakfast bar/kitchen island. An adjoining formal dining room seats 6. Friends and family will love the living area, which is decorated in subtle nods to the beach, like 2 coral-colored armchairs and matching accent pillows on the wraparound sectional couch. There is also 1 guest bedroom located on this floor.

On the second floor are 3 inviting guest bedrooms that are perfectly spaced, allowing everyone privacy while still being connected. The modern yet conventional design will make guests feel cozy—just like home!

On the third floor, a bonus room has additional living space and 2 bunk beds—perfect for game nights. This home also features 2 laundry rooms and a grill.

Guests can explore 30A on 5 included adult bikes. With these excellent amenities in such a prime location, La Maison Bleue will book fast! Book this fantastic property today.

THIRD FLOOR:
Bunk Room: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
Additional Living area: Full-over-Full Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

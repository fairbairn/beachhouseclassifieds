Seacrest Beach "On The Beach" 8028 E County Hwy 30A
Seacrest Beach, FL
Bedrooms 6
Beds 11
Sleeps 26
Full Baths 7
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gulf-front with private beach access
- Private heated pool and sitting spa **Pool & spa heating available October 1-May 1 for $50 per day**
- Complimentary beach setups at the waterline each morning include 8 beach chairs with 4 umbrellas (Mar 1-Oct 31)
- SONOS System
- 6 adult bikes
- Game Room
- 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 7 dedicated parking spots
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Located in beautiful Seacrest Beach, this gorgeous beachfront home, "On the Beach," offers sweeping panoramic views of the Gulf from its multiple wrap-around balconies. The ample indoor, outdoor space is perfect for entertaining your large crowd!

The first floor houses a game room complete with TVs, arcade games, and more. Off the game room are the first floor bedrooms. Make your way upstairs to the second floor for the luxurious open-concept living and dining area, including the fully-stocked, top-of-the-line kitchen. The third floor boasts an impressive 3 king primary suites and stunning views from Panama City all the way to Destin!

Take advantage of the private beach walkover from the house and have your toes in the sand from the second you arrive! When you're ready to cool off, take a dip in the private pool and make sure you take advantage of the included bikes to explore nearby shopping and dining in Seacrest and neighboring Rosemary Beach and Aly's Beach. Check availability and book your luxurious vacation at "On The Beach" today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 26):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen-over-Queen Bunk, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
- Living Area: Queen-over-Queen and Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Two Queen Beds with Queen sleeper sofa, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

THIRD FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed with additional Queen Sleeper Sofa, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

Parties, weddings or events held at the property must be pre-approved in writing by 360 Blue. Fees apply. Contact us for additional details.

Pets are not allowed in this home. Failure to adhere to this rule may result in immediate eviction and loss of deposit. Guests are fully responsible for all damages.

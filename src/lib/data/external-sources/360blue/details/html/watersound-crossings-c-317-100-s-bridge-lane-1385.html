WaterSound Crossings C-317 - 100 S Bridge Lane
Watersound, FL
Bedrooms 3
Beds 4
Sleeps 7
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Gated Community with on-site Security
- Great views of the Gulf and natural sand dunes
- Fantastic porch spaces
- Pool and beach are just yards away
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach -service set (2 beach chairs and 1umbrella)
- Private deeded beach access
- Free WiFi
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

*The property does not come with dedicated parking spaces, however, 2 vehicles can enter the community for the opportunity to park in the first come first serve parking. Please note first come first serve parking is limited and not guaranteed. This condo permits a maximum of 2 vehicles through the community security gate.

*Beginning in March 2025 and continuing through 2026, the WaterSound Crossings condominiums will undergo an extensive exterior refurbishment project. During this period, residents and guests should expect significant construction activity, including the boarding of windows and restricted access to balconies in affected buildings. These measures are necessary to ensure safety and facilitate the successful completion of the work.

LOCATION: This home is just a few steps from the beach access.

DETAILS: Newly remodeled kitchen with beautiful marble countertops, white cabinets and appliances. Freshly painted with all new bedding and carpets. Calming natural colors provide the backdrop for a vacation to remember in this third-floor condominium with accommodations for eight in the Crossings District. The spacious living area allows for comfortable mingling with friends and family with a beautiful view of the Gulf of Mexico. Enjoy the open floor plan that flows easily from the kitchen, furnished with Viking stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and wraparound breakfast bar with seating for three, to the dining area with a breakfast table for four and dining table for six. Additional features include a laundry room with a full-size washer and dryer and a half bath is conveniently located off the living area. The master has fabulous gulf views and is furnished with a king bed. In the spa-like master bath, guests can relax in the large tub, or separate walk-in shower. The second bedroom has a queen bed that its crisp colors reflect the beach view from the bedroom windows. Plenty of bedding exists in the third bedroom as there is a twin Bed and another twin bed with a trundle. Both the second and third bedrooms come complete with private baths.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 7):
- Master Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower and Tub Separate
- Guest Bedroom: Queen, Private Bathroom With Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Twin Bed and Twin Bed with Trundle, Private Bathroom With Shower/Tub Combo

AMENITIES INCLUDE: Community Pool, Gulf Views, and 1 parking spot. Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout.

360 BLUE STAFF & SERVICES: Exclusive Guest Experiences, Concierge Services, Grocery Delivery, Private Chef & More (Extra costs & advanced notice may be required)

*Weddings or parties/groups of people or guest of guest are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

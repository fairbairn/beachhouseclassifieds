WaterColor "The Cottage on Scrub Oak" 235 Scrub Oak Circle
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 5
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
-Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 10 wristbands.
- 150 yards to the Famous Frog Pool
- 5 award-winning WaterColor community pools and tennis courts
- Private heated pool with sitting spa! **Pool heating Oct 1-May 1 for $45 per day** The spa and pool are heated together
- High-speed wireless internet
- Sonos sound system
- TVs in all the bedrooms
- Fully-equipped gourmet kitchen
- Propane grill
- 4 adult bikes
- 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- 2 dedicated parking spots with additional parking throughout community
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, a second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and a new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: "The Cottage on Scrub Oak" is just what discerning guests are looking for in a beach vacation with a beautifully furnished 4-bedroom/4-bath home on a charming, quiet street with nature rather than neighbors behind the home. This home is convenient to all Watercolor amenities and the beach, especially with a 6-seater Low-Speed Vehicle and 4 adult bicycles!

Off the front porch, you're just steps from the kids' favorite - the "Frog Pool." Out the back door, you'll find peaceful nature trails. A short walk or bike ride to the left will take you to the tennis center and path into Seaside. Take a right out the back of the home, and you'll be heading towards the Boathouse on Western Lake for fishing, YOLO, or the pool, then on to the Beachclub pool and beach. Kids of all ages absolutely love riding in a Low-Speed Vehicle - a fun outing to Camp Watercolor or maybe pizza for dinner? And easy for the grocery store!

Back at The Cottage, relax on either of the 2 large screened-in porches overlooking the beautiful private pool and the nature area beyond the back gate. Settle into comfy couches by the beautiful shell fireplace in the open concept main living area. A gourmet kitchen with gas cooktop and huge dining table to seat 8 plus 4 barstools at the counter. Gorgeous, unique walnut hardwood floors. An oversized laundry room and TVs/WiFi in every bedroom. All of this plus little Mom touches like books, board games, and puzzles. All you need to do is bring the family ready for fun and relaxation.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 10):
First Floor:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower

Second Floor:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower
- Guest Bedroom: Room: Two Full Beds with Twin Trundles, Private Bathroom with Shower/ Tub Combo
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property, forfeit any rental payments, and be subject to fines.

Unfortunately, this home does not allow pets at any time.

WaterColor "Heart's Desire" 467 East Royal Fern
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 6
Sleeps 14
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
-Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 14 wristbands.
- 5 adult bikes provided
- 6 Seater Low-Speed Vehicle
- Private Heated Pool & Spa. Pool and sitting spa are heated together. **Pool heating is available for this property from October 1st through May 1st for $25 per day**
- Wrap around porch
- Carriage house with full kitchen
- This property comes with 6 dedicated parking spots. Additional parking can be found throughout the community.
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the WaterColor Beach Club. It features a main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well as an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Spacious, cool and luxurious, "Heart's Desire" is designed to evoke the quintessential Seaside vacation. Located in WaterColor's Crossing District, this picture-perfect cottage sleeps 14, with two primary bedrooms - one up and one down - with private baths in the main house, and two upstairs double bedrooms sharing a Jack and Jill bath. The private carriage house adds a fifth bedroom hideaway with its own kitchen, living room and bath. Professionally decorated, the open floor plan of "Heart's Desire" welcomes you with a fully-equipped gourmet kitchen flowing into the dining and living room that leads you to a wraparound porch overlooking the heated pool and spa . At the top of the stairs is a landing with a pull-out sofa and TV, a perfect nook for kids or an afternoon nap. Enjoy sunbathing by the pool by day and stargazing at night while you soak in the spa. Relax with an early morning cup of coffee on the screened porch while watching the outdoor TV or have a a cool drink on the front porch letting time stand still. A 6-seater golf cart is available for your use at Heart's Desire, convenient and fun for quick trips and easy parking on jaunts to the beach or downtown restaurants. Or just zip over to the nearby Publix for your last minute grocery needs. This elegant and comfortable retreat is the perfect place to bring family and friends and create the Seaside memories of your own heart's desire.

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 14):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/ Tub Combo

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/ Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/ Tub Combo
- Landing: Queen Sleeper Sofa

Carriage House:
- Queen Bed and Queen Sleeper Sofa, Private Bathroom with Shower/ Tub Combo


Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community and 360 Blue fines.


*Unfortunately, this home does not allow pets at any time.

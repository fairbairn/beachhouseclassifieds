- Private heated pool **Pool heating is available for this property from October 1 through May 1 for $60 per day**

- Complete Clean Linen Participant—ALL linens, including comforters laundered upon every checkout.

DETAILS: Prepare to “Seas the Day” when you book your stay at 524 Defuniak Street. This gorgeous 4-story Grayton Beach home is perfectly located just steps from the beach and close to some of the best spots in 30A. This home offers 7 bedrooms, 7.5 bathrooms, a private pool, exquisite emerald coast views from three balconies, and wonderful accommodations for up to 22 guests!

Tranquil blue interiors and refreshing coastal décor set the stage for a relaxing and peaceful getaway. The first floor’s open-concept floor plan welcomes guests to relax and enjoy time together. The living room offers plush seating—including a large sectional sofa—and a flat-screen television, perfect for enjoying a family movie. The kitchen is perfectly equipped for a large group, with one full-size stainless steel refrigerator/freezer, stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and ample storage space. Two long tables in the nearby dining area provide lots of guest seating.

This home’s second and third floors house the main guest rooms and offer a variety of sleeping accommodations. The second floor features a king bed guest room with an attached bathroom; a bunk room with two twin-over-full bunk beds and an attached bathroom; and a spacious guest room with two cozy king beds and a private bathroom. On the third floor are three guest rooms: The first room features a queen sleigh bed and an adjoining bunk room with a twin-over-twin bunk bed and an attached bathroom with a single vanity and shower/tub combo. The final guest room on this level offers a beautiful queen bed, a flat-screen television, and an attached bathroom with a shower/tub combo.

On the fourth floor is a king bed guest room which has access to a jack-and-jill bathroom with a shower/tub combo. Also on this level is the entertainment and game room, a flat-screen television, and access to the fourth-floor balcony with stunning emerald coast views.

Guests in this home will love the sparkling private pool (which can be heated seasonally) and spacious pool deck with lots of seating. Enjoy a freshly prepared dinner on the new large gas grill or walk across the street and enjoy the delectable bites and delicious drinks at Chiringo or Red Bar—two of Grayton Beach’s best local spots. Take the short walk across the street to the sugar white sand beach, where complimentary beach setup of 2 chairs and 1 umbrella await each morning. This home also offers 7 adult beach cruiser bikes, perfect for discovering the best that Grayton Beach and our 30A towns have to offer.

It’s time to “Seas the Day”! Book your stay in this exquisite home today!

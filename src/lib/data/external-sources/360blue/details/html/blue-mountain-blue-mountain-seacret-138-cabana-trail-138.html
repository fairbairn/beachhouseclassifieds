Blue Mountain "Blue Mountain Seacret" 138 Cabana Trail
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 8
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- 4 minute walk to the beach
- Located in the community of Lakeside at Blue Mountain Beach
- 6 seater gas Low-Speed Vehicle
- Community pool (Heated during Spring Break, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's)
- Professionally decorated
- 4 miles to Seaside/Watercolor. Beautiful paved bike path the whole way
- 4 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant—All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Blue Mountain Seacret is an adorable 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home that’s a short bike ride from the beach, has access to a large community pool, and sleeps 12. Guests will love the 2 spacious front porches, premium appliances, and professionally decorated interiors.

A cute front porch and a large gas grill welcome guests to Blue Mountain Seacret. Inside, guests will find a delightful open-concept living, dining, and kitchen area professionally decorated in soothing shades of blue and white. A wrap-around queen sleeper sofa and armchairs surround a 65” Smart TV, and an adjacent dining table seats 6 with room to spare. Gleaming white countertops and deep blue chevron tiles in the kitchen beautifully frame a Viking 6-burner gas range and Viking premium appliances. Tucked behind the kitchen is a comfortable guest bedroom with a king bed, a 50” Smart TV, a private bathroom with a double vanity, and a spacious shower with a built-in bench.

Upstairs is a primary bedroom, bunkroom, and guest bedroom. The guest bedroom has a king bed and shares a hall bathroom with a shower/tub combo with the bunkroom. In the bunkroom, 2 twin-over-twin built-in bunk beds are designed with cute ship-lap walls, shelving, and criss-cross bed guards. Guests staying in the primary bedroom will enjoy private access to the second-floor porch. Guests can put their feet up, unwind on a cushioned wicker sofa, and enjoy beach breezes and views of the neighborhood. Inside the room is a king bed, a 50” Smart TV, a spacious bathroom with a soaking tub, a double vanity, and a glass-enclosed shower with a built-in bench.

It’s easy to explore Blue Mountain Beach and surrounding 30A communities with 4 included adult bikes and 6 passenger Low-Speed Vehicle. Popular spots like Grayton Beach State Park, WaterColor, and Seaside are within a 4-mile bike ride on a paved bike path. Book Blue Mountain Seacret today.

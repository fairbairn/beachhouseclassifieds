DETAILS: Indulge in a luxurious Seagrove Beach vacation at St. Charles. This expansive home accommodates 26 guests in immaculate luxury featuring 8 beautiful bedrooms, 8 bathrooms, and 2 half baths, perfect for family reunions, bachelorette trips, or groups seeking a delightful escape. Revel in blissful days lounging by the heated pool, savoring tasty barbecue from the outdoor kitchen, toasting the sunset on the observation deck, and strolling to the beach—only a 2-minute walk away!

The lavish first floor evokes a playful spirit with a pool table and wet bar. A stunning, private oasis awaits out the glass doors and features an outdoor kitchen with a gas grill, a refrigerator, and a TV for leisurely entertainment. Slip into the private pool’s cooling waters or bask in the sunshine on the loungers. When it’s time to retire, 2 guest bedrooms invite unwinding with soft king beds and private bathrooms. The bunk room is a great option for the younger guests, with 1 queen-over-queen bunk bed, 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds, and a private bathroom.

The impeccably designed second floor features the main living space, where a stunning natural wood ceiling and abundant natural light create a warm, inviting setting. Cooking enthusiasts will love the chef's kitchen, which is perfectly equipped with top-of-the-line appliances. The adjacent dining table’s long bench and 6 chairs allow for ample seating with additional accommodations at the 4-stool breakfast bar. In the adjoining living space, watch TV or relive favorite memories from the day while relaxing on the 2 couches and armchairs. A covered, well-furnished deck welcomes guests outside. Enjoy a peaceful night in the spacious primary bedroom, featuring a king bed and a private bathroom.

On the third-level landing, discover an extra living space, a sleeping nook with 2 built-in, twin-over-twin bunk beds, and a charming balcony with cushioned seating and a spiral staircase to the rooftop deck. The luxe primary bedroom and 3 lovely guest bedrooms each offer a plush king bed; the primary and 2 of the guest bedrooms include a private bathroom.

Experience breathtaking Gulf views from the fourth-floor deck, outfitted with 2 alfresco tables.

St. Charles offers an exceptional Seagrove Beach experience, easy exploration with 8 adult bikes and a 6-passenger low-speed vehicle, as well as quick beach access—only 400 feet away at Azalea-Camelia Beach Access. Book today!

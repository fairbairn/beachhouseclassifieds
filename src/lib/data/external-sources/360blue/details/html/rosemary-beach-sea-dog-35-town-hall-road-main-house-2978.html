Rosemary Beach "Sea Dog" 35 Town Hall Road Main House
Rosemary Beach, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 10
Sleeps 14
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Located in the heart of Rosemary Beach, just a few blocks from the beach
- South of 30A
- Courtyard and fountain
- Pet-friendly
- 5 adult bikes
- Grill
- Community pools
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Discover an exciting coastal getaway at Sea Dog, an inviting pet-friendly retreat nestled in the heart of Rosemary Beach—only a 4-minute stroll from the sparkling shoreline! This 5-bedroom, 5-bathroom haven offers 2,700 square feet of elegant living space, comfortably accommodating up to 14 guests. Unwind in the serene private courtyard near the peaceful fountain, enjoy home-cooked meals from the gourmet kitchen, and delight in the children exploring the expansive third-floor loft. With access to community pools and thoughtful amenities, this 3-story home is designed for relaxation and fun.

Step onto the rustic farmhouse porch and open the front door to a welcoming space with 3 inviting bedrooms and a laundry room. The primary bedroom features a luxurious king bed, perfect for unwinding after a day at the beach, while 2 other guest bedrooms offer cozy queen beds—each with its own private bathroom. A charming courtyard with cozy seating creates the perfect setting for enjoying a good book or watching the dogs run and play in this enclosed area.

The second floor is the heart of this home, exuding subtle coastal charm with warm wood floors, natural textures, and abundant light. Gather with loved ones in the peaceful living area, relax on the white slip-covered furniture, or enjoy a family movie night on the TV. Easily prepare meals in the gourmet kitchen, equipped with high-end appliances, spacious cabinetry, a wine cooler, and sizeable island with built-in gadgets. The breakfast bar seats 3 for coffee and after-beach snacks. A sunlit dining area seats 8 under an iron chandelier with candle-like lighting. Settle into the second-floor primary bedroom, featuring a king bed, TV, private bathroom, and balcony access. From the great room, a double set of French doors open to a wraparound deck with a porch swing, Adirondack chairs, and neighborhood views.

A spiral staircase leads up to the spacious third floor, where a kid-friendly loft with 4 twin beds, 1 twin-over-twin bunk, and a private bathroom provides the perfect spot for game nights and other fun.

Enjoy the best of Rosemary Beach at Sea Dog! With on-site parking and 5 complimentary adult bikes, exploring Scenic 30A is a breeze. For beach access, head left on Town Hall Road, follow the path by Pescado Seafood Grill to Water Street, turn left, and continue to Western Green. At the end of the green is the boardwalk and beach! Book now!

FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary bedroom: King bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

THIRD FLOOR:
- Loft area: 4 Twin Beds, 1 Twin over Twin Bunk Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

You also have access to these fantastic Rosemary Beach amenities:
- Coquina Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.
- Sky Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 6 am to 10 pm (Adult Only Hours, 6 am-9 am & 8 pm-10 pm)
- Cabana Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.
- Barbados Pool: open daily to residents and guests from 9 am to 9 pm.

*Your rental property may share a courtyard or lot with a carriage house that is owner-occupied or rented to separate guests from your party.

Parties, weddings, or events held at the property must be pre-approved in writing by 360 Blue. Otherwise, guests will be subject to HOA, community, and other associated fines.

*This home is pet-friendly with an additional, non-refundable pet fee of $400, plus tax.

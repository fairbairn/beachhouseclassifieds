Seacrest Beach "Gotta Lovitt" 150 Walton Gulfview Drive
Seacrest Beach, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 9
Sleeps 14
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Short walk to the beach
- Short bike ride to The Hub and Rosemary Town Square
- Deeded beach access
- Across the street from Gulf
- Private pool **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for $50 per day**
- Outdoor entertainment area with outdoor summer kitchen, fire pit, and grill
- 5 adult bikes
- 6-passenger gas-powered Low-Speed Vehicle
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

Details: Just minutes from the sugar-white sand and emerald green waters, "Gotta Lovitt" at 150 Walton Gulfiew Drive in Seacrest is a stunning 5-bedroom, 5.5-bathroom house that can sleep up to 14 guests. Some of its notable features include a private pool and incredible outdoor entertainment area, 6-seater Low-Speed Vehicle, fire pit, and outdoor kitchen. It's the perfect vacation home for multi-generational families or a group of friends looking to stay in the middle of it all on 30A. The screened outdoor patio has a full sectional, dining table, grill, and flat-screen TV overlooking the pool deck. Guests will also have complete privacy in the backyard and lounge chairs for those who prefer to sunbathe. There is one bedroom on the first floor with two full over full bunk beds and private bathroom.

The main level of the home is on the second floor, where you'll find a large open kitchen and living area. The kitchen features sparkling white quartz countertops and stainless-steel appliances. The dining table and island can seat a combined 18 guests for a formal or informal dinner. The living room is also spacious enough for everyone to hang out and relax around the TV. A minibar and half-bathroom are nearby for added convenience when entertaining. Additional seating is available on the second-floor balcony, which overlooks the quiet street. A primary bedroom with a king bed and private bathroom is down the hall as well.

The third floor hosts the remaining bedrooms. The first 2 have king beds and private bathrooms attached. One is the primary bedroom, which has its own balcony overlooking the street. The last guest room is a bunk room, which has 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds and a private bathroom.

You're just a short bike ride away from the famous Rosemary Beach, where you'll find award-winning lounges and restaurants, kid-friendly activities, a weekly farmer's market, and seasonal events for all ages. Cruise to the beach on the 5 adult bikes provided with the home, or take the Low-Speed Vehicle for added convenience when toting beach gear. You don't want to miss your opportunity to vacation at "Gotta Lovitt," book today!

THIRD FLOOR
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Two Twin-over-Twin Bunk beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

**Please note that one tank of gas is provided for the Low-Speed Vehicle.

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments and be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

**Unfortunately, we can not allow animals in this home. The owner has a severe pet allergy and failure to adhere to this rule will allow in an immediate eviction and loss of deposit. Guest is fully responsible for all damages.

**No Smoking Allowed

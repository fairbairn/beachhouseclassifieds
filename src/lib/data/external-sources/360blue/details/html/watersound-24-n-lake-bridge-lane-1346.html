Watersound 24 N. Lake Bridge Lane
Watersound, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 4
Sleeps 8
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS
- Coastal Dune Lakefront
- Gulf views
- Located in a secure, gated community with on-site security
- Private Deeded Beach Access
- Golf cart
- 4 adult bikes
- From March 1st to October 31st, the rental of this property includes one beach -service set (2 beach chairs and 1umbrella)
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout
- This property come with 3 designated parking spaces. This home permits a maximum of 3 vehicles through the community security gate.

DETAILS: 24 N. Lake Bridge Land is a 4–bedroom, 4.5–bathroom home that sleeps 8 in the WaterSound Bridges community. This posh beachhouse overlooks a rare coastal dune lake, enjoys Gulf views and has luxe interiors designed to spoil guests.

A cozy covered porch welcomes guests to the home. The first floor has 3 guest bedrooms with private bathrooms, a laundry area, and a lovely screened back porch. Guests may choose between a calming neutral guest room with a king bed, a fanciful guest room with a king bed and delightful art, or a guest room with wooded views of the community, a king bed, and a small writing desk. Past a large laundry area is the home’s screened back porch. Guests will enjoy the comfort of curling up on the sofa or challenging loved ones to a game of ping pong!

In the second–floor living spaces, glittering touches of gold and silver accent luscious views of Camp Creek Lake and the dunes dividing it from the Gulf. A wall of glistening gold tiles adds glamour to the open kitchen. Sparkling chandeliers above a marble island draw the eye towards the exposed timber beams extending across the formal dining table to the living area. A separate secondary kitchen gives guests a more private space to fix coffee or breakfast. A waterfall–edged marble bar is practically set ablaze with the twinkle from its metallic tile backsplash in the living area. Colorful artwork and a fireplace surround a long, comfortable couch, creating an inviting space for guests to relax. French doors from the living and kitchen area open to a balcony with gorgeous views of Camp Creek Lake and the dunes beyond.

A primary bedroom and personal balcony occupy the entire third floor. The serene space is finished in white and punctuated with baby blue, cream, and gold décor. An arched ceiling and a wall dedicated to views of Camp Creek Lake add depth to the king bed and sitting area. A sliding barn door hides the elegant bathroom with shower. The balcony has views of the dune lake, the pedestrian bridge, elevated boardwalk, the dunes, and the Gulf beyond.

Guests will love exploring the beauty of WaterSound, and the surrounding 30A communities, on 4 included adult bikes. Book your next dream getaway! 24 N. Lake Bridge Lane will book quickly with this location and these amenities.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION
*Fireplaces and elevator are not for guest use.
*This is a peaceful community. To ensure everyone's enjoyment, weddings, parties, gatherings exceeding a reasonable number of guests, and guests of guests are strictly prohibited. Violation of this policy may result in immediate removal from the property, forfeiture of any remaining rental fees, and potential fines from the HOA, community, and 360 Blue.

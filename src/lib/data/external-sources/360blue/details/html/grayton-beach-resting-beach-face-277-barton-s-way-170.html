Grayton Beach "Resting Beach Face" 277 Barton's Way
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 6
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Private beach access at Sanctuary by the Sea
- New furnishings
- Tennis/Pickleball
- 2 community Pools(One pool is heated only between the dates of 11/15-12/2, 12/16-1/6, and March spring break)
- Clubhouse amenities
- 6-Passenger Low-Speed Vehicle**
- 5 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Resting Beach Face is a custom-built home with 5 bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms that sleeps 12 comfortably. Located in the coveted neighborhood of The Preserve, this Grayton Beach home provides access to one of the best beaches on 30A, located .25 miles east of the community entrance. Guests will love this spacious 2-story home with access to the community pool and tennis courts.

Resting Beach Face has a tranquil gray façade crowned with vibrant mint shutters and a cozy sitting porch that leads to the first floor. Guests will admire the customized elements of this open floorplan, such as the white oak-colored floors, white shiplap walls, and decorative beams in the great room. Awash in natural lighting, friends and family can unwind in the living area on oversized sofas and armchairs that surround the TV. The adjacent dining area is perfect for a sit-down meal with its dining table with 5 chairs and bench seating on one side. The gourmet kitchen features a 4-stool breakfast bar/kitchen island with quartz countertops, decorative glass door cabinets, and stainless-steel appliances—including a wine fridge and ice maker. For extra comfort, there is 1 primary bedroom and 1 bunk room with queen-over-queen beds on this floor.

After a long day at the beach, guests can retire in comfort to the 3 guest bedrooms on the second floor. All the bedrooms feature a private bathroom—including one with a free-standing tub for a spa-like experience. There is also a tiled laundry room on this floor with a full-sized washer and dryer.

Outside this home is a lovely space for hosting a cookout. With a magnificent screened-in porch and a small patio with a propane grill, invite family and friends over for an al fresco dining experience with everything you need right outside the back door. Don’t forget to use the cedar-enclosed outdoor shower after returning from the beach—perfect for getting the sand off!

Resting Beach Face ensures a fun and relaxing vacation on the Emerald Coast! Find some adventure by exploring the local area on the 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle(seasonal only) or 4 adult bikes provided with the property. Book Resting Beach Face today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 12):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen-over-Queen Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate

*Please note that there is nearby construction that can be heard from time to time.

*The fireplace is not for guest use.

**Please note that in order to utilize the Low-Speed Vehicle that comes with the home, you will be required to pay a $30 non-refundable deposit to 30a Gulf Carts.

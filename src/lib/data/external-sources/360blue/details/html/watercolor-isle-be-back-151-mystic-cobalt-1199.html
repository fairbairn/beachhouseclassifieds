Santa Rosa Beach, FL

Beds 5

HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 10 wristbands.
- Located in Watercolor's desirable Park District
- New furniture and updates completed in 2021
- Screened porches
- Sunroom
- Carriage house with kitchenette
- Outdoor gas grill
- 3 dedicated parking spots and 1 community parking hang tag
- 4 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, a second pool deck with additional lounge seating, a third pool with zero-entry, and an enhanced WaterColor Grill and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Open the doors to the charming screened porch and enter this stunning Park District home! The first floor of this home welcomes you in with an open floor plan. The living and dining room wrap the gourmet kitchen, creating the perfect space for entertaining. The kitchen is equipped with top-of-the-line Viking appliances and a massive custom island. Just off this area, you will find a beautiful set of French doors that lead you out to a sunroom, ideal for your morning coffee! Just off this sunroom, you will find a built-in gas grill and outdoor living area!

All bedrooms in this home conveniently include a mounted TV. The first bedroom features a queen bed with a trundle and access to the front screened porch with hallway bathroom access. The second guest room is equipped with a king bed, a private ensuite bath, and access to the back porch. You will find the luxurious primary just across the hall, boasting a plush king bed, shared porch access, and an en suite primary bath with dual vanity sinks!

There is an outdoor shower, covered parking, and a private carriage house just off the back of the house. This carriage house is all charm with a queen bed, full bathroom, kitchenette, and a lofted nook. This is a perfect private escape for extra guests!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (Sleeps 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Queen with Twin Trundle, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

CARRIAGE HOUSE:
Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed and Twin Sleeper, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

The house is in a great location (at the front of Watercolor), it's clean, comfortable and has everything we needed and more. We enjoyed biking, walking, playing games, eating out, shopping- and even though it was chilly, we did sit poolside and relaxed with a book and a drink! The name is so appropriate, and I hope "Isle Be Back"...

Seagrove Beach "McDunes" 81 Chivas Lane
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-092500
Bedrooms 6
Beds 10
Sleeps 16
Full Baths 6
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Breathtaking Gulf views
- Steps from deeded private beach access
- Private boardwalk to the beach
- Complimentary beach set up each morning includes 2 beach chairs and 1 umbrella (Service provided Mar 1-Oct 31)
- Private heated pool **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for $30 per day**
- Large porches with outdoor living space
- 6 Adult Bikes
- Elevator
- 6-passenger Low-Speed Vehicle
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Escape for a lavish Seagrove Beach getaway at McDunes! Tucked along the Gulf Coast, with beach access out the back gate, this 6-bedroom, 6.5-bathroom home accommodates 16 guests in luxe comfort. Picture tranquil mornings taking in the resplendent sunrise views from the multiple balconies before sunbathing by the pool or stretching out on the white-sand beach—only seconds away!

Contemporary coastal style defines the interior of this magnificent 4-story home. Designed for entertaining, the first floor hosts a wet bar that connects to the pool deck. Slip outside with a drink in hand to discover the fenced-in space. A 6-seat patio table invites guests to relax as the overhead fan chases away the heat, or bask in the sunshine on the loungers beside the shimmering private pool. This level hosts 2 guest bedrooms with a queen and king bed, respectively.

Take the stairs or elevator to the second level to find 2 lush primary bedrooms, each with a king bed, private bathroom, and balcony with Gulf views. The second primary also boasts a wet bar, beverage fridge, and office. A guest bedroom with a soft queen bed completes the level.

The elegant third-floor great room begins with a gourmet kitchen, fully equipped with top-of-the-line appliances. Uncork a bottle from the wine chiller to entertain guests at the 5-stool breakfast bar before adjourning to the 6-seat table for dinner. Or, head onto the balcony to grill and dine alfresco at the 6-person table. After eating, wind down the evening in the outdoor sitting area or head back inside to the living space, featuring plenty of soft seating and a 65” TV.

Kick back and relax in the fourth-floor sitting space, furnished with a queen sleeper sofa, TV, and wet bar. Glass doors lead to a wraparound balcony with loungers and incredible coastal views. This level hosts a charming bunk room with 2 built-in twin-over-twin beds.

Convenience and coastal luxury await at Seagrove Beach’s McDunes. Easily explore with parking for 5 vehicles—plus 6 complimentary adult bikes and a 6-passenger low speed vehicle. For beach access, take the boardwalk on the east side of the home. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (Sleeps 16):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom One: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Primary Bedroom Two: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

FOURTH FLOOR:
- Bunk Room: Two Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Second Living Area: Queen Sleeper, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

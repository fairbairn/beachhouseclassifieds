Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, a second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and a third pool with zero-entry, as well as an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and a new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Enjoy a relaxing, carefree getaway at 114 Royal Fern Way, a stunning 3-bedroom, 3-bathroom WaterColor home that sleeps 6 in the Forest neighborhood. Take advantage of the many WaterColor amenities, including 3 community pools, Camp WaterColor, and the private beach club. Surrounded by natural green space and parks, this beautiful home provides privacy, pet-friendly accommodations, and is one of the first completely ADA accessible homes in the area—complete with a roll-in pool!

This gorgeous coastal home’s front porch welcomes guests with rocking chairs and swings for unwinding in the fresh air, while a ramp on the side of the home offers accessibility. Head inside to discover a spacious great room boasting tall ceilings, wood floors, and warm lighting. The interior design showcases a mix of natural colors and textures, enhanced by vibrant accents of blue. Sink into the plush furniture in the living area, where a wall-mounted TV provides entertainment for cozy nights. Prepare meals in the sleek white kitchen equipped with high-end appliances, and enjoy the convenience of a large island, which doubles as a breakfast bar for 4. The traditional dining area features an elegant candlelight fixture above a table that comfortably seats 6. Step onto the side deck for outdoor dining options, where a propane grill and a 2-chair dinette set are available. Outside, guests will also find the inviting heated pool for leisurely days in the sun. When it’s time for bed, choose between 3 comfortable bedrooms, each with a private bathroom—2 primary bedrooms with king beds and 1 guest bedroom with a queen bed. The first primary bedroom has a wheel-in shower, which can be used with the property’s plastic wheelchair. A washer and dryer offer convenience for extended stays.

Comfort, style, and convenience create the perfect getaway at WaterColor’s 114 Royal Fern Way. With on-site parking for 2 vehicles and 3 complimentary adult bikes, it's easy to explore the local area. For beach fun, head to Scenic 395 Beach Access at the end of 395 between Scenic 30A and Scenic 395. The community also offers ADA accessible ramp to the beach at WaterColor Beach Club. Book now!

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments, as well as be subject to fines.

*We are happy to welcome up to 2 dogs for an additional non-refundable pet fee of $400, plus tax.
 
*Per HOA Policy, all Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be made through The Electric Cart Company and will be limited to 80 vehicles throughout the community at any given time. Homes are permitted one Low-Speed Vehicle per home. Homes with owner provided Low-Speed Vehicles are not permitted to rent an additional Low-Speed Vehicle.

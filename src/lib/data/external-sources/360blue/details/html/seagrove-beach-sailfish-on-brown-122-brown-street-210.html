Seagrove Beach "Sailfish on Brown" 122 Brown Street
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-095208
Bedrooms 4
Beds 7
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Short walk to beach access
- Private pool- *Pool heating is available from October 1st through May 1st for an additional fee of $45 per day
- 4 adult bikes
- Gas grill
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Sailfish on Brown is a 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home that sleeps 12 in the quintessential beach town of Seagrove Beach—one of the original 30A communities. With 2,400 square feet of living space, including a private pool, and 2 screened-in porches. Guests will love being indoors as much as they love being outdoors. It's a quick walk to the nearest beach access, less than a quarter of a mile away!

This stunning 2-story home welcomes guests to the first floor with a beautiful screened-in front porch that exudes coastal charm. The shiplap walls and coastal accents create a relaxed and inviting atmosphere throughout the home, while the spacious great room seamlessly combines the living, dining, and kitchen areas. Plush furniture surrounds the living area, with a 58" TV mounted overhead for entertainment. Enjoy access to an outdoor screened-in porch from the great room, with ample seating, fresh air, and a gas grill. Just off the porch is the private pool area, surrounded by comfortable Adirondack loungers and a fabulous alfresco dining table for outdoor meals. Inside, a sleek and modern kitchen features white cabinets and plenty of counter space for meal preparation. There's also a quaint kitchen island and a 2-stool breakfast bar, perfect for a casual meal. For a sit-down dinner, the dining area provides seating for 6 adorned with a modern light fixture, adding a touch of elegance to the space. A laundry area in the hallway offers added convenience with a full-sized washer and dryer. When it's time to wind down, 1 primary bedroom with a comfortable king bed and a private bath awaits guests.

Experience the stunning beauty of the Emerald Coast from the second floor, boasting 3 spacious bedrooms with great views. There are 2 luxurious guest bedrooms with sizeable king beds and 1 bunk room with 2 full-over-full beds—perfect for kids or last-minute guests.

Sailfish on Brown offers everything needed for a comfortable and stylish stay. With on-site parking for 4 vehicles—plus 4 complimentary adult bikes—it's easy to explore the local area, including nearby Eastern Lake. Book now and experience the best of coastal living today!

- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Shared bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Two Full-over-Full Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

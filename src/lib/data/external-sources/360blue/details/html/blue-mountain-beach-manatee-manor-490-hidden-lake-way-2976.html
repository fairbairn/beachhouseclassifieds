Blue Mountain Beach "Manatee Manor" 490 Hidden Lake Way
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 6
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 3
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- 2 Community pools
- 4 adult bikes
- Pet-friendly
- 6-passenger Low speed vehicle
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Escape for carefree beach days and aquatic fun at Manatee Manor, a 4-bedroom, 3-bathroom Blue Mountain Beach home comfortably accommodating 10 guests and up to 2 furry friends. Enjoy relaxing by the 2 community pools, 1 is right across the street, or sampling the area’s dining options, including The Perfect Pig and Shunk Galley Oyster Bar. With peaceful porches, backyard dining, and walkable beach access—only 11 minutes away—this home is a perfect 30A retreat.

Step inside this charming coastal cottage to find a reverse floor plan. Unwind in the inviting primary bedroom, featuring a plush king bed and access to a furnished porch, or retreat to the guest bedroom with a queen bed. Both bedrooms include a private bathroom. In the backyard, a 4-seat dining table beckons for slow-paced meals or afternoon cocktails in the gentle breeze.

On the second level, vintage-inspired beach décor, knotted jute rugs, and beadboard accents set the scene for laid-back living. The open kitchen features high-end appliances, granite countertops, and a 3-stool breakfast bar. Share flavorful feasts at the classic wooden dining table for 6. In the living space, gather on the soft sofa and 3 armchairs surrounding the TV for cheerful nights catching up or watching movies. A glass door opens to the covered porch, where a 2-person dinette set offers a quiet spot for savoring a cup of coffee in the fresh air. The 2 bunk rooms are great for kids—the first has a full-over-full bunk bed and a private bathroom, while the second has a twin-over-full bunk bed. A laundry closet by the kitchen makes it easy to refresh swimwear after trips to the beach.

Manatee Manor has everything guests need for a delightful Blue Mountain Beach getaway, including on-site parking and 4 complimentary adult bikes. For the nearest beach access, follow Hidden Lake Way to West County Highway 30A, turn left onto South Spooky Lane and follow to Spooky Lane Beach Access. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (SLEEPS 10):
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
-Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Full-over-Full Bunk Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
- Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Full Bunk Bed

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger.

**Please note that in order to utilize the Low-Speed Vehicle that comes with the home, you will be required to pay a $30 non-refundable deposit to 30a Gulf Carts.

*We are happy to welcome up to 2 dogs for an additional, non-refundable pet fee of $400, plus tax.

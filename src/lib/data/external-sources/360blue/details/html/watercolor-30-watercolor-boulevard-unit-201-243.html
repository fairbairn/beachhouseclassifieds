HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
*Per Watercolor HOA, please include all guests aged 5 and older in your total count of adult guests to receive amenity and pool access wristbands. Sleep arrangements are provided as a courtesy and are not considered in the occupancy count.
**This home is allowed 4 amenity access bands.**

- Walking distance to the beach
- Centrally located in WaterColor and walking distance to Town Center
- Washer/Dryer
- Parking can be found throughout the community.
- 2 bikes provided
- Newly remodeled master bathroom
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, a third pool with zero-entry, an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and a new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Situated in the heart of world-famous WaterColor, Florida, and located directly above Wine World, this 1-bedroom, 2-bathroom condo offers an idyllic getaway for couples or a small family. This unit comfortably accommodates 4 guests and is within walking distance to WaterColor's shops, dining venues, and the Emerald Coast pristine beaches. Recent updates to the kitchen, reflect brand new cabinetry and stainless-steel appliances. The kitchen is fully equipped to prepare a gourmet meal. There is also a dining table that seats 4, with 2 additional seats at the breakfast bar. The primary bedroom has a king bed with a flat-screen TV and newly remodeled bathroom in 2024!

There is also a bunk room off the living area with a twin-over-full bunk bed and direct access to the screened-in porch with new outdoor seating. This bunk shares a bathroom with shower with additional guests to utilize and a back porch with sunny views of the neighborhood.

Cerulean Park rests directly across from this unit and offers a great place to relax with your favorite book. The 2 adult bikes provide a great alternative way to explore WaterColor's winding roads and the surrounding 30A area. When you're ready to venture out of WaterColor, be sure to check out the fabulous shopping and dining venues in neighboring Seaside and Seagrove. Check availability and book with 360 Blue today!

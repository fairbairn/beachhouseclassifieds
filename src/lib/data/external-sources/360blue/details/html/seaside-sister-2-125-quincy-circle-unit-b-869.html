Seaside "Sister 2" 125 Quincy Circle Unit B
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 2
Beds 3
Sleeps 6
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 0
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Short walk to the beach, community pools, and amenities
- Steps from the famous Seaside Town Square featuring plenty of shopping and dining
- Wifi
- Parking for 1 vehicle
- 2 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: The "Sister 2" in Seaside's Ruskin Place is a 2 bedroom, 2 bathroom condo that sleeps 6 and provides stunning views of Seaside's art district, downtown Seaside, and the Gulf. Take in spectacular views from the balcony, and relax in a modern interior. Enjoy a vacation that is mere steps from Seaside's famous amphitheater, community pools, boutiques, restaurants, and private beach access.

"Sister 2" is located above a Ruskin Place business, providing bird's eye views of this famous New Urbanist community from every room. An open-concept kitchen, dining, and living room with warm copper accents and rich wood details welcome guests. Premium stainless-steel appliances and a curved kitchen island with seating for 2 surround cabinetry and shelves crafted from the same beautiful Maple as the wall opposite. A glass-top dining table with seating for 6 blends seamlessly into the comfortable living room. A slip-covered sleeper sofa and a loveseat face a flat-screen TV and enjoy natural light from double doors that open onto a large balcony.

You might catch a bit of a New Orleans vibe outside on the balcony. Cushioned chairs are ideal for sitting and watching people stroll through the oak-covered park in Ruskin Place to Seaside's bustling shopping area.

Off the kitchen is a guest bedroom with a king bed with floor-to-ceiling windows and a private bathroom with a shower. A primary bedroom off the living room has a king bed and a private bathroom with a shower.

A balcony with a small table and chairs offers phenomenal views of the Gulf, downtown Seaside, and the roofscapes of famous cottages and homes throughout this beloved 30A community. It's a fantastic spot to toast the sunset or share a romantic dance underneath the stars.

Don't wait to book Sisters 2. With views and the ability to explore all Seaside offers on 2 included adult bikes, the Sisters 2 will not last long. Book an unforgettable Seaside vacation today.

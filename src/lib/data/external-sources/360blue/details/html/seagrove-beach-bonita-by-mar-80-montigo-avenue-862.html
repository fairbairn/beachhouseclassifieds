Seagrove Beach "Bonita By Mar" 80 Montigo Avenue
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-221328
Bedrooms 6
Beds 10
Sleeps 22
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 2
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Located in the heart of Seagrove Beach
- 4-minute walk to the beach
- Private pool *Pool heating is available from October 1st through May 1st for an additional fee of $40 per day
- 6 adult bikes
- Low Speed Vehicle- Golf Cart
- In-home elevator
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Blissful, balmy days await at Bonita By Mar, a contemporary Seagrove Beach home. Boasting 6 bedrooms, 5 bathrooms, and 2 half baths, this 3-level home comfortably accommodates 22 guests. Enjoy 3,965 square feet of luxury living, a private pool, and walkable beach access—only 3 minutes away!

A spacious front porch with a cushioned swing sets the peaceful tone the moment guests arrive. Venture inside to an open great room designed in a soothing, beach-inspired aesthetic of pale woods and muted colors. The gorgeous kitchen is equipped with top-of-the-line appliances and overlooks the living and dining spaces for seamless conversation. Curl up on the soft sectional for a laid-back evening watching the Smart TV with friends and family. Beneath a beaded chandelier is an 8-seat table, with additional seating for 5 at the breakfast bar. Enjoy the temperate weather on the back porch’s cushioned seating or in the enclosed backyard, outfitted with a private pool, chaise lounges, a grill, and a firepit. When bedtime arrives, there is a guest bedroom with a king bed, a private bathroom, and a Smart TV on this level, as well as a laundry room for convenience.

Take the in-home elevator or stairs to the second level to find 1 bunk room with 2 queen-over-queen beds and 3 lovely guest bedrooms—the first guest bedroom has 2 queen beds, and the other 2 guest bedrooms each have a king bed.

On the top level, a second living space with a queen sleeper sofa, armchair, Smart TV, beverage fridge, and coffee maker offers room for the group to spread out. Step onto the secluded balcony to sip drinks at the round 6-seat table or at the arrangement of comfy patio chairs. The beautifully appointed primary bedroom with a plush king bed and a luxe private bathroom is on this floor.

Discover an idyllic Seagrove Beach getaway at Bonita By Mar. There’s parking for 6 vehicles and 6 complimentary adult bikes for guests who prefer slow-paced exploring. Santa Clara Regional Beach Access is a 5-minute walk. Head south on Montigo Avenue, turn right onto E. County Highway 30A, and the beach access will be on the left. Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS (SLEEPS 22):
FIRST FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
-Guest Bedroom: Two Full Beds, Utilizes Common Area Bathroom with Shower Only
-Guest Bedroom: Two Queen-over-Queen Bunk Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

THIRD FLOOR:
-Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Separate
-Additional Sleeping Area: Queen Sleeper Sofa

As of August 2025, there is construction in the vicinity of this home.

Rosemary Beach "Blue Door Cottage and Carriage" 121 Round Road
Rosemary Beach, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 12
Sleeps 18
Full Baths 5
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- South of Hwy 30A and steps to private beach access
- Private heated pool and jacuzzi spa. **Pool heating available for $35/day**
- Outdoor kitchen
- Flat-screen TVs in every bedroom
- 5 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Located south of 30A, Blue Door Cottage and Carriage House is a beautifully renovated 5-bedroom, 5-bathroom property in Rosemary Beach that sleeps 18. Steps from private beach access, an adjacent boardwalk leads to the sandy shores, Coquina pool, and nearby Rosemary Beach l shops and eateries. Enjoy the private pool and professionally decorated interiors—boasting new furnishings and fixtures, , refinished hardwood floors, and fresh paint—in both the Main House and Carriage House!

The heart of this 2-story Main House resides on its second floor, adorned with new coastal furnishings and décor that sets the stage for a memorable vacation. The inviting great room is bathed in natural light, featuring vaulted ceilings and a spacious balcony that stretches across the front of the home. The beautifully furnished open living area boasts a large flat screen TV for movie nights, powder bath and a designated workspace. The kitchen is a chef's delight with Sub-Zero and Wolf appliances, 2 additional refrigerator drawers, a walk-in pantry with a built-in microwave, and quartz countertops. Enjoy a traditional meal together at the 8-seat dining table, or grab a quick bite at the adjacent kitchen island with 4 barstools. Dine alfresco on the balcony's 8-person dining table and unwind afterwards on the comfy sectional. A primary bedroom on this floor has a king bed, flat screen TV, and private bathroom with double vanities and a walk-in shower.

The Main House's first floor boasts 2 primary bedrooms with king beds, plush bedding, flat screen TVs, and private bathrooms Each bathroom has double vanities and a walk-in shower, while one also includes a gorgeous soaking tub. An adjacent bunk room has 2 twin-over-full beds, a flat screen TV, desk and its own bathroom with a shower/tub combo-perfect for kids! Amenities also include a washer and dryer. Doors open to a covered porch and stunning private courtyard with luscious landscaping, an outdoor kitchen, private pool and sitting spa, providing plenty of outdoor space for the entire family.

The 2-story Carriage House sleeps 6 and features a comfortable living area, dining table for 6, queen bed, private bathroom with double vanities and a walk-in shower, and stacked washer and dryer. The kitchen features a Sub-Zero fridge, whisper-quiet Bosch dishwasher, and glass stovetop. Upstairs is a bunk bedroom with 4 built-in twin beds and a flat screen TV. Each bed has a built-in storage drawer

The Blue Door Cottage and Carriage House will book quickly with its incredible location close to the beach and new luxurious furnishings. Guest parking is available in front near the 5 complimentary adult bikes. Book now!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS
FIRST FLOOR:
Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Freestanding Tub and Shower
Guest Bedroom: Two Twin-over-Full Bunk Beds, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

SECOND FLOOR:
Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

CARRIAGE HOUSE:
Living Area: Queen Bed with Built-in Storage Drawers, Private Bathroom with Shower
Guest Bedroom: Four Twin Beds with Built-in Storage Drawers, Shared Bathroom with Shower

The outdoor fireplace is not for guest use.

Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments as well as be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

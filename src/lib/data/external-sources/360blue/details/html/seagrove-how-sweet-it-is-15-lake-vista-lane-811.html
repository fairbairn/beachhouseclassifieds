Seagrove "How Sweet It Is" 15 Lake Vista Lane
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 4
Beds 5
Sleeps 10
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- 4th Floor lookout tower with Gulf views
- Deeded beach access directly across the street
- Private pool **Pool heating available Oct 1–May 1 for $40 per day**
- Walkable distance to The Greenway Station - Shopping & Dining
- 6 Seater Low-Speed Vehicle
- 2 Dishwashers
- Sonos
- Stand Alone Ice Maker
- Washer/Dryer on multiple floors
- 4 adult bikes
- Complete Clean Linen Participant—ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: How Sweet It Is in Seagrove Beach is a 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom luxury beach cottage that sleeps 10 with Gulf views and a private pool. Guests will love the lookout tower and the convenience of being right on 30A, with beach access directly across the street.

On the first floor of this elegant home is a guest bedroom with a king bed, a 50” Smart TV, and a private bathroom with a shower. A hallway door opens to a screened porch with a dining table and a covered patio with a 65” Smart TV and cushioned wicker chairs. Guests can stretch out in the sun on a chaise lounge chair or take a refreshing dip in the pool.

Pale wood floors, white shiplap walls, and elegant furnishings in the open living, dining, and kitchen space on the second floor create a soothing environment for guests. French doors from the living area open to a covered porch with views of the neighborhood and the Gulf. In the living area, a sectional sofa and 2 armchairs face a 75” Smart TV. A dining table seats 7 and enjoys beautiful views of Seagrove Beach. A chef’s kitchen is perfection with a waterfall marble island that seats 3, a farmhouse sink, 2 dishwashers, a gas cooktop, and stainless-steel appliances. A door off the living area opens to a guest bedroom with a queen bed and built-in twin-over-twin bunk beds with storage drawers. A private bathroom has a shower/tub combo and double vanity.

A primary bedroom and a guest bedroom are on the third floor. The guest bedroom has a king bed, a 50” Smart TV, and a private bathroom with a shower. Guests staying in the primary bedroom will enjoy a private balcony overlooking Seagrove Beach, a sitting area with 2 armchairs, and a walk-in closet. A king bed faces a 65” Smart TV in a room filled with natural light. A lovely private bathroom has marble tile floors, a double vanity, a make-up bench, and a water closet. A soaking tub enjoys views of Seagrove and the Gulf, and a glass-enclosed shower has 2 showerheads.

A lookout tower on the fourth floor has beautiful Gulf views and is partially covered. Toast sunset from a high-top table that lets guests enjoy meals beneath the stars. A covered outdoor living area has a sofa and chairs surrounding a 65” Smart TV.

Guests will love the elegant finishes, porches, Gulf views, and the private pool at How Sweet It Is. It’s easy to explore Seagrove Beach and nearby restaurants and shops on the 6-passenger golf cart or 4 included adult bikes. Book How Sweet It Is today. With this location, it won’t last long!

Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom With Shower Only

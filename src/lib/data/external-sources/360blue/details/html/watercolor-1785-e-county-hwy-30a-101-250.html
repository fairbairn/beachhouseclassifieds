WaterColor 1785 E. County Hwy 30A #101
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 2
Beds 3
Sleeps 6
Full Baths 2
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
*Per Watercolor HOA, please include all guests aged 5 and older in your total count of adult guests to receive amenity and pool access wristbands. Sleep arrangements are provided as a courtesy and are not considered in the occupancy count.
**This home is allowed 6 amenity access bands.**

- 5 minute walk to the beach, the Watercolor Beach Club, nature trails and dune lakes
- Sonos Premium Sound Bar in family room and master bedroom
- Screened porch
- Located in desirable WaterColor
- Washer/Dryer
- Convenient to tennis courts, and 6 community pools
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: This 2-story condo is located in Watercolor less than a minute walk to the beach, shops, and the Watercolor BeachClub. On the first floor, the home offers a primary bedroom with a King bed, wall-mounted 55” Smart TV, a spacious walk-in closet, and a screened-in porch with a porch swing. Enjoy the music on your smartphone with a wireless Sonos system. There is a large primary bath with double vanity, jetted tub, and a separate shower featuring new stone, tile, countertops, flooring and faucets. The guest bedroom has a king bed, wall-mounted Smart TV, and renovated full bath with tub and shower. There is also a washer/dryer on the first floor.

On the second floor, the sun-drenched living space opens to a porch that is great for people watching or dining. The family room with a large Smart TV and SONOS speaker provides a casual spot to relax in overstuffed chairs and a large sofa (queen sleeper). The kitchen has new granite countertops and tile backsplash and is fully equipped with a new Sub-Zero refrigerator, Bosch dishwasher, ceramic cooktop, stainless oven, microwave, and glass-front cabinets. The kitchen opens to the dining area with a table that comfortably seats 6. There is also a newly renovated private half bath on the second floor for your convenience.

FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Tub
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

SECOND FLOOR:
- Living Room: Queen Sleeper

*Watercolor Community HOA prohibits the rental of Low-Speed Vehicles for guests staying in the Town Center.

*Guests who are 25 years of age or younger must be accompanied by a responsible parent or guardian in any vacation rental managed by Callista by 360 Blue. We do not rent homes to those 25 years of age or younger who are unaccompanied by a responsible parent or guardian. Moreover, there must be a ratio of at least (1) parent or guardian over the age of 25 for every four (4) young adults who are 25 years of age or younger.

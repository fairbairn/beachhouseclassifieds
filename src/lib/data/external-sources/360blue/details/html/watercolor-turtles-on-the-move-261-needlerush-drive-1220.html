Watercolor "Turtles on the Move" 261 Needlerush Drive
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 5
Beds 10
Sleeps 14
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 14 wristbands.
- Private heated pool and sitting spa **Pool heating available Oct 1-May 1 for $35 per day**Pool and sitting spa are heated together
- Located in the Lake District of WaterColor
- Newly renovated as of June 2023
- Access to community pools, nature trails, and dune lakes
- Conveniently located near Publix & restaurants
- 5 Adult Bikes
- 6-Seater Low-Speed Vehicle
- Sprawling Porches
- Propane Grill
- 4 parking spots
- 2.2 miles to Seaside
- 1.7 miles to WaterColor Beach Club and Beach Access
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Experience an elegant stay at Turtles on the Move located in the Lake district of WaterColor. This beautiful 3-story home offers 5 bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms, and sleeps 14. Conveniently situated, this home is just minutes from the beaches of the Gulf and a short walk to multiple community pools. Guests also have access to the luxurious amenities of the WaterColor community.

Turtles on the Move welcomes guests through the spacious porch entryway into the first floor of this stunning Gulf home. Here guests will find 4 dreamy bedrooms, including a primary suite with lavish soaking tub, 2 guest rooms with king-sized beds, and bunk room thoughtfully outfitted with 2 twin-over-twin beds.

A grand staircase leads to the second floor of this luxurious home. The spacious open-concept living, dining, and kitchen areas offer plenty of natural lighting and high ceilings with exposed wooden beams. The gourmet kitchen has stainless steel appliances, a farmhouse-style sink, marble countertops with island, microwave, and beverage cooler. The dining table seats 10 guests, and the living room offers 2 comfortable sofas, club chairs, and TV. It is an ideal gathering space for family and friends! Adjacent to the main area is a relaxing queen bedroom with shared bath.

The third-floor loft features an additional living space with queen sleeper sofa, twin-over-queen bunk beds, and a half bath.

This stately home offers spacious wraparound porches on the first and second floors. On the porches, guests will find plenty of outdoor seating, and a relaxing swing. Enjoy your time outside with the private heated pool and sitting spa, giving your family and friends a place to relax after coming back from a day at the beach!

Explore the WaterColor area on the 5 adult bikes or 6-seater Low-Speed Vehicle included with the home, and enjoy the sights. Don’t wait to book Turtles on the Move! Book today!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS:
FIRST FLOOR:
- Primary Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower and Soaking Tub
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
- Bunk Room: Two Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds

SECOND FLOOR:
- Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower Only

THIRD FLOOR:
- Bunk Room: Twin over Queen Bunk Bed
- Additional Sleeping: Queen Sleeper Sofa
- Half Bath: Toilet Only

PLEASE NOTE: Effective March 15, 2014, Low-Speed Vehicle rentals in WaterColor must be made through The Electric Cart Company and will be limited to 80 vehicles throughout the community at any given time. Only one cart may be rented per home and rentals will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis.

** Unfortunately, this home does not allow pets at any time.
** Unfortunately the fireplace is not for guest use.
** Unfortunately the ladder leading to the roof is not for guest use.

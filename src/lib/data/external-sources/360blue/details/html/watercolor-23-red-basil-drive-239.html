WaterColor 23 Red Basil Drive
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Short Term Rental #STR-035564
Bedrooms 5
Beds 9
Sleeps 12
Full Baths 4
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
- Per WaterColor HOA, all guests 5 years of age or older must be included in your adult guest total in order to receive wristbands to access amenities and pools. This home is allotted 12 wristbands.
- Located in The Crossings district of WaterColor
- 5 adult bikes provided
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - ALL linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

Guests staying in WaterColor will have access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club. It features a new main pool and lounge area, second pool deck with additional lounge seating, and third pool with zero-entry, as well an enhanced WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. All have scenic views of the Gulf of Mexico.

DETAILS: Situated in The Crossings at WaterColor, this stunning 5-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom retreat offers a luxurious 30A getaway, accommodating up to 12 guests within 2,517 square feet of thoughtfully designed living space. Guests will love the resort-style amenities of this coastal haven, including 4 sparkling pools, tennis courts, and 499 acres of lush parks, scenic gardens, and walking trails. Unwind on the tranquil screened-in porch and breezy second-floor balcony, or enjoy a quick 4-minute bike ride or scenic 14-minute walk to the breathtaking Emerald Coast beaches.

A beautiful screened-in front porch welcomes guests to this charming 2-story property, offering inviting seating, including rocking chairs and a cozy dinette set for 2, with a ceiling fan gently circulating the air. Shiplap walls accented by natural colors and textures create a subtle coastal-inspired environment in the great room. Soft natural light fills the living area, highlighting the comfortable seating and serene coastal-inspired environment. Settle into the sofa and 2 plush armchairs for quiet moments or gather with loved ones around the sleek TV for movie nights. Pristine white cabinets and sleek countertops provide a crisp, clean aesthetic in the gourmet kitchen, while high-end appliances make it easy to prepare meals. Socialize with friends at the 4-stool breakfast bar or craft a cocktail at the wet bar before enjoying a delightful meal at the 8-person dining table. Retire to the peaceful primary bedroom, featuring a comfy king bed and private bathroom. A convenient washer and dryer keep beach gear fresh, while the outdoor shower helps rinse off sandy feet. After a day on the water, return home to grill fresh fish on the BBQ.

Unwind on the second-floor landing, which features a cozy living area for relaxed entertainment. This floor also features 2 bunk rooms that are perfect for groups—one includes a twin-over-twin bed, while the other boasts a unique configuration of 2 twin-over-twin-over-twin beds. Two guest bedrooms with king beds and private bathrooms complete this floor—one opens to a partially covered balcony with incredible views!

23 Red Basil Drive enjoys access to the newly expanded WaterColor Beach Club—featuring a new main pool, second pool deck with lounge seating, and a third zero-entry pool. Check out the delicious WaterColor Grill, and new sunset bar, Costa Chica. Enjoy on-site parking, 5 complimentary bikes, and nearby beach access at Seagrove Beach East. Book now!

SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (Sleeps 12):
FIRST FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only

SECOND FLOOR:
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower Only
-Guest Bedroom: King Bed, Private Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
-Guest Bedroom: Twin-over-Twin Bunk Bed, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo
-Guest Bedroom: 2 Sets of Twin-over-Twin-over-Twin Bunk Beds, Shared Bathroom with Shower/Tub Combo

*Weddings are not allowed at this home. If this policy is violated, you may be asked to vacate the property and forfeit any rental payments, as well as be subject to HOA, community, and 360 Blue fines.

*Garage is not for guest use.

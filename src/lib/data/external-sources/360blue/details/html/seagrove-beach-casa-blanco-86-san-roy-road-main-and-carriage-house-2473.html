Seagrove Beach "Casa Blanco" 86 San Roy Road- Main and Carriage House
Santa Rosa Beach, FL
Bedrooms 8
Beds 12
Sleeps 22
Full Baths 7
Half Baths 1
HOME HIGHLIGHTS:
*Newly constructed home*
- Lake front
- Gulf views
- Private heated Infinity pool and sitting spa **Pool heating available October 1-May 1 for $100 per day**
- Complimentary beach setups provided each morning include 2 beach chairs and 1 umbrella at the Walton Dunes Beach Access, located just a 5-minute drive from the house (service provided March 1–October 31)
- 2 Yolo paddle boards
- 6-passenger low-speed vehicle
- 8 adult bikes
- Wine bar
- Multiple Garages
- Full office
- Complete Clean Linen Participant - All linens, including comforter covers, are laundered upon every checkout

DETAILS: Discover the epitome of seaside sophistication at Casa Blanco, a lakefront Main House and Carriage House in the beloved Seagrove Beach community, accommodating 22 guests in luxurious comfort. Custom-built with 8 bedrooms, 7.5 bathrooms, a private pool, sitting spa, and media room, this exceptional home promises a lavish getaway for large groups or multi-family trips to 30A. Imagine slow-paced afternoons by the pool, paddling across Eastern Lake, or swimming in the turquoise Gulf—less than 1 mile from beach access!

A sophisticated fusion of modern and Mediterranean design, this sprawling 3-level home features sandy beige and bleached wood décor for an ethereal, beachy atmosphere. The stately living space, furnished with soft couches, boucle armchairs, and a Smart TV, flows effortlessly into the opulent wine bar and a gourmet kitchen with top-of-the-line appliances. Indulge in catered meals of succulent seafood at the elegant dining table for 12, with additional seating for 5 at the spacious breakfast bar. Down the hall, a stylishly adorned media room invites laughter-filled movie nights. Step outside to savor the peaceful ambiance poolside, unwind in the sitting spa, or enjoy an alfresco dining experience in the outdoor kitchen. When it’s time to retire, a lavish primary bedroom, outfitted with a plush king bed, private bathroom, and fireplace, awaits on this level.

An open staircase leads to the second floor, where a private suite awaits, with a living, dining, and kitchen area—and its own private entrance. Here, guests can relax in 2 guest bedrooms with king beds and a shared bathroom. Inside the second floor of the main house is a Peloton-equipped gym and office space for added convenience, alongside 1 guest bedroom with a king bed, twin-over-twin bunk bed, and private bathroom. The primary bedroom on this level is opulently appointed with a king bed, fireplace, and balcony access for great views!

The third floor hosts a sumptuous lounge area with a wet bar, enclosed balcony featuring matching porch swings for blissful unwinding, as well as 2 guest bedrooms with king beds and private bathrooms, and 1 bunk room with 2 twin-over-full beds.

Casa Blanco delivers an unforgettable Seagrove Beach experience with a prime location for beach-lake adventures. Explore the area with on-site parking for 8 vehicles, a 6-passenger low speed vehicle, and 8 complimentary adult bikes—or take to the water on the 2 provided paddleboards. Enjoy nearby Walton Dunes Beach Access—only 4 minutes away by bike and 5 minutes by car! Book today!

Seagrove Beach "Casa Blanco" 86 San Roy Road- Main and Carriage House
Santa Rosa Beach, FL

- Waterfront home with private dock

- 4 living rooms + 2 full kitchens + 3 fireplaces

DETAILS: Among the largest luxury lakefront homes on Eastern Lake, Casa Blanco is a lavish custom-built estate spanning more than 8,600 square feet across a double lot in Seagrove Beach. Offering rare Eastern Lake and Gulf views from every level, this extraordinary Main House and Carriage House accommodate up to 22 guests with 8 bedrooms, 7.5 bathrooms, multiple living areas, 2 full kitchens, a private pool, lovely outdoor spaces, and more. Perfectly positioned on Eastern Lake, spend mornings fishing from the private dock and afternoons paddleboarding across the lake—or enjoy an evening stroll to the beach, only steps from San Roy's Neighborhood 11A Beach Access!

A sophisticated fusion of modern and Mediterranean design, this sprawling 3-story home boasts breathtaking water views from every level and an ethereal coastal aesthetic. The open layout seamlessly connects the great room and wine bar to the gourmet kitchen with professional-grade appliances, a 12-person dining table, and 5-stool breakfast bar. Down the hall, a stylish media room invites laughter-filled movie nights. Step outside to savor the poolside ambiance, unwind in the sitting spa, or enjoy an alfresco dining experience in the outdoor kitchen. When it’s time to retire, the first-floor primary bedroom provides the ultimate retreat with a king bed, private spa-inspired bathroom, fireplace, and private entrance with an outdoor shower—perfect for rinsing off after the beach!

An open staircase leads to a Peloton-equipped gym, office space, and laundry room, alongside the second-floor primary with a king bed, fireplace, private bathroom, and balcony overlooking Eastern Lake. There’s also a family-friendly guest bedroom featuring a king bed, twin-over-twin bunk nook, private bathroom, and balcony access. Use the private or shared entrance to access the Carriage House—a Palm Beach-inspired residence with more than 1,100 square feet of living, dining, and kitchen space, plus 2 guest bedrooms with king beds and laundry.

The third floor is perfect for entertaining, boasting a spacious living area with a wet bar, refrigerator, and freezer next to the oversized balcony with matching porch swings and breathtaking views. Unwind in the 2 guest bedrooms with king beds and private bathrooms, or enjoy the all-ages bunk room with 2 XL twin-over-full beds and private bathroom.

Endless fun awaits at Casa Blanco, boasting 2 paddleboards, 8 bikes, a low-speed vehicle, and parking for 8. Walk to San Roy or Sugar Dunes beach access, or drive 5 minutes to Walton Dunes for complimentary chair setup. Book now!

WaterColor Cottage Retreat | Sleeps 10

Description

Welcome to WaterColor Waves, a beautifully renovated Florida cottage in the highly sought-after WaterColor community of Santa Rosa Beach. This single-story home combines coastal charm with modern finishes, featuring 3 spacious bedrooms and 3 bathrooms. Perfectly located just 0.3 miles from the WaterColor Beach Club — only a 2-minute bike ride or 5–7 minute walk — you’ll also be steps from the Boathouse and Seaside. This retreat is ideal for families or couples looking to enjoy 30A’s premier beach destination.

The Space

Step inside to soaring beamed ceilings, natural light, and an open-concept living area designed for comfort. A cozy living room anchors the space, while the updated kitchen is fully stocked with high-end appliances and granite counter tops — perfect for home-cooked meals. The layout is ideal for a relaxing vacation, with all bedrooms on one floor: Primary Bedroom – King bed, en-suite bathroom with soaking tub, double vanity, and walk-in shower. Guest Bedroom 1 – King bed with private bathroom. Guest Bedroom 2 – Custom Full over Full Bunk Bed, full bathroom nearby. Hallway- Twin over Twin Custom Built-In Bunk Bed Enjoy both a spacious front porch and a screened-in back porch, perfect for morning coffee or evening cocktails. Highlights Sleeps 10 3 Bedrooms / 3 Bathrooms Single-story cottage layout (1,680 sqft) Recently renovated with designer finishes Screened back porch + spacious front patio Vaulted ceilings Parking for 2 vehicles Outdoor Space Outdoor Grill Unwind on the porches or stroll along the walking paths that connect directly to Seaside. Guests also enjoy easy access to tennis courts, playgrounds, and the community pools.

Guest Access

Guests have full access to the entire cottage, including: Front porch and back screened in porch Full kitchen and dining area Laundry room with washer/dryer Water Color community amenities: Beach Club, pools, tennis, Boathouse, Camp Water Color, and more.

Neighborhood

Water Color is one of 30A’s most exclusive communities, known for its pristine beaches, resort-style amenities, and charming coastal architecture. From your doorstep, you’re just minutes from Seaside’s shops and dining, as well as bike trails, fishing spots, and kayaking at Western Lake as well as a quick walk to the Water Color Beach club.

Interaction

Our team is available throughout your stay to ensure a seamless experience. Message us anytime with questions or concerns, and we’ll respond quickly.

Other things to note

The primary renter must be 25+ years old. No pets allowed. Complimentary digital guidebook provided after booking.

Getting Around

Parking is available for 2 vehicles. Biking is the preferred way to explore WaterColor and Seaside — golf cart rentals are also available in the area. The nearest airport is Destin–Fort Walton Beach (VPS), about 40 minutes away.

Seaglass Villa: Bikes, Golf Cart & Beach Access!

Description

Discover your dream getaway in this pristine coastal home in NatureWalk, featuring a relaxed atmosphere and elegant design. Enjoy beautiful views from spacious porches, access to heated pools, and a prime location near Scenic 30A. Conveniently located near WaterColor and Seaside, providing easy access to activities while ensuring a peaceful retreat.

The Space

This relaxed and elegant coastal home is perfectly situated in a premier corner location within the NatureWalk community in Seagrove, Florida. Overlooking an expansive lawn, serene pond, and shaded by lush trees, the home offers a peaceful retreat while still placing you minutes from the vibrant heart of Scenic 30A. Bordering WaterColor and just five minutes from Seaside, this location strikes the perfect balance between convenience and tranquility. The home features two master suites with king beds—one conveniently located on the main floor—making it an ideal setup for families or groups. With accommodations for up to eight guests, you'll find a fully appointed kitchen with stainless steel appliances, luxury interiors, resort-quality linens, and high-speed internet with cable and streaming channels. Two spacious covered porches provide outdoor seating, a foosball table, and beautiful views of the surrounding green space. Professionally managed by Luxe30A, a boutique property management company, the home is maintained with a high standard of care. Luxe30A is dedicated to guest satisfaction, providing 24-hour support and ensuring a smooth and memorable experience. Guests are asked to enjoy and respect the space as they make lasting memories. As part of the NatureWalk community, guests have access to an impressive list of resort-style amenities including: Seasonally heated pools and hot tubs Fire pit, sand volleyball court, sport courts, and hammocks A network of nature trails through the nearby Point Washington State Forest A private "Lilly Lane" beach trail leading to 30A’s world-renowned beaches Nestled between the preserves of Point Washington State Forest and the WaterColor Beach Resort, NatureWalk is a 155-acre master-planned community designed to rejuvenate the soul and spirit. The Gathering Place, NatureWalk’s amenity hub, features two pools, shaded lounges, and plenty of space to relax. You’re also within biking distance to dining, shopping, and Publix at WaterColor Crossing.

Guest Access

Guests of Seaglass Villa have exclusive access to the entire home, including the two master suites (one on the main floor), fully equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances, luxury interiors, and resort-quality linens. Enjoy the comfort of two spacious covered porches with outdoor seating and a foosball table. As part of the NatureWalk community, you'll have access to a range of resort-style amenities, including seasonally heated pools and hot tubs, a fire pit, sand volleyball court, sport courts, and hammocks. Explore the network of nature trails or take the private 'Lilly Lane' beach trail to reach the world-renowned beaches of 30A. Bikes are available for exploring the area at your leisure.

Neighborhood

Hidden between the preserves of the Point Washington State Forest and the Watercolor Beach Resort lay 30A’s newest gem, NatureWalk. A 155 acre master planned resort community built to enhance your vacation experience and rejuvenate the soul and spirit. Surrounded by the area’s natural beauty and abundant nature trails, NatureWalk, invokes a sense of sanctuary and tranquility in all of its guests. The community is within biking distance of our famous sugar white sand beaches and within steps of all the dining, shopping, and fitness options located at WaterColor Crossing (including a Publix). At the heart of NatureWalk is “The Gathering Place”, a vacationer’s wonderland complete with 2 pools, hot tubs, fire pit & hammocks.

Getting Around

Getting around Seaglass Villa is a breeze! While a car is recommended for exploring the area, you'll find free parking right on the premises. For those looking to venture out without driving, the community offers bike rentals, perfect for leisurely rides to nearby shops, restaurants, and the beach. The closest major airport is Northwest Florida Beaches International Airport (ECP), approximately a 30-minute drive away. Consider using ride-sharing services or taxis for convenient transportation to and from the airport or other destinations along 30A.

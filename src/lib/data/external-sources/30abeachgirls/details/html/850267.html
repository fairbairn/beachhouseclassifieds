Welcome to Emerald Lagoon, a bright and inviting one-bedroom retreat just minutes from the sandy shores of Panama City Beach. Whether you're here for a relaxing beach getaway or an action-packed vacation, this charming condo offers everything you need for a memorable stay.
Step inside to find a sunny living room with comfortable seating and a TV, creating the perfect space for winding down after a day of adventure. The fully equipped kitchen makes it easy to prepare home-cooked meals or grab quick snacks before heading to the beach. Sleeping options include a comfortable queen bed and a twin-over-twin bunk bed, bringing the total occupancy to four.
Enjoy the outdoors on the private patio, complete with outdoor seating and a fan to catch the ocean breeze. Emerald Lagoon provides access to a range of community amenities, including a pool, a lagoon pier, and tennis courts for some friendly competition.
Located just minutes from the beach and nearby local attractions, including shops, dining, and entertainment, you'll have everything you need to experience Panama City at its best.
AvantStay provides a personalized hospitality experience to elevate your stay. With our Concierge Service, guests have access to our tech-enabled services like fridge stocking, private chefs, massages, transportation, special occasion celebrations, baby gear rentals, ski gear, beach gear, and more. For anything you need, we're at your fingertips.
Panama City Beach is one of Florida's most welcoming coastal destinations, known for its 27 miles of sugar-white sand, emerald Gulf waters, and a vibrant mix of family-friendly attractions, waterfront dining, and outdoor adventure. Guests can spend their days exploring St. Andrews State Park, spotting dolphins on Shell Island excursions, savoring fresh seafood at local institutions like Schooners, or enjoying the lively entertainment and shopping at Pier Park. With its easygoing atmosphere, year-round sunshine, and endless blend of relaxation and recreation, PCB offers something for every type of traveler---from beach lovers and boaters to families, foodies, and groups looking to unwind by the water.
Local Attractions: Pier Park, Dolly Pirate Ship, Top Golf, Shipwreck Island Waterpark, Russell-Fields Pier, St. Andrews State Park, Thomas Drive, Shell Island, Conservation Park, WonderWorks, Ripley's Believe It or Not, Camp Helen State Park, Frank Brown Park, Panama City Beach Winery, Aaron Bessant Park, Laguna Beach

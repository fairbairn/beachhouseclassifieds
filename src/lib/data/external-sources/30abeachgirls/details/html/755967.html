Welcome to Bluewater Bliss & Seeley by the Sea by AvantStay! These two homes are right across the street from each other, with a combined total of 9 bedrooms, sleeping 20 guests---an ideal option for family trips or groups looking to spread out.
Bluewater Bliss:
Step into your very own oasis of Bluewater Bliss, where every moment is infused with the essence of relaxation and joy. Nestled amidst the picturesque landscape of Blue Mountain Beach, this charming new beach home beckons you with its promise of tranquility and comfort. With ample space to accommodate up to 10 guests, it serves as the perfect sanctuary for families and friends alike. Located in the heart of one of 30A's newest communities, this stunning beach retreat boasts a backyard porch that invites you to immerse yourself in the beauty of the outdoors. Picture yourself grilling the day's freshly caught seafood while laughter fills the air and memories are made. Whether you choose to spend your days soaking up the sun on the pristine shores or strolling along the sandy white beaches in search of the perfect sunset, every moment promises to be unforgettable. Inside, the home reveals itself as a haven of comfort and entertainment. Two distinct living areas cater to both adults and children, ensuring that everyone finds their own slice of paradise. While the grown-ups unwind in one space, the kids can indulge in endless hours of fun in their dedicated area, complete with an arcade game that sparks excitement and laughter. From the moment you step foot into Bluewater Bliss, you'll find yourself swept away by its undeniable charm and the promise of unforgettable moments spent in the company of loved ones while enjoying life's simple pleasures.
Seeley by the Sea:
Welcome to your dream getaway! This spacious, light-filled home offers the perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and coastal charm. Inside, you'll find bright, open-concept living spaces with high ceilings and stylish decor, creating a breezy, inviting atmosphere. The gourmet kitchen features sleek quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a large island perfect for cooking, gathering, or enjoying a casual meal. With five beautifully appointed bedrooms---including a luxurious primary suite with a spa-style bathroom---everyone will have their own peaceful space to unwind. Multiple full bathrooms offer walk-in showers, double vanities, and even a soaking tub for the ultimate relaxation. For added convenience, the home includes in-home laundry, high-speed Wi-Fi, smart TVs, and all the comforts of home. Step outside to enjoy covered balconies and porches where you can sip your morning coffee or wind down at sunset. A private grill and outdoor seating area make it easy to enjoy alfresco dining or a casual barbecue. Whether you're unwinding on the balcony with a glass of wine, preparing a beachside brunch in the gourmet kitchen, or sinking into plush beds after a sun-soaked day, this home is designed to make your stay unforgettable!
ABOUT THE AREA: Blue Mountain Beach is a quaint coastal community along 30A between Grayton Beach and Gulf Place. Known for it's laid back vibe yet with all the amenities we look for in a beach vacation. Just steps to the Blue Mountain Regional Beach Access and lots of restaurants like Red Fish Taco, and the Blue Mountain Creamery (best ice cream) nearby. And if you love cinnamon rolls, grab a cup of coffee and a cinnamon roll from Blue Mountain Bakery, you won't regret it. What more could you ask for on your 30a vacation....biking or short golf cart ride distance to the beach and ice-cream!
BEACH ACCESS: Just a 15-minute walk or quick bike or golf cart ride from the house to Blue Mountain Regional Beach Access. There are restrooms, shower, some parking and coolers, wagons and single inflatable toys are permitted, as well as kayaks and paddle boards.

30A is a 20-mile stretch of beaches and coastal towns along Florida's famous Emerald Coast. From Seaside down past Laguna, you'll find white sands, azure waters, and laid-back vibes. This region takes a relaxed approach to oceanfront living, with boutique shops and local seafood on every main street. Each town along 30A has its own unique identity, so we encourage you to explore them all!

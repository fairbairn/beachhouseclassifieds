ABOUT THE HOME: Totally relax and make beautiful beach memories and traditions starting here, at Totally Beachin. Located in the Seacrest Beach neighborhood between Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach on famous 30A, boasts 8,400 square feet of living space will have you recharged in no time. With room to comfortably accommodate 18, everyone will find a comfortable place to stay in one of the 5 large king suites and the bunk room with 4 bunk beds with a total of 8 twin beds. Other features of this stunner of a home include 2 fully equipped kitchens, 2 living rooms and dining rooms, 2 outdoor built-in grills (one in the screened-in patio with a refrigerator and television), and 10 porches and patios.
ABOUT THE AREA: Your days could be spent relaxing at the sugar sand beach or hanging by the resort-style swimming pool less than 5-minute walk away -- no matter what you do, Totally Beachin is designed with your comfort in mind. This home is amid everything you'll love about 30A -- restaurants, cafes, boutiques and art galleries, all no more than a 5-minute walk away! It's just minutes from the private deeded beach, the pool, and biking trails that will have you passing by some of the most beautiful scenery in the world. The coastal dune lakes are rare and magnificent. This home is ideal for family and friends that are getting together to have an unforgettable experience. Luxury speaks loudly, but Totally Beachin also has the feeling of "home" from every room in this vacation house.
AMENITY ACCESS: In order to park and have access to Seacrest Beach amenities, guests are required to complete the online Seacrest Beach HOA Guest Registration packet, prior to their arrival. A link will be sent after the reservation is made. Upon arrival, you can pick up your parking passes and wristbands at the Guest Service Window by the lagoon pool. Guests can enter the lagoon area amenity complex at any of the six gates. There will be a queue outside of the Guest Services office marked by safety yellow beach umbrellas.
Attention Guests, please note that there is an additional fee of $2 per day per guest staying at this property in Seacrest Beach. We are required by the Seacrest Beach HOA to collect this fee in order for you to stay in this community and use their amenities. We will collect this fee prior to your arrival.
BEACH ACCESS: Seacrest Beach community features a private beach on the south side of 30A. There are tram stops though out the community that operate on demand. Upon entering the beach to the left (east), there is onsite chair rental service. La Dolce Vita is the exclusive provider at Seacrest Beach. Guests may bring their own chairs to the beach for set up on the right (west) side of the beach. Prohibited items at the private beach access: wagons, wheeled coolers, tents, and canopies.

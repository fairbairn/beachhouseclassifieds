ABOUT THE HOME: Step into your very own "Piece Of Heaven". This lovely beach home, comfortably sleeps 14 guests and is located in the heart of Blue Mountain Beach. This beautiful, pet friendly, beach home is located south of 30A in a quiet cul-de-sac with a backyard oasis and private pool! Truly a one-of-a-kind property on 30A featuring 3 bedrooms, 3 balconies and a large rooftop balcony made for kicking back in the sun or taking in the gulf breeze. The home is tastefully furnished and ready to make great beach memories.
ABOUT THE AREA: Blue Mountain Beach is a quaint coastal community along 30A between Grayton Beach and Gulf Place. Known for it's laid back vibe yet with all the amenities we look for in a beach vacation. Just steps to the Blue Mountain Regional Beach Access and lots of restaurants like Red Fish Taco, Blue Mable Smokehouse and the Blue Mountain Creamery (best ice cream) nearby. And if you love cinnamon rolls, grab a cup of coffee and a cinnamon roll from Blue Mountain Bakery, you won't regret it. What more could you ask for on your 30a vacation....walking distance to the beach...private pool and ice-cream!
BEACH ACCESS: Just a quick walk from the house to Blue Mountain Regional Beach Access. There are restrooms, shower, some parking and coolers, wagons and single inflatable toys are permitted, as well as kayaks and paddle boards.

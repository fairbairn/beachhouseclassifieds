Description

Welcome to “Blessings By the Sea” – our renovated 4-bedroom, 4.5-bathroom home in WaterColor.

LOCATION:
  • Located in the Crossing District of WaterColor - one of the most highly sought-after communities along Scenic Highway 30A
  • On a quiet street just around the corner from the Dragonfly Pool
  • 4-minute golf cart ride or 5-minute bike ride to the WaterColor Beach Club
  • Minutes from the town centers of WaterColor and Seaside featuring restaurants, shopping, and family activities

PROPERTY OVERVIEW:

1st Floor:
  • Open concept kitchen, dining, and living area
  • Dining table seating for eight with an additional four seats at the large kitchen island
  • Gourmet kitchen with stainless steel appliances
  • Spacious screened-in porch with seating
  • Bedroom 1: Primary owner suite: King bed + fully-renovated bathroom (walk-in shower, double vanity)
  • Office nook
  • Fully-renovated powder room
  • Laundry room

2nd Floor:
  • Living area with flat-screen TV
  • Bedroom 2: Secondary owner suite: King bed + twin bed with trundle (sleeps up to 4), flat-screen TV, and private balcony with porch swing. Fully-renovated bathroom (walk-in shower)
  • Bedroom 3: King bed with fully-renovated bathroom (walk-in shower, double vanity)
  • Bedroom 4: Bunk room: Custom twin beds (x2)
  • Fully remodeled hallway bathroom (shower/tub combo)

AMENITIES:
  • 6-Seater Golf Cart included with rental
  • Four premium beach cruisers
  • Access to WaterColor Beach Club (amenity passes for up to 10 guests)
  • Access to all six heated WaterColor pools
  • Private 2nd floor balcony with porch swing
  • Outdoor shower
  • Coffee makers: Keurig + standard drip
  • Community green space directly behind home

To enhance your stay, we also provide a starter supply of premium, eco-friendly guest essentials, including:

Body Wash, Shampoo & Conditioner
Hand Soap, Dish Soap & Dishwasher Pods
Toilet Paper, Paper Towels & Facial Tissues
️ Trash Bags & Dish Sponge
Laundry Detergent

GUEST SUPPORT:

Need assistance? Coast’s dedicated management team is available to help with anything you need—just give us a call!

IMPORTANT NOTES:

Age Requirement: The primary guest must be 25 or older to book.
Parking: The home comes with driveway parking for a car, as well as community parking permits.
Check-In Policy: Please do not arrive before your scheduled check-in time.
No Pets & No Smoking: This home has a strict no-pet policy. Unauthorized pets will result in immediate eviction and loss of rental fees, with the guest responsible for any damages. The home is also strictly non-smoking.

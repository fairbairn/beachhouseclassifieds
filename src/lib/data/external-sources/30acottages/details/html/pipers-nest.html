Description

PIPERS NEST
Rosemary Beach Community
East Long Green Road, Rosemary Beach, FL 32461

Home Highlights

  • 4 Bedrooms (see “Room Details” tab)
  • 3.5 Bathrooms
  • Sleeps up to 12 Guests
  • 2,700+ sq. ft. of indoor space
  • Fully stocked chef’s kitchen with premium appliances
  • Open-concept living & dining areas
  • Private walled courtyard with one of the largest pools in Rosemary Beach
  • Circulating spa/hot tub (no jets) — pool and spa cannot be heated simultaneously
  • Screened-in second-floor porch overlooking the pool
  • Gas grill & multiple outdoor seating areas
  • Laundry room with washer & dryer
  • Two-vehicle driveway + 1 overflow space (1 community parking pass issued)
  • Four 26” adult bikes included (seasonal: Mar–Nov)
  • Indoor fireplace is decorative only
  • Note: Bedrooms are on the ground floor. Pool alarms installed; no pool fence.

Luxury Retreat on 30A
Escape to Pipers Nest, a stylish 4-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom vacation home in the heart of Rosemary Beach. This 2,700+ sq. ft. retreat comfortably sleeps up to 12, perfect for families, group getaways, or special celebrations.

Designed for Comfort & Style
The fully equipped chef’s kitchen with premium appliances makes entertaining effortless. Open living and dining areas flow to a screened-in second-floor porch, offering serene views of your private pool and spa within a lush, walled-in courtyard. Multiple outdoor seating areas and a gas grill create the perfect environment for relaxing or entertaining under the Florida sun.

Rest & Relaxation
Four beautifully appointed bedrooms offer flexible sleeping arrangements. The serene primary suite is ideal for unwinding, while the additional bedrooms and a den with a daybed provide cozy spaces for kids, friends, or quiet reading. An on-site washer and dryer make packing light easy.

Outdoor Oasis & Prime Location
Enjoy your private pool and circulating spa, or explore Rosemary Beach on seasonal bikes provided with your stay. Stroll across the street to East Long Green Park, or wander to the beach and Rosemary Town Center with its shops, dining, and coastal charm.

Amenities You’ll Love

  • Private pool & spa in walled courtyard
  • Screened-in porch overlooking pool & greenery
  • Fully stocked kitchen with premium appliances
  • Gas grill & multiple outdoor seating areas
  • Four adult bikes (seasonal)
  • Washer & dryer, linens provided
  • Comfortable accommodations for up to 12

Why Pipers Nest?
This Rosemary Beach vacation rental blends luxury, privacy, and convenience. Walk to the beach, explore local dining and shops, or simply relax poolside — Pipers Nest is more than a rental, it’s your home base for creating unforgettable memories on Florida’s Emerald Coast.
Tip: For multiple homes nearby, check out Costa Verde Cottage.

Community Amenities

  • Four community pools (some heated seasonally)
  • Private community beach access
  • Rosemary Square: shopping & dining
  • Playground, parks, and green spaces
  • Outdoor amphitheater
  • Pedestrian paths & lush landscaping
  • Fitness center & racquet club (fee-based)
  • Note: Golf carts are prohibited in the community.

Pool & Spa Heating

  • Available Oct–Apr for an additional fee
  • Pool heating only available when outdoor temps are 50°F–80°F
  • Pool and spa cannot be heated simultaneously

Parking

  • 2 driveway spots + 1 overflow
  • 1 community parking pass issued
  • Street parking allowed in lined spaces with a valid permit
  • HOA enforces towing & fines strictly

Important Notes

  • No pets (service animals allowed with documentation)
  • Homeowners have dogs (may affect those with allergies)
  • No smoking
  • Fireplace is decorative only
  • Minimum age to rent: 25 years

Booking & Policies

  • 50% due at booking, balance due 60 days prior
  • Cancellation >60 days: Refundable minus 5% fee
  • No full refund once processed
  • Travel insurance available on request
  • Check-in: 4:00 PM CST | Check-out: 10:00 AM CST

Description

Petite Palm
Magnolia Dune Community – Sandalwood Dr, Santa Rosa Beach, FL 32459

Home Highlights:

  • 5 Bedrooms (see “Room Details” for configurations)
  • 4 Full Bathrooms
  • Sleeps 12 Guests
  • 2,341 Sq. Ft.
  • Paved Grill Area with Outdoor Shower
  • Spacious Open Floor Kitchen with Stainless Steel Appliances & Marble Countertops
  • 1st Floor King Guest Bedroom with Private Full Bath & Walk-In Shower
  • 2nd Floor King Master Suite with Walk-In Closet & Full Bath (Double Vanity & Walk-In Shower)
  • 2nd Floor King Guest Suite with Private Full Bath & Walk-In Shower
  • 2nd Floor Queen Guest Bedroom with Shared Hall Bath
  • 2nd Floor Bunk Room with Shared Hall Bath
  • Two–Three Vehicle Driveway
  • Six-Seater Golf Cart
  • Four 26" Adult Bikes Included Seasonally (Dec–Feb not included; additional rentals available)

About the Home:
Luxuriously designed by Kate Marker Interiors, Petite Palm perfectly embodies Seagrove Beach’s curated, casual coastal style. The home features four elegant adult bedrooms (three kings, one queen) plus a bonus bunk room, comfortably sleeping up to 12 guests. Vaulted ceilings and wall-mounted TVs in every bedroom, including the bunk room, create a relaxing, modern retreat.

The open-concept kitchen is a chef’s dream with marble backsplash and countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a commercial-size refrigerator/freezer. Ample island and dining seating flow seamlessly to a screened porch for outdoor dining. Three additional porches, including a double porch swing, provide inviting spots to unwind. An outdoor shower adds beachside convenience.

Explore Seagrove with four included adult bikes or take a dip in the nearby heated community pool. With two beach access points just across the street, Petite Palm offers the ultimate coastal getaway.

Community Amenities:

  • Community Pool (heated during Thanksgiving, Christmas, and March for Spring Break)
  • Two Community Beach Access Points (Beachwood Villas & Seagrove Place, 0.2 mi / 3–4 min walk; pedestrian only)
  • Closest beach access with parking: Santa Clara Regional Beach Access, 3 min drive

Golf Cart & Parking:

  • Six-seater golf cart provided for guest use (subject to availability; not guaranteed by 30A Cottages)
  • Driveway parking for 2–3 vehicles; street parking not allowed
  • No house trailers, modular structures, or travel trailers permitted

Policies & Booking Info:

  • No Smoking
  • No Pets (service animals allowed with prior notice & documentation)
  • Rates do not include tax and fees
  • 50% due at booking, balance due 60 days prior (or full if within 60 days)
  • Cancelations: 5% fee; initial 50% refundable if canceled >60 days prior
  • Optional travel insurance available (purchase before final payment)
  • Check-in: 4:00 PM CST | Check-out: 10:00 AM CST
  • Guests must be 25+ years old

Amenities

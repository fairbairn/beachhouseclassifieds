HOME HIGHLIGHTS: - AMAZING Old Seagrove location The Hickory St Beach Access marks the beginning of the largest stretch of Neighborhood Beach Access points on 30a. This means there is no public parking that leads to busy beaches, but it is accessible to you by just walking down Hickory St. You can bring your own chairs/tents/umbrellas or rent and enjoy with minimal crowds - Open air side porch perfect for entertaining - 3 KING bedrooms, 2 on the main level w/ en-suite renovated baths - 2 bikes - Outdoor shower - Wifi and streaming TVs - 6 Beach Chairs & Beach Wagon - Fully stocked kitchen complete with a standard drip coffee maker - NEW Tesla Charger on site! - Sandpiper Linen Program Participant: All linen, bedding and bath, are the highest quality. Oversized towels in each bathroom are provided. OPTIONAL ADD ON: **Optional Cancellation Protection**- Allows cancellation for ANY reason up to 48 hours in advance of stay and guarantees 50% of all monies paid returned ($150 fee applies at booking). Located just east of Seaside and only one block to the nearest beach entrance, the Lollypop House is perfectly located for your dream beach getaway in Seagrove. The house is beautiful and spacious, featuring 4 bedrooms and comfortably sleeping 12. The unique layout includes 3 king bedrooms (2 with additional beds), a four-bed bunk room, and a designated game room. The Lollypop House is best described as a multi-family house, and the owners (Lolly and Popsie) have focused on creating a vacation home that meets the wants and needs of children and adults alike. Whether you prefer indoor or outdoor living, there are spaces for everyone to congregate as well as room to escape for a little alone time. The large, covered front porch is a family favorite to drink a cup of coffee in the morning and hear the beach waves in the distance. The screened-in side porch boasts several chairs and a cozy bed swing, which makes a fantastic place for an afternoon nap! The uncovered side porch has a large table for everyone to enjoy dinner fresh off the gas grill. For those times that indoor living is best, you will love the variety of options the Lollypop House provides. Whether you choose to read a book on the couch in the entrance nook, eat a family meal at the large dining room table, or watch a movie on the 60” smart TV in the spacious living room, there is an option for you. The gourmet kitchen will not disappoint, and its open concept design allows you to prepare a meal while still engaging with the family. The stainless steel appliances, gas stove, sub-zero refrigerator, and granite counter tops will please the family chef, and there is plenty of room for a few sous chefs to help as well! Just off of the living room is the king master suite, including a flat screen TV, private bathroom with a spacious shower, and a separate loft space with a spiral staircase and twin bed. This room is perfect for a couple, or for an entire family unit with the kids sleeping in the loft. Upstairs you will find the third king bedroom. This room is perfect for families as well, including a nook with a twin bed, a private balcony, extra space to set up a pack and play for a little one if needed, and a wall-mounted smart TV. Just across the hall is a kids’ bunk room with 2 twin over full bunk beds, as well as plenty of space to lounge on the couch and watch TV. A third bathroom with a large shower separates the two bedrooms and completes the second story. Up the stairs and on the third story you will find the new family favorite: a game room! Whether you decide to get creative in the art corner, play video games on the smart TV, or have a family Pac Man competition on the vintage gaming console, everyone will enjoy spending time here. ACCOMMODATIONS: Main Level: Bedroom 1: King master with loft twin, ensuite bathroom Bedroom 2: King bedroom, ensuite bath Second Floor: Bedroom 3: King bedroom, ensuite twin Bedroom 4: Bunk room with two twin over full bunk beds Bedrooms 3 and 4 share a hallway bathroom FREE WIFI

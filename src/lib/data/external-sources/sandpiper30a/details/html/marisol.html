Marisol

  • 3 Bedrooms
  • 3 Baths
  • 9 Sleeps
  • 1806 sqft
Favorite
Share
Price Alert
HOME HIGHLIGHTS: - Located on one of the largest lots in Seaside - Access to the Seaside private beaches at TUPELO pavilion - You will have a total of 9 RFID access cards for access to the private beaches in Seaside. - Walking/biking distance to Seaside, beach, shops and restaurants - Private driveway plus 2 parking passes - Fully stocked kitchen complete with a standard drip coffee maker - Two adult beach cruiser bikes - Sandpiper Linen Program Participant: All linen, bedding and bath, are the highest quality. OPTIONAL ADD-ONS: - There is an additional cost to use the 3 pools and fitness center in Seaside. The cost for this cottage is $84/day. Please alert us after booking should you wish to add the "Seaside Amenity Passes" to your reservation. -Vacation Cancellation Protection- Allows cancellation for any reason up to 48 hours in advance of stay and guarantees 50% of all monies refunded ($125 fee applies at booking) Marisol is just off Tupelo Street on the east side of Seaside proper just steps to 2 of Seaside's 3 pools, the tennis courts and fitness center. The main level consists of a spacious living room with flat screen TV, dining room with seating for 8 and/or, a gourmet kitchen with stainless appliances, butcher block table with seating for 3 and bar seating for 2 more. Also on the main level is the QUEEN guest bedroom with 32 inch Flat Screen TV (not pictured) and private bath (shower only). Upstairs you'll find the QUEEN master retreat with private bath/shower combo, small private balcony off the bathroom, and a lofted space with queen bed and 42 inch TV for additional sleeping space and entertainment. The large upstairs balcony is accessible from this loft space. The kids bunk guest bedroom (sleeps 4 in a bottom full size bed with top twin bunk and twin trundle) completes the upstairs with private bath (shower only). A beautiful balcony runs the length of the upstairs and is easily accessible from all bedrooms. Enjoy views of the town of Seaside from up here or kick back and work on your tan! SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (sleeps 9) Bedroom 1: 1st floor - queen, TV (please note this bedroom is slightly open near the ceiling. It is private, but open air at the top- see pictures) Kitchen, Dining area and Living Room Bedroom 2: 2nd floor - bunk room (twin over full and twin trundle)- sleeps 4 Hall full bath, shower only Bedroom 3: 2nd floor- queen / private bathroom (bath/shower combo) Walking through the master bathroom you will find another Loft space with a queen bed and TV Located on one of Seaside's largest lots, Marisol is a gorgeous and spacious 3 bedroom/ 3bath cottage with loft space. Featuring wrap around gazebo like decks, gourmet kitchen, and a full bathroom for each bedroom. If you know Seaside, you know the best of Seaside is to be experienced outdoors and the same can be said of Marisol. Beautiful and spacious indoors and boasts wrap around balcony with 3 rocking chairs (not pictured) and lots of green space and paving to enjoy sitting out under the Seaside stars. You'll enjoy the very best of Seaside when you stay at Marisol! IMPORTANT DETAILS * Parking for 2 cars * Sorry, no pets allowed ** There is an additional cost to use the 3 pools and fitness center in Seaside. This charge is optional with Sandpiper Vacation Rentals. The cost for this cottage is $84/day. Please alert us after booking should you wish to add the "Seaside Amenity Passes" to your reservation. Seaside requires the passes to be purchased for the entirety of your stay when ordered. * Additional fees are required to reserve Pickleball and Tennis courts paid directly to the Seaside amenity Center. * Seaside Chapel with Sunday services, Walkable Town Center from rental home including shopping, restaurants and upscale grocery market. *Certificate #018896 TDT #21300 FREE WIFI

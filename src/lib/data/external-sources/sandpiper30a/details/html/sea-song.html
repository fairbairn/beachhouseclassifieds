**SNOWBIRD PROMOTION- Save 50% PER NIGHT January and February for monthly stays!** HOME HIGHLIGHTS: - Access to the private beaches in Seaside at the TUPELO pavilion - Fully stocked kitchen - Spacious upper balcony - Two adult beach cruiser bikes - Sandpiper Welcome Tote included for all rentals - You will have a total of 6 RFID access cards for access to the private beaches in Seaside. - Sandpiper Linen Program Participant: All linen, bedding and bath, are the highest quality. OPTIONAL ADD-ONS: - There is an additional cost to use the 3 pools and fitness center in Seaside. The cost for this cottage is $84/day. Please alert us after booking should you wish to add the "Seaside Amenity Passes" to your reservation. -Vacation Cancellation Protection- Allows cancellation for any reason up to 48 hours in advance of stay and guarantees 50% of all monies refunded ($125 fee applies at booking) Sea Song's whimsical, third-floor, tower, 'fish' bedroom with queen bed has a bird's eye view of Seaside rooftops and the gorgeous Gulf of Mexico. The second-floor master suite with king bed and full bath opens onto a huge, wonderful porch shaded by the trees and perfect for after-beach relaxing. Another second-floor queen bedroom has a hall bath and lots of books to peruse. Downstairs is an open living area with comfy sofa, love seat and adjoining the full kitchen with granite countertops and dining area, and a bath with an inside-outside shower. SLEEPING ARRANGEMENTS: (sleeps 6)- all bedrooms are upstairs Floor 1- great room with TV, Kitchen and eating area, Full Bath (shower only) Floor 2- Bedroom 1: KING master with private bath (tub/shower combo) with access to a beautiful HUGE outdoor sitting area Bedroom 2: QUEEN guest room with hall full bath (tub/shower combo) Floor 3- Bedroom 3: QUEEN loft with beautiful views of the town! You'll be singing your own "Sea Song when you stay in this beach cottage, right in the heart of Seaside, with huge porches and lots of outdoor seating. NEW granite countertops in the kitchen, updated furnishings and blinds. There is a 40-inch flat screen TV in the living room and additional TVs in 2 of the 3 bedrooms. Sea Song is only a two-minute walk from the glistening white sand beach and a two-minute walk to Seasides' town square, as well. The cottage is also only two doors from the Tupelo gazebo where children and adults love to gather and make new friends. If you have never visited Seaside with its landscape of white picket fences and fairy tale cottages, you must grant yourself this very special experience. You will fall in love with Seaside's extraordinary architecture, narrow brick streets, winding paths and fun things for families and friends to do. Shady Lady is a half block from tennis, swimming pools, a fitness center and children's playground, and just two streets from the center of town. There are dune lakes, golf courses and 20 miles of bike paths nearby. Seaside has movies and live concerts on the grass, live theater, an open-air market, great art galleries, all kinds of fun shops, a bookstore, great restaurants, a neat coffee shop, and the famed Modica Market with its exotic foods and outdoor cafe. Life is so laid-back and easy here. If you need room for a larger group, Beach Tune, which sleeps four and has two baths, sits right behind Sea Song and can be rented with our larger cottage. IMPORTANT DETAILS: * Parking for 2 cars * Sorry, no pets allowed ** There is an additional cost to use the 3 pools and fitness center in Seaside. This charge is optional with Sandpiper Vacation Rentals. The cost for this cottage is $84/day. Please alert us after booking should you wish to add the "Seaside Amenity Passes" to your reservation. Seaside requires the passes to be purchased for the entirety of your stay when ordered. * Additional fees are required to reserve Pickleball and Tennis courts paid directly to the Seaside amenity Center. * Seaside Chapel with Sunday services, Walkable Town Center from rental home including shopping, restaurants and upscale grocery market. Sea Song

We just left Sea Song and absolutely loved our time there! It's a 2-3 minute walk to the beach and 4-5 minute walk to the center of Seaside. The unit was extremely clean upon arrival and the furniture was comfortable. The stairwell is quite narrow, but it added to the character of the home! The kitchen was furnished with everything we needed and the washer/dryer was a huge added plus! Sandpiper Vacation Rentals made renting Sea Song a wonderful experience and was extremely communicative, from the moment of booking to the day of checkout.

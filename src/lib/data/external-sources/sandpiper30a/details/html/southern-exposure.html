Feel right at home in this three-story townhouse just minutes from the beach. Southern Exposure features multiple outdoor spaces, a quick walk to the Gulf Place Town Center, and the perfect location to enjoy the pristine beaches on the Emerald Coast. Jump in your personal 6-seat golf cart and let the good times roll! ($1500 REFUNDABLE damage deposit required for use. Must be 25+ yrs old to drive the cart). Drop your bags in one of the three bedrooms and settle in. The master offers a king bed with private balcony and en suite bath, along with comfy bedding and a flat screen TV. Additional guests can enjoy the dual queen guest bedroom or the dual twin bedroom with skylight ceiling. The townhome also features 2.5 baths and laundry on the first floor, making long stays and multiple guests a breeze. In the morning, enjoy a group breakfast in the full kitchen with stainless steel appliances, Viking refrigerator and Wolf oven, or head downtown and let someone else do the work. Grab a coffee at Sunrise Coffee Co. and head to the beach, just minutes from your rental. Feel free to head back this way for lunch and grab the kids a pizza or enjoy a cold beverage and delicious meal at The Perfect Pig Grill and Fish House. The Gulf Place community is great for families and friend groups looking to take it easy and have food and fun right at their fingertips. Whether you’re looking to host a family gathering, hang with friends, or work from home on 30A, Gulf Place is the perfect spot to rest, relax, and enjoy the turquoise waters you’ve been dreaming of. Rent this high-end townhome and start planning for a picture-perfect getaway!

About the Community of Gulf Place Courtyards on 30A

Tucked away between the pristine beaches of the Emerald Coastline is the community of Gulf Place Courtyards on 30A. This coastal area has a laidback vibe with 19 miles of dedicated bike path to get around the area with ease! The neighborhood features high-end condominiums with dedicated outdoor space, tennis courts, shuffleboard, corn hole, private pools, and easy beach access. Check out the Gulf Place Town Center for an incredible selection of food and shopping options, along with one of the many state parks in the area! From food to fun, Gulf Place on 30A is the perfect coastal location for those looking to rest, relax, and enjoy all that 30A has to offer. Amenities such as golf, tennis, biking, boating, water sports & more are available locally at additional costs.

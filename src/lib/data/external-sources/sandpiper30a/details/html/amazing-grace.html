Amazing Grace

  • 4 Bedrooms
  • 3 Baths
  • 8 Sleeps
  • 2132 sqft

HOME HIGHLIGHTS: ✦One house from the walkover to the private Seaside beach at WEST RUSKIN and directly behind the town square! Can't beat this Location! ✦ 2 KING rooms ✦ You will have a total of 8 RFID access cards for access to the private beaches in Seaside. ✦ 2 adult sized beach cruisers included ✦ Sandpiper Welcome Tote included for all rentals ✦ Charcoal grill ✦ Fully stocked kitchen ✦ WIFI and streaming available on TVs ✦ Sandpiper Linen Program Participant: All linen, bedding and bath, are the highest quality. OPTIONAL ADD-ONS: → There is an additional cost to use the 3 pools and fitness center in Seaside The cost for this cottage is $112/day. Please alert us after booking should you wish to add the "Seaside Amenity Passes" to your reservation. → Optional Cancellation Protection → Allows cancellation for ANY reason up to 48 hours in advance of stay and guarantees 50% of all monies paid returned ($150 fee applies at booking). HOME DETAILS: Amazing Grace features a larger and fully redesigned kitchen, appliances, bathrooms, floors and even walls. Same great location, but an even greater Amazing Grace space! With amazing grace and stature, this 4-bedroom Seaside cottage sits just 2 houses back from the beach and 1 street over from Seaside's town square with gulf views from 3 different balcony locations throughout the house! Amazing Grace boasts sprawling porches and balconies on every level, starting with the front porch you'll walk through as you enter this beautiful cottage. ACCOMMODATIONS: (Sleeps 8): **Three of the 4 bedrooms are located on the First floor.** ▪️First Floor: Bedroom 1: King bed with flat screen TV Bedroom 2: 2 Twin beds Shared hall spa designed bathroom complete with 7 head tile shower Bedroom 3: King master suite with flat screen TV, sitting area, desk, and private bathroom with shower only. Off the back of the house, also on the first floor, is an outdoor enclosed shower. ▪️Second Floor: A kitchen with stainless appliances, bar seating for 6 and a dining space that seats 8. The main living room offers a 65' HD flat screen TV with SONOS Arc Speaker, dual couches and a nook with additional seating and dining space. A 3rd full bath is also on this level (tub/shower combo). Some of the best parts of the upstairs living space can be found outside!! Off the kitchen is an oversized balcony with lots of seating with glimpses of the town and ocean! Off the front of the house is the screened in front balcony with gulf views as well. ▪️Third floor: Bedroom 4: Queen bed with a private balcony located up the spiral staircase. IMPORTANT DETAILS ✦There is an additional cost to use the 3 pools and fitness center in Seaside. This charge is optional with Sandpiper Vacation Rentals. The cost for this cottage is $112/day. Please alert us after booking should you wish to add the "Seaside Amenity Passes" to your reservation. Seaside requires the passes to be purchased for the entirety of your stay when ordered. ✦Sorry!! This is not a pet friendly property. ✦Must be 25 to rent ✦ Additional fees are required to reserve Pickleball and Tennis courts paid directly to the Seaside amenity Center. ✦ Walkable Town Center from rental home including shopping, restaurants and upscale grocery market.

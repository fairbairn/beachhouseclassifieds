DETAILS:
Welcome to 379 East Royal Fern Way, a charming 4-bedroom, pet-friendly oasis in WaterColor's Crossing District. Designed for families and friends, this spacious home invites connection with its open floor plan that seamlessly flows between the kitchen, dining area, and cozy living room. The well-equipped kitchen features stainless steel appliances and stunning granite countertops, perfect for preparing meals together. Gather at the bar for snacks or enjoy family dinners at the table that seats six.

Step outside to enjoy expansive porches with plenty of seating, ideal for soaking up the Florida sun or catching up with loved ones. A second media room on the first floor provides a fun space for kids to play and unwind.

When it's time to rest, you'll find comfort in the thoughtfully designed sleeping areas. The first-floor guest bedroom offers a king bed and an adjoining bathroom with a walk-in shower. Upstairs, the luxurious master suite features a king bed and a private bath with a soaking tub, while two additional bedrooms with queen beds ensure everyone has a cozy retreat. A charming bunk room with four twin beds is perfect for kids, and all bedrooms come equipped with TVs for easy relaxation.

For added convenience, this home includes a 6-seater golf cart and four adult cruiser bikes, making it easy to explore 30A. Just a short bike ride takes you to the shops and restaurants of Seaside or the WaterColor Beach Club. At 379 East Royal Fern Way, you'll experience the perfect blend of luxury, convenience, and charm for an unforgettable getaway!

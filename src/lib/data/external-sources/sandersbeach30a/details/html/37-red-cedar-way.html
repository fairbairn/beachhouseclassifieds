Description

Located just one block from Camp WaterColor, this single-level home offers a seamless blend of comfort, convenience, and relaxed coastal charm. With an open-concept layout, spacious bedrooms, and thoughtful amenities, 37 Red Cedar Way provides the ideal setting for a family vacation or group getaway in the heart of WaterColor.

Property Highlights

  • Exclusive Access to the WaterColor Beach Club and Community Pools with Up to 10 Passes Included
  • 4 Adult Bikes Included in Rental
  • Parking for 2 Vehicles
  • Propane Gas Grill
  • Exterior Shower
  • 2-Minute Walk to Camp WaterColor
  • 5-Minute Bike Ride to the WaterColor Beach Club Access
  • 5-Minute Bike Ride to Seaside

Experience the Home

Welcome to 37 Red Cedar Way, a charming single-level retreat designed for easy living and relaxation. Perfectly positioned in the Camp District, this home places guests just steps from the community pool, playground, and trails.

The open floor plan features a bright and inviting living area with comfortable seating, a large flat-screen TV, and direct access to the dining and kitchen spaces. The dining area offers ample seating for eight, while the breakfast bar provides extra space for casual meals and morning coffee. The kitchen is equipped with upgraded stainless steel appliances, ample counter space, and everything needed to prepare family meals during your stay.

The primary suite offers a private retreat with a king bed, flat-screen TV, and spacious bathroom featuring a soaking tub and walk-in shower. The second bedroom includes two queen beds and a private bathroom, ideal for friends or family members. The third bedroom is designed with kids in mind, featuring queen bunk beds with a queen trundle bed and access to a nearby shared bathroom.

Outdoors, enjoy a peaceful morning coffee or evening drink on the front porch surrounded by the tranquil WaterColor setting. With four adult bikes included, it’s easy to explore nearby Camp WaterColor, Seaside, and the Beach Club at your own pace.

Thoughtfully designed and ideally located, 37 Red Cedar Way combines the comfort of home with the best of 30A living for an unforgettable coastal escape.

Sleeping Arrangements

  • Primary Bedroom: King Bed (Sleeps 2), Private Bathroom with Soaking Tub and Walk-In Shower
  • Guest Bedroom: Two Queen Beds (Sleeps 4), Private Bathroom with Tub/Shower Combination
  • Guest Bedroom: One Set of Queen Bunk Beds with Queen Trundle (Sleeps 6), Shared Bathroom with Tub/Shower Combination
  • Living Area: Queen Sleeper Sofa (Sleeps 2)
  • Washer and Dryer

Life in WaterColor

Nestled within the Camp District, 37 Red Cedar Way provides easy access to all that WaterColor has to offer. Spend the day at Camp WaterColor, explore the nearby trails, or enjoy the shops and dining in Seaside just a short ride away. With its one-level layout, spacious living areas, and prime location, this home provides the perfect setting for your next 30A vacation.

Guests may rent a cart exclusively through The Electric Cart Company. Please note that WaterColor allows only one electric cart per home, and rentals are available on a first-come, first-served basis. Advance reservations are strongly recommended, especially during peak seasons.

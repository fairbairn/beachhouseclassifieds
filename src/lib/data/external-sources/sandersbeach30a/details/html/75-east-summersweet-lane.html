DETAILS:
Discover your dream getaway at 75 Summersweet Lane, nestled in the charming WaterColor Crossing District. Just steps from the serene Dragonfly pool, this beautifully appointed 3-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home promises the perfect blend of luxury and comfort.

As you step inside, you're greeted by an inviting open-concept layout filled with natural light and soothing decor. The well-equipped kitchen, featuring high-end appliances, invites you to create culinary delights, while the dining area comfortably seats six. After a day of sun-soaked adventures, unwind in the spacious living area or step outside to your private deck, where you can enjoy morning coffee surrounded by lush greenery. The adjacent bar area, complete with a wine fridge, is perfect for hosting friends.

On the first floor, a king bedroom with a private bath offers convenience and comfort. Upstairs, the luxurious master suite boasts a double vanity, a walk-in shower, and a private porch for quiet moments. An additional guest room with two queen beds and a cozy nook with a twin bunk bed provides ample space for families.

With four adult bikes and a six-seater electric cart at your disposal, exploring WaterColor is a breeze. This pet-friendly home welcomes families, including furry friends, ensuring everyone feels at home.

Enjoy world-class amenities like the beach club, multiple pools, and scenic coastal lakes. At 75 Summersweet Lane, paradise awaits—book your unforgettable escape today!

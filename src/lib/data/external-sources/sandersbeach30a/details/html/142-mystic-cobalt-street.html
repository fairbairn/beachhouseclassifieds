Description

Located in the highly sought-after Park District, 142 Mystic Cobalt Street offers a classic WaterColor experience just two blocks from the Beach Club, shops, and Seaside. This inviting, pet-friendly retreat accommodates up to 10 guests and includes a 6-passenger electric cart, four adult bikes, and Beach Club access for a seamless 30A vacation.

Experience the Home

Welcome to 142 Mystic Cobalt Street, a beautifully designed WaterColor cottage combining Southern charm and modern coastal comfort. Nestled just steps from the Beach Club and Seaside, this home features open living areas, stylish décor, and inviting porches that capture the essence of relaxed 30A living.

The first floor greets guests with tall ceilings, an elegant floor-to-beam fireplace, and an open-concept layout designed for gathering. The fully equipped kitchen includes stainless steel appliances, a gas range, and a large granite bar that seats four. The adjacent dining area accommodates eight comfortably, creating an ideal setting for family meals or evening conversation.

The primary suite on the main level offers a king bed, private bathroom with soaking tub and walk-in shower, and direct access to the screened porch. A second bedroom with a full bed and private bath provides an additional cozy retreat for guests.

Upstairs, a spacious vaulted family room offers flexibility for larger groups, featuring a king bed, built-in twin bunk beds, and an additional bunk area in the hallway. A shared bathroom with a walk-in shower completes this level, offering comfort and convenience for all guests.

Outdoors, enjoy your morning coffee or evening cocktail on the screened porch while overlooking the quiet street lined with WaterColor’s signature coastal cottages. With four adult bikes and a brand-new 6-passenger electric cart, guests can easily explore the Beach Club, Seaside, and the surrounding community amenities.

Pet-friendly and perfectly located, 142 Mystic Cobalt Street is the ideal home for families seeking a classic WaterColor escape with modern comforts and effortless charm.

Life in WaterColor

Ideally located in the Park District, 142 Mystic Cobalt Street places guests just moments from the Beach Club, Camp WaterColor, and the dining and shopping of Seaside. Whether relaxing on the porch, exploring by bike, or spending the day at the beach, this home offers the perfect blend of style, comfort, and location.

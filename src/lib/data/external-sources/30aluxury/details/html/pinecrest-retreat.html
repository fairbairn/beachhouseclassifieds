Pinecrest Retreat - Nestled in the prestigious enclave of Inlet Beach, this elegant residence combines timeless sophistication with modern comforts. Featuring five bedrooms and five and a half bathrooms, this home has been thoughtfully designed to offer a spacious and refined coastal living experience.

Enhancing its charm, the property includes a private guest house, a heated pool, and is located less than 100 feet from the pristine shores of Rosemary Beach. Upon entering, you’ll find warm, welcoming living spaces filled with natural light that highlight the hardwood floors and tasteful finishes.

The first floor is thoughtfully designed for both comfort and convenience. The centerpiece is a gourmet kitchen, outfitted with premium stainless steel appliances, custom cabinetry, and a spacious center island—ideal for casual meals or hosting guests. Just off the kitchen, a formal dining area comfortably seats eight, offering a stylish space for gatherings. A powder room and a fully equipped laundry room add practical functionality to the layout. The main level also includes an elevator to all three floors and a private primary suite with a stunning wood-inlay ceiling and a serene ambiance. The luxurious en suite bathroom boasts a soaking tub, walk-in shower, and dual vanities, creating a spa-like retreat within your own home.

Upstairs on the second floor, a cozy common lounge features a sleeper sofa and flat-screen TV, ideal for movie nights or casual relaxation. Two guest suites, each with a king bed and private en-suite bathroom, offer refined comfort. A custom-designed bunk room with twin-over-twin built-ins and its own walk-in shower bathroom provides the perfect space for children or teens.

The top level offers a tranquil sitting area, a private balcony, and an open space with a TV ideal for yoga, stretching, or home workouts—perfect for guests seeking balance and mindfulness during their stay.

The detached guest house over the garage is a peaceful, self-contained retreat steps from the primary residence. This stylish studio-style suite includes a king bed, sitting area, queen sleeper sofa, kitchenette with fridge, oven, microwave, sink, and a private full bath with walk-in shower. With its own entrance, it’s ideal for in-laws, couples, or staff. The top level offers a tranquil sitting area, a private balcony, and an open space with a TV that’s ideal for yoga, stretching, or home workouts—perfect for guests seeking balance and mindfulness during their stay.

Located in Inlet Beach neighborhood of Pincrest, right next to Rosemary Beach.

**This home is a brand new rental property, hence the lack or reviews. We would love for you to stay here soon and leave an amazing review!**

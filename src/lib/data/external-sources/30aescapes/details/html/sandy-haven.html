Description

30A ESCAPES presents…“Sandy Haven,” a beautifully designed vacation rental in Rosemary Beach, reflecting the original design philosophy of this iconic coastal community. Located 15 seconds from Coquina Pool and just 2 minutes from the beach access, this pet-friendly cottage blends coastal charm with modern comforts. Perfect for relaxing in serene outdoor spaces, exploring with included beach bikes, or enjoying tranquil views from the lovely porch or balcony. Learn more below…👇

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Across the street from the Coquina Pool - 15-second walk
  • 2-minute walk to the beach access point
  • Pet-friendly - bring your pup along!
  • Serene porch, private courtyard and balcony views for relaxing mornings or evenings
  • Includes 4 beach bikes for venturing along scenic 30A

*** LIVING AREAS ***

Step inside Sandy Haven and feel an immediate sense of relaxation in the warm, inviting living spaces. With creamy neutral tones and calming blue and green accents, the main living area offers the perfect retreat after a day of sun and sand. Sink into the plush seating and cozy up for movie nights on the large flat-screen TV.

  • Comfortable main seating area with a relaxing coastal vibe
  • Large flat-screen TV for family entertainment
  • French doors opening directly onto the boardwalk
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Thoughtfully designed for both cooking and gathering, the kitchen features all stainless steel appliances, an open layout, and a cozy adjacent sitting area with an additional TV for shared moments. The tranquil dining room, with seating for six, is perfect for family meals or lively game nights.

  • Fully equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances
  • Adjacent sitting area with four comfy chairs and flat-screen TV
  • Dining space for up to six people
  • Full-size washer and dryer for the comforts of home
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Relax in the peaceful outdoor spaces that make this home unique. Sip your morning coffee on the porch facing the lovely boardwalk, take in views from the second-floor balcony, dive into the refreshing waters of Coquina Pool moments away or enjoy an evening stroll down the boardwalk. Gather around the outdoor fireplace for cozy evenings and family s'mores time after a BBQ dinner al-fresco.

  • Enjoy your morning coffee on the charming front porch
  • Step directly onto the scenic boardwalk from your doorstep
  • Take in stunning pool views from the second-floor balcony
  • Relax on the private courtyard, featuring cozy furnishings and a built-in gas grill station
  • Gather around the inviting outdoor fireplace
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor:

  • Half Bathroom: Conveniently located off the Kitchen

2nd Floor:

  • Primary Suite: King-sized bed, soothing light green accents, flat-screen TV, private balcony and En-suite with dual vanities and walk-in shower.
  • Full Guest Suite: Two Full-sized beds, flat-screen TV, and tranquil decor ideal for kids or additional guests.
  • Queen Guest Suite: Queen-sized bed, flat-screen TV, shabby chic dresser, and coastal-themed accent lighting.
  • Shared Guest Bathroom: Single vanity and shower/tub combo shared with the Full and Queen Guest Suites
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Located in the heart of Rosemary Beach, this charming home offers easy access to scenic coastal beauty and vibrant community charm. Explore boutique shops, discover award-winning restaurants, and unwind at one of the four luxurious community pools.

  • Rosemary Beach Community: Beachside destination with award-winning architecture
  • Racquet Club – one of the world’s top 75 tennis spots
  • Multiple parks & greens with playgrounds, a splash pad, butterfly park, and hidden boardwalks with fountains
  • 15 Second Walk to Cabana Pool
  • 2-minute walk to the beach access
  • 4 Minute Walk to the Heated Sky Pool
  • 5 Minute Walk to Fitness Center and Tennis Courts
4 RESORT POOLS
  • Barbados Pool: Gulf-side, French West Indies style with a fountain
  • Coquina Pool: Gulf-side, with a relaxing negative edge overflow
  • Sky Pool: Park-side, indoor, heated year-round with radiant heated decking in winter
  • Cabana Pool: Park-side, cabana-style with a children’s pool & playground nearby
*** PET POLICY ***
  • Pets are not allowed on furniture or beds
  • Must be kept in a crate when guests are not with their dog
  • With non-refundable pet fee $250 + tax
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included in our quotes. It can be purchased during the reservation process or obtained independently
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment
  • Waiver required to get access to the 4 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes
  • LSVs are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.

Description

30A ESCAPES presents… “Pappas Carriage”, a cozy Tuscan-style retreat perfectly situated in Rosemary Beach. Just 30 seconds from the white sands and a 2-min stroll to the Coquina Pool, this rental is thoughtfully designed for couples. Enjoy vaulted ceilings, modern mid-century décor, a charming balcony with Eastern Green views, and 2 guest bikes for exploring nearby shops and restaurants. Learn more below…

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Directly in front of Eastern Green, offering scenic views
  • 30-second walk to the beach with restrooms
  • 2-minutes to Coquina Pool for a relaxing swim
  • Tuscan-inspired design with mid-century modern touches
  • Private balcony to enjoy morning coffee or evening sunsets
  • Fully equipped kitchenette and cozy dining area
  • Includes 2 bikes for exploring Rosemary Beach

*** LIVING AREA ***

Step inside Pappas Carriage, a Tuscan delight enhanced with vaulted ceilings and modern mid-century décor. Immerse yourself in a blend of Tuscan elegance and mid-century modern charm. The open living area is bathed in natural light, featuring vaulted ceilings and cozy furnishings—perfect for unwinding after a day at the beach or pool!

  • Mid-century modern furniture and décor
  • Flat-screen TV for movie nights
  • Private balcony with seating to enjoy morning coffee or sunsets
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

The compact yet fully functional kitchenette is equipped with everything needed to enjoy home-cooked meals during your stay. A cozy dining nook provides an intimate setting for meals for two.

  • Stainless Steel appliances including a mini fridge and gas range
  • Essential cookware and utensils
  • Cozy dining table for 2
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***
  • Queen Primary Suite: A cozy queen-sized bed to ensure restful nights. Vaulted ceilings and calming décor create the perfect atmosphere for relaxation.
  • Full Bathroom: Modern bathroom featuring a curved shower and elegant finishes, a perfect blend of form and function.
*** ADDITIONAL BOOKING INFORMATION ***
  • Max Occupancy: This unit accommodates a maximum of 2 people. Additional overnight guests require prior approval from the management. An extra fee will apply for reservations exceeding the occupancy limit.
  • Parking: The garage is shared with the main house & one space is available for the carriage house. There is additional street parking available at a first come, first serve basis.
  • A main house is also available for rent. For more information, contact our 30A Escapes team to book.
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Perfectly situated in Rosemary Beach, “Pappas Carriage” places you just steps from coffee shops, restaurants, and boutique stores. Hop on one of the included bikes to explore the beautiful surroundings, or walk 2 minutes to the Cabana Pool. With access to trails, parks, and the sparkling Emerald Coast, relaxation and adventure are always within reach.

  • Rosemary Beach Community: Beachside destination with award-winning architecture
  • Racquet Club – one of the world’s top 75 tennis spots
  • Multiple parks & greens with playgrounds, a splash pad, butterfly park, and hidden boardwalks with fountains
  • Fitness Center with massage and yoga
4 RESORT POOLS
  • Barbados Pool: Gulf-side, French West Indies style with a fountain
  • Coquina Pool: Gulf-side, with a relaxing negative edge overflow
  • Sky Pool: Park-side, indoor, heated year-round with radiant heated decking in winter
  • Cabana Pool: Park-side, cabana-style with a children’s pool & playground nearby
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included but can be purchased during checkout or independently
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment
  • Waiver required to get access to the 2 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes
  • There is currently active construction across the green from this property.

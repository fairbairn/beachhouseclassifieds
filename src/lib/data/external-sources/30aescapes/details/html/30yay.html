Description

30A ESCAPES presents… “30Yay!” in Seagrove Beach. Welcome to a stunning coastal retreat with a private heated pool, spa, and spacious fenced backyard—your personal oasis for family fun or relaxing in the sun. With 5 luxurious suites, there’s space for everyone to recharge. A short stroll (4-min walk) takes you to the sugar-white sands of the Gulf, and with 4 bikes included, you can explore the beauty of 30A with ease. Ready to turn your dream getaway into reality? Learn more below…

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • 4-Min Walk to the Beach – Enjoy effortless access to sun, sand, and sea, perfect for morning strolls or sunset views.
  • Private Heated Pool & Spa – Indulge in ultimate relaxation, whether soaking in the spa or taking a refreshing dip in the pool.
  • Spacious Fenced Backyard – Gather with family and friends in the outdoor dining area, fire up the grill, or unwind in the cozy lounging spaces.
  • 3 King Suites with En-suite Bathrooms – Experience luxury and privacy with beautifully designed suites perfect for restful nights.
  • 7 Bikes Included – Explore the charming 30A area on two wheels, from scenic paths to local hotspots.
  • Wrap-around Balcony with Pool Views – Feel the tranquility as you sip your coffee or wine, overlooking the serene pool below.
  • Close to Seaside, Watersound, and Upscale Dining – Immerse yourself in the vibrant coastal life, with boutique shops, fine dining, and picturesque communities just minutes away.

*** LIVING AREA ***

Step into the stunning open-concept living space, thoughtfully designed for both comfort and connection. Expansive floor-to-ceiling windows flood the room with natural light and provide breathtaking views of the backyard oasis. A plush seating area and large-screen TV create an inviting space for family gatherings, movie nights, or quiet relaxation.

  • Expansive windows with panoramic pool views
  • Luxurious, cozy seating and a smart TV for entertainment
  • Seamless flow into the modern kitchen and dining areas, perfect for hosting and everyday living
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

The upscale kitchen is perfect for home chefs, featuring navy cabinetry, granite countertops, and premium appliances. Gather around the island or enjoy meals in the spacious dining area.

  • Fully equipped kitchen with high-end appliances
  • Granite counters and ample cabinet storage
  • Dining table seating 8, plus 4 bar stools at the island
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Transform your backyard into a private oasis, featuring a luxurious heated pool and spa that invites you to relax in style. The covered patio is designed for both comfort and fun, offering a dining area, cozy lounge seating, and an outdoor TV—perfect for lively gatherings or quiet evenings.

  • Luxurious private heated pool and spa for ultimate relaxation
  • Elegant covered patio with a dining table, plush lounge seating, and a flat-screen TV
  • Outdoor grill perfect for family barbecues and entertaining
  • Expansive yard ideal for games, sunbathing, or simply unwinding in the sun
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor:

  • Luxurious King Suite: Features a king-size bed, private patio access, spacious walk-in closet, flat-screen TV, and a spa-like En-suite complete with a soaking tub, double vanity, and walk-in shower for ultimate relaxation.
  • Convenient Half Bath: Perfectly situated near the common areas for easy access during gatherings.

2nd Floor:

  • King Suite 1: Enjoy a king-size bed, a private balcony with stunning pool views, and an elegant En-suite with dual sinks and a walk-in shower.
  • King Suite 2: Offers a king-size bed, access to two balconies, and a stylish En-suite featuring a single vanity and a walk-in shower.
  • Fun Bunk Room: Equipped with two twin-over-twin bunk beds, ideal for kids or extra guests looking for a cozy space.
  • Queen Guest Room: Includes a queen-size bed and access to a shared hall bathroom, complete with dual sinks and a tub/shower combo for added convenience.
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Located in the heart of Seagrove Beach, “30Yay!” is your ideal 30A escape, blending relaxation, adventure, and coastal charm. From sun-soaked beaches to boutique shopping and mouthwatering dining, everything you need for the perfect getaway is just moments away.

  • Eastern Lake Beach Access (4-Min Walk) – Step into paradise with soft, powdery white sand and serene, crystal-clear waters just a short stroll from your door.
  • Café Thirty-A & Old Florida Fish House (4-Min Drive) – Treat your taste buds to upscale dining or savor fresh coastal classics at these iconic 30A restaurants.
  • Greenway Station (2-Min Drive) – Discover casual dining and shopping options within perfect proximity in Seagrove Beach.
  • Seaside Beach (10-Min Drive) – Dive into the vibrant beach town vibe with charming boutiques, local artistry, and gourmet food options that make Seaside a must-visit destination.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Spacious Driveway Parking for Up to 3 SUVs!
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included but can be purchased separately.
  • Please note there is active construction take place in front of this property. We do not have a completion date at this time.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • Waiver required to get access to the 4 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • There is an active Google doorbell camera at the front door

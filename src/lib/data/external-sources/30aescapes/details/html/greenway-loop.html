Description

30A ESCAPES presents…“Greenway Loop,” your perfect luxury getaway in Seacrest Beach. Start your mornings on the breezy screened porch, head to the Lagoon pool or beach just moments away, and enjoy evenings with live music and great dining at Peddler’s Pavilion. Luxury details like 10’ ceilings, designer furnishings and new mattresses make you feel right at home! With 4 suites, a chef’s kitchen, and 6 bikes, it’s the ideal spot to create unforgettable memories.

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Steps away from the 12,000 sq ft Lagoon Pool – Dive into paradise with waterfalls and a fun-filled splash zone, perfect for both relaxation and family fun.
  • Luxury touches throughout – 10’ ceilings, tile and wood floors, designer furnishings, ultra-comfy mattresses, and an outdoor shower for the ultimate experience.
  • Large Screened Porch – Unwind with a gentle breeze, enjoy al fresco meals, or curl up with a book in ultimate comfort.
  • Chef’s kitchen – Cook like a pro in a modern, stylish kitchen designed for making memories over delicious meals.
  • 6 Bikes included – Cruise along scenic trails and beaches, soaking in the laid-back coastal charm of 30A.
  • Seasonal Tram Service – Effortlessly reach the stunning coastline and enjoy the crystal-clear waters at your leisure.
  • Close to boutique shopping, dining, and Rosemary Beach – Enjoy unique shops, great restaurants, and the beauty of nearby Rosemary Beach.

*** LIVING AREAS ***

Designed for both comfort and style, the open-concept living space is perfect for family time. Relax on plush seating, enjoy movie nights on the smart TV, or step out onto the screened porch with serene greenway views. Luxury details include 10’ ceilings, tile and wood floors throughout, and designer furnishings for an elevated experience.

  • Comfortable seating with designer furnishings and a large smart TV
  • Seamless flow between living, dining, and kitchen areas
  • Access to the screened porch to embrace indoor-outdoor living
*** KITCHEN & DINING ***

The spacious kitchen is a chef’s dream with modern appliances, granite countertops, and a large island. Whether it’s a quick breakfast or a gourmet dinner, this space is fully equipped to handle it all.

  • Fully stocked kitchen with stainless appliances
  • Granite island with bar seating for 3
  • Formal dining area with seating for 8
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Savor the outdoors with spacious screened back porch, offering greenway views and a peaceful atmosphere—perfect for dining al fresco or unwinding in comfort. Enjoy a furnished front porch—perfect for sipping iced tea and soaking in the surroundings. Take advantage of the bikes provided to effortlessly explore nearby beaches, charming shops, and scenic trails.

  • Screened porch with outdoor dining spaces and a bed swing
  • Furnished front porch—ideal for sipping iced tea
  • 6 bikes included for guest use
  • Greenway views and a peaceful atmosphere
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor:

  • King Guest Bedroom: Luxurious king bed, flat-screen TV, and access to a dedicated full bathroom.
  • Full Guest Bathroom: Custom-tiled shower/tub combo and double vanity.

2nd Floor:

  • King Primary Suite: Includes private balcony access, soaking tub, walk-in shower, double vanities, smart TV, and vaulted ceilings.
  • Twin/Bunk Room: Queen-over-Queen custom bunk bed and a Twin bed (sleeps 5) with access to a shared Jack and Jill bathroom.
  • Queen/Twin Loft Bedroom: Twin-over-Queen custom bunk bed with access to the shared Jack and Jill bathroom.
  • Jack and Jill bathroom: Double vanity and walk-in shower
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Located in the heart of Seacrest Beach, Greenway Loop offers easy access to all of 30A’s best attractions. From boutique shops to gourmet restaurants and pristine beaches, everything you need is just minutes away.

  • 1-Min Bike Ride to Lagoon Pool and Splash Zone
  • Walk to Rosemary and Alys Beaches
  • Nearby boutique shops, cafes, and seasonal live entertainment
*** LOCATION, OUTDOOR & COMMUNITY AMENITIES ***
  • 1-Min Bike Ride to 12,000 sq ft Lagoon Pool – A stunning paradise with a zero-entry splash zone, waterfalls, and plenty of loungers, plus a poolside café and picnic areas.
  • Beach Access – Enjoy a seasonal tram for easy transportation or take a short 10-minute stroll to the emerald Gulf waters.
  • 6 Bikes Included – Perfect for exploring nearby attractions like Peddler’s Pavilion, Rosemary Beach, and Alys Beach.
  • Peddler’s Pavilion – Just a 2-minute bike ride or 5-minute walk to boutique shops, live music, dining, and seasonal nightlife.
  • Scenic Walking & Nature Trails – Picturesque paths just steps from the house, ideal for exploration and relaxation.
  • Walkable, Bike-Friendly Community – Conveniently access beautiful destinations all around.
  • South Walton’s Tallest Sand Dunes – A unique and stunning natural feature of the area.
  • Rosemary Beach (8-min walk) and Alys Beach (10-min walk) – Explore charming coastal communities nearby.
  • Carillon Beach (7-min drive) – More coastal beauty just minutes away.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included in our quotes. It can be purchased during the reservation process or obtained independently.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • This property has 6 bikes for guest use. To utilize the bikes, a waiver must be signed before the code is provided to the guest. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • LSVs are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.
  • There is an active Ring doorbell camera at the entrance door of the home.

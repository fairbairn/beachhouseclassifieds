Description

30A ESCAPES presents… “All Our Summers,” a luxurious retreat on Seagrove Beach’s coastal dune lake. Start your morning coffee on the dock as the sun sparkles on the water, paddle across the lake, or cruise to the beach on one of 8 included bikes. Evenings bring fire pit gatherings under starry skies. This newly renovated, 6-bedroom home sleeps 16 and features a private dock, kayaks, paddle boards, and an LSV (Golf Cart) for beach trips. Create unforgettable memories in luxury and style! Learn more below...

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Lakefront Living: Enjoy the tranquility of Eastern Lake with your own private dock—perfect for sunrise paddles or peaceful evenings by the water.
  • Premium Amenities: Elevate your stay with luxury touches, including an LSV (Golf Cart), 8 beach bikes, kayaks, and paddle boards—everything you need for endless outdoor adventures.
  • Steps to the Beach: A quick 2-minute bike ride or leisurely stroll takes you to pristine beach access, making every day feel like a seaside escape.
  • Renovation Complete: Revel in the fresh, modern vibe of freshly renovated interiors, blending style with comfort for the ultimate coastal retreat.
  • Perfect for Large Groups: With 6 spacious bedrooms accommodating up to 16 guests, this home is made for unforgettable gatherings with family and friends.

*** LIVING AREA ***

Gather with your crew in the cozy lakeside living room, where relaxed coastal design and comfort come together. Oversized windows frame views of the water, while plush furnishings create the perfect setting for connection and downtime.

  • Comfortable seating including sofa and armchairs
  • Flat-screen TV for movie nights or rainy-day fun
  • Door leading directly to the lakefront balcony for seamless indoor-outdoor flow
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Entertain with ease in the open-concept kitchen and dining area, designed for both functionality and style. Whether you’re prepping beach snacks or hosting a sunset dinner, this space delivers everything you need for culinary joy.

  • Stainless steel appliances and ample storage space
  • Bar seating for 4—Ideal for casual breakfsts or socializing while cooking
  • Breakfast table for 4 with views of the lake
  • Formal dining table with seating for 8
  • Convenient door from the kitchen to the lakefront balcony for effortless al fresco dining
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Your ultimate outdoor paradise is here, featuring a private dock, a cozy fire pit, and ample seating to immerse yourself in Seagrove's breathtaking beauty fully. Launch your paddleboard or kayak straight from your backyard and glide directly to the Gulf, or unwind in the evenings under a starlit sky by the warmth of the fire. Every moment is designed to inspire and relax.

  • Exclusive private dock complete with paddle boards and kayaks—perfect for endless water adventures
  • Cozy outdoor fire pit and premium BBQ grill for unforgettable nights under the stars
  • Versatile covered seating areas designed for both sun seekers and shade lovers
  • LSV (Golf Cart) and 8 high-quality bikes included, making exploring the beauty of 30A fun and effortless
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor:

  • Primary King Suite – King bed, flat-screen TV, serene sitting area, direct access to the lakefront patio, and a private En-suite featuring dual vanities and a spacious walk-in shower.
  • King Guest Suite – King bed, flat-screen TV, lakefront patio access, and a beautifully appointed En-suite with dual vanities and a walk-in shower.
  • Bunk Room – Features one Twin-over-Twin and one Full-over-Full bunk, flat-screen TV, and is conveniently located next to a shared guest bathroom with dual vanities and a shower/tub combo—ideal for kids or multi-family stays.

2nd Floor:

  • King Guest Suite 1 – King bed, flat-screen TV, and shared access to the adjacent guest bathroom.
  • Guest Bathroom – Dual vanities and a shower/tub combo shared between both upstairs king suites.
  • King Guest Suite 2 – King bed, flat-screen TV, and access to the shared guest bathroom.
  • Primary King Suite – King bed, flat-screen TV, and a private En-suite with a single vanity and a walk-in shower.
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Seagrove’s stunning lakeside beauty and timeless beach-town charm make it the ultimate vacation destination. Whether you're seeking serene relaxation or thrilling adventures, this picturesque haven offers the perfect blend of both. Get ready to discover your new favorite escape.

  • Old Florida Fish House (0.6 mile away) – Enjoy a laid-back bike ride to this iconic spot, known for its fresh seafood and casual, friendly vibe.
  • Timpoochee Trail (1.4 miles away) – Step right into adventure with this scenic trail, perfect for biking, walking, and soaking in the natural beauty of the area.
  • Seaside and Watersound Boutiques & Restaurants (2.1 miles away) – Explore charming shops and dine at fantastic local restaurants that capture the heart of beachside living.
  • Eastern Lake Beach Access (1.2 miles away) – Stroll down to the soft white sand and turquoise waters, your perfect spot for relaxation and coastal bliss.

  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.

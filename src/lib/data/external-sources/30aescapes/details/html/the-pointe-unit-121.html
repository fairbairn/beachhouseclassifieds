Description

⚠️ PLEASE NOTE: Wooden beams and framing have been installed on the balconies as part of preparations for future community projects. These structures partially reduce the views from the balconies, including the pool area.🛑The elevator in Building 1 is out of service & not accessible.


30A ESCAPES presents…“The Pointe Unit 121,” a luxury 2nd floor condo at The Pointe on 30A. This spacious 3-bedroom retreat features three private balconies, upscale interiors, and resort-style amenities—all just a 5-min walk to beach access and Rosemary Town Center. Learn more below…👇

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Prime Location: 5-minute walk to Rosemary Beach Town Center
  • Three Private Balconies: Outdoor spaces for relaxing, lounging, or dining al fresco
  • Spacious Layout: Open concept kitchen, living, and dining areas
  • Upscale Kitchen & Dining: Quartz counters, stainless appliances, island seating for 4 + dining for 6
  • Three Comfortable Bedrooms with spa-inspired bathrooms
  • Resort Amenities: Pool, hot tub, fire pits, grills, and fitness center
  • On-Site Dining: 'Big Bad Breakfast' for Southern-style breakfast and lunch
  • Bikes Included: 3 adult bikes for exploring 30A
*** LIVING AREAS ***

With a bright and open floor plan, this living space invites guests to relax and gather comfortably. Enjoy the gas fireplace, flat screen TV, and stylish furnishings in a layout that’s perfect for unwinding after a day at the beach.

  • Open-concept living and dining area
  • Gas fireplace and flat screen TV
  • Stylish and comfortable furnishings
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Designed for convenience and elegance, the full-size kitchen is equipped with quartz countertops, stainless appliances, and a large island with seating. A separate dining table accommodates six guests inside, while one of the balconies offers an outdoor dining space perfect for al fresco meals.

  • Full-size kitchen with stainless steel appliances
  • Quartz countertops and ample cabinetry
  • Island with seating for 4
  • Dining table with seating for 6
  • Balcony dining table with seating for 4—ideal for al fresco meals
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Enjoy three private balconies—ideal for sipping morning coffee, al fresco dining, or relaxing after a beach day. Whether you're soaking in the fresh air or heading down to the resort-style pool, The Pointe delivers everything needed for a restful 30A escape.

  • Three private balconies with lounge seating
  • One balcony includes a dining table—seating for 4, perfect for al fresco meals
  • Resort-style pool and oversized hot tub
  • Gas grills, fire pits, and shaded lounge areas
  • On-site restaurant ‘Big Bad Breakfast’
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***
  • Primary Suite: King-size bed, flat screen TV, office desk, private En-suite with soaking tub, walk-in shower, dual vanities, and private balcony access
  • King Guest Suite: King-size bed, En-suite bathroom with soaking tub, walk-in shower, and dual vanities
  • Queen Guest Suite: Two Queen-size beds, En-suite bathroom with walk-in shower and dual vanities, plus access to a private balcony
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Located near Rosemary and Inlet Beach, this condo places you at the heart of 30A’s most desirable attractions. Spend your days exploring boutique shops and dining at 30Avenue, wandering the charming streets of Rosemary Beach, or adventuring through the natural beauty of Camp Helen State Park, where hiking trails and coastal dune lakes await.

  • 5-minute walk to Rosemary Beach Town Center
  • 5-minute walk to Winston Lane Public Beach Access
  • Steps to ‘Big Bad Breakfast’ and other local favorites
  • 2-minute bike ride to 30Avenue—shopping, dining, and seasonal events
  • 10-minute drive to Camp Helen State Park—hiking, paddling, and natural beauty
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included but can be purchased separately.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • Waiver required to get access to the 3 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes
  • Wooden beams and framing have been installed on the balconies as part of preparations for future community projects. These structures partially reduce views from the balconies, including the pool area.
  • The elevator in Building 1 is out of service & not accessible.
*** YOUR STAY - OUR PRIORITY ***

At 30A Escapes, we’re committed to providing a luxurious, safe, and stress-free vacation experience. Enjoy 24/7 support and an immaculate home designed for comfort and relaxation.

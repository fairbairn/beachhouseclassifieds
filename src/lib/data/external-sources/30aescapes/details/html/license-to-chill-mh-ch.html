Description

30A ESCAPES presents… “License To Chill MH + CH,” where luxury meets laid-back vibes in Seagrove Beach! Unwind by your private heated pool & spa or host movie nights in the home theater. Just a 5-minute stroll to sugar-white sands, this dreamy retreat sleeps 20 with room to spare. Cruise around on 7 included bikes or a LSV (Low-Speed Vehicle) and whip up unforgettable meals in a chef’s kitchen. Perfect for family reunions or groups, this is where memories are made. Learn more below...

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Steps to the Beach - Just 2 blocks away! A quick 5-minute stroll brings you to the pristine sands of Seagrove Beach.
  • Private Heated Pool & Spa - Unwind in your own oasis with a luxurious heated pool, bubbling spa, loungers, covered seating, and a grilling area in the enclosed courtyard.
  • Home Theater Experience - Enjoy cinematic perfection with six reclining theater chairs for the ultimate movie night.
  • Elegant & Spacious Living Areas - Open-concept spaces with stylish décor, abundant natural light, and plenty of room to relax or entertain.
  • Chef’s Dream Kitchen - Featuring premium appliances, a wine fridge, ice maker, and generous counter space to craft gourmet meals with ease.
  • Sleeps 20 in Comfort & Style - 5 beautifully designed bedrooms with versatile sleeping options, including cozy bunk rooms and luxurious en-suite bathrooms.
  • Bonus Carriage House - A private retreat with its own full kitchen, living space, and sleeping quarters for extra convenience and privacy.
  • Bikes & LSV (Golf Cart) Included - Explore the beauty of 30A effortlessly with 7 custom bikes and a Low-Speed Vehicle, perfect for beachside adventures.

*** LIVING AREA ***

Indulge in luxury from the moment you step into this stunning home. The open-concept first-floor living room is a haven of relaxation and connection, featuring expansive windows that flood the space with natural light and offer stunning views of the private pool. Elegant, stylish furnishings create an inviting atmosphere where everyone can unwind in comfort. For added privacy and convenience, the charming carriage house provides an additional living space, perfect for those seeking their own retreat. Every detail is designed to elevate your stay and make you feel at home.

  • Spacious open living room featuring stunning pool views and luxurious, plush seating for ultimate comfort
  • Charming carriage house living room with a private entrance, offering a cozy and exclusive retreat
  • Stylishly transformed garage turned into a vibrant bunk/game room, complete with a foosball table and large-screen TV for endless entertainment
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Create culinary masterpieces in the expansive, fully equipped Chef’s kitchen, complete with top-of-the-line appliances, a sleek wine fridge, and ample counter space for effortless meal prep. Savor your gourmet dishes at the elegant dining table or indulge in relaxed al fresco dining by the sparkling pool.

  • Luxurious Chef’s kitchen featuring premium stainless steel appliances for a top-tier culinary experience
  • Stylish wet bar complete with a wine fridge and built-in ice maker, perfect for entertaining
  • Elegant dining table accommodating 8 guests, complemented by an inviting outdoor dining space for alfresco meals
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Escape to a serene private outdoor courtyard designed for ultimate relaxation. Take a dip in the heated pool, soak away your stress in the luxurious spa, or lounge in one of the cozy seating areas surrounded by tranquil vibes. Fire up the grill in the covered cooking area and savor a delicious meal. End your day unwinding under the starry sky in the soothing warmth of the outdoor sauna—your perfect retreat awaits.

  • Indulge in a luxurious heated private pool and spa, perfect for year-round relaxation and unwinding in style
  • Entertain effortlessly with a fully-equipped grilling station, complete with a premium BBQ and elegant covered seating for unforgettable gatherings
  • Immerse yourself in tranquility with a serene outdoor sauna, thoughtfully designed for ultimate relaxation and rejuvenation of mind and body
  • Escape to a picturesque courtyard, adorned with plush loungers and inviting shaded nooks, creating the perfect oasis of comfort and peace
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

Luxuriously spacious and elegantly designed bedrooms, combined with sleek, modern bathrooms fully equipped with premium amenities, guarantee unparalleled comfort and relaxation for every guest.

  • 1st Floor
    • King Guest Bedroom: Features a luxurious king bed and an en-suite bathroom with a walk-in shower for ultimate comfort.
    • Garage Bunk Room: Perfect for kids or groups, includes 2 twin-over-twin bunk beds, a pool table for entertainment, and a mini fridge for snacks and drinks.
    • Full Guest Bathroom: Conveniently located for easy access.
  • 2nd Floor
    • Primary Suite: Indulge in the king bed, en-suite bathroom with a soaking tub, walk-in shower, and a private balcony offering stunning pool views.
    • King Guest Bedroom: Relax in another king bed with an en-suite bathroom featuring a tub-shower combo.
    • Queen Guest Bedroom: Includes a cozy queen bed and an en-suite bathroom with a walk-in shower.
    • Home Theater: Enjoy movie nights in style with comfortable reclining chairs.
  • Carriage House
    • Queen Bedroom & Kitchenette: A private living space with a separate entrance, featuring a queen bed, a kitchenette for convenience, and a private bathroom.
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Located just 1.1 miles from the charming towns of Seaside and WaterColor, Seagrove offers the perfect blend of relaxation and adventure. Explore boutique shopping, indulge in world-class dining, and immerse yourself in endless outdoor activities. Bike along the scenic Timpoochee Trail, savor a gourmet meal at the renowned Café Thirty-A, or simply soak in the natural beauty of the area. Seagrove truly has something special for everyone.

  • Seagrove Beach East - Only 0.2 mile away, this beach is perfect for laid-back vibes, soft white sand, and crystal-clear waters, ideal for relaxing under the sun.
  • Seaside Beach - A 5-minute drive brings you to this iconic, picturesque spot with its charming coastal town vibe, colorful cottages, and vibrant local shops.
  • Grayton Beach State Park - Just a 9-minute drive away, this serene escape offers pristine nature trails, calm waters, and stunning natural beauty for a peaceful retreat.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included but can be purchased separately.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • Waiver required to get access to the 7 provided bikes and the LSV. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • Pools can only be heated between the dates of September 1st - April 30th.
  • There is an active camera on the back of the carriage house overlooking the backyard as well as a camera over the driveway.
  • The fireplace & firepit are not available for guest use.
  • Please note there is active construction taking place across the street. We do not have a completion date at this time.

Description

30A ESCAPES presents…“Casita By The Sea,” your perfect Rosemary Beach getaway! This pet-friendly 2-bedroom, 3-bath home is steps away from the heated Sky Pool and the Cabana Community Pool. Complete with parking for 2, modern appliances, and 4 bikes for cruising the beautiful neighborhood, it's the ideal retreat for up to 6 guests. Enjoy peace, style, and relaxation. Learn more below…

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Designer Décor and Vaulted Ceilings for a luxurious, airy vibe
  • Pet-Friendly Home with Secluded Outdoor Space
  • Steps to the Heated Sky Pool (3 min walk) and Cabana Community Pool (5 min walk)
  • Parking for 2 Vehicles in the Driveway
  • Includes 4 Bikes for Exploring Rosemary Beach
  • Located in the Heart of the Award-Winning Rosemary Beach Community

*** LIVING AREAS ***

Step into an open-plan living area where vaulted ceilings and oversized windows create a bright, inviting space. Relax on the plush sofas while streaming your favorite movies using the surround sound system. The thoughtful designer touches and natural light make this home both stylish and comfortable.

  • Bright Open Floor Plan with Vaulted Ceilings and Oversized Windows
  • Generous Seating Area with Surround Sound System
  • Stylish Designer Décor for a premium feel
  • Access to a Front Porch
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

For foodies and casual cooks alike, the kitchen offers stainless steel appliances, granite countertops, and everything you need to prepare your meals. Dine indoors at the cozy table or savor al fresco meals in the secluded outdoor oasis.

  • Gourmet Kitchen with Stainless Steel Appliances
  • Spacious Granite Countertops for meal prep
  • Dining Table with Seating for 6 + Kitchen Bar seats 3 more
  • Natural Gas Grill for Outdoor Cooking
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

Whether you're traveling with friends, family, or little ones, the thoughtfully planned bedroom layout ensures there's space and privacy for everyone.

1st Floor:

  • Full Guest Bathroom with Rainfall Shower, perfect for rinsing off after a day at the beach

2nd Floor:

  • Front Primary Suite: King Bed, Private Balcony, French Doors, and En-suite Bathroom with Dual Vanity and Walk-In Shower
  • Rear Primary Suite: King Bed with Private En-suite Bathroom featuring a Glass-Enclosed Shower
  • Bunk Nook: Twin-Over-Twin Bunk Bed (Perfect for Kids!)
*** ROSEMARY BEACH COMMUNITY ***

Perfectly situated in Barrett Square, everything you need is within a short walk. Enjoy casual and fine dining, boutique shopping, and scenic pathways. The renowned Rosemary Beach white sand beach is just a stone's throw away. Plus, access to 4 pristine community pools ensures that relaxation is always within reach,

  • Rosemary Beach Community: Beachside destination with award-winning architecture
  • Racquet Club – one of the world’s top 75 tennis spots
  • Multiple parks & greens with playgrounds, a splash pad, butterfly park, and hidden boardwalks with fountains
  • Fitness Center with massage and yoga
4 RESORT POOLS
  • Barbados Pool: Gulf-side, French West Indies style with a fountain
  • Coquina Pool: Gulf-side, with a relaxing negative edge overflow
  • Sky Pool: Park-side, indoor, heated year-round with radiant heated decking in winter
  • Cabana Pool: Park-side, cabana-style with a children’s pool & playground nearby
*** PET POLICY ***
  • Non-shedding, housebroken dogs only
  • Pets are not allowed on furniture or beds
  • Dogs must be crated when left unattended
  • $250 non-refundable pet fee + tax
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included but can be purchased during checkout or independently
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment
  • Waiver required to get access to the 4 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • Fireplace is not available for guest use.
*** YOUR STAY - OUR PRIORITY ***

At 30A Escapes, we’re committed to providing a luxurious, safe, and stress-free vacation experience. Enjoy 24/7 support and an immaculate home designed for comfort and relaxation.

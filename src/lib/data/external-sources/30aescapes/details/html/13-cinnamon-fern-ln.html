Description

30A ESCAPES presents "13 Cinnamon Fern Ln"—your next unforgettable getaway! Located in NatureWalk, just 1.8 miles from the beach, this pet-friendly retreat feels like home. Start your day with a coffee, enjoy the community pools, or cruise around in the 4-seater LSV. With 6 beach bikes, explore Seaside and WaterColor at your own pace. Perfect for families or friends, this spacious home hosts up to 10 guests. Relax, unwind, and create lasting memories! Learn more below...

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Pet-Friendly Retreat: Bring along your furry companion (fees and restrictions apply).
  • Olympic-Sized Pools: Unwind and soak up the sun just steps from your doorstep.
  • 4-Seater LSV & 6 Bikes: Effortlessly explore the scenic 30A area in style.
  • Elegant Interiors: Open-concept design with cozy seating and plenty of room to relax.
  • Gourmet Kitchen: Equipped with premium appliances and cookware for memorable meals.
  • Spacious Porches: Ideal for savoring your morning coffee or enjoying evening cocktails.
  • Prime Location: A short ride to the charming attractions of Seaside and WaterColor.

*** LIVING AREA ***

The open-concept main living space is thoughtfully designed to blend comfort and connection seamlessly. Unwind on luxurious, plush seating while streaming your favorite shows, or bask in the abundance of natural light streaming through oversized windows. Need more room to spread out? Head upstairs to the additional entertainment area—perfect for a kids' hangout or a peaceful retreat.

  • Expansive open-plan living, kitchen, and dining areas for effortless flow and functionality
  • Bonus upstairs entertainment room offering versatile extra space
  • Multiple TVs and Blu-ray players throughout for ultimate convenience and entertainment
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

The chef’s kitchen is designed to make meal preparation effortless and enjoyable, boasting state-of-the-art appliances, a sleek gas stove, and plenty of counter space for all your cooking needs. The elegant dining area features a spacious table that comfortably seats eight, perfect for gatherings, while the stylish breakfast bar offers additional seating for three, ideal for casual meals or morning coffee.

  • Modern, fully equipped kitchen with premium appliances and a gas stove for gourmet cooking
  • Elegant dining table with seating for up to 8, perfect for family meals or entertaining
  • Chic breakfast bar with seating for 3, creating a cozy spot for quick bites or conversations
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Relax and recharge on not one, but two charming covered porches—ideal for sipping your morning coffee or unwinding with evening wine as the sun sets. After a fun-filled day at the beach, freshen up in the outdoor shower, then gather the family for a delicious BBQ feast.

  • Two inviting covered porches with cozy seating for ultimate relaxation
  • Full-sized BBQ grill, perfect for outdoor dining and family gatherings
  • Convenient outdoor shower to wash away the sand and enjoy a refreshing rinse
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor:

  • Primary Suite: King bed, luxurious en-suite with walk-in shower and plenty of storage
  • Half Bathroom: Conveniently located for guests, featuring modern fixtures

2nd Floor:

  • Primary Suite: King bed, spacious en-suite with a walk-in shower and premium finishes
  • Queen Guest Room: Queen bed with cozy linens, shares a full bathroom with stylish decor
  • Bunk Room: Full-over-full bunk beds, ideal for kids or teens, with ample sleeping space and fun vibes

All bathrooms feature plush towels, starter toiletries, and thoughtful touches. Don’t forget to pack your beach towels for outdoor adventures!

*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

NatureWalk offers a serene retreat nestled in nature, all while placing you just moments away from 30A’s premier attractions. Immerse yourself in the perfect harmony of relaxation and adventure with these incredible nearby destinations:

  • Lily Lane Beach Trail (0.2 miles) – Stroll through a peaceful, scenic trail leading to the serene San Juan beach access, perfect for soaking up the sun and salty breeze.
  • Point Washington State Forest (0.7 miles) – Immerse yourself in nature with hiking and biking trails surrounded by lush greenery and tranquil forest vibes.
  • Seaside and WaterColor (2.8 miles) – Explore these iconic coastal towns, known for their charming streets, vibrant community vibe, and unforgettable sunsets.
  • Nearby Restaurants, Shopping, and Publix – Conveniently located for quick bites, boutique shopping, and essentials to make your stay effortless.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included but can be purchased separately.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • 2 Car Driveway Plus Additional Street Parking
  • Waiver required to get access to the 6 provided bikes and LSV. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • Pet-friendly home with a $250 non-refundable fee + tax.
  • LSV rentals are NOT permitted in Rosemary, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.

Description

30A ESCAPES presents..."Emerald Coast Oasis", a one-of-a-kind retreat in Seacrest West! Relax by your private pool or take a short stroll to the sugar-white sands just steps away. As the sun sets, savor al fresco dining and cocktails under a canopy of stars. Designed with a seamless indoor-outdoor layout and refined custom details, this stunning retreat perfectly blends comfort with sophistication. Plus, enjoy 6 beach cruisers to explore the scenic beauty of 30A in style! Learn more below...

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Steps to the Gulf Coast with deeded beach access – Enjoy effortless access to pristine white sands and turquoise waters.
  • Private Pool – Relax in your own serene oasis, perfect for sunny afternoons or starlit evenings by the pool.
  • Spacious Fenced-In Backyard - Enjoy a lush, green outdoor space with ample room for relaxation and play. Features cozy covered porches and beautifully maintained greenery.
  • Seamless Indoor-Outdoor living on a Single Level – Experience an open, thoughtfully designed layout that prioritizes comfort, connection, and unforgettable moments shared together.
  • Chef’s Kitchen with Premium Appliances and Dual Islands – Cook and entertain in style with a kitchen built for culinary creativity and shared meals.
  • 6 Bikes provided to explore 30A – Discover the charm of nearby beaches and towns on two wheels, with scenic rides perfect for adventure or a leisurely pace.

*** LIVING AREA ***

The open-concept living areas are designed to bring the indoors and outdoors together effortlessly, offering stunning views of the pool at every turn. Expansive floor-to-ceiling windows blur the lines between inside and out, flooding the space with natural light and creating a true indoor-outdoor sanctuary. Plush sofas, a sleek cocktail bar, and smart TVs add comfort, making it the perfect retreat for relaxation.

  • Seamless indoor-outdoor living with breathtaking pool views from the living and dining spaces
  • Spacious open-concept area with plush seating, a stylish cocktail bar, and a smart TV for ultimate comfort
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

This chef-inspired kitchen is a dream come true for food enthusiasts. Featuring two spacious islands and state-of-the-art appliances like ice maker, it streamlines meal preparation with ease. The farmhouse-style dining table with serene pool views comfortably seats up to 10 guests, perfect for gatherings. A dedicated bar area, complete with a wine fridge, invites you to craft the perfect cocktails in style.

  • Six-burner gas stove, dual ovens, and a spacious fridge/freezer to handle all your cooking needs
  • Two sinks and a cocktail bar with a wine fridge for effortless prep and entertaining
  • Two large islands for extra workspace and additional seating
  • Farmhouse dining table for 10, plus seating for 5 at the kitchen island
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Step into your own private oasis! Bask in the Florida sunshine by the seasonally heated pool, surrounded by comfortable loungers, or enjoy a meal in the shaded dining area. Complete with a grill and stylish wet bar, this space is perfect for relaxing days under the sun or magical evenings stargazing beneath the night sky.

  • Private pool seasonally heated from September to April.
  • Outdoor cooking area featuring a gas grill, mini fridge, and sink
  • Spacious shaded patio complete with a lounging area and dining table that seats 8
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

This exquisite villa features four beautifully designed bedrooms and three contemporary bathrooms, perfectly combining comfort and luxury. The primary suite offers direct access to the pool deck, creating a serene private retreat. The additional bedrooms are thoughtfully arranged to accommodate various group needs, each providing convenient access to the modern bathrooms.

  • Primary Suite: King bed, smart TV, and patio access. En-suite with a Jacuzzi tub, double vanity, and rain shower.
  • King Guest Suite: King bed, en-suite with standing shower and stylish black-and-white tiling.
  • Queen Guest Suite: Queen bed, shares access to a spacious hallway bathroom.
  • Queen Guest Suite: Queen bed conveniently located near shared hallway bath.
  • Bonus Room: Two custom Twin-over-Twin bunks, smart TV and lounge area with pool deck access - perfect for kids!
  • Hallway Bathroom: A spacious, well-appointed bathroom featuring a walk-in shower. Conveniently located near the bunk room, it also offers easy access to the pool for added practicality.
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Nestled in the sought-after Seacrest community, “Emerald Coast Oasis” offers the perfect getaway just moments from pristine beaches, exceptional dining, and premier shopping. Discover local favorites at The Big Chill or take a scenic bike ride to the charming neighborhoods of Alys Beach and Rosemary Beach for even more adventure!

  • Deeded beach access – Just a 6-minute walk away, perfect for soaking up the sun and enjoying serene coastal views.
  • The Big Chill – A short ride (4-minute drive) to a lively spot for live music, delicious local eats, and a vibrant community atmosphere.
  • Alys Beach and Rosemary Beach – Both under 5 minutes drive away, offering stunning architecture, boutique shopping, and a relaxed, upscale beach-town vibe.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included but can be purchased separately.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • 3 cars can park off to the side of the house, and 2 cars can park in the front driveway, but the garage is not for guest use.
  • Waiver required to get access to 6 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • The private pool is heated from September 1 to April 30.
  • LSVs are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.

Full Honest Review

We have been coming to this area for 10 years and this house way by FAR the best location & easiest access to the beach ever, which is private btw. Typically when we vacation we have a bike ride to the beach with all our stuff but this time we didn't need them. Literally a walk across the street. It was AMAZING. Also, at some of the previous places we have stayed - there are rows and rows of chairs at the beach and it's overwhelming and unenjoyable. This is not the case here. Maybe 10 sets of chairs and the rest of the area was open and private. The best beach access and privacy ever. Now, let's get into some other stuff, that I wish we knew before renting this home. KITCHEN - fully stocked with keurig and coffee maker, blender and plenty of dishes & cups, All appliances worked beautifully and very easy to use. The fridge was huge, there is a wine cooler and a mini fridge outside - those were nice surprises and we used them all. We bought extra paper towels and toilet paper for the week per the only provide a few per bathroom. You will need trash bags as well. We did wash a fair amount of dishes and the kitchen soap provided was plenty. Kitchen is as appears in all photos. TRASH - we arrived on a friday and the trash can was already Full. Trash days are Monday and Thursday. When we arrived, there was no place to put any trash until Monday when they came. They absolutely need 2 cans and only one is provided so be prepared for overflow and no recycling cans provided. We had an abundance of trash every day because of this. The rental company comes and places the cans on the street and then they return later in the day to put the can back. We ended up just moving the cans back and forth to the street so they didn't have to do that. NO EXTRAS - There are NO extras in regards to pillows or blankets. We were looking for blankets to snuggle and watch movies and found none. Not a big deal at all - just something to note if you have littles. BEDROOM - by front door - this used to be an office that they turned into a bedroom, there is NO ceiling fan in that room - so we had to buy a fan for that room per it was super hot and muggy since it's the front of the house, we flew to florida so we left the fan there since we couldn't take it home. It got hot in there. Also, there is NO closet in that room (per it was an office) and it lacks privacy - the doors are french doors (glass) with white light see through curtains. Not a huge problem, but something to note. Not private. The Other Queen Bedroom - it did NOT have a ceiling fan, but stayed much cooler and it has a closet. All other bedrooms were as expected and nothing to report on those. All the bedrooms are beautifully decorated, light in color and very beautiful. BATHROOMS - were as expected, they provided plenty towels, wash cloths and hand towels, all beautifully decorated and as expected. LAUNDRY - was as expected and very roomy and easy to use. It was nice having that space and they have hooks provided for hanging items to dry. Very convenient. POOL AREA - the pool is amazing and they have a company clean it 2 times a week. Very clean and coming back from the beach to the pool was amazing. The furniture provided outside was very nice and we used all if it. The large table was nice for family dinners, and the grill was clean and we used it several times. The only negative thing I can say about the outside area is the landscaping - it's NOT like it appears in the pictures, it's overgrown out front by front porch and door and in the backyard - it definitely needs attention and trimming, however, this would not stop us from renting this home again, just don't be disappointed if it doesn't appear as in pics. We were concerned about this house being on main road and the noise, but that is not the case. It's quiet and feels private and we had no issues with anything like that. NO EXTRAS - this is important because we ended up buying a lot of stuff that was no provided. I understand in the description of this rental that they do not provide any beach stuff, etc. But this is the first rental in 10 years of us coming to this area that literally means it - there wasn't anything. Normally there is a bucket of balls or a football or something.. So we bought 2 tubes to float on in the pool, football, skip ball, flashlights, crab net, & crab shovel. We were able to bring it all home so that was nice, but it would be nice if there were something. Again, wouldn't stop us from renting this home. Overall, this home is beautiful, decorated well, spacious, very clean, convenient and we would ABSOLUTELY rent this home again. We love florida and we had an amazing trip.

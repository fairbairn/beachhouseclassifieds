Description

30A ESCAPES presents... "Driftwood Dream," where coastal charm meets laid-back luxury in Seagrove Beach. Nestled in a gated community with tranquil pools and just a breezy 2-minute bike ride to sugar-white sands, this 4-suite retreat, perfect for 10, welcomes you to unwind with its retro kitchen vibe, dreamy screened porches, and 6 beach bikes ready for adventure. It’s more than a stay—it’s a sun-soaked escape made for families and friends to create lasting memories! Learn more below...

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Prime Location in Gated Seagrove Community - Experience the exclusivity and tranquility of a private, secure neighborhood nestled in the heart of Seagrove.
  • 2-Minute Bike Ride to Beach Access - Feel the ocean breeze as you pedal to the sparkling shoreline in just minutes.
  • Access to 2 Community Pools - Relax and refresh with not one, but two serene pools just steps from your door.
  • 6 Bikes Included - Explore the charming surroundings on two wheels—adventure is ready and waiting!
  • Spacious 4 BDR/4.5 Bath Layout (Sleeps 10) - Bring the whole crew! This roomy retreat is perfect for families and groups, with space to unwind and make memories.
  • Retro-Inspired Kitchen with Orange Appliances - Cook in style with a playful, vintage twist that adds character and charm to every meal.
  • Screened-In Porches Overlooking Wooded Scenery - Savor your morning coffee or evening wine while soaking in peaceful views of the lush, natural surroundings.
  • Proximity to Seaside (5-Min Bike Ride) & Nearby Restaurants - Enjoy the perfect blend of convenience and adventure, with access to vibrant Seaside and a variety of local dining options just a short ride away.

*** LIVING AREA ***

Immerse yourself in coastal serenity with the thoughtfully designed first-floor open layout. The living space exudes warmth and charm with decorative fireplace, luxurious sectional seating, and bright, beach-inspired décor that perfectly reflects the beauty of the surrounding shoreline.

  • Expansive First-Floor Living Area with Comfortable, Stylish Seating
  • Charming Second-Floor Lounge with a Large TV & Screened Porch for Relaxation
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Whether you're whipping up a hearty family breakfast or crafting a luxurious evening feast, this retro-style kitchen is sure to inspire. Adorned with vibrant orange appliances and practical open shelving, it combines distinctive charm with functionality. Gather around the sophisticated dining table or enjoy casual moments at the stylish kitchen bar seating, creating memories over delicious meals in a space that’s as inviting as it is unique.

  • Stylish Retro Kitchen with Open Shelving & Vibrant Orange Appliances
  • Spacious Dining Table Perfect for Hosting Up to 6 Guests
  • Comfortable Additional Seating for 4 at the Sleek Kitchen Island
*** OUTDOOR COMMUNITY AMENITIES ***

Immerse yourself in a tranquil outdoor lifestyle with unparalleled access to two sparkling community pools just moments from your doorstep. The spacious patio is an ideal haven for grilling and entertaining, while the surrounding wooded scenery creates a serene backdrop, elevating your sense of peace and relaxation.

  • Two Stunning Community Pools Just Steps from Your Door
  • Beautifully Paved Patio with Outdoor Shower & Premium Propane Grill
  • Spacious Screened-In Porches with Peaceful Views of a Wooded Backyard

  • Hall Bathroom: Full Bath and Walk-In Shower Conveniently Located Across from the Bunk Room
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Tucked away in the exclusive gated community of the Hammocks of Seagrove, Driftwood Dream offers the perfect blend of convenience and tranquility. Explore the scenic Timpoochee Trail on a leisurely bike ride, soak in the charm of Seaside, just a quick 5-minute pedal away, or treat yourself to mouthwatering meals at local gems like Café Thirty-A and Surfing Deer. Whether you're seeking adventure or relaxation, Driftwood Dream places you at the heart of it all while ensuring your peace and privacy.

  • Seagrove Beach East (9-Min Walk) – A peaceful escape with soft sands and gentle waves, perfect for a relaxing stroll.
  • Seaside (6-Min Bike Ride) – A charming, vibrant town with pastel cottages, local shops, and a welcoming small-town vibe.
  • Grayton Beach State Park (11-Min Drive) – A natural haven for outdoor lovers, offering serene trails, crystal-clear waters, and unspoiled beauty.
  • Local Dining Spots Nearby – Enjoy delectable bites at spots like Café Thirty-A and Market Café, blending cozy atmospheres with incredible flavors.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included but can be purchased separately.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • Waiver required to get access to the 6 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • LSV Rentals are NOT permitted in Rosemary, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.
  • Please note this property does NOT have cable television - streaming services only.
  • There is an active Ring doorbell camera at the entrance door of the home.

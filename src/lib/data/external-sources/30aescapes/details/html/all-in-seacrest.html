Description

30A ESCAPES presents ...“All In - Seacrest”, your ultimate Seacrest West retreat where luxury meets laid-back living. The highlight of this property is the outdoor space, featuring a private pool, a spacious fenced-in yard, and plenty of room for sunset dinners or starry-night chats. Just a 3-minute stroll to sugar-white sands with deeded beach access, it’s perfectly located between Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach. Explore scenic 30A with loved ones on 8 bikes provided. Learn more below...

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Steps to the Beach: Just a leisurely 3-minute walk to pristine sands and turquoise waters
  • Private Poolside Oasis: Relax in the spacious fenced backyard with a seasonally heated pool, fire pit, and lounging areas
  • Designed for Entertaining: Outdoor kitchen with grill, screened-in porch, and plenty of seating for group dinners
  • Spacious Accommodations: 2634 sq ft including kid-friendly rooms and multiple en-suites
  • Perfect Location: Situated between Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach with easy access to dining, shops, and activities
  • 8 Bikes Included: Explore scenic 30A with 8 guest-use beach bikes

*** LIVING AREA ***

Step into a welcoming open-concept living space designed for comfort and connection. The newly expanded living room is perfect for gathering, with a plush sectional sofa, smart TV, and French doors that open directly to the backyard oasis. Enjoy the screened-in porch with outdoor seating and a dining area, perfect for relaxing or sharing meals while surrounded by nature. Meanwhile, the second-floor kids’ den offers a playful retreat with arcade and board games.

  • Living Room - 1st Floor: A spacious sectional, seamless pool access, and a smart TV with YouTube TV for family fun.
  • Screened-In Porch- 1st Floor: Comfortable outdoor seating and a dining area for relaxing or entertaining.
  • Kids’ Den - 2nd Floor: A game table, arcade games, board games, puzzles, and cards, along with access to a private deck.
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Create memorable meals in the fully renovated kitchen featuring granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and an oversized island with barstool seating. With room for everyone, the dining area offers two large tables, accommodating up to 12 guests in total.

  • Chef’s Kitchen: Fully equipped with cookware, crockpot, blender, and both a drip coffee maker + Keurig
  • Ample Dining: Two dining tables with seating for 10, plus kitchen island for 4 and more - perfect for large groups
  • Outdoor Dining Options: Screened-in porch with dining for 6 and 2 picnic tables accommodating 12 more by the outdoor kitchen
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Your dream backyard awaits! The center of attention is the private seasonally heated pool, surrounded by loungers, picnic tables, and two shaded umbrellas. End evenings under the stars, sharing laughs around the fire pit or grilling fresh seafood on the propane BBQ.

  • Private Pool: Perfect for relaxation year-round, seasonally heated between Sept-April
  • Dining Al Fresco: Outdoor kitchen with built-in grill and alfresco dining options for all the crew
  • Outdoor Entertaining: Wood-burning Fire Pit with outdoor seating
  • Fenced-In Yard: Ideal for both fun and privacy

  • Queen Guest Bedroom: Queen bed with smart TV and a full en-suite bathroom featuring a glass-enclosed shower

*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Nestled between the charming neighborhoods of Rosemary Beach and Alys Beach, All In - Seacrest perfectly captures the laid-back coastal lifestyle. Located at 78 Pelican Glide Lane, this home offers easy access to the best of 30A, all within walking or a short driving distance. Here's what’s nearby:

  • Deeded Beach Access (3-minute walk): With easy walking distance to the deeded beach access point All In - Seacrest puts you at the heart of 30A’s charm, making it the perfect base for your coastal getaway.
  • The Big Chill (4-minute drive): A vibrant hub featuring world-class entertainment, live music, movies on a jumbo screen, family-friendly fun, and coastal shops. With its diverse dining options and lively atmosphere, it’s the perfect spot to relax and soak in local vibes.
  • Alys Beach (5-minute bike ride): Stunning architecture, scenic pathways, and a serene atmosphere make this a must-visit destination for anyone seeking beauty and tranquility.
  • Rosemary Beach (5-minute drive): A picturesque storybook town known for its cobblestone streets, upscale dining, boutique shopping, and a laid-back, coastal charm ideal for a leisurely afternoon.
  • Publix Supermarket (6-minute drive): Conveniently located for all your grocery needs, from fresh ingredients to snacks, ensuring your stay is as easy as it is enjoyable.

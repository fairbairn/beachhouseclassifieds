Description
30A ESCAPES presents..."Happy Place", a stunning penthouse in Seacrest Beach. Start your day with coffee overlooking the Lagoon Pool. This isn’t just a stay—it’s where luxury meets comfort. Dive into fresh pool waters, cook together in the Gourmet Kitchen, explore 30A on 6 included bikes, or stroll to the beach to unwind by the waves. Perfect for family vacations or friend getaways, "Happy Place" is exactly how you’ll feel here. Ready to make it yours? Learn more below…👇*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Top Floor Condo with Private Elevator Entrance: Enjoy the exclusivity of a private elevator opening directly into your top-floor retreat.
  • Lagoon Pool Views: Unwind with serene views of the sparkling lagoon pool from your private oversized balcony.
  • Fully Equipped Gourmet Kitchen: Cook like a pro with a modern kitchen featuring sleek stainless steel appliances.
  • Spacious Accommodations for 10: Enjoy 2 primary suites and balcony access, plus a Queen bedroom and a bunk room with a shared Jack and Jill bathroom.
  • 6 Bikes Included: Explore 30A on included bikes for a fun, scenic ride.
  • Close to Beaches and Local Attractions: Easily access Rosemary Beach, Alys Beach, and nearby shops and restaurants.

*** LIVING AREAS ***

Relax in the open-concept living space with plush seating and stunning Lagoon Pool views through floor-to-ceiling sliding glass doors. Perfect for family movie nights or enjoying the game.

  • Comfortable sofas with ample seating
  • 65” Smart TV
  • Direct access to an oversized balcony for seamless indoor-outdoor living
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Cook like a chef in the spacious gourmet kitchen, featuring high-end appliances, quartz countertops, and a marble backsplash. The adjacent dining area seats 10, ideal for shared meals or game nights.

  • Fully equipped kitchen with stainless steel appliances
  • Quartz countertops and marble backsplash
  • Dining table with seating for up to 10
  • Additional seating for 4 at the kitchen island for casual meals
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Step onto the oversized covered balcony for breathtaking Lagoon Pool views garnished with palms and a poolside cafe. This peaceful spot is perfect for morning coffee or evening cocktails.

  • Oversized balcony with lounge seating and dining space for 6
  • Stunning Lagoon Pool views
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***
  • Primary Suite: King-size bed, private balcony access, flat-screen TV, and En-Suite with jetted soaking tub and walk-in shower
  • King Guest Room: King-size bed, private balcony access, flat-screen TV, and En-Suite with tub/shower combo
  • Queen Guest Room: Queen-size bed, private balcony, shared Jack-and-Jill bathroom
  • Bunk Room: 2 Twin-over-Twin bunk beds (sleeps 4), flat-screen TV, and shared Jack-and-Jill bathroom access
  • Shared Bathroom: Jack-and-Jill style with tub/shower combo
  • Half Bathroom: Additional convenience for guests
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Nestled in the heart of Seacrest Beach, "Happy Place" is steps away from boutique shops, dining, and local attractions. Explore nearby Rosemary and Alys Beaches or enjoy easy tram service to the beach.

  • Lagoon Pool – A 12,000 sq. ft. paradise Directly Out the Door, featuring a zero-entry splash zone, waterfalls, and plenty of loungers.
  • Beach Access – Seasonal tram service makes getting to the beach easy, perfect for carrying your gear.
  • Peddler’s Pavilion – A quick 5-minute walk to boutique shops, live music, dining, and seasonal nightlife.
  • Rosemary Beach – Only a 7-minute walk to this scenic coastal destination.
  • Alys Beach – Just an 11-minute walk for picturesque views and charming experiences.
  • Scenic Walking & Nature Trails – Explore beautiful trails just steps away.
  • Bike-Friendly Community – Enjoy the included bikes for easy access to nearby spots.
  • Outdoor Amenities – Relax with outdoor seating, loungers, picnic tables, and a grill station.
  • South Walton’s Tallest Sand Dunes – A unique natural feature of this stunning area.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included in our quotes. It can be purchased during the reservation process or obtained independently.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • This property has 6 bikes for guest use. To utilize the bikes, a waiver must be signed before the code is provided to the guest. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • LSVs are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.

Description

30A ESCAPES invites you to “Endless Summer Escape” in Seacrest Beach! This pet-friendly retreat is just a 4-min walk to the 12,000 sqft Lagoon Pool and close to Rosemary and Alys Beach. Enjoy a fully-equipped kitchen, stylish décor, and spacious living areas. Included are 6 beach bikes for exploring nearby shops, dining, and seaside views. Perfect for families, friends, and their pets, this getaway blends relaxation and adventure. Create lifelong memories—book your stay today!

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Just a 4-Min Walk to the Stunning Lagoon Pool – 12,000 sq ft of clear water, a splash zone, and ultimate relaxation await nearby.
  • Pet Friendly – Bring your dog on your vacation (fees and restrictions apply)!
  • 6 Bikes Included – Cruise through the scenic 30A area, feel the ocean breeze, and discover hidden gems effortlessly.
  • Seasonal Tram Service – Stress-free beach days await! Let the tram take you straight to the soft, sandy shores.
  • Walking Distance to Rosemary and Alys Beach – Walk to charming shops, dining, and the vibrant energy of iconic 30A destinations in under 10 minutes.
  • Perfect for Groups – Plenty of space for family or friends to relax, bond, and make memories together.

*** LIVING AREA ***

Unwind in a spacious, sunlit living area infused with coastal charm. A cozy sectional sofa and smart TV set the stage for perfect movie nights, while the inviting front porch provides a serene spot to soak up the breeze.

  • Open layout with beach-chic décor
  • Sectional sofa with a smart TV
  • Front porch with seating for four
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Cook, dine, and BBQ with ease in the fully-equipped kitchen and outdoor spaces. Inside, enjoy stainless steel appliances and a wet bar with a wine fridge. Outside, fire up the grill on the porch for perfect BBQs. Gather around the dining table for 8 or enjoy quick meals at the breakfast bar.

  • Modern kitchen with stainless steel appliances
  • Wet bar with wine fridge for entertaining
  • Dining table (seats 8) and breakfast bar (seats 2)
  • Grill on the back porch
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor:

  • Guest Bedroom: King Bed with dedicated shared bathroom
  • Full Bathroom: Walk-in Shower, Single Vanity

2nd Floor:

  • Primary Suite: King Bed, private balcony, flat-screen TV, En-suite with Walk-in Shower, Jacuzzi Tub, Double Vanity
  • Guest Bedroom: Queen Bed, shared Jack & Jill Bathroom
  • Bunk Bedroom: Twin-Over-Full Bunk (shared Jack & Jill Bathroom)
*** LOCATION, OUTDOOR & COMMUNITY AMENITIES ***

Nestled in the vibrant Seacrest Beach community, this home offers easy access to local attractions and top-notch amenities. Walk or bike to the Lagoon Pool, Rosemary Beach, and Alys Beach, or explore the shops, dining, and live music at Peddler’s Pavilion. Equipped with 6 beach bikes, it makes exploring the area both easy and enjoyable.

  • 12,000 sq ft Lagoon Pool – A stunning paradise with a zero-entry splash zone, waterfalls, and plenty of loungers, plus a poolside café and picnic areas.
  • Beach Access – Enjoy a seasonal tram for easy transportation or take a short 10-minute stroll to the emerald Gulf waters.
  • 6 Bikes Included – Perfect for exploring nearby attractions like Peddler’s Pavilion, Rosemary Beach, and Alys Beach.
  • Peddler’s Pavilion – Just a 2-minute bike ride or 5-minute walk to boutique shops, live music, dining, and seasonal nightlife.
  • Scenic Walking & Nature Trails – Picturesque paths just steps from the house, ideal for exploration and relaxation.
  • Walkable, Bike-Friendly Community – Conveniently access beautiful destinations all around.
  • South Walton’s Tallest Sand Dunes – A unique and stunning natural feature of the area.
  • Rosemary Beach (8-min walk) and Alys Beach (10-min walk) – Explore charming coastal communities nearby.
  • Carillon Beach (7-min drive) – More coastal beauty just minutes away.
*** PET-FRIENDLY ***
  • Pets are not allowed on furniture, beds, or in the pool.
  • Dogs must be crated when left unattended by guests.
  • Non-refundable pet fee: $250 + tax.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included in our quotes. It can be purchased during the reservation process or obtained independently.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • This property has 6 bikes for guest use. To utilize the bikes, a waiver must be signed before the code is provided to the guest. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • LSVs are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.
  • Pet-friendly with a non-refundable pet fee of $250 + tax.

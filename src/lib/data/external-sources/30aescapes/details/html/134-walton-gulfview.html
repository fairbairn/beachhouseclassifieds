Description

30A ESCAPES presents… "134 Walton Gulfview", a coastal gem in Seacrest Beach West. This luxurious retreat features a private heated pool, exclusive deeded beach access, and large outdoor living space, including an outdoor kitchen and fireplace. With spacious interiors and a prime location near 30A hotspots like Alys Beach, Seagrove, and Rosemary Beach, your perfect beach escape awaits. Learn more below…👇

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Steps to the Beach: Just a 3-minute walk to private deeded beach access
  • Private Pool: Year-round enjoyment surrounded by lush landscaping. The pool is heated September-April
  • Outdoor Living Space: Large area with an outdoor kitchen, fireplace, and seating - perfect for al fresco dining.
  • Culinary Haven: Chef’s kitchen with Quartzite countertops and Thermador appliances
  • Sleeping Arrangements: 4 bedrooms for up to 11 guests, each with private baths
  • Bikes Included: 6 beach bikes for exploring the 30A area
  • Prime Location: Minutes from Alys Beach, Seagrove, and Rosemary Beach

Welcome to "134 Walton Gulfview", a luxurious retreat perfectly nestled in Seacrest Beach West. Just a short stroll to the beach, this stunning property offers privacy, modern amenities, and thoughtful touches for a relaxing getaway.

Whether lounging by the heated pool, enjoying views from the balcony, or dining al fresco in the outdoor kitchen, this home is designed for unforgettable moments.

*** LIVING AREAS ***
  • Open-concept layout: Modern coastal decor
  • Large windows: Allowing natural light throughout
  • 83-inch Smart TV and SONOS sound system: For entertainment
  • Direct access: To outdoor living areas
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***
  • Thermador appliances: With Quartzite countertops
  • Large island: Seating for casual dining and conversations
  • Dining table: Seating for 8
  • Fully stocked: With cookware, utensils, and small appliances
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***
  • Private heated pool: With tanning ledge surrounded by lush landscaping
  • Expansive covered porches: For relaxing and entertaining
  • Outdoor kitchen: With grill, sink, and seating
  • Outdoor fireplace: Perfect for cozy evenings under the stars
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***
  • 1st Floor Primary Suite: King bed, soaking tub, and dual-head shower
  • 2nd Floor Bedrooms: 2 King suites with private bathrooms
  • 3rd Floor Bunk Area: Queen-over-queen bunk bed, Twin Trundle, suite with walk-in shower, and porch
*** SEACREST WEST BEACH COMMUNITY ***
  • Proximity to the Beach: Just a 3-minute walk to the emerald waters and sugar-white sandy beach via deeded beach access
  • Biking Convenience: A short 5-7 minute bike ride to Alys Beach, Seagrove, or Rosemary Beach
  • Safety First: Exterior security cameras are in place for peace of mind
  • Quiet Atmosphere: Perfect for families and small groups looking for relaxation
  • Local Favorites: Enjoy nearby communities, boutique shopping, and fine dining at 30A hotspots
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance: NOT included in our quotes. It can be purchased during the reservation process or obtained independently
  • Payment Policy: We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • Pool Heating: Pools can only be heated between the dates of September 1st - April 30th.
  • Bikes Provided: This property has 6 bikes for guest use. To utilize the bikes, a waiver must be signed before the code is provided to the guest. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes
  • Exterior Security Cameras: Active cameras located on the front porch, back patio, and facing the pool equipment
  • Construction: There is ongoing construction taking place next door with expected completion of March 31, 2026.

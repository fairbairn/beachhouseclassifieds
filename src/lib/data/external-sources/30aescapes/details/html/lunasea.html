Description

30A ESCAPES proudly presents... “LunaSea”, a renovated luxury home in the heart of Seagrove Beach. Just a 5-min walk to the sugar-white sands and a breezy bike ride to the iconic Seaside, this stunning retreat is your gateway to 30A. Unwind on the spacious screened porch, explore the area with 4 included bikes, and create unforgettable memories in a home that comfortably hosts up to 8 guests. Ready to experience the ultimate coastal getaway? Your dream escape starts here! Learn more below…👇

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • 5-Min Walk to Seagrove Beach’s white sands – Feel the gentle sea breeze and enjoy easy access to pristine, powdery shores.
  • 5-Min Bike Ride to Seaside shops & restaurants – A quick, carefree ride brings you to charming boutiques and delicious dining options in the heart of Seaside.
  • Screened Porch with ample seating & TV – Relax in comfort on the cozy porch, perfect for unwinding after a long day at the beach.
  • Renovated in 2022 with modern finishes – Enjoy the sleek, updated design that blends contemporary style with coastal charm.
  • 3 Adult Bikes Included for exploration – Discover the neighborhood and coastal beauty on your own schedule with free bikes to roam.
  • Spacious 3 Bedrooms & 3 Baths for a group of 8 – Plenty of room for everyone to gather, relax, and create lasting memories together.

*** LIVING AREA ***

Experience the perfect blend of comfort and style in LunaSea’s beautifully designed open-concept living spaces, crafted to create memorable moments with family and friends. Step onto the screened-in back porch, a versatile second living area where you can savor your morning coffee, bask in the fresh air, or relax while enjoying your favorite shows. Every detail is thoughtfully designed to make LunaSea your ultimate retreat.

  • Spacious, sun-filled living room with plush seating and a large smart TV for ultimate comfort and entertainment
  • Inviting screened porch featuring a dining table for 4, cozy sofa, comfortable chairs, and a charming porch swing bed—perfect for relaxing or dining alfresco
  • Warm and cozy second living area with an outdoor TV, ideal for enjoying the big game or a movie night under the stars
*** KITCHEN & DINING ***

At the heart of LunaSea lies its stunning, state-of-the-art kitchen, designed to impress and inspire. Outfitted with premium, top-of-the-line appliances and every essential tool imaginable, it’s perfect for crafting anything from a relaxed morning breakfast to an extraordinary gourmet feast that will leave the entire crew in awe.

  • Modern, sleek stainless steel appliances that combine style and durability, perfect for any contemporary kitchen.
  • Bright, open-concept layout seamlessly connecting the dining and living areas—perfect for family gatherings.
  • Elegant dining table for 6, complemented by extra seating for 3 at the stylish island.
  • Screen porch area featuring a dining table that comfortably seats 6, ideal for enjoying meals outdoors.
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Unwind in your beautifully crafted backyard oasis. Whether you're grilling a delicious feast for friends, lounging under the stars on the cozy screened porch, or simply soaking up the serene ambiance, the outdoor space at LunaSea offers the perfect blend of comfort and charm, creating an unforgettable retreat.

  • Premium gas grill, perfect for effortless casual cookouts
  • Spacious screened porch with a large TV and ample, comfortable seating for everyone
  • Vibrant, lush backyard framed by Seagrove Beach’s iconic live oaks, offering a serene escape
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

Spanning two expansive levels, LunaSea is the perfect retreat for families or groups, offering thoughtfully designed sleeping arrangements that combine privacy, luxurious comfort, and ample space to relax, recharge, and make lasting memories.

1st Floor:

  • Bunk Room – Perfect for kids or group stays, this room features 2 cozy Twin-over-Twin bunk beds (sleeps 4) and is conveniently located next to a full bathroom.
  • Full Bathroom – Easily accessible, offering comfort and convenience for all guests.

2nd Floor:

  • Primary Suite – Relax in luxury with a King-sized bed, a private balcony to soak in the views, and an En-Suite bathroom featuring dual sinks and dual shower heads for a spa-like experience.
  • Queen Guest Bedroom – A serene retreat with a Queen-sized bed and a private En-Suite, complete with a beautifully tiled tub/shower combo for ultimate comfort.
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

LunaSea puts you right in the heart of everything 30A has to offer. Stroll just 5 minutes to sink your toes into the pristine white sands of the beach, or venture to nearby Seaside, a charming coastal town filled with unique boutiques, award-winning restaurants, and a laid-back vibe that invites you to relax and explore. Whether you're seeking sun-soaked adventures or a taste of local culture, LunaSea is your perfect home base.

  • Hickory Street Beach Access – Just a 7-minute walk away, this serene beach spot offers soft sand, gentle waves, and beautiful sunsets for a relaxing escape.
  • Seaside Town Center – Only 0.7 miles (a quick 3-minute bike ride), this charming hub is filled with boutique shops, local art, and a vibrant coastal vibe that’s perfect for exploring.
  • Grayton Beach State Park – A 9-minute drive will take you to this breathtaking natural oasis, where lush trails meet pristine beaches, creating the perfect blend of adventure and tranquility.
  • Café Thirty-A & Surfing Deer Restaurants – Located nearby (1.5 miles), these restaurants offer a modern, upscale dining experience with fresh, coastal-inspired cuisine to satisfy your cravings.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included but can be purchased separately.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • Waiver required to get access to the 4 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • LSV (Low-Speed Vehicle) is NOT allowed in Rosemary, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.
  • There is an active camera located outside of the home by the second-floor window.

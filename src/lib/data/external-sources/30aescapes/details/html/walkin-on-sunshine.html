Description

30A ESCAPES presents… “Walkin' on Sunshine”, your dream Seacrest Beach getaway! Wake up to Gulf views in this 3-story home, perfect for families and friends to make memories. Just a 4-minute stroll to the 12,000 sq ft Lagoon Pool or sugar-white sands! With 4 private suites, everyone has space to unwind. Plus, 6 bikes are included to explore Seacrest, and nearby Alys Beach, and Rosemary Beach. This isn’t just a trip—it’s your best beach life. Ready to feel the sunshine? Learn more below…

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Steps to the Lagoon Pool and Beach Access (3-4 Minute Walk) – Enjoy effortless relaxation with just a quick stroll to the shimmering 12,000 sq ft Lagoon pool or the sandy beaches—pure paradise at your doorstep.
  • Amazing Gulf Views from 3rd-Floor Balcony – Savor tranquil moments with stunning, subtle Gulf views from the third-floor balcony—a perfect spot for morning coffee or sunset cocktails.
  • Fully Updated with Designer Furniture and New Roof – Indulge in modern luxury with stylish designer touches and the peace of mind of a freshly updated space.
  • Sleeps 14 Comfortably: 4 Suites, 3 Private Balconies – Plenty of room for family and friends to unwind, with spacious accommodations and private balconies that offer cozy spots to relax.
  • Includes 6 Bikes to Explore Scenic 30A – Adventure awaits! Cruise along the scenic 30A trails and explore the beauty of the area and nearby Alys and Rosemary Beach.

Relax in the spacious first-floor living area with plush seating and modern coastal design. It's the perfect spot to unwind after a fun day exploring 30A. Gather the family for movie nights or meals in the open-concept space.

  • Open-Concept Living and Dining Area
  • Smart TV and Ample Comfortable Seating
  • Access to a Relaxing First-Floor Porch

Head up to the third-floor sitting area for even more relaxation. Enjoy a cozy space with a Smart TV and step out onto the balcony for breathtaking Gulf views to enjoy with a beverage of your choice in your hand.

  • Third-Floor Sitting Area with Smart TV
  • Balcony with outdoor furniture Gulf Views Access
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Cook up a storm in the fully updated chef’s kitchen, equipped with top-of-the-line appliances and all the cookware you need. The dining area is perfect for large groups or casual meals.

  • Fully Renovated Kitchen with Modern Appliances
  • Dining Table Seats 6 + Additional Bar Seating for 2
  • Ample Dishware, Utensils, and Cooking Tools
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor:

  • King Guest Bedroom: 1 King Bed with private patio access, smart TV and dedicated bathroom with walk-in shower

2nd Floor:

  • Primary Bedroom: 1 King Bed with smart TV, dedicated work space and en-suite bathroom with dual vanities and glass-enclosed shower
  • Queen Guest Room: 2 Queen Beds with private terrace access, smart TV and en-suite bathroom with walk-in shower

3rd Floor:

  • Bunk Room: 2 Twin-over-Full Bunk Beds with en-suite bathroom with tub/shower combo
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Enjoy the fresh coastal breeze from 3 private balconies or take a quick stroll to the 12,000 sq ft Lagoon Pool. Bikes are provided to explore all that 30A has to offer. Plus, enjoy easy access to boutique shops, restaurants, and live music at Peddler’s Pavilion, with neighboring Rosemary and Alys Beach just minutes away.

  • 4-Minute Walk to 12,000 sq ft Lagoon Pool with Waterfall Features – Enjoy the tropical vibes of this stunning pool, designed for ultimate relaxation and fun. Plus, easy beach access adds to the coastal experience.
  • 6-Minute Walk to Alys Beach – Immerse yourself in the sophisticated, Mediterranean-inspired beauty of Alys Beach, with its iconic white architecture and serene atmosphere.
  • 10-Minute Walk to Rosemary Beach Shops and Restaurants – Stroll through the lively streets of Rosemary Beach, filled with boutique shops, charming eateries, and a vibrant, welcoming vibe.
  • 6 Bikes Provided – Cruise along the picturesque 30A trails, feeling the fresh breeze and exploring the charm of the area with ease.
  • Ample Seating and Scenic Views – Relax and soak in the peaceful surroundings, perfect for morning coffee or evening sunsets.
  • South Walton’s Tallest Sand Dunes – A unique and stunning natural feature of the area.
  • Carillon Beach (7-min drive) – More coastal beauty just minutes away

  • Travel Insurance is NOT included in quotes but can be added at checkout.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • Waiver required to get access to 6 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • LSVs are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.

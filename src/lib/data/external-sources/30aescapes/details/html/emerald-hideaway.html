Description

30A ESCAPES presents... "Emerald Hideaway," an incredible beachfront retreat in the exclusive Carillon Beach community at Pinnacle Port! With unobstructed Gulf views, access to private beach entry steps away, two pools, tennis courts, fitness center, and a community dock on Powell Lake, this completely renovated condo with 3 en-suites is a haven for relaxation and fun. Immerse yourself in luxury with new furnishings, tasteful décor, and modern resort-like amenities. Learn more below !

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • 180° Gulf Views: Enjoy stunning, unobstructed views right from your private beachfront balcony.
  • Steps From the Sand: Direct community beach access just a few moments away.
  • Amenity-Rich Community: Includes two pools, a fitness center, dock access, tennis courts, and a sauna.
  • Luxury Interiors: Fully renovated condo featuring all-new furniture and modern finishes.
  • Fully-Equipped Kitchen: Features stainless steel appliances, cookware, utensils, and everything needed to prepare meals just like at home.
  • Bedroom Setup: Gulf Front Primary Suite, Guest Room with Queen and Twin Bunk Bed with En-suite, and Queen Room with dedicated hall bathroom.
  • Convenient Dining Options: Just a 10-minute walk to Carillon’s best shops and restaurants.
  • Family Friendly: Includes a travel high chair and beach chairs for guest use
  • Beach Bikes: Enjoy exploring the area with 4 adult beach bikes provided for guest use.
*** BEACHFRONT LIVING ***

Welcome to the ultimate beachfront oasis, where waking up with the sound of waves is your daily routine. "Emerald Hideaway" is a beautifully renovated 7th-floor condo located in the exclusive Carillon Beach community. With three bedrooms and three bathrooms, it comfortably accommodates up to 8 guests. The expansive Gulf views set the stage for unforgettable sunrises and sunsets—all visible from your wrap-around balcony. Whether you’re lounging by one of the two community pools, taking a paddle on Powell Lake, or enjoying a quiet moment on the beach, this retreat provides the rest and adventure you need.

*** LIVING AREA ***
  • Gulf-view living room with large windows and balcony access

  • Flat-screen Smart TV for ultimate entertainment
  • Gulf-front wrap-around balcony with seating & sunrise/sunset views
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***
  • Fully stocked kitchen with modern appliances, including a Keurig coffee maker
  • Dining table seating for 6 & additional bar stools at the breakfast counter
  • Open layout perfect for entertaining
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***
  • Primary Suite: King bed, en suite bathroom with dual vanity & glass shower, Gulf views
  • Guest Bedroom: Twin over twin bunk bed + queen bed, en suite bathroom with tub/shower combo
  • Second Guest Room: Queen bed, smart TV with access to the dedicated hallway bathroom featuring a walk-in shower
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***
  • Two Community Pools: One heated, one partially covered
  • Poolside Movies: Enjoy movies on Tuesday evenings during the summer season
  • Grilling Stations: Perfect for outdoor cooking

  • Powell Lake Dock: Private access for paddleboarding or kayaking

  • Gated Community: Ensuring privacy and security

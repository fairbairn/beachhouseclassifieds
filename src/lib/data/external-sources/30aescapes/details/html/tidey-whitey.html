Description

30A ESCAPES presents…"Tidey Whitey", a luxurious Seacrest Beach West retreat featuring a private heated pool, spa, and a charming carriage house. Accommodating up to 14 guests, this home offers a chef’s kitchen with top-tier appliances, multiple living areas, and expansive porches overlooking the pool. Just a short walk to prime beach access and close to Alys and Rosemary Beach, it's the perfect destination for families or groups seeking a premium 30A getaway.

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Steps to the Beach: Just a short walk to Seacrest West's pristine white sands.
  • Private Heated Pool & Spa: Enjoy a luxurious 34x16 ft pool with a bubbling spa.
  • Carriage House: Includes a king bedroom, bunk room, full kitchen, and living space.
  • Chef’s Kitchen: Equipped with quartz countertops, Sub-Zero refrigerator, Wolf range, and 8’ island.
  • Spacious Living Areas: Open-concept design with shiplap paneling and multiple gathering spaces.
  • Outdoor Kitchen & Grill Area: Perfect for al fresco dining and entertaining.
  • Expansive Porches: Multiple furnished decks overlooking the pool.
  • Beach Bikes: Includes 7 adult bikes for exploring the scenic 30A trails.
  • Proximity to Attractions: Close to Alys Beach, Rosemary Beach, and The Hub for shopping and dining.

*** YOUR 30A BEACH ESCAPE ***

Experience unparalleled luxury at "Tidey Whitey", where modern amenities meet coastal charm. This vacation home in Seacrest Beach West, professionally decorated by the infamous 2 Hags Swag, was built by Romair Construction—both renowned for their quality craftsmanship and beachy chic décor.

Unwind in abundant indoor and outdoor spaces, by the private heated pool and spa, or prepare gourmet meals in the state-of-the-art kitchen. With plenty of room and generous parking, it’s ideal for bigger groups without compromising privacy. Choose from 4 main house bedrooms or retreat to the serene carriage house for added privacy. With prime beach access just a short stroll away and close proximity to vibrant local communities, this home offers the quintessential 30A experience.

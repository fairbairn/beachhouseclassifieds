Description

30A ESCAPES presents... The Sand Castle! This luxurious gulf-front property in Panama City Beach offers unparalleled elegance and comfort for your beach getaway. With 3 living areas, 8 spacious en-suite bedrooms, a private heated infinity pool, large outdoor spaces, direct beach access, elevator, and entertainment galore, The Sand Castle is perfect for hosting up to 40 guests! Enjoy breathtaking Gulf views, stylish amenities, and memories that will last a lifetime.

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Gulf-Front Luxury: Stunning views and private boardwalk to the beach for your perfect seaside escape.
  • Private Heated Infinity Pool: Relax poolside or enjoy the Gulf breeze from the large sundeck furnished with loungers, seating, and dining options.
  • Entertainment for All Ages: Foosball, shuffleboard, pool table, and communal spaces designed for fun and relaxation.
  • Spacious Elegance: Multiple indoor-outdoor living areas, 8 bedrooms, 8 full baths, and 2 half baths designed to host up to 40 guests with ease.
  • Chef’s Dream Kitchen: Fully equipped kitchen, paired with al fresco dining and a Big Green Egg grill for the ultimate feast just steps from the shore.
  • Convenience for Large Groups: Elevator access, dual laundry rooms, and thoughtful layout and amenities to make hosting big groups seamless and stress-free.
  • Beach Service: 4 Sets (8 Chairs and 4 Umbrellas) Year Round!

*** LIVING AREAS ***

On the first floor, the welcoming entryway leads to a bonus living space that opens to the pool deck. This area includes a wet bar with plush seating, making it the perfect spot to relax after a swim or gather for snacks and drinks. Catch the game on the big-screen smart TV or unwind after a day in the sun.

On the second floor, you'll find the main living area designed for comfort and connection. This open-concept space includes a living room with panoramic windows, a Smart TV above the electric fireplace, and professionally designed interiors. The adjoining kitchen and dining area are fully equipped and spacious, ideal for hosting gatherings. Step out onto the large balcony to enjoy breathtaking, unobstructed Gulf views.

The fourth floor is a fun retreat packed with entertainment. The billiards room features a Foosball table, pool table, shuffleboard table, and cozy seating for relaxation. There’s also a TV, cocktail tables for card games, and a wet bar to keep refreshments close by. It’s a great hangout space for both kids and adults.

  • First Floor Bonus Living Space: Wet bar, plush seating, TV, and access to the pool deck, plus two bunk rooms.
  • Second Floor Main Living Area: Open-concept design with panoramic windows, Smart TV, electric fireplace, spacious kitchen, dining area, and large balcony with stunning Gulf views.
  • Fourth Floor Billiards Room: Includes Foosball, pool, shuffleboard, wet bar, TV, and cozy seating for games and relaxation.
*** KITCHEN & DINING ***

This luxurious home is designed with convenience and entertainment in mind, offering multiple kitchen, bar, and dining spaces to suit every occasion. Whether you're preparing a gourmet meal, enjoying a casual snack, or mixing up cocktails, each space is thoughtfully crafted to enhance your experience.

  • First Floor Wet Bar: Perfect for mixing cocktails or grabbing refreshments while enjoying the pool or relaxing in the bonus living space.
  • Second Floor Main Kitchen: Chef’s dream with modern appliances, ample counter space, and stunning Gulf views, perfect for preparing meals for family and friends.
  • Second Floor Dining Areas: Enjoy meals indoors at the spacious dining table with seating for 10; the kitchen island has an additional 4 bar seats. Step outside onto the large balcony for outdoor dining with seating for 10, all while soaking in breathtaking views.
  • Fourth Floor Kitchenette: Convenient space for quick snacks or drinks, ideal for game nights in the billiards room.
*** YOUR OUTDOOR OASIS AWAITS ***

Step out onto the pool deck and let the breathtaking Gulf views and refreshing sea breeze transport you to paradise. Relax in style by the heated infinity pool, or gather with loved ones at the spacious Gulf-front deck, complete with ample seating for lounging and dining. Fire up the Big Green Egg grill and enjoy an al fresco meal as the sun sets over the horizon. With private beach access just steps away, you can stroll down the sandy boardwalk to the pristine shoreline, then rinse off in the outdoor shower before diving back into the luxurious infinity pool.


  • Pool Deck (1st Floor): Gulf-front heated infinity pool with ample seating, including lounge chairs and tables. Perfect for soaking up the sun or enjoying the gentle beach breeze.
  • Private Beach Access: Exclusive boardwalk leads you directly to the Gulf’s sandy shoreline for a day of fun in the sun.
  • Spacious Beachside Deck: Relax or entertain with plenty of seating and unparalleled views. Features a Big Green Egg grill for outdoor cooking and dining.
  • Outdoor Shower: Conveniently rinse off after a beach day before stepping back onto the deck or into the pool.
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor:

  • Bunk Room 1: Features two queen-over-queen bunks, accommodating up to 8 guests. Includes a private en suite bathroom with two sinks and a walk-in shower.
  • Bunk Room 2: Gulf-front room with one king-over-king bunk and one queen-over-queen bunk, sleeping up to 8 guests. Sliding glass doors provide direct access to the pool deck. Includes a private en suite bathroom with two sinks and a walk-in shower.

2nd Floor:

  • King Suite: Spacious room with a private en suite bathroom with walk-in shower and single vanity.
  • Queen Suite: Elegant suite with a private en suite bathroom with walk-in shower and double vanity.
  • Powder Room: Conveniently located on this level for guests.

3rd Floor:

  • Gulf-Front Primary Suite: Luxurious Primary Suite with a private sitting area with TV, wet bar, gas fireplace, en-suite with soaking tub, walk-in shower, dual vanities, and a private balcony offering breathtaking Gulf views.
  • King Suite: Comfortable suite with private en suite bathroom featuring a copper soaking tub and rain walk-in shower, fireplace, wet bar, and balcony access.
  • Bunk Room: Queen-over-queen bunk room with an en suite bathroom featuring a single sink and walk-in shower.

4th Floor:

  • Gulf-Front Bunk Room: Spacious and kid-friendly, featuring three queen-over-queen custom-built bunk beds that sleep up to 12 guests. This room also includes a cozy living area, access to the balcony with stunning Gulf views, and a shared bathroom equipped with dual sinks, a round soaking tub, and a shower for ultimate comfort.
*** OTHER AMENITIES ***
  • Elevators for easy access to all floors.

*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Nestled along the pristine shores of Panama City Beach, this oceanfront area offers the best of coastal living. Just a short drive from the vibrant 30A and Rosemary Beach communities, you'll find endless opportunities for shopping, dining, and exploring local art galleries. Outdoor enthusiasts can enjoy water sports, fishing, or simply relaxing on the sugar-white sands. With breathtaking Gulf views and a welcoming community vibe, this location is perfect for creating unforgettable moments by the sea.

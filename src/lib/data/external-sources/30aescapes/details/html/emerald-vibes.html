Description

30A ESCAPES presents… “Emerald Vibes,” where coastal luxury meets pure relaxation in Seagrove Beach! Imagine mornings by your private heated pool, evenings in a chef-worthy kitchen, and endless laughter in a home that feels like it was made for your crew. Hop on one of the 9 bikes or the LSV (Low-Speed Vehicle) and feel the ocean breeze—it’s just 2 minutes to the beach! Perfect for families or friends, this retreat is all about unforgettable moments and carefree 30A vibes. Learn more below...

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Private heated pool surrounded by lush greenery – Relax in your own tropical oasis, perfect for ultimate tranquility and privacy.
  • Main house and carriage house with 7 spacious bedrooms – Plenty of room for family and friends to gather, offering both comfort and elegance.
  • Chef’s kitchen with Viking range and quartz countertops – Cook up gourmet meals in a sleek, modern kitchen that’s both functional and stylish.
  • Just a 2-minute bike ride to the beach – Feel the ocean breeze and enjoy quick, easy access to pristine shores.
  • LSV (Low-Speed Vehicle) and 9 bikes included – Explore the area effortlessly with everything you need for fun and adventure right at your fingertips.

*** LIVING AREA ***

Step into Emerald Vibes and experience a retreat of elegance and comfort, where expansive, light-filled interiors invite you to unwind in style. The main living area boasts soaring 12’ ceilings, gorgeous shiplap walls, and a decorative fireplace that exudes charm. For entertainment, the 2nd living area on the 2nd floor offers a cozy escape with plush seating and a state-of-the-art flat-screen TV—perfect for unforgettable movie nights.

  • Expansive living area with luxurious, elegant furnishings
  • State-of-the-art media room designed for unforgettable movie nights
  • Stunning hardwood floors paired with serene, coastal-inspired décor
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Create culinary masterpieces in the chef’s kitchen, featuring premium Viking appliances, sleek quartz countertops, and a spacious oversized island perfect for both prepping and entertaining. Host elegant dinners around the dining table or savor relaxed, casual meals at the inviting breakfast bar.

  • Gourmet chef’s kitchen featuring a premium Viking range for culinary excellence
  • Spacious dining table perfect for hosting memorable group meals
  • Sleek breakfast bar, ideal for casual breakfasts or quick snacks on the go
*** CARRIAGE HOUSE ***

Above the detached garage, the Carriage House offers an additional living area that combines convenience with modern luxury. This private retreat includes a fully equipped kitchen with high-end appliances, making it perfect for preparing meals with ease. A stacked laundry unit ensures practical functionality, while the cozy living area provides a comfortable space to unwind. The Carriage House also features two spacious bedrooms, each furnished with a queen bed and upholstered headboards, ensuring every guest enjoys a restful sleep with 2 en-suite full bathrooms.

  • Luxurious Private Carriage House above a detached garage
  • Gourmet kitchen fully equipped with premium, high-end appliances
  • Convenient in-unit stacked washer and dryer for hassle-free laundry
  • Two beautifully designed bedrooms featuring plush queen beds for ultimate comfort
  • Perfect for hosting guests or enjoying extended, comfortable stays
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Unwind in style by the heated pool, a serene oasis surrounded by lush greenery and backed by peaceful, protected wetlands for unparalleled privacy. The fully-screened patio invites you to relax and enjoy al fresco dining, with comfortable seating perfectly arranged for memorable outdoor moments.

  • Luxurious private heated pool with plenty of comfortable seating for relaxation
  • Spacious, fully screened patio offering stunning views of tranquil wetlands
  • Premium BBQ grill perfect for unforgettable outdoor cooking experiences
  • Convenient outdoor shower for easy rinsing after a swim
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

This stunning property features 7 elegantly designed bedrooms split between the main house and the charming carriage house. Each room has been meticulously crafted with attention to detail, offering a perfect blend of luxury, comfort, and tranquility to ensure an unparalleled experience.

Main House:

  • 2nd Floor:

*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Nestled in the heart of Seagrove Beach, Emerald Vibes offers the perfect blend of relaxation and adventure, placing you just moments away from the area’s best attractions, dining, and shopping. Stroll or bike along the scenic Timpoochee Trail, browse unique local boutiques, and savor unforgettable meals at renowned spots like Café Thirty-A and Old Florida Fish House. Experience the vibrant charm of Seagrove Beach, all within easy reach of your stay!

  • Seagrove Beach Access: Just a 2-minute bike ride away, perfect for quick and easy access to the sand and surf.
  • Seagrove Beach East: A 4-minute bike ride will take you to this peaceful, laid-back spot ideal for unwinding.
  • Seaside Beach: An 8-minute bike ride to a charming, vibrant beach town with stunning views and a lively atmosphere.
  • Grayton Beach State Park: A 10-minute car drive to this natural paradise, offering scenic trails and pristine, untouched beaches.

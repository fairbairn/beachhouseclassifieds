Description

30A ESCAPES presents...“Gloria”, A stunning, pet-friendly coastal retreat in the heart of Prominence South on 30A. Boasting luxurious interiors, a spacious 4243 sq ft to spread out, and family-friendly amenities, this 6-Suite home is steps from the pool and a quick walk to The BIG CHILL! Best part? Includes a 6-seater LSV (Low Speed Vehicle) and bikes for exploring the best of 30A. Ready for a glorious beach escape? Learn more below…

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • 6-Seater LSV (Low Speed Vehicle) & 8 Bikes – Explore 30A with ease, including nearby beach accesses
  • Steps to the Pool – Just a 1-min walk to the Prominence South side pool
  • The BIG CHILL Proximity – Only a 3-min stroll to upscale dining, shopping, and live entertainment for the entire crew
  • Luxurious Bedrooms – Five Primary Suites plus a kid-friendly Bunk Room with en-suite
  • Open Concept Living Space – Ideal for large families or groups with high-end finishes and ample seating
  • Pet-Friendly – Bring your furry friends (with approval and additional fee) for a family holiday for all!
  • Elevator Access – Enjoy the added convenience of an elevator, perfect for luggage or mobility needs!
  • Resort-Style Amenities – Enjoy two resort pools (one with zero entry) and a natural gas grilling station conveniently located poolside!
  • Beach Proximity – Just a short ride to stunning Gulf Coast beaches with sugar-white sand and crystal-clear water.

*** LIVING AREAS ***

Step into a tastefully designed living space where comfort meets coastal charm. Garnished with tasteful decor, the second-floor open-concept living area is perfect for hosting family gatherings and movie nights. Relax on plush seating while streaming your favorite shows on the flat-screen Smart TV.

  • Second-Floor Living Area – Spacious layout with comfortable seating and a Smart TV with streaming capabilities (no cable though)
  • Screened Porch – Enjoy warm evenings on the first-floor screened porch with seating for the whole family
  • Balcony Living – A second-floor balcony with ample seating and gorgeous sunset views
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Unleash your inner chef in Gloria’s well-equipped gourmet kitchen, complete with high-end appliances and ample counter space to cook and serve your favorite staples. Everyone can gather around the large dining table or kitchen island to enjoy meals together.

  • Fully Equipped Kitchen – Includes premium appliances, sprawling countertops, and all essential cookware and dinnerware
  • Dining Area – Dining table with seating for 6 plus a kitchen island accommodating 6 more
  • Outdoor Dining – Balcony with dining table for 6 to share meals with a view
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor

  • Primary Suite – King-size bed with a private en-suite with glass-enclosed shower and direct porch access

2nd Floor

  • Primary Suite – King-size bed with en-suite bath with glass-enclosed shower and balcony
  • King Guest Room – King-size bed with dedicated en-suite with shower/tub combo

3rd Floor

  • Primary Suite – King-size bed with en-suite featuring a soaking tub, glass-enclosed shower, and double vanities
  • King Guest Room – King-size bed with private bathroom with shower/tub combo
  • Bunk Room – Features 2 sets of bunk beds (1 Twin-over-Twin + TRIPLE stacked FULL beds) with private en-suite with glass-enclosed shower (sleeps 8)
*** PROMINENCE COMMUNITY OVERVIEW ***
  • Two Resort-Style Community Pools – Including one with zero-entry for easy access
  • Natural Gas Grilling Station – Conveniently located poolside for cookouts
*** LOCATION ***

Nestled between Seacrest and Seagrove, Prominence South along 30A is the ultimate destination for fun and relaxation. Just minutes away, you'll find pristine beaches, the vibrant Big Chill entertainment district, and stunning state parks.

  • The Big Chill Entertainment District – Enjoy live music, movies, sports on the JumboTron, boutique shopping, casual dining, and family-friendly activities
  • Deer Lake State Park – Less than a 5-minute drive for beach access
  • Seacrest Beach – 3-minute drive for more white-sand shores and turquoise waters
*** PET POLICY ***
  • Non-shedding, housebroken dogs only
  • Pets are not allowed on furniture or beds
  • Must be kept in a crate when guests are not with their dog
  • Non-refundable pet fee of $250 + tax applies
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included in our quotes. It can be purchased during the reservation process or obtained independently.
  • Payment is NOT accepted through wire transfers
  • Waiver required to get access to the 8 provided bikes & LSV. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes
  • LSVs are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest, or Alys Beach Guests have access to one garage, where the LSV is located.
  • This home is not equipped with cable television but comes supplied with WIFI smart TVs.
  • There is an active Ring doorbell camera at the entrance of the home. There is also an active camera overlooking the driveway of the home.

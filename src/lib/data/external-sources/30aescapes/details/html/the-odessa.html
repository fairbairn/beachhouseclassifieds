Description

30A ESCAPES presents… “The Odessa”, luxury meets the sea in dreamy Seagrove Beach. Wake up to breathtaking Gulf views, spend days lounging by the heated pool, and stroll down the beach access (61 ft away). With space for 12, it’s perfect for unforgettable family trips or group getaways. Picture mornings on beachfront balconies, cozy evenings indoors, and meals in the chef’s kitchen. Plus, enjoy 6 beach bikes to explore the beauty of 30A. Your ultimate seaside retreat awaits! Learn more below...👇

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Gulf Front Location with Private Heated Pool – Relax and unwind with stunning oceanfront views and the luxury of your own private heated pool, perfect for any season.
  • 61 Feet of Private Beach Frontage and Direct Access – Step out straight onto the sand and enjoy your own slice of paradise with exclusive beachfront access.
  • Luxurious Suites and Kid-Friendly Retreats with Gulf Views – Enjoy breathtaking Gulf vistas from 3 primary suites with en-suites, plus a custom bunk room designed for kids, complete with its own bathroom for added convenience.
  • Chef’s Kitchen with High-End Appliances – Cook like a pro in a state-of-the-art kitchen, perfect for creating memorable meals with family and friends.
  • Expansive Balconies Featuring Unforgettable Sunsets – Soak in the golden hues of the sunset from spacious balconies that offer serene and picturesque views.
  • 6 Beach Bikes to Stroll 30A – Explore the beauty of 30A with included bikes, perfect for leisurely rides along the scenic coastline.

*** LIVING AREA ***

"The Odessa" is a stunning retreat that perfectly combines coastal elegance with family-friendly comfort. Featuring a picturesque and spacious setting, this home is designed to impress. The open-concept main living area boasts breathtaking Gulf views, a cozy gas fireplace, and a sleek flat-screen TV, creating the ideal space for relaxation and entertainment. On the first floor, an additional living area with a stylish wet bar opens directly to the porch and pool, offering seamless access to ultimate relaxation and memorable moments by the water. On the second floor, you'll find a cozy sitting room equipped with a mini fridge and a ceiling fan, perfect for unwinding after a day in the sun. Every detail of "The Odessa" is crafted to elevate your coastal getaway.

  • Stunning main living room with breathtaking Gulf views, a cozy fireplace, and a large flat-screen TV for ultimate relaxation
  • Elegant designer interiors featuring upscale beach chic décor, blending style and comfort seamlessly
  • Spacious additional living area on the 1st floor, complete with a wet bar and direct access to a charming porch and sparkling pool
  • Cozy sitting room on the 2nd floor, featuring a mini fridge and a ceiling fan for extra comfort
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Bring your loved ones together in the stunning chef’s kitchen, designed for functionality and style. This culinary haven features high-end stainless steel appliances, a professional-grade six-burner gas range, and everything you need to create memorable meals. The elegant dining table comfortably seats 8 guests, with an additional 5 seats at the spacious kitchen island—perfect for casual conversations or quick bites. For even more entertaining options, the 1st-floor living area offers a cozy dining table for 4 alongside a sleek wet bar, creating the ultimate space for relaxed gatherings and unforgettable moments.

  • Modern, state-of-the-art Chef's kitchen equipped with premium stainless steel appliances for effortless cooking
  • Spacious dining table seating 8, complemented by additional seating for 5 at the sleek kitchen island
  • Cozy dining nook for 4 on the 1st floor, perfectly situated by the living area, and a fully stocked wet bar
  • Beautiful custom shaker cabinets and a generously sized refrigerator to store all your beach-day essentials
*** ADDITIONAL HOME AMENITY ***
  • Elevator access to all floors, ensuring convenience and ease for all guests, whether carrying luggage or simply enhancing mobility

*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Nestled in the heart of Seagrove Beach, “The Odessa” is your gateway to the best the area has to offer. Enjoy effortless access to top attractions, gourmet dining, and charming boutique shops. Discover nearby gems like the iconic Seaside and picturesque Watersound, or immerse yourself in adventure with paddleboarding on the serene waters of Eastern Lake. Your perfect coastal getaway starts here.

  • Timpoochee Trail (4-minute drive) – Perfect for biking or jogging, this scenic trail offers a peaceful escape surrounded by natural beauty
  • Old Florida Fish House (4-minute drive) – A local favorite offering fresh seafood and a laid-back atmosphere, perfect for a casual meal
  • Café Thirty-A (6-minute drive) – A popular spot for fine dining, known for its elegant ambiance and delicious coastal-inspired cuisine
  • Greenway Station (4-minute drive) – Enjoy a variety of shops and restaurants that capture the essence of coastal flavors
  • Seaside (13-minute drive) – A charming coastal town with a great choice of shops and restaurants

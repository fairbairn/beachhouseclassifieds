Description

30A Escapes presents... “HP 16E Beach Treat” at High Pointe Resort, located in Seacrest Beach! Wake up to stunning ocean views, stroll to the community pool, and feel the salty breeze as you explore nearby Rosemary and Alys Beach. With world-class dining, unique shopping, and seasonal beach chair setups waiting for you, this 3-suite unit is more than a getaway—it’s your perfect escape. Soak up the sun, savor the moments, and let the beach vibes take over. Ready to unwind? Learn more below...

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Gulf-Front, Ground-Level Condo with Stunning Views – Wake up to the soothing sound of waves and breathtaking ocean vistas right outside your door. This is beachside living at its finest.
  • Lagoon-Style Community Pool with Waterfall, Hot Tub, and Kiddie Pool – Relax in a tropical paradise with a resort-style pool that feels like an escape to your own private oasis. Fun for the whole family!
  • Steps to Rosemary Beach, Alys Beach, and Peddlers Pavilion – Immerse yourself in the charm of nearby coastal towns, with boutique shops, dining, and entertainment just a short stroll away.
  • Beach Chair Set-Up – Enjoy stress-free beach days with chairs ready and waiting for you, so you can sink into relaxation without lifting a finger.
  • Spacious Living Area and a Fully Equipped Modern Kitchen – Feel at home with plenty of space to unwind, cook, and gather—perfect for creating unforgettable memories with loved ones.

*** LIVING AREA ***

This condo offers a bright and airy open-concept living space, perfect for unwinding after a day at the beach. The floor-to-ceiling windows provide panoramic Gulf views, and the patio opens directly to lush green spaces, perfect for kids to play while you relax.

  • Expansive open-concept living room with stunning, panoramic Gulf views that will take your breath away
  • Elegant flat-screen TV paired with plush, cozy seating made for ultimate relaxation and quality family time
  • Oversized game table designed for unforgettable family game nights filled with laughter and fun
  • Outdoor dining table for 4 on the patio, plus 4 extra seats for enjoying meals with a view
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

The fully equipped kitchen is a chef's dream, featuring state-of-the-art appliances, sleek finishes, and a spacious, open layout perfect for preparing meals with ease. Gather your group around the elegant dining table that comfortably seats 8, or enjoy a more relaxed atmosphere at the versatile game table, ideal for casual meals, board games, or puzzles.

  • Stunning modern kitchen with sleek stainless steel appliances, combining style and functionality for a polished, sophisticated look
  • Expansive dining room table for 8 designed to bring everyone together, perfect for hosting unforgettable family dinners and celebrations
  • Multi-purpose game table, crafted for endless entertainment—whether it’s board games, cards, or quality time with loved ones, the fun never stops
  • Breakfast bar for 3, ideal for casual meals or morning coffee chats
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

Every bedroom is carefully crafted to offer a peaceful retreat, perfect for unwinding after a day of adventure. With plush linens, elegant décor, and plenty of storage, the space strikes a perfect balance between comfort and style. The bathrooms are equipped with spa-inspired features, providing a serene spot to relax and recharge.

  • Primary King Suite: Relax in luxury with a King-size bed, a private En-suite featuring a double vanity, a serene garden tub, and breathtaking Gulf views to wake up to.
  • Queen Guest Suite (Left): Comfortably sleeps 3 with a cozy Queen bed, a Twin bed, and a private En-suite bathroom for added convenience.
  • Queen Guest Suite (Right): Sleeps 3 with a Queen bed and Twin bed, plus an attached full bathroom easily accessible from the hallway for seamless functionality.
*** HIGH POINTE RESORT OVERVIEW ***

High Pointe Resort delivers everything you need for seaside serenity and luxurious living. You’re steps away from countless amenities that enhance the ultimate vacation experience.

  • Exclusive Gulf Side Resort in a Gated Community in Seacrest Beach.
  • Expansive Pool Deck Featuring a large Pool, Serene Waterfall, Kiddie Pool, and Relaxing Hot Tub
  • Beach Front Bar and Grill – Crabby Steve’s, Serving Delicious Meals with Stunning Ocean Views
  • Exclusive Private Beach Boardwalk for Convenience and Privacy
  • Amenity-rich 15-Acre Tropical Paradise Perfect for Families and Beach Enthusiasts
  • Well-Maintained Tennis Court and Four Pickleball Courts for Sports Lovers
  • Children's Playground for the Little Ones
  • Shuffleboard Court for Family Fun
  • BBQ Grills - Charcoal for Outdoor Dining
*** LOCATION AND COMMUNITY ***

Ideally situated between the charming Rosemary Beach and the picturesque Alys Beach, “HP 16E Beach Treat” boasts an unbeatable location in the heart of 30A. Stroll to boutique shops, savor gourmet dining experiences, or hop on a bike to explore the stunning scenic pathways nearby. Your perfect coastal getaway starts here.

  • Peddlers Pavilion (Right Across the Street) – A lively gathering place, offering a diverse selection of vendors that appeal to both locals and tourists, complemented by live music on most summer evenings.
  • Rosemary Beach (6 min walk) – Cobblestone streets, boutique shops, and a relaxed vibe.
  • Alys Beach (0.9 mile) – Elegant architecture and serene atmosphere.
  • Carillon Downtown & Market Street (8-minute drive) – a vibrant hub for shopping and dining.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included in our quotes. It can be purchased during the reservation process or obtained independently.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • Bicycle rentals are available for an additional fee and managed by La Dolce Vita.
  • LSVs are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest (including High Pointe), or Alys Beach.
  • High Pointe HOA does not allow unregistered guests or visitors on-site.
  • This property has 1 included beach set-up available 3/6 to 10/31, featuring 2 beach chairs and 1 umbrella.

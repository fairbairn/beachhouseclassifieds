Description

30A ESCAPES presents…"HollieBird House", your slice of paradise in Watersound West. Freshly updated, this home offers the perfect blend of comfort and style. Wake up to the sound of ocean breezes, stroll down a private path to sugar-white sands, and let time slow down as you bike scenic trails (4 bikes included!). This 3-bedroom retreat, designed for up to 11 guests, feels like home with a touch of magic—coastal charm, cozy spaces, and room to gather or simply breathe. Tucked in a gated haven, this is where memories unfold. Your getaway starts here! Learn more below…👇

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Newly Furnished & Updated Home – Enjoy modern comfort and style in a home that’s been freshly updated with brand-new furnishings.
  • Steps to Private Beach Walk-Over – Experience the ultimate serenity with easy access to a secluded beach, just moments away from your doorstep.
  • Resort-Style Pool Across the Street – Dive into relaxation at the luxurious pool, perfect for unwinding after a day of adventures.
  • Gated Watersound West Community – Enjoy peace of mind and exclusivity in this prestigious, secure coastal community.
  • Multiple Outdoor Entertaining Spaces – Host unforgettable gatherings or simply soak up the sun in beautifully designed outdoor areas.
  • Sleeps 11 Comfortably with 3 Suites – Bring the whole family or a group of friends—there’s plenty of space for everyone to relax in comfort.

*** LIVING AREA ***

A welcoming open floor plan creates a bright and inviting living space perfect for relaxation and quality time. The spacious main living area features ample seating for everyone, adorned with stylish and comfortable furnishings. Large windows flood the room with natural light, highlighting the beautiful hardwood flooring throughout the home. On the 3rd floor, a cozy secondary living area offers a relaxed hangout space with a twin daybed, perfect for lounging, reading, or accommodating an extra guest.

  • Spacious and inviting living room featuring a large, cozy sectional perfect for relaxing or entertaining
  • Bright and airy open-concept design seamlessly connecting the living, kitchen, and dining areas for effortless flow
  • Dedicated office nook, ideal for remote work, studying, or quiet reflection
  • Bonus 3rd-floor living area with a twin daybed and TV, offering a flexible space for lounging or additional sleeping
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

The fully equipped kitchen boasts premium stainless steel appliances, sleek modern fixtures, and an abundance of counter space, perfect for all your culinary creations. The adjoining dining area offers elegant seating for six, complemented by additional seating for two at the stylish kitchen island, creating a seamless space to cook, dine, and entertain effortlessly.

  • Premium stainless steel appliances for seamless and efficient meal preparation
  • Elegant dining table seating for 6, complemented by a stylish island with seating for 2
  • Outdoor side patio featuring a high-end grill and a dining table for 6, perfect for al fresco meals
  • Meticulously designed with modern, sleek finishes and a highly functional, open layout

Experience the ultimate in outdoor living with multiple serene spaces designed to let you fully embrace the Florida sunshine. Start your day with a fresh cup of coffee on the tranquil screened-in porch, surrounded by nature. After a fun-filled beach day, refresh yourself in the luxurious private outdoor shower. Every detail of this home is crafted to provide the perfect blend of comfort, style, and premium outdoor enjoyment.

  • Luxurious resort-style community pool just steps from your front door
  • Relax on the screened-in porch or entertain in the expansive outdoor living spaces

*** LOCATION AND COMMUNITY ***

Watersound West is renowned for its exclusivity, stunning natural beauty, and access to 30A’s top attractions. Stroll over the dunes to the sugar white sands of the Emerald Coast or cruise the scenic route on one of the provided bikes. Nearby attractions include Deer Lake State Park, boutique shopping in Seaside, and waterfront dining options in the vibrant 30A community.

  • WaterSound Private Beach Walk-Over - Just steps away, offering serene ocean views and the perfect place to unwind with the sound of waves.
  • Deer Lake State Park - A 5-minute bike ride to a peaceful haven of untouched nature, ideal for a relaxing escape into scenic trails.
  • Seagrove Beach - Only a 4-minute drive to this charming spot, full of laid-back vibes and beautiful sandy shores.
  • Seaside Town Center - A 10-minute drive to a vibrant, picturesque community with unique shops, dining, and a lively coastal atmosphere.

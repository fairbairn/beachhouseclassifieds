Description

30A ESCAPES presents…“Ocean's Six,” a luxury condo in the heart of Rosemary Beach. Overlooking North Barrett Square, this chic retreat offers 2 balconies, designer furnishings, and an unbeatable location just steps from shops, restaurants, and 4 exclusive pools, including a heated indoor option. Includes beach bikes and private parking! Learn more below…👇

  • Private Garage Parking Spot for 1 vehicle

*** ABOUT OCEAN'S SIX ***

Welcome to your dream beach escape at Ocean's Six! This spacious, stylishly designed condo combines luxury, comfort, and location, making it the ultimate getaway for couples, families, or friends. From its stunning interiors to unparalleled community amenities, "Ocean's Six" guarantees a vacation experience you'll cherish forever.

*** LIVING AREA ***

Step into the light-filled open-concept living space, featuring distinctive designer furnishings and modern coastal charm. Relax on the plush couch after a day at the beach while enjoying your favorite shows on the large flat-screen TV. The living area seamlessly flows into the dining space and kitchen for easy entertaining. The furnished balcony off of the living area is the perfect spot for morning coffee or sunsets and overlooks Barrett Square.

  • Spacious Living Area with Cozy Couch and Ample Seating
  • Flat-Screen TV for Entertainment
  • Furnished Balcony with Scenic Views of Barrett Square
*** KITCHEN & DINING ***

Elevate your culinary experience in the fully equipped kitchen boasting natural stone countertops, stainless steel appliances, and all the tools you need to whip up a gourmet meal or quick beach snack. Gather around the elegant dining table that comfortably seats 6 or enjoy casual bites at the breakfast bar for 4.

  • Fully Stocked Kitchen with Modern Stainless Steel Appliances
  • Dining Table for 6 and Breakfast Bar for 4
  • Washer & Dryer in Kitchen Pantry
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***
  • Primary King Suite: King bed, flat-screen TV, dedicated workspace, and spa-like En-suite with dual vanities, soaking tub and walk-in shower
  • King Guest Suite: King bed, flat-screen TV, private balcony access and En-suite bathroom with single vanity and shower/tub combo.
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Nestled in the heart of Rosemary Beach, “Ocean's Six” offers unparalleled convenience and charm. Step outside and find yourself moments away from cozy coffee shops, gourmet restaurants, and unique boutique stores. Take advantage of the included bikes to explore the picturesque surroundings, or enjoy a quick 2-minute stroll to the serene Cabana Pool. With easy access to scenic trails, lush parks, and the breathtaking Emerald Coast, this retreat perfectly blends relaxation and adventure, ensuring an unforgettable getaway.

  • Rosemary Beach Community: Beachside destination with award-winning architecture
  • Racquet Club – one of the world’s top 75 tennis spots
  • Multiple parks & greens with playgrounds, a splash pad, butterfly park, and hidden boardwalks with fountains
  • Fitness Center with massage and yoga
  • A Short Elevator Ride to Barrett Square
  • 3 Minute Walk to Cabana Pool
  • 4 Minute Walk to the Heated Sky Pool
  • 5 Minute Walk to Fitness Center and Tennis Courts
4 RESORT POOLS
  • Barbados Pool: Gulf-side, French West Indies style with a fountain
  • Coquina Pool: Gulf-side, with a relaxing negative edge overflow
  • Sky Pool: Park-side, indoor, heated year-round with radiant heated decking in winter
  • Cabana Pool: Park-side, cabana-style with a children’s pool & playground nearby
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included in our quotes. It can be purchased during the reservation process or obtained independently
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers
  • Waiver required to get access to the 3 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes
  • LSVs are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.

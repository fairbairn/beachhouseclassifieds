Description

30A ESCAPES presents…"Hammock By The Sea," where coastal charm meets pure relaxation. Tucked inside the serene, gated community of The Hammocks in Seagrove Beach, this retreat invites you to unwind and reconnect. Stroll to the sparkling community pool in 2 minutes, cruise to the beach on one of the 4 provided bikes, or explore nearby Seaside’s vibrant shops and restaurants. With cozy suites and space to gather, it’s the perfect setting for unforgettable memories on 30A. Learn more below...👇

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • 2-Min Walk to Pool: Just steps away from the community pool, offering the perfect mix of relaxation and activity right at your doorstep.
  • Prime Location Near 30A Attractions: Less than 1 mile from Seaside and Watercolor, with dedicated bike paths for easy, scenic travel.
  • Elegant Luxury Features: Soaring vaulted ceilings, stunning reclaimed hardwood floors, and a gourmet kitchen designed for memorable family gatherings.
  • 8-Min Walk to the Beach: Stroll to the breathtaking Seagrove Beach, with convenient access via Scenic 395 Beach Access.
  • Family-Friendly Fun Included: Comes with 4 adult bikes—perfect for exploring the iconic Timpoochee Trail and 30A’s natural beauty.
  • Spacious & Stylish Retreat: Comfortably sleeps 10 with 3 beautifully designed bedrooms and 4 stylish bathrooms, perfect for a relaxing getaway.

*** LIVING AREA ***

The 2 living spaces are thoughtfully crafted to blend comfort with elegance. Featuring an open-concept design, soaring vaulted ceilings, and stunning wide plank hardwood floors, every detail exudes sophistication and warmth. From the moment you step inside, you'll feel an inviting charm that makes you feel at home.

  • Expansive first-floor living area featuring luxurious plush sectional seating and a state-of-the-art Smart TV
  • Second cozy living area perfect for relaxation or quiet reading
  • Beautiful coastal-inspired décor complemented by abundant natural light pouring through oversized windows
  • Ideal for unforgettable family movie nights or unwinding in style after a sun-filled day at the beach
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Prepare to create unforgettable family meals in a stunning Chef's kitchen, thoughtfully designed for both intimate dinners and lively gatherings. From crafting fresh seafood dishes to sharing stories around the dining table, this inviting space is built to foster connection and bring loved ones closer together.

  • Fully-equipped Chef's kitchen with state-of-the-art appliances
  • Large island with bar seating for 4, have casual meals and conversation
  • Adjacent dining area with a family-sized table for 8
*** OUTDOOR COMMUNITY AMENITIES ***

Your vacation extends far beyond the four walls! At "Hammock By The Sea," you'll enjoy access to not one but two sparkling community pools, surrounded by lush outdoor spaces designed for the perfect balance of relaxation and adventure.

  • Two stunning community pools just a short stroll away
  • Easy access to Bayou Walk for unforgettable paddleboarding or kayaking adventures
  • Scenic, dedicated bike paths are perfect for cruising around the beautiful 30A area
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

Indulge in the epitome of luxury with expansive, elegantly designed bedrooms tailored for your ultimate comfort and tranquility. Pair this with stunning, state-of-the-art bathrooms featuring premium fixtures and thoughtful details, perfectly crafted to exceed your every expectation.

1st Floor:

  • Primary Suite: Spacious king-size bed, luxurious en-suite with a sleek glass-enclosed shower, and a Smart TV for ultimate relaxation
  • Full Bathroom: Convenient shower and tub combo, ideal for guests or a relaxing soak

2nd Floor:

  • Primary Suite: Elegant king-size bed, private en-suite with a serene balcony perfect for morning coffee, and a Smart TV for entertainment
  • Bunk Room: Fun and functional with 2 Twin-over-Queen bunk beds, complete with an en suite bathroom featuring a shower/tub combo—great for kids or groups!
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Seagrove Beach is where coastal charm meets unforgettable experiences. Relax on pristine, sun-kissed shores, explore upscale shopping and dining, and enjoy endless family-friendly activities. Whether you're seeking tranquil beach vibes or lively adventures, Seagrove Beach offers the perfect blend of relaxation and excitement just moments away.

  • 8-Min Walk to Seagrove Beach - Stroll to this peaceful, laid-back beach, ideal for relaxing in the sun or enjoying a quiet getaway.
  • 5-Min Drive to Seaside Beach and Shops - Immerse yourself in the vibrant coastal charm of Seaside, with its boutique shops, delicious eateries, and lively atmosphere.
  • 9-Min Drive to Grayton Beach State Park - Experience the natural beauty and tranquility of this stunning park, perfect for hiking, kayaking, and spotting wildlife.

  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • Waiver required to get access to the 4 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.

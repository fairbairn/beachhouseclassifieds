Description

30A ESCAPES presents… “Sand Pointe,” A delightful coastal escape nestled in the heart of Inlet Beach. Just steps from the community pool and a quick 350-yard walk to the Wall Street Public Beach Access, it’s ideally situated for beachside relaxation. Featuring a spacious layout with two Primary Suites and tranquil views, it’s perfectly designed for multi-family vacations or group getaways. Located just two blocks from Rosemary Beach, this retreat promises a serene and unforgettable seaside experience! Learn more below...

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Prime Coastal Location: Just steps from the community pool and a short 350-yard stroll to Wall Street Public Beach Access.
  • Close to Rosemary Beach: Only two blocks from charming boutique shops, acclaimed restaurants, and vibrant nightlife.
  • Stunning Views: Delight in peekaboo Gulf views from the 2nd and 3rd floors, along with serene pool views from all the porches.
  • Perfect for Families: Designed with comfort in mind, featuring 2 Primary Suites, a king guest room, and a playful bunk room for the kids.
  • Spacious and Inviting: An open-concept living area ideal for shared meals, relaxation, and quality family time.
  • Explore with Ease: Includes 6 adult beach bikes, perfect for discovering the beauty of the surroundings.

*** LIVING AREA ***

The first floor welcomes you with a spacious and open living area featuring cozy seating, a large flat-screen TV, and natural light that pours in through the windows. Perfect for gathering as a family after a day at the beach, it provides a comfortable space to unwind. As the evening winds down, make your way to the third-floor crows' nest and drink in your favorite sunset colors over the Gulf!

  • Open-concept living room with flat-screen TV.
  • Bright and airy space with tasteful coastal décor.
  • Comfortable seating for family movie nights.
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

The well-equipped kitchen is a home chef’s dream, featuring stainless steel appliances, a gas range, and ample counter space for meal prep. The adjoining dining area seats six, with additional seating at the breakfast bar for four.

  • Stainless steel appliances and gas range.
  • Dining table with seating for 6 + breakfast bar seating for 4.
  • Fully stocked with cookware, utensils, and serving ware.
*** OUTDOOR & COMMUNITY AMENITIES ***

Step outdoors to enjoy this property’s fantastic location and shared amenities. Whether you’re taking a refreshing dip in the community pool seconds away or enjoying the Gulf views from the third-floor balcony, you’ll find plenty of opportunities to soak up the seaside charm.

  • Steps to the community pool.
  • Gulf views from 2nd and 3rd-floor balconies.
  • Outdoor seating to enjoy the coastal breeze.
  • 6 beach bikes for exploring the area.
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor

  • Powder Room: Conveniently located off the Kitchen
  • Primary King Suite: King bed, flat-screen TV, En-suite featuring double vanities, soaking tub and walk-in shower.

2nd Floor

  • King Guest Suite: King bed, flat-screen TV, En-suite with single vanity and walk-in shower.
  • Bunk Room: Two Twin Bunk beds, Jack-and-Jill bathroom access.
  • King Guest Suite : King bed, flat-screen TV, Jack-and-Jill bathroom access.
  • Jack-and-Jill Bathroom: Single Vanity and Shower/Tub Combo.
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Sand Pointe is located in the laid-back neighborhood of Inlet Beach, offering quick access to beaches, dining, and outdoor activities. Neighboring Rosemary Beach is just two blocks away, perfect for exploring boutique shops, restaurants, and seasonal events.

  • Wall Street Public Beach Access: 350 yards.
  • Rosemary Beach: 2 blocks.
  • Powell Lake & Nature Trails: 10 mins.
  • 30Avenue and Shoppes: Local dining, shopping, and live music within a 5-minute drive.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included in our quotes. It can be purchased during the reservation process or obtained independently
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers
  • Waiver required to get access to the 6 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes

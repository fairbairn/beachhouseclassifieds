Description

30A ESCAPES presents…“Sea Star,” your ultimate Seacrest vacation rental! This exquisite and freshly renovated 7-bedroom Gulf-front home offers panoramic views, a private oceanside pool deck, daily beach setup year around, and direct, private beach access. Enjoy a professionally designed interior, game room, elevator and 10 bikes to explore nearby gems like Alys and Rosemary Beach. Perfect for upscale group getaways or family gatherings! Learn more below…👇

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Gulf-front private pool – Enjoy stunning ocean views while relaxing in your private, seasonally heated pool, for year-round comfort.
  • Direct, private boardwalk beach access – Step out of your door and onto the sand with your own private path to the beach for ultimate convenience and serenity.
  • Daily beach setup with 6 chairs and 3 umbrellas – Soak up the sun hassle-free with a premium beach setup waiting for you every day.
  • Game room with billiards, ping pong, and arcade games – Bring everyone together for fun-filled game nights in a lively and entertaining space.
  • 7 spacious bedrooms, each with a dedicated bathroom – Perfect for large families or group getaways, offering private sanctuaries for comfort and relaxation.
  • Professionally remodeled interiors with high-end appliances – Experience luxury living with stylish, modern design and top-of-the-line amenities like an elevator that feel like home—only better.
  • 10 bikes included for exploring Seacrest and beyond – Take in the beauty of the area with effortless rides, whether you’re cruising along the coast or exploring charming neighborhoods.

*** LIVING AREAS ***

Enter the open-plan living space where comfort meets elegance. Located on the second floor, the main living area is thoughtfully designed to bring families and groups together. With its expansive layout and stunning emerald Gulf views, it offers the perfect setting for both relaxation and entertainment.

  • Plush seating designed for ultimate comfort, perfect for cozy movie nights with family or lively group chats with friends
  • A fully equipped game room on the first floor, featuring billiards, ping pong, and a variety of arcade games to keep everyone entertained
  • Convenient elevator access to all floors, making it easy to move around the space effortlessly, whether you're carrying groceries or just want a smoother experience
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Create culinary masterpieces in the stunningly remodeled chef’s kitchen. Featuring top-of-the-line appliances and generous counter space, it’s perfectly designed for both elegant entertaining and relaxed, everyday dining.

  • Gourmet kitchen featuring a high-end Wolf Gas Range for precision cooking and a spacious Viking Refrigerator to keep all your ingredients fresh
  • A dining table that comfortably seats 8 guests, along with a convenient breakfast bar for quick bites or casual dining
  • 1st floor Kitchenette with a full-size refrigerator, wine fridge, and convection oven for added convenience
  • 2 Miele Coffee Makers, perfect for brewing your favorite coffee or espresso to start your day right
  • A large poolside dining table that comfortably seats 10+ guests, ideal for family meals or entertaining with a stunning Emerald Coast backdrop
  • Pool deck with a built-in BBQ – perfect for grilling fresh seafood from the market
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

The outdoor spaces at Sea Star offer the ultimate in relaxation. Soak up the Florida sunshine by the exclusive Gulf-front pool, or unwind on the furnished sundeck, where gentle ocean breezes create the perfect coastal escape.

  • Private seasonally heated pool with comfortable loungers and spacious seating areas, perfect for relaxing or entertaining guests
  • Direct boardwalk access to a pristine Gulf beach, ideal for morning strolls or sunset views over the water
  • Outdoor shower for easy cleanup after sandy beach adventures, keeping your space fresh and tidy
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

Designed for both comfort and elevated coastal living, Sea Star offers beautifully appointed suites across three levels, with a thoughtful balance of privacy, spaciousness and Gulf-front serenity.

First Floor

  • Gulf-Front King Guest Suite: King bed, flat-screen TV and just steps from a nearby full bathroom
  • Rear Shared Guest Bathroom: Single vanity and walk-in shower, conveniently located to serve the Gulf-front King guest suite
  • Front King Guest Suite: King bed, flat-screen TV and just steps from a nearby full bathroom
  • Front Shared Guest Bathroom: Single vanity and shower/tub combo, conveniently located to serve the front King guest suite

Second Floor

  • Right Front King Guest Suite: King bed, flat-screen TV and En-suite with single vanity and shower/tub combo
  • Right Gulf-Front King Guest Suite: King bed, flat-screen TV, Gulf-front balcony access and En-suite with dual vanities and walk-in shower
  • Left Front Bunk Room: Two twin-over-twin bunk beds, flat-screen TV and En-suite with single vanity and shower/tub combo
  • Left Gulf-Front King Guest Suite: King bed, flat-screen TV, Gulf-front balcony access and En-suite with dual vanities and walk-in shower
  • Powder Room: Conveniently Located off the Kitchen

Third Floor

  • Gulf-Front Primary King Suite: A private top-floor retreat featuring a king bed, flat-screen TV, sitting area and wet bar with mini fridge and coffee maker. Enjoy a private Gulf-front balcony with sweeping views, along with a spa-inspired En-suite offering dual vanities, a soaking tub and a walk-in shower
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Sea Star boasts an unparalleled location, just steps from the shores of Seacrest Beach and a scenic bike ride from 30A’s most sought-after destinations, including the charming communities of Alys Beach and Rosemary Beach.

  • The Big Chill Entertainment Hub (3-min drive) – A lively spot for the whole family, featuring casual dining, outdoor movies, and live music to keep the good vibes going a short drive away.
  • Rosemary Beach (9-min drive) – A charming and vibrant town with cobblestone streets, boutique shops, and an upscale yet cozy atmosphere.
  • Alys Beach (10-min drive) – A picturesque and tranquil seaside escape, perfect for strolling and soaking in the serene, whitewashed architecture.
*** OTHER THINGS TO KNOW ***
  • Travel Insurance is NOT included but can be purchased separately.
  • We DO NOT accept wire transfers for payment.
  • This property has 10 adult bikes for guest use. To utilize the bikes, a waiver must be signed before the code is provided to the guest. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes.
  • Pool is heated September 1 - April 30.
  • Please Note: Beach Chair Service is provided year-round!
  • LSV (Low Speed Vehicles) are NOT allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.

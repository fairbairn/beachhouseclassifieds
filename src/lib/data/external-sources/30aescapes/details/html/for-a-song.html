Description

30A ESCAPES presents “For A Song”, your perfect Seacrest West retreat! Pet friendly and thoughtfully designed, this inviting home features a private pool and spa, a fenced yard ideal for pets, and a screened-in patio perfect for relaxing together. Admire the unique art collection and don’t forget to bring your favorite vinyl for the turntable! Have fun with the ping pong table or take a quick 3-minute ride to the beach using the 6 bikes provided. Nearby, explore local shops, delicious dining, and the vibrant atmosphere of Alys and Rosemary Beach. Everyone is welcome at this coastal escape, paws included! Learn more below…👇

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Private seasonally heated pool and spa with loungers: Relax in your own oasis, perfect for unwinding after a long day or soaking up the sun.
  • Pet Friendly Home with Fenced Yard: Bring your four-legged family members and enjoy a secure outdoor space for play and relaxation
  • Screened-in porch with outdoor dining and fireplace: Enjoy cozy evenings under the stars with a warm fire and al fresco meals, rain or shine.
  • 6 bikes for easy access to the beach (3 mins away): Feel the ocean breeze as you pedal down to the beach in just minutes for fun-filled days by the water.
  • Spacious backyard with a ping pong table: Gather with family and friends for laughter and friendly competition in your private outdoor retreat.
  • Gourmet kitchen with upgraded appliances: Cook up delicious meals effortlessly in a chef-inspired kitchen with everything you need to impress.
  • Beautiful outsider art collection and music options: Add to the ambiance with unique artwork and play your favorite records on the turntable or stream music via Bluetooth.
  • 3 King + 1 Bunk Room, Sleeps 10: Plenty of room to spread out and create unforgettable memories with your loved ones.

*** LIVING AREA ***

Enter a bright and welcoming living space bathed in natural sunlight. Wrap-around windows frame stunning views of the lush backyard, creating a seamless connection to the outdoors. A cozy sectional invites relaxation, complemented by a Smart TV for entertainment. Whether you’re curling up by the fireplace or hosting a lively game night, this charming space is perfect for unwinding and making memories.

  • Spacious, open-concept layout with expansive windows flooding the space with natural light
  • Cozy seating area complemented by a Smart TV and a charming fireplace
  • Seamless access to a beautifully screened-in patio
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

The gourmet kitchen is a chef’s dream, equipped with state-of-the-art appliances and ample counter space for meal preparation. The island offers seating for three, while the nearby dining table accommodates six, making it ideal for everything from casual breakfasts to family dinners.

  • Modern gourmet kitchen fully equipped with premium appliances
  • Stylish island seating for 3, complemented by a dining table for 6
  • Perfect for casual meals or hosting memorable gatherings
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

The backyard is nothing short of spectacular and perfect for both guests and pets. Unwind by the private heated pool, soak in the spa, or relax on the screened-in patio with a cozy fireplace. The fenced yard offers peace of mind while your dog enjoys the outdoors. For added fun, enjoy a lively game of ping pong, offering entertainment for all ages!

  • Enjoy a seasonally heated pool and spa
  • Relax on a screened-in patio featuring dining for 6, fireplace with lounge area, and a gas grill
  • Have fun with a ping pong table and a large, fenced yard
  • Unwind on poolside loungers, perfect for soaking up the sun
*** BEDROOMS AND BATHROOMS ***

This spacious villa hosts up to 10 guests, featuring 4 stylish bedrooms and 3 modern bathrooms. Each bedroom offers a Smart TV for unwinding and each bathroom comes with fresh towels and complimentary toiletries. A washer and dryer are also provided, making this villa both relaxing and practical.

1st Floor

  • Bunk Room: Double bed + Twin-over-Twin bunk bed
  • King Guest Bedroom: King bed
  • Shared Bathroom: Double vanity, glass shower

2nd Floor

  • Primary Suite: King bed, private balcony, En-suite with rain shower and soaking tub
  • King Guest Suite: King bed, En-suite with farmhouse sink and glass shower
*** LOCATION AND COMMUNITY ***

Nestled in the heart of Seacrest Beach, this home places you within arm’s reach of 30A’s top attractions. Take a bike ride to the beach, browse charming local shops, or head to The Big Chill for live music and delicious dining. The scenic communities of Alys Beach and Rosemary Beach are just a short ride away, offering even more to explore!

  • Seacrest Beach: Just a 12-minute bike ride away! Feel the ocean breeze and soak up the laid-back coastal vibes.
  • The Big Chill: (4-minute drive). A quick drive to entertainment, dining, and live music. The perfect spot for a lively night out or family fun.
  • Alys Beach: A scenic 5-minute drive. Stroll through its stunning white architecture and enjoy the sophisticated, relaxed charm.
  • Rosemary Beach: Only 6 minutes by car. Explore its quaint streets, boutique shops, and warm, welcoming atmosphere.

  • Pool heating available September 1–April 30.
  • LSVs are not allowed in Rosemary Beach, Seacrest, or Alys Beach.

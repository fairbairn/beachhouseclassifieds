Description

30A ESCAPES presents… "Sail Away,” your dream retreat in Seacrest Beach! This pet-friendly getaway features 5 spacious suites, a game room, and easy access to local shops, dining, and the beach. Cruise around on 7 included bikes or relax by two community pools. Enjoy added perks like an elevator, EV car charger, and thoughtful amenities for a stress-free stay. Perfect for making lifelong memories, “Sail Away” is ready to make your vacation unforgettable. Learn more below…

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Pet-Friendly Home – Bring your furry friends along and enjoy a warm, welcoming space where pets are part of the family.
  • Zero-Entry Pool with Splash Pad & Grills – Enjoy family fun with splash pad fountains for the little ones, pool & spa for adults and gas grills with poolside dining, all just steps from your door!
  • Game Room – Enjoy endless entertainment with ping pong, arcade games, TV, and game console connections for family-friendly fun!
  • Located Steps From Boutique Shops, Dining & Live Music – Immerse yourself in the lively coastal atmosphere, with unique shops, delicious food, and live music just around the corner.
  • Elevator, Onsite Parking for 2 cars and Electric Car Charger – Enjoy modern comforts and effortless convenience, perfect for stress-free living.
  • 7 Bikes Included – Explore the beauty of Seacrest Beach on two wheels, making every ride a breezy, scenic adventure.

  • Zero-Entry Pool with Splash Pad – Enjoy family-friendly, resort-style pools with a fun splash pad, adult pool and community hot tub for everyone's fun.

Description

30A ESCAPES presents...“100 Lifeguard Loop East,” a stunning Seacrest Beach retreat! This pet-friendly home features 3 living areas, a gourmet kitchen with a Wolf gas range, and an outdoor grill. Walk 5 minutes to the renown Lagoon Pool and deck, 7 minutes to the beach, or hop on the free tram shuttle! Perfect for families, it boasts 4-suites featuring a luxurious primary suite and a fun bunk room for kids. Relax, explore, and make unforgettable memories. Learn more below...

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Spacious Layout with 3 Living Areas Across Each Level, Perfect for Large Groups
  • Prime Location in Seacrest Beach, Minutes from Rosemary Beach
  • 4 Bedrooms, 5 Bathrooms, Sleeps 15 Comfortably
  • Custom Kitchen Equipped with Wolf Gas Range and Modern Appliances
  • Outdoor Gas Grilling Station and Covered Porch with Flat-Screen TV
  • Free Access to 12,000 sq ft Lagoon Pool with Waterfalls and Zero-Entry Splash Zone (5 Minutes Away)
  • Free Beach Shuttle and 6 Bikes Provided for Exploring the Stunning 30A Area
  • Pet-Friendly (Fees and Restrictions Apply)

*** LIVING AREAS ***

Step into the bright and open main living area, designed for gathering and relaxation. Plush seating and a large smart TV make it the perfect place to unwind after a day of beach adventures. The seamless flow between the living, dining, and kitchen spaces creates a welcoming atmosphere for your group. A convenient wet bar on the 1st floor adds an extra touch of luxury and practicality for entertaining.

  • Open-concept layout connecting living, dining, and kitchen spaces
  • Large smart TV and comfortable seating for family movie nights
  • Bright and airy atmosphere with ample natural light
  • Wet bar on the 1st floor for entertaining
*** KITCHEN AND DINING ***

Prepare gourmet meals in the state-of-the-art custom kitchen, equipped with a Wolf gas range and stainless steel appliances. The adjoining dining area features banquet-style seating for 10, making every meal a special occasion.

  • Wolf Gas Range and modern stainless steel appliances
  • Banquet-style dining table with seating for 10
  • Fully stocked with cookware, utensils, and a KitchenAid mixer
*** OUTDOOR PRIVATE OASIS ***

Experience the best of outdoor living with a state-of-the-art gas grilling station and a generously sized covered porch on the 1st floor. Unwind in cozy seating, indulge in a family barbecue, and catch your favorite shows on the flat-screen TV, all while enjoying the perfect blend of comfort and leisure.

  • Outdoor gas grilling station for summer cookouts
  • Covered porch with additional seating and flat-screen TV
  • Easy access to the lagoon pool and beach tram
*** BEDROOMS & BATHROOMS ***

1st Floor:

  • Guest Bathroom: Conveniently located off the Living Area with single vanity and walk-in shower

2nd Floor:

  • Primary King Suite: King bed, flat-screen TV, private balcony with seating and En-suite with double vanities and walk-in shower.
  • Queen Guest Suite: Queen bed, flat-screen TV and En-suite with single vanity and shower/tub combo.
  • Full Guest Suite: Two Full beds, flat-screen TV and En-suite with single vanity and walk-in shower.

3rd Floor:

  • Kid-Friendly Bunk Room (Sleeps 7): Two Twin-over-Full bunk beds, one cozy Twin daybed, play table, flat-screen TV, and En-suite with dual vanities and shower/tub combo
*** LOCATION & COMMUNITY ***

Discover the best of 30A from your prime location in Seacrest Beach. Take advantage of 6 beach cruisers and bike to nearby Rosemary Beach or explore the boutiques, restaurants, and amenities of the area. Enjoy unforgettable memories as you relax and explore this charming coastal community.

Guests have access to the incredible 12,000 sq ft lagoon pool, complete with waterfall features, a zero-entry splash zone, and a poolside cafe with a picnic area. Take a short stroll to the beach or enjoy the convenience of the free community beach shuttle. Explore the paved paths to nearby boutiques, restaurants, and attractions in the Village of South Walton and Rosemary Beach.

  • Village of South Walton (short stroll)
  • Rosemary Beach (2-minute bike ride)
  • Alys Beach (13-minute walk)
  • Pier Park (15-minute drive)

  • Waiver required to get access to the 6 provided bikes. Additional bikes can be rented through 30A Escapes
  • There is an active Ring doorbell camera at the entrance door of the home.
  • A $250 non-refundable pet fee + tax applies for non-shedding, housebroken dogs.
  • There is an active Ring doorbell camera at the entrance door of the home.

Description

30A ESCAPES presents… "103 E Kingston," your luxurious retreat in Rosemary Beach Community. Featuring a private heated pool, a detached carriage house, and outdoor loggia with a fireplace and grill, this beautifully designed spacious 5 King suite home for up to 12 guests offers everything you need for a sophisticated 30A vacation. Located near world-class restaurants, shopping, and just steps from the heated Sky Pool. Learn more below…

*** HIGHLIGHTS ***
  • Private Heated Pool with Waterfall Feature, seasonally heated for year-round enjoyment
  • Spacious Layout with 4 King Suites in the main house
  • Detached Carriage House with 1 King Suite, plus an additional loft for 2 extra guests
  • Stunning Loggia with Fireplace and Gas Grill, perfect for alfresco dining
  • Chef’s Kitchen with High-End Appliances for gourmet cooking
  • Steps to the Heated Sky Pool and St. Augustine Green
  • Includes 7 Bikes for exploring the 30A area
  • Driveway Parking for 3 Cars – a Rosemary rarity!

*** ROSEMARY BEACH COMMUNITY ***

Immerse yourself in the charm and luxury of the Rosemary Beach community, a stunning beachside destination known for its award-winning architecture and idyllic ambiance. Whether you're seeking adventure, relaxation, or romance, this vibrant locale offers something for everyone.

  • Rosemary Beach Community: Beachside destination with award-winning architecture
  • Racquet Club – one of the world’s top 75 tennis spots
  • Multiple parks & greens with playgrounds, a splash pad, butterfly park, and hidden boardwalks with fountains
  • Fitness Center with massage and yoga
4 RESORT POOLS
  • Barbados Pool: Gulf-side, French West Indies style with a fountain
  • Coquina Pool: Gulf-side, with a relaxing negative edge overflow
  • Sky Pool: Park-side, indoor, heated year-round with radiant heated decking in winter
  • Cabana Pool: Park-side, cabana-style with a children’s pool & playground nearby

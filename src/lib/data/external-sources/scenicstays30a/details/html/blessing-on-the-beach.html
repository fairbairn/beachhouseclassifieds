Description
Welcome to Blessing on the Beach, a magnificent Gulf-front home located in Panama City Beach! With a private pool, game room, elevator, and more, this spacious beachfront paradise is ideal for year-round vacationing and the perfect home for a large family retreat!

Blessing on the Beach is a gorgeous three-story home with five bedrooms and five and a half baths accommodating up to 16. Beautifully furnished and fully appointed, this peaceful retreat welcomes you to experience the ultimate in luxury living. The home's first level features a private pool, a game room, and a cozy bedroom with a large walk-in closet and an en suite bathroom. A wet bar just steps from the pool deck ensures cool drinks are always close by!

Use the stairs or elevator to access the second floor with a spacious, beautifully appointed kitchen, upgraded appliances, plenty of counter and cabinet space, and everything needed to prepare delicious meals for friends and family. Map out the day’s excursions at the large dining table, curl up on the cozy pit sofa, and catch up on your favorite shows on the flat-screen TV. The balcony will surely be a favorite spot for grilling burgers and steaks or just relaxing and enjoying the sea breezes. The third level of this stunning retreat includes a guest suite with two queen-sized beds and a gorgeous en suite bathroom. The kids will delight in having their own space, complete with two twin bunks, a flat-screen TV, and a private bathroom. A stunning master suite offers a comfortable king-sized bed, an en suite bathroom, and incredible views of the Gulf of Mexico. Start your day each morning on the balcony with a fresh cup of coffee, watching breathtaking sunrises over the sugar-white sands below. A full-sized washer and dryer are additional conveniences to enjoy during your stay!

Private beach access from this peaceful retreat will lead straight to some of the world's most beautiful beaches. Relax the days away, soaking up the sun, but don’t forget you’re in the perfect location for exploration. Head west and hit the coastal communities of 30A, with Inlet Beach, Rosemary Beach, and Alys Beach just minutes away. Head east and hit Pier Park, Gulf World Marine Park, and Shipwreck Island Water Park for days of adventure!

Book your stay at this Scenic Stays property today and create lasting memories in this coastal paradise.  Parties, events, bachelor and bachelorette parties are strictly prohibited, and an adult chaperone must accompany guests under 25 throughout their stay. Smoking is strictly forbidden, and pets are unfortunately not welcome and subject to immediate departure if found to be inhabiting a property. Guests found in violation are subject to eviction with no refund.

Great Location
For the 13th year in a row my family and I have gathered from multiple states to celebrate Thanksgiving together. We’ve always rented houses on the beach in Panama City Beach, usually east of Pier Park. This time we chose this house, west of Pier Park and were pleased with the location. The house itself was a bit smaller than houses in the past and the parking was definitely less roomy than before. The elevator was a big plus since the house is tall and slender with LOTS of stairs! The front door only had one key and no key pad and a VERY tricky locking system which took us a while to figure out. This tricky-locking system included all the doors, front and back. We never did figure out the pool-door alarm system since pushing the button caused the alarm to go off rather than keeping it from sounding. The kitchen had plenty of dishes, glasses, pots and pans, and cutlery. Only one refrigerator was a bit of a handicap during the week of Thanksgiving since everyone brought food and the storage was limited. We finally had to resort to using ice chests. An extra refrigerator in the “game room” would be a nice addition. There was no cable available for the TV so no football on Thanksgiving day which was a bummer for all the football fans. The unheated pool was used once by one person. The internet was sporadic and needed a booster since one of our party had to work all week and required a strong connection. On the whole we are an amiable group and our week was enjoyed by all.

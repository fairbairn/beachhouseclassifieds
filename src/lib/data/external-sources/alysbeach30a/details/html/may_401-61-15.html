May 401

3 Bedrooms 3.5 Bathrooms 8 Sleeps 2 King1 Queen1 Twin/Twin Bunk Not pet friendly
  • Description
  • Rooms
  • Amenities
  • 3D Tour
  • Calendar
  • Location

This beautiful condominium on the top floor of The May building offers expansive views across the amphitheater and town center. Just steps from restaurants and merchants, this is the perfect location for exploring Alys Beach. The spacious family room and kitchen provide for a separate sitting and dining area and opens to a large balcony with ample outdoor seating. The primary suite with a king-sized bed is located off the family room and also provides direct access to the balcony. The two additional bedrooms, one with a king-sized bed and the other with a queen-sized bed feature private bathrooms and have views to the East. Each of these bedrooms opens to a small balcony with additional outdoor seating. Finally, in an alcove in the main hallway, there is one set of bunk beds for additional sleeping. Parking for this residence is one designated space in a parking court.

Guests staying in Alys Beach vacation rental properties enjoy personalized concierge services, exclusive access to the Caliza Pool and Restaurant, ZUMA Wellness Center and Café, advance access to purchase tickets to many of our signature events, a stunning new racquet sports venue, and private beach access. Each property includes Wifi, cable TV, YOLO coffee, kitchen supplies and 24-hour security included with the Resort Fee. An interactive vacation rental guest portal will be available to you and your guests to further help you plan your best vacation experience at Alys Beach. Beach chair set ups, beach food and beverage delivery, bicycle rentals, water sports equipment, private signature experiences and family portrait photography sessions (through our Preferred Photographer program) are also available at additional costs – our concierge will be happy to make recommendations.

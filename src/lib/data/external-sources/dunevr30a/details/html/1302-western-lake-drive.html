1302 Western Lake Drive

1302 Western Lake Drive | Camp District | WaterColor, FL
1302 Western Lake Drive, Santa Rosa Beach, FL 32459

Steps away from Camp WaterColor and the Lazy River, “1302 Western Lake Drive” is a charming, coastal home with a screened-in porch, which includes an outdoor grill kitchen as the heart of the home! This 4BR/4BTH home, including a carriage house, is located in the Camp District of Watercolor Enjoy the 6-seater golf cart/LSV for when you are ready to head to the WaterColor Beach Club for the day! 

This home greets you with a beautiful front porch that is the ultimate spot to relax and enjoy the community.  A great corner lot, the house backs up to a natural landscape and sits across from the community park and Bocci Ball lawn.  The first floor of the home is an open-flow floor plan so everyone can be together in the living, dining room, and kitchen. The kitchen features stainless steel appliances, a pantry and countertop barstool seating for 3 guests, in addition to the adjacent large dinning table accommodating 8 guests. The laundry room is off of the kitchen for easy access. There are two downstairs bedrooms with the first offering a California king bed and private bathroom.  The second bedroom offers a queen bed and a full bathroom with both bedroom and hall access.  The master suite on the second floor includes a king bed, large walk-in closet and home-office nook.  The master bathroom offers a soaking tub, walk-in shower, and dual vanity sinks.  All bedrooms and the master bath include flat screened televisions. 

The uniquely situated screened-in porch is the perfect retreat to enjoy the salty breeze with plenty of comfortable seating! This outdoor space comes complete with a TV, beverage refrigerator, sink, burner and grill station.  A Sonos speaker system is accessible in the kitchen, living, and screened porch areas.  The garage provides access to an ice machine for guest convenience, storage, and a parking pad for the golf cart/LSV.  

The carriage house features a fourth bedroom with a king bed, full kitchen, living and dining seating, and a bathroom with a walk-in shower and laundry. 

The perfect vacation home for a single family, small multi-generational family, or group of friends!

- The HOA issues one parking pass per reservation. Guests are encouraged to park their vehicles in the driveway or garage of their rental home. Reservation/Booking Policy
- Minimum age: All properties require a minimum of one adult over the age of 26 be staying at the property throughout the rental term.
- A 50% deposit is due at the time of booking.
- Except for a $250 Cancellation Fee, deposits are fully refundable if cancellations are given 60 days prior to the arrival date.
- Balance of payment is due 60 days prior to the arrival date.
- Check-in begins at 4:00 PM and check-out is by 10:00 AM.
- Cleaning: Reservations made for more than 12 nights require a full clean during your stay.  This will be added onto your total when checking out.  Reservations for less than 12 days, have the option to add a mid-stay clean onto your reservation.

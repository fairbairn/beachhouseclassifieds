Goin’ Coastal offers a refreshing blend of coastal charm and upscale comfort in the sought-after Magnolia Cottages neighborhood. With its professionally designed interiors, soaring ten-foot ceilings, and light-filled spaces, the home instantly soothes the senses. Guests are welcomed by a breezy screened-in porch perfect for morning coffee or evening cocktails, and an open-concept living space featuring plush seating, a 50” Smart TV, and a chef’s kitchen with quartz countertops and premium appliances. Whether gathering around the custom dining banquette or enjoying time outdoors, this home invites connection and relaxation at every turn. Ideal for families and couples alike, the home comfortably accommodates up to seven guests, featuring two elegant king suites and a playful bunk room, each with a private en-suite bathroom. Children and pets are especially well cared for, with thoughtful touches like games, dog beds, and a fenced yard. Extras such as a six-seater street-legal golf cart, beach gear, and access to a heated community pool elevate every beach day. Perfectly situated between The Big Chill and Rosemary Beach, Goin’ Coastal is a luxurious retreat where every detail is designed for effortless vacation living.

A 6-seat street-legal golf cart and 6 beach chairs, towels, and a beach wagon are included. The neighborhood sits directly across 30A from a deeded beach access point — a short walk or quick golf cart ride. Note that the access itself is chair-rental territory (2 chairs + umbrella set, operated by a third-party vendor); walk two minutes right or four minutes left to reach free public access.
Both primary bedrooms have private en-suite baths. The first-floor king has a walk-in shower with floor-to-ceiling tile and dual vanities; the second-floor king mirrors it. The bunk room (twin over full) has its own full bath with tub/shower and is on the upper level. A half bath rounds out the main floor.
The community pool is resort-style and heated, subject to ambient temperatures. The HOA controls seasonal availability — common closure windows include April–May and September–November. Confirm pool status before booking if that's a deciding factor.
The home has a fenced corner lot backyard — a genuine plus for groups traveling with dogs. The $250 pet fee covers up to two dogs under 40 lbs; puppies are not permitted.
Golf cart use requires a signed waiver plus a photo of your driver's license and proof of insurance. Minimum operator age is 25. The cart is not permitted in Rosemary Beach or Alys Beach. Exterior surveillance cameras are present at entry points.

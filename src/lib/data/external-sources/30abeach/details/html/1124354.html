Orleans 3B Description

New for 2026: Elegance meets coastal sophistication in this luxury Gulf-View condominium, perfectly positioned south of 30A in the heart of Rosemary Beach. Located within The Orleans, one of the area's most exclusive and architecturally refined addresses, this residence offers a seamless fusion of modern design, privacy and timeless coastal charm. Just steps from the sand, boardwalks G and H, and Rosemary's Main St, this rare offering is ideally suited for those seeking a refined beach lifestyle with all the comforts of a high-end retreat. Thoughtfully designed for comfort and privacy, the spacious layout features two king bedrooms, each with an ensuite bath and private balcony, along with a charming twin bunk room with its own full bath. Gulf views and rooftop panoramas of Rosemary Beach are showcased from three distinct balconies, providing a serene escape morning to evening.

Proudly presented by 30-A Beach Properties:
74 Town Hall Road, Unit 3B, Rosemary Beach. 
We provide passes to access all 4 Rosemary Beach Community pools, and any of the 9 Beach Walkovers

All towels, bed linens, blankets, comforters, and pillow/mattress protectors are professionally laundered for every new reservation!

Finishes throughout the home reflect a curated sense of luxury: PID high-performance hardwood floors, waxed Calcutta marble, Caesarstone countertops, and custom cabinetry by Trustily are complemented by top-of-the-line appliances, including a Wolf range, Sub-Zero refrigerator, Asko dishwasher and Blanco Quatrus sink. The open kitchen and living space are outfitted with Waterworks and Dornbracht fixtures, Visual Comfort lighting and a blend of clean lines and coastal textures, designed by Cappoli Interiors Palm Springs. With one assigned parking space in the enclosed garage and access to Rosemary Beach's renowned amenities --four community pools, and 9 private beach walkovers.

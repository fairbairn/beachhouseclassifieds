Yaya's Beach Cottage Description

30-A Beach Properties Presents Yaya's Cottage. Refreshed for 2025. Located 3rd row from the Gulf in the estate lot section of Rosemary Beach, with views over boardwalk F from the 2nd and 3rd floors. Yaya's is a 1 minute walk to Main St and Town Center and receives 5 Star hotel quality bed and bath linens in every room. Yaya's Carriage is also available if extra room is needed. (VRBO #2546346) Subject to availability.

We provide passes to access all 4 Rosemary Beach Community pools, and any of the 9 Beach Walkovers

Property Highlights:
- Impressive gulf views from the the 2nd and 3rd floor balconies.
- Large corner lot close to the Western Green and Pearl Hotel
- Ample parking
- Kitchen dining and living open onto massive wraparound balcony 
- 2 Kings
- 1 Queen
- 4 Bunk Twins 
- 5 full bathrooms 

All towels, bed linens, blankets, comforters, and pillow/mattress protectors are professionally laundered for every new reservation!

This stunning location in Rosemary Beach captures wide views of the sparkling blue gulf waters from several balconies. Boardwalk F is adjacent to our cottage providing direct access to the beach or the nearby Western Green. This four bedroom with five full baths offers three levels of living space. On the first floor you'll find three bedrooms all with ensuite bathrooms: the master suite, bedroom 2, and the bunk room that easily sleeps four. The second level presents the open living, dining and kitchen plus media room. The centerpiece is the large wrap around balcony that spans the width of the home. Arranged on the third level is a large guest bedroom with ensuite bath and a private balcony offering amazing gulf views. 

This large corner lot location comes with a garage space and a wide trellis parking area.  

Yaya's Carriage House (rented separately, vrbo #2546346) provides a detached accommodation above the garage with its own living, dining, kitchen, bath and lofted bedroom. You'll have a view of the gulf from the balcony. 

Designed by Tom Christ, this large Rosemary Beach corner lot offers expansive balconies and porches enjoyed from copious French doors. The Rosemary Beach Town Center is only a block away offering shops and restaurants. You'll have gate access to the four community pools and beaches. This rare close to beach and village location will provide many memories. We look forward to hosting you!

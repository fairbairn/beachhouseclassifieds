Come experience a stay at The Mirage, a beautiful 3-story Gulf-front home that can sleep up to 14 guests. This luxurious 5-bedroom, 5-bathroom beach retreat features a beautiful interior and a private Gulf-front heated pool.

This home showcases stunning views of the Gulf from all 3 stories! Beach access? Just out your back door, you will find a private boardwalk that leads you steps down to our pristine, white-sanded beaches. With your stay between March 1 and October 31 you'll receive complimentary beach chair service of one umbrella and two chairs.

The Mirage is a one-of-a-kind rental, with a private heated pool, and two balconies overlooking the Gulf - you and your guests are certain to have a week to remember!

The first floor welcomes you with an open-concept living area, a full kitchen, and a breakfast nook, along with a convenient laundry room equipped with two sets of washers and dryers. You'll enjoy easy access to the Gulf-front private pool and gas grill via the patio. Two inviting bedrooms on this floor include a queen bed with a private bathroom, and a king bed with a private bathroom.

On the second floor, discover a second kitchen and living area, a spacious dining room with seating for eight, and additional bar seating for five. Step onto the Gulf-front balcony to dine and relax while soaking in the stunning views. Two more bedrooms await, including Bedroom 3 with a queen bed and private bathroom featuring a shower, and Bedroom 4 with 2 sets of twin-over-full bunk beds and a desk area. A hallway bathroom with dual sinks and a tub/shower combo completes the floor.

On the third and final floor, you'll find Bedroom 5, featuring a king bed, TV, and private balcony offering panoramic Gulf views. This luxurious bedroom also boasts a private bathroom with dual sinks and a large shower, providing a serene retreat for relaxation.

Sleeping Arrangements:
1st Floor, Bedroom 1: Queen bed and private bathroom with shower.
1st Floor, Bedroom 2: King bed and private bathroom with tub/shower combo.
2nd Floor, Bedroom 3: Queen bed, private bathroom with shower.
2nd Floor, Bedroom4: 2 sets of twin-over-full bunk beds and desk area.
Hallway bathroom with dual sinks and tub/shower combo.
3rd Floor, Bedroom 5: King bed, TV and private balcony. Private bathroom with dual sinks and large shower.

Seacrest Beach:
This property could not be in a more convenient location with just 6 miles separating it from Seaside, WaterColor, and Grayton, and only 1.6 miles from Rosemary and Alys Beach. If wanting to hop off the quaint communities that line 30A for a change of scenery, Destin, FL is only a short 40 minute drive to shopping & entertainment of all kinds. Panama City Beach, is only a short 15-minute drive to shopping, dining, and entertainment at Pier Park.

Your stay at The Mirage comes with Xplorie’s best activities! one ticket per day, per activity! (over $800 in nightly value) Tee off at Emerald Bay or Regatta Bay, zip through Baytowne Adventure Zone, and sail on the Sea Blaster Dolphin Cruise. Enjoy Big Kahuna’s Water Park, Black Light Mini Golf, and scenic bike rides with complimentary rentals. Explore 30A with ease and adventure!

* Pool heat can be added for an additional fee
* Parking for 6 cars in driveway; no garage access
* Sorry, no pets allowed.

An initial Benchmark signature box of amenities is provided for all guests. For the kitchen this includes: 1 roll of paper towels, 1 dish sponge, 1 dish soap, 2 dishwasher pods, 1 pack of kitchen wipes and liners for each trashcan plus 2 washing machine pods. For each bathroom it includes: 1 roll of toilet paper, 1 set of facial and bath soaps/body wash/shampoo/conditioner/lotion. For towels you will receive: 1 body towel/1 washcloth per guest and 2 hand towels per bathroom.

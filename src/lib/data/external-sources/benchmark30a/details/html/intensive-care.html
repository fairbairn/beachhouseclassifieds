Relax and unwind with your loved ones at 'Intensive Care'. Located in Seagrove just steps from the Gulf, this sensational 6 bedroom, 5 bathroom home sleeps 12. Spread over 3 floors, this home offers true coastal luxury including an elevator, private heated pool, and amazing Gulf views. Enjoy use of the golf cart as well for your stay. And, this home is dog friendly too!

You will love relaxing by the private heated pool with a good book. Head to the beach with just a few steps - the beach access is right in front of the home. The sugar white sands of 30A are unparalleled and some of the most beautiful.

There is plenty of space for everyone to have privacy in this massive home. 4 bedrooms are on the ground floor plus a living area. The living room has a 50” TV and DVD player plus a game table with 4 chairs. From this floor, you can access the pool area out the back door. From the front door, you can relax or simply make your way to the beach!

The 2nd floor features a large open living area featuring beautiful views, a 60” LCD TV plus stocked bookcases and a gas fireplace. The kitchen has gorgeous stone counters, a huge Subzero fridge, plus a Viking 6-burner gas range. There is a separate dining room with seating for up to 10. The terrace off of the dining room has a gas grill and outdoor dining for 4. Off the kitchen is a large laundry room.
The top floor has more room to relax and watch TV. There's also a wet bar/kitchenette. Head out on the balcony to enjoy the Gulf breeze. Just imagine mornings spent here with coffee in your hand as the saltwater breeze blows through your hair.

Sleeping Arrangements:
1st Floor, Bedroom 1: Queen bed, TV.
1st Floor, Bedroom 2: 2 twin beds. Shared hallway bathroom with tub/shower combo.
1st Floor, Bedroom 3: King bed, TV. Private bathroom with shower.
1st Floor, Bedroom 4: Twin-over-twin bunks. Attached hallway bathroom with shower.
2nd Floor, Bedroom 5: King bed, TV and balcony access. Private bathroom with jetted tub and separate shower.
2nd Floor, Bedroom 6: King bed, TV. Attached hallway bathroom with shower.

Beach Access:
From the front of the home head directly across the street to your beach access at Greenwood Ave.
Distance: 100 feet | Time: 1 minute walking

Seagrove Beach:
Named for the thick grove of oak trees lining its coastline, Seagrove is in the heart of 30A. There's no better spot to experience everything 30A has to offer! Feeling adventurous? Rent a bike and jump on the 20-mile Timpoochee bike trail that runs right around corner from the home. Visit Seaside and Grayton Beach State Park to the West. Head East and discover more of Seagrove Beach plus the wonderful coastal communities of Alys Beach and Rosemary Beach. Enjoy exploring the boutique shops, fabulous restaurants, and beautiful state parks in each of these communities.

Your stay at Intensive Care comes with Xplorie’s best activities! Tee off at Emerald Bay or Regatta Bay, zip through Baytowne Adventure Zone, and sail on the Sea Blaster Dolphin Cruise. Enjoy Big Kahuna’s Water Park, Black Light Mini Golf, and scenic bike rides with complimentary rentals. Explore 30A with ease and adventure!

* Pool heat can be added for an additional fee
* Parking for 3 cars.
* Benchmark cannot guarantee/service owner-provided amenities
* Pet Friendly: This property is pet friendly with approval! If bringing a dog, please contact Reservations as an additional non-refundable pet fee will be added to your booking. A maximum of 2 dogs is allowed at a maximum of 15 lbs. Please see our rental policies for further details.

An initial box of amenities is provided for all guests. For the kitchen this includes: 1 roll of paper towels, 1 dish sponge, 1 dish soap, 2 dishwasher pods, 1 pack of kitchen wipes & liners for each trashcan plus 2 washing machine pods. For each bathroom it includes: 1 roll of toilet paper, 1 set of facial & bath soaps/body wash/shampoo/conditioner/lotion. For towels you will receive: 1 body towel/1 washcloth per guest and 2 hand towels per bathroom.

Come experience a stay at Sweet Home 30A, the beautiful 3-bedroom/2-bunk room home conveniently located along 30A in Prominence! This unique property is in the perfect location, just steps separating you from the happening activities at The Big Chill (formerly The Hub), directly across 30A from the 5,000 sq ft pool, and only a quick bike to the beach, you are sure to enjoy this modern home with pristine finishes. Plus, your stay includes the fantastic amenity of 4 complimentary bikes, allowing you to explore the scenic surroundings with ease.

As you pull into Sweet Home 30A, you are welcomed by a darling front porch you can enjoy at all times of the day. Stepping into the home, you enter into an open-concept floor plan with a large dining, sitting, and kitchen area great for all types of entertaining. The living room has plenty of space with extra seats to pull up at any moment (not to mention a queen pullout couch). The dining room seats up to 8, with 4 stools at the kitchen island, and the kitchen has a large island for gathering around at breakfast or preparing meals. Directly off of this family/living area is a conveniently located half bath.

Also on the 1st floor, you will find a large king master suite. Directly connected to this room is a large master bath with a walk-in shower, closet, and double vanity. Steps from the master suite, you find a spacious laundry room.

Moving up to the 2nd floor, you will find 2 more guest bedrooms. One bedroom has a queen bed with a connecting full bathroom, and the other has 2 full beds. Bunkroom “nooks” located off the upstairs hallway are a sweet characteristic of this home. One nook holds a twin bunk bed sleeping 2 more of your guests, and the other has a twin bed. The kids will love these rooms!

Sweet Home 30A is roughly 1 mile (about a 15 minute walk or 5 minute bike ride) to both a public beach access as well as Deer Lake State Park.

Prominence:
With a stay in Prominence, you'll enjoy access to fantastic amenities including a 5,000 sq. ft. resort-style community pool (heated to 80°F from Oct 1–Nov 30, Dec 21–31, and Mar 1–Apr 30), poolside BBQ grills and outdoor dining, and complimentary bikes for exploring 30A. Located just across the street from The Big Chill (formerly The Hub), you’ll have easy access to family-friendly restaurants, boutique shops, a children's play area, and a covered event pavilion for sports games, movie nights, and concerts. Plus, the scenic 30A bike path is only steps away—stretching over 19 miles from Rosemary Beach to Cypress Dunes.

Your stay at Sweet Home 30A comes with Xplorie’s best activities! one ticket per day, per activity! (over $800 in nightly value) Tee off at Emerald Bay or Regatta Bay, zip through Baytowne Adventure Zone, and sail on the Sea Blaster Dolphin Cruise. Enjoy Big Kahuna’s Water Park, Black Light Mini Golf, and scenic bike rides with complimentary rentals. Explore 30A with ease and adventure!

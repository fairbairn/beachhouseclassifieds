Margarita Days is a charming 2-story beach cottage tucked behind Bella Vista on Tupelo Street in Seaside. It's the perfect getaway for a couple or small family as it sleeps 2 to 4 with a queen bedroom and full bath on the first floor, and a sleeper in the living area for 2 children if needed. It has a kitchen and living/dining area all on the second floor with a small balcony in the treetops.

The Tupelo pavilion is only a few steps away to the unspoiled white sand beach. Spend your day at the beach or enjoy Seaside's private pool and fitness facilities.

Your stay at Margarita Days comes with Xplorie’s best activities! Tee off at Emerald Bay or Regatta Bay, zip through Baytowne Adventure Zone, and sail on the Sea Blaster Dolphin Cruise. Enjoy Big Kahuna’s Water Park, Black Light Mini Golf, and scenic bike rides with complimentary rentals. Explore 30A with ease and adventure!

* Saturday to Saturday rental Spring Break and Summer season.
* Parking for 1 car.
* Sorry, no pets allowed.

Rate (excluding monthly) includes up to 4 amenity passes to Swim and Fitness Club.

If you need more room, add Bella Vista to your reservation. It's Margarita Days big sister house that sleeps 6 in a 2-bedroom, 2-bath cottage with large screened porch right next door.

Thanks to our exclusive partnership with Xplorie, every Benchmark Management reservation includes access to complimentary daily activities. Enjoy one free admission per day to unforgettable experiences like dolphin cruises, golf, ziplining, and more.

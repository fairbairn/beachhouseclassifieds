Tucked just off scenic 30A in Seagrove Beach, Sunset on Sawgrass is more than just a stay, it’s where families and friends come together to create lasting memories. This newly built three-story home features upscale finishes, five spacious bedrooms, and six and a half bathrooms. It comfortably accommodates up to 10 guests and welcomes your furry companions. Located near multiple beach access points, including One Seagrove Beach Access, and just steps from a community pool, the home places you at the heart of 30A’s coastal charm and easygoing lifestyle.

Inside, you’ll find a bright open-concept living area filled with vibrant coastal accents and natural light from large windows. Gather on the plush sofas for movie nights or conversations after a day in the sun. The fully equipped kitchen includes a coffee maker, toaster, microwave, and everything needed for group meals. Enjoy dinner around the dining table with seating for seven and additional seating for six at the kitchen island. This level also includes a guest bedroom with a private bathroom, plus a convenient half bath.

On the second floor, a cozy lounge area with a TV offers a great place to unwind. The spacious primary suite features a king bed, TV, ensuite bathroom, and private balcony, perfect for slow mornings with a cup of coffee. Another guest bedroom on this floor also includes a king bed and TV, located next to a full hall bathroom. A dedicated office nook and laundry area provide added functionality, especially for longer stays or guests working remotely.

The third floor includes another lounge area with a TV and a full hallway bathroom. Two guest bedrooms each offer king beds, TVs, and ensuite bathrooms. One of these bedrooms also includes its own private balcony. A second office space is available on this floor, providing even more room to spread out.

Nearby Attractions:
Sunset on Sawgrass places you within easy reach of some of the most beloved spots along 30A. The beach is just a short walk from the home, with multiple public access points nearby, including One Seagrove Beach Access. Golf carts are permitted in the neighborhood, making it simple to explore the area at your own pace. You’re just minutes from local favorites like Café Thirty-A, Surfing Deer, Seagrove Market, and The Perfect Pig, ideal for everything from casual lunches to dinner with a view. Greenway Station is also nearby, offering a mix of restaurants, coffee shops, and boutique shopping.

For even more to do, take a short ride to Seaside and WaterColor for charming streets lined with shops, galleries, and weekend farmers' markets. Families will enjoy The Big Chill, a lively outdoor venue with live music, food options, and open-air seating. And if you're craving a classic Gulf Coast seafood dinner, Old Florida Fish House provides a laid-back waterfront dining experience. Whether you're in the mood for relaxing beach days, scenic bike rides, or evenings filled with local flavor, everything you need is just minutes from your door.

(over $800 in nightly value) Tee off at Emerald Bay or Regatta Bay, zip through Baytowne Adventure Zone, and sail on the Sea Blaster Dolphin Cruise. Enjoy Big Kahuna’s Water Park, Black Light Mini Golf, and scenic bike rides with complimentary rentals. Explore 30A with ease and adventure!

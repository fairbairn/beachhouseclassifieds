Welcome to BMV 11

This one-bedroom beachfront condo sits tucked away off South Walton’s Scenic 30A in its own private oasis at Blue Mountain Villas, which sits atop a sandy dune overlooking the jewel toned waters below. Blue Mountain Villas 11 provides a very romantic setting as a special beach getaway spot for two. Views of the glistening gulf waters extend as far as the eye can see from the covered patio that is at ground level beside a lush green lawn that borders the beautiful sugar white sand beach. It is a heavenly view since the expanse of the gorgeous gulf is also highlighted by the panoramic view of the sky above where flocks of pelicans can sometimes be seen riding the wind. A staircase leads you quickly down from the lawn to the water’s edge where you may enjoy the fun as well.

The main living area is an open living space that features a full size sleeper sofa and a nice dining table that can seat four. A large TV is wall mounted and available for evening entertainment or sporting events. The openness of this room and the wide expanse of glass windows with sliding doors leading to the patio provide lovely beachfront views of the gulf throughout. The kitchen has stainless-steel appliances and a bar with a gleaming charcoal colored granite countertop. The cozy bedroom which is decorated in a nostalgic old Florida style has a king-sized bed and another wall mounted TV for night time viewing. It has easy access to the full hall bathroom that doubles as a convenient Powder Room.

AREA ATTRACTIONS:

Welcome to Blue Mountain Beach, a hidden gem along Florida’s scenic Highway 30A! Known for its breathtaking white sand beaches, vibrant turquoise waters, and relaxed coastal charm, this community is the perfect destination for those seeking great food, sweet treats, shopping, and outdoor adventures.

Start your day with a delicious breakfast at Blue Mountain Bakery, then swing by For the Health of It for quick grocery essentials and organic treats. When it’s time for lunch or dinner, savor fresh seafood and coastal flavors at Red Fish Taco, Shunk Gulley, or Cowgirl Kitchen, all just a short bike ride away. Enjoy some shopping at The Stalls on 30A, where you’ll find unique local treasures and coastal-inspired finds. And when it’s time for something sweet, indulge in homemade ice cream from Blue Mountain Beach Creamery, just a quick stroll from your doorstep.

Whether you’re here to unwind on the beach, explore the area, or simply soak in the laid-back 30A lifestyle, you’ve found the perfect place to stay!

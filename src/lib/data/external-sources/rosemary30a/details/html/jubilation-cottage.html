Jubilation Cottage Description

Property includes dedicated Rosemary Beach® community Beach Access via 9 Private Beach Walkovers. Beach chair service available for an additional cost through RBPOA Beach Service. Complimentary access to Rosemary Beach® Fitness and complimentary court time at Rosemary Beach® Racquet Club also included.


Nestled behind Barrett Square, Jubilation Cottage is centrally located in Rosemary Beach!

As you walk through the front doors, you’ll note the cream-colored walls and cherry wood floors. The comfortable living area flows into the dining area which is perfect for family meals. The kitchen is slightly cut off with a lovely breakfast bar to sit at as you keep the chef company. Explore further, and you’ll find the first guest bedroom sporting a king bed as well as a 27” television, making it perfect for any younger guests.

As you walk upstairs, you will find the master bedroom complete with a comfortable queen bed and a private bathroom. However, you are sure to be even more pleased when you discover the private balcony which is perfect for sitting outside in the early morning hours enjoying a cup of coffee. Exploring the upstairs further, you’ll find the second guest room fitted with a single queen bed with a private bathroom. Same as with the master, you’ll be pleased to discover it comes with a balcony as well, ensuring that all guests have a room that provides privacy and a little something extra to make the stay special.  

Parking for two vehicles.

This property requires a 7 night minimum stay, Saturday to Saturday during spring break and summer and a 4 night minimum stay during shoulder season.

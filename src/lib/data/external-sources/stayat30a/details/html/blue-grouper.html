DUNE ALLEN BEACH – Blue Grouper
Steps to Beach & Restaurants | Private Pool | Sleeps 10

DESCRIPTION
Blue Grouper is a custom-designed beach retreat on the quiet West End of 30A. With 4 bedrooms, a sleeping nook, and over 2,500 sq ft of comfortable living space, this home is perfect for families and friends looking for both relaxation and convenience. Just 300 yards from the public beach and steps from local favorites like Stinky’s Fish Camp, this home puts the best of 30A right at your doorstep.

OUTDOOR LIVING & BEACH ACCESS
Relax by the private pool in the fenced backyard with plenty of lounge space, a grilling area, and shaded seating. Pool heating is available from November 1 – May 31 for an additional fee. Multiple covered porches and patios provide plenty of spots for morning coffee or evening cocktails.
The Dune Allen Regional Beach Access is an easy walk, or you may choose to drive down and park, complete with restrooms, changing areas, and a convenient ramp to the sand.

HOME FEATURES
4 bedrooms + sleeping nook | 3 full + 2 half baths | Sleeps 10
Private pool (heating available for an additional fee)
Covered porches, patios & breezeway lounge
Garage hangout with ping pong & TV
Gorgeous custom kitchen (with all the needed amenities)
4 bikes included
Parking for up to 6 cars

DINING & ENTERTAINMENT
Steps to Stinky’s Fish Camp (and breakfast spot next door)
Walk or bike to 3 Son’s BBQ, Shunk Gulley, Pecan Jack’s, Perfect Pig, and more at Gulf Place
Quick drive to Sandestin for dining, golf, and attractions

WHY STAY HERE?
Steps from beach access, dining, and entertainment
Private pool with multiple outdoor living spaces
Quiet West End location, close to Gulf Place and Sandestin
Spacious home designed for comfort and connection

SLEEPING ACCOMMODATIONS
1st Floor: King bedroom with en-suite bath
2nd Floor: King primary suite with en-suite bath
2nd Floor: King bedroom with shared Jack & Jill bath
2nd Floor: Twin bunk room with shared Jack & Jill bath
Sleeping nook with Queen sleeper (best for kids or one small adult)

Blue Grouper is the perfect mix of coastal charm, convenience, and comfort. Your family’s next 30A beach getaway awaits.

Welcome to Playa Maya, a beautifully designed three-story luxury retreat in Inlet Beach just steps from Rosemary Beach and 30Avenue! With four bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms this stunning home offers the perfect combination of luxury, comfort, and convenience for families and groups. Step outside and discover some of Playa Maya's most unique features. The home offers two private courtyards, including one with a private pool and another with a relaxing outdoor setting. Enjoy lounging poolside, gather around the outdoor dining area, or retreat to the private back patio or balconies for a quiet evening. The home features stylish coastal furnishings, a cozy fireplace, Smart TVs in every bedroom, and an elevator providing easy access. The fully equipped kitchen includes everything to prepare meals for your group. After a day of beach adventures, gather in one of the comfortable living areas or enjoy a relaxing evening on one of the home's private outdoor spaces. Playa Maya comfortably sleeps up to 10 guests across four bedrooms. Three bedrooms feature King beds, while the fourth bedroom offers two Queen beds, making the home a great option for families traveling together. Playa Maya is the perfect upscale coastal retreat for your next 30A getaway.

Area Attractions:

Rosemary Beach is only minutes away, with shops and restaurants, including the Old World-style town center w/coffee shop, bike and surf rentals, and unique shops. Check out the open-air markets on the weekends. 

Enjoy the beautiful sugar-white sand beaches that made 30A world famous. Enjoy walking and biking everywhere around Rosemary, Seacrest, and Alys Beach. Don't miss the newly opened 30Avenue for even more dining and shopping options. 30Avenue is just north of Rosemary across Hwy 98, near both Shades Sports Bar and The Donut Hole. This laid-back, fun family atmosphere awaits you! Come stay and play and see for yourself what makes our community and the 30A area so special! 

The snorkelers in your group will love the new artificial reef called Grouper Reef, deployed in 2017. Located just 970 feet out from the Inlet Beach access, it is becoming a haven for sea life. Each of the 4 snorkeling reefs comprises 40 acres of permitted sea bottom. The Grouper Reef is at a depth of 12-21 feet. We strongly recommend that snorkelers use a kayak, paddleboard, or other flotation device when visiting the snorkel reefs. Sea conditions can and do change quickly and often. Enjoy!

INLET BEACH – Blue Fin
Luxury Home | Private Heated Pool & Spa | Sleeps 26

DESCRIPTION
Blue Fin is an exquisite luxury retreat, designed for ultimate relaxation and privacy. With 8 spacious bedrooms, 8 full bathrooms, and 1 half-bath, this home comfortably accommodates up to 26 guests. Located at the end of a quiet road and just ¾ mile from Rosemary Beach, this retreat offers both tranquility and access to excellent shops and restaurants nearby. Indulge in the beauty of a chef’s kitchen, high-end finishes, and a private pool area perfect for making memories with family and friends.

OUTDOOR LIVING & BEACH ACCESS
Relax in the private heated pool or enjoy the spacious patio area, perfect for unwinding under the Florida sun. Beach access is steps away via the Lupine Beach Neighborhood Beach Walkover (note: includes stairs and is not handicap accessible).

HOME FEATURES
8 bedrooms | 8 full bathrooms & 1 half-bath | Sleeps 26
Private heated pool and spacious outdoor patio
Chef’s kitchen with top-tier appliances
High-end finishes throughout the home
Smart TVs, and WiFi
Pet friendly (additional pet fee applies)

DINING & ENTERTAINMENT
Close to 30A dining
Nearby boutique shopping, and restaurants
Secluded Inlet Beach location with easy access to the Gulf
Situated at the end of the road, with the breathtaking Camp Helen State Park to the east, you'll relish tranquility and seclusion.

WHY STAY HERE?
Perfect for large families or group getaways
Luxury design with ample space to relax and entertain
Tranquil location with easy access to local attractions
Enjoy a getaway where everyone, including your well-behaved fur baby, feels right at home.
Elevator for added comfort and convenience

SLEEPING ACCOMMODATIONS
6 King bedrooms
Third-floor bedroom with 2 King beds and 2 Queen beds
Fourth-floor bedroom with two Queen beds
Bonus Sleeper sofa in the game room on the first level and an additional sleeper sofa located on the top floor in the living room

Blue Fin is more than a vacation home. It’s an exceptional retreat designed for unforgettable moments and luxurious coastal living on 30A.

Please Note: The neighborhood is growing, and with that comes a bit of construction nearby, including in front of the house. While it’s entirely out of our control, we always want to be upfront so there are no surprises. We appreciate your understanding as this beautiful area continues to develop.

Home Highlights:

● Tennis court
● 4 Pickleball courts

● Six folding beach chairs, two beach umbrellas, and a beach wagon
● Free wifi
● Home is stocked with Starbucks coffee pack, creamer, sugar, bottles of water, and a starter amenity package with a roll of paper towels, dish soap, sponge, kitchen wipes, dishwasher soap, laundry detergent pods, pillow spray, shampoo, conditioner, aloe, lotion, bathroom soaps, and a roll of toilet paper in each.

Welcome to Gulf Getaway, a stylish 3-bedroom, 2.5-bathroom townhome nestled in the heart of the Gulf Place community along 30A. This home offers the perfect blend of coastal comfort and convenience, as it overlooks one of the community pools and is close to the beaches of 30A! Sleeping up to six people makes it an ideal retreat for families and small groups.

Outdoors, the home's patio offers the perfect spot for morning coffee or evening cocktails, with the sparkling heated pool steps away. When you're ready to explore, Gulf Place's vibrant community is outside your door. Bike or stroll to charming boutiques, art galleries, and local dining spots, or take a short walk to the sugar-white sands and emerald waters of the Gulf of Mexico.

As you step inside, you will discover a thoughtfully designed interior with an open-concept living space, perfect for gathering and relaxing. The fully equipped kitchen boasts modern appliances and plenty of space for whipping up family meals, while the adjacent dining area invites you to enjoy quality time together.

Upstairs, you'll find two beautifully appointed bedrooms. The luxurious king-sized primary suite includes an en-suite bathroom and private balcony, creating a peaceful haven to retreat after a day at the beach. The second spacious king bedroom also includes its own en-suite bathroom. On the fist floor, there's an additional queen bedroom and pocket doors for privacy. A convenient laundry unit is also located on the first floor to ensure your stay is as stress-free as possible.

Gulf Getaway is your ticket to a relaxing 30A vacation filled with unforgettable memories. The home includes access to Gulf Place's amenities, including multiple pools, a hot tub, a tennis court, four pickleball courts, and free entertainment at the amphitheater. Walk to boutique shops, dining, and deeded beach accesses, or relax with the provided beach essentials, including six folding beach chairs, two beach umbrellas, and a beach wagon.

Property Description
Welcome to your Blue Mountain Beach retreat, perfectly positioned along Florida’s iconic 30A and designed for families and friends seeking comfort, convenience, and style. From the moment you arrive, this beautifully appointed four-bedroom, three-and-a-half-bathroom home invites you to unwind and enjoy the best of the Gulf Coast.

Located in one of Santa Rosa Beach’s most sought-after neighborhoods, this home offers the ideal balance of resort amenities, coastal charm, and modern conveniences. With a six-seater street-legal golf cart included, you can easily venture to nearby beach accesses, grab fresh pastries at local bakeries, or cruise along 30A to explore dining, boutiques, and outdoor activities.

Living Spaces Designed for Relaxation
Inside, the open-concept main floor features a bright and airy living room with a sleeper sofa sectional, providing additional sleeping accommodations for larger groups. Thoughtfully chosen furnishings, soft coastal tones, and abundant natural light make this space the heart of the home. Whether you are unwinding after a day at the beach or planning tomorrow’s adventures, this is the perfect gathering place.

A game table, board games, and a great selection of books ensure entertainment for all ages, rain or shine.

Chef-Ready Kitchen
The fully stocked kitchen is ideal for cooking your favorite meals with ease. It offers:
• Quality cookware and bakeware
• Full sets of dishes, utensils, and serving pieces
• Modern appliances
• Plenty of counter space for prepping meals
• Breakfast bar seating for casual mornings
A dining area adjacent to the kitchen provides plenty of room for meals together and family game nights.

Luxurious Bedrooms
This home features four beautifully appointed bedrooms, including two king ensuite bedrooms, perfect for couples or guests who value privacy and comfort.

Bedroom Highlights:
• Two king bedrooms with their own private bathrooms
• Two thoughtfully designed rooms offering a queen bed, twin bunks, and private vanities
• Sleeper sofa provides additional flexible sleeping space for larger groups
• Plush mattresses, soft linens, and thoughtful touches throughout
• Spacious closets and coastal décor that creates a relaxing retreat

Convenience for Longer Stays
A dedicated laundry room with a large washer and dryer makes extended stays effortless, allowing you to pack light and stay fresh all vacation long.

Outdoor Living
Step outside and enjoy the coastal lifestyle. The home features:
• An outdoor grill for cooking fresh seafood and casual family dinners
• Patio seating ideal for morning coffee or evening cocktails
• Access to a resort-style community pool, offering the perfect alternative to the beach
And with your six-seater golf cart, reaching the beach is a breeze—no juggling parking or loading up the car.

Unbeatable Location
Blue Mountain Beach is beloved for its laid-back charm, sugar-white sand, and turquoise water. This home places you close to:
• Scenic 30A bike paths
• Incredible local restaurants and coffee shops
• Boutiques, markets, and specialty stores
• Public beach accesses with soft sand and stunning Gulf views
• Outdoor recreation including paddleboarding, hiking trails, and coastal dune lakes
Whether you are seeking adventure or relaxation, this home provides the perfect launching point.

Your Perfect 30A Getaway Awaits
With its unbeatable location, thoughtful amenities, comfortable layout, and family-friendly touches, this Blue Mountain Beach vacation home offers everything you need for a memorable stay along 30A. Guests love the convenience, the comfort, and the effortless vacation experience this home provides.

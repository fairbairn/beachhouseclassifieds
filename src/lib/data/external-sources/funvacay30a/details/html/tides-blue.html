Located in the exclusive, gated Bluewater Landing community on the sought-after south side of 30A, Tides of Blue offers coastal luxury just a short walk from the beach. This spacious 5-bedroom, 3.5-bathroom home sleeps up to 12 guests and comes equipped with two 6-seater golf carts, perfect for exploring the nearby sandy shores, top-rated restaurants, and the vibrant shops of Blue Mountain Beach.

Designed for relaxation and comfort, Tides of Blue is flooded with natural light and features elegant coastal décor that blends luxury with a laid-back vibe. The open-concept living and dining areas provide the perfect space for unwinding after a day at the beach or for hosting memorable gatherings. With hardwood and tile flooring throughout, the home exudes sophistication while maintaining a welcoming beach house ambiance.

The fully equipped, gourmet kitchen is a highlight, boasting a large island, top-of-the-line appliances, and ample space for cooking and entertaining. Step outside to the expansive back deck, furnished with comfortable seating—ideal for morning coffee or evening cocktails. Beach essentials, including four backpack chairs and umbrellas, are provided to make your beach days effortless.

Outdoor living is enhanced with a covered patio, private balcony, and an outdoor shower for refreshing after a day in the sun. Each of the five bedrooms is a peaceful retreat with plush bedding and tasteful décor. The primary king ensuite offers a spa-like bathroom, while a second king ensuite, two queen bedrooms, and a bunk room with two queen bunk beds ensure space and comfort for everyone.

Located minutes from Blue Mountain Beach’s pristine shoreline, top dining spots, and eclectic boutiques, Tides of Blue offers the perfect blend of luxury and convenience for your next 30A getaway. Whether you’re soaking up the sun on the beach, exploring the local area with the golf carts, or simply relaxing in the home's stylish interior, this vacation rental is designed to provide an unforgettable experience.

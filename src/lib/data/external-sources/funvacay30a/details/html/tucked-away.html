A Luxury 30A Vacation Rental Designed for Elevated Coastal Living

Welcome to Tucked Away, a stunning luxury 30A vacation rental located in the heart of Seagrove Beach on the highly desirable south side of Scenic Highway 30A. Designed to comfortably accommodate up to 14 guests, this spacious coastal retreat blends modern luxury, outdoor entertainment, and effortless beach access — making it ideal for families, multi-generational groups, and friends seeking the ultimate Emerald Coast getaway.

From the moment you arrive, the charming front porch with Adirondack chairs and a relaxing swing sets the tone for a memorable stay. Inside, the open-concept living area offers plush seating, abundant natural light, and a large Smart TV — the perfect gathering space after sun-filled beach days.

Resort-Style Outdoor Living with Private Saltwater Pool
The outdoor spaces at Tucked Away are designed to rival a private resort.

The large saltwater pool features a sun shelf ideal for children and relaxing afternoons under the Florida sun. Lounge chairs surround the pool deck, creating space for both relaxation and connection.

As the sun sets, gather around the gas fire table for cozy evenings and conversation. Enjoy grilling fresh Gulf seafood on the gas grill and dining alfresco on the covered deck overlooking tranquil Tresca Lake.

Whether swimming, lounging, or entertaining, this backyard oasis is built for unforgettable memories.

6-Seater Street Legal Golf Cart Included
One of the most sought-after amenities on 30A — your stay includes a 6-seater street legal golf cart, making it effortless to reach Walton Dunes Beach Access in just minutes.

Cruise Scenic Highway 30A, explore Seagrove Beach, visit Seaside, or head to nearby dining and boutiques without ever needing to move your vehicle. Combined with the complimentary bikes, your group can easily experience everything 30A is known for.

Chef-Ready Gourmet Kitchen and Elegant Dining
The fully stocked gourmet kitchen is both stylish and functional, featuring:
• Premium stainless steel appliances
• Large island with bar seating
• Wine refrigerator
• Ice maker
• Quality cookware and serveware

Enjoy meals at the elegant dining table seating ten guests or step outside for lakeside dining under the covered deck.

The open layout ensures everyone stays connected, whether preparing meals, sharing stories, or planning the next beach adventure.

Spacious Bedrooms for Comfort and Privacy
Tucked Away offers thoughtfully designed sleeping arrangements for up to 14 guests.

First Floor
• Primary king suite with private bathroom and walk-in shower

Second Floor
• Two king suites with private ensuite bathrooms
• Two queen bedrooms with shared full bathroom
• Bunk room featuring twin-over-twin bunks perfect for children or teens

Each bedroom offers comfort, privacy, and high-quality furnishings to ensure restful nights after full beach days.

Prime Seagrove Beach Location on the South Side of 30A
Located just five minutes from Walton Dunes Beach Access, this luxury Seagrove Beach vacation rental places you close to:
• Eastern Lake
• Scenic 30A bike trail
• Seaside
• Alys Beach
• Boutique shopping and dining
• Local coffee shops and live entertainment

The lakeside setting offers tranquility while remaining near the vibrant energy of 30A.

Designed for Convenience and Modern Travel
Tucked Away includes:

• Starter amenities including Starbucks coffee and bottled waters

Every detail has been thoughtfully curated to make your stay seamless and comfortable.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is the golf cart included?
Yes. A 6-seater street legal golf cart is included with your stay.
How far is the beach?
Walton Dunes Beach Access is approximately five minutes away by golf cart or a short walk.
Does the home have a private pool?
Yes. The home features a large private saltwater pool with a sun shelf.
Is there EV charging?
Yes. The property includes a Juicebox EV charging station.

With its private saltwater pool, included 6-seater street legal golf cart, bikes, gourmet kitchen, lakeside setting, and prime south side 30A location, Tucked Away stands out as one of the most desirable luxury vacation rentals in Seagrove Beach.

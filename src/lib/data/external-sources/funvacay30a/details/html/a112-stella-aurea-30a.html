• Located in a gated WaterSound community offering privacy, security, and resort-style amenities
• Dedicated office space with ample desk area, perfect for remote work and extended stays
• Four bedroom, three and a half bathroom layout that comfortably sleeps eight guests
• Two king ensuite bedrooms including a primary suite with garden tub, walk-in shower, and private outdoor seating
• Additional queen ensuite bedroom with private bathroom and walk-in shower
• Twin over twin bunk space with curtain enclosure, perfect for children
• Open concept living area with floor-to-ceiling Gulf views
• Fully stocked kitchen with luxury finishes and modern appliances

Gulf Front Luxury Living in WaterSound on Scenic Highway 30A
Welcome to Stella Aurea on 30A, a stunning Gulf front vacation rental in WaterSound designed for guests seeking a peaceful, luxurious escape along Florida’s Emerald Coast. Located on the south side of Scenic Highway 30A, this beautifully appointed condo offers direct access to the sugar white sand beaches and emerald waters that make 30A one of the most desirable beach destinations in the country.

Stella Aurea on 30A reflects a warm, inviting atmosphere paired with elevated coastal design, creating the perfect setting for family vacations, couples getaways, and relaxing beach retreats.

Open Concept Living with Unmatched Gulf Views
Step inside and experience a bright and spacious open concept living area where the Gulf takes center stage. Large windows and glass doors fill the space with natural light and frame the incredible water views from nearly every angle.

The living room is designed for comfort and connection, offering plush seating, refined coastal décor, and a relaxing space to unwind after a day at the beach. Whether enjoying a movie night on the Smart TV or simply watching the waves roll in, this space creates the perfect gathering place.

The living area flows seamlessly into the dining space and kitchen, making it ideal for entertaining and spending quality time together.

Fully Stocked Kitchen Designed for Convenience
The fully stocked kitchen at Stella Aurea on 30A offers everything needed to prepare meals with ease during your stay.

Features include:
• Modern appliances
• Countertops for meal preparation
• Full sets of cookware, dishes, and utensils
• Ample storage and functional layout

Whether you are preparing a casual breakfast before heading to the beach or enjoying a family dinner, this kitchen is designed to meet every need.

Incredible Balconies with Seating and Gulf Views
One of the most special features of this home is the expansive balcony overlooking the Gulf of Mexico, along with a private ground floor deck off the primary suite. These outdoor spaces are thoughtfully furnished with comfortable seating, making them the perfect place to enjoy:
• Morning coffee with the sound of the waves
• Afternoon relaxation with coastal breezes
• Evening sunsets over the Emerald Coast

The views are truly unforgettable and provide a peaceful backdrop throughout your stay.

Comfortable and Private Bedroom Layout
Stella Aurea on 30A features a well-designed layout that offers both privacy and comfort for every guest.

• Private bathroom with shower and tub combination

• Private bathroom with walk-in shower
Twin over Twin Bunk Space - 1st Floor
• Curtain enclosure for added privacy

Each bedroom is thoughtfully furnished with comfortable bedding and calming coastal décor to create a restful retreat.

Resort Style Amenities at WaterSound
Guests of Stella Aurea on 30A enjoy access to WaterSound resort amenities, known for their exclusivity and beautifully maintained surroundings.

Amenities include:
• Community pools
• Fitness center
• Walking paths and boardwalks
• Gated community with security

WaterSound offers a peaceful and upscale environment while still being close to everything along Scenic Highway 30A.

Everything You Need for the Perfect Beach Day
This home is fully equipped to make your beach days effortless and enjoyable.

Included with your stay:
• Bikes for exploring Scenic Highway 30A
• Beach wagon for easy transport
• Beach chairs and beach umbrella
• Direct access to the beach just steps away

Whether you are spending the day relaxing by the water or riding bikes along 30A, everything you need is provided.

Family Friendly Features for a Stress Free Stay
Stella Aurea on 30A is designed with families in mind, offering thoughtful amenities that make traveling easier.

These include:
• High chair
• Pack and play
• Games and books
• Children’s toys

These details ensure that guests of all ages feel comfortable and at home.

Prime Location on Scenic Highway 30A
Located in WaterSound on the south side of 30A, this property offers both seclusion and convenience. Guests are just a short drive or bike ride from:
• Seaside
• Seagrove Beach
• Alys Beach and Rosemary Beach
• Boutique shopping and dining
• Scenic bike paths and coastal attractions

This ideal location allows you to enjoy the peaceful setting of WaterSound while still having easy access to the best of 30A.

Your Perfect 30A Beach Escape
With its Gulf front location, luxury design, resort amenities, and thoughtful family-friendly features, Stella Aurea on 30A offers everything guests look for in a WaterSound vacation rental and 30A beach getaway.

From relaxing on the balcony with sweeping Gulf views to spending your days on the beach or exploring Scenic Highway 30A, every moment here is designed to be memorable.

Book your stay at Stella Aurea on 30A and experience the perfect blend of coastal beauty, comfort, and relaxation along Florida’s Emerald Coast.

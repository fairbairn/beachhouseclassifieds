About

Wish You Were Here – Luxury 30A Vacation Rental with Private Pool, Gulf Views, and Carriage House in Santa Rosa Beach, FL

Home Highlights:
• On 30A across the street from the beach
• Gulf views from balconies and rooms
• Large private pool with lounge chairs, outdoor dining, and umbrellas
• Two fully stocked kitchens
• Three living rooms
• Large open concept living areas with ample seating
• Outdoor grill and ample outdoor seating
• Short walk to the beach
• Close to dining, shopping, activities, and entertainment
• Four parking spaces
• Free wifi

Located on Scenic Highway 30A in Seacrest Beach, directly across the street from the Gulf of Mexico, Wish You Were Here is a luxurious four-story beach house with a separate carriage home, an expansive private pool, and panoramic Gulf views. This Santa Rosa Beach vacation rental is just steps from the beach and offers the perfect combination of space, comfort, and style for large families or groups.

The second floor serves as the heart of the home, featuring a spacious open-concept layout with a chef-inspired kitchen, large dining table, and a comfortable living area with a flat-screen TV. Glass doors open to the balcony, where guests can relax with coastal breezes and dine al fresco while taking in beautiful Gulf views.

On the first floor, two guest bedrooms each feature custom Twin-over-Queen bunk beds, sleeping up to eight guests on this level alone. A separate living area with a wet bar, mini-fridge, and microwave makes this level ideal for families with kids or teens. One bathroom features a tub and shower combination, while the other has a walk-in shower.

The third level includes two additional bedrooms: a serene King Primary Suite with dual vanities, a combination tub and shower, and a private sunning balcony, and a stylish Queen guest bedroom with its own ensuite bathroom and walk-in shower.

At the very top of the home, a cozy Crow’s Nest offers a sleeper sofa, mini-fridge, and some of the best views in the house—an ideal retreat for reading, relaxing, or sunset watching.

The charming carriage house is a private oasis with its own fully stocked kitchen, banquette-style dining area, and inviting living space. A sun-filled King bedroom and a guest bedroom with a Twin-over-Twin bunk bed share a full bathroom with a glass walk-in shower.

With a large private pool, modern outdoor kitchen, multiple balconies, and Gulf views throughout, Wish You Were Here is the perfect setting for memory-making on 30A. Guests enjoy close proximity to beachfront dining, boutique shopping, local attractions, and world-famous sugar-white sand beaches.

Whether you're hosting a family reunion or planning a weeklong escape with friends, this Santa Rosa Beach luxury rental delivers the ideal mix of coastal elegance and laid-back charm.

Sleeping Arrangements (Sleeps 16):

First Floor:
• Guest Bedroom: One Twin / Twin over Queen Bunkbed, Shared Bathroom with Combination Tub/Shower
• Guest Bedroom: One Twin / Twin over Queen Bunkbed, Shared Bathroom with Walk-In Shower

Third Floor:
• Primary Suite: One King Bed, Ensuite Bathroom with Combination Tub/Shower
• Guest Bedroom: One Queen Bed, Ensuite Bathroom with Walk-In Shower

Carriage House:
• Primary Bedroom: One King Bed, Shared Bathroom with Walk-In Shower
• Guest Bedroom: One Twin over Twin Bunkbed

Experience Unmatched Coastal Luxury at Sugar Sand Lookout

Welcome to Sugar Sand Lookout, a stunning Santa Rosa Beach vacation retreat where luxury, comfort, and breathtaking Gulf views come together to create the ultimate 30A getaway. Located inside a private gated community and just steps from the beach, this extraordinary home is designed to impress from the moment you arrive.

Perfect for families, multi generational groups, and large gatherings, Sugar Sand Lookout offers six bedrooms with ensuite bathrooms, two master suites, two living rooms, an elevator, a private pool, and expansive balconies on every level. From sunrise coffee on the balcony to sunset views over the Gulf, every moment here feels unforgettable.

Third Floor: Endless Gulf Views and Open Concept Living
The entire third floor is the heart of the home, featuring dramatic floor to ceiling Gulf views from the open concept living room, dining area, breakfast nook, and chef’s kitchen. Step out onto multiple balconies to feel the coastal breeze and take in views that stretch endlessly across the emerald water.

This floor includes:
• A stunning chef’s kitchen fully stocked with everything needed for family meals
• A spacious dining area perfect for gathering
• A beautifully furnished living room with panoramic water views
• Multiple balconies designed for lounging, dining, and soaking up the sun

This breathtaking top level ensures the whole group stays connected with plenty of space to relax and enjoy the beauty of the Gulf Coast.

Second Floor: Three Bedrooms with Ensuite Bathrooms, Balconies, and Gulf Views
The second floor boasts three beautifully appointed bedrooms, each with its own ensuite bathroom and private balcony. Two of these are spacious master king suites with luxurious, spa inspired ensuites. Wake up to soft Gulf breezes, unwind with a quiet afternoon nap, or sip wine while enjoying the view—these suites are peaceful retreats designed for relaxation.

This floor also offers beautiful Gulf views, making it another gorgeous space to enjoy the coastal scenery.

First Floor: Second Living Room, Kitchenette, Three Bedrooms, and Pool Access
Designed with comfort and convenience in mind, the first floor offers a spacious second living room complete with cozy seating, a television, a game table, and plenty of games, puzzles, and cards for all ages. A convenient kitchenette makes snacks and drinks easily accessible throughout the day.

This floor also includes three ensuite bedrooms:
• One bedroom with two queen beds
• One bedroom with two sets of built in twin bunks
• One bedroom with one queen bed

Private Pool and Outdoor Living
The backyard of Sugar Sand Lookout is a private oasis featuring:
• A large private pool
• Comfortable lounge chairs and shaded seating
• An outdoor grill for cookouts
• A private outdoor shower for rinsing off after the beach

Whether you spend your days swimming, grilling, or lounging by the water, this space is perfect for unwinding beneath the Florida sun.

Endless Entertainment for Guests of All Ages
Sugar Sand Lookout is designed to keep everyone entertained. Guests enjoy:
• Ping pong table
• Paddle boards
• Bikes
• Basketball
• Cornhole
• 4 Backpack beach chairs, one beach umbrella, and floats
• Outdoor games
• Indoors: puzzles, board games, cards, sewing kits, and drawing art sets

No matter the weather, there is always something fun to do.

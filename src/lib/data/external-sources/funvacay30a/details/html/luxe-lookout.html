About

Brand-New Coastal Luxury | Gulf Views | Private Pool | Steps to Beach Access
Unwind in Unbelievable Luxury at this Brand-New Construction Beach Home in Inlet Beach!

Welcome to your dream vacation on Florida’s Emerald Coast—where panoramic Gulf views, high-end coastal décor, and a private pool retreat come together to create the ultimate luxury beach getaway. Located in the quiet and upscale Inlet Beach community, just steps from beach access, this newly built luxury vacation rental offers the perfect blend of tranquility and proximity to all the excitement of 30A.

With 6 bedrooms, 5 full bathrooms, and 2 half baths, this stunning home is designed to host large families, multi-generational groups, or couples traveling together in style and comfort.

Property Highlights:
• Expansive Gulf Views from the second and third floors
• Private Pool with lounge seating and coastal landscaping
• Just Steps to Public Beach Access at Phillips Inlet
• Elevator for seamless access across all levels
• Two King Primary Suites with spa-style en-suite bathrooms
• Fully Stocked Chef’s Kitchen with premium appliances and oversized island
• Wet Bar perfect for entertaining
• Six Adult Bikes for exploring the area
• Golf Cart Option – Add a street-legal golf cart and receive 10% off the rental rate
• Free High-Speed WiFi and Smart TVs throughout
• Four Backpack Beach Chairs and One Beach Umbrella Included
• Two sets of washers and dryers
• Starter Amenity Pack with Starbucks coffee, creamer, sugar, water bottles, and household essentials

Coastal Comfort Meets Luxury Living
Every detail in this professionally decorated home is designed for comfort and relaxation. Two beautifully appointed king primary suites, one on the second floor and one on the third, offer spa-like privacy and ocean breezes. The additional four guest bedrooms feature a mix of queen beds and custom bunk configurations to accommodate families or groups with ease.

Each bathroom is stocked with plush towels and a thoughtful selection of starter bath products—including shampoo, conditioner, lotion, aloe, pillow spray, and more—to make your stay as refreshing as it is luxurious.

Chef’s Kitchen and Entertaining Spaces
The open-concept main floor is perfect for gathering. A bright and airy chef’s kitchen with high-end appliances, a large island, and ample seating makes cooking a pleasure. Whether whipping up a seafood dinner or serving up drinks from the wet bar, this home is designed to host memorable moments.

Stylish coastal furnishings and elevated finishes create a warm, inviting atmosphere throughout. And with the elevator, guests of all ages can access every level with ease.

Outdoor Living at Its Best
Spend your days lounging beside your private pool, cooling off under the sun, or enjoying a meal al fresco. Take in the Gulf views from balconies on the second and third floors, or relax with morning coffee and sea breezes. After a day at the beach, rinse off in the outdoor shower and unwind in your own serene outdoor sanctuary.

Prime Location: Inlet Beach with Easy Access to 30A
Located in the desirable Inlet Beach community, this home offers the perfect mix of peaceful surroundings and quick access to the best of Scenic 30A. You're just minutes from:
• 30Avenue – a vibrant lifestyle center with restaurants, boutiques, and entertainment
• Rosemary Beach – charming walkable streets, cafes, and shopping
• Alys Beach – known for its iconic white architecture and events

Explore the coast by bike with the six adult bikes provided, or opt for the street-legal golf cart rental to cruise to nearby beach towns, markets, and restaurants in style.

Thoughtful Extras Included
We want you to feel at home from the moment you arrive. That’s why we’ve included:
• Complimentary welcome pack with Starbucks coffee, bottled water, creamer, and essentials
• Kitchen and laundry starter supplies: dish soap, paper towels, sponges, dishwasher pods, laundry detergent, and more
• Beach gear so you’re ready to hit the sand
• Smart TVs and fast WiFi throughout the home

Whether you're planning a family reunion, a multi-family beach trip, or a couples' getaway, this Inlet Beach luxury vacation rental offers everything you need for a five-star coastal escape. Just minutes from the best dining, shopping, and beaches along 30A, this stunning new home offers the space, style, and setting for unforgettable memories.

Now accepting reservations – Book your stay in paradise today!

Discover the perfect blend of luxury and relaxation at Seacrest Sunset, a stunning coastal retreat located on the highly sought-after 30A. This spacious home features large balconies, an open-concept living area, and a private pool, providing everything you need for a memorable beach getaway. Whether you’re gathering with family or friends, Seacrest Sunset is designed for comfort and enjoyment.

One of the highlights of this property is the rooftop deck, where you can enjoy breathtaking sunsets or sip your morning coffee while taking in the tranquil coastal views. Inside, the open-concept living area flows seamlessly into a fully equipped kitchen with all the modern amenities you need. For added convenience, a second refrigerator is located in the nearby laundry room, perfect for storing extra drinks, snacks, or beach essentials.

The first floor offers exceptional accommodations with a king room and queen room, both featuring doors that open to the pool, and a shared full bath in the hallway. There’s also a unique space with three sets of twin-over-twin beds, a cozy living area with a couch and TV, and an ensuite bath, as well as an additional queen bedroom off this area. On the second floor, you’ll find two luxurious king bedrooms, one with its own ensuite bathroom, offering privacy and relaxation. There is a second full bath across from the second king bedroom.

With plenty of parking available and easy beach access, Seacrest Sunset is ideal for those who want to explore all that 30A has to offer. Spend your days lounging by the private pool or strolling along the beach, then return to your oasis for an evening swim or a sunset on the rooftop deck.

Located just minutes from the best dining, shopping, and entertainment along 30A, Seacrest Sunset is the perfect spot for both relaxation and adventure. Whether you're visiting for a weekend or an extended stay, this coastal gem promises a luxurious and unforgettable experience.

Book your stay at Seacrest Sunset today and start making lasting memories!

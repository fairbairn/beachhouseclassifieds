Your Private Beach Retreat Awaits
Welcome to SPF 30A, a bright and spacious three-story beach vacation home tucked just off Scenic Highway 30A in Santa Rosa Beach. Perfectly situated on a prime corner lot and only a short stroll from Gulfview Heights Beach Access, this five-bedroom 30A vacation home invites you to embrace the laid-back luxury of Florida's Emerald Coast. With a large private pool, airy interiors, multiple balconies, and an incredibly convenient location, SPF 30A is ideal for families, friend groups, and travelers seeking sun, style, and relaxation.

Open Concept Living Designed for Connection
The entire second floor serves as the main gathering hub, where walls of windows fill the open living, dining, and kitchen areas with warm natural light.
Relax on plush seating after a day at the beach, enjoy family meals at the large dining table, or step out onto the balcony to feel the Gulf breeze drift through the treetops.

The fully stocked kitchen includes:
• Stainless steel appliances
• Granite countertops
• Breakfast bar seating
• All cookware, serveware, and essentials for preparing meals
• Spices

Whether hosting a family feast or grabbing a quick bite before the beach, this space is built for convenience and comfort.

Bedrooms on Every Floor for Privacy and Flexibility

First Floor
A fantastic setup for families, early risers, or guests wanting easy pool access:
• King bedroom with patio access
• Bunk room with two twin bunk beds and a sleeper sofa
• Hall bathroom with tub and shower combo
Guests can step directly from the bedrooms into the fenced backyard and private pool area.

Second Floor
• Bunk room with twin over full bunk
• Hall bathroom with tub and shower combo
• Living room queen sleeper sofa for additional accommodations
• Convenient laundry room with full-size washer and dryer

Third Floor – Two Private King Suites
The entire top level is reserved for comfort and relaxation with:
• Two spacious king bedrooms, each with an ensuite bathroom
• Beautiful balcony access from both suites
• Peaceful views and elevated privacy

Perfect for couples or grandparents seeking a bit more luxury.

Outdoor Living at Its Best
Your private pool sparkles beneath the Florida sunshine, offering a refreshing retreat after beach days or bike rides.
Enjoy:
• Furnished patio with lounge seating
• Outdoor dining area
• Outdoor shower for sandy feet
• Covered and open balconies for unwinding throughout the day

SPF 30A makes it easy to enjoy the outdoors from sunrise to sunset.

Walk to Dining, Coffee Shops, Markets, and Live Music

This home is surrounded by some of 30A’s best local gems.
Walk to:
• Gulfview Snack Shack for casual beachside bites
• Goatfeathers Seafood Market and Grill for fresh Gulf seafood
• Local Catch Bar and Grill for live music and great food

Bike to:
• Gulf Place Town Center
• Coffee shops, boutiques, art galleries, and more
• Scenic 30A’s iconic beach communities

Whether strolling the shoreline, enjoying seafood al fresco, or exploring 30A on bikes, SPF 30A keeps you close to everything.

Floor Plan Overview
First Floor: King bedroom, bunk room (two twin bunks), hall bath, patio and pool access
Second Floor: Living room with queen sleeper sofa and balcony access, dining, kitchen, bunk room (full over twin), hall bath, laundry
Third Floor: King primary bedroom with ensuite and balcony, second king suite with ensuite and balcony

Your Perfect 30A Vacation Starts at SPF 30A
With its unbeatable walkability, large private pool, bright open living spaces, and flexible bedroom layout, SPF 30A offers everything you need for a memorable Emerald Coast escape. Relax, recharge, and reconnect in a home designed for effortless coastal living.
